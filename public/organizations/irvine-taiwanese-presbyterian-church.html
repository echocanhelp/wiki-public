Irvine Taiwanese Presbyterian Church (爾灣台灣基督長老教會 / ITPC)

Summary

Irvine Taiwanese Presbyterian Church (ITPC, 爾灣台灣基督長老教會) is a Taiwanese-language Presbyterian congregation in Orange County, California, affiliated with the Presbyterian Church in Taiwan (PCT, 台灣基督長老教會). Founded around 2012, the church serves a community of Taiwanese immigrants and retirees in the Irvine and Laguna Woods area who have relocated from across the United States. The congregation worships at the chapel of Geneva Presbyterian Church, located at 24301 El Toro Rd, Laguna Hills, CA 92637.

History

ITPC was established around 2012 as a Taiwanese-language congregation for immigrants and retirees in the Southern California area, particularly in Irvine and Laguna Woods Village. The church found a home through a partnership with Geneva Presbyterian Church, which opened its chapel for ITPC’s worship services. The community consists largely of Taiwanese-born retirees who relocated to the area after retiring from careers across the United States.

The church has maintained a continuous presence for over a decade, celebrating its 515th anniversary with a commemorative booklet in 2023. The congregation has grown from a small gathering to a community of approximately 120 members who worship in the Taiwanese (Hokkien/台语) language each Sunday.

In August 2025, Rev. Li Fu-Jen (李輔仁牧師) began his pastoral ministry at ITPC, succeeding Rev. Chen Meihui (陳美蕙) and Rev. Zhang Xuanxin (張宣信), who had served the congregation in various capacities over the years.

Leadership

Current Pastor: Rev. Li Fu-Jen (李輔仁牧師)

Rev. Li Fu-Jen began his pastoral ministry at ITPC in August 2025. He brings a unique background that bridges technical engineering and theological education.

Early Life and Conversion: Li Fu-Jen was born into a non-religious but loving family as the sixth of seven siblings. His father was a self-made businessman who studied at Waseda University’s electrical engineering department in Japan. His mother was a daughter of the 台北六張犁黃家 (Huang family of Liuzhangli), a prominent Taiwanese Christian family. Despite the family’s lack of formal religious affiliation, both parents provided a nurturing environment with considerable freedom.

As a child, he occasionally attended Sunday services and youth fellowship at Taipei Chengjian Christian Presbyterian Church (台北建成基督長老教會) with his elder sister. His conversion occurred during his second year of high school at Tamsui High School, when a primary school classmate convinced him to attend a summer camp — where, on the final day, he responded to the pastor’s call and committed his life to Christ. His deeper faith reflection began during his final year at Tunghai University, as he sought God’s guidance for his uncertain future.

Engineering Career: After university graduation, Rev. Li spent 15 years working as a mechanical engineer at an aerospace company in the United States. During this period, his faith underwent a profound transformation. Through God’s mercy, he re-evaluated the meaning and purpose of his life and ultimately responded to God’s call to full-time ministry.

Theological Training: Beginning in 1997, Rev. Li pursued theological education at evangelical seminaries, including 正道神學院, Fuller Theological Seminary, and Gordon-Conwell Theological Seminary. He was ordained in 2004 after completing seminary.

Ministry Career: After ordination, Rev. Li served in evangelical churches and organizations in Los Angeles, then moved to Houston, and returned to Los Angeles, always within evangelical church contexts. In August 2025, he came to ITPC to serve as pastor, describing it as another experience of God’s special grace.

Family: Rev. Li’s wife is a fifth-generation Christian who met and married him at a church in Los Angeles. Before and after Rev. Li entered ministry, she has been his greatest helper — dedicating herself to family, supporting his seminary studies through her own work, and exercising spiritual gifts in musical ministry. Her calling has taken her to Houston, Los Angeles, Taiwan, China, and Eastern Malaysia for music ministry.

They have two adult children: their daughter works at a kindergarten in Houston, and their son works in Tokyo, pursuing his passion for animation. Though separated across three cities, the family maintains a weekly video call every Saturday morning for fellowship and prayer.

Mission Statement

ITPC’s mission is articulated through nine guiding principles drawn from Scripture:

  1. 尊主為大 (Great is the Lord) — Colossians 3:15
  2. 永生神的家 (The household of the living God) — Hebrews 3:6
  3. 心靈和誠實敬拜 (Worship in Spirit and in Truth) — John 4:23
  4. 學像基督 (Become like Christ) — Ephesians 4:13
  5. 研讀聖經 (Study the Bible humbly and seriously) — 1 Timothy 4:15-17
  6. 愛人如己 (Love your neighbor as yourself) — Matthew 22:39
  7. 相愛合一 (Enjoy loving, united fellowship) — John 13:34-35
  8. 承擔大使命 (Embrace the Great Commission) — Matthew 28:19-20
  9. 金灯台 (Golden Lampstand) — Revelation 1:20 — “We hope to become a lampstand in this community, so that all people may be blessed through us” (Genesis 12:2-3)

電腦教室 (Computer Class)

A non-religious community service offering digital literacy training for seniors, taught primarily in Taiwanese with Chinese and English technical terms.

Features:

  • Taiwanese-language instruction
  • Small class sizes (under 10 students)
  • Hands-on practice with immediate instructor support
  • No tuition — only a small registration/materials fee

Courses offered (each 5-6 weeks, once per week, 90 minutes):

  • PC101: Basic computer skills (5 weeks) — hardware/software overview, desktop, mouse, Windows 7, Chinese input, Internet basics, email

  • PC201: Chinese input methods and file management (5 weeks)

  • PC205: Microsoft Word (5 weeks) — professional document creation and editing

  • PC206: Microsoft PowerPoint (5 weeks) — creating professional presentations with text, images, graphics, and video

  • PC208: Picasa (5 weeks) — photo import, organization, editing, email sharing, slideshows, CD/DVD creation

  • PC305: Website building and maintenance (6 weeks) — XHTML, CSS, domain registration, hosting, FTP

  • iPad101: Basic iPad functions (5 weeks) — navigation, settings, Safari, email, App Store, camera, photos

  • iPad201: Photo organization and editing (5 weeks) — Camera, Photos, iPhoto

  • iPad205: Application software (5 weeks) — Dropbox, Notability, Skype, LINE, etc.

  • 總務 (Admin): 林幸花

The 長青教室 (Senior Class) is ITPC’s flagship educational program — a weekly lecture series for seniors featuring talks by professors, doctors, engineers, pastors, and community leaders. With 100+ regular attendees, it has become a cornerstone of the Taiwanese community in Orange County.

Program Details:

  • Schedule: Wednesday mornings
  • Attendance: 100+ regular participants
  • Format: Expert lectures with Q&A sessions
  • Archives: Full YouTube video recordings available for nearly all lectures since 2009
  • Scope: Over 15 years of lectures (2009–2026), covering health, culture, faith, science, technology, and current events

春風合唱團 (Spring Wind Choir)

A 50-member choir performing Taiwanese folk songs (台灣歌謠). The choir regularly performs at ITPC services, Senior Class programs, and community events. Led by 黃幸惠 and 張佩仙, the choir is an integral part of ITPC’s worship and cultural ministry.

拿娥美事工 (Nao Mei Ministry)

An elderly care ministry in partnership with the Nao Mei Foundation (拿娥美基金會), providing:

  • Home-delivered senior meals (送餐)
  • Transportation services (交通服務)
  • Day care center support (日照中心)
  • Home care assistance (居家照護)
  • Long-term care for seniors (在宅長照)
  • Dementia caregiver training (失知者照顧訓練)

關懷小組 (Care Group)

A Tuesday care group providing holistic support for members, including spiritual care, emotional support, and practical assistance.

日文班 (Japanese Class)

A Japanese language class led by 劉純真, serving members interested in learning Japanese.

姊妹會 (Sisters’ Fellowship)

A women’s fellowship group led by President 梁淑慧 and Vice President 張銀花, with active members including 黃美琇, 林霜瑜, 林淑華, 鍾瑞美, 吳瑋玲, and 蘇玉容.
