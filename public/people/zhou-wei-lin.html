Zhou Weilin (周威霖)

Zhou Weilin (周威霖) is a Taiwanese American community figure who served as Secretary General (秘書長) of the Taiwanese American Historical Society (TAHS) and contributed multiple profiles to the 2017 TAHS Publication (台美菁英錄).

Identity Snapshot

  • English: Zhou Weilin / Weilin Zhou
  • Chinese: 周威霖
  • Role: Secretary General (秘書長), Taiwanese American Historical Society (TAHS)
  • Title: Founding Secretary, TAHS (2013)
  • Era: 2013–present (TAHS leadership)
  • Geography: Los Angeles, California (Southern California)
  • Organizations: Taiwanese American Historical Society (TAHS)
  • Publication: 2017 TAHS Publication (台美菁英錄) — editorial team member, profile author
  • Profiles authored: 徐新宏 (Hsu Hsin-hung), 王桂榮 (Wang Gui-rong), 王能祥 (Wang Neng-xiang) (co-author with 楊嘉猷)
  • Preparatory member: Attended first TAHS preparatory meeting, August 28, 2013, Fullerton, CA
  • Note: Distinct from 周威廉 (Zhou Weiliang), who also served as an editor/compiler and wrote the 緣起與展望 founding essay. Both names appear in the 2017 publication’s editor list; they are separate individuals.
