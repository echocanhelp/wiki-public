Story

Lai Ching-te was born on 1959-10-06 in the Liukeng coal-mine district of Wanli; his miner father 賴朝金 died of mine gas poisoning on 1960-01-08, and he was raised single-parent by his mother 賴童好. He trained as a physician at 國立成功大學 and worked as an internist (內科醫師), turning to politics in 1994 as chief convenor of 陳定南’s 全國醫師後援會. He was elected a 國大代表 in 1996 and won the 2010 mayoral race in 臺南, serving as 臺南市市長 until 2017 after re-election in 2014. His 2014 North America tour as mayor set the pattern of a lifelong diaspora relationship: at a New York 僑界晚會 he read 陳菊’s handwritten letter on the 81 gas explosions, and at a San Francisco 僑宴 he pitched the 亞洲矽谷 idea to supporters who had traveled north from Southern California. He served as 行政院院長 from 2017 to early 2019, and a 2019-12-30 VOA interview reframed him for US audiences as a “務實台獨工作者” — the emphasis on 務實, with the Taiwan Relations Act, not the 九二共識, as the 定海神針 of cross-strait relations. After losing the 2019 primary he became 蔡英文’s running mate and served as 副總統 from 2020, took over as DPP chair in 2022, and registered the 賴蕭配 ticket on 2023-11-20; at his campaign headquarters launch days earlier he recalled through tears that his miner father had left only “父親過世後留下的唯一遺產就是貧窮”. He took office as president on 2024-05-20 with his wife 吳玫如 at the ceremony and nearly 500 townspeople from his hometown 萬里 chartering buses north — the latest chapter in a two-way record with the diaspora that runs from 林榮松’s 海外助選團 to the first overseas 後援會 formed in New York.
