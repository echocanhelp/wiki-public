### President of TACNC – 廖俊惠 Chinhuei Liao’s Journey

TAH Taiwanese Americans Oral History Project

Join us for an inspiring conversation with Chinhuei Liao, President of the Taiwanese American Center of Northern California 2024- 2025. In this interview, Chinhuei shares his journey of leadership, dedication to the Taiwanese American community, and vision for empowering future generations. Part of our TAH Taiwanese American Oral History Series Project.
