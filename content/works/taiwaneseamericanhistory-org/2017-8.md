# 8. Taiwanese American Association/Minnesota Chapter won two “Award of Excellence” for their Taiwanese Booth ( The theme is “Traditional Wedding”) in Festival of Nations (FON)/MN, voted by student hours and public hours on 5/4-7/2017. A great victory for promoting Taiwanese culture and heritage. The win is the 24th consecutive year and the 29 times in the past 31 years.
