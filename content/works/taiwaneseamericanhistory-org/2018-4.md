# 4. Taiwanese American Association/Minnesota Chapter won three “Award of Excellence” for their Taiwanese booth ( The theme is “Now and Then”) in Festival of Nations/MN, an award for the Design, and two awards voted by Student Hours and Public Hours on 5/3-6/2018. A great victory for promoting Taiwanese culture and heritage. The win is the 25th consecutive year and the 30 times in the past 32 years. This is the only T. A. organization still involved in the international festival in the U. S.
