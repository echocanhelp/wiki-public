### President of TAAGPC – 謝己 James Jii Shieh’s Journey

TAH Taiwanese Americans Oral History Project

Join us for an inspiring conversation with James Jii Shieh, President of the Taiwanese Association of America, Greater Philadelphia Chapter 2024 and Chairman of Taiwanese American Conference Ease Coast in 2024. In this interview, James shares his journey of leadership, dedication to the Taiwanese American community, and vision for empowering future generations. Part of our TAH Taiwanese American Oral History Series Project.
