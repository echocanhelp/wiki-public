### Chairwoman of THSH – 徐秋蓉 Evelyn Hsu’s Journey

TAH Taiwanese Americans Oral History Project

Join us for an inspiring conversation with Evelyn Hsu, Chairwoman of the Taiwanese Heritage Society of Houston (THSH) 2024- 2025. In this interview, Evelyn shares her journey of leadership, dedication to the Taiwanese American community, and vision for empowering future generations. Part of our TAH Taiwanese American Oral History Series Project.
