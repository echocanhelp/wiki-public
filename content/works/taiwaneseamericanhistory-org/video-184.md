### CEO of SDTC/TAF – 歐春美 Chunmei Ou Lin’s Journey

TAH Taiwanese Americans Oral History Project

In this interview, Chunmei shares her journey of leadership, dedication to the Taiwanese American community, and vision for empowering future generations.
