## Conference record (absorbed 2026-11-18, frontier t_fa186a95)
- **Event:** 第28屆 NATPA 年會 (28th Annual Conference of the North America Taiwanese Professors' Association), **July 26–31, 2008**, on a **Carnival Cruise Line** sailing from New York City to Saint John and Halifax, Canada.
- **Theme:** Our Values, Our Vision, Our Mission, Our Plan (年會主題：如何聯合海內外知識份子，推動第二次台灣文化啓蒙運動).
- **President (host):** 黃界清 Je-Chin Han, 2007–2008 NATPA President; welcome letter signed 2008-07-04.
- **Print date:** 2008/07; posted to the TAH site 2015/12.
- **Keynote:** former Education Minister 杜正勝 Tu Cheng-sheng, 「台灣主體教育之困境與展望」 (Dilemma and Prospect of Taiwan Centered Education). HOLD: 黃界清's own 2009-07-15 blog side-note says 杜正勝 did not attend in person (特別費案) and the keynote was his written article — invitation-vs-delivery retained, see GAPS.
- **Program structure in the letter:** panels 責任與義務 / 前瞻與展望 / 建國運動中的角色 / 工作目標; sessions 會員大會、Taiwan Experiences、NATPA Young Scholars Program (5 awarded PhD students from four US universities)、選戰的回顧與檢討 (three evening sessions); 2008-07-30 climax session on NATPA–TAUP collaboration; new seven-member NATPA–TAUP Liaison Committee (co-sponsoring 極光電子報 Aurora e publication + summer/winter lectureships in Taiwan).
- **Cross-confirmation:** the NATPA headquarters roster row 28 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natpa|1. NATPA 總會]], roster dated 2017-04-13) lists NO.28 = 7/26–7/30/2008, Carnival Cruise from New York to Halifax, Canada, same theme, president 黃界清, program book 已有. HOLD: end date 7/31 (letter) vs 7/30 (roster), not merged. Same table republished in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/activities-of-natpa|北美洲台灣人教授協會的活動]].
