The TAH Foundation is dedicated to preserving, documenting, and sharing the rich history and cultural legacy of Taiwanese Americans. Through this exhibition, the Foundation presents a selection of its special collections alongside the stories of generations of Taiwanese Americans whose contributions have helped shape the cultural, educational, scientific, and economic landscape of the United States. From the arrival of early immigrants following the Immigration and Nationality Act of 1965 to the accomplishments of today’s vibrant community, this exhibition celebrates a journey of resilience, identity, and achievement.

Developed with the exhibition design expertise of 70 Degrees, this presentation combines historical artifacts, photographs, archival materials, and interpretive narratives to create an engaging and educational experience. The TAH Foundation hopes this exhibition will inspire visitors to learn more about the Taiwanese American experience, appreciate its enduring contributions to American society, and recognize the importance of preserving these stories for future generations. The exhibition opened in January 2025 and remains on display today. For those unable to visit the exhibition in person, we invite you to scroll down and explore the virtual exhibition online.

The exhibition has been ongoing since January 2025
