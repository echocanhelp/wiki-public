Date: Saturday, April 6, 2013

Time: 2:00pm – 3:00pm

Location: UCLA

Address: 405 Hilgard Avenue, Los Angeles, CA

Facebook event page: https://www.facebook.com/events/517703268270892

Come and enjoy an interesting community panel on Sat 4/6 2-3pm hosted by the Intercollegiate Taiwanese American Students Association (ITASA) West Coast Conference at UCLA! Exact location will be released to Eventbrite RSVPs: http://evolutionoftacommunitypanel.eventbrite.com/. Learn more about the history of the Taiwanese American Community and how it has evolved.

Suggested donation of $10 will be collected from attendees not already registered in the ITASA West Coast Conference to support the program.

Moderator:

JULIE CHAO – TUF Secretary and TAP LA President

Panelists:

1) SIMON CHENG – TUF Past President and Current Boardmember

2) PETER CHEN – TAA National Vice President and FAPA General Counsel

3) ALEX CHEN – ACT Metrics and Member List Manager

4) BEN LING – TACL Past National Chairman and President

5) TBA

Workshop Structure:

2:00 – 2:10pm – Welcome and Introduction of Taiwanese immigration

2:10 – 2:30pm – Panel discussion and sharing of stories and experiences

2:30 – 3:00pm – Audience Q&A

Questions? Please contact:

Julie Chao, President at julie.chao@tap-la.org
