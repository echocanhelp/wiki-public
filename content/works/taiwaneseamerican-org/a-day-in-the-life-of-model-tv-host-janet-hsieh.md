Janet Hsieh 謝怡芬, the beautiful model and TV show host of “FunTaiwan” on the Discovery Travel & Living Channel, is one busy girl who is always working and jet-setting worldwide. She shares her explorations with a growing audience in Taiwan and throughout Asia.

While in Taipei, TaiwaneseAmerican.org’s Ho Chie Tsai drops in to say hello between photo shoots and appointments to hear what she’s been up to during her recent adventures.
