Date: Saturday, February 8, 2014

Time: 1:00pm – 3:00pm

Location: Oakland Asian Cultural Center

Address: 388 Ninth Street, Suite 290, Oakland, CA

Facebook event page: https://www.facebook.com/events/1455793634633255/

Volunteer with Taiwanese American Professionals – San Francisco (TAP-SF) this Lunar New Year! We’ll be supporting the Oakland Asian Culture Center (OACC) on their Lunar New Year Celebration, and assisting with set-up, registration, and stage support, while also enjoying various dance and music performances.

Note: This event has limited slots. You MUST email Community Service Chair, Howard Chang at howard.chang@tap-sf.org to confirm attendance for this event. RSVP on the Facebook event page does not confirm your attendance. Further details will be provided after confirming via email.
