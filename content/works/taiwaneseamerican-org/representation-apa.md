There are some pretty cool and accomplished individuals in our Taiwanese American network. During our recent interview with the first Asian American Miss Louisiana, Justine Ker, TaiwaneseAmerican.org’s Ho Chie Tsai posed a question to the friends gathered: “In the work you do or the role you hold, what does REPRESENTATION in the ASIAN AMERICAN community mean to you?”

In this informal and completely impromptu discussion, Justine Ker (Miss Louisiana 2016 and medical student), Crystal Lee (Miss California 2013 and 1st Runner-up in Miss American 2014), Bin Chen & Andrew Chau (Co-founders of Boba Guys and Tea People) discover a little bit more about each other’s personal experiences and backgrounds. As accomplished role models, entrepreneurs, and influencers of Taiwanese heritage, they have more in common than one might expect.

Listen as they share their personal experiences, stories, and thoughts. Toward the latter half, they share concrete advice to young people in the community.

We only wish that our friends and followers can know people just as inspiring and motivated to do good things in this world.
