Date: Saturday, April 30, 2011

Time: 9:00am – 12:00pm

Location: Los Angeles River (@Lower Compton Creek)

Facebook event page:http://www.facebook.com/event.php?eid=141448509258137

Come and join Taiwanese American Professionals – Los Angeles (TAP-LA) in assisting Friends of Los Angeles River (www.FOLAR.org) to help clean up the LA River in one of fourteen locations — this event is also part of a series of events, celebrating Taiwanese American Heritage Week coming up in May.

*The Lower Compton Creek CleanUp takes place along the Metro Blue Line Station, located on Del Amo Blvd at Santa Fe Avenue in Rancho Dominguez.

*Signed disclaimer required, please print, sign & bring with you to the event.

Disclaimer form: http://folar.org/wp-content/uploads/documents/folar_liability_form.pdf

*Prizes such as restaurant gift certificates will be raffled off during event!

*What to wear or bring:

• Clothes to Get Dirty In & Gloves

• Sturdy Shoes

• Hats & Sunscreen

• Drinking Water Bottle

• Rubber Boots or Waders (optional)

• Hand Tools for Prying Trash from vegetation (optional)

• Extendable Grabber Arm Tools (optional)

DIRECTIONS:

Bike: Take the Lario bikeway (on the LA River and Rio Hondo from El Monte to the Pacific Ocean) and exit at Del Amo Blvd. Go west on Del Amo.

Transit: Take the Metro Blue Line and get off at Del Amo Blvd.

Driving: Take the 710 Freeway, exit at Del Amo Blvd. Go west to the Del Amo Metro Station.

Parking: Park in the south end of the Metro Station parking lot.

This is a FREE event, and we welcome everyone to join us, members and non-members alike. For any questions or concerns, please contact TAP Community Development Chair, Julie Chao at julie.chao@tacl.org or TAP Officer at Large, Andy Chang at andy.chang-chien@tacl.org
