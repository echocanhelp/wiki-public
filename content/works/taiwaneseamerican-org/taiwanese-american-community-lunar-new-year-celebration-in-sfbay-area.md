Date: Saturday, January 22, 2011

Time: 10:00am – 3:00pm

Location: Dynasty Seafood Restaurant, Rooms V2 and V3

Address: 10123 North Wolfe Road #1688, Cupertino, CA

Facebook event page: http://www.facebook.com/event.php?eid=192036770809754

1st Generation Invite Page: http://www.tafnc.org/sbta/newWeb/doc/2011/tafnc2011annualparty.html

Celebrate the Year of the Rabbit with Taiwanese American Citizens League – Leading Youth Forward (TACL-LYF)! Tackle the new year with gusto, games, free food, and friends during the Taiwanese American community’s annual celebration hosted by the Taiwanese American Federation of Northern California (TAFNC).

The young folks will be gathering in reserved rooms to play Taiwanese Jeopardy, math games, puzzles, and other fun group icebreaker games. Tyson Mao, one of the world’s top competitive Rubik’s Cube solvers, will be teaching a class. Bring your own Rubik’s Cube or purchase one at $10 each. For lunch, they’ll chow down on fried rice, noodles, and BBQ buns. Plus, they’ll be watching movies, playing board games, and doing some arts and crafts.

More info at http://lyf.tacl.org/events/lunar_new_years_2011.php

If you would like to join the “grown ups” program in the dining areas, check out the “1st Generation” link above and pre-purchase your lunch tickets!
