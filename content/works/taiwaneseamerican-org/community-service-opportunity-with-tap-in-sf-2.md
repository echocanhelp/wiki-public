Date: Saturday, January 25, 2014

Time: 8:30am – 4:30pm

Address: 1 Capitol Ave., San Francisco, CA

Facebook event page: https://www.facebook.com/events/415281325269359

Kick off 2014 right by making a difference in the community! Taiwanese American Professionals – San Francisco (TAP-SF) is partnering with Habitat for Humanity on their new 28 single-family home development project. This is the perfect opportunity to learn how to build, pick up new home improvement skills, or even fulfill that handyman/joe plumber fantasy you might have had for a while.

No experience needed! All training and equipment will be provided on-site. Why should you join? Because it feels awesome to give back!

You must email Community Service Chair, Howard Chang at howard.chang@tap-sf.org to confirm attendance for this event. RSVP on the Facebook event page does not confirm your attendance. Further details will be provided after confirming via email.

This event is FREE. Priority for this event will be given to TAP members first. Sign up to be a member today at http://tap-sf.org/membership/
