Date: Sunday, December 11, 2011

Time: 1:00pm – 4:00pm

Location: Bayview/Hunters Point, San Francisco, CA

Facebook event page: http://www.facebook.com/events/280044532033152/

The holiday season is in full swing and it’s the perfect time to get into the spirit of giving. Taiwanese American Professionals – SF / Bay area (TAP-SF) will be working with Meals on Wheels of San Francisco to prepare holiday bags and make holiday cards that will go to seniors throughout the city and brighten their holidays.

The type of work on this day may include sorting, wrapping, or stuffing holiday gifts and bags. For those on the more creative end, we will also be creating holiday cards that go along with each gift.

Spend this day with fellow TAP members and MOWSF volunteers for a very rewarding experience. As the event begins just after lunch hours, there will be a restaurant nearby we can meet beforehand. The exact address will be distributed after sign-up is confirmed. Driving to the event is recommended. Public transportation is available but limited. Please contact me directly if you have any questions.

Please note:

*This event is limited as far as the number of attendees. Priority will be given to TAP members first so please become a member if you aren’t already. You can become a member by following online directions here: http://tap-sf.org/membership/ This event is FREE for both members and non-members.

**You MUST email Wilson Tsao at wilson.tsao@tap-sf.org to confirm your attendance for this event. Responding via this event page only does NOT confirm your attendance.
