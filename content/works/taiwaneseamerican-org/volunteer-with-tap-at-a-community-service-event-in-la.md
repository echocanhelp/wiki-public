Date: Saturday, November 17, 2012

Time: 3:30pm – 7:00pm

Location: Kentia Hall, Los Angeles Convention Center

Facebook event page: https://www.facebook.com/events/111895082303484/

Volunteer with Taiwanese American Professionals – Los Angeles (TAP-LA) at the Green Festival as we support efforts in building a more sustainable community!

The Green Festival is one the nation’s premier sustainability event, where you will see the best in green. Enjoy more than 125 renowned authors, leaders and educators; great how-to workshops; cutting-edge films; fun activities for kids; organic beer and wine; delicious vegetarian cuisine and diverse live music. Shop in our unique marketplace of more than 300 eco-friendly businesses-everything from all-natural body care products and organic cotton clothing to Fair Trade gifts and beautiful kitchen tiles made from renewable resources. Check out details about the event at www.greenfestivals.org

Volunteers receive:

– Free access into the festival all weekend (Nov 17 & 18).

– An exclusive organic cotton t-shirt

– 10% discount at the Green America and Global Exchange stores.

– Snacks in the Volunteer Lounge

– An amazing networking opportunity and a fun, rewarding experience!

Please RSVP to connie.wu@tap-la.org no later than THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 15.

Questions? Email Connie Wu, Community Service Director, at connie.wu@tap-la.org
