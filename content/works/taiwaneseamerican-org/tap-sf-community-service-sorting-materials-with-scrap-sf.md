Date: Sunday, February 5, 2012

Time: 10:00am – 1:00pm

Location: TBA, San Francisco

Facebook event page: http://www.facebook.com/events/358668614160310/

Do you like discovering new ways to reuse material that most people throw away? This event is for anyone interested in being creative while helping the environment. Taiwanese American Professionals – SF / Bay area (TAP-SF) will be working with Scrap (Scrounger’s Center for Reusable Art Parts) to sort & organize various materials that can be reused for art.

The type of work on this day will entail sorting, organizing, rolling and putting out fabric, greeting cards and other material. No prior experience is necessary. You just need to bring a positive attitude and willingness to help out.

Spend this day with fellow TAP members for a very rewarding experience. As the event ends around lunchtime, there will be nearby restaurant we will go to afterwards. The exact address will be distributed after sign-up is confirmed. Driving to the event is recommended as public transportation is limited. Please contact me directly if you have any questions.

Please note:

*This event is limited as far as the number of attendees.

* Questions? Email Wilson Tsao at wilson.tsao@tap-sf.org
