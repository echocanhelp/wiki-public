Join Chicago Taiwanese American Professional’s Volunteer/Community Service team as we team up with Horizons for Youth for a mentoring outing. Volunteers will pair up with students from Horizons for Youth (grades K-7) as they spend the morning together.

Grant Wu 847.710.1688 | grant@tapchicago.org

About Horizons for Youth:

Horizons for Youth is a community committed to helping children successfully graduate high school, continue onto college and ultimately reach their full potential. In partnership with dedicated families, we provide 170 hard-working students need-based scholarships, a summer program, one-on-one mentoring, monthly enrichment outings, tutoring, and college preparation. Our program has outstanding results. With over 100 graduates, Horizons for Youth alumni maintain a 99% high school graduation rate and 80% college attendance rate. For more information, visit their website: http://www.horizons-for-youth.org/
