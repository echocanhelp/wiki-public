Date: Saturday, January 5, 2013

Time: 8:30am – 11:00am

Address: 500 East Villa St, Pasadena, CA

Facebook event page: https://www.facebook.com/events/450896118297709

Join Taiwanese American Professionals – Los Angeles (TAP-LA) members as we use reading as an educational platform to empower at-risk youth in Pasadena. Kids Reading to Success (KRS)’s mission is not only to instill a love of reading in youth but also help them to use literacy as a tool to succeed in school and beyond.

KRS students are in the first to sixth grades, ages 5 to 13. Students are encouraged to read aloud and discuss their reading material verbally and in writing with their volunteers.

If you are a NEW volunteer, please fill out a volunteer application form found here: http://www.kidsreadingtosucceed.org/p/get-involved.html and e-mail it to KRS or bring it with you on Saturday when you check in.

Schedule:

By 8:30am: Sign-in for NEW volunteers

8:45am – 9:00am: Orientation for new volunteers

By 9:00am: Sign-in for RETURNING volunteers

9:00am – 10:00am: Kids read with volunteers

10:00am – 11:00am: Special presentation by guest speakers!

If you have any questions, please contact Connie Wu, Community Service Director, (connie.wu@tap-la.org).
