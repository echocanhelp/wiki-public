Date: Saturday, July 13, 2013

Time: 10:30am – 4:30pm

Location: Ed Roberts Campus

Address: 3075 Adeline Street, Berkeley, CA

Facebook event page: https://www.facebook.com/events/487289211352851/

Would you like spend a Saturday helping others while improving your own professional skills? Join Taiwanese American Professionals – San Francisco (TAP-SF) and Upwardly Global in helping highly-skilled and accomplished immigrant professionals practice their job interviewing skills and prepare for professional networking in the US.

TAP-SF is participating in Upwardly Global’s Mock Interview Workshop. In this workshop, you’ll be helping new immigrants improve their interview and networking skills through one-on-one interviews and networking sessions. Volunteers will provide valuable feedback for the job seekers upon the completion of the mock interview. No prior experience is necessary. You just need to bring a positive attitude and willingness to help out.

Spend this day with fellow TAP members for a very rewarding experience. As the event runs over lunch time, lunch will be provided by Upwardly Global. The exact address will be distributed after sign-up is confirmed. Driving to the event is highly recommended as public transportation is limited. Please contact me directly if you have any questions.

Please note:

This event is limited as far as the number of attendees. Priority will be given to TAP members first so please become a member if you aren’t already. You can become a member by following online directions here: http://tap-sf.org/membership/ This event is FREE for both members and non-members.

You MUST email Diny Huang at diny.huang@tap-sf.org to confirm your attendance for this event. Responding via the Facebook event page only does not confirm your attendance.
