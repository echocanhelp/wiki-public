The Intercollegiate Taiwanese American Students Association (ITASA) West Coast Conference is for college students to come together to celebrate all there is to being Taiwanese American and/or Asian American. Join in for three days of fun times with guest speakers, social events, a concert, banquet, and much more! The keynote speaker features The LEGO Movie producer Dan Lin on the topic, “Made in Taiwan: How Being Taiwanese Shaped my Career.”

What can you expect?

– Indulging in authentic Taiwanese cuisine – all meals included

– Learning, reflecting, and acting upon our Taiwanese and Asian American identities with our three-part workshop series

– Embracing wisdom from our keynote speaker, producer Dan Lin

– Gravitating together with an “Across the Galaxy” Scavenger Hunt

– Embarking on “A Night with the Stars” Concert

– Traveling to an Intergalactic Banquet

– Celebrating in an Out-of-this-World After Party
