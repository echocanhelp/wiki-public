Date: Saturday, June 1, 2013

Time: 11:00am – 1:00pm

Location: Hamilton Heights School

Address: 1750 Amsterdam Ave, New York, NY

Facebook event page: https://www.facebook.com/events/511575065576066/

Global Language Project (GLP) and Taiwanese American Professionals – New York (TAP-NY) are collaborating to introduce and share Chinese culture with underprivileged children in low-income communities through an afternoon of Chinese calligraphy, paper-cutting, Shuttlecock kicking, Chinese yo-yo, origami, and many more!

We are looking for volunteers who are proficient in Chinese and have some familiarity with the activities we will be teaching the kids for this event.

To volunteer, register here: http://glpvolunteer2013.eventbrite.com/

To learn more about GLP, visit their website at http://www.globallanguageproject.org/

Please email Jennifer.Lin@tap-ny.org with any questions
