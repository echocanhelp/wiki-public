NATWA2 & TAP-LA presents “Treasures of the Taiwanese American Community: Undocumented & Uncovered”

Come roll with us Taiwanese style as we dig into our rich history and have an interactive demonstration about a treasured Taiwanese dish-lunpia*. Over our great creations, we will also listen to personal stories of our fellow Taiwanese and discuss about undocumented Taiwanese students and how the DREAM ACT is important to our community. So bring your tummies and minds and be ready to be nourished.

*Nope, we didn’t spell lumpia wrong. Come to our workshop to find out why.
