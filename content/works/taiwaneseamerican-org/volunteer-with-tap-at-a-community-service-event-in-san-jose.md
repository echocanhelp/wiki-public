Date: Saturday, November 10, 2012

Time: 5:00pm – 7:30pm

Location: The Fairmont San Jose Luxury Hotel

Address: 170 S Market St, San Jose, CA

Facebook event page: https://www.facebook.com/pages/The-Fairmont-San-Jose-Luxury-Hotel/121233511223911

Fashion meets philanthropy as the Asian American Cancer Support Network (AACSN) hosts its fundraiser gala, Hatwalk 2012. Meant to celebrate the importance of hats to cancer survivors, the highlight of Hatwalk will be a fashion show modeled by Asian American pageant winners and cancer survivors. As a unique twist, audience members are then able to buy the hat off the models’ heads. The gala will also feature a performance by a 14 year old piano prodigy, stories from cancer survivors, as well as a silent auction. All proceeds will be going to AACSN. Learn more here about the event here: http://www.aacsn.org/hatwalk.html

Taiwanese American Professionals – San Francisco (TAP-SF) will be assisting in a variety of volunteer tasks, which may include: silent auction, fashion show, payments, registration, and raffle.

Please note:

This event is limited as far as the number of attendees. Priority will be given to TAP members first so please become a member if you aren’t already. You can become a member by following online directions here: http://tap-sf.org/membership/. This event is FREE for both members and non-members.

Please email Diny Huang at diny.huang@tap-sf.org to RSVP for this event and receive additional volunteer details (and to ask about carpool arrangements).
