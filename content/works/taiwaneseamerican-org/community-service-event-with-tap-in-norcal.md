Date: Saturday, March 22, 2014

Time: 10:00am – 3:00pm

Location: Silicon Valley Bicycle Exchange

Address: 2566 Leghorn Street, Mountain View, CA

Facebook event page: https://www.facebook.com/events/257175094460601/

Help make a child smile! Taiwanese American Professionals – San Francisco (TAP-SF) is partnering with Silicon Valley Bicycle Coalition to help repair donated bikes for children and individuals in need. Your journey to becoming a SF bicycling hipster also starts with learning these basic repairs.

No experience needed! All training and equipment will be provided on-site.

Note: This event has limited slots. You MUST email Community Service Chair, Howard Chang at howard.chang@tap-sf.org to confirm attendance for this event. RSVP on the Facebook event page does not confirm your attendance. Further details will be provided after confirming via email.
