Date: Saturday, December 14, 2013

Time: 8:00am – 10:00am

Location: Lakeside River Park Conservancy

Address: 12108 Industry Rd, Lakeside, CA

Facebook event page: https://www.facebook.com/events/231391210355511/

Want to work off weights gained during Thanksgiving (and get ready for the crazy Xmas/New Year feasts) while giving back to the mother nature? Join Taiwanese American Professionals – San Diego (TAP-SD) for Riverbed Cleanup Hike guided by local biologist! We will have special permission to walk as a group in the riverbed and will be guided by the River Park’s biologist!

Through storm drain pollution, illegal dumping and camping, the sensitive habitat of the San Diego Riverbed needs our help! Join us to make our local San Diego community a safer and cleaner place to live. Remember, what flows down stream affects our Ocean too!

Bring your friends and family. This will be a great family/friend outdoor activities and rewarding experience. Since December is the month of giving and caring, let us give mother nature its pristine look back to welcome 2014. This is also your last chance to make it onto Santa’s Nice List!

Please see below for detail on location and requirement:

Meeting Place: Intersection of Pecan Park Ln and Rios Canyon Rd, El Cajon, CA 92021. (just off Old Highway 80) Parking is available on streets.

Carpooling is recommended. Below is the link to the sigh-up sheet:

https://docs.google.com/a/tap-sd.org/spreadsheet/ccc?key=0AlmOrMdHcT6YdDdWNXVyV1ltRUgyWmprUk5uclRFVmc#gid=0

Requirements:

– Must be at least 18 years old or have a parent co-sign the waiver of liability.

– Possess the physical ability to walk/hike outdoors in various weather conditions and on natural terrain.

– Complete and bring all necessary forms and waiver of liability.

– Wear long pants, hiking or good out-door shoes, a hat and sun screen.

– Bring at least 32oz of water per person.

Please email Julia Kao at jkao@tap-sd.org to confirm attendance for this event. A waiver of liability will be sent out. RSVP via this event page only does NOT confirm your attendance.

Any questions, please contact Julia @ jkao@tap-sd.org or 626-379-7529
