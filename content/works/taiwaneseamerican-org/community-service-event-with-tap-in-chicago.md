Date: Saturday, March 2, 2013

Time: 4:00pm – 6:30pm

Location: A Just Harvest

Address: 7649 N Paulina St, Chicago, IL

Facebook event page: https://www.facebook.com/events/561434720534543/

Join Chicago Taiwanese American Professionals at A Just Harvest where we will be serving at Chicago’s largest community soup kitchen. A Just Harvest is focused on providing a welcoming and respectful environment in which food is served, as those in need are often treated without the respect most of us are accustomed to. This is a fantastic opportunity to team with other Chicago TAP volunteers in aiding less fortunate Chicagoans through this relentless winter.

A Just Harvest’s Community Kitchen began in 1983. Originally serving meals a few days per week, the Community Kitchen now serves hot meals 365 days a year with the help of more than 40 partnering organizations and nearly 10,000 volunteer hours. In 2012, the Community Kitchen served more than 54,000 meals.

REQUIREMENTS:

Volunteers must wear pants and closed toed shoes. Volunteers will be handling hot food and beverages; dressing accordingly will help protect volunteers against burns or other accidents.

COST:

Free! Please reserve a free ticket for one of 15 available volunteer spots: www.wepay.com/events/tapharvest
