Join Taiwanese American Professionals – Los Angeles (TAP-LA) for this community service event.

Kids Reading to Succeed (KRS) goals are three-fold: (1) to inspire and impart a love of reading in our youth; (2) to improve and develop their reading comprehension and analytical skills; and (3) to encourage our youth to succeed and rise above the challenges of their environment.

KRS students are in the first to sixth grades, ages 5 to 13. Our KRS volunteers meet with these students on the first Saturdays of each month. Students are encouraged to read aloud and discuss their reading material verbally and in writing with their volunteers.
