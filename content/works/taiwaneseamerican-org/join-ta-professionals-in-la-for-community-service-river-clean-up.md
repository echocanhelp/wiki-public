Date: Saturday, April 28, 2012

Time: 9:00am

Location: Los Angeles River (@Lower Compton Creek)

Facebook event page (TAP-LA): https://www.facebook.com/events/124215864378155/

Facebook event page (TUF): http://www.facebook.com/events/341465802575260/

Come and join Taiwanese American Professionals (TAP-LA), Taiwanese United Fund (TUF), and other Taiwanese American community organizations in assisting Friends of Los Angeles River to help clean up the LA River in one of fifteen locations. This event is also part of a series of events, celebrating Taiwanese American Heritage Week coming up in May.

RSVP with TAP Officer-at-Large, Julie Chao at julie.chao@tacl.org, or register on the FOLAR website: http://folar.org/?p=1079. A signed disclaimer is also required. Please print, sign, and bring the disclaimer form with you to the event. Disclaimer form: http://folar.org/wp-content/uploads/documents/folar_liability_form.pdf

This is a FREE event, and we welcome everyone to join us, members and non-members alike. Prizes will be raffled off during the event! For any questions or concerns, please contact TAP Officer-at-Large, Julie Chao at julie.chao@tacl.org or TAP Community Service Director, Annie Chou at annie.chou@tacl.org.
