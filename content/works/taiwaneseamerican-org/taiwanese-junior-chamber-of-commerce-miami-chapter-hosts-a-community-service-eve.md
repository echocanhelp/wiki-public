The Taiwanese Junior Chamber of Commerce – Miami (TJCC Miami Chapter) invites you to join them for a community service event.

Camillus House – a local homeless shelter – owns several lots throughout South Florida. These lots have been donated by the county in order to help Camillus House’s efforts in housing the homeless. The majority of these lots contain structures which are in dire need of renovation.

TJCC-Miami is gathering volunteers for this ongoing charity work with Architect for Humanity and UP Lab, and they need people to help paint and work on landscaping for the property.
