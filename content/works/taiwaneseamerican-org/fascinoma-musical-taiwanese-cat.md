## Summary
TaiwaneseAmerican.org’s Ho Chie Tsai catches up with Taiwanese American singer-songwriter Alanna Lin aka Fascinoma as she works on her latest project recording covers of Beatles songs. She has had a strong following since her days performing shows around Los Angeles and since one of her songs became “quasi-famous amongst fans of American Dad on FOX”. […]
