Date: Saturday, February 9, 2013

Time: 9:00am – 12:00pm

Location: Los Angeles Regional Food Bank

Address: 1734 East 41st Street, Los Angeles, CA

Facebook event page: https://www.facebook.com/events/538684939489881/

Come join Taiwanese American Professionals – Los Angeles (TAP-LA) and ITASA (Intercollegiate Taiwanese American Student Association) in a JOINT volunteer event at the LA Regional Food Bank to fight hunger! We will be sorting and assembling food packages for thousands of low-income seniors, women with infants, and children in Los Angeles County. The food packages consist of canned fruits, canned vegetables, rice, cereal, and other non-perishable food items. Friends and family welcome.

Check-in time: 8:35am – 8:55am

Please REGISTER to volunteer at the LA Food Bank here:

http://www.lafoodbank.org/volunteer-member-password.aspx?event=906B943B14D24ECF97DBCA572714DD10

Password: cherry88

You must sign and bring WAIVER FORM with you. The form can be found on the Facebook event page.

Attire: All volunteers must wear closed-toe shoes.

Questions? Please contact Jane Huang, Volunteer Coordinator, at Jane.Huang@tap-la.org
