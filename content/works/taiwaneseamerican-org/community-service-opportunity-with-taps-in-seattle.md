Date: Sunday, July 28, 2013

Time: 10:00am – 3:00pm

Location: FareStart, bottom floor

Address: 700 Virginia St Seattle, WA

Facebook event page: https://www.facebook.com/events/144719175724717/

Come help feed the needy with Taiwanese American Professional Society of Seattle (TAPS) at FareStart, a culinary job training and placement program for homeless and disadvantaged individuals. FareStarts delivers meals 365 days a year to several area homeless shelters. We have 8 spots available to help prepare meals. Please email ker.chang@gmail.com with your name and phone number if interested and we will send you a confirmation email and the check-in procedures. Chefs will assign tasks based on individual skill and comfort level.

We ask that you commit to volunteering for the entire time, from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. We also ask that in the case of cancellation you provide us with as much notice as possible—this is very important as we need a certain number of volunteers to produce meals and often need to recruit replacements.

For more information about the organization we will be volunteering with, see www.farestart.org.
