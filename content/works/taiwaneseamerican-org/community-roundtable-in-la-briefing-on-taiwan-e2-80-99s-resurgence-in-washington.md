Date: Saturday, July 30, 2011

Address: 3001 Walnut Grove, Rosemead, CA

Facebook event page: http://www.facebook.com/event.php?eid=140235826058838

The Formosa Foundation hosts a “Community Roundtable” to be held at the Taiwan Center in Rosemead on Saturday, July 30 at 2:00 PM. This event will provide an opportunity for the Taiwanese-American community to be briefed on recent events and to get insights into what’s next on the agenda in Washington. The “Roundtable” is intended to build on the recent success of the community and to discuss ideas for moving forward and ways that you and/or your organization can be involved.

Topics:

· Overview of June 16 House Foreign Affairs Committee Hearing

· 2011 Formosa Foundation Ambassador Program

· Recent Action on Capitol Hill

· Announcement of What’s Next on the Agenda

· How You Can Make a Difference

Join in on this very special event as your continued participation is important to the future of Taiwan’s democracy.

Admission is free but seating is limited. Please RSVP to Formosa@formosafoundation.​org or by calling 213.625.1991
