# Zhang Ai-Hui (張愛惠)

**Zhang Ai-Hui (張愛惠)** is a Taiwanese-American Presbyterian leader serving as **會計** (Treasurer) of the [[organizations/national-taiwanese-presbyterian-council||NTPC]] 執委會 for **2025/26** (西南區). She is also **NTPW 會長** (President) for 2025.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Esther Pan / Ai-Hui Zhang
- **Chinese:** 張愛惠
- **Era:** Contemporary (NTPC Treasurer 2025/26)
- **Geography:** United States (Southwest District; Henderson, NV)
- **Core roles:** NTPC Treasurer; NTPW President

## Role in NTPC

Per the 2025/26 執委 letter on ntpc-usa.org:

> **財 務： 張愛惠長老(西南區)NTPW 主席**

She handles NTPC financial matters including annual dues and conference fees. The 2026 invitation states: "費用請用 Zelle 轉帳到 ID: estherraypan@gmail.com (memo 請註明教會名稱），或以支票抬頭：NTPC，寄給會計張愛惠長老 Elder Esther Pan，地址：559 Pacer Terrace, Henderson, NV 89002。"

## Role in NTPW

Per ntpc-usa.org/ntpw/ (2025):
