## Role in the Community

- Brown is memorialized in the TAH community corpus: the encyclopedia entry [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/88-pei-ning-ku-e9-a1-a7-e8-93-93-e5-af-a7|TAH #88, Roland P. Brown 薄柔纜]] (2014-11-15) and the feature 「譜寫山高水長的門諾醫療奉獻精神」 [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories159|TAH My Stories #159, 11/03/11]] — both record his Menor's (門諾) medical-dedication legacy rather than a press-kit biography.
- The wider Hualien Menno mission network appears in adjacent corpus works: the 黃勝雄醫師門諾醫院榮退特輯 [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1057|TAH Publications #1057, 06/2015]] documents the hospital Brown founded continuing to draw台式 American physicians back to Taiwan (e.g. 黃勝雄 as later 院長, also cited in the physician overview [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys291|TAH Our Journeys #291, 05/2017]]).
- HOLD: corpus works spell his name both "Roland" (TAH #88) and "Rowland" (TAH #159 title); treated as the same person, spelling conflict not merged.
