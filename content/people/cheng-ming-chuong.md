# Cheng-Ming Chuong (鍾正明)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Cheng-Ming Chuong
- **Chinese:** 鍾正明
- **Era:** 1952
- **Geography:** Taipei, Taiwan
- **Core roles:** scientist, physician, professor, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who
- **Also known for:** feather/skin-appendage developmental biology; his "Birth of birds" review was selected as one of *Science*'s 2014 top 10 breakthroughs (source: external source page / en.wp).

## Community Service (NATPA)
- **NATPA S Cal Chapter President, 1999–2000** — listed in the chapter's own presidential roster (鍾正明(1999 – 2000)) in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natpa-s-cals-pearl-anniversary|26. Brief History of NATPA – S CAL 南加州分會的介紹]] (TAH, 2017-10-30), the same roster that anchors [[people/milton-m-chen|Milton M. Chen]]'s 1997–1998 presidency.

## Scientific Career
- Elected **Academician of Academia Sinica (中央研究院院士), 2008**, 27th class, Division of Life Sciences (生命科學組) — official academy roster: academicians.sinica.edu.tw id=471 (source: external source page).
- **AAAS Fellow, elected 2014** — the only USC faculty member elected that year, per USC Keck School news, 2014-12-13 (source: external source page).
- **Kligman/Frost Leadership Award, 2016**, Society for Investigative Dermatology (source: TAH Who's Who; corroborated by the Academia Sinica CV).
- Career track per the Academia Sinica CV: Rockefeller Assistant Professor 1983–1987; USC Pathology Assistant Professor 1988, Associate Professor 1992, Professor 1998–present (source: external source page); NIH-funded research since 1988 per en.wp.
- Research profile: skin appendages (feathers, hair), regeneration, stem-cell morphogenesis; worked with Gerald Edelman at Rockefeller (source: external source page).
- Publication record: 200 research articles can be found in PUBMED, or lab website http://www-hsc.usc.edu/~cmchuong/ (source: TAH #606); indexed counts on 2026-11-12 were 291 (PubMed esearch "Chuong CM[Author]") and 333 (OpenAlex raw_author_name search) — counts are query-dependent.
