## The double arm transplant (2012–2013)
- The TAH Foundation profile record itself carries a press pointer — `Source from自由時報 http://news.ltn.com.tw/news/focus/paper/1195384` — attached to the self-reported accomplishments of this record (source: [[sources/tah-whoswho-dr-w-p-andrew-lee-capture|TAH Who's Who capture #2102]], Captures 1/2).
- 葉榮秋 (Daniel J. Yeh) names him in his preface to 曾恆利's 林書豪 book: 「Dr. W. P. Andrew Lee (李為平), a surgeon at Johns Hopkins University, recently did a double-arm transplant for a veteran successfully」, listed alongside 李遠哲, 吳季剛, 曾雅妮 and 林書豪 as 台灣人 making world news — third-party community corroboration of both identity and the operation (source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications293-2|296. 台灣之子 福爾摩沙之光]] preface; archived dump `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/publications293-2.md`).
- 曾恆利's account of writing that book quotes 李正三/葉榮秋's review naming 李為平 in the same roll of 台美人 achievements — 「李遠哲，吳季剛，李為平，曾雅妮，現在又有林書豪，李安」 — 「台灣人逐漸在世界各領域展露頭角」 (source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories8|8. 編寫「台灣之子，福爾摩沙之光、林書豪的成功故事」新書之心路歷程]]; dump `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/mystories8.md`).
- Press corroboration found 2026-11-12 by web search — **pointer only, not archived on this box** (see GAPS below): Johns Hopkins Medicine reports the bilateral arm transplant on Brendan Marrocco, operated **Dec 2012**, press conference **2013-01-29** (per hopkinsmedicine.org/transplant/news-events/double-arm-transplant). 華視新聞網 2013-01-30 (per news.cts.com.tw/cts/international/201301/201301301187399.html) describes 來自高雄的李為平, 15 歲赴美, leading a team of over ten, a 13-hour operation, the 7th double-hand transplant case in the U.S., and the most complex of five similar operations Lee had performed, as 約翰霍普金斯醫院整型外科主任. ETtoday 2013-01-29 (per ettoday.net/news/20130129/158820.htm) dates the operation to **2012-12-18** and notes three similar operations already done during his Pittsburgh period.
- Institutional/press quotes (pointer only, unarchived): U.S. Army Combined Arms Center (#AAC25, per asc.army.mil) records DoD/AFIRM funding and quotes Lee as director of the JHU School of Medicine Dept. of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery — 「nerves regenerate at the maximum speed of one inch per month」; Johns Hopkins News-Letter 2013-03-07 (per jhunewsletter.com) quotes Lee on the first-U.S.-hand-transplant controversy (「One of the people objecting … was myself」).

## Leadership (self-reported on TAH record #2102)
- Chair of the American Board of Plastic Surgery; President of the American Society for Surgery of the Hand (source: [[sources/tah-whoswho-dr-w-p-andrew-lee-capture|capture #2102]], `Accomplishment` field).
- Chairman of the Plastic Surgery Research Council, **2002**; President-Elect of the American Society for Reconstructive Transplantation, **2008**; President of the Robert H. Ivy Society of Plastic Surgeons, **2010–2011** (source: same `Accomplishment` field).
- Kappa Delta Award and Sumner Koch Award — no year, no awarding body on the record (source: same field; see GAPS).
- Publication (non-professional): *Hand Transplantation*, an Issue of Hand Clinics, **2011** (source: same record).
- Caveat: the record repeats these seven items verbatim in a second `Notable Accomplishments` field — repetition within one editable self-report is **not** corroboration (per [[sources/tah-whoswho-dr-w-p-andrew-lee-capture|capture #2102]] twin check).
