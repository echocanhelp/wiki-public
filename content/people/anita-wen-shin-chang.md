## Filmmaking & Diaspora Record (absorbed from vault corpus)

Writing as **Anita Chang**, she is an independent filmmaker born to parents who immigrated to the U.S. from Taiwan in the 1960s, and grew up in Akron, Ohio and Massachusetts (bio recorded in the vault essay [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/taiwanese-american-film||Taiwanese American Film — TAH Magazine feature]]). Her 52-minute documentary **62 Years and 6,500 Miles Between** (2005) was showcased in that feature as part of the emerging Taiwanese American film canon, and the same essay credits her with the moment the Center for Asian American Media (formerly NAATA) finally added "Taiwanese" as an ethnicity category in its catalog. A later Duke senior thesis on Taiwanese American film analyzed *62 Years and 6,500 Miles Between* alongside Jean Cheng's *Han Chee*, evidence of her place in the canon.

Her film on leprosy patients in Taiwan and her move to teach at National Dong Hwa University marked a turn toward work made in Taiwan itself; her community-historical record is held as TAH encyclopedia entry [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/141-anita-wen-shin-chang-e5-bc-b5-e6-96-87-e9-a6-a8||TAH #141 (2014-12-08)]].
