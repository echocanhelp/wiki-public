# Bob Yang (楊英育)

**Bob Yang** (楊英育) served as president of the Formosan Association for Public Affairs (FAPA) 2008–2011, per FAPA's official presidents roster. FAPA's first Emerging Leaders Workshop was held during his presidency (FAPA history 2012–2022).
