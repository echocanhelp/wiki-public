## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Tzyy-Choou Wu

- **Era:** Birth Place
- **Geography:** 台灣
- **Core roles:** scientist, physician, professor

Professor of Pathology, Oncology, Obstetrics and Gynecology, and Molecular Microbiology and Immunology
