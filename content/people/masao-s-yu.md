## Role in the Community

- **1983-10** — Founding participant, 克城台灣人醫師協會 (Cleveland Taiwanese Medical Association), established at the 楓林閣 restaurant in Cleveland with 24 others (陳克孝, 周烒明, 許世模 et al.); 游祥修 listed among the Taipei Medical College contingent. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys74|74. 1983至1990的北美洲台灣人醫師協會]] (band A memoir by 周烒明).
- **1984-07-14** — Founding participant, 北美洲台灣人醫師協會 (NATMA), formally established at Western Michigan University, Ypsilanti, MI, before ~40 physicians; attended in the Cleveland delegation listed by name (陳克孝, 陳哲雄, 林尊昌, 吳秀惠, 周烒明, 許世模, 張嘉榮, 游祥修, 林一輝, 粱俊華, 張高文). Same source; English account at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys74-eng|Our Journeys #74 (EN)]]. His Cleveland-base community and civic hubs in the vault are [[organizations/taiwanese-american-association-greater-cleveland|TAA Greater Cleveland]] and [[organizations/fapa-cleveland|FAPA Cleveland]] (see HOLD below).
- **FAPA organizing, Ohio** — Credited in the movement memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys228|Our Journeys #228]] as the 北俄州 physician whose long-term cultivation with the FAPA 克里夫蘭分會 (FAPA Cleveland chapter) produced Senator Sherrod Brown's repeated pro-Taiwan legislative support. The same memoir dates the payoff: HCR 88 (美國對台六項保證), cleared by the House on 2016-05-16 on the eve of 蔡英文's inauguration, was carried for Ohio by Rep. Steve Chabot and, per the account, delivered after Hsiao (夏波) secured co-sponsors through FAPA — Cleveland chapter co-founder 李青泰 (FAPA former 總會長) is credited with the Cincinnati case; 昆布勞/Coen Blaauw (27 years on FAPA headquarters staff) supplied the parliamentary narrative.
