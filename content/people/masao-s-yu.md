## Role in the Community

- **1983-10** — Founding participant, 克城台灣人醫師協會 (Cleveland Taiwanese Medical Association), established at the 楓林閣 restaurant in Cleveland with 24 others (陳克孝, 周烒明, 許世模 et al.); 游祥修 listed among the Taipei Medical College contingent. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys74|74. 1983至1990的北美洲台灣人醫師協會]] (band A memoir by 周烒明).
- **1984-07-14** — Founding participant, 北美洲台灣人醫師協會 (NATMA), formally established at Western Michigan University, Ypsilanti, MI, before ~40 physicians; attended in the Cleveland delegation listed by name (陳克孝, 陳哲雄, 林尊昌, 吳秀惠, 周烒明, 許世模, 張嘉榮, 游祥修, 林一輝, 粱俊華, 張高文). Same source; English account at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys74-eng|Our Journeys #74 (EN)]]. In the vault his Cleveland base sits with [[organizations/taiwanese-american-association-greater-cleveland|TAA Greater Cleveland]] and, on the physicians' side, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natma-cleveland-chapter|NATMA 克里夫蘭分會]] (both Cleveland organising hubs; a dedicated FAPA Cleveland chapter page is still absent).
- **FAPA Cleveland chapter (Ohio)** — Credited in the movement memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys228|Our Journeys #228]] as the 北俄州 physician whose long-term cultivation with the FAPA 克里夫蘭分會 (with chapter member 陳正義/Peter Chen) is credited for Sherrod Brown's 多次 pro-Taiwan votes. The same memoir dates the Ohio payoff: the HCR 88 (美國對台六項保證) case brought by Steve Chabot, who is described as the Ohio Taiwanese community's other "超挺台灣" representative. Related community records: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/chabot-honoring-party-5-1-18|TAH #105, Chabot's 2018-05-01 Cleveland-area dinner with 李青泰]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/rep-steve-chabot|TAH #22, community small-donation support for Chabot]], and the 2016-05-16 article record [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240530121512_root_bc8636f97a1b22b2|民報: 美眾院無異議通過對台6保證决议案]].
