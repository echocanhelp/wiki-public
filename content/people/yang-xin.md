# Yang Hsin (楊信)

## Identity Snapshot
- **Born:** 1944, Puxin Township, Changhua County, Taiwan
- **Era:** First-generation Taiwanese American
- **Geography:** Southern California (Los Angeles area)
- **Core roles:** CEO of Seville Classics, Inc.; FGB community bank chairman

## Overview
- 楊信 (Jackson Yang) immigrated to the United States in his early 40s after a career in international trade in Taiwan
- He is the CEO of Seville Classics, Inc., a globally leading innovator and manufacturer in housewares and storage, generating annual revenue of over $200 million
- Seville was recently awarded "Vendor of the Year" by Walmart
- He is the Chairman of FGB (First Guangfu Bank), a community bank focused on serving small businesses and individuals in Southern California
- In 2012, FGB ranked first among the nation's best forming community banks by SNL Financial
- As of December 31, 2016, FGB's total assets exceeded $840 million, operating 4 branches across the Greater San Gabriel Valley and Orange County
- He was President of the Taiwanese Chambers of Commerce of North America, initiating programs including "Second Generation Assistance"
- He served as President of the World Taiwanese Chambers of Commerce (2013–2014)
- He was elected President of the Los Angeles Chinese American Chamber of Commerce in 2004
- He was elected 20th President of the World United Council of Chinese Chambers of Commerce (2006)
- He organized a TPP lobbying trip to Washington DC, meeting with US Congress members including Rep. Ed Royce
- He has been an honorary advisor to the Greater Los Angeles Taiwan Center since 2002, donating $5,000 annually
- In 2014–2015, he contributed $50,000 to renovate the Taiwan Center's corridors and restrooms
- He organized fundraising events for US Congress members including Ted Lieu, Ed Royce, and John Chiang
