## Role in the Community
- **Youngest chapter founder in the North American Taiwanese community**: founded the **Athens (Georgia) chapter of the North American Taiwanese Community Association / 全美台灣同鄉會** during president 許和瑞's term, one of 9 new chapters that brought the association to 43 chapters nationwide — corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76|76. 全美台灣同鄉會 / 吳木盛 / 2014-12]].
- **1984 blacklist-breaking return to Taiwan**: one of 9 members of the 「南加州台灣同鄉會回台訪問團」(with 謝清志, 江昭儀, 蔡明憲, 歐煌坤, 李英男, 張雲彥, 許英智, 蔡淑敏) — the first hometown-association delegation to return to Taiwan after the blacklist era. At the airport the KMT demanded each traveler sign a pledge (no public statements, no anti-government speech, no participation in tang-wai activities); **only 林心智 refused to sign**, holding out while the rest deplaned until the pledge issue was settled — corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys66|66. 南加州同鄉會組團回台 — 1984突破黑名單 / 謝清志 / 2014-11]].
- **Brother of 林弘宣**, the美丽岛 eight political prisoner — the corpus notes the KMT deliberately feared his return and had tried to bar him from boarding because of that family tie. HOLD: corpus says brother of 林弘宣; no sibling field on the TAH profile to reconcile.
- **Taigi children's-verse author**: wrote the 台灣地理童謠 series — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publication1305|1305. 台灣地理童謠 / 2003]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publication1306|1306. 台灣節日童謠 / 2004]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publication1307|1307. 青菜、小動物童謠 / 2006]] — and the poem [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/videos62|62. 佇母親節的時]].
