## Role in the Community
- **TAH encyclopedia record**: his own bibliographic record in the TAHS story corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-sim-ti-lim|587. Sim Ti Lim 林心智 /2015/09]].
- **Chapter founder in the North American Taiwanese community**: founded the **Athens (Georgia) chapter of the North American Taiwanese Community Association / 全美台灣同鄉會** during president 許和瑞's term, one of 9 new chapters that brought the association to 43 chapters nationwide — corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76|76. 全美台灣同鄉會 / 吳木盛 / 2014-12]].
- **1984 blacklist-breaking return to Taiwan**: one of 9 members of the 「南加州台灣同鄉會回台訪問團」(with 謝清志, 江昭儀, 蔡明憲, 歐煌坤, 李英男, 張雲彥, 許英智, 蔡淑敏) — the first hometown-association delegation to return to Taiwan after the blacklist era. At the airport the KMT demanded each traveler sign a pledge (no public statements, no anti-government speech, no participation in tang-wai activities); **only 林心智 refused to sign**, holding out while the rest deplaned until the pledge issue was settled — corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys66|66. 南加州同鄉會組團回台 — 1984突破黑名單 / 謝清志 / 2014-11]].
- **Family tie documented in the corpus**: brother of 林弘宣, one of the 美麗島事件八大受刑人 — the corpus notes the KMT deliberately feared his return and had tried to bar him from boarding because of that family tie. HOLD: corpus says brother of 林弘宣; no sibling field on the TAH profile to reconcile.
- **Taigi children's-verse author**: wrote the 台灣地理童謠 series — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publication1305|1305. 台灣地理童謠 / 2003]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publication1306|1306. 台灣節日童謠 / 2004]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publication1307|1307. 青菜、小動物童謠 / 2006]] — plus 台灣點心歌 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publication1308|1308 / 2008]]) and the set to music 水果童謠 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/614-e6-b0-b4-e6-9e-9c-e7-ab-a5-e8-ac-a0-e6-9e-97-e5-bf-83-e6-99-ba201009music-e9|614 / 2010-09]]), and the poem [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/videos62|62. 佇母親節的時]]. His 228台灣節日童謠 (詞曲 林心智, line 「二二八是台灣ê最疼」) was quoted in the 世界台灣文化論壇's 75th-228 紀念宣言 ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2022/20220706142144_2022_02_21_第22回世界台灣文化論壇_75th-228祭_732b8bb735393dbe|TJJ, 2022-02-21]]). Also essay 「百合是台灣花」 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/29-e7-99-be-e5-90-88-e6-98-af-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e8-8a-b1-e6-9e-97-e5-bf-83-e6-99|29 / 2015-11]]).
- **Pen name 林大白 on 台灣之音**: 張富雄's radio-history memoir records the program reading his children's poems (兒童詩) under the name 「林大白」 and airing his guitar performances — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys2|2. 《台灣之音》的回顧 / 張富雄 / 2014-09]].
- **Southern-California 生活座談會 member**: joined the 南區生活座談會 (centered on Orange County, founded 1974) alongside 蔡明憲, 謝清志, 張雲彦 and others — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys212|212. 生活座談會簡史 / 劉天良 / 2016-03]].
- **Painter**: his 畫展作品專輯（粉彩．蠟筆）「居心地」 is in the corpus ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publication1304|1304 / 2014]]); the 黃根深 exhibition note ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys256|256. 寫在畫展之前 / 黃根深 / 2016-10]]) records him as 1944年生台中市人, 台大畢業, 喬治亞大學心理學博士, 曾任美國大學教授, and winner at the 1982 Ferrum College (Virginia) faculty-student exhibition — consistent with the TAH profile and Employment/Education tables.
