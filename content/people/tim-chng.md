## Role in the Community
- Co-founder of the Intercollegiate Taiwanese American Students Association (ITASA); credited as 台美大專學生會發起人 and TAU (Taiwanese Americans United) 管理員 in the community open letter 「守護台灣民主」 — he served as a panel moderator alongside civil-rights lawyer Jennifer Lai 賴瑀琤: [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/bilingual-chinese-english-an-open-letter-to-the-taiwanese-american-community-sec|給台美人的一封公開信：守護台灣民主]].
- Son of [[people/strong-chuang|Strong Chuang 莊秋雄]], who was blacklisted from returning to Taiwan in 1965 while in the United States (UFAI founding delegate, Kansas); Tim appears with his father in oral-history material on transitional justice linked from [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/remembering-the-228-massacre-readings-resources-on-taiwans-white-terror-era|Remembering the 228 Massacre]] and was among signers of [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/pew-research-center-taiwanese-american-statement|the community response to Pew Research hiding Taiwanese identity]].
- Profiled in the TAH Foundation Who's Who: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whoswho1688|1688. Tim Chng 莊騰程 / 05/2017]].
