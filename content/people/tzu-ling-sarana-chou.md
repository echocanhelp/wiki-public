## Role in the Community

Corpus memoirs place Chou inside the Rochester Taiwanese community during her Eastman years. In the [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/15-e7-be-85-e5-be-b9-e6-96-af-e7-89-b9-e8-87-ba-e7-81-a3-e5-90-8c-e9-84-95-e6-9c|羅徹斯特臺灣同鄉會愛鄉合唱團]] concert program, she is listed as 伴奏 (accompanist, 代) for 愛鄉合唱團 — a choir formed in 1993 by Taiwanese expatriates devoted to 台灣歌, which rehearsed biweekly, performed at 羅徹斯特臺灣同鄉會 events and in Toronto, Cornell, and Rochester sister-city celebrations, and in that year shared the stage with the Rochester Chamber Orchestra. The same program records her community-side biography: 台灣作曲家, went to the US in 1998, degrees at 茱麗亞音樂院 and the University of Chicago, then Eastman on full scholarship; winner of the ASCAP (American Society of Composers, Authors, and Publishers) composition competition — 歷年來唯一一位非美國籍作曲家獲得此殊榮 — with works performed at contemporary music festivals in Brussels, Miami, San Diego, and Buffalo in 2004–05. Her own TAH encyclopedia profiles are [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/10-tzu-ling-sarana-chou-e5-91-a8-e5-ad-90-e9-88-b4-composer|TAH #10 (2014)]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/443-tzu-ling-sarana-chou-e5-91-a8-e5-ad-90-e9-88-b4-201506|TAH #443 (2015)]].
