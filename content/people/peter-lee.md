# Peter Lee (李文智)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Peter Lee
- **Chinese:** 李文智
- **Born:** 台灣台南市
- **Geography:** Taiwan (Tainan) → United States
- **Core roles:** countertenor, professor, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who's Who

台灣台南市人，畢業於美國約翰霍普金斯大學琵琶地音樂院 (Peabody Conservatory of Johns Hopkins) 研究所，雙主修「聲樂演唱」與「早期音樂」。曾榮獲第二十屆金曲獎傳統藝術類「最佳演唱」獎，是活躍於美國樂壇的假聲男高音（countertenor）。

## Absorbed Facts (from TAH cites, 2026-09-10)
- Born in 台南市, Taiwan
- Johns Hopkins University awarded him the 傑出校友 **Knowledge of the World Award** in **2011-10** — he was the **first Taiwanese alumnus** to receive this award
- Won the 第二十屆金曲獎傳統藝術類「最佳演唱」獎 (20th Golden Melody Award, Traditional Arts category, Best Vocal Performance)

## Timeline
- **20th Golden Melody Awards** — won 傳統藝術類「最佳演唱」獎
- **2011-10** — received the Knowledge of the World Award from Johns Hopkins University as a 傑出校友 (first Taiwanese recipient)

## Education
- Peabody Conservatory of Johns Hopkins — B.S. 聲樂演唱 & 早期音樂 (dual major, 研究所)
- Shenandoah Conservatory — Ph.D. 聲樂演唱

## Performance Career

- 美國華府國家大教堂 — 男高音獨唱
