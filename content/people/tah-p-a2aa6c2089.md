The memoir is a first-person record of the author's own surgical career and friendships: he and his classmate 馮光憲 served in 1970 as 總住院醫師 of 台大醫院 一般外科 and 胸腔外科 respectively (the author 胸腔外科, matching the 寶刀未老記 caption 「he had been 胸腔外科總住院醫師 in 1970」), were 醫科 classmates and colleagues for eleven years, and after graduation 張武誼、陳維昭、尤耿雄 and the two of them went their separate ways in 1970 when 馮光憲 left for the U.S. while the author stayed on as 台大 胸腔外科 主治醫師; the author was 馮光憲's sole 伴郎, mediated the couple's pre-wedding quarrel, visited him twice in 雪城 (Syracuse), reunited with him at the 北美台灣人醫師學會 希濶亞 (Sequoia) trip in 2000 and at the 醫科 40-year graduation 環島 reunion in 2005 (涵碧樓 高歌), arranged the 台大 李伯皇 consultation and the liver-transplant decision after 馮光憲's tumor was found, and took his farewell phone call in which 馮光憲 said 「謝謝你！」 and 「我…不想說話！」 seven days before passing; the author also lectured 死亡的藝術 at a 北美校友會 (as 馮光憲 recalled hearing). 馮光憲 (a 肝臟移植 survivor who later died of metastatic cancer after moving to 瀋陽) — the memoir's subject — has no vault page: single-obituary depth, mention-only, no mint, no merge; 馨玲 (馮's spouse), 小莉 (馮's daughter), 汝晉兄, 張武誼／陳維昭／尤耿雄／李伯皇 are 欄內被報導人物, 不代筆添條. — [美洲台灣日報](https://taiwandaily.net/%e5%8f%b0%e7%be%8e%e6%96%87%e8%97%9d/62437/)（存檔 `knowledge/web-archives/taiwandaily-net-62437-62437.md`；dedup 寫入前 `grep 62437 content/people/` = 0 hits）
