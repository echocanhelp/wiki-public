# Kwei-Yang Ben Chang (張貴洋)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Kwei-Yang Ben Chang
- **Chinese:** 張貴洋
- **Era:** -
- **Geography:** 台灣
- **Core roles:** Taiwanese American (TAH Who’s Who)
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Kwei-Yang Ben Chang (張貴洋) is listed in the TAH Foundation Who’s Who Taiwanese American profiles.

## Role in the Community

- Served as **秘書長 (Secretary-General)** of 北美臺灣客家公共事務協會 (Taiwan Hakka Association for Public Affairs in North America), under 會長 徐寶乾 (TAH Our Journeys 記錄 #290, 徐民忠, 2017/05).
- **2008 華府台灣同鄉會 (TAA Washington D.C.) 會長** — listed as president of the 2008 理事會 roster (secretary 吳玉琴 Janice Wu), and named in the 創會先鋒獎 photo caption as the sitting 華府會長 alongside chapter founders and 現任巴城會長 沈郁良 (TAH Our Journeys 記錄 #138, 陳淮崇, 華府與巴城TAA的誕生, 2015/05).
- Email contact postchang@aol.com / Tel 240-839-7047 recorded in the 北美臺灣客家公共事務協會 leadership listing ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys290|Our Journeys 記錄 #290]]).
