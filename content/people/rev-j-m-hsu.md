# Rev. J. M. Hsu (許錦銘)

**許錦銘牧師** (Rev. J. M. Hsu, 1932 Changhua – 1995) was a 台神 graduate who took **U.S. TPC / PCUS / RCA** pulpits. TAH: first pastor of [[organizations/formosan-presbyterian-church-in-los-angeles|FPCLA]] **1973–1979**. GSTPC40 黃德利: one of **seven PCT-trained pastors** then in SoCal who helped the 1980 GSTPC plant; deceased by 2021. **Pipeline:** PCT seminary → U.S. Taiwanese pulpit. Not a PCT congregation pastor in the U.S.
