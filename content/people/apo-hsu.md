# Apo Hsu (許瀞心)

**Apo Hsu** (許瀞心; also **Apo Ching-Hsin Hsu** on English concert listings) is a Taiwan-born conductor and teacher. TAH Who’s Who lists her as conductor, educator, musician, and professor. She is **not** [[people/ahhee-hsu||Ahhee Hsu (許丕龍)]].

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Apo Hsu; concert listings also Apo Ching-Hsin Hsu
- **Chinese:** 許瀞心
- **Born:** year not given on TAH; birthplace 基隆 / 台灣
- **Geography:** Keelung → US training (Hartt) → US podiums → Taiwan (TAH address field: 台灣)
- **Core roles:** conductor; NTNU symphony conductor (from 2003); US orchestra music director / artistic director posts
- **Source layer:** TAH Who’s Who + TAH #369 / #1310; EN Wikipedia on [[people/tyzen-hsiao||Tyzen Hsiao]] / *Ilha Formosa*

## Overview

TAH’s Who’s Who card (updated 19 June 2026) is a **directory record**, not a full biography. It places her birth in Keelung, a B.S. in music at 台灣師範大學, an M.S. at the Hartt School of Music, and a stack of conducting posts in Taiwan and the United States. Family fields on that card are empty (`Husband/Daughter/Son: -`). First US city/year is also `-`.

The Tyzen Hsiao literature is the named US–Taiwan concert link: she conducted Hsiao’s *Ilha Formosa* requiem and the 2007 **Formosa Dreaming** program.

## Tyzen Hsiao / *Ilha Formosa*

On [[people/tyzen-hsiao||Tyzen Hsiao]]’s page (EN Wikipedia / *Ilha Formosa* article):

- Apo Hsu conducts the **NTNU Symphony Orchestra** and **Formosa Festival Choir** in Hsiao’s *Ilha Formosa: Requiem for Formosa’s Martyrs*.
- **2007 Formosa Dreaming** — concert of works for orchestra and voices by Hsiao and Fan-Long Ko (柯芳隆). US tour after a sendoff at Taipei 中山堂 (the hall of Hsiao’s first 蕭泰然樂展, 1975). EN Wikipedia video note: DVD, concert **9 September 2007**, Apo Ching-Hsin Hsu conducting NTNU Symphony + Formosa Festival Chorus.

柯芳隆 has **no** Echopedia person page yet — do not invent a slug.
