- Parents — mother 顏一清, a mathematics professor and one of very few women in that post at the time; father a banker who had majored in economics; the household was not wealthy but spent spare money on books and classical-music records — per 世界新聞網 亞裔月 report 2025-05-01 (記者張庭瑜, 洛杉磯), `https://www.worldjournal.com/wj/story/122903/8712089`.
- Siblings — 「我們家有四個孩子」 (four children); her older brother works in the arts and, in her own joke, 「把所有的繪畫細胞都拿走了」; two siblings still live in Taiwan, whom she visited weekly while teaching in Taipei in 2007 — per 世界新聞網 2025-05-01 (`https://www.worldjournal.com/wj/story/122903/8712089`) and 台大物理系《時空》 magazine interview no. 31, `https://spacetime.phys.tw/vol_31/v31_007_yeh.pdf`.
- Mother's death — 顏一清 died in April 2007; Yeh returned to Taiwan that summer to teach an intensive course partly so she could see her mother often — per 台大物理系《時空》 no. 31 interview (conducted 2007-09-01), `https://spacetime.phys.tw/vol_31/v31_007_yeh.pdf`.
- Marital status / children — unmarried, no children, described by herself as a deliberate choice (「一個 conscious decision」) made against the demands of full-time research; she kept a long-term partner 里克 of about ten years as of the 2007 interview and has nephews and a niece she spends school holidays with — per the 《時空》 no. 31 interview, `https://spacetime.phys.tw/vol_31/v31_007_yeh.pdf`. Note: the same interview reports her secondary school as 台北市立復興中學, which conflicts with the北一女 report in her own 2010 autobiographical statement (see Source Notes); both kept.

## From the record

Captured and cross-checked 2026-11-14 into [[sources/tah-whoswho-prof-nai-chang-yeh-capture]]; labels follow that page (self-report = `alleged`, third-party = `sourced`).

- Born 一九六一年十二月在嘉義出生 (December 1961, Chiayi) — per `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/pride-prof-nai-chang-yeh.md` (上游 自由時報 2005-08-16 專訪「葉乃裳 揚名國際 物理界」).
- Entered NTU physics with a perfect math score and a total above NTU medicine; graduated 台大物理系 at 二十一歲 (early admission one year), 每學期都得書卷獎 — per the 自由時報 專訪, archived at `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/pride-prof-nai-chang-yeh.md`.
- At MIT, Mildred Dresselhaus (爵瑟郝絲) volunteered to supervise her through the department office, per her own retrospective 「Mildred S. Dresselhaus (1930-2017)」 (PMC5530714), which dates the first meeting September 1983; the PhD thesis 「Electronic and magnetic properties of graphite intercalation compounds」 was completed January 1988 (per `https://yehgroup.caltech.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/YehNC_2024_March_-bio_one-page.pdf` and the Library of Congress authority `https://id.loc.gov/authorities/names/no2011163439.html`).
- After one year of postdoctoral research on high-temperature superconductivity at IBM, she passed assistant-professor applications at Harvard and Caltech and chose Caltech for its push into condensed matter physics — per the 自由時報 專訪 archived at `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/pride-prof-nai-chang-yeh.md`.
- 三十三歲 she became Caltech's first tenured Asian woman professor in its hundred-year history and the physics department's first woman professor — per 自由時報 2005-08-16 (`pride-prof-nai-chang-yeh.md`); Wikipedia dates the first female professorship to her 1989 joining, per `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nai-Chang_Yeh`.
- Time magazine's 「Galaxy of Stars」 (Special Issue on California, Vol. 138 #20, p.73, Nov 18, 1991) named her one of the most promising young scientists — per `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nai-Chang_Yeh`; her own group CV dates the Time citation to 1991, per `https://yehgroup.caltech.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/YehNC_2024_March_-bio_one-page.pdf`.
- Academia Sinica elected her to the 34th academy (第34屆, 2024), 數理科學組, per `https://academicians.sinica.edu.tw/index.php?id=822&r=academician-n%2Fshow`; 台大物理系 congratulated its 1983 級系友, per `https://www.phys.ntu.edu.tw/News_Content_n_46542_s_238539.html`.
- Her Caltech group CV (March 2024) records 160+ papers, 9 patents, APS Board of Directors 2024-2026, NSF CEOSE member 2016-2023, and President of OCPA 2013-2014, per `https://yehgroup.caltech.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/YehNC_2024_March_-bio_one-page.pdf`.
- Honors roster (Academia Sinica biography): Louis Meyer-Schüzmeister Memorial Award (1986), Sloan (1990-1992), Packard (1992-1997), APS Fellow (2004), AAAS Fellow (2007), 吳健雄傑出講座獎 (2012), 教育部亮點玉山學者／臺師大玉山學者傑出講座教授 (2022-2028), 清華榮譽講座教授 (2026-2029) — per `https://academicians.sinica.edu.tw/index.php?id=822&r=academician-n%2Fshow`.
- Since 2022 she teaches at NTNU as a 玉山學者 — 「奈米科學與技術導論」 (2022-07) and 「群論在凝態物理的應用」 (2024-09-09 to 10-08), per 臺師大理學院新聞 2024/07/15, `https://rh.acad.ntnu.edu.tw/tw/news/content/166`.
- In her autobiographical statement 「A Journey of No Return and No No Regrets」 (Kaohsiung J Med Sci 26(6S): S17-S21, 2010) she credits her mother, a mathematics professor, and Dresselhaus as role models — per `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nai-Chang_Yeh` citing Yeh 2010; the TAH self-report `Family` rows are blank (`family_filled: false`, `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/person/prof-nai-chang-yeh.json`).
- Caltech's Heritage Project holds an oral history of her by David Zierler from interviews on 2022-06-27, 07-14, 08-23, 09-15 and 11-22, per `https://heritageproject.caltech.edu/interviews/nai-chang-yeh`.
- Caltech's own faculty record (Division of Physics, Mathematics and Astronomy) now titles her **Thomas W. Hogan Professor of Physics** and prints the complete rank ladder: B.S. NTU 1983; Ph.D. MIT 1988; Assistant Professor Caltech 1989-95; Associate Professor 1995-97; Professor 1997-2022; Hogan Professor 2022-; Fletcher Jones Foundation Co-Director, Kavli Nanoscience Institute 2013-19 — per `https://www.pma.caltech.edu/people/nai-chang-yeh` (fetched 2026-11-17) and the identical record at `https://www.kni.caltech.edu/people/nai-chang-yeh` and `https://www.directory.caltech.edu/personnel/ncyeh`.
- The Hogan named professorship is corroborated by the Caltech named-professor announcement indexed on her own PMA page (`Caltech Introduces New Named Professors and Leadership Chairs`, `https://www.pma.caltech.edu/people/nai-chang-yeh` Related News, fetched 2026-11-17).
- Her Kavli co-directorship is dated by two Caltech releases: EAS appointed her and Keith Schwab the first endowed Fletcher Jones Foundation co-directors of KNI for a 3-year term on 2013-07-11, and the university-wide release followed on 2013-08-08, naming them successors to Michael Roukes and Oskar Painter — per `https://www.eas.caltech.edu/news/kni-welcomes-new-co-directors` and `https://www.caltech.edu/about/news/yeh-and-schwab-named-kavli-nanoscience-institute-codirectors-40054`. The end (2019) is dated by Julia Greer's appointment as Fletcher Jones Foundation Director effective 2019-10-01, replacing Oskar Painter and Nai-Chang Yeh — per `https://www.caltech.edu/about/news/julia-greer-named-director-kavli-nanoscience-institute-caltech`.
- PMA also lists two awards absent from the Academia Sinica roster: Outstanding Achievement Award, Tsien Excellence in Education Program, Tsinghua University (Beijing), 2019, and the Highlight Yushan Fellow / NTNU Yushan Distinguished Professor terms 2022-2028 — per `https://www.pma.caltech.edu/people/nai-chang-yeh` (Selected Awards, fetched 2026-11-17).
- Personal journey, first person: 「A Journey of No Return and No Regrets」, Kaohsiung J Med Sci 26(6S): S17–S21, published online 2010-06-09 (Crossref DOI 10.1016/S1607-551X(10)70053-X, ISSN 1607-551X / 2410-8650, publisher Wiley, sole author Nai‐Chang Yeh, affiliation Department of Physics, Caltech; her contact string `ncyeh@caltech.edu` is the keyed identifier) — per `https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1016/S1607-551X(10)70053-X` and `https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20538242/`, self-report.
- In that 2010 statement she fixes several dates the TAH self-report leaves bare: she decided to become a physicist at age 12; entered 台北市立第一女子高級中學 (Beinu) with the highest score in the entrance examination; entered NTU physics as her top choice again with the highest entrance score and the only perfect mathematics score; won the Dean's award every semester (top 5% per department); was admitted to the MIT physics PhD program in 1983, having never boarded a plane or left Taiwan before; slept on the floor of a Chinese students' social club for her first week in Boston; completed the PhD thesis in 4 years 3 months; began the IBM T. J. Watson Research Center postdoc (Yorktown Heights) in January 1988, one month after the Bednorz/Müller Nobel award; and within a year held tenure-track offers from Caltech, Harvard, Columbia and the University of Pittsburgh — none of which then had any woman on its faculty. She joined the Caltech faculty in summer 1989 and writes that she was granted tenure in 1995 — per `https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20538242/` (self-report).
- The 2010 statement also names her secondary school as 北一女, while the 《時空》 no. 31 interview has her recalling the first physics course at 台北市立復興中學 at age 12 — the two are kept as dated snapshots, not harmonized; see Family.
- Harassment/research-integrity record, first person: she reports groundless attacks on her work by a couple of white men who did not believe a result of hers could be good, plus competitive backstabbing in anonymous proposal and paper review — per 「A Journey of No Return and No Regrets」, `https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20538242/` (self-report). The 2025 世界新聞網 report has her recall a formal investigation lasting three months over suspicion of fabrication, closed when other groups reproduced her results — per `https://www.worldjournal.com/wj/story/122903/8712089` (secondary; the two accounts are consistent but not shown to be the same case).
- Her signature experiments are named by the 2025 report: pairing-symmetry experiments on high-temperature superconductors as the work she is proudest of, and plasma-enhanced CVD growth of graphene at room temperature converting methane and other greenhouse gases into high-value material; current focus on 2D materials and semiconductor device scaling limits — per 世界新聞網 2025-05-01, `https://www.worldjournal.com/wj/story/122903/8712089` (secondary).
- Teaching-pipeline role in Taiwan and China: she introduced learn-by-doing undergraduate research and served as gifted-program consultant at 清華大學 and 交通大學, and in the 2007 《時空》 interview she taught the NTU physics summer course 「凝態量子場論專題」 — per 世界新聞網 2025-05-01 and `https://spacetime.phys.tw/vol_31/v31_007_yeh.pdf`. The same interview records her participation in the 8th 吳健雄科學營 in summer 2005 and her return for the NTU physics department's 60th-anniversary 系慶 late 2005.

- Research-interest framing from the employer page: experimental condensed matter physics — quantum materials and nanoscience; correlated electrons (high-Tc superconductors, colossal magnetic perovskites), topological materials (topological insulators/superconductors), low-dimensional van der Waals systems (graphene, hBN, TMDs), and energy research (photovoltaics, lithium-ion batteries, supercapacitors) — per `https://www.pma.caltech.edu/people/nai-chang-yeh`.
