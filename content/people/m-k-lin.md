## Timeline
- 1918-12-17 — born in 頂茄萣, 湖內庄, 岡山郡, 高雄州 (Taiwan under Japanese rule; 大正七年), to farmer 林騫 and 林蔡鴦 — per `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/mystories515.md`
- (year unrecorded) — married 許金英, a nurse from 桃園, while both served at 田獨礦山病院 (Hainan, near 榆林港)
- 1946-03-01 — married 許金英 in 海南島 — per `knowledge/web-archives/…/posts/mystories515.md`
- 1953 — moved the family from 茄萣 to 鹽埕區, 高雄 (光復街, behind 大新百貨公司); serving 省立高雄醫院婦產科 — per `knowledge/web-archives/…/posts/mystories515.md`
- 1954 — founded and opened 杏林婦產科 in 高雄 — per `knowledge/web-archives/…/posts/mystories515.md`
- 1979 — retired; immigrated to the U.S. via family reunion, settling in San Diego, CA — per `knowledge/web-archives/…/posts/mystories515.md`
- 2000 — wife 許金英 died in her sleep after ten years bedridden, whom he had cared for day and night — per `knowledge/web-archives/…/posts/mystories515.md`
- 2004 — at age 86 took part for a full week in the 阿扁 (Chen Shui-bian) presidential campaign — per `knowledge/web-archives/…/posts/mystories515.md`
- 2008 — returned to Taiwan campaigning with a DPP presidential candidate's team — per `knowledge/web-archives/…/posts/mystories515.md`
- 2010-12 — began dialysis for hypertension-related kidney failure — per `knowledge/web-archives/…/posts/mystories515.md`
- 2017-01-14 — died in San Diego at age 99 (虛歲 100), having stopped dialysis in early 2017 and chosen palliative care with no intubation — per `knowledge/web-archives/…/posts/mystories515.md`

## From the record
- **Birth (alleged — family record, 2017):** 1918-12-17 (大正七年), 頂茄萣 in 湖內庄, 岡山郡, 高雄州; parents 祖父林騫 / 祖母林蔡鴦; he was the eldest son and fourth child — per `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/mystories515.md`
- **Schooling (alleged — family record, 2017):** 成功小學 (故鄉) → 台南州立第二中學校 (台南一中前身), five years → 台北帝國大學附屬醫學專門部 (台大醫學院前身), four years; OB/GYN training after graduation at 赤十字社病院 — per `knowledge/web-archives/…/posts/mystories515.md`
- **Wartime posting (alleged):** during WWII he took a physician post at 田獨礦山病院, 海南島 (near 榆林港), run by 石原產業株式會社 iron-ore mining — per `knowledge/web-archives/…/posts/mystories515.md`
- **Post-war (alleged):** returned to Taiwan aboard 宜興號; held a joint 謝神/宴客 with his physician brother 淵泉 (returned from 京都帝大醫學); at the time 茄萣鄉 had never produced a physician — 「一時傳為佳話」 — per `knowledge/web-archives/…/posts/mystories515.md`
- **228 period (alleged):** had just reported for duty at 台南省立醫院 when 228 broke out; returned to 茄萣 and worked at the 漁會診所 — per `knowledge/web-archives/…/posts/mystories515.md`
- **Clinic practice (alleged):** the 杏林婦產科 sign was plain and unlit — 「父親個性保守，不爭名利」 — which kept business modest but left him time to raise seven children, all of whom completed university — per `knowledge/web-archives/…/posts/mystories515.md`
- **Honour (alleged):** 「被聖地牙哥同鄉會推選為模範父親」 (elected model father by the San Diego 同鄉會), for nursing his bedridden wife for ten years without visible weariness — per `knowledge/web-archives/…/posts/mystories515.md`
- **Advocacy (alleged):** traveled to Geneva with 北美洲台灣人醫師協會 (NATMA) 「利用隨北美洲台灣人醫師協會到日內瓦宣達〝Taiwan for WHO〞」, touring Switzerland, the Netherlands, France and Belgium — per `knowledge/web-archives/…/posts/mystories515.md`; the memoir prints photos captioned 「左二為林明昆醫師」 at the Geneva Taiwan for WHO rally and 「中為林明昆醫師」 at a 2008 DPP campaign event
