# Lin Qiong-xian (林瓊仙)

**Lin Qiong-xian** (林瓊仙) was a prominent member of the Fengyuan family and the mother of biochemist 廖述宗 (Liao Shu-zong). She was known for her热心公益 (enthusiasm for public welfare), matchmaking skills, and dedication to her family.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Lin Qiong-xian
- **Chinese:** 林瓊仙
- **Hometown:** 豐原 (Fengyuan), Taiwan
- **Spouse:** 廖繼春 (Liao Jichun), famous painter
- **Children:** 8 (including 廖述宗 as third child)
- **Education:** Graduate of 彰化高女 (Changhua Girls' Normal School), 2nd class
- **Geography:** Fengyuan, Tainan, Taiwan; Chicago (later)
- **Core roles:** Community volunteer; art teacher; matchmaker; mother of 8

## Background and Education

Lin Qiong-xian came from a prominent family in 豐原 (Fengyuan), Taiwan. She graduated from 彰化高女 (彰化女子高級中學 / Changhua Girls' Normal School), the 2nd class of its graduating cohort — an achievement that was notable for women in Taiwan during that era.

## Public Service and Community Work

She was known for being 热心公益 (enthusiastically involved in public welfare causes). Beyond her community work, she also taught art at a school in Tainan, following in the artistic spirit of her husband 廖繼春.

## Matchmaking and Family

Lin Qiong-xian was skilled at matchmaking. When her son 廖述宗 received his PhD from the US without a wife, she took matters into her own hands. She arranged a meeting with a fellow townsman's daughter who was coming to Chicago for graduate studies — resulting in the marriage of 廖述宗 to 郭淑卿 (Guo Shu-qing) in 1960.

## Family Life

The family ran a stationery shop called "文藝社" (Wenyi She) at home. Lin Qiong-xian raised eight children with great dedication, managing household affairs, the family shop, and supporting her husband's artistic career.
