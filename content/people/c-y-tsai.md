## Role in the Community
- Founding participant of the North America Taiwanese Professors' Association (NATPA): among the sixteen professors at the first organizing meeting, University of Chicago international house, 1980-02-16, per a founding memoir ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys69|ourjourneys69]], EN: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys69-eng|ourjourneys69-eng]]).
- Elected a General Director (一般理事) at NATPA's first council; later served as NATPA's **second president**, term beginning 1981-08-01, elected at the 1981 annual meeting co-hosted with the Taiwanese American Conference–East Coast and TAA (same memoir, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys69-eng|ourjourneys69-eng]]).
- With fellow ex-president 賴義雄, the most active volunteer as principal/deputy liaison when NATPA's 10th-anniversary annual meeting was held in Taipei in 1990 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys107|ourjourneys107]]).
- **Tenth-term president of NATPA (from 1989)** — his own memoir narrative in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys47|ourjourneys47]] records that he served as deputy (副手) to ninth-term president 陳文彥 in 1988–1989, then succeeded him as tenth-term president and drove NATPA's first-ever Taiwan-hosted annual meeting (June 1990, 國賓飯店 Taipei, ~150 members and spouses attending), serving on the organizing team as 副總協調人 and 農業組召集人. The "second president" note above refers to his 1981–1982 term (listed as 第二屆會長 in the same roster); both terms are confirmed by the same record — no conflict.
- Attended the 1990-04-21 second preparatory meeting for NATPA's 10th-anniversary Taipei annual meeting at 賴義雄's home in Washington D.C. (with 胡勝正, 陳文彥, 吳漢南 and others); in May 1990 NATPA sent him back to Taiwan for two weeks to invite participating institutions and scholars, and he co-founded the in-Taiwan preparation committee with 吳樹民, handling venue, lodging, and reception logistics ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys107|ourjourneys107]]).
- Lectured on "以基因工程解決缺糧困境" (solving food shortages via genetic engineering) at a community program ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys47|ourjourneys47]]); TAH encyclopedia entry [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/603-e8-94-a1-e5-98-89-e5-af-85-201509|603. C. Y. Tsai / 蔡嘉寅 / 2015/09]].
