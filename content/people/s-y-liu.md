## Role in the Community
- Her TAH Foundation Who's Who encyclopedia record is held in the corpus as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-1846-s-y-liu|1846. S. Y. Liu 劉淑媛]] (published 2017-08-15, band B). Other corpus "Shu-Yuan" hits (159/389 賴淑媛, 蔡淑媛 private collections) are different people — not merged.
- Recorded in 朱真一's essay [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys270|270. 早期留學北美的台灣女士–戰後初期]] (12/2016, band A) as one of the early church-linked women who went to North America to study (with 林淑卿、蕭永真): she passed a Taipei university after high school, studied one year, then went alone to the US in 1954 to study music — consistent with the Education table (Huntingdon College B.A. Music; later Westminster Choir College and Northwestern, M.S./M.M.).
- Editor/author of 《春風化雨–劉主安校長百年誕辰紀年集》(新希望教育基金會, 台南縣, 2004), cited as a source in that essay; commemorates principal 劉主安 (1904–1994). Her editorial work on the memoir volume is her recorded community contribution beyond the encyclopedia entry.
