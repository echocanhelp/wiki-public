## Role in the Community
- Food-history writer alongside his academic career; the corpus holds three of his contributions:
  - [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories550|550. 曼哈頓的辣椒味道：蜀湘園集團的故事 / 郭忠豪 /05/2017]] — essay on the Szu-Xiang Yuan Sichuan-Hunan restaurant group in Manhattan (2017-05-31).
  - [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories540|540. 揮別台灣前往紐約打拼，他在法拉盛開設記憶中的台灣料理 / 郭忠豪 /04/2017]] — on a Flushing restaurateur recreating Taiwanese cuisine from memory (2017-04-10).
  - [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1084|1084. 食光記憶：12則鄉愁的滋味 / 胡川安, 郭婷, 郭忠豪 /03/2017]] — co-author of this book on food and nostalgia (2017-03).
- His essays treat restaurant history as immigrant/ethnic memory, matching his NYU history training and UIUC East Asian Studies appointment.
