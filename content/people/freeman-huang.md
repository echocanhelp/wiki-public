# Freeman Huang (黃樹人)

**Freeman Huang (黃樹人)** is the publisher associated with **taiwanjustice.net** (台灣公義網 / Taiwan Justice), a Taiwanese-American news and opinion site whose public web presence closed around October 2025. On the **TAHS 2025 organization chart** he is listed as **Secretary (秘書長)** of the [[organizations/taiwanese-american-historical-society|Taiwanese American Historical Society (TAHS)]].

## Identity Snapshot
- Era: Contemporary (site active into mid-2020s; closed ~2025-10)
- Geography: Taiwanese American media / diaspora audience
- Core roles: Publisher, taiwanjustice.net (台灣公義網); **TAHS Secretary (秘書), 2025**

## Overview

Under Huang’s publishing role, Taiwan Justice carried large volumes of Traditional Chinese reporting and commentary on Taiwan, US affairs, and community topics. After the live site closed and no private backup was available, Huang authorized Echopedia (TAHS) to copy and preserve the public historical corpus from web archives (LINE, 2026-07-24): the site had closed the previous October; he welcomed an Echopedia copy effort.
