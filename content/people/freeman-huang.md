# Freeman Huang (黃樹人)

**Freeman Huang** (黃樹人) is a Taiwanese American journalist, publisher, and community leader. He is a founding member of the Taiwanese American Historical Society (TAHS) and has been a prominent voice in the Southern California Taiwanese American community through his work in publishing and media.

## Career

### New Asian Weekly (新亞週報)
Freeman Huang served as **Publisher of New Asian Weekly** from 1994 to 2010, making him one of the longest-serving publishers of a major Taiwanese American newspaper. Under his leadership, the publication served as a vital communication bridge for the Taiwanese American community in Southern California.

### Taiwanese American Channel (南加州台灣人影音頻道)
In 2015, Huang became **Producer of the Taiwanese American Channel**, a video platform serving the Southern California Taiwanese American community. The channel provides news, cultural programming, and community coverage.

### Taiwanjustice.net (台灣公義網)
Also in 2015, Huang became **Publisher of [[organizations/taiwanjustice-net|taiwanjustice.net (台灣公義網)]]**, a platform focused on Taiwan-related social and political issues.

## TAHS Founding

Freeman Huang was among the **initial preparatory members** who attended the founding meeting organized by Charles Yang (楊嘉猷) on August 28, 2013. The group included:

> 鄭炳全, 楊嘉猷, 周威霖, 王耀廷, 黃樹人, 黃根深, 林衡哲, 謝清志, 王廷宜, 李成奎, 鄭英松, 洪珘美, 楊子清, 吳光明, 周實, 陳隆, 曾俊嘉, 楊平猷, 楊文顯, 魏博基, 林小慧

The founding conference was held on December 11, 2013, at the Hilton San Gabriel, where the organization was formally registered.
