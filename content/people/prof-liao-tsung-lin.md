## From the record
- Taught 交流電機 (AC machinery), the required EE-course staple; the Japanese-legacy campus 機電設備 (發電機、馬達) could only be maintained by him (陳秋官 memoir, [alumni.ncku.edu.tw](https://alumni.ncku.edu.tw/p/404-1004-63303.php?Lang=zh-tw), 2010-03-01) — `alleged` (single account).
- Kept 40 years of student 成績簿 (grade books) in one thick volume — memoir anecdote — `alleged`.
- Notable students named by the memoir: 許文龍 (奇美企業創辦人), 劉炯朗 (前清華大學校長), 鄭崇華 (台達電董事長), 王康隆 (奈米電子研發中心 Director) — `alleged` as a roster claim from one memoir.
- Library catalogs corroborate the textbook independently of both local artifacts: 國立宜蘭大學馆藏 `電機機械(上)／林料總著／三民／1985`; 高師大馆藏 `電機機械／林料總／臺北市:三民／民60` — print-imprint rows, bibliographic-only (see capture page `## Web arms searched`).
