## Academic career (absorbed from TAH profile tables)

王美珠 earned her B.A. in Accounting and Banking at National Taiwan University (1961), then completed both her M.A. (1965) and Ph.D. (1967) in Economics at the University of Rochester. Her teaching record runs from an assistant professorship at New York University (1967–1969) through visiting (1975–1976) and assistant (1981–1987) appointments at National Taiwan University's Department of Business, to the University of Colorado Denver economics department, where she was associate professor (1987–2000), professor (2000–2008), and professor emerita from 2008. TAH's encyclopedia records her distinction as the first Taiwanese American woman to reach this career milestone (see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/firstfamous-389-prof-mei-chu-wang-hsiao||TAH #389]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-1559-mei-chu-wang-hsiao||TAH #1559]]).

## Community involvement
