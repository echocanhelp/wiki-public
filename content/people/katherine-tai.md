## Timeline
- 1974 — born (Era per TAH directory)
- 1996 — B.A. (History), Yale; English teacher at Sun Yat-sen University, Taiwan (1996–1998)
- 2001 — J.D./Ph.D. (Law), Harvard
- 2007–2014 — U.S. Federal Government, General Attorney
- 2011–2014 — Chief Counsel for China Trade Enforcement
- 2014–2017 — Trade Consultant, U.S. House Committee on Ways and Means
- 2021– — Trade Representative, Office of the United States Trade Representative (nominated Jan 2021)
