# Thomas Barclay (巴克禮)

**Thomas Barclay** (巴克禮, POJ: Pa-khek-lé, 21 November 1849 – 5 October 1935) was a Scottish missionary of the Presbyterian Church of England. He arrived in Takow (Kaohsiung) on 5 June 1875 and spent about 60 years in southern Taiwan. Not [[people/george-leslie-mackay||馬偕]] (north) and not [[people/james-laidlaw-maxwell||馬雅各]] (south, 1865).

## Timeline
- **1875-06-05** — Arrived Takow
- **1876** — Founded Tainan Theological College and Seminary (台南神學院)
- **1903-05** — With Elizabeth Barclay (伊莉莎白), opened the East Gate 福音堂 that became [[organizations/tainan-east-gate-barclay-memorial-church||東門巴克禮紀念教會]]
- **1935-10-05** — Died in Tainan
