# Ching-Jang Yao (姚慶章)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Ching-Jang Yao
- **Chinese:** 姚慶章
- **Era:** 1941-2000
- **Geography:** 台灣台中
- **Core roles:** artist
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Ching-Jang Yao (姚慶章), born September 16, 1941 in Taichung, Taiwan. Graduated from Fine Arts Department of National Taiwan Normal University in 1965. Held his first solo exhibition at the Art Gallery of National Taiwan Arts Center, Taipei. In 1969, received Honorary Award at the 5th International Youth Artist Exhibition in Tokyo. In 1970, attended the 10th Saint Paul International Art Exhibition in Brazil, then moved to the USA with his family and continued creating art. In 1991, participated in ‘Taipei-New York: Confrontation of Modernism’ at Taipei Fine Arts Museum. In 1994, received the Overseas Painter Contribution Award of ROC Art Gallery Variety Exhibition, and was listed as a representative super-realist in the publication Photorealism (New York: Harry N. Abrams, Inc.). In 1998, participated in exhibitions at Ping Tung Hsien Cultural Center, Miaoli Hsien Cultural Center, Tai Nan Cultural Center, and Guam University. In 1999, participated in the 15th National Art Exhibition, Taiwan, and held an invited solo exhibition at Hsin Chu Hsien Cultural Center. In 2000, held a solo exhibition at Longmen Gallery featuring ‘Circle’ and ‘Diamond’ series paintings. He passed away suddenly
