## Role in the Community

The Baltimore–DC community memoir ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys-138|大華盛頓區台灣同鄉會四十週年回憶（Our Journeys 138）]]) places him at the core of the early Taiwanese physician community there:

- In the early 1960s the Taiwanese population in Baltimore and Washington DC numbered only about a hundred, half of them doctors and their families, concentrated around Johns Hopkins as interns and residents. 黃錫勳 is named among that founding circle (with 陳庵君, 洪朝煌, 曾伯元, 許日章, 陳建南, 鄭顯綬, 石岫, 黃碩文, 卓金泳 and others).
- The memoir's retrospective, written for the Greater Washington DC Taiwanese American Association's 40th anniversary, lists him among departed friends no longer living ("多人甚至不復在世——黃際鑫、黃錫勳、洪朝煌、曾伯元、石岫、陳炳耀"), consistent with the 1994 end-date on his profile.

He also wrote for the community press: an essay 《醫學趣談》 appeared in the Life/生活 section dated 04/1994 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1219|1219. 醫學趣談 / 黃錫勳 / 04/1994 / Life生活]]). His TAH encyclopedia records are archived at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-2194-shi-shung-huang|2194. Prof. Shi-Shung Huang 黃錫勳教授]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/137-prof-shi-shung-huang|137. Prof. Shi Shung Huang 黃錫勳教授]].

  (widow's memoir, per `…/bfnn-0493.htm`). Mackay Memorial Hospital is the pre-emigration post the TAH form omits.
- **1962 vs 1963 — arrival-year conflict, kept as dated snapshots** — his own 1994 自序 says 「一九六二年來美國接受臨床訓練」
  ([TAH publications1219](https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/publications1219/), per `…/pubs1219.html`), the same
  page's third-party 簡介 says 「一九六三年赴美巴爾底摩深造」, and the TAH form's arrival field is `1963/Baltimore/MD`.
  Unresolved (GAPS); the 1962 reading is consistent with a Mackay year ending 1962 + a 1963 Baltimore start.
- **1966 — 應聘至巴爾地摩醫學中心，負責設立「社區醫療保健中心」** — dated appointment line from the widow's memoir
  (per `…/bfnn-0493.htm`). This is the only dated anchor anywhere for the clinic career the self-report dates only
  as "1970's", and it precedes the decade the self-report claims for the "first Open Clinic".
- **The clinic's catchment and caseload (documented, single third-party witness)** — 「距市中心點只有十條街…包括五個政府為窮人設置的公寓區」,
  「附近一哩半週邊地區，有百分之七十五的家庭沒有男人」, 「廿七年來，這個中心看過五萬五千余名的病患，都屬貧民，約占巴城人口的百分之八」, and
  「曾被編入國會紀錄，列為全國社區醫療中心的典范」 (widow's memoir, per `…/bfnn-0493.htm`).
- **Ed Koch endorsement + 國會紀錄 (alleged, quoted in widow's memoir / his own 自序)** — 「美國纽約州衆議員Ed Koch ,稱讚『大巴爾底摩家庭
  健康中心是全美國最佳的社區家庭健康中心』為全美國的典範，並列入國會紀錄」 (per `…/pubs1219.html`). No congressional-record citation on this box (GAPS).
- **1991-10 — diagnosis**: a chest X-ray taken before a trip to Taiwan found 「肺部有個二公分（直徑）大的瘤」; the diagnosis was
  腺型肺癌 (widow's memoir, per `…/bfnn-0493.htm`). Left lung + two ribs removed at 約翰霍普金斯大學醫學院; chemotherapy from 1992-01;
  spread to brain and spine by 1993-10 (same source).
- **1992 spring — 因病不得不提早退休** per the widow's memoir (「一九九二年春，黃錫勳因病不得不提早退休」, per `…/bfnn-0493.htm`);
  his own 自序 says 「一九九三年春天，因病決定提早退休」 (per `…/pubs1219.html`). Kept as a dated conflict (GAPS).
- **March 12 — the proclamation is dated 1993 by two independent accounts of the same event**: the widow writes
  「一九九三年四月廿六日，著名的太陽報在其早報及晚報中特別報導黃錫勳的成就事蹟與退休消息」 right after 「巴爾地摩市長更宣布三月十二日為巴爾地摩市的
  『黃錫勳醫師日』」 (per `…/bfnn-0493.htm`), and he himself writes 「巴爾底摩市長Kurt L. Schnok於三月十二日宣佈該日為『黃錫勳醫師日』。
  隨後巴爾底摩太陽報於同年四月廿六日大幅報導」 (per `…/pubs1219.html`). Both place the proclamation in the 1992/93 retirement sequence, i.e. **March 12, 1993**,
  naming mayor **Kurt L. Schmoke** (the page prints `Kurt L. Schnok`, sic). Still single-event sourcing — the city
  proclamation text itself is not on this box (GAPS/Wayback).
- **Post-retirement publications beyond the TAH form** — during his illness he 「著手編著《醫學趣談》及《回憶錄》」 and assisted
  中華婦女聯合會大華府分會 in publishing 「《中英文醫療用語手冊》、《醫學保健手冊》」 (widow's memoir, per `…/bfnn-0493.htm`); his 自序 names
  陳昌平醫師 as editor and 藝軒出版社 as publisher, and states that as a university student (1953-1961) he 「先後發表了一兩百篇文章」
  (per `…/pubs1219.html` — `alleged`, self-count in his own preface).
- **1994 — died 1994 after ~2.5 years of treatment**, per the widow's narrative (per `…/bfnn-0493.htm`), consistent with the
  TAH form's `1934-1994` and the ourjourneys-138 departed-friends list.
