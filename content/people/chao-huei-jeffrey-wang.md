## Role in the Community
- Anchor of the Chicago-area Taiwanese community: he and his wife 王黃信惠 (Cindy) were famed hosts of the 北濱會, a monthly dinner-and-rotating-credit (標會) club of Chicago's north suburbs that ran without interruption for more than a decade and grew from about ten families to more than twenty. The couple hosted holiday gatherings at their home; they appear in the 2004 members' group photo.
