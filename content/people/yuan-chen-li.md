# Yuan-Chen Li (李元貞)

**Yuan-Chen Li (李元貞)** is a Taiwan-born composer and educator listed in the [[organizations/tah-foundation||TAH Foundation]] Who’s Who (#1088, 2016/06) and musician series (#12, 2014/10). The TAH musician profile dates her birth to **1980** and places her 2016–2017 teaching at **Reed College**, Portland, Oregon. This page absorbs the **vault archive** of those two TAH posts (`knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/musician12.md`, `…/whoswho1088.md`). It is **not** a live résumé.

## Overview

Li first arrived on the contemporary music scene in Taiwan with her chamber piece **Zang (the funeral / 《葬》)** in **2000**. In **2003**, her orchestral work **Awakening / 《春日醉起言志》** won a prize at the Asian Music Festival in Tokyo from the Asian Composers’ League and was premiered by the Tokyo Philharmonic Orchestra. TAH’s English musician copy describes later work as transforming Chinese phonology, Taiwanese chamber and aboriginal music, Asian traditional arts, literature, and Buddhism into technique for solo, chamber, and orchestra on Western and Chinese instruments.

The Chinese musician copy adds early study with **吳丁連** (composition / classical) and piano with **梁孔玲**; college study at **臺北藝術大學** with **洪崇焜** and **楊聰賢**; guqin and phonology with **李楓** and **李殿魁**; nō-kan study with **瀧澤成實** during the 2000 Asia-Pacific arts festival. U.S. study (from 2006) led to a Yale Artist Diploma (2008, Martin Bresnick and others) and a University of Chicago Ph.D. in music composition (2015). Primary Chicago advisors named: composers **Marta Ptaszynska** and **Shulamit Ran**, conductor **Cliff Colnot**, musicologist **Martha Feldman**, theorist **Lawrence Zbikowski**. Dissertation work: cello concerto **「漂點」 / “Wandering Viewpoint”** plus a paper on vocal composition and secondhand Holocaust experience.

**Degree-label conflict inside TAH:** Who’s Who #1088 lists Taipei University of the Arts **B.A.** / **M.S.**; musician #12 lists **B.F.A. (2003)** and **M.F.A. (2006)**. This page records both labels rather than choosing one.

## Timeline

- **1980** — born, Taiwan (musician #12).
- **1998** — enters 臺北藝術大學 (Chinese musician copy).
- **2000** — chamber work **Zang / 《葬》**; nō-kan study during 亞太藝術節.
- **2001** — 關渡新創新聲 (Kuan-Du Musical Soiree).
- **2003** — B.F.A. (musician #12); **Awakening / 《春日醉起言志》** prize, Asian Music Festival, Tokyo; Tune in to Taiwan; New Ideal Dance Festival.
- **2005** — 《間奏曲：參》教育部文藝獎室內樂首獎 (Chinese copy).

- **2006–08** — New Music New Haven (Yale).

- **2008** — Yale Artist Diploma; The Female Form (New York); Composer/Pianist series (New England, 2008–9).
- **2009** — Peace Cross-strait Orchestra Concert; University of Chicago admission / full scholarship (Chinese copy).
- **2010** — Cité Internationale des Arts, Paris (Taipei Artist Village); 《福爾摩沙之民歌新聲集》 arrangement project.

- **2012** — ACL Tel-Aviv; Thailand International Composition Festival; IMANI Winds (New York); NPAC 「樂典」 commission 《間奏曲：商》.

- **2016-06-18** — TAH Who’s Who #1088 published.

## Quotes

Chicago Classical Review on “Wandering Viewpoint” (cited 2015.4.24 in musician #12): Li’s compositional voice “original and somewhat difficult to describe,” “engaging.”

連憲升 (2008), on 《春日醉起言志》, as quoted in the Chinese TAH copy: 「某種程度似乎總結了近半個世紀台灣追求二十世紀『音樂現代性』的成果」.

李子聲 (2010), on 《間奏曲：參》: the qin is not treated as a conventional soloist but mixed as an equal among ten Western instruments.
