## Role in the Community

Cheng is named in the movement's own memoirs as an enabler of community organizing in Los Angeles: the memoir describes him as 辦公室主任 of the DPP US West Coast office (民進黨美西黨部辦公室) at the 洛杉磯會舘, whose relationship allowed the group's founding meetings (first meeting 2009-08-16, with 王泰和, 洪珠美, 許輕甫, 鄧昇東 and others) to use the space free of charge — matching the TAH table entry CEO 民進黨美西黨部辦公室 (2009-2010). See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys265|Our Journeys #265]]. He also appears among the signatories of the 2018 NTU-alumni (農經) protest letter against 南加州台大校友會 inviting 管中閔 — see [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2018/20180721091303_2018_07_20_抗議-南加州台大校友會邀管中閔來年會做專題演講_a428dcebbceacf38|Taiwan Justice 2018-07-20]].
