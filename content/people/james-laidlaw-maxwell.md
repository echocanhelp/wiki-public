# James Laidlaw Maxwell (馬雅各)

**James Laidlaw Maxwell Sr.** (馬雅各, 18 March 1836 – 6 March 1921) was a Scottish physician and the **first** Presbyterian missionary to Taiwan (Formosa). He served with the English Presbyterian Mission. **Not** [[people/george-leslie-mackay||George Leslie Mackay (馬偕)]].

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** James Laidlaw Maxwell
- **Chinese:** 馬雅各 (POJ: Má Ngá-kok)
- **Born:** 18 March 1836, Scotland
- **Died:** 6 March 1921
- **Sent by:** Presbyterian Church of England (English Presbyterian Mission)
- **Field:** Southern Taiwan — Taiwan-fu / Tainan, then near Cijin (Kaohsiung)
- **Medicine:** University of Edinburgh, 1858

## Timeline
- **1864** — Sent to Taiwan
- **1865-06-16** — First Presbyterian church in Taiwan (Tainan). PCT still marks this as its anniversary
- **1868** — Married Mary Anne Goodall in Hong Kong; work near Cijin
- **1872** — Advised Mackay to begin in the north, near Tamsui
- **1885** — Retired to London; first secretary, Medical Missionary Association
- With his sons, oversaw **Sin-lâu / 新樓** hospital in Tainan — first Western-style hospital in Taiwan
