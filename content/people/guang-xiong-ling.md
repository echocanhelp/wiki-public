## Role in the Community

- 1970 US-East Taiwanese Summer Institute (第一屆美東夏令會): as a 和平契友 (Heping Church fellowship member) then doing his PhD in Connecticut, he attended the inaugural 1970 summer institute; the memoir's author later relied on him as an email source for reconstructing the event: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys268|Our Journeys 268 — 1970夏令會 history]].
- Church leadership: as 凌光雄長老 of the greater-Washington Taiwanese church, he was one of the church's representatives in negotiations with 世界和平協會 chair 王清珠, which resulted in a land-and-cash donation supporting the church building fund: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys217|Our Journeys 217 — 華府教會建堂 history]].
- His TAH Who's Who entry is held in the corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-2228|2228. Guang-Xiong Ling 凌光雄/11/2019]] (recorded 2019-11-12).

Corpus memoirs consistently describe him as an elder (長老); the TAH table records only employment (NY, NJ 聯邦牛奶市場管理處; 美國農業部華府農業合作社服務局 1978-). No date conflicts observed.
