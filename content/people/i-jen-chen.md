## From the record

All rows below are the subject's own record unless a label says otherwise: the self-reported TAH
Foundation Who's Who record #1452, archived on this box in two artifacts and preserved in full at
[[sources/tah-whoswho-i-jen-chen-capture|TAH Who's Who profile capture]], and his own memoir essay

- **1999 年秋天** — enrolled again at 華盛頓大學 (University of Washington) for the hospital-administration
  master's programme while in active clinical practice: 門診 by day, master's courses and long-term-care
  ward rounds by evening, weekend study in cafés. — self-reported ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys281|281. 如是，我為]])
- **2000** — began recording talks on ageing in his examination and waiting rooms and sending the tapes to
  公共電視台 on a regular schedule: 老年醫學養生、老人社會福利、晚年財務規劃、個人經驗分享. — self-reported ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys281|281. 如是，我為]])
- **2001** — received the 醫管碩士 (master's in hospital administration) and subsequently passed the
  長照管理專科文憑 (long-term-care management specialty diploma). — self-reported ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys281|281. 如是，我為]])
- **2002 年春天** — founded the non-profit 「亞裔銀髮族關懷基金會」 (Asian Senior Concerns Foundation) with a small
  group of volunteers and began fundraising, opening with weekly Saturday-morning lectures in a venue rented
  three hours a week from a community activity centre, with volunteer-led tai-chi/qigong and later social
  dance and 老人茶. — self-reported ([[organizations/asian-senior-concerns-foundation|Asian Senior Concerns Foundation]] · [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys281|281. 如是，我為]])
- **2003** — in summer co-hosted a one-day health-lecture event with a local hospital; in autumn held a large
  health fair (保健園遊會) in a borrowed parking lot with several Mandarin-speaking local physicians, with
  hundreds from the 僑社 visiting booths including senior social-welfare and medical-information stands. — self-reported ([[organizations/asian-senior-concerns-foundation|Asian Senior Concerns Foundation]] · [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys281|281. 如是，我為]])
- **2004** — volunteers and participants outgrew the Saturday slot, so members donated to open an account,
  lease a fixed venue and donate furniture, and taught classes themselves. The Saturday programme
  standardised into themed weeks (慶生會/經驗分享, medical, social welfare, travel and personal topics,
  financial planning) plus full-day classes (電腦、排舞、太極拳、氣功、社交舞、國標舞、書法、水墨畫、攝影、棋藝、麻將).
  He took trainer certification through his own professional qualification and, with local social-welfare and
  public-health agencies, ran 慢性病／糖尿病自我管理、預防跌倒與保持平衡、災難緊急狀況處置與準備、銀髮族安全駕駛
  courses; the group grew into a respected local Asian-American and diaspora organisation. — self-reported ([[organizations/asian-senior-concerns-foundation|Asian Senior Concerns Foundation]] · [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys281|281. 如是，我為]])
- **annual** — led participants in a year-end results exhibition performed before an audience of about 500,
  used to observe patients' habits first-hand and give practical medical advice. — self-reported ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys281|281. 如是，我為]])
- **2017-02-21 10:27** — signed the memoir essay as 「陳一仁醫師、亞裔銀髮族關懷基金會 (美國西雅圖) 創辦人」, the only
  dated byline in the corpus naming his founder title. The essay carries its own timestamp, distinct from its

  founder title corroborated by the TAH #23 roster ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/columns23|TAH #23]])
- **2017** — by his own account had entered 長輩之年 and had been following and leading the same patients for
