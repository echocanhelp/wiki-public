## Role in the Community
- From her family's memoir in the corpus, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys33|33. 一家人做伙來打拼 追求台灣建國的感人故事 (張炎憲/2014/09)]]: 張月英 is the fifth daughter of the 張 family, graduated from 輔仁大學哲學系, and taught at 基隆信義國中 and 台北市新興國中 before emigrating. She arrived in the US in July 1979 and stayed with her eldest sister.
- Political journey per the same memoir: she had joined the KMT as a university junior out of enthusiasm, but after reading 王育德's 《苦悶的台灣》 and 《台獨》月刊 in America she felt deceived by the KMT and soon joined 台獨聯盟 — her household's independence-activism arc is documented alongside husband [[people/allen-chang|張伯寬]] in the section 「四、張伯寬與張月英」.
- Community leadership: the 台灣公論報停刊 reminiscence [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys49|49. 《台灣公論報》創刊]] identifies her as 婦女會總會會長 (president of the Women's Association), the officer consulted to confirm the paper's shutdown; the article also names fellow contributors 姜瑞香, 張秀滿, 張雅美, and 歐春美 around the 公論報 women's page.
- Who's Who record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-anna-chang|985. Anna Chang 張月英 / 2016/05]] (band B). IRS Enrolled Agent per the TAH profile.
- HOLD: education — corpus memoir records 輔仁大學哲學系; the TAH Who's Who profile carries no education field, so the two are non-conflicting but the memoir is the sole source and is held unverified.
