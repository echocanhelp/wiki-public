# Jackson Kuo (郭敏俊)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Jackson Kuo
- **Chinese:** 郭敏俊
- **Era:** 1950
- **Geography:** 台灣 屏東 番仔寮
- **Core roles:** artist, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

HERMIT Jackson Kuo is born in Taiwan in 1950. He is not a real hermit but in spiritual level he wishes himself as a hermit, and practices a simply form of life style.

## Role in the Community
- **2016-07** — published 《Hermit Jackson Kuo's Sketch Book 郭敏俊の寫生帖》: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1007|TAH #1007]] (2016-07-26).
- **2016-08** — "Car for Taiwan": donated the proceeds from the sale of his sketch book to 繁華教會 in 屏東, his hometown — community story co-credited to [[people/prof-helen-lee|Prof. Helen Lee]] at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories453|TAH mystories 453]] (2016-08-05). Confirms 番仔寮/屏東 roots as an active church-giving act, not just birthplace.
- **2017** — two art collections recorded in the corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1159|TAH #1159 郭敏俊畫集 第一輯]] (2017-10) and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1160|TAH #1160 郭敏俊畫集 第二輯]] (2017-11). HOLD: #1159's byline reads 郭俊敏 (name characters transposed vs 郭敏俊 everywhere else) — treated as a source typo, not merged.
- **2016-07** — authored 郭敏俊專欄 (Taiwan e-News column series): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/columns9|TAH #9 郭敏俊專欄(台灣e新聞)]] (2016-07-07) — establishes him as a regular community columnist, not only a visual artist.
- **2017 (recorded 2018-01-04)** — art collection [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1158|TAH #1158 Paint My Hometown: GreenBrook, Danville and Beyond]] — hometown-painting series alongside the 畫集 第一/第二輯.
- **2014 late–2017** — taught the Japanese class at the 東灣台灣人長樂會 (East Bay Taiwanese Association): under his instruction students read Japanese, sang Japanese songs, and staged Japanese plays; the class ended summer 2017 — remembered as instructor in a community memoir, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-689|TAH #689 Christine L. Yang, My Language Learning Journey]] (2019). Corroborates the Japanese-studies/teaching role beyond the CSU/De Anza instructor listings.
