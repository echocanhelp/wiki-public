# Charles Yang (楊嘉猷)

## Identity Snapshot
- **Era:** First-generation Taiwanese American
- **Geography:** Southern California (Los Angeles area)
- **Core roles:** Founding President of TAHS, community leader, 228 memorial organizer

## Overview
- 楊嘉猷 (Charles Yang) is the founding president of the Taiwanese American Historical Society (TAHS), established December 11, 2013
- He was elected as founding president at the first preparatory meeting in August 2013, following recommendation by 鄭炳全
- He co-authored the profile of 王能祥 and wrote the memorial tribute for 廖述宗
- He has served as a community organizer for 228 memorial services and various Taiwanese American cultural events
- He stepped down after one term; Franklin Ping Cheng served as president 2014–2017
