# Fang-Ming Chen (陳芳明博士)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Fang-Ming Chen
- **Chinese:** 陳芳明博士
- **Era:** 1947
- **Geography:** Kao-Hsiung, Taiwan
- **Core roles:** professor, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Author of numerous publications on Taiwanese history and literature, including works on the 228 Incident, Yang Kui's literary career, and a biography of Hsieh Hsueh-hung

## Role in the Community

- **Editor-in-Chief of《台灣文化》Taiwan Culture** — invited by President Maysing Huang (黃美幸) of the Taiwanese American community's publishing effort. The memoirs credit him directly: his talent and hard work made the bimonthly an extremely high-quality pure literary-arts magazine, "the pride of the overseas Taiwanese people," deliberately unlike ordinary association newsletters. Taiwan Culture also recorded historical facts about the 228 Incident in its early issues. See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys8-eng|Our Journeys 8 (EN)]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76|Our Journeys 76]].
- **Own TAH literary work** — 461. 荊棘的閘門 (文學, 1992/09), archived in the TAH corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/461-e8-8d-8a-e6-a3-98-e7-9a-84-e9-96-98-e9-96-80-e9-99-b3-e8-8a-b3-e6-98-8e-e6-9|荊棘的閘門]].
- **Speaker at Midwest Taiwanese Summer Conference** — listed among speakers alongside Lung-Chi Chen (陳隆志) and others: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys123-eng|Our Journeys 123 (EN)]].
