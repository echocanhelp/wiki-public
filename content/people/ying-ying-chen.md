# Ying-Ying Chen (陳盈穎)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Ying-Ying Chen
- **Chinese:** 陳盈穎
- **Era:** -
- **Geography:** 台灣
- **Core roles:** entrepreneur, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Currently resides in New York City, Ying-Ying Chen is a native from Taiwan. She’s a musician, an educator, a music arranger and a game designer. After graduating from National Sun-Yet Sen University in Kaohsiung, she came to NY to pursue her Master’s degree at NYU in Flute Performance. As an active performer and more than 15 years of teaching music lessons, she has recently created and launched Shoot That Note, a mobile app designed to help music learners with better and faster sight-reading ability. Please visit www.yuppyfun.com for more information.
