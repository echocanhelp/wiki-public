## Role in the Community

- 2014 — Served as **大會理事長** (chairman of the organizing board) of the 美東台灣人夏令會 (US-East Taiwanese American Summer Conference), held 7月6日於賓州 East Stroudsburg. The conference's closing-plenary 大會聲明 was drafted by [[people/tsu-yi-jay-loo|盧主義]] and completed with 陳初雄's approval as board chairman; the memoir's author records personally reading the statement aloud in Taiwanese at the closing ceremony ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys260|夏令會閉幕聲明 record]]). The same memoir situates the conference's fundraising around pro-Taiwan activists returning from the US to Taiwan, 太陽花運動 youth groups, and US-East Taiwanese American civic organizations.
