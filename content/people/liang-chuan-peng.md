Liang Chuan Peng (彭兩泉) is listed in the TAH Foundation Who’s Who Taiwanese American profiles.

## Role in the Community
- Second president (第二屆會長, 1975) of the 北加州台灣同鄉聯合會 (Northern California Taiwan United Association), founded 1973-09-22 — during his term the 美西夏令營 (US-West summer camp) began, giving Taiwanese community members more time together. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys38|38. 協志會會史 (ourjourneys)]]
- Listed among 協志會 alumni who became leaders of Bay Area Taiwanese organizations, in the union-presidents roster of the association history. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys37|37. 台灣協志會 (ourjourneys)]]
- Named subject in the same records' subject lists ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whoswho803|803. Liang Chuan Peng 彭兩泉 / 2016/02]]).
