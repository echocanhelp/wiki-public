# Carolyn Kuan (關琦安)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Carolyn Kuan
- **Chinese:** 關琦安
- **Era:** Birth Place
- **Geography:** First year, first city and state in the U.S.
- **Core roles:** artist
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

With her appointment as the 10th Music Director of the Hartford Symphony Orchestra beginning in 11/12, Carolyn Kuan’s reputation as a skilled interpreter and impressive podium presence is recognizable on a national level. Ms. Kuan has also enjoyed long and successful associations with top tier orchestras, opera companies, ballet companies, and festivals worldwide, and continues to distinguish herself as a conductor of extraordinary versatility. Ms. Kuan’s commitment to contemporary music, both at home in Hartford and as a guest conductor, has defined her approach to programming, and established her as an international resource for new music and world premieres.

## Career notes
- Appointed the 10th Music Director of the Hartford Symphony Orchestra beginning in the 2011/12 season, serving from 2011 onward.
- Earlier posts: Associate Conductor of the Seattle Symphony Orchestra, Assistant Conductor of the Baltimore Opera Company, and Artist-in-Residence with New York City Ballet.
- Educated at Smith College, the University of Illinois (Master of Music), and the Peabody Conservatory (Performance Diploma).
- Known for commitment to contemporary music and world premieres, working as a guest conductor with orchestras, opera companies, ballet companies, and festivals worldwide.
