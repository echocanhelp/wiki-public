# Dr. Binsiong Ou (胡民祥博士)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Dr. Binsiong Ou
- **Chinese:** 胡民祥博士
- **Era:** 1943
- **Geography:** Tainan, Taiwan
- **Core roles:** engineer, journalist
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

4. Published 14 Taiwanese books and many articles in T. A. newspapers, newsletters, and magazines.
