## Role in the Community

- **1953 — 來台赴美 :** Left Taiwan 1953-10-01 after two years of surgical residency at 台大醫院; came to St. Louis (Baptist Hospital), then moved to Barnes Hospital/Washington University for neurosurgery residency from 1954-07. Passed the neurosurgery specialty exam 1958.
- **1959 — 日本 :** With his US specialty certification, helped found the neurosurgery department at a Christian hospital in Japan — a founding figure of Japanese neurosurgery specialty accreditation and a permanent member of its exam review board.
- **Washington University 最傑出校友獎 :** Received WashU's most-outstanding-alumni award in St. Louis; the 台大醫學院校友會 and local 醫師協會 jointly held a luncheon in his honour (account by 朱真一, published 2016).
- **1982 — 矽谷 :** With his brother 黃世雄 under the 台灣慶豐集團, invested heavily in Silicon Valley and founded five high-tech companies, including Cogito (hard disk drives), Magnex (disk-drive heads), and Genoa (graphics cards).
- **醫師史定位 :** Recorded in 黃哲陽's台美人醫師 overview as the pioneer (先峰) of brain-neurosurgery training from Taiwan who then went to Japan to teach neurosurgery and returned to Taiwan to run world-scale enterprises (三陽工業).
