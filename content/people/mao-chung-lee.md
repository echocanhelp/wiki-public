## Career notes
- Born 1940 in Miaoli (苗栗), Taiwan.
- 1964: graduated from the Art & Design Department of the Taiwan Academy of Art (國立台灣藝專, today's National Taiwan University of Arts); 1965: studied arts and crafts in Japan.
- 1968–1970: part-time lecturer at Chinese Culture University (中國文化大學) and Shih Chien College of Home Economics & the Arts (實踐家專); 1969–1971: lecturer and head of the ceramics department at Fuxing Junior College of Engineering (復興工專).
- 1972: sent to the U.S. for a touring solo exhibition of ceramics and sculpture; 1973: remained in the U.S. to work in modern ceramic sculpture; 1973–1975: studied sculpture at the Art Department of the New School for Social Research, New York.
- 1979–1985: director (董事) of the Chinese-American Arts Center (美國美華藝術中心); 1984: honorary Master of Fine Arts from the Columbia Institute of Art, Maryland (馬利蘭州哥倫比亞藝術學院); 1985: special lecturer at New York University's art department and the Greenwich ceramics academy in New York (紐約格林威治陶藝學院).
- 1997: returned to his hometown Miaoli and re-established the «純青窯創作室» studio; 2003: public artwork installed at the Ching-Shuei Rest Area on the National Highway.
- Author of 泥香集—李茂宗陶藝, 現代陶藝與設計, 李茂宗陶藝, 李茂宗陶彫, and 李茂宗的泥土世界.
