## Role in the Community
- Per 黃正源's 2014 memoir of the San Diego Taiwan Center, J. P. Lee served on the **first board of directors of the Taiwan Center Foundation** as the 社團董事 representing the Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce (San Diego chapter), whose president he was. He pledged **US$100,000** — the first such pledge among the society directors — which accelerated fundraising for the Center's permanent building. See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys26||26. 聖地牙哥台灣中心的前半生與轉型 / 黃正源 / 2014/09]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys26-eng||English translation]].
