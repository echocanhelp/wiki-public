## Role in the Community

- Recorded in the TAH corpus as Who's Who entry [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-2080-stephanie-syd-yang|2080. Stephanie Syd Yang]] (published 2018-03-27).
- Filmmaker and media artist: profiled in the TaiwaneseAmerican.org Taiwanese-American film record as a mixed-race (Taiwanese / European American) artist based in the Bay Area who began making films in 2002 after studying film theory in grad school, founded shiftingnarratives.com — a community of filmmakers using the short narrative form to give voice to marginalized people ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/taiwanese-american-film|Taiwanese-American film record]]).
- NATWA II community activist: conference records document her leading a "Healthy Body, Healthy Mind" workshop and co-leading (with Jennifer Kuo) the generational-communication workshop "Can We Talk?" at the NATWA II conference ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/natwa2con|NATWA II conference record]], [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/letter-of-advice|NATWA II attendee account]]).
- Writer: her memoir Release: A Bulimia Story (Blue Jaguar Healing Arts) is recorded among the notable Taiwanese-American releases of 2019 ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/19-things-that-happened-in-taiwan-taiwanese-america-in-2019|Taiwanese America in 2019]]); her corpus bio describes her as a mixed-race, 2nd-generation Taiwanese-American queer writer, energy healer and spiritual coach (Blue Jaguar Healing Arts) with a short story in the Taiwanese-American anthology Chrysanthemum, and she joined a 2018-04-22 panel on Taiwanese and Taiwanese-American filmmakers and parent-daughter relationships.
