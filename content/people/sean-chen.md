# Sean Chen (陳宣堯)

**Sean Chen (陳宣堯)** is a Taiwanese American pianist documented in the [[organizations/tah-foundation||TAH Foundation]] Who’s Who and encyclopedia entries (November 2015). TAH records him as born in 1988 in Margate, Florida, raised in Oak Park in the Los Angeles area, and trained at Juilliard and Yale. He is **not** the same person as [[people/shante-shawsean-chen||Shante Shaw Sean Chen (陳善哲)]].

Parents are described in TAH encyclopedia #312 as coming from Taiwan. This page absorbs vault copies of TAH #312 and #689 only; later career after the 2015 snapshot is not invented here.

## Overview

TAH encyclopedia **#312** (Pianist, 2015/11) opens: the American pianist Sean Chen was born in Florida and grew up in the Los Angeles area of Oak Park, California. Pre-college honors named there include an NFAA ARTSweek award, a prize at the California International Young Artist Competition, the Los Angeles Music Center’s Spotlight Award, the Evelyn Bonar Storrs Scholarship, and the Glenn Miller Scholarship. Combined with academic offers from MIT, Harvard, and Juilliard, he chose music and earned Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees at Juilliard (2006–2012).

While at Juilliard he won the 2010 Gina Bachauer Piano Competition, the 2010 Munz Scholarship, first prize at the 2008 Juilliard Concerto Competition, and the 2010 Paul and Daisy Soros Fellowship for New Americans.

As of the same 2015 text he lived in New Haven, Connecticut, pursuing an Artist Diploma at the Yale School of Music as a George W. Miles Fellowship recipient, studying with [[people/hung-kuan-chen||Hung-Kuan Chen (陳宏寬)]] and Tema Blackstone. Former teachers named: Jerome Lowenthal, Matti Raekallio, and teacher-mentor Edward Francis. (The previous stub cut off at “Mat”.)

Chinese #312 copy: 父母來自台灣、出生於美國佛羅里達、在加州長大.

## Career and honors (as of TAH 2015)

TAH #312 and the Who’s Who table (#689) list:

- **2011** — 2nd prize, Seoul International Music Competition
- **2013** — American Pianists Association DeHaan Classical Fellowship (首獎); TAH Chinese text calls this one of the most lucrative U.S. piano prizes
- **2013** — 3rd prize (Crystal Award / 水晶獎) at the 14th Van Cliburn International Piano Competition; TAH: first American to reach the finals since 1997
- **2013** — 3rd prize, Morocco Philharmony International Piano Competition
- **2015** — Leonore Annenberg Fellowship Fund for Performing and Visual Arts (莉奧洛．安納伯格表演與視覺藝術獎金)
