## Education
- Stanford University — B.A. Political Science and Human Biology, 1988 (`Juman` in the form dump is a typo; third-party fix, thirdparty §1)
- UC Berkeley — M.S. Health and Medical Sciences + M.P.H. Epidemiology, 1989–1992
- UC San Francisco — M.D., 1989–1994 (UC Berkeley–UCSF Joint Medical Program per Berkeley SPH)
- UC Irvine — Residency, Obstetrics and Gynecology, 1994–1998

## Employment
- US Department of Health and Human Services — Director of the Maternal and Child Health Bureau (self-report `Present` row is STALE: third-party record puts the tenure at 2012–2017)
- UCLA Schools of Medicine and Public Health — professor (later associate professor) of obstetrics, gynecology, and public health; lead investigator, National Children's Study LA–Ventura County; Director, UCLA Preconception Care Clinic
- George Washington University, Milken Institute School of Public Health — professor and senior associate dean for academic, student & faculty affairs (until 2019)
- UC Berkeley School of Public Health — Dean, effective 2019-07-01; reappointed to a second five-year term 2024-06-06
