## Role in the Community
- Per the family memoir ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys340]]): she **majored in piano** (主修鋼琴). When the teenage 陳毓襄 (Yvonne Chen) — then newly arrived from Taipei — visited with her parents, 瀛如 recognized her extraordinary talent at age 12 and **personally introduced her to 蕭泰然**, whose enthusiastic encouragement launched Yvonne Chen's path to the Juilliard master's, international prizes, and the US premiere of the 蕭泰然 Piano Concerto with NTSO. The memoir situates her inside the 蕭泰然 / 北美室內樂團 circle of the LA Taiwanese music community.
