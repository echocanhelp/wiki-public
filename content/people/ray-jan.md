# Ray Jan (冉瑞宜)

**Ray Jan (冉瑞宜)** is listed as **副秘書長** (deputy secretary-general) on the official board of the [[organizations/taiwan-center|Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館)]]. The current officer listing is the 2026-07-16 archive of https://www.taiwancenter.org/board (`taiwancenter-org-board.md`). Earlier Taiwan Justice coverage already in the vault shows the same Chinese name in related community roles: **董事** at a 2020 Taiwan Center gala press conference; **執行副總召** of 2021 台美人傳統週; **會長** of 世界華人工商婦女企管協會南加分會 (世華南加分會) at a 2020 PPP webinar; and a 2021 presidential remark naming 冉瑞宜 among overseas “十大傑出青年” honorees in the business category. This page absorbs those vault hits. It does not invent education, employer, or family.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Ray Jan

## Overview

Taiwan Center is a **501(c)(3)** (EIN **95-4679702**) at 901 S. Fremont Ave. #118, Alhambra, CA 91803. Official structure already on the org page: 董事長兼會館會長; 正副監事長 / 財務長 / 秘書長; 會長以下六位副會長; committees. The public board table names **27** people. Jan sits in the **officer** block as **副秘書長**, immediately after 秘書長 [[people/ken-wu|Ken Wu (吳兆峯)]] and before 財務長 [[people/lisa-liu|Lisa Liu (劉雅薇)]].
