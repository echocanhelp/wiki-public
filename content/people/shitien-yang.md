## Role in the Community

- **Chicago Taiwanese cultural movement (1987):** Yang was the lighting director (主掌燈光) for 「咱的鄕土咱的歌」, the major Taiwanese song-and-dance gala staged by the Chicago Taiwanese Cultural Promotion Association (芝加哥台灣文化促進會) on Nov 21, 1987 at Downers Grove High School, before an audience of about a thousand. The production record — 林中's account later preserved in the TAH our-journeys series ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys167|167. 咱的鄉土，咱的歌–芝城台灣文化促進會公演記實]], Band A community history) — lists him alongside producer 邱伸彥, director 陳淸風, and stage-set designer 王文隆, documenting him as a technical backbone of Chicago's Taiwanese cultural scene in addition to his nuclear-engineering career.
