## Role in the Community

- Served 18 years as a pastor in Brazil, where he built 9 churches; retired in Brazil and came to Austin in 1993.
- Organized the Austin Taiwanese church community from fewer than 20 family-church members: the congregation began in 1984 as home Bible studies among six Christian Taiwanese American families (王國雄/李音音, 林發祥/許錦屏, 潘以淳/黃美選, 楊明源/何秀琴, 李弘道/張媺, 蘇世明/陳瑞霞), met at Hope Vacation Church (11512 Olsson Dr.) after 1986, and under Pastor Yeh bought 3 acres at 5300 Duval Road and completed a main sanctuary and fellowship hall on May 5, 2001.
- The resulting church is the only one in the Austin area that identifies itself as a Taiwanese church and preaches in both native Taiwanese (台語) and English.

## Third-party community records

- 1999-07-02–05 — preacher and lecturer at the 30th 美東台灣人夏令會 (TACEC 1999), July 2–5, 1999 at Bloomsburg University, PA. The pre-event announcement in 台灣公論報 第1732期 (03/27/1999) calls him 「享譽國際的葉明翰牧師」and schedules his Sunday sermon; the follow-up report (台灣公論報 第1751期, 06/05/1999) names him 「來自德州奧斯丁台灣長老教會的葉明翰牧師」, credits him with a 台灣民俗 lecture series and the Sunday service, and says he 「精通命理，解夢、風水、輪迴及生命奧秘」— `sourced`（third-party press：`knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/tacec-1999.md`, per 台灣公論報 第1732期 03/27/1999 與 第1751期 06/05/1999, archived in the TAH corpus at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tacec-1999|TACEC 1999]]）.
- 1999 — delivered three lectures at that camp on July 3 and 4: 「命相的原理」、「作夢解析」and 「民俗宗教」, before a full house. The same report gives his backstory: 「由於身世坎坷，年輕時專注命運與風水的探討，替人相命多年，贏得「葉半仙」之稱，後來在得胃癌消沉之際，回歸基督教到聖經學院硏讀，畢業後在台灣、巴西和美國等傳教四十年」— his own account, as reported, of a fortune-teller past (「葉半仙」), a gastric-cancer crisis, and forty years of ministry across Taiwan, Brazil and the U.S. — `sourced`（third-party reporting of his own lectures：same archive file, per 台灣公論報 第1751期 06/05/1999）.
- 1999 — at a camp forum he was the third speaker on folk religion and evangelism: 「信仰耶穌後，發揮所學從人性的弱點爲切入點…傳教必需先瞭解台灣民間的風俗習慣，不只教育，文化要本土化，宗教亦需本土化。葉牧師所到之處，有多人跟隨問道、問理、求祝福」— `sourced`（third-party report：same archive file, 大會手冊 section）.
- c. 1990s Austin years — first Taiwanese-language teacher of Chen-Chen Wu (吳貞貞, b. 1974): her mother Pearl Wu tried to start a Taiwanese school in Austin and 「couldn't find a teacher at first. Fortunately it turned out the local Austin Taiwanese Presbyterian Church Pastor John Yeh(葉明翰) knew how to teach Taiwanese and he became our first teacher」. The memoir itself is dated 2019-03-20; the teaching sits in the Austin years (after 1993 per the Austin memoir's chronology), so the event date is inferred — `sourced`（third-party memoir：`knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/677-uc-berkeleys-taiwanese-language-class-1992-chen-chen-wu-03-2019.md`, bylined Chen-Chen Wu 吳貞貞, published 2019-03-20）.
- 2012-10-09–13 — one of six named lecturers (Dr. Andrew Dearman, 董俊蘭牧師, 黃芯芯博士, 葉明翰牧師, 布興大立牧師, 林俊育長老) at the 北美教會協會 (TCCCNA) annual meeting and 北美神學研究會, Oct 9–13, 2012, hosted at 休士頓台灣基督長老教會, theme 「現代人的福音：生命的視野」— `sourced`（third-party organizational record：`knowledge/web-archives/ntpc-usa-org-活動記錄摘要.md`, crawled 2026-08-18 from https://www.ntpc-usa.org/活動記錄摘要/, source hub [[sources/ntpc-usa-org|NTPC USA]]）.
