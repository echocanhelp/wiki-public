## Role in the Community
Dr. Jen-Yih Chu (Albert Chu, chuj@slu.edu) is a prominent historian of the Taiwanese and Taiwanese American experience, documenting early generations of Taiwanese physicians who studied in Europe and the US.

- **Presidency, North American Hakka Association for Public Affairs (北美客協):** served as the association's second president, 1992–1993, in the succession from 陳秋鴻 (1991) to 鍾博史 (1994–1995) — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/99-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e4-ba-ba-e9-a1-a7-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-7-e5-ae-a2-e5-ae-b6-e5-|99. Care for Taiwan by Taiwanese American: 客家台灣文化的推手]].
  - Independently corroborated in two further corpus records: 徐民忠's association history lists the same succession (1991 陳秋鴻 → 1992–1993 朱真一 → 1994–1995 鍾博史 → …) and notes 朱真一 (Albert Chu, chuj@slu.edu) among officer contacts — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys290|290. 北美臺灣客家公共事務協會 / 徐民忠 / 05/2017]]; 魏武雄's sketch of 北美客協 gives the identical president list — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys252|252. 續談美洲台灣客家聯合會並簡介北美台灣客家公共事務協會 / 魏武雄 / 09/2016]]. No conflicts with the existing record.
- **Author:** wrote 『台灣早期留學歐美的醫界人士』, cited as a source on early Taiwanese medical professionals abroad in the same work.
- **Co-author:** 「醫界溫馨與關懷」 with 賴其萬 — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1146|1146. 醫界温馨與關懷]].
- **Author:** 「向史明先生致敬——祝百歲偉大生涯」 (12/2017) — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories610|610. 向史明先生致敬]].
- Featured among Taiwanese American physicians in 黃勝雄's essay — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys285|285. 台美人中的醫師]].
- **Archival collection:** his personal holdings are preserved as a named archive in the corpus — 「11. Collection of Prof. Jen-Yih Chu 朱真一教授的收藏」(2017) — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/collection-of-prof-jen-yih-chu|TAH 檔案 11, 朱真一教授的收藏]]; own encyclopedia record at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/8-dr-jen-yih-chu|TAH 檔案 8, 2018]].
- **NATMA early infrastructure:** under 廖坤塗's presidency (1983–1990), the Saint Louis branch's 朱真一, 沈武典, and 陳澤華 were responsible for publishing the eight issues of the NATMA 會刊 sent to medical-school libraries in Taiwan — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys74|74. 1983至1990的北美洲台灣人醫師協會 / 周烒明 / 2014-12]].
- **Contributor to 賴其萬's language memoir:** 賴其萬 records a story 朱真一 told him — a Hakka classmate's father who grew up hearing neither 「國語」 nor 台灣話 on television — as a formative anecdote in 「台灣的語言－我的心路歷程」 — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys200|200. 台灣的語言 / 賴其萬 / 2016-03]].
