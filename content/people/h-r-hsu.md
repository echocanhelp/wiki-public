## Role in the Community

TAH movement memoirs (romanized "Ho Rui Hsu" / "Ho Ru Hsu") place this figure among the founding generation of the Taiwanese Association of America (全美同鄉會, TAA):

- **1970-01-28** — co-signed (for the Taiwanese Association of Los Angeles) the letter with Yi-Ho Cheng (East) and Chin-hung Lin (Chicago) proposing a national body; the TAA was founded in New York on 1970-07-01, with H. R. Hsu elected **first Vice-president** under President Yi-Ho Cheng (term to 1972-06-30). [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76-eng|Our Journeys 76 — TAA history (memoir)]]
- **TAA President** — in a later term, nine new chapters were added (Atlanta, Pittsburg, Purdue, San Francisco, Syracuse, Urbana, N.Y. Capital, New Jersey, Athens), taking the TAA from 34 to 43 chapters. Term work, per Vice-president Chiung-hui Ke's summary: a central membership register compiled from chapter lists; a TAA **emblem, anthem, and flag**; cooperation with the publishers of *May Flower* (梅花). [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76-eng|Our Journeys 76 — TAA history (memoir)]]
- **TACL founding (1982–1985)** — attended the 1982-01-21 SCTAI-vice-president Y. C. Hsu meeting that first proposed a Taiwanese American Citizens League; on 1984-12-01 joined the five-member revived preparatory committee; handled contact with lawyer Chung-yuan Liao for state incorporation and was assigned preparation of the founding general assembly (division of labor, 1985-01-29). [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys272-eng|Our Journeys 272 — TACL founding memoir]]
