## Story

Gary Marvin Davison was born in Dallas, Texas, in 1951, studied political science at Southern Methodist University in 1973, and taught in high schools of Texas and Minnesota from 1973 to 1993. His own blog bio adds ESL teaching in Taiwan, prison GED teaching at Missouri Eastern Correctional Center, world history and civics at Cannon Falls High School, and Fulbright lectures in Mandarin with field research among farmers in Guantian Xiang. He took degrees in history at the University of Iowa and, in 1993, at the University of Minnesota, defending a dissertation on the fate of Taiwan's farmers while teaching at an alternative North Minneapolis high school. His son Ryan Davison-Reed was born at Taiwan Adventist Hospital in Taipei in 1989, dating the family's residence there. From 1994 he directed the New Salem Educational Initiative, a role the record carries through 2017 marked Present, alongside a television show on K-12 education and eight claimed books — among them Culture and Customs of Taiwan, written with his wife Barbara E. Reed. Community records remember him helping move the heavy wedding sedan chair at the Festival of Nations booth in 2017 and speaking with his wife at the 2019 Insight into Taiwan seminar for TAA/MN. A 美洲台灣日報 columnist still cited his 2003 book in 2025, though the assessment there was the columnist's, not his own.
