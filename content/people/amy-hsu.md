## Role in the Community
- **1960, University of Minnesota:** reported to the incoming class in August 1960 together with 陳秉虔; both were already church believers in Taiwan, and they did not know each other before Minnesota. One of five co-organizers (with 殷宗舜, 李春美, 陳秉虔, 謝伯芳) of the weekly Friday 「Mpls 台灣人祈禱查經會」 launched fall 1960, which grew from about ten to over twenty Taiwanese students — an early node of Taiwanese student fellowship in Minneapolis — per the memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys-369|明州台灣人早期記憶]]. HOLD: conflict — memoir has 蘇惠美 enrolling at Minnesota in 1960 vs TAH bio showing a 政大 B.A. in 1963.
- **TAA Minnesota chapter:** appears in the chapter's annual leadership roster for 1997 in the chapter history [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys123-eng|明州台美人百年大事 (Brief History of TAA–Minnesota)]].
- **台灣獨立聯盟:** contributed a record of the 台灣獨立建國券 (nation-building bonds issued 1983 by 台灣獨立聯盟 US headquarters, donated toward台灣建國) — preservation provenance 「源自 蘇惠美(許永華太太)」 in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys147|台灣獨立聯盟建國券]].
- **Author:** her own essay 「看無人」 (03/2017) is held as a community historical record at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories533|看無人 / 蘇惠美 / 2017-03]]; her TAH Who's Who entry is [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-508|TAH #508]].
