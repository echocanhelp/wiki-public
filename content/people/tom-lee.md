## Role in the Community
- **TACL founding generation (1984):** In December 1984, SCTAI (Southern California Taiwanese Association) president 沈英忠 convened a dozen community members to revive the stalled 台美公民協會 (TACL) organizing effort; 李木通 was among the twelve attendees of that 12/1 holiday-inn symposium, per 楊嘉猷's community memoir — placing him in the founding circle of TACL alongside 吳澧培、許和瑞、張文志、楊嘉猷 and others. ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys272|Our Journeys 272 — memoir]])
- **Subject of a community memoir:** 楊遠薰 wrote 269. 李木通和王瑾瑾 南加奮鬥史 (2015/01) on the couple's struggle and success in Southern California. ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories269|TAHS story #269]])
- **TAH encyclopedia profile:** #703 Dr. Tom Lee 李木通 (2015/11); the encyclopedia entry 101. Dr. Tom Lee 李木通博士 (2018-09-19) is held in the corpus as a community historical record. ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-tom-lee|TAHS story #703]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/101-dr-tom-lee|TAHS story #101]])
- **Life Discussion Society circles (1966-):** Liu Tianliang's 生活座談會簡史 memoir records 李木通 among the UCLA-centered weekly study group (陳嶄新、黃根深、王秋森、陳松貞 et al.) from 1966 that seeded the movement circles, and as an original member of the 西區生活座談會 founded January 1978; during the 太平洋時報 crisis, fellow members 黃森元、陳惠亭 and 李木通先后出來擔任發行人、社長. ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys212|Our Journeys 212 — 劉天良 memoir]])
- **Memoir author:** Co-authored his own reminiscence 240. 半世紀前 UCLA的那些日子 with 黃樹人 (2016/08). ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys240|TAHS story #240]])
- **Still active in community endorsements (2025):** Listed among 台美人社區領袖 attending Michelle 朴銀珠's congressional campaign gathering in 柑縣 as 台灣會館前董事長. ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250808005844_台美人社區領袖力挺柑縣國會眾議員朴銀珠競選連_2fc3294ed01f2d2a|taiwanjustice 2025-08-08]])
- **Community media host:** As 社長 of 美洲台灣日報 he hosted the 台美人物誌 video interview series, e.g. the 黃茂清律師專訪 (2016-11-30). ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/videos48|TAHS video #48]])
- **Essayist:** Published 730. 從陳文成命案談起 in the TAHS story corpus (2020/05). ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-730|TAHS story #730]])
- **Interviewer of movement elders:** Conducted the 台美人物誌 video interview of 人權鬥士、台獨先鋒 王泰 (with 攝錄：黃樹人、文字整理：Edda Huang), preserved in the community article record 化作千風—懷念台美人的人權鬥士 台獨先鋒王泰. ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250811013132_化作千風_懷念台美人的人權鬥士_台獨先鋒王泰_31b7081a5d37939f|taiwanjustice article]])
- **台美人物誌 series organizer:** The 台美人物誌 video/text interview series ran as a joint 大洛杉磯台灣會館、美洲台灣日報、美國鷹社 sponsored project, 李木通策劃 / 黃樹人文字整理 — e.g. the 王克雄夫婦專訪 and the 半世紀前UCLA的那些日子 座談 (與談人 李木通、[[people/thomas-liu|劉天良]]、[[people/huang-gen-shen|黃根深]]). ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240905070707_root_a4f51e89fe5a4080|王克雄 memoir article]], [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240520023218_root_89e6683c93156205|UCLA座談 article]])
- **Fundraising convenor:** As convener (召集人) with 陳文石, delivered remarks at the 洛杉磯信賴之友會募款餐會大進場. ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240520024652_root_4536db9927402596|taiwanjustice event record]])
