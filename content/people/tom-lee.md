## Role in the Community
- **TACL founding generation (1984):** In December 1984, SCTAI (Southern California Taiwanese Association) president 沈英忠 convened a dozen community members to revive the stalled 台美公民協會 (TACL) organizing effort; 李木通 was among the twelve attendees of that 12/1 holiday-inn symposium, per 楊嘉猷's community memoir — placing him in the founding circle of TACL alongside 吳澧培、許和瑞、張文志、楊嘉猷 and others. ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys272|Our Journeys 272 — memoir]])
- **Subject of a community memoir:** 楊遠薰 wrote 269. 李木通和王瑾瑾 南加奮鬥史 (2015/01) on the couple's struggle and success in Southern California. ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories269|TAHS story #269]])
- **TAH encyclopedia profile:** #703 Dr. Tom Lee 李木通 (2015/11). ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-tom-lee|TAHS story #703]])
- **Community media host:** As 社長 of 美洲台灣日報 he hosted the 台美人物誌 video interview series, e.g. the 黃茂清律師專訪 (2016-11-30). ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/videos48|TAHS video #48]])
- **Essayist:** Published 730. 從陳文成命案談起 in the TAHS story corpus (2020/05). ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-730|TAHS story #730]])
