# Joe Hu (胡永全)

**Joe Hu (胡永全)** is listed as **個人董事** (individual director) on the official board of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館)]]. The live board page (archived 2026-07-16 as `taiwancenter-org-board.md`) pairs the English name **Joe Hu** with 漢名 **胡永全** and the role **個人董事**. He is not an officer on that snapshot (officers are 董事長 / 執行長 / 副董事長 / 秘書長 / 副秘書長 / 財務長).

This page does **not** invent a private biography. What the vault supports is the board listing, the Center’s published description of what directors do, and later community documents that name 胡永全 among Taiwan Center directors.

## Overview

Official board copy (same archive): 「27位來自各行各業的傑出董事，以專業知識與社區熱忱共同引領會館發展。我們不僅是決策者，更是台灣文化傳承使命的守護者，為台美人社區的未來承擔責任。」 Joe Hu appears in the **個人董事** block of that roster, between [[people/james-yang||James Yang (楊榮勝)]] and [[people/peter-lin||Peter Lin (林冠豪)]] in the site order.

The organization page records **36** directors in the intro text and a **27**-name current board table. Hu is in the individual-director row of that table. Occupations, education, family, and birth year are **not** in this source set.
