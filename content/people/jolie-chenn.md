# Jolie Chenn (陳芃佑)

**Jolie Chenn (陳芃佑)** is listed as **副董事長** on the official board of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館)]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Jolie Chenn

- **Role:** 副董事長
- **Organization:** [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center (TWCLA)]]

## Timeline

- **2024-11** — named among the 38位董事 in the 第26周年年會晚宴 announcement

- **Current site snapshot** — **副董事長** on the archived taiwancenter.org/board page (one of five 副董事長 alongside 蔡漢成, 劉玲華, 賴淑遠, 李賢群)

## Leadership record (vault-sourced)

- Serves as 副董事長 of [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center]], one of five 副董事長 alongside [[people/roger-tsai||蔡漢成 (Roger Tsai)]], [[people/linda-liu||劉玲華 (Linda Liu)]], [[people/shu-lai||賴淑遠 (Shu Lai)]], and [[people/tony-lee||李賢群 (Tony Lee)]]

- Remains among the 38位董事 named in the 第26周年年會晚宴 announcement, 2024-11
