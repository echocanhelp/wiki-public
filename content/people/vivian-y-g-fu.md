## Role in the Community

- **NATWA 月曆 (founding editor/curator, 1991–1996):** the first NATWA "臺灣人月曆" appeared in 1990-11; the inaugural 1991 edition (theme 「臺灣傳統建築之美」) was planned and coordinated (統籌策劃) by 林郁子, who kept running the calendar until she was elected NATWA 副會長 in 1996 (succeeded by 程韻如). The calendar's seed money (US$10,000) came from 郭榮桔, seeded by 呂秀蓮's suggestion to the incoming president 王明玉 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys60|60. 咱的故事—NATWA的歷史 / 吳秀惠、黃美惠 / 2014/11]]; her own account of the project — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys160|160. NATWA 月曆 / 林郁子 / 2015/08]]. The calendar is credited in NATWA's history with laying the organization's economic base.
- **NATWA structural reforms as officer:** while an NATWA leader she established the 「開源組」 and 「基金組」, set up a 演講局 to circulate year-conference lectures to local chapters, and composed the association's 會歌 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys60|60. 咱的故事—NATWA的歷史]].
- **TAC-WC non-profit incorporation:** per the TAC-WC founding history, "Vivian Fu" of San Diego handled the non-profit status work while 柯耀宗/Steve Lin drafted bylaws ahead of the TAC-WC Board's announcement in May 2017 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys352|352. History of TAC-WC / 黃東昇 / 2020/03]].
- **TAH Who's Who record:** bibliographic profile #2298 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-2298-y-g-fu|2298. Vivian Y.G. Fu 林郁子]].
