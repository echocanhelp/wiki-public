# Rev. Jonah Chang (張瑞雄)

**Rev. Jonah Chang (張瑞雄)** was a former minister of the Presbyterian Church of Formosa who, while serving the United Methodist Church in Fresno, California, led the **one-month experimental Formosan church** in Los Angeles in **August 1970** that catalyzed the permanent Formosan Christian Church / FPCLA.

## Role in FPCLA Origins

### August 1970 Experimental Church

From Dr. Albert S. Lai's 1971 dissertation:

- In **August 1970**, Rev. Jonah Chang started a **one-month experimental church** to test feasibility of a Formosan congregation in Los Angeles.
- **First experimental Sunday worship: August 9, 1970** — **22 adults and 11 children** attended.
- After four Sundays, when Chang returned to Fresno at month's end, the majority decided to organize a permanent church **without a settled pastor and without outside financial support**.
- Early facility support came via **Centenary United Methodist Church** and Rev. Peter Chen (Wesley Hall) until the move to Wilshire Crest Presbyterian Church in July 1971.

### Taiyou Church (太陽教會) Context

Later republication materials and wiki narrative also associate Chang with an earlier **Taiyou Church (太陽教會)** experimental effort (c. 1967) using Japanese Methodist facilities — treat as **content priority B** context pending fuller primary citation in the archive chunks.

## Timeline

- **Early 1960s** — Came to the United States
- **1967** — Switched to the United Methodist Church
- **1967** — Assigned to a Japanese church in Fresno, Central California
- **August 9, 1970** — First experimental Sunday worship; 22 adults and 11 children attended
- **August 1970** — Conducted one-month experimental church ("Taiyou Church") to test feasibility of a Formosan congregation in Los Angeles
- **August 30, 1970** — Experimental church ended; congregation decided to organize a permanent church
- **September 6, 1970** — Church officially established as Formosan Presbyterian Church in Los Angeles
- **Post-1970** — Returned to Fresno to continue pastoral work
