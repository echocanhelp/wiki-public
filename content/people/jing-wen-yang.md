## Role in the Community
- Founding pioneer of the Plains Region Formosan Autumn Convention (平原區台灣人秋令會): in 1983, then in Manhattan, Kansas, he and fellow 同鄉 范良正, 刁明華, and 黃金來 first conceived the convention; the idea was pressed forward by 徐福棟's active proposal and prepared/convened by 林啓東, and the convention was founded in 1985 at Rock Spring (a 4-H Club campsite near Manhattan, Kansas), covering the central-plains states (Colorado, Iowa, Kansas, Missouri, Oklahoma). The first annual meeting's theme, set by 林啓東, was 台灣人「大家來，來做堆」, deliberately separating the Taiwanese from the Chinese; a later convention was hosted with the Kansas City 同鄉會. See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys296|Our Journeys #296 — 平原區台灣人秋令會緣起]].
- Literary contributor to the TAH encyclopedia: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/148-e4-bf-97-e8-aa-9e-e7-ac-91-e8-a9-b1-e6-a5-8a-e6-99-af-e6-96-87-2000-11-e6-96|148.俗語笑話 (2000.11, 文學)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/114-e6-9c-9d-e6-b0-a3-e8-a9-a9-e6-ad-8c-e6-a5-8a-e6-99-af-e6-96-87-2002-02-e6-96|114. 朝氣詩歌 (2002.02, 文學)]], and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/113-e7-94-b1-e6-ad-b7-e5-8f-b2-e5-9c-b0-e7-90-86-e7-a4-be-e6-9c-83-e8-83-8c-e6-9|113. 由歷史地理社會背景看吳鳳 (2004.01, 傳記)]].
