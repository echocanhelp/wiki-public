## Role in the Community
Echo Lin (林錫湖) was one of the five founding members of **Formosans' Free Formosa (3F)**, organized in Philadelphia in 1956 — the first post-war Taiwanese independence organization in the US, reorganized in 1958 as the **United Formosans for Independence UFI (台獨聯盟)**. Corpus records:
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/198-e6-9e-97-e6-a6-ae-e5-8b-b3-e3-80-81-e6-9e-97-e9-8c-ab-e6-b9-96-e3-80-81-e9-9|198. 林榮勳、林錫湖、陳以德、楊東傑和盧主義 — The members of Formosans' Free Formosa (3F) / 1956-01-01]] — the founding-members record naming him.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys85-eng|85. 自由的號角—3F之起源 / The Call of Freedom – The Origin of 3F / Tsu-Yi Jay Loo]] — co-founder Loo's memoir: Echo was born into a prominent Tainan family (father Chuan-fu Lin, a well-known entrepreneur; the Chinese text names him 林全福 — see HOLD below), held an M.S. from the University of New Mexico, and began doctoral studies in Organic Chemistry at the University of Pennsylvania in 1955, not long before his girlfriend Dolores moved from New Mexico to Philadelphia. Co-founders: [[people/tom-yang|Tom Yang 楊東傑]], [[people/john-lin|John Lin 林榮勳]], Edward Chen, and [[people/tsu-yi-jay-loo|Tsu-Yi Jay Loo 盧主義]]. Per the same memoir, of the five founders all except John Lin were 台南一中 graduates and all except Echo were Christians.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys33|33. 一家人做伙來打拼 / 張炎憲 / 2014-09]] — historian Chang Yan-shian's account dating the 3F founding to 1956 in Philadelphia, 1958 reorganization into UFI.
- [[organizations/united-formosans-for-independenceufi|United Formosans for Independence UFI (台獨聯盟)]]
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-1894-echo-lin|1894. Echo Lin (Who's Who)]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whoswho1010|1010. Echo Lin 林錫湖 / 2016-05]] — his own encyclopedia entries.
