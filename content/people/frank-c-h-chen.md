# Frank C.H. Chen (陳治煌)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Frank C.H. Chen
- **Chinese:** 陳治煌
- **Era:** -
- **Geography:** 台灣
- **Core roles:** engineer
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Frank C.H. Chen (Conductor). Frank C.H. Chen holds a BS and MS degrees in Electrical Engineering from the National Taiwan University and the University of California at Santa Barbara, respectively. He served as conductor and music teacher of the National Taiwan University Chorus (NTUC) for three years while he was a student at the university. He also directed, conducted and produced the first NTUC public performance of the Opera “Yeomen of the Guard” by Gilbert & Sullivan. After moving to Maryland, Mr. Chen co-founded the Haitien Chorus and served as conductor for nine terms. Frank and his wife [[people/amanda-hsu||Amanda Hsu 許惠敏]] have been serving as NTUAC-DC Chorus conductors since 2012.
