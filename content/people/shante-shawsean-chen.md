# Shante Shaw Sean Chen (陳善哲)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Shante Shaw Sean Chen
- **Chinese:** 陳善哲
- **Era:** 1968
- **Geography:** Washington D.C.
- **Core roles:** artist
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who's Who

Industrial-design trained artist (Carnegie-Mellon B.S. 1990) working in Washington D.C.; comic-book penciller and illustrator for Valiant Comics and Marvel (Iron Man, Nova series; Marvel Creative Services Division). Documented in the TAH Foundation Who's Who and artist encyclopedia entries (#61, #870) — see [[sources/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org||taiwaneseamericanhistory.org]].

## From the record

Captured verbatim from the TAH Foundation archive artifacts on this box; full-field capture page: [[sources/tah-whoswho-shante-shawsean-chen-capture|TAH Who's Who profile capture — #870]].

- **Discovered and launched:** Chen started his comics career after being discovered by Barry Windsor-Smith and began at Valiant Comics penciling their flagship title X-O Manowar, plus Bloodshot, Harbinger, and Rai and the Future Force — per `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/artist61-shante-shawsean-chen.md`.
- **Debut book sales:** his debut book, Rai and the Future Force #9 (1992-era Valiant launch), sold over 900,000 copies per the same artist entry — the figure also appears in the self-reported `Accomplishment` field of `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/person/shante-shawsean-chen.json`.
- **Marvel tenure:** after Valiant he moved to Marvel Comics, where he drew Iron Man for over three years; other credits include Wolverine, Elektra, and the maxi-series X-Men: The End — per `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/artist61-shante-shawsean-chen.md`.
- **Licensing and design work:** at Marvel's Creative Services Division he contributes to style guides and licensing art, including box-cover illustrations for the Iron Man video game and promotional lunchboxes, T-shirts, and posters; outside comics he designs furniture and home renovations, specializing in kitchens and molded concrete countertops, and launched a line of designer toys and figurines — same artist entry.
- **Captain America mural:** the record attributes to him a giant 145-foot-long mural promoting Captain America: Civil War (2016) — stated in `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/artist61-shante-shawsean-chen.md` and repeated in the Notable Accomplishments block of `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/person/shante-shawsean-chen.json`.
