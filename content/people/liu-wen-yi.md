# 劉文義

Columnist and essayist writing on Taiwanese identity and community affairs.

## Story

劉文義 is a regular opinion columnist — for [[sources/taiwanjustice-net|台灣公義電子報]] as 劉文義 (byline mark ◎) from at least 2021, and among [[sources/taiwandaily-net|美洲台灣日報]]'s standing columnists by 2026, writing essays on identity, memory, and community life in the diaspora. Much of his reportage centers on the Taiwanese community around [[organizations/laguna-woods-village-taiwanese-club|Laguna Woods Village]] in Southern California.
