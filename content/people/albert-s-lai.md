# Dr. Albert S. Lai (賴信雄)

**Dr. Albert S. Lai (賴信雄, Lai Sin-hiong)** is a Taiwanese American pastor, theologian, and foundational interpreter of Taiwanese immigrant church formation in North America.

## Historical Context
Dr. Lai’s life-work sits at the junction of migration-era pastoral care and institution formation. In 1970 he participated in founding-era momentum around Formosan Presbyterian Church in Los Angeles (FPCLA), and in 1971 produced a dissertation that connected historical memory, migration pressure, identity tension, and communal mission.

## Contributions and Legacy
1. Documented an early Taiwanese American migration/church framework in Toward A Community of Hope — A Mission to Formosan Community in Los Angeles. — Source: Toward A Community of Hope — A Mission to Formosan Community in Los Angeles (A)
