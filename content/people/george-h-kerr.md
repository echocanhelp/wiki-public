## Role in the Community
- The TAH story corpus holds an encyclopedia entry for him: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/george-h-kerr|TAH #912, 2016-03]].
- Community memorialization is documented in the corpus: "30. George H. Kerr Memorial Foundation 美國柯喬治紀念基金會": [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/george-h-kerr-memorial-foundation|TAH #30]]. HOLD: naming conflict 葛超智 (TAH Who's Who page) vs 柯喬治 (memorial-foundation record) — two romanization-era Chinese names for Kerr, not auto-merged.
- Mentioned in the TJJ column on KMT party assets (2016-08-03): [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20210228235931_2016_08_03_國民黨不告訴你的歷史-不當黨產背後是無數個台_fb35181c1f66975b|TJJ column, 2016-08-03]].
- 盧主義 (Jay Loo) memoir: as a student he first encountered Taiwan scholarship through Kerr's two autumn-1947 Far Eastern Survey articles — one on the origins and course of the 2/28 incident, the second ('The March Massacre') describing KMT troops killing close to 30,000 Taiwanese including urban social elites; the memoir credits Kerr's analysis of the KMT regime's corruption as having a profound politically-awakening effect on him ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys85|TAH Our Journeys #85]]).
- Same memoir: after UFI was founded, 盧主義 began corresponding with prominent US politicians and scholars, including George Kerr then at the Hoover Institution in California ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys126|TAH Our Journeys #126]]).
- 林衡哲's shareholder-letter memoir (TAH Our Journeys #70, 各位敬愛的股東同仁平安): reading Kerr's《被出賣的台灣》— alongside 彭敏明's exile memoir「Taste of Freedom」, 楊逵's「送報夫」and 史明's「台灣四百年史」— is credited as part of the reading that brought about his 「台灣文化意識終於全面覺醒」in the early 1970s ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys70|TAH Our Journeys #70 / 林衡哲]]).
- Kerr's《被出賣的台灣》(Formosa Betrayed) translation campaign: in 1967 the 全美台灣獨立聯盟 decided to translate the book, with 陳榮成 as overall lead; 陳希寬 and 郁彬 translated chapter 12 ('The February Incident, 1947'), published under the name of 陳希寬's daughter 陳南穎; the Chinese translation was formally published in 1973 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys33|TAH Our Journeys #33, 張炎憲, 2014-09]]).
- The book's title lived on in community cultural memory: Will Tiao 刁毓能's 2009 political-thriller feature — the first movie produced by a Taiwanese American, titled《被出賣的臺灣》/ "Formosa Betrayed" (plot drawn from the 江南案, 陳文成命案, 美麗島事件 and 林宅血案 rather than Kerr's 1945-49 narrative) — was financed by touring US Taiwanese investors at US$25,000 minimum each, raising over NT$300 million; box office fell short and Tiao recorded feeling he had let the backing 同鄉 down ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/40-will-tiao-e5-88-81-e6-af-93-e8-83-bd-the-first-movie-producer-formosa-betraye|TAH #40, Will Tiao / 2009]]; same recollection in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys279|TAH Our Journeys #279]]).
- The corpus holds a library record for the book itself: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1094|TAH #1094, Formosa Betrayed / George H. Kerr / 1992]]. HOLD: Kerr's era on this page lists 1911-1992; the TAH #1094 record's 1992 date is the publication record's era field, not a sourced death date — not merged.
