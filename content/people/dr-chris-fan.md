## Role in the Community
- Recruited onto the Taiwanese American Foundation of San Diego board on the recommendation of Dr. 陳秋山 and elevated to chairman; under him the Foundation professionalized, first proposing a salaried full-time center director at the Taiwan Center (the prior director 潘富鵬 had served unpaid and part-time while handling daily operations) [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys26|Our Journeys 26]].
- His tenure saw the Taiwan Center transform and draw mainstream attention, with directors 賴淑卿 and 鄭德昌 (an experienced co-founding couple) joining the board and making it more dynamic [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys26|Our Journeys 26]].
- Profiled by 楊遠薰 (2014/09) as a biotech entrepreneur in 「黃金白兔─生物科技創業家范清亮等人的故事」 [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/16-e9-bb-83-e9-87-91-e7-99-bd-e5-85-94-e2-94-80-e7-94-9f-e7-89-a9-e7-a7-91-e6-8a|黃金白兔]].
- Community profiles: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/32-dr-ching-liang-chris-fan|TAH record 32]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-880-chris-fan|TAH Who's Who 880]].
