## Role in the Community

- **NATMA 總會長 (2021):** the corpus press record [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20251110223518_大紐約地區31個僑團挺台參與who_台灣駐紐約辦事處_4c312d1526674bdf|大紐約地區31個僑團挺台參與WHO]] (Taiwan Justice, 2021-05-21) reports him speaking as **北美洲台灣人醫師協會總會長** (NATMA national president, not merely a chapter member) at an online forum hosted by 僑務委員莊振輝 on WHO participation, alongside 紐約台灣會館理事長蘇春槐 and 紐約台灣商會會長江俊霖. Community role: leader of the largest Taiwanese-American medical organization's campaign for Taiwan's WHO participation during the worst phase of the pandemic.
- The TAH profile ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-1904-pofu-hsieh|TAH encyclopedia #1904]]) lists only NATMA New York Chapter membership; the 2021 corpus record supersedes it as a later career stage (member → 總會長), not a conflicting fact.

HOLD: he is documented as a dentist (NYU D.D.S., NYU dental faculty) serving as president of the Taiwanese *Medical* Association — the corpus does not explain the dental/medical distinction; recorded as-is, no reinterpretation.
