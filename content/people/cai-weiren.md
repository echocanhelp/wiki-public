# Rev. Cai Weiren (蔡維仁)

**蔡維仁牧師** wrote the GSTPC40 essay「數算恩典邁步前行」as **傳道及第二任牧師 (2006–2016)**. He also served GSTPC as youth counselor / deacon **1981–1986**. Wife **純雅** led eight STM Taiwan bilingual camps from 2008 (GSTPC40).

## Identity Snapshot
- **Chinese:** 蔡維仁
- **Wife:** 純雅 (GSTPC40)
- **GSTPC:** deacon 1981–86; preacher/pastor 2004–2016; **second settled pastor 2006–2016**

## Timeline
- **1981–1986** — GSTPC 青、少年輔導 / 執事
- **2003** — Returned to SoCal; 正道福音神學院 (GSTPC40)
- **2004–2016** — 神學生、傳道、牧師 at GSTPC
- **2006–2016** — 第二任牧師
- **2008–** — eight 台灣短宣 seasons (純雅領受異象; GSTPC40)
