# Su Shih-ying (蘇拾瑩)

## Story

蘇拾瑩 is a Taiwan-born journalist and author who has lived in Sydney for decades and writes columns, under the bylines 「作家、資深媒體人、現旅居雪梨」 and 「澳洲台僑」, about Taiwan, the Australian Chinese community, and Beijing's influence operations abroad. She came from a newsroom career in Taipei — reporter at 工商時報、中國時報、聯合報, then 總編輯 and 副總編輯 roles — and after emigrating to Sydney in 1993 she became publisher of the Australian Chinese-language daily 華聲日報 and the Sydney-based correspondent for 中國時報 and 新新聞週刊.

She is also a published author of心灵/faith books (《一念之間》、《愛的信念》、《一億元的婚姻》、《窮鬼翻身》、《抉擇的智慧》) and, later, a lay preacher with a Master of Divinity from Morling College in Sydney. In her columns she repeatedly positions herself within the overseas Taiwanese community (台僑): she recounts attending 雙十國慶 with 台灣鄉親 for thirty years, and she has been a voice against 紅色滲透 in the Sydney 僑界.
