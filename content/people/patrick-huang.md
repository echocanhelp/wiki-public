## Role in the Community
- Recorded in 鄭家隆's institutional memoir of the New York Taiwan House (紐約台灣會館) as one of four volunteers — with 陳隆豐, 樊豐美, 賴弘典 — who kept fundraising for the House when it faced closure, and whose report to the incoming director helped launch its 1992 turnaround ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys27|27. 紐約台灣會館：在困境中更新 / 鄭家隆 / 2014-09]], value band A).
- Author in the community record: his own memoir essay 摩托車萬里行雜記 is held as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories48|48. Motorcycle Trip 摩托車萬里行雜記 / 黃再添 / 2014-09]] (band B bibliographic record) — companion to the TAH #52 record of the first cross-U.S.A. motorcycle ride with his son.
- Active in the 台灣筆會 community: the pen-association's longtime chronicler 柯哲洋 (Kin Ko) recalls 黃再添 emailing congratulations after a successful annual conference ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys12|Our Journeys #12 筆會 memoir]]).
- He is also the author of the Taiwan House institutional memoir 台灣會館建館委員會的誕生, an English account of how the building committee that secured the New York Taiwan Center came into being ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys9-eng|9. The Birth of the Taiwan Center Building Committee / Patrick Huang]], value band A; Chinese original [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys09|9. 第一聲號角:台灣會館建館委員會的誕生 / 黃再添 / 2014-09]]). The record preserves the near-miss detail: the committee had negotiated a price on the Bowne Street Veterans' Club in Flushing, but the seller backed out the day before signing after a New York Times piece predicted a Flushing property-price surge; the Northern Boulevard property, formerly a nightclub, became the community's home instead (corroborated in press record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys304|Our Journeys #304]], citing 陳隆豐's recollection).
- Blacklisted movement figure: Christina Hu's documentary Blacklist profiles him with 賴弘典 (Hong-Tien Lai) and Mark Kao as three Taiwanese American leaders who arrived as graduate students, were exposed to forbidden political writings, founded Taiwanese student organizations, and were barred from returning to Taiwan for almost two decades — denied even weddings and parental funerals ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/blacklistfilm|Blacklist (Christina Hu)]]).
- His personal art collection is itself a TAH corpus record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/collection-of-mr-patrick-huang|38. Collection of Mr. Patrick Huang 黃再添先生的收藏]]; the collection's fundraising acknowledgments list him under 台灣研究社 alongside 吳作樂, in a donor roll spanning hometown associations nationwide.
- Couple profile in the community record: 楊遠薰's [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories267|267. 堅守理念的人生 — 紐約黃再添與楊淑卿的故事 / 2014-12]] — matching the family page's wife [[people/s-c-yang|楊淑卿]]. Within it, the 1988-06 tenth 台灣人年會 (Converse College, SC) is recorded with 黃再添 present as a representative of overseas Taiwanese revolutionary bodies alongside 史明 and 張金策.
- 2017, age-60s civic record: his own signed 聲明 for joining the 救扁 hunger strike ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories569|569. 紐約台僑黃再添參加救扁絕食聲明 / 黃再添 / 2017-08]]) and a profile by his son 黃兆平 of his 藝站 art-station path ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories564|564. 紐約爸爸 黃再添藝站之路無怨無悔 / 2017-08]]).
- 鄭家隆's memoir exists in an English edition too, naming the four fundraising volunteers — Lung-Fong Chen, Fon-may Fan, Patrick Huang, Hong Tien Lai — who reported to the incoming director before the 1992 turnaround ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys27-eng|Our Journeys #27 ENG]]).
- His own TAH encyclopedia entry is held in the corpus as a work ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-884-patrick-huang|TAH #884 encyclopedia]]; bibliographic record only), alongside the record of the 2007 cross-U.S.A. motorcycle ride with his son ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ff52|TAH #52]]).
