# Felix Fan (范雅志)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Felix Fan
- **Chinese:** 范雅志
- **Era:** 1976
- **Geography:** Boston
- **Core roles:** entrepreneur, artist
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Felix Fan is a celebrated young cellist whose eclectic music-making and leadership reflect a 21st Century sensibility rare among musicians. He is a musical explorer whose journey takes him from a performance of Tan Dun’s music one day to a Devorak Concerto the next.

## Role in the Community
- Performed at TUF (台灣人聯合基金會)「台灣名家演奏系列」as the series' cellist, alongside 林昭亮、陳慕融、蘇顯達、陳泰成、陳毓襄、葉綠娜、楊文信、陳建安 etc. — the series, started 1992 with 吳澧培's sponsorship, opened with 胡乃元's violin recital. See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys294|TUF 藝文系列 record]].
- 1990 — at 南加州《台灣文化之夜》(世紀廣場大飯店, ~700 同鄉出席), the presidential-award-winning 范雅志 performed 聖桑《天鵝》and the first movement of 蕭泰然's new cello concerto with the composer himself accompanying — the piece's unofficial first performance ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys301|301. 南加州TUF的誔生、回顧與展望 / 林衡哲]], 2017).
- 1995 — gave the world premiere of 蕭泰然's full cello concerto, performed with the San Diego Symphony under TUF's auspices, pushing Taiwanese music onto the international stage (same memoir, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys301|ourjourneys301]]).
- Held two TAH Foundation story-corpus records: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/50-felix-fan-e8-8c-83-e9-9b-85-e5-bf-97-cellist|TAH #50, Cellist profile (2014)]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/291-felix-fan-e8-8c-83-e9-9b-85-e5-bf-97201502|TAH #291 (2015)]].
