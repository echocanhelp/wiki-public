# 潘意玲 (Irene Fang)

## Identity Snapshot
- **Era:** Contemporary (active 2024–2026 in LA records)
- **Geography:** Los Angeles Chinatown / SGV, Chinese-language community
- **Core roles:** community organizer (chinese american) — president, [[organizations/ai-education-foundation|AI Education Foundation]]; ROC 僑務委員 (Overseas Affairs Commissioner)

## Record

- **Digital-literacy movement for the overseas Chinese community:** founded and runs hands-on **AI/smartphone courses** (AI手機功能教學課程 — AI search, voice assistants, LINE, Google Maps, anti-fraud digital safety), first cohort **Feb 12, 2025**, community iterations **Feb 12, 2026**; deliberately **in-person** — "科技價值在於讓人更有力量，而非拉開距離"; stated mission: no one left behind by technological progress (縮短數位落差).
- Co-teaches with instructor 謝逸琦; partners with **洛杉磯華僑文教服務中心 (OCAC LA)** — courses held at the Chinatown library reading room (Jun 2025).
- **Stated worldview:** 僑務 work's core is "連結與傳承"; digital literacy is how the overseas Chinese community stays connected to the world; frames AI education as showing **"台灣的軟實力"** — Taiwan soft power.
- **Health-diplomacy convening:** SoCal convener-in-chief for the **Taichung VGH (臺中榮總) US visit & health lectures** (LA + OC sessions; co-organizers included 南加州台大校友會, 大洛杉磯台灣客家會, 榮光會, Foguang Society 新港分會 — a cross-section of LA's Taiwan-linked civil society).
- **Taste of Taiwan 2026:** as AI Education Foundation president introduced the Oct 2–11 Rowland Heights event; balloon-blessing "為**台灣及台美友誼**祈福" (World Journal, 2026-09-26).
