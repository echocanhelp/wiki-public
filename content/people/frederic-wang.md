## Role in the Community

Corpus-linked (absorbed 2026-09-18), outranking the press-kit bio:

- 1969 — **President of the Taiwanese Association of America, Greater Washington chapter** in its founding year class, per 陳淮崇's founding memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys-138|138. 華府與巴城TAA的誕生]] (2015-05-27, band A).
- Named a 創會先鋒獎 (Founding Pioneer Award) recipient: the memoir's group photo caption lists him among the pioneer presidents (1967 陳淮崇, 1969 王穎裕, 1971 賴經都, 1972 張學賢, 1978 陳文源, 1974 江應鐘) alongside current 華府/巴城 leadership.
- His own TAH encyclopedia entry is held in the corpus as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whoswho1379|1379. Frederic Wang 王穎裕]] (2016-11-06).

Note: the press-kit Snapshot says "Era: 1932" but lists a 1960 Ph.D.; no corpus record fixes a birth year, so the era stays as recorded.
