All rows below are third-party (not the TAH self-report); excerpts archived at `knowledge/web-archives/rev-j-s-chou-thirdparty-20261117/`. Key: 鄧慧恩〈許多「第一」的周再賜〉《新使者雜誌》123 期 (2011-04-10, pp.50–54); 蔡蟳〈同志社首位台藉學生周再賜〉; 《台灣教會公報》2301 期 (1996-04-07)〈史話353 傳道周步霞的子女〉; 大嶋果織 (Oshima Kaori) 共愛学園前橋国際大学論集 series.

- **1888-07-06** — Born in 阿猴 (modern Pingtung city), per 鄧慧恩《新使者雜誌》123期 (2011-04-10) https://newmsgr.pct.org.tw/Magazine.aspx?strISID=123&strMagID=M2011042102809&strTID=1 — CONFLICT: 蔡蟳 (蔡蟳按語, laijohn.com) prints 1888.8.13; both kept as dated snapshots, see GAPS. Source: 鄧慧恩
- **1846–1923 (father)** — Father 周步霞 (號耀彩), 長老教會 preacher baptized 1869-09-12 by Rev. Hugh Litchie at 府城亭仔腳禮拜堂; one of the earliest Taiwanese preachers. Source: 《台灣教會公報》2301期 1996-04-07 史話353 per http://www.laijohn.com/book4/353.htm and 鄧慧恩
- **Sibling set** — 周步霞 and wife 蔡銀 had 5 sons / 6 daughters; three sons (福全、錫英、再賜) and three daughters (慈愛、慈玉、慈好) alive at 步霞's death. The TAH form's blank Daughter/Son fields on THIS page are his own children, not siblings. Source: per http://www.laijohn.com/book4/353.htm
- **Brother 周福全** — eldest brother went to Japan 1895-12 (Meiji Academy 明治學院), often cited as the first Taiwanese to study in Japan; returned 1897, graduated 總督府醫學校 1904, became 嘉義's first Western-trained physician. Source: 鄧慧恩, corroborated per http://www.laijohn.com/archives/pc/Chiu/Chiu,Csu/brief/Chhoa,Sun.htm
- **1899** — Family moved to 台北大稻埕 (father's ministry); he entered 大稻埕公學校 四年級; teacher 小竹德吉 (also teacher of 杜聰明 and 施乾) encouraged him to sit the 國語學校 exam. Source: 鄧慧恩《新使者雜誌》123期, https://newmsgr.pct.org.tw/Magazine.aspx?strISID=123&strMagID=M2011042102809&strTID=1
- **1900–1904** — 總督府國語學校: admitted 1900, graduated 1904 in 2nd place and received the class diploma on stage for the 國語部 graduates; after graduation worked at the 樟腦專賣局 earning 15 圓/month on a self-imposed 5-圓 budget while deciding on Japan. Source: 鄧慧恩《新使者雜誌》123期, https://newmsgr.pct.org.tw/Magazine.aspx?strISID=123&strMagID=M2011042102809&strTID=1
- **1905-04** — Entered 京都同志社普通學校 (secondary) as the FIRST Taiwanese student at Doshisha (chose it over 明治學院 after a teacher's introduction letter; chose via a lottery per 蔡蟳). Source: 鄧慧恩; entry month + first-student claim per http://www.laijohn.com/archives/pc/Chiu/Chiu,Csu/brief/Chhoa,Sun.htm
- **1909-03** — Graduated 同志社普通舊制中學校, entered the theology track; classmate/senior 林茂生 (1887–1947) sat in 普通學校 四年級 in 1908 as a fellow Taiwanese preacher's son. Source: 鄧慧恩《新使者雜誌》123期
- **1910–1915** — 同志社大學神學部, graduated 1915 — third-party corroboration of the TAH `1910-1915 B.S.` row; 阪口直樹 (Kyoto 2000) lists him FIRST in the 同志社 Alumni-Directory Taiwanese list (普通學校 1909) and records him graduating 大學神学部 in 1915. Source: 阪口直樹〈戦前の同志社と台湾留学生〉3(2) 267-292 (2000-12-31) http://doi.org/10.14988/pa.2017.0000004346
- **1915 (post-graduation)** — Denied a Japan pulpit appointment on ethnic-discrimination grounds (the only graduate in his class without one) and left that summer for the U.S. Source: 鄧慧恩《新使者雜誌》123期; same account per 蔡蟳 http://www.laijohn.com/archives/pc/Chiu/Chiu,Csu/brief/Chhoa,Sun.htm
- **1919** — Union Theological Seminary (紐約協和神學院), 專修宗教哲學 — a 4th U.S./Japan study row the TAH form omits entirely (it lists only Doshisha/Oberlin/Chicago). Source: 《台灣教會公報》史話353 (1996-04-07) per http://www.laijohn.com/book4/353.htm, corroborated by 蔡蟳 (Union神學校)
- **1921-03** — Returned to Japan and was appointed 助教授 (assistant professor) at 同志社大學 — 鄧慧恩 and 《台灣教會公報》 say 文學部; 蔡蟳 says 大學神學部; courses taught: 宗教心理學、基督教社會學、英語 plus student-affairs duties. Rank ladder corroborates the TAH `1921- Assistant Professor of Theology` row. Source: 鄧慧恩 https://newmsgr.pct.org.tw/Magazine.aspx?strISID=123&strMagID=M2011042102809&strTID=1; department conflict named in GAPS
- **1922** — Applied for and received 按手礼 (ordination examination) through the 日本組合教會教師試驗委員會, the recorded first Taiwanese to obtain pastor qualification in Japan — corroborates the TAH `1923 Pastor` row at 1922; 《台灣教會公報》/蔡蟳 both date the qualification 1923. Dated snapshots, not harmonized. Source: 鄧慧恩《新使者雜誌》123期
- **1925 (resignation)** — Resigned Doshisha after clashing with 総長 海老名弾正 over mass expansion vs quality (he circulated campus leaflets criticizing the president per 蔡蟳; 鄧慧恩 frames it as a 精緻教學 dispute). Source: 鄧慧恩; leaflet episode per http://www.laijohn.com/archives/pc/Chiu/Chiu,Csu/brief/Chhoa,Sun.htm
- **1925-09 → 1965-12** — 9th principal (第9任校長) of 群馬県前橋市 共愛女學校 (共愛學園), from 1925-09 until retirement 1965-12 — pins month ends the TAH row left open-ended on the start; called the school's 「中興之祖」. Source: 《台灣教會公報》史話353 per http://www.laijohn.com/book4/353.htm; 第9任 per 蔡蟳; see also 大嶋果織 (2017) https://kyoai.repo.nii.ac.jp/records/172
- **School origin context** — 前橋共愛女學校 was founded 1888 by 熊本洋學校/同志社 alumni (the 熊本團) as 前橋英和女學校 (共愛社), Gunma's oldest women's school, with 新島襄-lineage ties to Doshisha; principals were Doshisha-connected, which is how the post reached him. Source: 蔡蟳 per http://www.laijohn.com/archives/pc/Chiu/Chiu,Csu/brief/Chhoa,Sun.htm
- **KYOAI building/settlement era** — Unified 校友會/互助會, founded 《共愛時報》, built 共愛館 and 共勵館 within a decade, and cleared pre-existing + construction debt 20 years into his tenure. Source: 鄧慧恩《新使者雜誌》123期
- **1931-02-09** — Sunday sermon 「人格的發現」 (John 8:1–11) arguing Jesus as 婦女解放的先驅者 — his signature women's-dignity theology, from the 50th-anniversary-era writings. Source: 鄧慧恩 https://newmsgr.pct.org.tw/Magazine.aspx?strISID=123&strMagID=M2011042102809&strTID=1
- **Publication (fills the TAH `Publication --` blank)** — Four book-length works attested by 大嶋果織: 《基督教要説》(警醒社, 1941), 《信仰の真実を求めて》(警醒社, 1942, 52 sermons), 《焦土に嘆く》(共愛文庫, 1946), 《日訓》(共愛同信教會, 1957). Source: 大嶋果織〈周再賜の信仰と神学（2）〉per https://kyoai.repo.nii.ac.jp/records/268 and Gunma Institutes abstract; (1) at https://kyoai.repo.nii.ac.jp/records/172 (論集 17, 91-104, 2017-03-31, DOI 10.34443/00000166, ISSN 2187-333X, NCID AA11548903)
- **1945-08-05** — 前橋大空襲 burned the whole KYOAI campus except two buildings; eyewitness accounts describe him collapsing watching the fire. He rebuilt it postwar as 共愛學園 with a junior/e senior high added. Source: 鄧慧恩《新使者雜誌》123期
- **1954-01** — Naturalized Japanese, together with wife 勝見千代 (marriage corroborated). Source: 《台灣教會公報》史話353 (1996-04-07) per http://www.laijohn.com/book4/353.htm
- **Childless** — He and 勝見千代 had no children (the blank TAH Daughter/Son fields are an unfilled form AND absence per two independent ZH sources); he called his students his 「共愛子」. Alumni later formed 「賜千會」 (his 賜 + her 千) to honor him; the society published 清水安三《周再賜先生の生涯》(群馬: 賜千會, 昭和51/1976). Source: 鄧慧恩; 賜千會 per http://www.laijohn.com/archives/pc/Chiu/Chiu,Csu/brief/Chhoa,Sun.htm
- **Doctorate** — 同志社 alumni rosters style him 文学博士 (Ph.D. in Letters) — a degree the TAH form never carries. Source: 《同志社と台湾》150周年記念冊 alumni list per https://mypaper.pchome.com.tw/carawayseed711/post/1382327260 (secondary; degree-award date still a NEED)
- **1969-12-02 (death place unresolved)** — Died — pins the month/day the TAH footer `Passed Away 1969` left bare. Source: 蔡蟳 按語 (1888.8.13-1969.12.2) per http://www.laijohn.com/archives/pc/Chiu/Chiu,Csu/brief/Chhoa,Sun.htm
- **Postwar rebuild** — Rebuilt the burnt campus, renamed it 共愛學園, added 中學 and 高校; left the principalship in 1965-12 during a 嬰兒潮-era board split over mass expansion vs an elite policy — the same quality-vs-scale fight he had lost at Doshisha. Source: 鄧慧恩《新使者雜誌》123期; board-split account per 蔡蟳 http://www.laijohn.com/archives/pc/Chiu/Chiu,Csu/brief/Chhoa,Sun.htm
- **Biographical literature (books about him, third-party)** — 管井吉郎《周再賜》(高崎: 群馬教壇社, 1947); 清水安三《周再賜先生の生涯》(賜千會, 1976); 《日本基督教学校教育史: 人物篇》(創文社, 1977); 阪口直樹《戦前同志社と台湾留学生》(白帝社, 2002). Source: 延伸閱讀 list, 鄧慧恩《新使者雜誌》123期
- **Scholarly record (research about him)** — 大嶋果織, 周再賜の信仰と神学 (1) 2017 論集 17:91-104 (DOI 10.34443/00000166), (2) wartime writings, (3) postwar writings 論集 23:169-181 (2023-03-31, DOI 10.34443/00000257) — he is an active research subject in Japanese liberal-theology/social-gospel scholarship. Source: https://kyoai.repo.nii.ac.jp/records/172 , https://kyoai.repo.nii.ac.jp/records/268
