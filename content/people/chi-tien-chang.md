## Role in the Community
From his own oral memoir, 張啟典醫師的回憶 — 1977年TAC/EC (楊遠薰, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys338|Our Journeys #338]]):
- Grew up in Wanhua/Monga, Taipei in a grass-root family; barred from teaching jobs in Taiwan for having no connections and refusing to join the KMT — this shaped his quiet support of the Taiwanese people's movement in the US.
- 1969 (3rd year of study in Boston): founded the [[organizations/boston-taiwanese-christian-church|波士頓台灣基督教會]], having grown up in the elder-elder church (長老教會) in Monga.
- 1970: traveled to Downington, PA for the 1st U.S. East Coast Christian Taigi Summer Assembly (美東基督徒台語夏令會) initiated by [[people/chiung-hsiung-ou|歐炯雄]], becoming a charter member of what became the 美東台灣人夏令會 (U.S. East Coast Taiwanese Summer Assembly).
- 1977: president of the Boston Taiwanese Association (波士頓台灣同鄉會) and convener of the 8th 美東台灣人夏令會 at Brown University — the first held on a U.S. university campus, ~400 attendees. Speakers included pastors [[people/heng-liong-lin|林興隆]] and 郭榮敏, plus 陳錦芳, 洪哲勝 and 李豐明; 張金策 reported on Taiwan's human-rights situation. Chang personally screened out a suspected KMT agent who tried to register twice.
- 2009: attended the 40th 美東台灣人夏令會 (Caribbean cruise), reflecting on the end of racial segregation and the black-list era.
- Also profiled in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whoswho1576|TAH #1576 Chi-Tien Chang 張啟典]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/178-prof-c-d-chang|TAH #178 Prof. C. D. Chang]].

## Timeline
- 1963 — B.S., National Taiwan University (chemistry); emigrated to the U.S. in 1966 (Greyhound coach LA→Boston).
- 1969 — founded the Boston Taiwanese Christian Church.
- 1970 — charter member, 美東台灣人夏令會 (1st summer assembly, Downington PA).
- 1973 — Ph.D. in organic chemistry, Northeastern University (Boston).
- 1977 — Boston Taiwanese Association president; convened the 8th 美東台灣人夏令會 at Brown University.
- 1980–1984 — Boston University medical school; obtained Massachusetts medical license; then pathology research and teaching until retirement.
