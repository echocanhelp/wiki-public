## Role in the Community

Corpus-linked (absorbed 2026-09-18), from 曾啟明's Minnesota chronicle [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys123|123. 明州台美人百年大事]] (2015-03, band A; English version [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys123-eng|here]]):

- 1985 — President of the Minnesota Taiwanese club (同鄉會會長) during his 3M St. Paul years; that year the club hosted writers/poets 巫永福, 李魁賢, 林享泰 and authors 楊青矗, 李喬 on visits.
- 1995 — Elected DPP 不分區立委 (per the same chronicle). Consistent with the Employment table's 3M tenure ending 1991 and 主委/教授 roles from 1991.
- He is also a corpus author: essays 「福爾摩沙的自我追尋」 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1076|1076]], 2016-12), 「美霞」 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories556|556. 美霞]], 2017-07), and 「花轎記」 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys322|322. 花轎記]], 2017-11).
- His TAH encyclopedia entry is held in the corpus as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whoswho1440|1440. Chin-Hsin Jason Liu 劉進興]] (2016-12).
