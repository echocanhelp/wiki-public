## Role in the Community
- Co-author (with 鄭憲章 and 李陳幸子) of the memoir **"第一個台灣同鄉會的會館：辛城台灣同鄉會"** — the record of how the Cincinnati Taiwanese Association acquired the first dedicated hometown-association hall (會館) of the overseas Taiwanese community; corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys293|293. 第一個台灣同鄉會的會館：辛城台灣同鄉會 / 鄭憲章、李陳幸子、魏妙圭 / 2017-06]] (English: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys293-eng|ourjourneys293-eng]], original in 台灣公論報 #912, 1990-11-15). Related org: [[organizations/taiwanese-association-of-cincinnati|Taiwanese Association of Cincinnati]].
- From the memoir text inside record 293 itself: the hall's location was provided by 「鄭信傳醫師、魏妙圭同鄉夫婦，很慷慨地為同鄉會提供會館場所，免繳租金」 — the couple donated the physical 會館 space rent-free, the decisive factor 鄭憲章 credits in his speech (「信傳，妙圭，眞多謝！」). 魏妙圭 also authored her own section of the memoir (「會館的成立是所有辛城同鄉的驕傲，是所有同鄉共同努力的成果呀！」) and is credited as photographer of the hall photo 「辛城台灣同鄉會前門 2017 (魏妙圭 攝 08/2017)」. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys293|Our Journeys 293 memoir text]].
- The couple's story was profiled in the 台美人物誌 interview series: 「很古典的姻緣 很現代的夫妻」— 鄭信傳、魏妙圭夫婦專訪 (台灣日報), corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/videos40|40. 台美人物誌專訪 — 鄭信傳魏妙圭夫婦]].
