## Role in the Community

TaiwaneseAmerican.org covered Stephanie Chuang twice as an early-career Taiwanese American journalist; the corpus records her community service alongside her broadcast career.

- 2008 — Featured in [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/behind-the-scenes-with-stephanie-chuang|Behind the Scenes with Stephanie Chuang]] shortly after graduating from Northwestern University, then a reporter and weekend anchor at KSBW Action News 8 (NBC affiliate, California Central Coast).
- 2011 — Follow-up interview and video profile, [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/stephanie-chuang-on-life-in-broadcast-journalism|Stephanie Chuang on Life in Broadcast Journalism]]: by then a morning reporter for KPIX (CBS) in the SF/Bay Area.
- Community service recorded in the 2011 profile: MC of the Taiwanese American Cultural Festival in San Francisco, speaker at the West Coast Intercollegiate Taiwanese American Students Conference (cf. [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/itasa-west-coast-and-midwest-conferences-2009|ITASA West Coast and Midwest Conferences 2009]]), and volunteer work interviewing candidates for an Asian American Journalists Association program.
- Named in [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/stephanie-lin-champions-womens-empowerment|Stephanie Lin Champions Women's Empowerment]] as one of the Taiwanese American mentors (with Kevin Lin and Allen Lu) offered through Stephanie Lin's Miss Asian America campaign.
- TAH Who's Who entry: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whowho1395|1395. Stephanie Chuang / 2016/11]].
