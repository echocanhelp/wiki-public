- 1994 — B.S. in **Animal Science and Technology**, National Taiwan University (exact major title
  and year, absent from the TAH form), per official site https://directory.uthscsa.edu/academics/profile/changt3
- Pre-graduate service — student intern / part-time research assistant in the **animal breeding and
  embryology lab at National Taiwan University**; first sight of cleaving mammalian embryos he
  describes as "one of the most fascinating moments in my life", per `official site`
  https://uthscsa.edu/biomedical-sciences/student-life/ocd/career-advisory-council/members/t-arthur-chang (`gsbs.html`, first-person Career Path)

  UW–Madison, per `official site` self-account `gsbs.html` (self-reported, but published by the employer)
- 2001 — M.S. Endocrinology & Reproductive Physiology, University of Wisconsin–Madison, per `official site` `gsbs.html`
- 2007 — Ph.D. Endocrinology and Reproductive Physiology, University of Wisconsin–Madison, per `official site` `directory.uthscsa.edu`
- 2007 — joins UT Health Science Center San Antonio as **Director, Embryology & Stem Cell Research
  Laboratory**, per `official site` appointments list `directory.html`
- 2009 — co-author (as **Tien-Cheng Chang**) of the review *Trophoblast Stem Cells: Models for
  Investigating Trophectoderm Differentiation and Placental Development*, **Endocrine Reviews 30(3):
  228–240**, listed under *Departments of Comparative Biosciences … and the Wisconsin National
  Primate Research Center, University of Wisconsin–Madison* — i.e. his UW training affiliation,
  documented https://academic.oup.com/edrv/article/30/3/228/2355001 and PMC2726840; also cited in
  `knowledge/web-archives/tiencheng-arthur-chang-thirdparty-2026-11-17/cv2026.txt`
- 2009 — abstract *Effects of in vitro culture techniques on gonadotropin-stimulated ICSI-derived
  baboon pre-implantation embryo development*, **Biology of Reproduction 81:615** (Chang TC, Eddy CA,
  Ying Y et al.), per the reference list of the 2011 Fertility & Sterility paper
  `fertstert-paywall-shell.html` (403 shell — quoted from the search-served full text; Wayback NEED)
- 2010-11-04 / 2011-06 — first author (as **Tien-Cheng Chang**), *Derivation and Characterization of
  Novel Nonhuman Primate Embryonic Stem Cell Lines from In Vitro-Fertilized Baboon Preimplantation
  Embryos*, **Stem Cells and Development 20(6):1053–1062**, DOI 10.1089/scd.2010.0372 — 3 baboon
  (`Papio cynocephalus`) ESC lines UT-1/UT-2 (male) and UT-3 (female); documented
  `crossref-10.1089-scd.2010.0372.json`, https://scholars.uthscsa.edu/en/publications/derivation-and-characterization-of-novel-nonhuman-primate-embryon/
- 2010-08-11 online / 2011-03-15 print — first author, *Ovarian stimulation, in vitro fertilization,
  and effects of culture conditions on baboon preimplantation embryo development*, **Fertility and
  Sterility 95(4):1217–1223**, DOI 10.1016/j.fertnstert.2010.06.095 — 1,924 oocytes from 49 cycles,
  63.5% ICSI fertilization, best blastocyst rate 37.7% with Sydney IVF + BRL feeder cells; supported
  by the **AT&T Foundation**; documented `crossref-10.1016-j.fertnstert.2010.06.095.json`
- 2011 — chapter *Assisted Reproductive Technology in Nonhuman Primates* (with Anthony W. S. Chan),
  in *Vertebrate Embryogenesis*, **Methods in Molecular Biology 770**, Humana Press. The publisher
  issued an **erratum (pp. E1–E2, DOI 10.1007/978-1-61779-210-6_25)** because chapter 13 was printed
