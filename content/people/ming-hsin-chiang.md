## Role in the Community

- 1977– — President of Yu Shan Company USA, Ltd.; 1989– — Founder & President, Casual Home Worldwide Inc. (TAH Who's Who record, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-2017-ming-hsin-chiang|2017. Ming Hsin Chiang 江明信]]).
- President of the Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce of New York (紐約台灣商會會長); by 2021 also a director of the World Federation of Taiwanese Chambers of Commerce (世界台灣商會聯合總會理事) — roles recorded in his own TAH story [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-797|797. HELLO TAIWAN行動 紐約台僑江明信牽成愛台善循環 (07/2021)]].
- Director, American Red Cross Greater New York Region — Long Island Chapter.
- 2021 — HELLO TAIWAN campaign: printed HELLO TAIWAN T-shirts and brought Taiwan-made HELLO TAIWAN masks and promotional materials on visits to US members of Congress, helping Taiwan's international voice during the US–Taiwan vaccine donation period (including liaison with legislators and the HHS Secretary); his Chinese/English double-"台灣" necktie became a symbol of Taiwanese-American advocacy (corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-797|my-stories-797]]; [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20211025200521_tag_hello-taiwan_001f1aaade1a3a82|TJJ HELLO TAIWAN record, 2021-10-25]]; see also 2021-07-18 entry below).
- 2020 — As NYC chamber president, owned one of three large蔡英文 portrait patchwork artworks displayed at the 1,000+ overseas Taiwanese gratitude banquet (圓山飯店) celebrating the 2020 re-election campaign (corpus: [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2026/20260118100343_千人台僑感恩餐宴_蔡總統_從未忘記要留下更美_4a1c56c9d937309b|TJJ 千人台僑感恩餐宴, 2026-01-18 刊]]).
