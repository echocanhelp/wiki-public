# San Francisco Theological Seminary

**San Francisco Theological Seminary** is the institution where [[people/albert-s-lai||Dr. Albert S. Lai (賴信雄)]] completed his Doctor of Ministry dissertation in 1971: Toward A Community of Hope.

## Significance in Taiwanese American Church History

> **Primary work page:** [[sources/toward-a-community-of-hope||Toward A Community of Hope (work & sources)]]  
> **Full text (GitHub):** [complete extract](https://github.com/echocanhelp/wiki-public/blob/gh-pages/knowledge/web-archives/albert-lai-toward-community-of-hope.md)


Through leaders trained there, the seminary is connected to early theological interpretation of Taiwanese immigrant church formation in North America.

## Identity Snapshot
- Type: Theological seminary
- Geography: San Francisco, California
- Core roles: theological education, research, ecclesial training

## Role in the Community

- The seminary (舊金山神學院) trained pioneers of Bay Area Taiwanese-language church formation. According to Rev. 張瑞雄's history in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-350|Our Journeys #350 美國舊金山灣區台灣基督教會史料簡介 (何義麟, 2019)]]: arriving from Tainan Theological College and Seminary in 1962, he entered San Francisco Theological Seminary in 1964 as an exchange student (introduced by Rev. 黃武東 under a Presbyterian Church arrangement after his Pacific Theological Seminary scholarship ran out), graduating in 1967 — then pastoring a Methodist Japanese church in Fresno before pioneering Taiwanese-immigrant evangelism in the Bay Area, which led to the 1975 founding of 舊金山灣區臺灣基督教會 (FUMC) and its later offshoots 東灣臺灣基督教會 and 迦南臺灣基督教會.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-357|Our Journeys #357 (何義麟, 2020)]] corroborates: SFTS-trained Rev. 張瑞雄 is described as the Bay Area Taiwanese church 開拓者, and the three North-California Taiwanese churches trace their origin to his ministry; their members also joined 同鄉會、聯合會、協志會.
- Also in the corpus: [[people/albert-s-lai|Dr. Albert S. Lai (賴信雄)]] completed his 1971 Doctor of Ministry dissertation here — [[sources/toward-a-community-of-hope|Toward A Community of Hope]].
- Note: 舊金山太平洋神學院 (Pacific Theological Seminary, where 張瑞雄 first enrolled 1962) is a distinct institution from 舊金山神學院 in the source text.
