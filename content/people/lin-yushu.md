# Lin Yu-Shu (林育武)

**Lin Yu-Shu (林育武)** is a Taiwanese-American Presbyterian leader serving as an **委員** (Executive Committee Member) of the [[organizations/national-taiwanese-presbyterian-council|NTPC]] 執委會 for **2025/26** (西北區代表).

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Lin Yu-Shu / Yushu Lin
- **Chinese:** 林育武
- **Era:** Contemporary (NTPC Exec Member 2025/26)
- **Geography:** United States (Northwest District)
- **Core roles:** NTPC Executive Committee Member

## Role in NTPC

Per the 2025/26 執委 letter on ntpc-usa.org:

> **委 員： 林育武長老(西北區) 2026 年屆滿**

He is one of the eight voting members of the NTPC Executive Committee, representing the Northwest District.
