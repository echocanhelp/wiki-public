# Rev. Kao Chun-ming (高俊明)

**Rev. Kao Chun-ming** (Chinese: 高俊明; Pe̍h-ōe-jī: Ko Chùn-bêng; 6 June 1929, Tainan – 14 February 2019) was a minister of the [[organizations/presbyterian-church-in-taiwan||Presbyterian Church in Taiwan (PCT)]]. He served as PCT General Secretary (1970–1989), was imprisoned (1980–1984) for sheltering Kaohsiung Incident dissident Shih Ming-teh (施明德), and later served as a Senior Advisor to the Office of the President under Chen Shui-bian.

## Identity Snapshot

- **Salutation:** Rev. / 高俊明牧師 (also Rev. Dr. Kao in church English)
- **Chinese:** 高俊明
- **POJ:** Ko Chùn-bêng
- **Born / died:** 6 June 1929 (Tainan) – 14 February 2019 (age 89)
- **Roles:** PCT General Secretary (1970–1989); Yu-Shan Theological College president (1957–1970); political prisoner (1980–1984); Presidential Senior Advisor (2000s)
- **Geography:** Tainan → England (Selly Oak) → Taiwan-wide PCT ministry

## Timeline

- **1929-06-06** — Born in Tainan. Grandfather Kao Chang (高長) was among the first Taiwanese baptized in James Laidlaw Maxwell’s mission; father Kao Tsai-te (高再得) was a physician; mother Hou Ching-lien (侯青蓮) later chaired Chang Jung Girls’ High School board. Related by marriage to Cai Peihuo (蔡培火).
- **c. 1939** — At age 10, sent to Aoyama Gakuin in Japan under Cai Peihuo’s care.
- **1950s** — Graduated Tainan Theological College and Seminary; studied at Selly Oak Colleges, England.
- **1957–1970** — President, Yu-Shan Theological College (玉山神學院).
- **1970** — Elected PCT General Secretary (to 1989).
- **1977-08-16** — PCT issued “A Declaration on Human Rights,” calling for Taiwan to become a new and independent country.
- **1979-12-10** — Kaohsiung Incident; afterward Kao helped Shih Ming-teh evade arrest.
- **1980-04-24** — Arrested for harboring a fugitive; notebooks, files, and tapes seized.
- **1980-06-05** — Taiwan Garrison Command court-martial: seven years’ imprisonment, five years’ deprivation of civil rights, property confiscated (with Hsu Ching-fu 許晴富).
- **1980–1984** — Imprisoned. Wife Ruth Kao organized support and international church awareness. Pope John Paul II’s envoy visited him in prison. Prison poem *莿帕互火燒* later won a 2006 Golden Melody Award (lyric).
- **1984-08** — Released.
- **1989** — Left the General Secretary post; became principal of PCT Songnian University (松年大學). Addressed the World Alliance of Reformed Churches general council in Seoul.
- **2000** — Appointed national policy advisor / Senior Advisor after Chen Shui-bian’s election.
- **2001** — Memoir *The Path to the Cross* / 《十字架之路：高俊明牧師回憶錄》 launched.
- **2006** — Publicly supported President Chen against Shih’s “Million Voices” resignation campaign.
- **2012-12-20** — Named Tainan Outstanding Citizen; Mayor Lai Ching-te called him a “人格者.”
- **2013 / later** — Publicly opposed same-sex marriage legislation.
- **2016** — Declined a Senior Advisor post under President Tsai Ing-wen.
- **2019-01** — With [[people/peng-ming-min||Peng Ming-min]], [[people/wu-lipei||Wu Li-pei]], and Lee Yuan-tseh, signed an open letter urging Tsai not to seek re-election.
- **2019-02-14** — Died of illness. State citation of merit read at the 22 Feb memorial (Vice President Chen Chien-jen representing President Tsai).
