## Letters, Medicine & Collections (absorbed from vault corpus)

After retiring from Presbyterian Anesthesia Associates and the chair of anesthesia at Presbyterian Matthews Hospital in 2008, Chen turned to letters: president of the 台美人筆會 [[organizations/taiwanese-american-pen-club-la||Taiwanese American Pen Club LA]] in 2014–2015 (parent page: [[organizations/taiwanese-american-pen-club||Taiwanese American Pen Club]]), and in 2015 publisher and chief editor of the 台美筆會 official website. He is a member and journal publisher for the medical alumni and diaspora-medical networks, including the North American Taiwanese Medical Association [[organizations/north-american-taiwanese-medical-association-foundation||NATMA]] and the Kaohsiung Medical University Alumni Association of America (KMUAAA), the alma mater where he earned his M.D. in 1968.

His material culture of the diaspora is itself a historical source: TAH encyclopedia entry [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/collection-of-dr-tong-y-chen||TAH #55, Collection of Dr. Tong Y. Chen 陳東榮醫師的收藏 (2018-01-08)]] records his collection as community historical record; his biographical entries are [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ota-255||TAH #255]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-303-tong-y-chen||TAH #303]].
