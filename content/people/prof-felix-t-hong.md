## Role in the Community
- Per the memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys321|321. 由陳文成紀念基金會的創立談起 / 許永華 /11/2017]]: after the 1981 Chen Wencheng (陳文成) killing, 洪正幸 — together with 林宜雄 and 許國助 — helped draft the memorial-service announcement, posted posters, assisted with purchasing flower wreaths, and handled logistics for the Detroit-area memorial meeting (Wayne State circle).
- Per the Minnesota Taiwanese-American chronicle [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys123|123. 明州台美人百年大事 / 曾啟明 / 2015-03]]: as Wayne State University professor he gave a talk on 《教育》 at the community 萬國節 cultural exhibition (Ethnic Celebration; alongside FAPA chair 陳文彥 and painter 王華東).
