# Ja Hsieh (謝家豪博士)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Ja Hsieh
- **Chinese:** 謝家豪博士
- **Era:** Birth Place
- **Geography:** 台灣彰化縣員林
- **Core roles:** artist, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Ja Hsieh received his Doctor of Musical Arts degree at the University of Washington where he studied percussion with Prof. Tom Collier. Dr. Hsieh was a recipient of the Boeing Endowed Fellowship for Excellence in Music as well as the Jane and David Davis Endowed Fellowship in Music. He earned a Master of Fine Arts degree and a BFA degree in Percussion Performance at TNUA from his native country, Taiwan.
