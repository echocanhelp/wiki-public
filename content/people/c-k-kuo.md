- Who's Who profile recorded by TAH (2017/01): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-1480-c-k-kuo|1480. C. K. Kuo 郭重國]].
- 1995 — the national presidency roster on the TAA page records 「13 郭重國 / Kuo, C. K. / 1995-96 / Atlanta, GA」 and 「14 郭重國 / Kuo, C. K. / 1997-98 / Atlanta, GA」 — two consecutive numbered terms, seated in Atlanta, the same geography as the profile's `Address: GA`. `sourced` (third-party institutional roster — `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/taiwanese-association-of-america-taa.md`, https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/taiwanese-association-of-america-taa/; resolves the 1996–97 presidency gap left by the profile's single `President of TAA/USA 1995-1998` line).
- 1995-07 (美西台灣人夏令會, Los Angeles) — 全美會會長郭重國 and 副會長魏妙圭 came directly to the camp after the 世台會 annual meeting in New Zealand; in his opening remarks he noted that all six TAA regions (美東、美東南、平原區、美南、中西部、美西) had held a 夏令會 that year. `sourced` (community report by a non-subject author — `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/tacwc-1995.md`, https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/tacwc-1995/).
- 1995 — during the election campaign for the TAA presidency he visited Cincinnati and proposed creating the 全美台灣文物資料中心 (Taiwanese Culture Center of America); after he and 魏妙圭 were elected, Cincinnati members began standing it up, with 鄭憲章 as first 執行長. `sourced` (bylined first-person account by 鄭憲章, author of the center's own report and not the subject — `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/publications1102.md`, https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/publications1102/, published 05/1995).
- 1996-07-11–14 (美東南區台灣人夏令會, Chattanooga) — the same community history also names him on the **political track** alongside 蔡明華、張燦鍙、林又新 (the page previously listed the venue only) — `sourced` ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys245|245. 美東南區台灣人夏令會簡史]]; track assignment corroborated by the contemporaneous campus announcement 張燦鍙、郭重國與蔡明華應邀演講, `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/setaa-1996.md`, https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/setaa-1996/).
- 1996-07 (中西部台灣人夏令會, Kent State University, Cleveland) — as 全台灣同鄉會會長 he co-taught 「台灣人對中國政策走向」 with 游祥修醫師. `sourced` (community report by a non-subject author — `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/mtsc-1996.md`, https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/mtsc-1996/).
- 1997 (美西台灣人夏令會, Las Vegas, Tropicana Hotel, 7/25–27) — 全美同鄉會會長郭重國 and 民視駐美代表陳隆將 each gave 「建國展望」 sessions; he argued 台灣正面臨生存與滅亡的關鍵 and urged three practices: 拒買中國貨、將二二八變成台灣人節日、繼續推動台灣共和國簽名運動向國際表達願望. `sourced` (community report by a non-subject author — `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/tacwc-1997.md`, https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/tacwc-1997/).
- 1997 (19th 美東南區台灣人夏令會) — the 報名截止 6/15 announcement lists him among the invited speakers alongside 黄昭堂、陳茂雄、林山田、童春發、王明哲、李南衡. `sourced` (`knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/setaa-1997.md`, https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/setaa-1997/).
- 1998-07-09–12 (中西部台灣人夏令會, Toledo University, OH; theme 「立足美國，關懷台灣」) — 全美會會長郭重國 delivered 「台灣情，美國景」, using his own 祖厝 and family to compare Taiwan and American life and the education and values of second-generation 台美人; the report notes 「郭重國來自Atlanta，是現任全美會會長」 and that he and the author had met days earlier at the Vancouver 世台會 annual meeting. `sourced` (community report by a non-subject author — `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/mtsc-1998.md`, https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/mtsc-1998/).
- 2001 — reported in the corpus as 世台會會長 (not merely coordinator): after a 世台會 tour bus crashed in Brazil he left the tour immediately for Rio to handle the aftermath, asked 林義德 (Canada) and 陳邦基 (巴西聖保羅) to stay in São Paulo to coordinate the transfer of the injured and their families, attended the funeral of 休士頓同鄉黃秀娥 on 8/11, and announced a 「車禍協助基金」 for the victims and families, financially administered by 姚繁基 with donations collected through the 王康陸博士紀念基金會. `sourced` (community report by a non-subject author — `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/4-brazil-tour-accident-2001.md`, https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/4-brazil-tour-accident-2001/; also 退任的世台會會長郭重國 speaking at the 台灣人協會南美總會 first member assembly, 智利 2003-10-27 — `knowledge/web-archives/taiwandaily-net-141795-141795.md`, https://taiwandaily.net/%e6%97%85%e9%81%a%a4%e5%a4%a9%e5%9c%b0/141795/).
- 2001-07-13–15 (23rd 美東南區台灣人夏令會, Wesleyan College, Macon, GA; theme 「團結惜台灣」) — 世台會會長暨世界台灣人聯合會召集人郭重國 was the conference's **first keynote** and explained the origin and effect of founding 世界台灣人聯合會; youth in the audience waved **WTC 綠旗**. `sourced` (community report by a non-subject author — `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/setaa-2001.md`, https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/setaa-2001/).
