# Rev. Hung-Jen Liu (劉弘仁)

**Rev. Hung-Jen Liu (劉弘仁牧師)** is a Taiwanese Presbyterian minister serving in the Bay Area under the PCUSA **Presbytery of San Francisco** (舊金山中會), listed in the TCCCNA directory of Taiwanese-American PCUSA pastors. Community reporting (2026-09-20, Leonard Hsu Jr.) assesses him as likely the same Rev. Liu Hung-jen who served as **Moderator of the Changhua Presbytery (彰化中會議長)** of the [[organizations/presbyterian-church-in-taiwan|Presbyterian Church in Taiwan]] in the mid-1970s — pending owner/congregation confirmation.

## Identity Snapshot
- Era: 1970s–present
- Geography: Changhua, Taiwan → San Francisco Bay Area (Tri-Valley: Pleasanton/Dublin, CA)
- Core roles: pastor, presbytery moderator, Taiwanese-American church leader

## Ministry
- **Taiwan (PCT)** — Moderator of the Changhua Presbytery (彰化中會議長), mid-1970s. On 1975-05-02 he officiated the founding and dedication (開設與獻堂感恩禮拜) of the Ren'ai Church (仁愛教會) of Xinbao village, Fangyuan, Changhua county, bringing it into the PCT. The church emerged from a split in the newly built Xinbao congregation (a cross-denominational pastoral intervention caused an ideological division); as a breakaway group it needed presbytery-level reception — which is why the Moderator himself officiated. The congregation built its own sanctuary in 1980, was elevated to full self-governing 堂會 status on 1984-08-04, and called its first settled pastor on 1984-12-30: [[sources/pct-renai-church-history||PCT church history]].
- **United States (PCUSA)** — Minister of record within the Presbytery of San Francisco (屬舊金山中會), Tri-Valley residence (Pleasanton/Dublin area). Listed in the TCCCNA PCUSA directory as **Rev. John Hung Jen Liu** with home phone 925-803-1608 and email jencat926@yahoo.com — no settled congregation shown (directory ◇ format = minister without pulpit / retired-supply status), alongside Bay Area peers Rev. Peter Wu (Immanuel Fremont), Rev. KokThai Lim (ATC Grace Walnut Creek), Rev. Noah Chen (First United SF): [[sources/tcccna-pcusadir-2009||TCCCNA directory]], compiled 2009, revised 2022-09-05.
- **Guest pulpit, Southern California** — Preached at the [[organizations/irvine-taiwanese-presbyterian-church|Irvine Taiwanese Presbyterian Church]] (ITPC, Laguna Hills): 「悔改與救恩」 2021-07-18 (video archived, [YouTube MEQMXi69yg4](https://youtu.be/MEQMXi69yg4)); 「祂的星」 01/25 listed on the ITPC sermons/videos page.

## Network
- **San Francisco lunch with Rev. Dr. Choan-Seng Song** — recalled in the 2024-12-25 Taiwan Church News memorial by Rev. Yang-I Hsieh (謝陽一, Atlanta; founding pastor of Lan David Memorial Church, Changhua, 1967, under Song's patronage): Hsieh phoned Song while visiting SF, and **Rev. Liu Hung-jen joined the lunch**. Topics: the neglected Guanziling camp (關子嶺營地) and the Tainan Theological College affair (南神事件), where Song described misunderstanding, board-room unfairness, and humiliation. Since Song left the US in 2013, the lunch predates that. Places Liu inside Song's elder-statesman circle — and, via Hsieh, directly in the same Changhua Presbytery cohort Liu led: [[sources/tcnn-song-memorial-2024||TCNN memorial]].

Liu wears an event lanyard; Leonard himself was not in the photo.
