# Rev. Hung-Jen Liu (劉弘仁)

**Rev. Hung-Jen Liu (劉弘仁牧師)** is a Taiwanese Presbyterian minister serving in the Bay Area under the PCUSA **Presbytery of San Francisco** (舊金山中會), listed in the TCCCNA directory of Taiwanese-American PCUSA pastors. Community reporting (2026-09-20, Leonard Hsu Jr.) assesses him as likely the same Rev. Liu Hung-jen who served as **Moderator of the Changhua Presbytery (彰化中會議長)** of the [[organizations/presbyterian-church-in-taiwan|Presbyterian Church in Taiwan]] in the mid-1970s — pending owner/congregation confirmation.

## Identity Snapshot
- Era: 1970s–present
- Geography: Changhua, Taiwan → San Francisco Bay Area (Tri-Valley: Pleasanton/Dublin, CA)
- Core roles: pastor, presbytery moderator, Taiwanese-American church leader

## Name Variants
- **劉弘仁** (Chinese)
- **Hung Jen Liu** — as printed by the TCCCNA PCUSA directory (primary source for the English form; Wade-Giles-style romanization of the Mandarin reading)
- **John Hung Jen Liu** — TCCCNA listing includes the English given name "John"
- **Hong Ren Liu / Hung-jen Liu / Liu Hung-jen** — pinyin and Tongyromaji-influenced variants of the same Chinese name; low confidence that the subject ever published under these forms
- Not to be confused with: 吳泓仁 Hung-Jen Wu (engineer, [[people/hung-jen-wu]]); Dr. Hung-Jen Liu 劉弘仁 (child psychiatrist, Taipei — different person)

## Ministry
- **Taiwan (PCT)** — Moderator of the Changhua Presbytery (彰化中會議長). On 1975-05-02 he officiated the founding and dedication (開設與獻堂感恩禮拜) of the Ren'ai Church (仁愛教會) of Fangyuan, Changhua county, bringing it into the PCT. Source: PCT official church history ([pct.org.tw C06034](https://www.pct.org.tw/ChurchHistory.aspx?strOrgNo=C06034)).
- **United States (PCUSA)** — Minister of record within the Presbytery of San Francisco (屬舊金山中會), Tri-Valley residence (Pleasanton/Dublin area), listed among Taiwanese PCUSA pastors in the TCCCNA directory (tcccna.org, compiled 2009, revised 2022-09-05).
- **Guest pulpit, Southern California** — Preached at the [[organizations/irvine-taiwanese-presbyterian-church|Irvine Taiwanese Presbyterian Church]] (ITPC, Laguna Hills): 「悔改與救恩」 2021-07-18 (video archived, [YouTube MEQMXi69yg4](https://youtu.be/MEQMXi69yg4)); 「祂的星」 01/25 listed on the ITPC sermons/videos page.

## Network
- In a 2024-12-25 Taiwan Church News memorial for the theologian **[[people/choan-seng-song|Rev. Dr. Choan-Seng Song (宋泉盛)]]** (d. 2024-11-26), the author (Rev. Yang-I Hsieh 謝陽一, Atlanta) recalls lunching with Song in San Francisco together with **Rev. Liu Hung-jen** — the conversation covered the neglected Guanziling camp (關子嶺營地) and the Tainan Theological College affair (南神事件), placing Liu inside the elder statesman circle of the Taiwanese Presbyterian church. Source: [tcnn.org.tw/archives/226932](https://tcnn.org.tw/archives/226932).
