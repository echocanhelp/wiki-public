## Role in the Community

The TAH community memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys305|Our Journeys 305 (大聖路易台灣人長老教會 founding memoir)]] records a Rev. 賴俊明 of Taipei Shuanglian Church (雙連教會) who was interviewed in Taiwan by 張理美醫師 — the delegate sent by the founding group's search committee — and invited to a founding-pastor position. He arrived in St. Louis in November 1984, holding a three-quarter pastor position with the remainder for doctoral studies at Eden Theological Seminary (聖路易伊典神學院). The congregation was formally registered as 大聖路易台灣人長老基督教會 in September 1988 and joined the Kinkeith–Lafayette Presbytery on 1988-10-02 — the first Taiwanese church in the US Midwest admitted to a US mainstream denomination.賴俊明牧師 was inducted as its first pastor on 1989-05-07 and immediately launched a Bible-study movement (with weekly Wednesday small-group study) that drew over half the congregation, growing membership to sixty or seventy.

Further founding-memoir detail (same source, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys305|Our Journeys 305]]): the search committee decided to join the US mainstream denomination (美國長老教會) after consulting Rev. 黃武東牧師 — former long-time General Secretary of the Presbyterian Church in Taiwan — then verified that finances met presbytery standards for a full-time pastor before inviting 賴俊明. After his arrival the congregation moved worship to the parlor of First Presbyterian Church in Kirkwood (克可伍德市第一長老教會) to allow Sunday-morning services. When the session (小會) was formed it comprised the pastor and six elders: 高銘憲、林逸民、張理美、陳克充、鄭紀昭、蘇希三.

## Historical Significance
Supported worship and special events including Father’s Day and regular Sunday services.

Beyond the GSTPC pulpit, the frontier pass (2026-11-18) documents a four-stage public ministry: (1) building pastor of 台北雙連教會 1970–1984 — first Taipei Presbyterian church to worship in a multi-storey building, and initiator of the 家庭小組 (household small-group) movement from 1980 that seeded small-group practice in the PCT; (2) founding/organizing pastor of 大聖路易台灣人長老基督教會 1984–1991 — first U.S.-Taiwanese church to purchase land and self-build its own sanctuary (the '台'-shaped building at 542 Ries Rd, Ballwin, dedicated 1991-08-17, per the church's own site; the ZH memoir prints "542 Res Road", a typo); (3) 總幹事 of 台灣聖經公會 (then 中華民國聖經公會) 1991–2009 — presided over financial independence from foreign subsidies and return of Taiwan Traditional-Chinese Bible rights from 1996, the 2003 name restoration to 台灣聖經公會, mother-tongue Bibles (阿美/布農/魯凱/泰雅爾 etc.), and the 2004 牛仔聖經 youth campaign (1,100+ youth, 57 churches); (4) assisting pastorate at 大阪中華基督教長老會 from 2010.
