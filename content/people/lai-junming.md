# Rev. Lai Junming (賴俊明牧師)

Rev. Lai Junming (賴俊明牧師) appears in GSTPC bulletin records as a visiting preacher in 2022–2023.

## Role in the Community

The TAH community memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys305|Our Journeys 305 (大聖路易台灣人長老教會 founding memoir)]] records a Rev. 賴俊明 of Taipei Shuanglian Church (雙連教會) who was interviewed in Taiwan by 張理美醫師 — the delegate sent by the founding group's search committee — and invited to a founding-pastor position. He arrived in St. Louis in November 1984, holding a three-quarter pastor position with the remainder for doctoral studies at Eden Theological Seminary (聖路易伊典神學院). The congregation was formally registered as 大聖路易台灣人長老基督教會 in September 1988 and joined the Kinkeith–Lafayette Presbytery on 1988-10-02 — the first Taiwanese church in the US Midwest admitted to a US mainstream denomination.賴俊明牧師 was inducted as its first pastor on 1989-05-07 and immediately launched a Bible-study movement (with weekly Wednesday small-group study) that drew over half the congregation, growing membership to sixty or seventy.
