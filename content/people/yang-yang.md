## Role in the Community

- **2012 — Subject of a Taiwanese American documentary:** Yang's UCLA solar-energy research was featured in the documentary *Catching the Invisible Light* (dir. Ho Chie), which reached the Focus Forward semi-finalist / Audience Award track at Sundance. In the accompanying community interview, the filmmaker — also Taiwanese American — described discovering Professor Yang's Taiwanese heritage while prepping the shoot: "He was very supportive of the documentary... it really shows how diverse we are as a community. I feel that it is important to support members of our community, especially when there are so few." Source: [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/catching-the-invisible-light|Catching the Invisible Light (TAO interview, 2012)]].
