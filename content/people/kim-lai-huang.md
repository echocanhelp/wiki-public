## Role in the Community

- 1962 — then at Kansas State University: named (with 洪宏模) among the K-State 學長 who, on 賴其萬's arrival in early 1962, turned him against KMT-era political indoctrination and toward the 台獨 camp — 「1962年初到達K-State不到一個星期，就受到各學長如黃金來和洪宏模等的啟示和感召」 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys-138|138. 華府與巴城TAA的誕生 / 陳淮崇]]).
- Helped build Baltimore/DC-area Taiwanese community organizing and the greater Baltimore/Washington 台灣人活動圈 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories372|372. 懷念黃金來教授—一位永遠不放棄希望的長者 / 賴其萬, 2015-12]]).
- 陳文成獎學金 — the first scholarship for Taiwanese people in North America: 「堪薩斯州的黃金來教授…一手包辦選拔辦法，召集審查委員會」 — the single largest contributor; by the time of writing the scholarship had run 8 years with 30+ recipients ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys8|8. 陳文成教授紀念基金會成立經過 / 林富文]] · [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys8-eng|English version]]).
- 1980-02-16 — one of sixteen professors at the first 北美洲台灣人教授協會籌備會議 at the University of Chicago (list: 張錦哲、張旭成、陳炳杞、黃員成、黃金來、賴義雄、林靜竹、孫錦德、商文義、陳黃義敏、蔡嘉寅、吳得民、伍焜玉、楊勝亮、嚴文亮、廖述宗); later elected 首屆一般理事 with 吳得民、蔡嘉寅、陳炳杞、賴義雄 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys69|69. 北美洲台灣人教授協會 / 廖述宗]] · [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys69-eng|English version]]).
- 2015-12 — memorialized by 賴其萬 as 「一位永遠不放棄希望的長者」 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories372|372. 懷念黃金來教授]]).
- 平原區台灣人秋令會 — in 1983 the idea for a Great Plains Taiwanese camp meeting was conceived in Manhattan, Kansas by 林啓東 together with local 同鄉 professors 范良正、楊景文、刁明華 and 黃金來; the first session was held in 1985 at Rock Spring (4-H Club grounds, Manhattan, KS) ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-351|平原區秋令會沿革]] · [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys296|296. 平原區秋令會]]).
