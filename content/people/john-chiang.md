# John Chiang (江俊輝)

## Identity Snapshot
- **Born:** ~1961 (elected State Controller at age 45 in 2006)
- **Era:** Second-generation Taiwanese American
- **Geography:** California (Sacramento / Los Angeles)
- **Core roles:** California State Controller, former prosecutor

## Overview
- 江俊輝 (John Chiang) was elected California State Controller in 2006 at age 45
- He served as a prosecutor for 12 years and was featured on the FBI's "Most Wanted" program's cooperation segment in the past
- He is known for his principled stance, notably refusing to follow an order from former California Governor Arnold Schwarzenegger in a dispute over California State Board of Equalization matters (2008)
- He has consistently given back to the Taiwanese American community throughout his political career
- He was a key beneficiary of community support from 楊信, who organized a fundraising event for his 2018 campaign alongside 田詒鴻 and other community leaders
- Chiang's political image was built on a step-by-step effort process and his commitment to the Taiwanese American community
