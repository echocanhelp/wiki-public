# John Chiang (江俊輝)

**John Chiang** (江俊輝, born 1962) is a Taiwanese American politician who serves as **California State Controller**, the state's chief financial officer. He was elected to the office in 2006 at age 45 and has served since. He previously worked as a prosecutor for 12 years.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** John Chiang
- **Chinese:** 江俊輝
- **Born:** 1962, New York City
- **Parents:** Taiwanese immigrants (father: 尹湘 Shen Yinhsiang)
- **Education:** Law degree from Georgetown University Law Center
- **Office:** California State Controller (elected 2006, re-elected 2010, 2014, 2018)
- **Previous role:** Prosecutor (12 years)
- **Geography:** California (Sacramento / Los Angeles)
- **Heritage:** Second-generation Taiwanese American

## Early Life and Education

John Chiang was born in New York City in 1962 to Taiwanese immigrant parents. His father, Shen Yinhsiang (尹湘), was an immigrant from Taiwan. Like many other East Coast Asian American politicians, Chiang studied law and earned his J.D. from **Georgetown University Law Center**.

## Prosecutor Career

Before entering elected office, Chiang worked as a prosecutor for 12 years. His prosecutorial background shaped his public image as a principled, no-nonsense public servant who focused on accountability and fiscal responsibility.

## California State Controller

In 2006, at age 45, Chiang was elected California State Controller — a position that makes him the state's chief financial officer, responsible for managing the state's finances, collecting revenues, and disbursing funds. He was re-elected in 2010, 2014, and 2018.

### Schwarzenegger Dispute (2008)

Chiang's political image was significantly shaped by his principled stance in a dispute with former California Governor Arnold Schwarzenegger over California State Board of Equalization matters in 2008. Chiang refused to follow the Governor's order, establishing his reputation for independence and integrity. This incident was widely covered and cemented his image as a politician who would not be swayed by political pressure.

### Community Support

Chiang has consistently given back to the Taiwanese American community throughout his political career. He was a key beneficiary of community support from **楊信 (Yang Hsin)**, who organized a fundraising event for Chiang's 2018 campaign alongside 田詒鴻 (Alan Thian) and other community leaders.

### Political Philosophy

Chiang's political image was built on a step-by-step effort process and his commitment to the Taiwanese American community. He is known for his fiscal conservatism, transparency, and dedication to serving the public interest.
