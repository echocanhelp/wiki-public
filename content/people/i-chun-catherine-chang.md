## Role in the Community
- ~2018: As Macalester College geography associate professor (地理系副教授), co-curated the Taiwan Online Library (台灣線上圖書館) exhibit booth content at the Minneapolis landmark-center City Exploration event, together with Bo-Fu Chen (陳柏甫, sociology ABD, University of Connecticut); the booth covered Taiwan's population and social development — Austronesian origins, mixing of indigenous/Chinese/Japanese ancestry, and the road to the 1996 direct presidential election. The event, now in its 12th year with 60+ countries exhibiting, was called by center director Amy Mino the richest, most colorful Taiwan exhibit yet. Source record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys218|Our Journeys 218]]. See also own profile record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-2129-i-chun-chang|TAH #2129]].
