## Role in the Community
- **1980-02-16** — Founding participant of the North American Taiwan Professor Association (NATPA, 北美洲台灣人教授協會) at its first preparatory meeting at the University of Chicago International House, one of sixteen professors convened by 廖述宗; later elected as NATPA's eastern-region regional director (東部區域理事). [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys69|69. 北美洲台灣人教授協會 / 廖述宗]]
- **1983** — Invited by the Akron (克城) 同鄉會 to lecture at the Midwest Taiwanese Summer Camp at Oberlin College alongside 周清玉、許榮淑、陳以德; the camp drew record attendance. [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys74|74. 1983至1990的北美洲台灣人醫師協會 / 周烒明]]
- **1987-02-28** — Among the scholars presenting at the 228 40th-anniversary academic conference in Palo Alto (Syntek Hall Center) with 彭明敏、林宗義、林宗光、林衡哲、陳芳明、張富美 et al. [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys37|37. 台灣協志會成立與大事記 / 紀哲嘉]]
- **1989-12** — Joined NATPA's delegation observing Taiwan's立委/縣市長/省議員 elections (with 陳文彥、廖述宗、林宗光); the delegation returned to the US and issued a statement condemning KMT misuse of public power, non-neutral military, and money politics. [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys47|47. 北美洲台灣人敎授協會首度回台召開年會的經緯和歷史意義 / 林靜竹]]
- The 廖述宗 memoir corroborates the table record below: after the 2000 political turnover, 張旭成 returned to Taiwan as a DPP 僑選立委 and later 國安會副秘書長. [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys69|69. 北美洲台灣人教授協會 / 廖述宗]]
