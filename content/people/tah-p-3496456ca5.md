## Role in the Community
- 發行人 of Asia Today US（西雅圖華聲報）; TAH profile #607 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/607-e5-91-a8-e6-98-ad-e4-ba-ae-201509|607. 周昭亮 / 2015/09]]).
- Author of 台灣文化推廣在西雅圖的成效與突破 (2014/05), his own account of Taiwanese cultural promotion in Seattle ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/189-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e6-96-87-e5-8c-96-e6-8e-a8-e5-bb-a3-e5-9c-a8-e8-a5-bf-e9-9|189. 台灣文化推廣在西雅圖的成效與突破 / 2014/05]]).
- Per Ed Huang's (黃東昇) history of the Taiwanese American Conference – West Coast, during 2016–17 he was one of the local leaders consulted to define TAC-WC's five regions, and he served as the Seattle-region director on the founding TAC-WC Board (announced May 2017) alongside Tony Lee 李賢群 (LA), Jim Wang 王進賢, Ken Wang 王克雄 (San Diego) and Ed Huang 黃東昇 (San Francisco–Sacramento) ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys352|352. History of TAC-WC / 黃東昇 / 03/2020]]).
- Prior tenure as 前西雅圖台灣同鄉會會長 is recorded in the corpus via FAHR's 2023-01-24 seminar notice (see From the record).
