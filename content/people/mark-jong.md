## Role in the Community

- **1990 — host of the TAA Plains Labor Day Camp (平原區秋令會).** This annual camp — conceived in 1983 by Prof. 林啓東 and colleagues and first held 1985 in Manhattan, KS, rotating across Kansas, Missouri, Iowa, Colorado, Oklahoma, and Nebraska — was held **1990-08-10 to 12 in Emporia, KS** under the theme 「新秧望，新年代 / New Hope, New Century」, hosted by the TAA Wichita chapter with 鍾茂萃 (listed Chung, M.T.) as the local host — consistent with his Wichita State University professorship.
