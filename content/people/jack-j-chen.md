# Jack J. Chen (陳宏傑)

**Jack J. Chen, PharmD** is a Taiwanese American clinical pharmacist and researcher, double board-certified in Pharmacotherapy and Geriatric Pharmacy Practice. He is a Full Professor and Founding Chair of the Department of Pharmacy Practice at Marshall B. Ketchum University College of Pharmacy.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Jack J. Chen
- **Chinese:** 陳宏傑
- **Born:** South Bend, Indiana
- **Raised:** Southern California
- **Education:** BA Animal Physiology, UC San Diego (1989); BS Pharmacy, MCPHS (1993); PharmD, University of Utah (1995)
- **Specialty:** Clinical neurology, neurology pharmacotherapy, movement disorders
- **Current role:** Full Professor, Founding Chair, Dept of Pharmacy Practice, Marshall B. Ketchum University COP
- **Geography:** Southern California
- **Credentials:** FCPhA, FASCP, FCCP
- **Family:** Married with twin daughters

## Education and Training

- **BA Animal Physiology**, UC San Diego (1989)
- **BS Pharmacy**, Massachusetts College of Pharmacy and Health Sciences (1993)
- **PharmD**, University of Utah (1995)
- **Specialty Pharmacy Residency** (Internal Medicine), Rush University Medical Center, Chicago (1996)
- **Clinical Neurology Preceptorship**, Movement Disorders Center, Loma Linda University (2003–2005)

## Career

### Huntington Memorial Hospital (1996–2000)
Clinical pharmacist at Huntington Memorial Hospital, Pasadena, CA.

### Western University of Health Sciences (2000–2005)
Assistant Professor at the Western University of Health Sciences School of Pharmacy, where he developed and taught the PharmD neurology therapeutics courses and served as Director of Advanced Pharmacy Practice Experiences and faculty advisor to the American Pharmacists Association (APhA) Academy of Student Pharmacists.

### Loma Linda University (2005–2014)
Associate Professor at Loma Linda University (LLU) School of Pharmacy, where he was instrumental in developing and teaching the PharmD neurology therapeutics course. He also served as the Director of Scholarly Activities to foster a departmental culture of scholarship. In 2013, he received an award as LLU School of Pharmacy Teacher of the Year. At LLU, Dr. Chen also established, obtained funding, and co-directed an interprofessional, clinical neurology movement disorders preceptorship program within the Department of Neurology.

### Marshall B. Ketchum University (2014–present)
Full Professor and Founding Chair for the Department of Pharmacy Practice at the Marshall B. Ketchum University College of Pharmacy.

## Research and Publications

Dr. Chen maintains a sustained record of scholarship in the field of Neurology Pharmacotherapy with an emphasis in Movement Disorders. He is an author on **160 peer-reviewed manuscripts, book chapters, and abstracts**.

His research focus includes:
- Neurology pharmacotherapy
- Movement disorders
- Clinical neurology
- Geriatric pharmacy practice
