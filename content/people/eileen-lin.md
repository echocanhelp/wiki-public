## Role in the Community
- As coordinator of the FAPA Young Professionals Group, she was the named media contact and spokesperson for the Jan 2011 multi-city demonstrations (Washington, Boston, New York, San Francisco) timed to Hu Jintao's visit, articulating the group's five demands on Taiwan's security and right to international participation — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/taiwanese-american-youths-voice-taiwans-right-to-self-determination-during-hu-ji|TAHS record of the 2011 FAPA-YPG actions]].
- Provided the English/Mandarin/Japanese news-coverage sampling on the Taiwan arms-sale hearing republished by TA online — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/why-taiwan-matters-part-ii|Why Taiwan Matters, Part II]].
- Appears in the 2012 Washington photo of the蔡英文 (Tsai Ing-wen) meeting with FAPA HQ staff (with 王巧蓉, Jean Wu, Gerrit van der Wees, Coen Blaauw, Janice Chen) preserved in the memoirs — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys228|Our Journeys 228]].
