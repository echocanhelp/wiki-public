# Dr. Jia-Rhon Chen (陳家榮醫師)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Dr. Jia-Rhon Chen
- **Chinese:** 陳家榮醫師
- **Era:** 1940
- **Geography:** Yunlin, Taiwan
- **Core roles:** physician, professor, artist, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Eleanor Heartney, a contributing editor to Art in America and author of Critical Condition: American Culture at Crossroads, wrote of Jia-Rhon Chen’s artworks: “Dr. Chen blends bold color and cubistic forms which might be seen as part of his western inheritance with sure quick brush strokes that hark back to the tradition of Asian calligraphy. This mixing of reference is evident in other works which perform a similar operation on the landscape of Taiwan, transforming it into a kaleidoscopic vision of movement and change….”

## Career & Community Service
*(absorbed from the TAH tables below and the encyclopedia sources cited on this page)*
- **1965** — MD, Medical, National Taiwan Univ.
- **1973–2010** — Neurologist, Veterans' Administration Medical Center, Brooklyn, NY; concurrently Associate Professor, SUNY Downstate Medical Center University Hospital
- **1991–2015** — First Chairman of the Board of Directors, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/39-formosa-chamber-music-society||Formosa Chamber Music Society, Inc.]] (TAH #39)
- **1995–1997** — President, National Taiwan University Alumni Association, Greater New York Chapter
- **2001–2003** — President, National Taiwan University Medical College Alumni Association, Greater New York Chapter
- **2003–2005** — Chairman of the Board of Directors, National Taiwan University Medical College Alumni Association of North America

## Role in the Community
- The community essay [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys291||Our Journeys (ourjourneys291)]] places him among the Taiwanese-American physician-artists — "陳家榮自小喜歡繪畫，一邊當醫師，一邊畫出自己的一片天" — alongside pathologist-painter 施哲三 and physician-painter 許明彥.
