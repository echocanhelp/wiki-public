## Role in the Community

- Served as **President of the North America Taiwanese Professors' Association (NATPA)**: in that role he backed holding the 31st NATPA Annual Conference jointly with the Taiwanese American Conference – West Coast (TAC-WC) at UC San Diego in July 2011 — the first TAC-WC in years, coordinated by Ken Huang (黃根深) and Darice Lee (洪珠美) ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys352|Our Journeys 352 — TAC-WC history]]).
- Wrote on Taiwanese identity and politics: essay 臺灣的品格 (2003/03, Politics) ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/25-e8-87-ba-e7-81-a3-e7-9a-84-e5-93-81-e6-a0-bc-e6-9d-8e-e5-ad-b8-e5-9c-96-2003-|TAH #25 臺灣的品格]]), 孕育台灣人意識50好書 (2007/09, Politics) ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/175-e5-ad-95-e8-82-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e4-ba-ba-e6-84-8f-e8-ad-9850-e5-a5-bd-e6|TAH #175 孕育台灣人意識50好書]]), and a piece in 閱讀台灣–人文100特展成果專輯 (2008/12) ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/305-e9-96-b1-e8-ae-80-e7-81-a3-e4-ba-ba-e6-96-87100-e7-89-b9-e5-b1-95-e|TAH #305 閱讀台灣]]).
- 2010-09-01 — as 教授會總會會長 (NATPA president) he phoned the Taiwanese American Conference–West Coast side proposing that the 2011 NATPA annual conference be held jointly with the 美西夏令會 (TAC-WC); the proposal was reported to the TAC-WC大会 and met with positive response ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys265|Our Journeys 265 — TAC-WC memoir]]).
- 2010-10-09 — attended the NATPA/TAC-WC joint planning meeting at the La Quinta Inn, Los Angeles (LAX), leading a NATPA delegation of the president plus 5–6 board members; the summer-conference side included 許輕甫、許和子、王泰和、洪珠美 and Daniel Lin ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys265|Our Journeys 265]]).
