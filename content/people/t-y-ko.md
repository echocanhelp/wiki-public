## Role in the Community

- President of the **North America Taiwanese Women's Association (NATWA)** — during her term she created the NATWA website and pushed chapter-level community service activities ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys60|Our Journeys 60 — NATWA history]]).
- After 阿香 (吳美芬) stepped down from the 公論報「婦女信箱」column (~1993), 柯翠園 was among the members who took over the column in rotation ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys60|Our Journeys 60]]).
- Named in the「點心擔」column history as former NATWA 總會長 who in 1999 identified the column as the「婦女習作園地」successor to 婦女信箱 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys154|Our Journeys 154]]).
- Listed among NATWA leadership/award records for 1993–1994 alongside 阿香、葉錦如、林美里、廖愛華、李素秋 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys161|Our Journeys 161]]).
- Speaker on 女性 topics at the 17th 北美台灣同鄉會年會 (1995-07, Converse College, SC; theme「新台灣，新希望」) ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys245|Our Journeys 245]]).
- Her own TAH encyclopedia record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-1629-t-y-ko|TAH #1629 T. Y. Ko 柯翠園]]; her memoir piece 被黑熊追趕的女人 (2003/05, Literature/文學): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/66-e8-a2-ab-e9-bb-91-e7-86-8a-e8-bf-bd-e8-b6-95-e7-9a-84-e5-a5-b3-e4-ba-ba-e6-9f|TAH #66 被黑熊追趕的女人]].
