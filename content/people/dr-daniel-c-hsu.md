## Timeline

Facts absorbed from this page's own TAH tables and accomplishment entry (no external sources).

- **1943** — Born in Changhua, Taiwan.
- **1967** — D.D.S., Chung Shan Medical Univ.
- **1978** — D.M.D., School of Dental Medicine, Tufts Univ. (Boston); dentist at Dr. Herman's Dental Clinic, New Jersey (1978–1980).
- **1981–** — Own practice: Daniel C. Hsu Dental Clinic.
- **1988** — President, Taiwanese Medical & Dental Association of Orange County (柑縣台灣醫師及牙醫師公會).
- **1993** — President, Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce of Orange County (柑縣台美商會) — [[organizations/taiwanese-chambers-of-commerce-of-orange-county||Taiwanese Chambers of Commerce of Orange County]].
- **1996** — President, Chung Shan Medical University Alumni Association.
- **1999** — President, Chinese American Dental Society of Southern California.
- **2004** — President, [[organizations/natma-southern-california||NATMA Southern California Chapter]].
- **2006–2007** — President (總會長), North American Taiwanese Medical Association (NATMA).
- **2008–2009** — President, [[organizations/north-american-taiwanese-medical-association-foundation||NATMA Foundation]].
- **2010–2018** — President (董事長), NATMA Foundation / S. CA Chapter.
- **2021** — Co-initiator (共同發起人) of the Southern California overseas community's joint statement supporting Taiwan's participation in the World Health Assembly; as one of three NATMA 總會長 who had led Geneva advocacy delegations (with 林榮松 and 邱俊杰), he joined the "Taiwan-in-WHO" forum at the 大洛杉磯台灣會館 (2021-04-18).
