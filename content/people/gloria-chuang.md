# Gloria Chuang (莊邑文)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Gloria Chuang
- **Chinese:** 莊邑文
- **Era:** Contemporary (DMA 1993; TAH profile 2017/02)
- **Geography:** 台灣
- **Core roles:** artist, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

At age 11, Gloria Chuang won the National Young Keyboard Artist Competition of Taiwan, under the tutelage of her father and Robert Scholz. Consequently, she played the Haydn Piano Concerto in D with the San Francisco Youth Symphony at the Groves. As a third-generation performer/teacher of piano in her family, Gloria enjoys performing and giving masterclasses abroad and at home in Madison, Wisconsin. She holds a Bachelors of Music and a Masters of Music from the Juilliard School and received her Doctor of Musical Arts in Piano Performance from the University of Michigan at Ann Arbor in 1993.
