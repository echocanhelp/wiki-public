## Story

郭泓志 was born in 1981 in Tainan, Taiwan and attended Chang Jung Christian Univ. He signed with the Los Angeles Dodgers as an international free agent in 1999 for a reported US$1.25M bonus, and after elbow problems and two Tommy John surgeries he debuted in the major leagues on 2005-09-02 as the fourth player from Taiwan. His breakout came as a reliever in 2008, and on 2010-07-11 he became the first Taiwanese-born player named an MLB All-Star, closing that season leading all relievers with a 1.20 ERA, a then Dodgers franchise record. In May 2011 the Dodgers placed him on the disabled list with what the team termed an anxiety disorder and later declined to tender him a contract; he then pitched for the Seattle Mariners in 2012, the Uni Lions from 2014 to 2016, and the Fubon Guardians from 2017 to 2018, announcing his retirement on 2018-10-22. Back in Taiwan he operated the 斯博森運動會館 gym in Tainan from 2015 to 2022 and began hosting the YouTube channel 「郭董懂不懂」 in 2022. STARLUX named him ambassador for its California Ontario route on 2025-05-14, and on 2025-09-15 he earned a 60th Golden Bell Awards 實境節目主持人獎 nomination with a host ensemble of 8 for 《人生幹大事上船了各位！》.
