- **Tenure ladder (official record)** — Texas House District 112, nine consecutive terms, **81st (Jan 13, 2009 – Jan 11, 2011)** through **89th (Jan 14, 2025 – Jan 12, 2027)**, Republican, home Garland/Dallas, district within Dallas County, per the Texas Legislative Reference Library member profile id 5666, archived `knowledge/web-archives/angie-chen-button-2026-11-16/lrl-texas-gov-member-5666.html`.
- **Committee chairs (official record)** — **89th (2025)**: Chair, Trade, Workforce, and Economic Development; member: House Administration, Ways and Means, Ways & Means S/C on Property Tax Appraisals. **88th (2023)** and **87th (2021)**: Chair, International Relations and Economic Development; 88th additionally Securing Texas from Hostile Foreign Organizations (Select). Per LRL profile id 5666 `knowledge/web-archives/angie-chen-button-2026-11-16/lrl-texas-gov-member-5666.html` (the house.texas.gov self-report and en.wp name the same chairs).

## Sourced facts (FLOOR-ANCHOR absorb 2026-11-12)

- Born **1954 in Taipei, Taiwan** to 外省人 parents who fled mainland China during the Great Retreat; per `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/person/angie-chen-button.json` (Birth Year 1954, Birth Place 台灣台北, re-synced 2026-06-19) and per the official Texas House biography `knowledge/web-archives/angie-chen-button-house-texas-gov-biography-2026-11-12.md`.
- Childhood: a family of **7** lived in a **300-square-foot** one-room hut without a kitchen or running water, per the official Texas House self-report `knowledge/web-archives/angie-chen-button-house-texas-gov-biography-2026-11-12.md` (mirrored by en.wp `knowledge/web-archives/angie-chen-button-en-wikipedia-2026-11-12.md`).
- Education per the official self-report: a **CPA** with **masters' degrees in Public Finance and Management Sciences**; met husband Darcy as a graduate student at **UT Dallas**, and the family has lived in **Richardson and Garland for 30 years** — per `knowledge/web-archives/angie-chen-button-house-texas-gov-biography-2026-11-12.md`.
- Assumed office **January 13, 2009** as State Representative for **House District 112** (portions of Garland, Richardson, Rowlett, Sachse, and Dallas in Dallas County), per the official self-report `knowledge/web-archives/angie-chen-button-house-texas-gov-biography-2026-11-12.md` and en.wp "Assumed office" `knowledge/web-archives/angie-chen-button-en-wikipedia-2026-11-12.md`.
- Ran after incumbent **Frank Hill** retired in **2008**; then-State Senator Florence Shapiro called and suggested she run, per en.wp (citing Spectrum News 2021-04-04) `knowledge/web-archives/angie-chen-button-en-wikipedia-2026-11-12.md`.
- Named one of the state's best legislators by **Texas Monthly in 2021** for work allocating child-care program funding, per en.wp citing Hooks, Texas Monthly **2021-07-15** `knowledge/web-archives/angie-chen-button-en-wikipedia-2026-11-12.md`.
- **2024** — joined the Texas economic delegation led by Gov. Greg Abbott that met President Lai Ching-te, per en.wp citing Dallas Morning News **2024-07-07** `knowledge/web-archives/angie-chen-button-en-wikipedia-2026-11-12.md`.
- Career before the House: International Marketing Manager at **Texas Instruments**, promoting American products abroad, per the official self-report `knowledge/web-archives/angie-chen-button-house-texas-gov-biography-2026-11-12.md` (TAH #1567 lists the same post per `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/whoswho1567.md`).
- Earlier public service: DART board member, Dallas Regional Mobility Coalition executive committee, Women's Museum Advisory Board, Dallas Assembly and Dallas Summit, per the official self-report `knowledge/web-archives/angie-chen-button-house-texas-gov-biography-2026-11-12.md`.
