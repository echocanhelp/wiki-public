## Role in the Community

As 會長 of 全美台灣同鄉會 (Taiwanese Association of America), 洪茂澤 appears at several points in the early-1990s movement record:

- 1993 春節晚會 (Bay Area): the 西區理事長張信行醫師 asked that the 全美同鄉會會長 visiting from Southern California be allowed five minutes to speak at the banquet; the 北加州聯合會 理事會 granted it, and the decision drew criticism from three association presidents who had pressed for KCC officeholder 詹憲卿 to be allowed to speak instead ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys142|Our Journeys 142 — 北加州聯合會 memoir]]).
- During his TAA presidency he attended the Taiwanese community summer camp in Columbus, Ohio, where he gave a visitor from Taiwan the "我愛台僑" badge ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys209|Our Journeys 209 — memoir]]).
- 1994: a removal drive and the associated incident over him as TAA president were documented in the movement record ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/disputes5|5. 罷免洪茂澤 1994]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/law4|4. 全美台灣同鄉會會長洪茂澤事件 / 1994]]).
- In-corpus TAH profile: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/265-joseph-hong-e6-b4-aa-e8-8c-82-e6-be-a4201502|265. Joseph Hong 洪茂澤/2015/02]].

HOLD: the Columbus summer-camp meeting is undated in the memoir ("今年七月上旬" only) and the outcome of the 1994 罷免 effort is not stated in the corpus — both left unresolved.
