## Role in the Community
- **TAC/West Coast reactivation (2011):** TAH record #336 identifies him as the famous artist and the coordinator who reactivated the West Coast Summer Conference in 2011, and records him as a 台獨盟 (UFI) member — consistent with the UFI/UFAI founding membership noted in his TAHS publication profile ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ff336|TAH #336 — Famous Artist & Coordinator of TAC/West Coast 2011]]).
- **His own account of the conference:** he wrote the retrospective essay 「2011年美西夏令會的回顧」(11/2016) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys265|TAH #265]].
- **FAHR leadership detail:** the 2014 全美台灣人權協會 roster (from 張丁蘭's story) lists him among the directors (理事: …黃根深…) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys75|TAH #75]] / [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys265|TAH #265]]. HOLD: the roster lists him as 理事/director for 2014 while the Who's Who table records him as FAHR President (un-dated) — retained as-is, likely different terms.
- **FAHR history co-author:** co-author, with 王廷宜(泰和) and 莊秋雄, of 「台灣人權協會 1970-1990年代的故事」(2015) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1040|TAH #1040]].
- **Profile by 陳婉真:** 「用畫筆搞台獨的黃根深」(04/2017) — his art as a vehicle for the Taiwan independence movement — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories545|TAH #545]].
- **Memorial exhibition:** the 黃根深教授紀念展 was held in San Diego, CA in 06/2018, shortly after his death (2018) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ken-huang-san-diego-ca-06-2018|TAH #32]].
- Additional community record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/64-ken-s-huang|TAH #64 — 黃根深 Ken S. Huang]].
