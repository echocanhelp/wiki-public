# Wang Taihe (王泰和)

**Wang Taihe** (王泰和) is a Taiwanese American artist and cultural figure. His profile, written by [[people/zheng-bing-quan||Zheng Bingquan]], highlights his contributions to the arts and the Taiwanese American community in the San Francisco Bay Area.

## Overview

Wang Taihe is an artist and cultural figure whose work contributes to the preservation and promotion of Taiwanese American culture. His profile in the 2023 TAHS Publication, written by Zheng Bingquan, includes details about his artistic work, his community contributions, and his impact on the local Taiwanese American community.

## Career

Wang's artistic career is rooted in the San Francisco Bay Area's vibrant Taiwanese American community. His work reflects the experiences of the immigrant generation and their efforts to maintain cultural connections while adapting to life in America.

## Legacy

Through his art and community involvement, Wang has helped to preserve and share Taiwanese cultural traditions with both the Taiwanese American community and the broader public. His work serves as a bridge between generations and between cultures.
