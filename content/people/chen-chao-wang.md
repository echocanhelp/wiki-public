## Role in the Community
- **Editor-in-chief (主編) of 台灣公論報 (Taiwan Tribune)** — corpus Band-A memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys278|278. 戰友們，珍重再見! / 王震昭 /03/2017]] (originally in 台灣公論報 Issue 2357, 2011-09-30; posted to the corpus 2017-03-08) is his farewell essay at the paper's end. He writes that his 主編 tenure spanned a third of the paper's 30-year run — roughly ten years ("我的主編任期正好是十個購物袋的長度", at ~48 issues/year).
- In the memoir he describes the Tribune's model: unpaid authors and long-term advertisers, reader "小額捐款" sustaining the paper for thirty years, and argues the center of gravity of the Taiwan independence movement should shift from overseas to inside Taiwan after 台獨聯盟本部遷台. This is primary material on the overseas movement's self-understanding in 2011.
- His role as journalist/editor is consistent with the press-kit core roles above; the corpus record substantiates the journalism side as a decade-long editorship rather than incidental contribution.
