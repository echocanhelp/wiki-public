## Role in the Community
- 1964-06 — introduced 楊宗昌 (then at Kansas State) into the 台灣獨立聯盟 (UFI) ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys33|33. 一家人做伙來打拼／張炎憲]]).
- 1966-06-18 — attended the 費城結盟會談 as one of the Oklahoma representatives (with 陳榮成、陳唐山) that resolved to found the 全美台灣獨立聯盟 (UFAI) on 1966-07-04 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys81|81. 早期威大台灣學生／周烒明起稿]]).
- Held the UFAI finance portfolio; his 1966-07 campaign book-sales tally (主席20本) is recorded in 陳希寬's memoir of the first 全美台灣同鄉通訊錄 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys58|58. 回憶編印第一本通訊錄／陳希寬]]).
- 1967 — succeeded 陳以德 as the second chairman (主席) of UFAI 美國本部 after 陳以德 moved to Ohio; under him the organization's center of activity shifted to 紐約 and 洛杉磯, and the alliance mailbox moved from 費城 to the 紐約 headquarters in 1968 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys234|234. 台獨聯盟美國本部]]).
- 1967-05 — after 陳以德's sudden resignation, met in emergency consultation with 周烒明、羅福全、蔡同榮 to hold morale and plan the June re-election of chairman ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys81|81. 早期威大台灣學生／周烒明起稿]]).
