## Role in the Community (vault corpus, 2026-09-14)
The TAH story corpus holds Ken Lee (李彥禎) as one of its most prolific community essayists — some 30 dated works from 2014 to 2021 — far beyond the press-kit Who's Who entry:

- **Family & community memoirist:** [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories174|174. 我倆的故事—李彥禎和張由吏]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories70|70. 壽席壽喜]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories71|71. 岳母的故事]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories-72-eng|72. 不怕死的病西施 One Brave Woman]].
- **Historian of the NC chapter he once led:** [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys241|241. 北卡台灣同鄉歡慶新春]] (band A) records the 北卡台灣同鄉會's 30+ year evolution — founding 1971, membership steady around 200, the leadership-relay ethic ("找有生之年，一定要至少花一年全心全力為台灣人服務") — written from his own vantage as 1979 chapter president. Also [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys239|239. 從參加美西台灣同鄉夏令會說起]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories467|467.「搬到加州去」]].
- **Commentator & writing advocate:** [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys191|191. 政論節目的貢獻及挑戰]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys264|264. 請大家來寫作]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-358|358.「樹」外桃源、解悶勝地]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-781|781. 當才女碰上騙子時]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-715|715. 養生之道，如走棋]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1026|1026. 尋找幸福]].
- **台美人筆會 (Pen Club LA, president 2007):** the Taiwan Justice / 台美史料中心 newsletter for June 2017 lists him on the editorial board of 《2017 台美文藝》 alongside 陳東榮、李芬芬、鄭炳全、秦雪華、林文政 (see [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2022/20221127052725_2017_06_11_2017台美文藝出版-台美人筆會六月十七日舉辦發表會_c76f11ecd2598ecb|2017 台美文藝發表會報導]]).
- His essay 不怕死的「病西施」 (translated by Chung Lin Hsu) was reprinted in the 台美史料中心 March 2021 newsletter ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20210419132127_2021_02_28_march-2021-newsletter-t-a-archives-台美史料中心_0832558e3b4e5ac7|TA-archives March 2021]]).
