- 第二屆台灣文化節 exhibition planning (1994). - Professor of epidemiology, UCSF and Stanford (2002). The author-bio block of her invited editorial 「Breast cancer in Asian women」 (Western Journal of Medicine 176(2), 2002-03) states: "Marion M Lee is professor of epidemiology at the University of California, San Francisco, and at Stanford University School of Medicine. She is a nutritional epidemiologist whose work has focused almost entirely on cancer. Her particular interest is in the conduct of comparative epidemiologic studies involving Chinese populations in different parts of the world" — per `knowledge/web-archives/marion-m-lee-thirdparty-2026-11-16/pmc1071672-wjm2002.html` — `documented` (third-party publisher bio; independently corroborates the self-reported UCSF employment and the 營養學 education track).
- Co-investigator, Marin Breast Cancer Watch adolescent risk-factor study (1999). The Marin Breast Cancer Watch newsletter 「Keeping Abreast」 (Summer 1999) reports the $500,000 California Breast Cancer Research Program study team "consists of UCSF epidemiologists Dr. Margaret Wrensch and Dr. Marion Lee, along with five Marin Breast Cancer Watch members" (600 Marin County women, half with breast cancer) — per `knowledge/web-archives/marion-m-lee-thirdparty-2026-11-16/zerobreastcancer-1999-summer.pdf` — `documented` (third-party community-organization record).
- Advisory board member, 新希望華人癌症關懷基金會 / New Hope Chinese Cancer Care Foundation. Her name 「李明瑱教授」 (listed "Marion Lee, PhD 李明瑱教授") appears on the foundation's team page under Advisory Board — per `knowledge/web-archives/marion-m-lee-thirdparty-2026-11-16/newhopecancer-team.html` (fetched 2026-11-16) — `documented` (third-party organizational roster; identity-consistent on EN+Chinese name, PhD, Bay Area, cancer field).
- Committee member, 北美台美專業人員協會 / North American Taiwan Professionals Association (NATPA). She (listed 「李明瑱 Marion Lee」) is named on the 黃界清 Prof. Liao Shutsung Memorial & Research Awards Committee on the association's leadership page — per `knowledge/web-archives/marion-m-lee-thirdparty-2026-11-16/natpa-officers.html` (fetched 2026-11-16) — `documented` (third-party organizational roster; identity-consistent on EN+Chinese name).
