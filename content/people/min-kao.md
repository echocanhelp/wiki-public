## Role in the Community
- Community memoir of the 東田納西台灣同鄉會 founding (張惠雄, 09/2017) records 高民環 as one of five UT students — 高民環、汪雅煌、陳耀宗、林正惠、李明輝 — who drove the 1974–75 push to form an independent, mother-tongue-speaking 台灣同鄉會 at the University of Tennessee in the white-terror era; the founding assembly drew 50–60 同鄉 on Thanksgiving Day 1975 at UT's Baptist Student Center. The memoirist notes that either 高民環 or 汪雅煌 first urged him to be founding 會長. Consistent with his UT Knoxville graduate enrollment already on this page; no date conflict, no HOLD — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys313|東田納西台灣同鄉會成立簡史]] (English translation: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys313-eng|Our Journeys #313, EN]]).
- Host community for the 1996 Plains Region Fall Camp in Overland Park, Kansas: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-351|Our Journeys 351 — Plains Region Fall Camp / 張錫清 / 01/2020]]. The camp's own roster-of-conveners record names him as the 1996 convenor on behalf of the Taiwanese Association of Greater Kansas City (1996-07-19–21, Overland Park KS, theme 「台灣前途之探討／Democracy for Independence 推動民主制憲、宣誓國家獨立」) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys296|Our Journeys 296 — 平原區台灣人秋令會的介紹 / 童海南 / 06/2017]].
- Fellow UT Knoxville memoirist 汪雅煌 recalls the same 1973 arrival cohort — 高民環、陳耀宗、林政惠、林正惠 variant spellings aside, sharing a house and a one-cook-a-day rotation with the senior students 楊學文、林國雄、雷添壽、李明輝、林文賢 — describing 高民環 as 「聰明過人，反應敏銳，極富創造力」 and already well-versed in American life before arriving; corroborates the same Knoxville student circle behind the 同鄉會 founding above ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys102|Our Journeys 102 — 諾城回憶 / 汪雅煌 / 2015-02]]).
- Profiled twice in the TAH encyclopedia as community record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-min-kao|TAH #128 Min Kao 高民環]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories184|TAH #184 台美人的榮耀：高民環博士 / 蔡山慶 / 01/2015]]; the original encyclopedia entry is archived in the corpus at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/66-dr-min-kao|TAH #66 Dr. Min Kao 高民環博士]].
