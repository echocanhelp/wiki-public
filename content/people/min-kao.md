## Role in the Community
- Community memoir of the 東田納西台灣同鄉會 founding (張惠雄, 09/2017) records 高民環 as one of five UT students — 高民環、汪雅煌、陳耀宗、林正惠、李明輝 — who drove the 1974–75 push to form an independent, mother-tongue-speaking 台灣同鄉會 at the University of Tennessee in the white-terror era; the founding assembly drew 50–60 同鄉 on Thanksgiving Day 1975 at UT's Baptist Student Center. The memoirist notes that either 高民環 or 汪雅煌 first urged him to be founding 會長. Consistent with his UT Knoxville graduate enrollment already on this page; no date conflict, no HOLD — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys313|東田納西台灣同鄉會成立簡史]].
- Host community for the 1996 Plains Region Fall Camp in Overland Park, Kansas: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-351|Our Journeys 351 — Plains Region Fall Camp / 張錫清 / 01/2020]].
- Profiled twice in the TAH encyclopedia as community record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-min-kao|TAH #128 Min Kao 高民環]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories184|TAH #184 台美人的榮耀：高民環博士 / 蔡山慶 / 01/2015]].
