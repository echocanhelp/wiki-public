## Role in the Community
- 1984-07-14 — attended the founding meeting of the North American Taiwanese Medical Association (NATMA) at Western Michigan University, Ypsilanti, MI, as one of the Michigan-area participants (with Ying-san Chen); ~40 people from Cleveland, Greater New York, Chicago and elsewhere formally established NATMA at the meeting, with Dr. Tsung-yi Lin and Dr. Cheng-te Lin as invited speakers.
