# C. S. Huang (黃千洵)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** C. S. Huang
- **Chinese:** 黃千洵
- **Era:** 1960
- **Geography:** 台灣彰化員林
- **Core roles:** entrepreneur
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who


## Role in the Community
- 1984-07-14 — attended the founding meeting of the North American Taiwanese Medical Association (NATMA) at Western Michigan University, Ypsilanti, MI, as one of the Michigan-area participants (with Ying-san Chen); ~40 people from Cleveland, Greater New York, Chicago and elsewhere formally established NATMA at the meeting, with Dr. Tsung-yi Lin and Dr. Cheng-te Lin as invited speakers. See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys74-eng|Our Journeys 74 — NATMA founding]].
- Connected to the TAH first study-group reading record with Dr. Tu Chen and Dr. C. C. Shir: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/92-dr-tu-chen-dr-c-c-shir-and-dr-c-s-huang-e9-99-b3-e9-83-bd-e3-80-81-e7-9f-b3-e|First Study Group 讀書座談]]. HOLD: that record's Chinese title names 黃介山 while this page records 黃千洵 — same abbreviation "C. S. Huang", identity unresolved.
- HOLD: name collision — TAH #1800 "Dr. C. S. Huang" is 黃慶三博士 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-1800-c-s-huang|1800. Dr. C. S. Huang 黃慶三博士]]), a different person from 黃千洵 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whoswho1391|1391. C. S. Huang 黃千洵]]); do not merge.
- **RESOLVED (2026-10-01, deepen-x slice 09261000-10):** "C. S. Huang" in the corpus is **not one person**. The 2017-11-25 大洛杉磯台灣會館 horticulture lecture article names 黃慶三 as the Washington DC speaker "黃慶三老師" ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2017/20171206222455_2017_11_25_11-12月份大洛杉磯台灣會館園藝講座12月2日舉行_黃慶_fbab3a803b6ef48f|園藝講座 2017-11-25]]), and 黃慶三 also took the 1997 華府/巴城 TAA leadership handover from 陳桂鈐 listed in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys-138|138. 華府與巴城 TAA 的誕生]] as "Ching-San Huang". Neither belongs to this page's subject 黃千洵, who is Yale M.S. 音樂學, ran 紐文工作室, and is husband of 胡乃元. The horticulture writer and #1800 (Dr. 黃慶三博士) are **one person, not** this page's subject.
- **NEW (2026-10-08, deepen-x slice 10062218-6):** the name **黃介山** carries substantial Bay Area movement history in our memoirs, recorded here because record 92 titles him "Dr. C.S. Huang": he lectured 「台灣的戶口制度」at the first 讀書座談 (1972-08-25, 石清正家, San Jose), was one of the seven 籌備委員 of 台灣協志會 (1973-04-06, with 陳都、石清正、黃仲義、莊東榮、曾照雄、謝鐐暲), hosted the 北加州台灣同鄉聯合會 preparatory meeting at his home (1973-08-17), co-founded Mayflower Partnership and Pan Clair, drove the overnight 舊金山→西雅圖 trip to meet 康寧祥 (1974), served on the 1978 「Joint Committee of Taiwanese American for 1980 U.S. Census」, was a 北美洲台灣人基督教協會 (TCCCNA) founding preparatory committee member, acted in the 蓬萊歌劇團, and was the稿與錄音負責人 of 灣區台灣之音 (1979-04-01–1982-12-31). He appears on the ROC blacklist roster in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys106|Our Journeys #106]] alongside 黃介洋 (his brother, who married 李麗珍's sister). Sources: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys37|Our Journeys #37 協志會成立與大事記]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys38|Our Journeys #38]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys39|Our Journeys #39]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-350|Our Journeys #350 灣區台灣基督長老教會史料]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-357|Our Journeys #357]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-738|My Stories #738]].
- **NEW (2026-10-10, deepen-x slice 10091316-32):** 黃介山's 灣區「台灣之音」tenure is corroborated from the other side. The 紐約「台灣之音」memoirist writes that when his production's equipment broke in 1982-02 he 「拜託風評甚佳的舊金山灣區『台灣之音』主持人黃介山先生接手」and publicly announced the Bay Area would take over from that date — matching the 1979-04-01–1982-12-31 tenure above ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys2|Our Journeys #2]]). The same memoir names 黃介山 in connection with the 紐約「台灣之音」home phone wiretapped 33 months under successive court orders.
- **HOLD (2026-10-10):** a 2017-07-24 楊遠薰 essay on 海台青/美東台灣人夏令會 credits its photographs to 黃慶三 ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20211207123518_2017_07_24_海台青與黑客松-_-楊遠薰-2017-07-24_2c9cb76838702dd7|海台青與黑客松, 2017-07-24]]) — photo attribution only; the corpus never states this is the 1997 TAA officer / horticulture 黃慶三, and none of it pertains to this page's subject 黃千洵.
- **HOLD (2026-10-08):** 黃介山 as documented above (Cornell→Stanford researcher, 樹林 origin, wife 郭秋薰, Palo Alto churches, active 1972–1982 in the Bay Area) does not line up with this page's subject 黃千洵 (台大外文, Yale M.S. 音樂學, 紐文工作室, husband of [[people/nai-yuan-hu|胡乃元]]) — most plausibly a third distinct person behind the "C. S. Huang" abbreviation; never merge without an explicit corpus statement.
- **Open sub-question (HOLD):** the horticulture article and its 2023-12-08 retrospective ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2023/20231208120554_root_46c96aaab38ad7f2|蔬果園藝交流在美洲 index, 2023-12-08]]) give the blog handle `cshuang2`. Whether that handle belongs to the same 黃慶三 as the 1997 TAA officer above is unresolved — the corpus never states it, and TAA and 蔬果園藝交流在美洲 (Sunny Growers) are separate bodies. Held pending an explicit match.
