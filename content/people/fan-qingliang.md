# Fan Qingliang (范清亮)

**Fan Qingliang** (Chris Fan) is a Taiwanese American biotech entrepreneur known for co-founding Pacific Bioscience, a company that started with rabbit serum and grew into a successful biotechnology enterprise.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Chris Fan (Fan Qingliang)
- **Chinese:** 范清亮
- **Core roles:** Biotech entrepreneur, co-founder of Pacific Bioscience
- **Geography:** San Diego, California
- **Education:** National Taiwan University, Chemistry Department
- **Hobbies:** Basketball, card games, violin

## Overview

Fan Qingliang is one of four Taiwanese American entrepreneurs who co-founded Pacific Bioscience in San Diego. Their story, as told in the 2023 TAHS Publication, begins with raising rabbits and grew into a successful biotechnology company that contributed to the Taiwanese American community.

## Early Life and Education

Fan is a Hakka descendant from Taiwan. He attended National Revolutionary High School in Taipei and later studied chemistry at National Taiwan University. He was not only academically gifted but also excelled in sports, playing basketball, card games, and violin. Known for his friendly personality and leadership qualities, he was seen as a natural leader.

## Career

After graduating from university, while his peers were serving in the military, Fan was assigned to Songshan Airport as a cadet instructor. He taught violin in the evenings to earn extra income. His good fortune continued as he was able to avoid the harsh military training that others endured.

Fan co-founded Pacific Bioscience with three other entrepreneurs — Chen Qiushan, Wu Mingxian, and Lai Shucong. Their company started with raising rabbits and producing rabbit serum, which became the foundation for their biotechnology ventures. They went on to create three successful biotech companies.

## Legacy

Fan Qingliang's entrepreneurial journey from rabbit farming to biotechnology represents the innovative spirit of Taiwanese American immigrants. His story, as documented in the 2023 TAHS Publication, highlights the intersection of scientific innovation, community building, and cultural identity.
