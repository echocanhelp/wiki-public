# Gail Lee (李秀文)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Gail Lee
- **Chinese:** 李秀文
- **Era:** -
- **Geography:** 台灣
- **Core roles:** professor, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Gail Hsiu-Wen Lee’s passion to bring live classical music to Central New Jersey was realized in 2008 when she founded Sinfonietta Nova, West Windsor’s own symphony orchestra. Now in her 5th Season as Artistic Director and Conductor, Ms. Lee’s ideas of presenting not only audience favorites, but also lesser known musical gems, new works, plus educational elements have met with critical acclaim and audience delight.
