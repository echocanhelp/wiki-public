## Role in the Community

- 劉德勇 served on the church-building delegation of the Washington D.C. Taiwanese church community: per the memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys217|217. 我參與了華府教會的建堂 (陳明賢)]], he joined 王能祥、蔡武男、凌光雄、王偉國長老 and the author in negotiations with 世界和平協會董事長王清珠女士, which secured a donation of three plots of land and cash split between the church and Bethesda長老教會 (the Rockville plot later rose sharply in value through urban renewal).
- He also supported the community's historical record: per [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys270|270. 早期留學北美的台灣女士–戰後初期 (朱真一)]], 柯大闢博士、劉德勇博士及劉克全先生 donated reference works to the project — 《蕭安居牧師-生平及其家譜》《春風化雨》《永遠的劉瑞山》.
- Linked in the church memoir alongside 陳建南 and 凌光雄 as part of the early D.C. Taiwanese church network.
