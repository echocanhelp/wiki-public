## Role in the Community
- Author of the biography collection 二十世紀台灣代表性人物(一) (2001, 傳記), held in the corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/407-e4-ba-8c-e5-8d-81-e4-b8-96-e7-b4-80-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e4-bb-a3-e8-a1-a8-e6-8|二十世紀台灣代表性人物(一)]].
- Prolific TAH essayist on Taiwanese musicians and writers: profiled 女高音陳麗嬋 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories312|312. 台灣的世紀女高音－陳麗嬋]], 2015/07), violinist 胡乃元 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories478|478. 第一位贏得伊莉莎白皇后大賽的台灣小提琴家胡乃元]], 2016/11), and memorialized writer 郭松棻 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories420|420. 一位永遠望鄉的理想主義台灣作家－懷念郭松棻]], 2016/03).
- A community memoir records him traveling from 花蓮 to Akron to witness the US performance of 蕭泰然's D調小提琴協奏曲 (world premiere 1992-11-13 by 林昭亮), calling it a milestone of Taiwanese music abroad; the memoir notes he wrote the essay 「我有一個夢」 in 1983 and has since alternated between 「做夢的人」 and making the dream come true for Taiwanese culture: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys269|ourjourneys269 memoir]].
- Profiled in the TA Archives as a famous writer: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/20-dr-hon-tze-lin-e6-9e-97-e8-a1-a1-e5-93-b2-e9-86-ab-e5-b8-ab-famous-writer-e8-|20. Dr. Hon-Tze Lin 林衡哲醫師 / Famous writer]]. HOLD: conflict on English romanization Jer-Shung vs Hon-Tze for the same person — never auto-merged; TAH Who's Who slug uses Jer-Shung.
