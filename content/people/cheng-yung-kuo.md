## Role in the Community

- Working photographer and art director (KCY Art & Photo Studio, 2005–2017; Manager of Marketing Design at Hydro, Inc., 2014–2017), trained at The School of the Art Institute of Chicago (BFA Studio Arts/Photography/Ceramics 2007; MFA Studio Art/Photography 2009).
- **Community leadership (Taiwanese chambers of commerce, Chicago)** — this corrects the earlier characterization of him as purely artist/professional-cohort:
  - 2013 — President, Junior Businessmen section, Chicago Taiwanese-American Chamber of Commerce (芝加哥台美商會青商部會長)
  - 2015 — President-General, Junior Businessmen section, Federation of Taiwan Chambers of Commerce in North America (北美洲台灣商會聯合總會青商部總會長)
  - 2017 — Board member, Chicago Taiwanese-American Chamber of Commerce (芝加哥台美商會理事)
- Died in an accident in 2017. His wife 黃聖家 credits him in the Chicago JC feature [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/820-e8-8a-9d-e5-8a-a0-e5-93-a5-e9-9d-92-e5-95-86-e6-bd-9b-e5-8a-9b-e4-b9-8b-e6-9|820. 芝加哥青商潛力之星黃聖家打造理財一片天 (01/2022)]] as "一位非常優秀的青年僑領" and her chief inspiration entering the insurance/financial-planning field — she took over the family's financial-planning practice after his passing.
- Corpus record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-cheng-yung-kuo|1610. Cheng-Yung Kuo 郭正雍]] (TAH Who's Who, 2017-04).
