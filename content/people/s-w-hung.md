## Role in the Community
- Listed in the TAH Foundation Who's Who profile record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whoswho1067|1068. S. W. Hung 洪順伍 / 2016/06]] (2016/06).
- **Name variant:** corpus memoirs consistently write **洪順五** (page frontmatter: 洪順伍); treated as the same person.
- President of **台灣協志會** (Bay Area Taiwanese Alliance for Interculture) circa the Formosa Incident: within a week of the 1979-12-10 Kaohsiung incident he went to New York and, without a general-assembly vote, signed the society into the newly formed **臺灣建國聯合陣線** (Coalition of Taiwan Independence) — making 協志會 a KMT-designated "rebel organization," with members' return visas harassed and many blacklisted ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys37|37. 台灣協志會成立與大事記]] · [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys37-eng|English version]] · [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-357|357. 舊金山灣區臺灣人的社團活動與家國意識之形成]]).
- DPP West Coast Committee Chairperson (民進黨美西主委) among the society's alumni leadership ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys38|38. 北加州台灣同鄉聯合會三十年回顧]]).
- **23rd president of the Taiwanese American Federation of Northern California**: barred from returning to Taiwan at Taoyuan airport on 1986-11-30 with Hsin-liang Hsu, sparking a police–civilian confrontation ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys38|38. 北加州台灣同鄉聯合會三十年回顧]]); led thousands of Taiwanese Americans in San Francisco street protests against China's 1996 Keelung missile launches on March 9 and 16 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys38|38. 北加州台灣同鄉聯合會三十年回顧]]); see also [[organizations/taiwanese-american-federation-of-n-california|TAFNC]].
- Co-founded **AnaSpec** (peptide-synthesis reagents) in 1993 with wife 傅紅玉; noted for contributions to the global fight against SARS ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys19|19. 在矽谷創業臺美人]]).
- Also remembered as a Penglai Opera Troupe performer in the 協志會 satires that became visa-interrogation pretexts for members ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys39|39. 蓬萊歌劇團]]); listed among 協志會 presidents who were blacklisted ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys106|106. Political Activities of Taiwanese Americans: 2, 黑名單]]).
