## Third-party community record

- 2006-05-01 — 楊宜宜牧師 (Eileen Chang) received a phone call from 「加州愛恩教會張玉明主任牧師(我前神學院教授)」 reporting that when second-generation youth went back to Taiwan to teach elementary and middle-school children English for a week, 75% of the children came to faith, and that Pastor Chang 「非常期盼我在美東推動『台灣宣教』」. The English section of the same document renders him as `Joseph Chang, senior pastor of Evangelical Formosan Church of Irvine in California, who was also my former seminary professor` — `sourced`（third-party bylined account：`knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/73-%e3%80%8c%e5%8f%b0%e7%81%a3%e5%ae%a3%e6%95%99%e5%9f%ba%e9%87%91%e6%9c%83%e3%80%8d%e7%b7%a3%e8%b5%b7-%e6%a5%8a%e5%ae%9c%e5%ae%9c%e7%89%a7%e5%b8%ab-200802.md`, bylined 楊宜宜牧師, dated 2-17-2008, posted to the TAH corpus 2014/12; work page [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/73-e3-80-8c-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-ae-a3-e6-95-99-e5-9f-ba-e9-87-91-e6-9c-83-e3-80|TAH #73]]）. The 75% figure is his own claim as relayed by her — one report, so `sourced` as to *what she said he said*, not as to the rate itself.
- End of January 2007 — 「一月底加州愛恩教會的張玉明牧師、莊澤豐牧師及林華山醫師討論的結果是：『妳應該在美東自己設立一個組織去推動，因為我們是一個教會。我們會繼續當妳的夥伴。』」 (EN: `a discussion between Pastor Joseph Chang, Pastor Tse Feng Chuang, and Dr. Howshan Lin of the California EFC Irvine Church`) — the three advised Chang to found her own East-coast organization and offered to stay partners. This is the dated event that led to 台灣宣教基金會 being incorporated 2007-04-24 and formally founded 2007-04-25 — `sourced`（third-party bylined account：same archive file, same author）. Co-named: [[people/tse-feng-chuang|莊澤豐牧師]]; 林華山醫師 (Dr. Howshan Lin) has no person page on this box yet.
- Church-name identification — the self-report's employer `台福爾灣基督教會` and this document's `加州愛恩教會` / `EFC Irvine` are read as the same Irvine congregation, `Evangelical Formosan Church of Irvine 愛恩台福基督教會`, listed in the TAH 宗教社團 roster at `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/pages/religious-org.md:31` under `Evangelical Formosan Church 台福基督教會` — `inferred`（two renderings of one Irvine congregation, reconciled against the roster line; no document states the equivalence）.
