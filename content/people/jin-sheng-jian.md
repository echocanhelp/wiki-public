## Role in the Community

簡金生 was an active organizer in the Taiwanese independence movement on US campus and in Los Angeles, remembered across several community memoirs; he is noted as deceased in those accounts.

- 1965-10-29~30 — as a University of Wisconsin member, attended the secretive 結盟大會 (21 signatories, oath and secrecy measures against KMT infiltration); attended with 陳昭星、利騰俊、許貞雄、賴松齡、黃啟明、林建一、田弘茂、洪銘水 et al. The memoir records him, 利騰俊 and 黃啟明 as "今俱已去世" — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys81|Our Journeys #81]]
- Nov 16 — joined 賴文雄 in the 全美台灣獨立聯盟 "自由長征" cross-country recruiting drive (east route reinforcement after 張燦鍙/陳榮成 and 羅福全 columns); the drive multiplied 《台灣通訊》 mailing lists from 400 to 4,000 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys234|Our Journeys #234]]
- 1980 — served as 總幹事 of the LA thousand-guest Kennedy fundraising dinner ($100,000 goal; 王桂榮 couple as liaisons, 王興宗 assisting) — the memoir calls him "已故的簡金生" — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys59|Our Journeys #59]]
- 1982-01-21 — among the 16 許英智 convened to found TACL (Taiwanese American Citizens League); nominated to the six-person 籌備小組 with 楊嘉猷、洪肇奎、周實、曾克中 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys272|Our Journeys #272]]
- Also named as the 1978 entry in a year-by-year leadership roster in memoir #378 (1976 李明雄 / 1979 王圭雄; organization context not stated in excerpt) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-378|Our Journeys #378]]
- TAH Who's Who record, 2019-11 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-2229|2229. Jin-Sheng Jian 簡金生/11/2019]]
