## Role in the Community

Earlier sweeps (2026-09-25/26) found only the own record in content/works + articles; the 2026-10-08 vault sweep surfaced memoir material below.
- **Memoir corpus (2026-10-08, deepen-x 10070600-23):** Kaohsiung Medical classmate 鄭瑞雄 recalls in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories216|mystories216 「選病理科當飯碗」 (2015/03)]] that 張簡俊一 was an internal-medicine resident at Jamaica Hospital (New York) and recommended 鄭瑞雄 for his first-year contract, then moved "隔年一月" to a nearby VA hospital for internal residency — consistent with the Brooklyn VA Medical Center 1970 residency in the TAH table. 張簡's wife was 鄭's wife's 台南女中 classmate, who looked after the couple in New York.
- **Chicago years:** in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories547|mystories547 「出故鄉台灣之路」 (陳欽明, 05/2017)]], 張簡俊一夫婦 hosted 陳欽明 in Chicago during his 1970s emigration transit; 陳 also recalled receiving 張簡's airmail letters from Chicago while he himself worked at 台療 — corroborating Chicago-based practice before the Berwyn Heights move.
- **Medical-career panelist, TAA/Washington Chapter:** listed as 張簡俊一醫師（內科/預防醫學/心臟病/代謝症候群/糖尿病/高血壓/血脂肪異常）in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taagwc-newsletter|the TAA/Washington Chapter newsletter]] — consistent with the Berwyn Heights, MD internal-medicine practice in the TAH employment table.
- HOLD / disambiguation (deepen-x 2026-10-07, slice 10052007-18): the compound surname 張簡 recurs in the memoir corpus as **張簡吉誠醫師 (Cleveland)** — a founding participant of the Cleveland Taiwanese Medical Association (1983, 楓林閣) per [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys74|Our Journeys 74]], and preacher of the "Get Yourself Connected" TAF sermon in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/senior-taiwanese-american-36|Senior Taiwanese American 36]]. No corpus evidence links him to Dr. Tsunie Chanchien 張簡俊一; never merge without confirmation.
