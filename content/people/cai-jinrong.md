# Cai Jinrong (蔡錦榮)

**Cai Jinrong** (蔡錦榮) is a Taiwanese American entrepreneur. Born in 1938 in Shihwa, Jinsha Township, Tainan County, he grew up in a large family of farmers and went on to build a successful life in the United States.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Cai Jinrong
- **Chinese:** 蔡錦榮
- **Born:** 1938, Shihwa, Jinsha Township, Tainan County, Taiwan
- **Core roles:** Entrepreneur, community figure
- **Geography:** United States (Taiwanese American)
- **Family:** Parents Cai Zhaoyi and Liu Lianzhi; 4 younger brothers, 2 sisters

## Overview

Cai Jinrong's journey from a rural Taiwanese farming family to a successful life in America exemplifies the immigrant experience. His story, as told in the 2023 TAHS Publication, is one of perseverance, family values, and the pursuit of the American Dream.

## Early Life

Cai was born in 1938 in Shihwa, Jinsha Township, Tainan County. He was the eldest of eight children. His father, Cai Zhaoyi, was a farmer who owned only two small plots of land. To make ends meet, his father worked various jobs including street vending, shoe-making, and even practicing dentistry without a license. His mother, Liu Lianzhi, was from another township in Tainan County and worked as a real estate agent after moving to Tainan City. She died of cervical cancer that spread to the lungs when Cai was 64.

## Immigration and Career

In 1939, when Cai was one year old, the Second World War broke out. His family's story continued through the post-war era and eventually led to immigration to the United States.

## Legacy

Cai Jinrong's story represents the broader narrative of Taiwanese immigration to the United States. His family's journey from rural Taiwan to American success is a testament to the values of hard work and perseverance.
