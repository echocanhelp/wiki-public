## Role in the Community

- The silicon-valley movement memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys19||Our Journeys #19]] records him as one of four founders of ECAD in 1982 (Electronic Design Automation), listed 1987 stock listing, later merged with SDA into Cadence, and a 1990 re-founding of Pic Design Systems which merged with Quickturn and listed in 1994. The memoir calls him a 發展EDA的元老 (EDA pioneer).
- This matches the TAH profile's 益華電腦 1982-1987 創辦人 entry once read as ECAD (Cadence's Chinese name) rather than a separate company.
