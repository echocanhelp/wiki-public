# Shawn Yeh (葉上毅)

**Shawn Yeh (葉上毅)** is listed as **個人董事** (individual director) on the official board of the [[organizations/taiwan-center|Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館)]] (board page archived 2026-07-16 as `taiwancenter-org-board.md`). He also appears in Taiwan Justice's coverage of the foundation's 2024 anniversary gala. This page records those sourced board facts and organization context already in the vault; it does not invent a personal biography, employer, or profession.

## Overview

Taiwan Center (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會; TWCLA) is a **501(c)(3)** nonprofit (EIN **95-4679702**) at 901 S. Fremont Ave. #118, Alhambra, CA 91803 — education, arts, and community service for Taiwanese Americans in Southern California; the site claims **800+** permanent individual members and **50+** group members. The public board table (archive 2026-07-16) names **27** directors; Yeh appears under **個人董事**, not under officers (董事長 [[people/alan-thian|Alan Thian (田詒鴻)]]、執行長 [[people/simon-lin|Simon Lin (林榮松)]]、副董事長 [[people/roger-tsai|Roger Tsai (蔡漢成)]] / [[people/linda-liu|Linda Liu (劉玲華)]] / [[people/jolie-chenn|Jolie Chenn (陳芃佑)]] / [[people/shu-lai|Shu Lai (賴淑遠)]] / [[people/tony-lee|Tony Lee (李賢群)]]、秘書長 [[people/ken-wu|Ken Wu (吳兆峯)]]、副秘書長 [[people/ray-jan|Ray Jan (冉瑞宜)]]、財務長 [[people/lisa-liu|Lisa Liu (劉雅薇)]]).
