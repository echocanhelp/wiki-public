## Role in the Community

- **Artist file in the movement record:** two TAH encyclopedia records — artist profile #7 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/artist7-echo-lew|TAH #7, 2014-09-05]]) and Who's Who #223 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos223-echo-lew|TAH #223, 2015-01-20]]); exhibition catalogue 『See the Light 希望之光』(2006/08) held as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/496-see-the-light-e5-b8-8c-e6-9c-9b-e4-b9-8b-e5-85-89-e5-8a-89-e7-99-bd200608-e7|TAH #496]].
- **Exhibitions documented in the corpus:** 南加棕櫚泉藝博會 with 陳文石 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/art-show-11|TAH #11, 2017-02-20]]); 「Time. Timeless」 with 陳宏文/Chenhung Chen at OCCCA ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/art-exhibitions-42|TAH art-exhibitions #42, 2019-09-14]]) — corroborating the Orange County Center For Contemporary Art instructor role in the employment table.
- **International exchange connector (primary account):** 台灣美術文化交流協會 memoir 楊平猷 records that 劉白 helped invite La artcore and the West Coast Drawing association (plus 澳洲蔡碧桃, 日本侯飛月) into the association's 2021 「後藝情國際交流展」 at 港區文化中心 — placing him as a bridge between the Taiwanese and US artist communities ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-371|Our Journeys #371, 2021-06-08, band A]]).
