## Identity resolution — Jeng = Jenq (third-party, 2026-11-17)
The English romanization on the TAH self-report (`Jeng`) differs from the romanization this
engineer used in his own IEEE publications (`Jenq`). The identification rests on four
independently printed corroboration points, all archived under
`knowledge/web-archives/prof-y-c-jeng-thirdparty-2026-11-17/`; treat the match as
**strong but inferential** — no single document co-prints the hanzi 鄭逸群 with `Yih-Chyun Jenq`.

- 2026-11-17 — Education ladder matches row-for-row: The Mathematics Genealogy Project record
  (MGP ID 74434, retrieved 2026-11-17, `mathgenealogy-74434.html`) prints
  `Yih-Chyun Jenq — Ph.D. Princeton University 1976`, dissertation *Analysis and Design of
  Digital Data Transmission Systems*, advisors Bede Liu and John B. Thomas. The TAH form prints
  Princeton Ph.D. 1976 and M.S. 1974 with no advisor/dissertation
  (`knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/person/prof-y-c-jeng.json`). Wikidata

  Math-Genealogy ID 74434 — the Wikidata item is machine-derived from the MGP record, so it is a
