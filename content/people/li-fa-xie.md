## Role in the Community
- Painter, novelist, and art historian of the Taiwanese movement generation; his own writings are held in the corpus:
  - 《珍重！阿笠》 (1977-07, LIFE) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/632-e7-8f-8d-e9-87-8d-e9-98-bf-e7-ac-a0-e8-ac-9d-e9-87-8c-e6-b3-95197707life-e7-|632. 珍重!阿笠 / 1977-07]]
  - 《廿世紀台灣畫壇名家作品集 第一冊》 / The Twentieth Century Taiwanese Paintings Volume One (1983-12) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1181|1181. 廿世紀台灣畫壇名家作品集 第一冊 / 1983-12]]
  - 《我所看到的上一代》 (1999, 傳記) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/300-e6-88-91-e6-89-80-e7-9c-8b-e5-88-b0-e7-9a-84-e4-b8-8a-e4-b8-80-e4-bb-a3-e8-a|300. 我所看到的上一代 / 1999]]
- His novel 《紫色大稻埕》 was adapted into a 三立電視臺 drama in 2016; a corpus essay on Bay Area Taiwanese community building cites the drama's 望海山莊 scene as an expression of overseas Taiwanese identity ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-357|our-journeys-357]]).
- Profiled in 《我的心路歷程—畫家謝里法》 by 劉玉山 (2018-03) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories633|633. 我的心路歷程-畫家謝里法 / 2018-03]]; TAH encyclopedia entry — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/20972|653. Li–Fa Xie 謝里法 / 2015-10]].
