# Dr. James L. Maxwell (馬偕博士)

## Identity Snapshot

- **Full name:** George Leslie Macdonald (馬偕)
- **Born:** 1838, Ontario, Canada
- **Died:** 1901
- **Role:** Presbyterian medical missionary, evangelist, educator
- **Mission field:** Northern Taiwan (1872–1901)
- **Known as:** 馬偕博士 (Dr. Maxwell)
- **Legacy:** Founded STM Taiwan, 偕醫中學 (Ochong Teachers College), and the Presbyterian Church in Taiwan

**Dr. George Leslie Macdonald** (馬偕, 1838–1901), commonly known in Chinese as **馬偕博士** (Dr. Maxwell), was a Canadian Presbyterian missionary who came to northern Taiwan in 1872 and spent the last 29 years of his life serving the Taiwanese people.

## Overview

馬偕博士 was a medical missionary, evangelist, and educator who made a profound impact on Taiwan. He arrived in Taipei (Tamsui) on August 23, 1872, and established churches, schools, and clinics throughout northern Taiwan. His work laid the foundation for what would become the [[organizations/presbyterian-church-in-taiwan|Presbyterian Church in Taiwan]].

## Key Contributions

- **Medical Mission:** Trained in medicine and brought medical care to underserved communities. Established the first Western medicine clinic in northern Taiwan.
- **Education:** Founded schools including the now 偕醫中學 (Ochong Teachers College, now part of TNU) and the Tamsui Theological College (now Taiwan Theological College and Seminary, STM).
- **Evangelism:** Established over 60 churches across northern Taiwan, baptizing more than 1,000 converts.
- **Language:** Learned the local language and wrote dictionaries and grammars to facilitate communication and translation of the Bible.
- **Community Service:** Built hospitals, orphanages, and provided relief during natural disasters and epidemics.

## Legacy

馬偕博士's 150th anniversary of missionary work in Taiwan was commemorated in 2022, including special worship services such as the 馬偕博士台灣宣教150週年紀念主日 hosted by [[organizations/good-shepherd-taiwanese-presbyterian-church|Good Shepherd Taiwanese Presbyterian Church]] on March 6, 2022.

His legacy continues through the many institutions he founded, including STM Taiwan (where [[people/mingyuan-hsu|Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu]] studied), and the Presbyterian Church in Taiwan.
