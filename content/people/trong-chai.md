# Trong Chai (蔡同榮)

**Trong Chai** (蔡同榮) was a Taiwanese-American political activist and the **founding president of the Formosan Association for Public Affairs (FAPA)**, serving 1982–1983 per FAPA's official presidents roster. He testified at the 1982 House Asian Subcommittee hearing on Taiwan's martial law in FAPA's founding year.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English name:** Trong Chai (also romanized Trong-Rong Tsai / Thio̍k Tâng-îng)
- **Chinese name:** 蔡同榮
- **Role:** Founding president, FAPA (1982–1983)
