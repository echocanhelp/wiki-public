# 葉英堃博士

## Role in the Community

Pioneer of Taiwanese psychiatric medicine (台灣精神醫療的開拓者): founding director of 台北市立療養院 and professor at 台大醫學系 / 台北醫學大學精神科主任 (per TAH Who's Who tables). The TAH Foundation corpus preserves his record independently of the press-kit listing:

- Dedicated biography: 吳佳璇,《台灣精神醫療的開拓者: 葉英堃傳記》(2005/08, Biography) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications301|301. 台灣精神醫療的開拓者: 葉英堃傳記]]
- TAH story-corpus entry #212, published 2015-01-19 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/212-e8-91-89-e8-8b-b1-e5-a0-83|212. 葉英堃/2015/01]]
- Co-author of 戴正德 主編《新時代的醫學人文》(2017/04) with 朱真一, 成令方 et al. — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1085|1085. 新時代的醫學人文]]
