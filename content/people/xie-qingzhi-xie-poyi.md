# Xie Qingzhi & Xie Poyi (謝清志、謝泊怡)

**Xie Qingzhi** (謝清志) is a Taiwanese American aerospace engineer, and **Xie Poyi** (謝泊怡) is a Taiwanese American medical doctor. Together with Xie's wife Cai Shumin (a nutrition specialist), they represent a family of professional achievement and deep commitment to Taiwan.

## Identity Snapshot

- **Xie Qingzhi:** Aerospace engineer, PhD in aerospace engineering from University of Michigan
- **Xie Poyi:** Medical doctor, specialist in muscle and nerve medicine
- **Xie Qingzhi's wife:** Cai Shumin (nutrition specialist)
- **Born:** 1942 (Xie Qingzhi), Tainan, Taiwan
- **Geography:** Southern California / Taiwan
- **Core roles:** Aerospace engineer, medical doctor, family

## Overview

The Xie family represents three generations of professional achievement and unwavering commitment to Taiwan. Dr. Xie Qingzhi, an aerospace expert who worked for Rockwell International and NASA's Aerospace Corporation, made the significant sacrifice of returning to Taiwan in 1995 to contribute to the country's space program. His son, Dr. Xie Poyi, is a medical specialist who continues the family's tradition of service.

## Career

### Xie Qingzhi
- 1965: Graduated from National Cheng Kung University, Department of Civil Engineering
- 1968: Master's degree in Civil Engineering from National Taiwan University
- 1973: Master's in Computer Information and Control from University of Michigan
- 1975: PhD in Aerospace Engineering from University of Michigan
- 1976-1979: Engineer at Rockwell International (navigation control and positioning analysis)
- 1979-1995: Engineer at Aerospace Corporation in California
- 1995: Returned to Taiwan to work for the National Science Council, sacrificing a stable career with retirement benefits

### Xie Poyi
- Medical doctor specializing in muscle and nerve medicine
- Accepted an interview at the TAHS office in October 2022

## Legacy

The Xie family's story demonstrates the depth of commitment to Taiwan among overseas Chinese professionals. Despite the personal cost — including financial sacrifice and the challenge of caring for family members — they chose to contribute their expertise to Taiwan's development. Their story is a testament to the values of service and patriotism.
