# Wanying Zhang aka "Heather"

**Community relevance (Tier E — living memory of transnational repression, CONTROL §1b):** This page is the community's **current-history event record** of the 2025–2026 FBI case alleging a PRC-directed surveillance operation on U.S. soil targeting the family of a senior Taiwanese official — the first such prosecution of its kind reaching the Taiwanese American community directly. It is **not a biography of the accused**. All facts below are **allegations** from the criminal complaint and affidavit; the defendant is presumed innocent until proven guilty.

## Identity Snapshot

- **Era:** Contemporary (case: June 2025 – Oct 2026)
- **Geography:** Irvine, California (Central District of CA); alleged travel: Changsha/Hangzhou, China → Seattle, Washington
- **Core roles (per complaint):** Licensed California real estate agent (JC Pacific Capital Inc.); naturalized U.S. citizen; defendant in *United States v. Wanying Zhang, aka "Heather"* (18 U.S.C. § 951)

## Case Record (allegations only)

- **Charge:** Acting as an unregistered agent of a foreign government, 18 U.S.C. § 951. Criminal complaint filed by FBI counterintelligence SA Kevin Holt (complaint dated 2026-10-03); arrest warrant executed **2026-10-04** at LAX as she prepared to board a flight to Shanghai ([DOJ press release](https://www.publicnow.com/view/55C8FFCD87B282B1DE78A7A986438B8A5D0F969E), [signed complaint/affidavit PDF](https://i.ntd.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id1177009-Zhang.Complaint_SK-Signed.pdf)).
- **Alleged tasking:** June 2025 trip to China; private-room meeting in **Changsha** where a surveillance mission was allegedly accepted ("I can take care of this thing"). A classmate from her alma mater, the **University of International Relations (UIR)** in Beijing — described in the affidavit as run by / closely affiliated with the MSS, which "recruits heavily" there — introduced her to "PRC Official 1"; "PRC Official 2" took over tasking from June 2025.
- **Alleged operation:** **2025-09-01**, Zhang and "Individual 1" flew Las Vegas → Seattle, rented a black Nissan Rogue, and surveilled the suburban home of a close relative of a high-ranking Taiwanese official. A neighbor's security camera recorded them "holding their phones out" filming the family arriving home. She allegedly sent her handler photos, video, boarding-pass photos, and two license plate numbers, with messages "Saw him, young male, it's the young one" and "I am videorecording." Returned to LA without incident.
- **Target identity:** The complaint names only "the Victim." Law enforcement sources told CBS News the target was **Lai Ting-yu (賴廷與)**, eldest son of President [[people/lai-ching-te|Lai Ching-te (賴清德)]], Seattle-area engineer (Case Western BSEE 2012, Johns Hopkins MSEE 2013, UW PhD bioengineering; Philips). CBS matched the security-camera images to a near-Seattle address owned by Ting-Yu.
- **Second target (suspected):** Per the TOC-reported affidavit (2026-10-07), FBI suspects she may also have been tasked on an **Irvine address linked to a vocal anti-CCP Taiwanese elected official** (redacted; SA Holt: "possible" PRC Official 1 tasked this at their January meeting).
- **Court posture:** Initial appearance **2026-10-05**, U.S. District Court, downtown Los Angeles, before Judge Steve Kim — no plea entered; remanded to U.S. Marshals; detention hearing set for Wednesday. Statutory maximum if convicted: 10 years. FBI Director Kash Patel on X: "Foreign adversaries take note: America is NOT your playground."
- **Tradecraft note (prosecution):** First Assistant U.S. Attorney Bill Essayli: she "never notified the Attorney General … a federal offense," used counter-surveillance tradecraft, and sent real-time updates to handlers.
