# Mei Shing Wang (王美幸)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Mei Shing Wang
- **Chinese:** 王美幸
- **Era:** 1944
- **Geography:** 台灣
- **Core roles:** artist
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Ms. Mei-Shing Wang was born in Taipei in 1944 and graduated from The Art Department of National Taiwan Normal University in 1968. She has held over twenty individual as well as united exhibitions in both Taiwan and U.S. The main mediums used in her paintings are oil paint and chalk, with flowers as the main subject in many of her paintings. Ms. Wang has also started creating art through glass fusing a few years ago. She continues to travel the world creating art, and of the few are works based on the beautiful scenery of Taiwan’s East Coast. Her pieces are often donated to fundraising charities.
