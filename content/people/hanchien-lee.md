# Hanchien Lee (李函蒨博士)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Hanchien Lee
- **Chinese:** 李函蒨博士
- **Era:** Birth Place
- **Geography:** Taiwan
- **Core roles:** pianist, professor, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Pianist Hanchien Lee has established herself as a successful soloist, recitalist, and chamber musician. Since her debut with the Philadelphia Orchestra at age sixteen, she has performed throughout North America, Europe and Asia, appearing at venues such as Steinway Hall in New York, Academy of Music, Kimmel Center in Philadelphia and Joseph Meyerhoff Symphony Hall in Baltimore, and has given recitals at the Philadelphia Chamber Music Society, Muhlenberg College Piano Series, Hillman Performing Arts Center, Texas International Piano Festival, Basilica San Pietro in Italy, Centro Cultural del Antiguo in Spain and Taipei Convention Center in Taiwan. She has appeared as soloist with the American Elite youth orchestra, Plainfield Symphony, Taiwan National Orchestra, Chamber Orchestra of Perugia and Poland’s Capella Cracovienses Chamber Orchestra.

## Timeline
- Debut as a soloist with the Philadelphia Orchestra at age sixteen.
- Trained at the Curtis Institute of Music (B.A.), then Yale University (Master of Music and Artists’ Diploma), then the Peabody Conservatory of the Johns Hopkins University (Ph.D.).
- Built a career as soloist, recitalist, and chamber musician across North America, Europe, and Asia — including Steinway Hall (New York), Academy of Music and Kimmel Center (Philadelphia), Joseph Meyerhoff Symphony Hall (Baltimore), Philadelphia Chamber Music Society, Muhlenberg College Piano Series, Hillman Performing Arts Center, Texas International Piano Festival, Basilica San Pietro (Italy), Centro Cultural del Antiguo (Spain), and Taipei Convention Center (Taiwan).
- Appeared as soloist with the American Elite youth orchestra, Plainfield Symphony, Taiwan National Orchestra, Chamber Orchestra of Perugia, and Poland’s Capella Cracovienses Chamber Orchestra.
- Served on the piano faculty of the Peabody Preparatory of the Johns Hopkins University and of the University of Pennsylvania.
