# Dr. Victor Shen (沈新欽博士)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Dr. Victor Shen
- **Chinese:** 沈新欽博士
- **Era:** --
- **Geography:** Tainan, Taiwan
- **Core roles:** professor, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Victor Shen, a native of Taiwan, began studying music theory and voice at the age of 14. He has received several first prize awards in music competitions in Taiwan. He holds a B.M. in voice from the University of Chinese Culture, Taiwan, and a M.M. in choral conducting from the Westminster Choir College, in Princeton, New Jersey. He is also a DMA candidate in choral conducting at University of Maryland.

## Biography

沈新欽 (Victor Shen), a Tainan native, is the son of the artist 沈哲哉. He built a dual career as a choral conductor and music educator across Taiwan and the United States.

**Teaching.** He joined the faculty of the Department of Music at Tunghai University from 1992, later taught in the Music Department at Kaohsiung Normal University (where he became Professor), and held music faculty posts at the University of Chinese Culture, including Huakang Arts School and Tainan Home Economics Junior College. He was a guest professor at Dickinson University's Blue Mountain Music Summer Camp (2003) and a guest conductor and lecturer at the Beijing Conservatory of Music (1997).

**Conducting.** He founded the New Era Group (台中新世紀合唱文教中心) in Taichung as its music director and served as music director of the Bel Canto Opera Company and Bel Canto Opera Chorus in New York. His conducting posts have included the Taipei Philharmonic Chorus, Taipei Chin-Suey Chorus (金穗合唱團), Taiwan University Chorus, Festival Chorus of Central Taiwan Universities, Taiwanese Symphonic Chorus, Chinese-American Choral Ensemble, Rutgers University Queens Choral, Vox Nova (NJ), Xiaofong Arts Group, Chin Yun Chorus, Taiwanese Festival Chorus of Greater New York, Yue Yue Chorus (guest), Trenton Children's Chorus (from 2004), and the Greater Trenton Symphony Orchestra (guest). He also hosted a choral-music program on Taichung Classical Radio Station.
