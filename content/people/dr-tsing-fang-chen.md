# Dr. Tsing Fang Chen (陳錦芳博士)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Dr. Tsing Fang Chen
- **Chinese:** 陳錦芳博士
- **Era:** 1936
- **Geography:** Tainan, Taiwan
- **Core roles:** artist
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Dr. T.F. Chen — a painter, art historian, writer, philosopher, visionary, and founder of the T.F. Chen Cultural Center and Arts for Humanity Foundation in SoHo, NYC. In 2001, he was honored with the Global Tolerance Award from the United Nations and designated a Cultural Ambassador for Tolerance and Peace, for his lifetime of work dedicated to peace and for his art of multiculturalism. Dr. Chen’s “Arts for Humanity World Tour 2005-2015” travels the world promoting Art Education and a Global Culture of Peace.

## Career & Honors

From the TAH Who's Who profile text and tables above:

- Born 1936 in Tainan; painter, art historian, writer, and philosopher.
- Education: National Taiwan Univ. B.A. Foreign Languages; Univ. de Paris M.A. Literature; Univ. de Paris Ph.D. Art history (1970) — described in TAH encyclopedia entry #102 as the first Oriental Ph.D. holder in Art from Paris.
- Founder: T.F. Chen Cultural Center / 陳錦芳文化基金會 (T.F. Chen Cultural Foundation, Taiwan), and the [[organizations/new-world-art-center||NEW WORLD ART CENTER（紐約市陳錦芳文化館）]] in SoHo, NYC; founder of the Arts for Humanity Foundation, SoHo.
- 2001: honored with the Global Tolerance Award from the United Nations; designated Cultural Ambassador for Tolerance and Peace (United Nations / Friends of the United Nations) for a lifetime of work for peace and multicultural art.
- "Arts for Humanity World Tour 2005–2015": world tour promoting art education and a global culture of peace.
- Community service: Secretary-General, World Federation of Taiwanese Associations.
