# Dr. Tsing Fang Chen (陳錦芳博士)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Dr. Tsing Fang Chen
- **Chinese:** 陳錦芳博士
- **Era:** 1936
- **Geography:** Tainan, Taiwan
- **Core roles:** artist
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Dr. T.F. Chen — a painter, art historian, writer, philosopher, visionary, and founder of the T.F. Chen Cultural Center and Arts for Humanity Foundation in SoHo, NYC. In 2001, he was honored with the Global Tolerance Award from the United Nations and designated a Cultural Ambassador for Tolerance and Peace, for his lifetime of work dedicated to peace and for his art of multiculturalism. Dr. Chen’s “Arts for Humanity World Tour 2005-2015” travels the world promoting Art Education and a Global Culture of Peace.

## Career & Honors

From the TAH Who's Who profile text and tables above:

- Born 1936 in Tainan; painter, art historian, writer, and philosopher.
- Education: National Taiwan Univ. B.A. Foreign Languages; Univ. de Paris M.A. Literature; Univ. de Paris Ph.D. Art history (1970) — described in TAH encyclopedia entry #102 as the first Oriental Ph.D. holder in Art from Paris.
- Founder: T.F. Chen Cultural Center / 陳錦芳文化基金會 (T.F. Chen Cultural Foundation, Taiwan), and the [[organizations/new-world-art-center||NEW WORLD ART CENTER（紐約市陳錦芳文化館）]] in SoHo, NYC; founder of the Arts for Humanity Foundation, SoHo.
- 2001: honored with the Global Tolerance Award from the United Nations; designated Cultural Ambassador for Tolerance and Peace (United Nations / Friends of the United Nations) for a lifetime of work for peace and multicultural art.
- "Arts for Humanity World Tour 2005–2015": world tour promoting art education and a global culture of peace.
- Community service: Secretary-General, World Federation of Taiwanese Associations.

## Role in the Community

Corpus material shows Dr. Chen as an active participant in the Taiwanese American movement, not only a celebrated artist:

- **Radio Formosa (台灣之音, NY):** Painter Chen donated his limited-edition print 「望鄉」at low price and dozens of copies of his recorded 「台灣史詩」tape to support the phone-in station's fund-raising, alongside Dr. 許登龍's family contributions. See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys2|Our Journeys #2]].
- **台灣四百年史詩 & mass meetings:** At a 美國南區台灣民衆大會 (southern-district mass meeting, undated in the memoir), the music epic 「台灣四百年史詩」 alternated music, narration, and Chen's **live recitation** of poetry; as World Federation representative he also presented the 世界台灣同鄉會 (WFTA) briefing at the meeting. See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys317|Our Journeys #317]].
- **台灣文藝沙龍:** In the mid-1970s, Chen co-founded the 台灣文藝沙龍 (Taiwan Literary Arts Salon) with 謝里法, 洪銘水 and others, per a memoir by the organizer. See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys70|Our Journeys #70]].
- **East-coast summer institutes (美東夏令會):** Invited as a featured speaker in the 1970s alongside 洪哲勝 and 李豐明. See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys338|Our Journeys #338]].
- **WFTA annual conferences:** As Secretary-General of the World Federation of Taiwanese Associations, Chen gave the 「台灣文化的展望」 talk and the WFTA introduction at international annual meetings with chapter leaders such as 郭榮桔. See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys283|Our Journeys #283]] and the [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/world-federation-of-taiwanese-associations-annual-conference|WFTA annual conference record]].
- **Patronage of younger artists:** Chen, printmaker 廖修平, and painter 謝里法 — the "New York SoHo / Paris trio" — established the 巴黎大獎 (Paris Prize) in Taiwan to mentor emerging artists. See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys284|Our Journeys #284]].
- **WFTA historiography:** Co-authored 「世界臺灣同鄉會（世臺會）簡史」 with 陳隆 and 盧榮杰, in 張炎憲 et al. (eds.), 《自覺與認同：1950–1990年海外臺灣人運動專輯》. See the footnote in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-357|Our Journeys #357]].
