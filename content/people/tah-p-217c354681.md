## Role in the Community

- His encyclopedia entry is held in the corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/637-e6-9e-97-e6-ad-a3-e5-89-9b-201509|637. 林正剛 / 2015/09]] (published 2015-09-27).
- Wife 林瑛惠 appears in the community record as a 華府客家鄉親: at the 2021 北美洲海外台灣人二二八紀念活動 she and their son 林冠宇 represented two generations together, reciting 「客語三行詩」 at the event — see [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2026/20260210064123_2021北美洲海外台灣人二二八紀念活動-台灣國家人權_28b0cc4e52acece2|2021 北美洲海外台灣人二二八紀念活動報導]]. This fits the family's 屏東萬巒 (Hakka-area) origin recorded above.
- A sustained Hakka cultural-leadership record in the DC/mid-Atlantic, corroborating the 華府客家 identity of the family:
  - 1994: won the TACEC 夏令會 拾音歌謠個人組 亞軍 singing 「一朶小紅花／日頭落山一點黃」 alongside wife 林瑛惠 (representing 維吉尼亞州), took the 客家話口譯比賽冠軍, and with 林瑛惠 represented in the 「台灣之夜」 獻唱 — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tacec-1994|TACEC 1994]] (full text in vault archive posts/tacec-1994.md).
  - 1996: 主講「客家音樂」 at the 美東客家同郷會 sessions co-hosted with TACEC at Delaware University — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tacec-1996|TACEC 1996]].
  - 1993: listed as 會長（美東）of the 全美台灣客家會 network in the acknowledgements of the 創刊號 — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publicationmagaine949|949. 全美台灣客家會 1993 年刊]].
  - 2015: serves as 顧問 on the 顧問團 of the 全美臺灣客家文化基金會 — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-hakka-culture-foundation-of-america|Taiwanese Hakka Culture Foundation of America]], whose 活動委員會 that year included 林瑛惠.
- Note: the engineer Identity Snapshot (GPO 電腦工程顧問) plus the above = the whole record; the community role is Hakka cultural advocacy, not press-kit biography.
