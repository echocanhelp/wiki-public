# Hsu Ling-yun (許凌雲)

**Hsu Ling-yun** (許凌雲; Tâi-lô: Khóo Lîng-hûn; given name 漢厚, courtesy name 凌雲, style 卓夫; 1862 – 14 September 1944) was a Qing-era *shengyuan* (秀才) from Watong (瓦硐), Baisha, Penghu; a Han-learning teacher; and an early Presbyterian Christian. He is known locally as the **Watong xiucai** (瓦硐秀才). His former home at Watong Village No. 51 is now the [[organizations/hsu-ling-yun-xiucai-memorial-hall|Hsu Ling-yun Xiucai Memorial Hall]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Salutation / common form:** 許凌雲秀才 / Hsu Ling-yun
- **Chinese:** 許凌雲（名漢厚，字凌雲，號卓夫）
- **Tâi-lô:** Khóo Lîng-hûn
- **Born / died:** 1862 (Tongzhi 1, lunar 6/29), Watong — 14 Sep 1944 (Shōwa 19)
- **Roles:** Qing *shengyuan*; Han academy teacher; Presbyterian educator (Tainan); deputy head of Dingshan-ao office (1897, one year)
- **Geography:** Watong, Baisha, Penghu ↔ Tainan

## Timeline

- **Ming Chongzhen era (family)** — Ancestors settled Watong; local gentry line.
- **c. 1871–1879** — Studied *Sanzijing* and Four Books under Zhao Jie (上瓦硐); then Zhu Xi commentaries and Five Classics; at 18 studied under Hsu Kuo-ying in Houliao.
- **1880** — Penghu *ting* exam, ranked 32nd (age 19).
- **1883** — Worked accounts at Ruihe cloth firm, Tainan.
- **1884** — Began teaching in Houliao; sat Tainan prefectural exam, failed.
- **1885–1892** — Taught in Watong, Dachikan, and Magong while sitting exams.
- **1889** — Married Chen Yin (陳吟 / 陳拎) of Zhenhai.
- **1891 or 1893** — Obtained *xiucai*. Yang Shih-yang’s *信仰偉人列傳* says Guangxu 17 (1891); Hsu Yu-ho’s research prefers Guangxu 19 (1893) Tainan exam under Gu Dao-xian.
- **1892** — Accepted Christianity (with elder brother Hsu Han-dun 許漢墩, who converted after medical care at a Christian pharmacy in Tainan).
- **1894** — Taught in Magong at Yeh Chao-hsien’s invitation; part-time director of Wenshi Academy (文石書院).
- **1895** — Treaty of Shimonoseki; Penghu under Japanese rule. PCT mission work in Penghu associated with William Campbell (甘為霖) and later evangelists in Chikan / Jiangmei / Watong.
- **1897** — Awarded Taiwan gentry badge (*紳士章*); 11 Jan became deputy head of Dingshan-ao office; left after one year when *ao* offices were abolished.
- **c. 1898–1916** — Taught ~18 years at Tainan Presbyterian middle school, girls’ school, and seminary. Noted pupils include Lin Yen-chen (林燕臣) and Lin Mosei (林茂生).
- **1918** — Family tutor to Lin Lieh-tang of Wufeng Lin family (one year).
- **1919** — Tutor to Kao Tsai-chu of Gangshan Kao family.
- **1920–1928** — Returned to Watong; taught Han literature at *Cunyangxuan* (存養軒), then at Watong Christian church (eight years in the village).
- **1933** — Lin Hsien-tang and Cai Peihuo visited; Lin’s diary notes Hsu, already blind, came to talk of old times (he had been tutor to Lin’s elder brother twenty years earlier).
- **1944-09-14** — Died, age 83.

## Penghu church

Before the 1911 Watong church building, gatherings used borrowed rooms, including Hsu Han-dun’s house. Hsu Ling-yun later set his village classroom in the church. The Hsu brothers are treated as important early figures in Penghu Presbyterian history.

## Family

Married Chen Yin (1889). Children: Hsu Yu (長女), Hsu Shui-chin (長子), Hsu Shui-tsung (三子), Hsu Kan (次女), Hsu Hsi-chou (四子). Second son Hsu Shui-chen died at five. Fourth son Hsu Hsi-chou returned from Tainan commerce to care for his blind father; his Magong watch shop (精巧堂, 1941) was destroyed in Allied bombing.
