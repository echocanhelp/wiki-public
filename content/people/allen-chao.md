- 1944 — born in Shanghai, spent childhood in Taiwan — per `wikipedia-en-allen-chao.html` (tertiary mirror; its biography references trace to the Purdue Distinguished Alumni page and UCI News — birth year not yet corroborated by a primary record, see GAPS).
- 1973 — after the Purdue PhD, research investigator at G.D. Searle & Co.; group leader of Pharmaceutical Development in 1977; Director of Pharmaceutical Technology and Packaging Development in 1979; concurrently enrolled in the Master of Management Program at Northwestern University — per `purdue-distinguished-alumni-1995-allen-y-chao.html` (Purdue College of Pharmacy Distinguished Alumni, class of 1995).
- 1983 / 1984 — Watson Pharmaceuticals founding year conflicts across independent sources: Purdue News and the 1995 Purdue alumni page say 1983; LA Times 1996-06-12 and UCI News 2017-06-26 say 1984 (vault self-report says 1984-2007). Both reported, not merged — per `purdue-news-2016-q4-purdue-gmp-center.html`, `latimes-1996-06-12-watson-pharmaceuticals.html`, `uci-news-2017-06-26-chao-family.html`.
- 1984 — co-founded Watson Pharmaceuticals with brother-in-law David Hsia (husband of his sister Phylis, a fellow Purdue alumnus); rebuffed by banks and VC, they raised start-up money from relatives and friends; the LA Times (1996-06-12) reports most of $4 million start-up capital raised from family, founded in Chicago, later moved to Corona, CA — per `uci-news-2017-06-26-chao-family.html` and `latimes-1996-06-12-watson-pharmaceuticals.html`.
- Company-name etymology — "Watson" from his late mother's maiden name Hwa + "son" — attested independently in the Purdue 1995 alumni page, UCI News 2017-06-26, and 工商時報 2025-05-13 (取自母親許華之名，Hwa's son) — per `knowledge/web-archives/allen-chao-webpass-2026-11-16/`.
- Pharmaceutical family — father 趙墀熊 John H.H. Chao (1914-1999), Tokyo Imperial University pharmacy; mother 許華 (Hsu Hwa), pharmacy graduate of l'Université Franco-Chinoise (上海/北京中法大學); maternal grand-uncle's generation founded 新亞藥廠 (New Asiatic Chemical Works) in Shanghai in 1926, described as China's first nationalist pharmaceutical enterprise; parents founded 新生藥廠, a mid-size generic firm, in Zhonghe, New Taipei — per `ihhk-new-asiatic-chemical-works.html` (Industrial History of HK Group) and `ctee-2025-05-13-tanvex-zhao-yutian.html` (工商時報 2025-05-13).
- 1993 / 1995 / 1994 — Watson went public February 1993 (LA Times); by 1994 grossed $67.6 million (Purdue alumni page); revenue $153 million in 1995 (LA Times) — per `latimes-1996-06-12-watson-pharmaceuticals.html` and `purdue-distinguished-alumni-1995-allen-y-chao.html`.
- 1994 — same Entrepreneur of the Year honor, attested by a non-self-report source: the Purdue College of Pharmacy 1995 Distinguished Alumni page adds he was runner-up at the national Conference of the Entrepreneur of the Year Awards — per `purdue-distinguished-alumni-1995-allen-y-chao.html`.
- 1995 — named a Purdue College of Pharmacy Distinguished Alumni — per `purdue-news-2016-q4-purdue-gmp-center.html` and `purdue-distinguished-alumni-1995-allen-y-chao.html`.
- 1995 onward — since the family's first gift in 1995, the four Chao siblings (Allen, Agnes, Phylis, Richard) with spouses and father have donated nearly $30 million to UC Irvine, anchoring the Chao Family Comprehensive Cancer Center — per `uci-news-2017-06-26-chao-family.html` (UCI News, 2017-06-26).
- 1999 — diagnosed with stomach cancer, successfully treated by UCI gastroenterologist Dr. Kenneth Chang; the diagnosis deepened the family's giving to UCI — per `uci-news-2017-06-26-chao-family.html`; same year (1999) donated US$1 million to his alma mater TMU's construction and served as a TMU board director — per `tmu-opa-outstanding-alumni-allen-chao.html` (北醫公共事務處 傑出校友 page).
- 2000 — honorary Doctor of Science confirmed by Purdue's own record (Purdue News 2001-10-24, 2016 Q4 release) — non-self-report corroboration of the TAH self-report row — per `purdue-news-2001-10-24-chao-5m-gift.html` and `purdue-news-2016-q4-purdue-gmp-center.html`.
- 2001-10-24 — Allen and wife Lee Hwa-Chao gave a $5 million gift to Purdue Pharmacy to fund the industrial pharmacy center (drugs-for-trial / molecules-to-market program) in the Purdue Research Park — per `purdue-news-2001-10-24-chao-5m-gift.html` (Purdue News).
- 2008 — retired from Watson when it was a ~$3 billion company; the firm later became Actavis Generics (2012) and was acquired by Teva in August 2016 — per `uci-news-2017-06-26-chao-family.html` and `purdue-news-2016-q4-purdue-gmp-center.html`.
- 2013 — co-founded Tanvex BioPharma (泰福生技) with Runway Group (潤泰) chairman 尹衍樑 to develop biosimilars — per `ctee-2025-05-13-tanvex-zhao-yutian.html` (工商時報); 工商時報 labels him 泰福創辦人 (founder), a third-party attestation against the vault self-report employment row.
- 2016 — with son Michael Chao and business leader Alfonso Chang, took over oversight of the Purdue GMP Center LLC, which after 11 years was renamed from the Chao Center for Industrial Pharmacy — per `purdue-news-2016-q4-purdue-gmp-center.html`.
- 2021 — withdrew from Tanvex operations before its filgrastim biosimilar obtained US approval in 2024 — per `ctee-2025-05-13-tanvex-zhao-yutian.html`.
- 2022-05-30 — elected independent director of Yong Xin Biopharm (永昕生醫, TWSE 4726) at its annual shareholder meeting, alongside re-elected chairman 林榮錦 — per `cna-2022-05-30-yongen-chao-independent-director.html` (中央社 CNA).

- 藥學系第四屆 (TMU pharmacy class of 1967, 4th cohort); graduated 1967 then completed military service before emigrating to the U.S. — per `tmu-opa-outstanding-alumni-allen-chao.html` (北醫公共事務處) and the 企業經營獎 laureate profile it links.
