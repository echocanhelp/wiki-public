# Zoe Huang (黃瑞芬)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Zoe Huang
- **Chinese:** 黃瑞芬
- **Era:** 1961
- **Geography:** Taiwan
- **Core roles:** Taiwanese American (TAH Who’s Who)
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Upon graduation from the National Art Academy in Taiwan, Zoe Huang entered the Julliard Music School where she received her Bachelor’s, Master’s degrees, and a vocalist diploma. In addition, she obtained four diplomas from the American Opera Center. During her school years, she received three vocalist awards and performed several concerts with the Juilliard Orchestra at the Lincoln Center. In 1988, she received third prize at the International Belgium Elizabeth Vocalists Competition. Later she received a vocalist award at the Aspen Music Festival. She was invited to perform at the Taipei Music Festival, the Belgium International Art Festival, the Monte Carlo Spring Festival, and many others.
