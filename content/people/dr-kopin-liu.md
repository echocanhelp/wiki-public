## Story

劉國平博士 was born on 25 January 1949 in Hsinchu, Taiwan; he calls the three years he spent at Hsinchu High School the most important period of his mental and intellectual growth. He studied Chemistry at National Tsing Hua Univ and took his bachelor degree there in 1971 by 免試保送, without an entrance examination, then left for the United States after two years of military service. The doctorate followed in 1977, and the academy CV, the NSTC prize profile and the NSYU chemistry dept page all name the doctorating institution as Ohio State University, where the TAH form recorded only `Ohio Univ`. Postdoctoral years at the University of Toronto from 1977 to 1979 with J C Polanyi, and at the University of Minnesota in 1979–1980 and 1981–1982 with W R Gentry, are missing from the TAH form altogether, and a 1981 row as Scientist II at the Georgia Institute of Technology is likewise absent from it. From 1982 to 1993 he was a researcher at Argonne National Laboratory, and in 1993 Academia Sinica president 李遠哲 invited him back to Taiwan to join the Institute of Atomic and Molecular Sciences, where he served as Researcher from 1993, Director from August 2001, and Distinguished Researcher from 2002. The honors run through those decades: fellowship of the American Physical Society in 1998, the inaugural Presidential Science Prize in 2001 as one of three recipients, Academia Sinica membership in 2004, a TWAS fellowship in Chemical Sciences in 2005, the Humboldt Research Award in 2011, and the Dudley R Herschbach Medal in 2019. He held the Distinguished Research Professorship at National Taiwan Univ from 2010 to 2018 per the academy CV, and he is now Fellow Emeritus and Director Emeritus at IAMS. As early as 2000 he is recorded as saying that returning to Academia Sinica meant a large pay cut but that he was driven to mentor future Taiwanese scientists — a quote kept out of verbatim form on this page until the Nature item itself is read.
