# Shawna Yang Ryan (楊小娜)

**Shawna Yang Ryan** (楊小娜) is a Taiwanese American novelist whose debut novel **Green Island** (《綠島》) won the **2017 American Book Awards**. Her work explores themes of Taiwanese history, diaspora memory, and the immigrant experience.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Shawna Yang Ryan
- **Chinese:** 楊小娜
- **Core roles:** Novelist
- **Notable work:** Green Island (《綠島》), 2017 American Book Awards winner
- **Heritage:** Second-generation Taiwanese American
- **Geography:** United States (East Coast / New York area)

## Green Island

Shawna Yang Ryan's novel **Green Island** (《綠島》) tells the story of a Taiwanese family during the White Terror period (1950s), when political dissidents were imprisoned on Green Island (綠島) off the southeast coast of Taiwan. The novel explores themes of political persecution, family separation, and the long shadow of authoritarian rule on Taiwanese families.

The book was recognized with the **2017 American Book Awards**, a prestigious literary honor that celebrates books of merit written by authors of color. This recognition placed Yang Ryan among a distinguished group of Asian American literary voices.

## Literary Achievement

Yang Ryan's work is part of a growing body of Taiwanese American literature that seeks to preserve and communicate the historical experiences of the Taiwanese diaspora. Her novel engages with the same history that the Taiwanese American Historical Society (TAHS) was founded to document — the political struggles, the White Terror, and the quest for identity among Taiwanese Americans.

Her appearance in the 2017 TAHS publication (菁英錄) alongside established community leaders and business figures underscores the breadth of Taiwanese American achievement — from corporate CEOs and politicians to literary artists who give voice to the community's historical memory.
