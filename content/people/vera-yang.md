# Vera Yang (楊悅英)

**Vera Yang (楊悅英)** is listed as **個人董事** (individual director) on the official board of the [[organizations/taiwan-center|Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館)]] (board page archived 2026-07-16 as `taiwancenter-org-board.md`). In a Taiwan Justice report on the 台美小姐 selection, she is identified as the pageant's **總召** (convener/chair) and is quoted speaking to the Central News Agency. This page records those sourced roles and the surrounding organization context already in the vault; it does not invent a personal biography.

## Overview

Taiwan Center (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會; TWCLA) is a **501(c)(3)** nonprofit (EIN **95-4679702**) at 901 S. Fremont Ave. #118, Alhambra, CA 91803. Its public board table (archive 2026-07-16) names **27** directors; Yang appears under **個人董事**, not under officers (董事長 / 執行長 / 副董事長 / 秘書長 / 副秘書長 / 財務長).

## Pageant role (sourced)

In [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2023/20231129212123_root_2635d99f22399c17|Taiwan Justice's 2023 report on the 台美小姐 selection]], **「台美小姐總召楊悅英」** told the Central News Agency that winner 張芳瑜 (Tiffany Chang) is a "理工女" whose talent portion was a robot she developed herself — 「模特兒不是她的志向，她參加選美是為了宣傳台灣」. Yang is quoted again saying Chang came to understand Taiwan's international position during her reign: winning represents not only the Taiwanese-American community but Taiwan itself. A related 2025 TJ story ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250218090803_tiffany-chang披taiwan彩帶贏得美國亞洲小姐-理工女孩張芳瑜喊台_fe9f5de517d1fd8a|Tiffany Chang at Miss Asian America]]) continues the same thread.
