## Role in the Community

From the movement corpus (primary memoir material):

- **1970 美東台灣人夏令會 NY contact.** 徐頌鵬's memoir of the first U.S. East Coast Taiwanese Summer Camp names 許登龍 as the main New York-area liaison in the 和平契友 (Taipei Peace Church fellowship) network that recruited attendees — the camp drew ~90 people. [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys268|268. 回憶第一屆美東台灣人夏令會 / 徐頌鵬]]
- **1977 NY Times ad for the PCT 人權宣言.** When the 台灣之音 broadcast team (張富雄) bought the 1977-09-21 New York Times ad ("Listen!! Outcry Within Taiwan: 'A New and Independent Country' / 台灣人民要獨立") after the PCT declaration, 許登龍醫師 drafted the ad's titles (中文毛筆字 by 許瑞峰); the ~US$10,000 cost was covered purely by donations. [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys2|2. 《台灣之音》的回顧 / 張富雄]]
- **Early patron of 台灣之音.** His brother 許丕龍 donated US$1,000 at 台灣之音's launch; 許登龍 was godfather at the baptism of 張富雄's eldest daughter (later Sister Mary Gloria). Another brother, Dr. 許左龍, donated 50 copies of 《大家唱》 songbooks as donor gifts. [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys2|2. 《台灣之音》的回顧 / 張富雄]]
- **Quiet longtime supporter.** Roger Liu's memoir recounts meeting 許登龍 after ~20 years at Rev. 羅敏珍's installation at Livingston NJ 台灣基督長老教會佳壇教會 (2018) to thank him again for a US$500 donation years earlier — he never mentioned the gift himself; described as "在大紐約地區很受人尊敬的基督徒". [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/349-our-journeys|349. 1974年多倫多壘球賽追憶 (後註) / Roger Liu]]
- **Family note (from corpus):** 黃瑞鳳 is listed in memoir correspondence as 黃瑞鳳（許登龍）, suggesting a marriage link — not merged, flagged for confirmation. [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys268|268. 回憶第一屆美東台灣人夏令會]]
