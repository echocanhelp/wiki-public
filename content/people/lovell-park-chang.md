# Lovell Park Chang (張若葳)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Lovell Park Chang
- **Chinese:** 張若葳
- **Era:** -
- **Geography:** -/NY
- **Core roles:** educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

New York raised trumpeter Lovell Park Chang holds a Bachelor’s Degree from Manhattan School of Music and is currently pursuing for a dual degree program in Masters of Music and Master of Education from Manhattan School of Music. His most recent accomplishments include organizing a spring recital and winning the Fuchs Competition for Chamber Music. He was the recipient of the Carmine Caruso Scholarship in 2012.

## Role in the Community
- 紐約幼獅青少年管弦樂團（Young Lion Youth Orchestra of New York） teaching faculty，負責管樂與銅管（wind and brass）；社團紀錄同時列他為團內小號手 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys306|Our Journeys 306（幼獅青少團紀錄）]]。
- 該團為紐約台美人社區音樂教育組織：10–18 歲团员經甄試錄取，每週六在皇后區 Bayside 的 Marie Curie Middle School（MS 158）排練；音樂總監暨指揮鍾啟仁，師資含林季靜（打擊）、黃東瀛/Tony Huang（弦樂）；藝術總監方秀蓉、行政總監張俊生。
- 團隊演出紀錄：林肯中心 Alice Tully Hall 年度音樂會（2017-05-20、2018-06-09）、法拉盛台灣會館音樂會、Cardozo High School 聖誕音樂會，以及社區長青中心與安養院（如 Silvercrest Center）公益演出。
