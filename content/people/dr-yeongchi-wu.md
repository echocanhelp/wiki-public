## Role in the Community
- Born in Chiayi (嘉義), per his TAH community profile [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/125-e5-90-b3-e6-b0-b8-e5-90-89-e5-b8-b6-e8-91-97-e4-b8-80-e8-a2-8b-e6-84-9b-e5-b|吳永吉帶著一袋愛]].
- Remembered in the movement memoir *Our Journeys* as a rehabilitation physician "帶著一袋愛心走天下" who used an improved vacuum-forming (真空成型) bracing technique to help many people with disabilities — counted among the artistically gifted Taiwanese American physicians [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys291|Our Journeys 291]].
- In Chicago he was among the同鄉 who provided special funding support for the 世界台灣人美術展覽專刊 (World Taiwanese Art Exhibition special publication), alongside 顏幸一、楊正聰、邱伸彥 [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys256|Our Journeys 256]].
- NATMA was founded in 1984 on the initiative of Prof. 周烒明 with 40 chapters; Wu later led the Chicago chapter (2017), consistent with the association's mission of uniting Taiwanese American physicians [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys291|Our Journeys 291]].
