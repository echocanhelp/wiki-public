## From the record

- 2019-02-05 — Released (發稿日) the 紐約台灣商會 press release announcing the joint 花車 with 紐約華僑文教服務中心 for the Flushing Lunar New Year Parade; the release closes 「祝 新年快樂! 紐約台灣商會秘書長 王劭文律師 敬上」 and prints `Source from 紐約台灣商會` — i.e. submitted by the Chamber, with 會長 江明信 and 總幹事 張育誠 as the named press contacts, not her. Source: `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/2-participation-in-lunar-new-yea-parade-in-flushing-ny-by-taiwanese-american-organizations-in-new-york.md` (third-party submission, `Source from 紐約台灣商會`).

The notice itself was circulated 「王劭文 敬上 (代發)」 and lists the speakers (会长 鍾艾霖, 副會長 林瑞葉, 東北區理事 王寶蓮; guests 方秀蓉, 黃開榮, 黃正杰). Source: `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/36-art-exhibition.md` (`Source from Taiwanus.net`) / [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/36-art-exhibition|TAH #36 婦女會紐約分會第九屆年度藝術展]].
- 2018 (年底) — Joined 江明信 as one of two 生力軍 who opened 「嶄新的一頁」 for the **Hello Taiwan** (喂台灣 = 為台灣) shared-identity-mark campaign: the annual event expanded to twice a year (萬聖節、復活節) and the 熱門音樂會 was reshaped into a 老中青三代 parent-child activity. Written by an organizer (`Source from Mr. P. Huang`), so third-party for her. Source: `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/hello-taiwan-2019.md` / [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/hello-taiwan-2019|Hello Taiwan/2019]].
