## Role in the Community

- **Taiwanese American Center of Northern California (北加州台灣會館) director.** The Center's own record ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys53|Our Journeys 53 北加州台灣會館的成長]]) names him among the directors who joined over the years and "worked together, giving their money and time without expecting any rewards" — alongside Chen-Ho Wu, Chin-tang Lin, Ye-Ming Wu, Henry Wu, Hsin-hung Li, Chun-chun Chuang, and Shih-chang Chang. They sustained the Center's annual large-scale concerts: the 2006 SEMISCON Vocal band concert, the 2007 *Dream of Formosa* concert (250+ choir singers and orchestra members from National Taiwan Normal University), and the 2008 concert with Chai Found Music Workshop — each drawing over 3,000 attendees at Jubilee Christian Church in Northern California. (English edition: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys53-eng|ourjourneys53-eng]]; Chinese: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys53|ourjourneys53]].)
- **Community philanthropy with his wife 林正子.** A 2015/01 feature in the TAHS story corpus, 「賴明堂 博士 林正子 女士 夫妻的愛心與奉獻 回報鄉里」 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/271-care-for-taiwan-by-taiwanese-american-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e4-ba-ba-e9-a1-a7-e5|271. Care for Taiwan by Taiwanese American (台美人顧台灣)]], via 台灣亞太發展基金會), records the couple's charity and service returning to their hometown.
