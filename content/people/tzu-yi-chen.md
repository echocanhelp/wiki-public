# Tzu-Yi Chen (陳孜怡)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Tzu-Yi Chen
- **Chinese:** 陳孜怡
- **Era:** 1984
- **Geography:** Taipei, Taiwan
- **Core roles:** entrepreneur, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

At the age of three, Tzu-yi Chen idolized her five-year-old brother. She wanted everything he had and even studied his first-grade textbooks. She insisted on sitting with him during his piano lessons instead of waiting with her mother. The teacher observed this quiet, attentive, toddler who showed an uncanny understanding, and soon enrolled her in private study. Tzu-yi said, “I was overtaken by the music.”

## Biography absorbed from the TAH profile

Restated from the TAH encyclopedia / Who's Who records the vault already holds — no new sources.

- **Early training:** began piano as a toddler, sitting in on her older brother's lessons; the teacher recommended private study after noticing her comprehension.
- **Study in Europe:** 法國國立巴黎高等音樂學院 (Conservatoire de Paris) and 德國國立卡厄斯魯爾音樂大學 (Hochschule für Musik Karlsruhe).
- **Doctorate:** 美國哥倫布州立大學音樂學院 — 鋼琴演奏博士 (D.M.A. in piano performance, Columbus State University).
- **Performance and teaching career:** concert pianist; on the piano faculty and staff accompanist at Columbus State University.
- **Entrepreneurship / education:** founder of **International Friendship Ministries Arts Academy**, which is the basis for the "entrepreneur, educator" role labels.
