- 2017-01-19 — QBG's own press release (third party to TAH): Board of Trustees at its December meeting unanimously elected Pauline Huang, President and Marketing Director at United Custom Service, Inc., as new Board Chair; Executive Director Susan Lacerte credits her with bringing Taiwan: A World of Orchids to the Garden three years in a row — `qbg-chair-2017.html`.
- 2018 (as of 2018-06-30) — QBG 2017–2018 Annual Report board roster lists Pauline Huang as Chair — per queensbotanical.org FY18 Annual Report PDF, official site (search-confirmed, not re-fetched).
- 2019-10-16 — CTWANT (third party, hanzi 黃百齡): 獲靜宜大學第15屆「傑出校友獎」; 畢業於靜宜大學商學系國貿組, 隔年赴美取得 Pace University 會計碩士; 婚後投入丈夫創辦的 United Customs Service 公司 (企業進出口及報關, 客戶含 Amazon) — `ctwant-2019.html`.
- 靜宜大學 official 傑出校友 roster (third party): 第十五屆 黃百齡, 76年/商學系國貿組, United Customs Service, Inc./Managing Director; 校友榮譽記載 107 年(2018) 08-17~19 第五屆「台灣蘭花世界」特展與 111 年(2022) 第九屆 — `pu-alumni-roster.html`.
- 2020-02-20 — QBG press release: former board members completing terms join QBG's Advisory Council, listing Pauline Huang, former QBG Board Chair, President & Marketing Director at United Custom Service, Inc. — `qbg-board-2020.html`.
- 2020-07-07 — QNS (third party): Pauline Huang coordinated a 5,000 face-mask donation from Taiwan to Flushing Town Hall with TCCNA/TCCNY; described as president of United Custom Service, consultant to the Overseas Chinese Affairs Commission (僑委會), QBG ambassador, and board chair of MuseConnect — `qns-masks-2020.html`.
