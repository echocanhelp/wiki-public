# Hung-Jen Wu (吳泓仁)

Hung-Jen Wu 吳泓仁 is a chemical engineer and professor — currently (as of 2026-11-16) Associate Professor and Director of Graduate Studies in Chemical Engineering at Texas A&M University — who came to the United States from 台灣 and earned his B.S. (1998) and M.S. (2000) in Chemical Engineering at 成功大學 and his Ph.D. (2006) at Texas A&M University. His career record on this page also lists Synamen Corp. consultant (2009–2010), postdoctoral work at UC Berkeley (Jay T. Groves group, 2007–2011) and Methodist Hospital Research Institute in Houston (Tony Hu lab), the 2016 KANEKA Junior Faculty Award, and a 2013 Outstanding Performance Presentation claim at the Society of Chinese Bioscientists in America; he was recorded in the TAH Who's Who encyclopedia (#1578, published 2017-03-23).
