# Lisa Hou (侯乃文)

## Overview

Taiwan Center (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會; TWCLA) is a **501(c)(3)** nonprofit (EIN **95-4679702**) with office at 901 S. Fremont Ave. #118, Alhambra, CA 91803. Official intro language already absorbed on the org page: education, arts, and community service for Taiwanese Americans in Southern California; **800+** permanent individual members and **50+** group members; they describe **36** directors and a public board table of **27** named people.

The board page heading (archive 2026-07-16) states that the directors come from many professions and jointly lead the Center’s development, and that they present themselves as guardians of a Taiwan-culture mission rather than only as decision-makers. Hou appears in that named table under **團體董事**, not under officers (董事長 / 執行長 / 副董事長 / 秘書長 / 副秘書長 / 財務長) and not under **個人董事**.

On the primary org page, group directors are listed as: [[people/shirley-yu|Shirley Yu (張欣珠)]], [[people/joyce-yeh|Joyce Yeh (葉怡蘭)]], [[people/josephine-pan|Josephine Pan (潘掬慧)]], [[people/joseph-teng|Joseph Teng (曾英銘)]], [[people/steven-wu|Steven Wu (吳尚誠)]], and **Lisa Hou (侯乃文)**. That is the full group-director slate in the current absorb. The site’s group-member directory is a separate list of member organizations; the board archive does **not** pair Hou with a specific group-member org, so this page does not guess one.
