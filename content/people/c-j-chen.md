## Role in the Community

Memoir corpus identifies 陳銓仁 (pharmacist) as the eldest son of [[people/m-l-chen|陳夢蘭]] and an active officer in early Southern California Taiwanese institutions:

- **Secretary, 南加州台灣長輩會 (elderly association).** When his father 陳夢蘭 served as founding president, he hired his eldest son, pharmacist C. J. Chen, as secretary; "matters were handled very smoothly." On the presidency changeover, all documents managed by C.J. Chen were transferred to 蕭華銓 (Hua-chuan Hsiao), the second president. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys54-eng|54. 海外台灣人第一所老人公寓一鶴園 / Kenjohn Wang]].
- **SCTFCU founding participant and Treasurer (財務).** Attended the first preparation meeting (July 24, per 周實's account, while 周實 was president of 洛杉磯台灣同鄉會) of 南加州台灣人聯邦信用合作社; on the first board of directors he served as Treasurer (財務) alongside 周實 (社長), 劉丁榮, 王喜雄, and 曾輝光. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys244|244. 曇花一現的南加信用合作社 / 周實]].
- **Promoter of the first Taiwanese professional business guild (1974).** As a pharmacist who owned the ROYAL INN motel and speaking for 南加州臺灣協會, he issued the 1974-08-13 letter urging the growing number of Taiwanese motel operators near Los Angeles to form their own trade guild; his 促成 — alongside 許錦鉻牧師's advocacy — led to the 1974-08-17 founding meeting at the Los Angeles Presbyterian Church, described as the first commercial professional guild organized by Taiwanese in the US, with veteran operator 蔡金裕 elected convener. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys295|295. 南加州台灣旅館業同業公會三十週年紀念專輯摘錄]] (quoted from the guild's 2005 30th-anniversary issue).
- **Founding member, 台語福音教會 (1970).** In early summer 1970 the lay leaders then serving at 羅省教會 (LA Presbyterian Church) — 丁昭昇、陳銓仁、林妙珠、陳慶霖、盧淑貞 and 許和瑞 — wanted to add a 台語堂; pastor 羅文 suggested they found a separate church, and 台語福音教會 was organized in October 1970, borrowing Alhambra Seventh-day Adventist church for two years before purchasing the El Sereno chapel in Feb 1973. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys257|257. 洛杉磯台福基督教會]]. Note: 林妙珠 (his wife, [[people/marian-miaw-ju-chen]]) appearing as a co-founder in the same line is direct corroboration that this mention is our C. J. Chen, not Dr. Donald C.J. Chen.
- **Chinese-text edition of the 鶴園 elder-home memoir.** The ZH version of the same record, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys54|54. 海外台灣人第一所老人公寓一鶴園]], adds that 陳夢蘭's re-election bid drew protest from 長老敎會派 under 蕭華銓, that 賴占鰲 (the elder association's initiator) lost money running a small restaurant to fund it, and that 賴 and others asked the author to persuade 陳夢蘭 to step down — the 檔案 C. J. Chen had kept were then handed over to 蕭華銓 as second president.
- TAH #180 record separately credits him as 洛杉磯台灣同鄉會第一任會長 (1968–1971): 180. 陳銓仁 / 洛杉磯台灣同鄉會第一任會長.
