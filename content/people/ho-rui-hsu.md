## Role in the Community
- Chair of the Los Angeles Taiwanese Club in 1970: on 1970-01-28 the LA club, together with the East Coast club (鄭義和) and Chicago club (林錦弘), co-initiated the founding of the Formosan Club of America (全美台灣同鄉會, founded 1970-07-01 in New York); 鄭義和 and 許和瑞 were elected first president and vice-president (per [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76|76. 全美台灣同鄉會 / 吳木盛]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-357|357. 舊金山灣區臺灣人的社團活動 / 何義麟]]).
- Active in the UW-era Taiwan independence movement circle: named among those who actively participated and helped publish《Formosangram》, and later seated on the decision-making council (決策會議) of the restructured league alongside 陳以德、陳希寬、呂天明 (per [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys81|81. 早期威大台灣學生在台灣建國運動所扮演的角色 / 周烒明]]).
- Founding figure of TACL (台美公民協會): attendee at the 1982-01-21 SCTAI- convened meeting of 16 that first resolved to form the league, and in 1984 one of the five founding-preparation committee members; with author 周實 he contacted attorney 廖重遠 to file the state registration (per [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys272|272. 台美公民協會的成長 / 周實]]).
- Founding member of the Los Angeles 生活座談會 (Life Seminar, founded 1971): 劉天良's memoir names 許和瑞 among the original circle (with 陳慶霖、李英男、李隆吉、黃根深、陳明章 and others) who combined mutual education, 輪流演講 and leadership training with 同鄉會 organizing, and credits them with major influence on the LA Taiwanese Association from the late 1960s into the 1970s (per [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys212|212. 生活座談會簡史 / 劉天良]]).
- Chapter-officer record: at the 1970 founding of the Formosan Club of America, the LA chapter under 許和瑞 was one of the 28 founding chapters, and the first officers elected were 鄭義和 (president), 許和瑞 (vice-president), 陳龍峰 (secretary), 吳希賢 (finance), 方政昇 (journal editor) (per [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76|76. 全美台灣同鄉會 / 吳木盛]]).
- Daughter 許秀聰 (Martha Hsu), the first female Taiwanese-American graduate of West Point, has her own 1991 record at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/first-137|137. 許秀聰 Martha Hsu VanDriel]].
