# Prof. Helen Lee (李雪玟教授)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Prof. Helen Lee
- **Chinese:** 李雪玟教授
- **Era:** --
- **Geography:** Pingtung, Taiwan
- **Core roles:** professor, artist, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Helen Lee 李雪玟, Soprano, is a faculty member in the Music Department at the University of Nevada, Reno. In May 2006 she received the Dean’s Award for Excellence in Teaching, and has served as president of the university’s international faculty honor society, receiving special recognition at the World Congress held in Montreal, Canada. She is frequently invited to sing the American national anthem at important events in Nevada and California, representing Taiwanese Americans with distinction. Professor Lee has performed successfully several times at Carnegie Hall and Lincoln Center in New York City. She was invited by the Suzuki Music Association of Japan to tour Japan, where she was interviewed and performed on Japanese television.
