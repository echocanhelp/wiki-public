## Role in the Community
- Fourth president of the Taiwanese Association of America (全美台灣同鄉會聯合會), term 1976-09-01 to 1978-12-31; shortly into his term he went abroad for personal reasons and was replaced by Eddie Chuang 莊承業 (Cheng Y. Eddie Chuang): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76|76. 全美台灣同鄉會 / 吳木盛]] (ZH+EN roster).
- Organized the first 美南台灣人夏令營 (Southern U.S. Taiwanese camp), 1976-04-16–18 at Hilltop Reservoir near Houston, ~100 attendees — the camp became an annual event: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys318|318. 漫談美南台灣人夏令會的濫觴 / 莊承業]].
- Cited among activists whose support for the 台獨運動 drew blacklist retaliation: 成大電機系出身, University of Florida 電機碩士, then at Brown & Root, 「任七七年全美同鄉會長，支持台獨運動遭忌」 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys106|106. 中華民國的黑名單 / 何文亮、莊峻華]]. He also authored the blacklist memoir 「黑名單故事–一個基督徒的良心」: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-708|708. 黑名單故事–一個基督徒的良心 / 11/2019]].
- Volunteer for the Houston Taiwanese community center build (大廳音響設備 with 林秋成、歐清南): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys233|233. 回顧休士頓台灣人活動中心的成立 / 莊承業]].
- HOLD: conflict in education — TAH profile lists University of Florida MS 1965; the 黑名單 essay says 甘斯維爾佛大電機碩士 (consistent) but lists 成大電機系 as his undergraduate, which the TAH profile omits. Term dates: TAH work-page roster says 1976-09-01–1978-12-31; essay 106 says 「七七年」 president — held, not merged.
