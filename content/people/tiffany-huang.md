# Tiffany Huang (黃咏婷)

**Tiffany Frances Huang** (黃咏婷) is a Taiwanese American filmmaker and director. She was selected by **National Geographic** among 100+ applicants to direct a short film as part of their "10 Days of Genius" campaign.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Tiffany Frances Huang
- **Chinese:** 黃咏婷
- **Education:** UC San Diego (Media major); Art Center College of Design (Master's in directing)
- **Role:** Filmmaker, director
- **Geography:** United States (Los Angeles area)
- **Notable work:** National Geographic "10 Days of Genius" campaign — "Moment" short film

## Education

- **UC San Diego** — Media major (undergraduate)
- **Art Center College of Design** — Master's in directing (graduate)

## National Geographic: "Moment"

National Geographic commissioned Tiffany to direct a short film as part of their "10 Days of Genius" campaign. The project required her to create a film based on one of Albert Einstein's ten famous quotes, with the constraint that the film could not exceed 6 minutes. National Geographic provided $15,000 for production.

Tiffany was given only three weeks to prepare, shoot, and edit the film. She chose the quote: **"DEATH IS NOT AN END IF WE HAVE LIVED ON OUR CHILDREN."**

Her father had passed away in June 2016, so she wrote a story about his spirit being forever remembered, combined with some of her previous works, and submitted it to National Geographic.

The project was highly competitive — National Geographic received over 100 applicants, and Tiffany was selected from the pool.

### Production Details

- **Director:** Tiffany Frances Huang
- **Cinematographer:** Jeremy Lundborg (also a director, Juilliard graduate in dance)
- **Lead Actress:** Chloe Crade (mother is Japanese, father is Irish; Juilliard dance graduate, full-time performer with TV and Broadway experience)
- **Supporting Role:** Tina Chang (張瑞菊) played the mother (she is Tiffany's mother)
- **Film Link:** https://youtu.be/raUyrYRiqt4

### Results

National Geographic's Facebook page received over 1 million views for the project. National Geographic also published additional coverage of the project. Tiffany's film "Moment" was used as the headline image for the coverage.

The new projects led to a significant increase in National Geographic's Facebook viewership, and injected new creative energy from younger generations into the content.

## Previous Work

Before "Moment," Tiffany had previously created work for church/missionary groups. She used her filming in Africa (where she had worked with a church evangelism group) to apply to Art Center College of Design for her master's program.
