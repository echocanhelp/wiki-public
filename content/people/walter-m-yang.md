## Role in the Community
- His wife [[people/christine-l-yang|林壽英 (Christine L. Yang)]] records their Northwestern years in her TAH memoir: 楊茂嘉 had arrived at Northwestern (Evanston) about two years before she left Taiwan in summer 1967; the couple married quickly before the fall term so married graduate students could share student housing. His American advisor Dr. Schwartz stood in as the groom's parents; 林光輝 gave the bride away and his wife 文女 lent the bride her wedding dress. The newlyweds lived in Dryden Hall within the Taiwanese grad-student circle — the 林光輝、謝嘉峰、陳光和、賴義雄、洪承延、陳冠宇、黃嵩德 families — sharing bridge nights, dumpling-making, and fishing trips. See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys275||275. 那段難忘的歲月 / 林壽英 / 2017/01]].
- With his wife he established the **Walter M. Yang and Christine L. Yang Endowed Fund** (2020) supporting the TAH historical record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/awards-92||92. Walter M. Yang and Christine L. Yang Endowed Fund]].
- His own TAH story record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/401-walter-m-yang-e6-a5-8a-e8-8c-82-e5-98-89-e5-8d-9a-e5-a3-ab-201505||401. Walter M. Yang 楊茂嘉 博士 / 2015/05]].
