## Role in the Community

**台美小姐選拔 trainer and pageant judge.** In 大洛杉磯台灣會館's 2016 台美小姐選拔 (Miss Taiwanese American pageant), the 開訓記者會 report names 施雅婷老師 as the first-day (2016-06-14) instructor for the twelve finalists' eight-week camp: she lectured on 選美須知, advocates 「身、心、靈俱美」, and is credited with 多年選美評審與專業美姿美儀教學經驗 — consistent with her directory role as 美姿美儀老師 at her own 學苑 (same-name match; no conflicting identifiers on record).
