## Role in the Community
The record places Dr. Hwang in the New York Taiwanese American medical community through
two independent layers. The self-report claims the top two offices of NTUMCAA-NA 北美台大醫學院校友會
— vice chairman 2017–2018, then chairman 2019–2020 ([[sources/tah-whoswho-dr-richard-r-hwang-capture|capture]]) —
which would make him the senior elected officer of the North American NTU medical-college
alumni body across that term, though no association-side document on this box attests the
election. Independently, the 2014 TAH community record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/16-e7-b4-90-e7-b4-84-e6-9d-8f-e6-9e-97-e7-94-b7-e8-81-b2-e5-9b-9b-e9-87-8d-e5-94|紐約杏林男聲四重唱]]
seats him as Tenor I in a Long Island vocal quartet founded in 1991 by medical-school alumni,
singing alongside fellow physicians 許登龍, 楊士宏 and others at 同學會, 醫學會, 同鄕會, 教會 and
wedding occasions — cultural community life inside the same physician cohort that the alumni
association organises. The quartet record is the only source on this box that names him
outside his own directory entry; the association offices remain his own statement.

**Frontier update (2026-11-19, FRONTIER-11190824-16).** The association offices are no longer
statement-only: ntumcaana.org — the association's own site — publishes him as 理事長 (M1977) in a
2022-08 convention 紀實, a 2022-12-30 卸任理事長的話 closing a COVID-extended 2019–2022 term, and its
2024 convention program roster, and bylines him as 大華府分會會長 from 2024 through at least 2026-03.
大紀元 2018-10-12 independently names him 副理事長 speaking at the 2018 annual convention. See
[[sources/dr-richard-r-hwang-frontier-11190824-16-2026-11-19|the frontier web pass]] for the full
arm log and the one held conflict (doximity's 1993–1998 residency vs the self-reported Bronx-Lebanon
row). External publication affiliations (Curr Urol 2016 / Springer 2022) independently corroborate the
St. Barnabas and CUNY rows.
