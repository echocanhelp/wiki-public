## Timeline
- **1979** — A.B., East Asian Languages and Civilizations, Harvard College.
- **1983** — M.D., SUNY-Buffalo School.
- **1986–1989** — Instructor of Pediatrics, Pennsylvania School of Medicine.
- **1990–2000** — Assistant in Pediatrics and Instructor, Massachusetts General Hospital.
- **2000–2004** — Associate Professor of Pediatrics, Duke University Medical School.
- **2004–2018** — Professor of Pediatrics & Cell Biology, University of Massachusetts Medical School.
- **2010–2014** — Member, Scientific Advisory Council, National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences (NIH).
- **2016–2017** — Past President, American Society of Andrology.
- **2017–2018** — Past President, Pediatric Endocrine Society.
- **2018–present** — Professor of Pediatrics, Sidney Kimmel Medical College; Chief Scientific Officer and Enterprise VP, Nemours Health Care.

## Professional Network
- Led the Pediatric Endocrine Society as President (2017–2018) and the American Society of Andrology as President (2016–2017).
- Served on the NIH National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences Scientific Advisory Council (2010–2014).
- Academic career spanning Pennsylvania, Massachusetts General Hospital, Duke, UMass Medical School, and Sidney Kimmel Medical College, with a concurrent senior scientific leadership role at Nemours Health Care from 2018.
