# Liao Jichun (廖繼春)

**Liao Jichun** (廖繼春, 1902–1976) was a renowned Taiwanese painter, celebrated as one of the pioneering figures of modern Taiwanese art. He served as an art teacher at 台中師範學校 (Taichung Normal School) and was the father of biochemist 廖述宗 (Liao Shu-zong).

## Artistic Career

Liao Jichun was a famous painter whose work became foundational to modern Taiwanese art. He taught art at 台中師範學校 (Taichung Normal School), where he influenced many students during his tenure. His painting style and creative approach had a profound impact on his son, 廖述宗, who later credited his father's artistic imagination as the source of his own scientific creativity.

Liao was known as a quiet, gentle man whose passion was expressed entirely through painting. He took a laissez-faire approach to raising his children, allowing them the freedom to develop their own talents and interests.

## Family Legacy

Liao's creative spirit and artistic vision extended beyond his own work. His son 廖述宗 described his father's influence as "无形而深远" (formless but profound), stating: "My creativity and discoveries in science came from his imagination and expression in art."

The family also ran a stationery shop called "文藝社" (Wenyi She) at home, which served as a cultural gathering place in their community.
