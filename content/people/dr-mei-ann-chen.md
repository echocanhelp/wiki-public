# Dr. Mei-Ann Chen (陳美安博士)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Dr. Mei-Ann Chen
- **Chinese:** 陳美安博士
- **Era:** --
- **Geography:** Kaohsiung, Taiwan
- **Core roles:** educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Music director of the Memphis Symphony Orchestra, Mei-Ann Chen is one of America’s most exciting and promising young conductors. As a conducting fellow sponsored by the League of American Orchestras, she has recently concluded a highly successful tenure as assistant conductor of the Atlanta Symphony and served a one-year appointment as assistant conductor of the Baltimore Symphony. In North America, Chen has appeared as guest conductor with the symphonies of Alabama, Atlanta, Colorado, Fort Worth, Honolulu, Oregon, Seattle, Toronto, as well as the Chicago Sinfonietta, Florida Orchestra and the Rochester Philharmonic.

## Career & Appointments

Absorbed from the profile and tables above (vault-internal only):

- Music Director of the **Memphis Symphony Orchestra** (2010–2016) and of the **Chicago Sinfonietta** (2011–).
- Assistant Conductor of the **Atlanta Symphony Orchestra** and, for one year, of the **Baltimore Symphony**; concluded the Atlanta tenure as a conducting fellow sponsored by the **League of American Orchestras**.
- Music Director of the **Portland Youth Philharmonic**.
- Guest conductor in North America with the symphonies of Alabama, Atlanta, Colorado, Fort Worth, Honolulu, Oregon, Seattle and Toronto, plus the Chicago Sinfonietta, Florida Orchestra and Rochester Philharmonic.
- Educated at the **New England Conservatory of Music** (B.S. and M.S., Conducting and Violin) and the **University of Michigan** (Ph.D., Music Art).
- **2005:** won first prize at the international **Malko Conducting Competition**, defeating 242 competitors worldwide ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys339|Our Journeys 339]]).
- **2018-08:** conducted the **National Symphony Orchestra of Taiwan (NTSO)** at the first Los Angeles **蕭泰然音樂節** in the Walt Disney Concert Hall — leading 出頭天進行曲, 來自福爾摩沙的天使, Dvořák's *New World*, and the first performance by her and soprano 陳麗嬋 of 蕭泰然's 《1947序曲》 with the 204-voice 南加州台美人合唱團, plus encores of 《擔心擔》 and a stage-and-audience unison 《台灣翠青》 closing the festival ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys339|Our Journeys 339]]).
- The vault additionally holds a Manhattan School of Music appointment notice (see Archival Records below).
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ff372|TAH #372]] records her as the first student in **New England Conservatory** history to earn master's degrees in both violin and conducting.
