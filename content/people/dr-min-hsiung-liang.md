## Role in the Community
- From the Houston community memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys106|Our Journeys 106]]: 出身東海大學物理系，奥克拉荷馬大學物理碩士，任職大老古公司. 曾任同鄉會（台美協會休士頓分支）理事，支持台灣獨立運動，獲罪不得順利返台 — he was barred from smoothly returning to Taiwan because of his support for the Taiwanese independence movement.
