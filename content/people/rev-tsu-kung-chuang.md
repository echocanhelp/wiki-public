## Role in the Community

Ancestral home 福建省晉江縣, born in Taiwan — per the 天恩出版社 author bio captured 2026-11-14 at `knowledge/web-archives/tsu-kung-chuang-thirdparty-2026-11-14/graceph-author-bio.md`.

He graduated from the National Taiwan University chemical engineering department in **1970** and earned his Ph.D. in chemical engineering from Northwestern University in **1983**, then served roughly two decades as a chemical engineer, including as head of a senior research division at the Industrial Technology Research Institute (工業技術研究院) in Hsinchu — corroborated independently by the Gordon-Conwell Seminary faculty page (Gordon-Conwell faculty biography, 2026-11-14, `knowledge/web-archives/tsu-kung-chuang-thirdparty-2026-11-14/gordonconwell-faculty-bio.md`) and by the BBN 真光之聲 program bio (BBN 聖經廣播網, 2026-11-14, `knowledge/web-archives/tsu-kung-chuang-thirdparty-2026-11-14/bbn-zhen-guang-zhi-sheng.md`).

In **1990** he resigned his industry post (BBN bio dates the resignation to 1990 after years in 臺灣石油化工業 and ITRI) and returned to the U.S. to study at Trinity Evangelical Divinity School in Deerfield, IL, earning a Ph.D. in Intercultural Studies (文化學) in **1995** (Gordon-Conwell faculty biography, 2026-11-14, `knowledge/web-archives/tsu-kung-chuang-thirdparty-2026-11-14/gordonconwell-faculty-bio.md`). His 1995 Trinity dissertation was published the same year by UMI as *The Factors Influencing the Conversion of Mainland Chinese Intellectuals toward Christianity in North America* (Niigata University library catalog record ba35194257, cited via web search 2026-11-14).

From **1995 to 2002** he was Senior Pastor of the Chinese Bible Church of Greater Boston (波士頓郊區華人聖經教會) at Lexington, MA, and from **2002** Senior Pastor of Emeth Chapel (波士頓真理堂) in the Burlington/Woburn area, where he and wife 莊劉真光 (Marie) serve as co-pastors — per the Gordon-Conwell faculty biography (2026-11-14, `knowledge/web-archives/tsu-kung-chuang-thirdparty-2026-11-14/gordonconwell-faculty-bio.md`) and the church's own site ([emethchapel.org](https://emethchapel.org/), captured 2026-11-14).

In **July 2002** the couple founded the T. K. & True Light Ministry (真光協會), based at 7 Overlook Ave., Burlington, MA — publisher date per the 天恩出版社 author bio (2026-11-14, `knowledge/web-archives/tsu-kung-chuang-thirdparty-2026-11-14/graceph-author-bio.md`); address per the ministry's own site ([tktruelight.org](https://www.tktruelight.org/), captured 2026-11-14). The ministry produces his expository preaching series and Sunday-school curricula and distributes them to Chinese-language churches; its program 真光之聲 airs Mon–Fri 11:30 pm on BBN 聖經廣播網 (BBN program page, 2026-11-14, `knowledge/web-archives/tsu-kung-chuang-thirdparty-2026-11-14/bbn-zhen-guang-zhi-sheng.md`).

At Gordon-Conwell Theological Seminary he joined in **2005**, coordinated the Chinese SemLink program from **2005 to 2014**, and has been co-mentor of the Chinese Doctor of Ministry program since **2015** (Gordon-Conwell faculty biography, 2026-11-14, `knowledge/web-archives/tsu-kung-chuang-thirdparty-2026-11-14/gordonconwell-faculty-bio.md`).

Book list with publisher dates is carried in Source Notes below. Note: the self-report labels the 1995 Trinity degree "Ph.D., Philosophy"; the seminary and publisher records say Ph.D. in Intercultural Studies (文化學).
