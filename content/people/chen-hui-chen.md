# Chen Hui-Chen (陳慧貞)

**Chen Hui-Chen (陳慧貞)** is a Taiwanese-American Presbyterian leader serving as **副主席** (Vice-Chair) of the [[organizations/national-taiwanese-presbyterian-council|NTPC]] 執委會 for **2025/26** (中原區代表). She also serves as **會計** (Treasurer) of the NTPC Historical Committee.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Chen Hui-Chen / Hui-Chen Chen
- **Chinese:** 陳慧貞
- **Era:** Contemporary (NTPC Vice-Chair 2025/26)
- **Geography:** United States (Central District)
- **Core roles:** NTPC Vice-Chair; NTPC Historical Committee Treasurer

## Role in NTPC

Per the 2025/26 執委 letter on ntpc-usa.org:

> **副主席：陳慧貞長老(中原區)2028 年屆滿**

She is the Treasurer for the NTPC Historical Committee's 2025 republication of *[[sources/toward-a-community-of-hope|Toward A Community of Hope]]*. The 2026 invitation states: "敬請各教會將今年度 NTPC 年費(禮拜人數 50人以下$100，禮拜人數 100 人以上$300，較大教會請增加。)請寄支票給會計陳慧貞長老 1429 Wembley CT. NE., Atlanta, GA 30329。"
