## Identity Snapshot
- **Chinese:** 林雅玲
- **Core roles:** community leader, pianist/organist (publisher-stated)
- **Geography:** New York / Taiwan 高雄縣 (birthplace, publisher-stated)

## Role in the Community
- **2011 — 第42屆美東台灣人夏令會 (TACEC 2011) 籌備會召集人**; host: 大紐約區台灣同鄉會.
  Theme 創新與和諧, 7/1~4, Poconos PA, 分兩地 (Woodland Resort / University of Scranton),
  「合計共有四百餘人參加」 — per the 台美史料中心 2018 print reprint of the 公論報 第2347期
  07/15/2011 report (double-sourced with the vault copy). Her invitation letter was printed in 台灣公論報 第2336期 (04/15/2011) and re-published on
  TaiwanYes.com (page dated 2011-05-19), and the publisher's own TACEC summary table lists
  row 42/2011 organizer 「林雅玲」 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tacec-2011]].
- **大紐約區台灣同鄉會 會長** (two-year term). 大紀元 2008-12-26: elected 2008-12-22 at the
  理事會 at 台灣會館, installed 2009-01-10 at the chapter's 40th anniversary annual meeting.
  大紀元 2011-01-07 (記者史靜): rotated out at the chapter's 42nd annual meeting (2011-01-08),
  succeeded by 林尚勝; she cites 「担任會長兩年」 and the UN 請願信 campaign (Sep 2010) as her
  signature effort. Dated snapshots, not merged.
- Per the TAH Foundation person directory (publisher self-page, sourced 「美東台灣人夏令會07/2011」):
  born 高雄縣; piano from age 6, prize-winning at 8 in a 高雄 piano competition; organ study
  under Richard Amend from 1984; 大紐約台灣同鄉會 鋼琴伴奏與助理指揮 from 2008; also described
  as conductor/educator/musician. `sourced-to-publisher`.
