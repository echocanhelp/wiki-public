# Joseph H. Chen (陳河田教授)

Joseph H. Chen（陳河田教授）is a food scientist and Cornell University Ph.D. (Food science, 1971) who came to the United States in 1965 and settled in Ithaca, New York. He was born in 台灣屏東, married 程美希 Mei-Hsi Chen in 1967, and served as president of the Ithaca/Cornell Taiwanese community association; his record comes from the TAH Foundation Who's Who (encyclopedia record #410).

## Role in the Community
- Came to Ithaca, NY in 1965 for doctoral study at Cornell University; served as **president of the Ithaca/Cornell Taiwanese community organization** (伊薩卡同鄉會, later 康大臺灣同鄉同學會), which under his generation grew to nearly 300 members including families.
- Per his wife 程美希 Mei-Hsi Chen's memoir of the association's history, the group's work centered on 同鄉互愛、互助: easing homesickness, cooking Taiwanese food, welcoming new students and sending off graduates, and handling weddings and celebrations. In the 1960s–70s the association conducted itself in Taiwanese.
- Married 程美希 in 1967, the same community circle the memoir documents.
- Corpus record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys310|310. 伊薩卡台灣同鄉會歷史 (中英) / 程美希 / 2015]]（英文 counterpart: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys310-eng|ourjourneys310-eng]]） — his own TAH encyclopedia entry [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-joseph-h-chen|410. Joseph H. Chen 陳河田 / 2015/05]].
- 2001 — linked 李登輝 to the 「李總統杯」 / President Lee Cup golf tournament in Upstate New York; the club's own history names him as the go-between and records that he and Lee were friends and golf partners when Lee did his Cornell Ph.D. — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/44-upstate-new-york-taiwanese-golf-club|TAH #44 (陳仲欽, 02/2016)]].
  HOLD: the club history asserts the Chen–Lee friendship/partnership at Cornell; no independent record on this box documents it, and the #410 self-report does not mention it either way.
- His 1965 arrival is the founding anchor his wife's chapter history gives for the TAA Ithaca NY chapter lineage — carried on [[organizations/taiwanese-association-of-america|TAA]] (Our Journeys #310 row), [[organizations/taiwanese-american-student-association-at-cornell|Cornell TASA]], [[organizations/cornell-taiwanese-student-association|Cornell CTSA]] and [[organizations/cornell-taiwanese-american-society|Cornell CTAS]].
  HOLD: continuity between the 1960s–70s 康大臺灣同鄉同學會 and today's registered Cornell student organizations is asserted by locality, not documented in the corpus.
