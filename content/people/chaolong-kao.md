# Dr. Charles C.L. Kao / Chaolong Kao (高昭龍)

**Dr. Charles C.L. Kao (Chaolong Kao, 高昭龍)** was the **organizing minister** of the [[organizations/formosan-presbyterian-church-in-los-angeles||Formosan Presbyterian Church in Los Angeles (FPCLA)]], called by the Presbytery of Los Angeles Southwest in September 1971 on behalf of the Formosan congregation.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English names:** Charles C.L. Kao; Chaolong Kao
- **Chinese:** 高昭龍
- **Era:** mid-20th century; active around FPCLA organization (1970–1971)
- **Geography:** Taiwan (TTCS) → United States (Harvard Visiting Scholar; Los Angeles)
- **Core roles:** Organizing pastor of FPCLA; former Assistant Professor, Tainan Theological College and Seminary (TTCS)

## Ministry and FPCLA

According to [[people/albert-s-lai||Dr. Albert S. Lai]]'s 1971 dissertation [[sources/toward-a-community-of-hope||Toward A Community of Hope]]:

- With financial assistance from the Synod Office of Southern California and National Missions, **Rev. Dr. Charles C.L. Kao** was called in **September 1971** by the Presbytery of Los Angeles Southwest as the Formosan congregation's organizing minister.
- He was a **native Formosan**, former **Assistant Professor at Tainan Theological College**, and before accepting the call was a **Visiting Scholar at Harvard University**.
- Related secondary narrative on the Albert S. Lai page also connects him to TTCS training under the era of President Shoki Coe and to teaching at TTCS while Lai was a student — treat detailed TTCS biography as **content priority B** pending additional primary sources.

**Disambiguation:** Co-founder [[people/daniel-kao||Dr. Daniel Kao (高光民)]] is a different person. Do not merge Charles C.L. Kao with Daniel Kao.


## Education

- **Tainan Theological College and Seminary (TTCS)** — student under President Shoki Coe era; graduated and taught as faculty
- **Harvard University** — Visiting Scholar (pre-call to FPCLA); earned master's degree, then doctorate
- **Nanjing Theological Seminary** — studied in the United States under Dr. Barclay

## Timeline

- **1932–1936** — Student at TTCS
- **1935** — Dr. Barclay died on October 5; Kao's father and classmates carried the coffin at the funeral (student council role)
- **Post-1936** — Taught at TTCS as Assistant Professor
- **1965** — During TTCS centenary, returned to the U.S. to attend Harvard Divinity School for doctoral studies
- **Post-doctorate** — Remained at Harvard conducting academic research
- **September 1971** — Called by Presbytery of Los Angeles Southwest as organizing minister of FPCLA
- **September 1971** — Assumed role of organizing pastor at Formosan Presbyterian Church in Los Angeles
- **First Sunday** — Preached in mother tongue (Taiwanese); sermon titled "The Blessed Land"
- **May 1972** — Pastor Kao flew to San Francisco Theological Seminary to attend Albert Lai's graduation
- **July 1972** — Officiated at Albert Lai's wedding at FPCLA (first wedding after church establishment)
