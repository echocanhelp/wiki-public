## Role in the Community
- Filmmaker: second-generation Taiwanese American, born in Boston, MA and raised in Portland, OR (consistent with the Boston geography on the TAH profile).
- First film 「Across a Paper Ocean」 (1993, award-winning) screened at festivals across the US and broadcast on public television; 「Han Chee (Sweet Potato)」 (1998, 30 min) was her M.F.A. thesis film at San Francisco State.
- Worked ~7 years with California Newsreel on 「RACE – The Power of an Illusion」「What's Race Got to Do with It?」 and the companion site for the PBS series 「Unnatural Causes」 (launched Winter 2008).
- Donor to UCSD Taiwan Studies Program: listed among year-end 2022 donors ("Bob and Jean Cheng").
- Corpus records: [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/taiwanese-american-film|TAH film record: Han Chee (Sweet Potato)]] · [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-384|UCSD Taiwan Studies year-end message 12/2022]]
