# Wan-Lin Tsai (蔡宛霖博士)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Wan-Lin Tsai
- **Chinese:** 蔡宛霖博士
- **Era:** Birth Place
- **Geography:** 台灣高雄
- **Core roles:** educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Wan-Line Tsai is originally from Taiwan. She has a Bachelor’s Degree in Elementary Education from the National Taipei University of Education. She also has a Master’s Degree and a Doctoral Degree in Violin Performance from the College-Conservatory of Music at the University of Cincinnati. She currently is the assistant teacher in the preschool Chinese program at Intercultural Montessori Language School in Chicago. She also helps local organizations with their musical direction and performance.
