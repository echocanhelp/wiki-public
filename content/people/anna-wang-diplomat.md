# Anna Wang (王涵)

## Story

When the U.S. Consulate in Nagoya named Anna Wang (王涵) as its Principal Officer in July 2024, the 台美史料中心 (TA Archives) noted the milestone twice over: she was the first woman to lead that consulate in its history, and her parents were both from Taiwan.

Wang had spent four years at the American Institute in Taiwan (AIT) — a posting that gave her direct experience with the island she came from — before moving to Washington to serve as Political Unit Chief in the Office of Pakistan Affairs at the Bureau of South and Central Asian Affairs. The TA Archives story corpus carried her appointment as story #909, 「曾任AIT官員 台裔王涵就任美國駐名古屋首席領事」, and the Center's September 2024 newsletter brought the news to the LA community reading list.
