## Timeline
- 1930 — born in Tokyo, Japan (era range 1930-2018)
- 1957 — M.D., National Taiwan University College of Medicine
- Ph.D., University of Wisconsin-Madison
- 1963- — resident physician / teaching assistant, University of Virginia School of Medicine
- 1981- — Director of Psychopathology, Cleveland Clinic
- 1965 — as head of the UW-Madison 台灣問題研究會, co-convened the 「麥迪遜會議」 with UFI chairman 陳以德 at UW-Madison, issuing a joint communiqué calling for North American Taiwanese unity
- Chairman of the Central Committee, United Formosans in America for Independence (UFAI, 全美台灣獨立聯盟), formed at the Philadelphia conference; with 羅福全 he raised the funds for the New York Times ad carrying 「台灣人民自救宣言」
- 1970 — one of four renowned T.A. (羅福全、彭明敏、周烒明、蔡同榮) speaking for the Taiwan Independent movement in Morgantown, West Virginia
- 1984 — founded the North American Taiwanese Medical Doctor Association (NATMA, 北美洲台灣人醫師協會) as its first president, with 40 chapters under it
- Co-founder, United Formosans in America for Independence (UFI, 全美台灣獨立聯盟)
- Co-founder, Taiwanese American Parkinson Association (台美人柏金森互助會)
- Deputy Chairman, American Neuropathological Society
- 2018 — TAH encyclopedia #121 published "Remembering Dr. Suy-Ming Chou 周烒明博士"

## Role in the Community (corpus-absorbed)

- NATMA's founding memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys74|Our Journeys #74]] records his recruitment campaign: he personally made phone calls encouraging Taiwanese doctors to join the association, working around the excuses 「居留問題」 and 「太忙」 by routing through existing groups (教授會、同鄉會) and enlisting trusted like-minded doctors city by city — 楊次雄 (Greater New York), 黃昭 (Michigan), [[people/prof-chin-chu-lin-m-d|林靜竹]] (Chicago), 陳惠亭 (Southern California), 蔡武男 (Washington D.C.) all responded enthusiastically (English version: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys74-eng|Our Journeys #74 (EN)]]).
- His own drafted memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys81|Our Journeys #81: 早期（1960〜1970年）威大台灣學生在台灣建國運動所扮演的角色（起稿 2014/12）]] documents the 1960s–70s 威大 Taiwanese-student generation's role in the Taiwan nation-building movement.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys259|Our Journeys #259（朱耀源, 10/2016）]] names him among the first movement figures allowed back to Taiwan around NATPA's first Taipei annual meeting (1990), after nearly six months of negotiation shrank the blacklist from 84 named applicants to 3.
- The couple's story is told in the memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-856|TAH #856: 周烒明醫師與吳秀惠醫師的故事（上）(10/2022)]] — corroborating his wife 吳秀惠 as a physician; an institutional memorial file is held at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/49897|TAH #649: 周烒明 / 吳三連台灣史料基金會 (09/2018)]]; part two continues in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-857|TAH #857: 周烒明醫師與吳秀惠醫師的故事(下) (10/2022)]], and a classmate's tribute is [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories324|TAH #324: 一對使我以台大人為榮的學長 周烒明 吳秀惠 醫師 (賴其萬, 09/2015)]].
- As chairman of UFAI's Central Committee he funded the 「萬里自由長征」 campus tour of 60+ universities with a bare US$400 budget — the 「能省則省」 road-trip of borrowed 同鄉 sofas and hamburger meals ran on his committee's program ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys7|Our Journeys #7]]).
- His 1965 co-convening of the 「麥迪遜會議」 with UFI chairman 陳以德 at UW-Madison, and the UFAI founding at the Philadelphia conference where he took the Central Committee chairmanship, are documented in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys234|Our Journeys #234]].
- As a founding-generation speaker at his first New York 同鄉會 gathering he shared the platform with 陳隆志 and 林榮勲 — the 「五位博士」 whose lectures awakened a later memoirist's Taiwanese political consciousness ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys70|Our Journeys #70]]).
- The NATMA founding memoir adds the 1984 detail: he 「振臂一呼」 to establish the association with 40 chapters, and the photo record of the 1970 Morgantown, West Virginia rally places him among the four renowned T.A. speakers ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys291|Our Journeys #291]]; [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/photo-albums-historical-15|Photo Album #15]]).
- At NATPA's landmark 1990 Taipei annual meeting he served as 生物醫學組召集人, personally inviting 彭明聰 and 吳昭新 to the biomedical session ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys47|Our Journeys #47]]).
