## Role in the Community
- Taiwanese American screenwriter of *The Sisterhood of Night* (dir. Caryn Waechter, released in theaters/VOD 2015-04-10), a modern re-telling of the Salem witch trials about cyber-bullying; she wrote in the story of three generations of a Taiwanese American family, inspired by her own. Recorded in the community interview [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/marilyn-fu|Meet Marilyn Fu – Screenwriter for The Sisterhood of Night]] (TaiwaneseAmerican.org, 2015-03-20, byline Ho Chie), and earlier in [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/interview-with-marilyn-fu-screenwriter-of-the-sisterhood-of-night|her 2012 Kickstarter-campaign interview]].
- Speaker at the 2015 NATWA II convention panel "Showcasing Ten Years of North American Taiwanese Women Artists," alongside Jennifer Chow, Tsuann Kuo, Karen Lin, and Victoria Linchong — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/natwa2015|NATWA 2015 record]].
- Related cast link: [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/sisterhood-of-night-catherine-huang|Catherine Huang record]] (the teen lead whose role Fu wrote).
