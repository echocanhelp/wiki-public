## Life facts

- Born 1921 in Taiwan; died March 2019 in New Jersey, US — per daughter 林淑麗's memoir 「716. 我的母親 / My Mother」 (`knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/my-stories-716.md`, bylined `Source from Shwu Lih Lin`, `Posted in 01/2020`).
- Maternal origin: 張簡 family of Daliao (大寮), now Kaohsiung; maternal grandfather (張簡 side) was village chief (村長) of Daliao during Japanese rule, and the family thrived in farming and sugar — per the same memoir.
- Graduated Showa Women's Pharmacy College (昭和女子藥學專門學校) in 1943 with a B.S. in Pharmacy (TAH self-report, `whos-who-2239`; the memoir adds that a botany class notebook from the 1940s pharmacy studies, with teacher's approval seals, is preserved by the family).
- Worked unpaid as pharmacist for her husband 林瑞雄's clinic while raising four children; after the children became independent the family moved to Japan, where a hospital pharmacist post was offered to her (father took a physician position in rural Ibaraki, later Okayama) — per the memoir.
- Japanese government pharmacist license issued 1984; the TAH self-report records "Acquired a Japanese pharmacist license at the age of 63", and the memoir says she "finally worked and got paid as a pharmacist at the age of 63" — the 1984 license date and age-63 account are consistent (1921 + 63 = 1984). License photo preserved by daughter 淑華 (Suhua).
- Retired from the Japan hospital in 1986 and first arrived in the U.S. that year in Metuchen, NJ (self-report first-year/first-city field `1986/Metuchen/NJ`, corroborated by the memoir's retirement year); parents then split time between Taiwan and the U.S. Husband died 1994 — per the memoir.
- In her U.S. years she became a regular at Shea Stadium (NY) for MLB games; a radio station sought a coverage interview via her English teacher, which she declined — per the memoir. Hobbies self-reported: Gardening, Cooking, Watching baseball.
- 2012-01: daughter 林淑麗 saw a Daliao 張簡 family-history exhibition at the Kaohsiung Historic Museum shortly after her sister's second son's wedding; 林張簡菊 attended a granddaughter's wedding in 2018 (memoir photo caption).
