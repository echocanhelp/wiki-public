## Role in the Community

- **NATMA founding, 1984.** At the founding convention of the North American Taiwanese Medical Association on 1984-07-14 in Ypsilanti, Michigan (Western Michigan University's Michigan Conference Center), attended by ~40 physicians, Hwang is recorded among the Michigan delegation — alongside 陳克孝, 陳哲雄, 張高文, 許世模 and others — corroborating his TAH-page status as a NATMA founding member ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys74|Our Journeys #74]], English edition [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys74-eng|Our Journeys #74 (EN)]]).
- **Chen Wen-cheng memorial, Ann Arbor.** In the memoir account of the Chen Wen-cheng (陳文成) memorial held in Ann Arbor — organized amid terror and common anger, with attendees flying in from Detroit, Lansing, Chicago — Hwang is named as the one who donated the funds necessary for the service, alongside Bernie Huang's (黃邦男) logistics, Rev. Ming-hsiung Wu's leadership of the service, and Chao-fang Chuang's elegy; the account records that students were deliberately not encouraged to attend for safety, yet several came anyway ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys321|Our Journeys #321]], English edition [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys321-eng|Our Journeys #321 (EN)]]).
- **Presbyterian & movement founding roles.** The TAH encyclopedia entry for Hwang records him as founder of the Taiwanese American Presbyterian Church and a founding member of FAPA, consistent with his NATMA founding role and his presidency of the Ann Arbor Taiwanese Association ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-2236-chiau-seng-hwang|TAH #2236 encyclopedia]]).
