## Role in the Community
- **1965-10** — Attended the Madison（威大）結盟大會 as the 明尼蘇達同鄉會 delegate, among the signatories from 台灣青年社、台灣共和國臨時政府、台灣人權委員會、台灣獨立聯盟 camps ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys81|81. 早期威大台灣學生在台灣建國運動所扮演的角色 / 周烒明]]).
- **1966-06** — Listed as the 雙子城（Minnesota）campus representative at the Philadelphia convention that founded 全美台灣獨立聯盟 (UFAI), and earlier among the campus 《台灣青年》/《台灣通訊》 circulation network ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys234|234. 台獨聯盟美國本部 / 台獨聯盟]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys81|周烒明 memoir]]).
- **1975–76, 1989–91** — 胡民祥's president考证 lists him first in the Pittsburgh 台灣同鄉會幹事團 in the 1974–75 同鄉錄 — presumed 會長 1975–1976 — and again for the 1989–1990 and 1990–1991 years ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys292|292. 早年匹茲堡台灣同鄉會簡介與歷屆會長考證 / 胡民祥]]).
- **1981** — Co-main organizer (with 許永華 in Ann Arbor) of the nationwide donation campaign after 陳文成's murder; 陳素貞 directed most of the US$50,000 raised into the 陳文成紀念基金會 scholarship; 許永華 notes 賴金德 was one of two同鄉 who helped 陳素貞 openly in Pittsburgh after 徐東龍 fell ill ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys321|321. 由陳文成紀念基金會的創立談起 / 許永華]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys8-eng|8. Professor Chen Wen-Chen Memorial Foundation (EN)]], [[organizations/professor-chen-wen-chen-memorial-foundation||陳文成紀念基金會]]).
