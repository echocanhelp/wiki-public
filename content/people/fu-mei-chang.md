## Role in the Community
- Recruited into the 全美台灣獨立聯盟 (Formosan League of American Community) during its 1966-67 "自由長征" campus tours, which consolidated Taiwanese student organizations and brought in members such as Harvard's 張富美 and Yale's 陳隆志 and 陳東壁 [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys81|Our Journeys 81]].
- Through 康寧祥's 1974 visit to the U.S., she became one of the Bay Area community leaders (with 艾琳達) who maintained long-term ties with the island's tang-wai (outside) opposition movement [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-357|Our Journeys 357 (協志會)]].
- In April 1978, the Bay Area churches, 協志會 and the 同鄉聯合會 formed the "Joint Committee of Taiwanese American for 1980 U.S. Census" and elected 張富美 as one of seven commissioners, urging Taiwanese to write in "Taiwanese American" on the census — a landmark in Taiwanese American identity-building [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-350|Our Journeys 350]].
- In 1993 she is recorded as National Assembly Member (張富美, legislator Annette Lu同期) attending the "China: A Turning Point in History" seminar at the University of Minnesota and speaking for the Taiwanese American community [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys123-eng|Our Journeys 123 (EN)]].
- A 北美台灣人教授協會 retrospective on the post-1990 return wave lists 國代張富美 among 台美人 who entered island politics alongside 台南縣長陳唐山、立委蔡同榮、許添財 — a second independent memoir record for the 國民大會代表 identity [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys259|Our Journeys 259]].

- Founding-era NATPA: elected the first 西部區域理事 at the 北美洲台灣人教授協會 founding board (with 張旭成 南部, 蔡丁財 東部, 廖述宗 中西部) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys69|Our Journeys 69]]. At NATPA's first return-to-Taiwan annual meeting (2004) she served as 文化組召集人 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys47|Our Journeys 47]].
- After the 美麗島事件 (1979-12-10), she invited Republican think-tank Professor Richard Kagan to Taiwan to observe the trials; Kagan's report back concluded the 黨外 marches were not rebellion — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys38|Our Journeys 38]].
- Named on the 北加州 blacklist (黑名單) of 1980s Taiwanese Americans whose ROC visas were refused or revoked — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys106|Our Journeys 106]].
- 協志會 20th-year record lists her as 民進黨海外國大代表, and as one of the scholars presenting at the 1987 二二八四十週年學術討論會 (Palo Alto, with 彭明敏、林宗義、陳芳明 等) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys37|Our Journeys 37]].
- NATWA founding memoir: at the 1988-03-13 organizing meeting under 呂秀蓮's leadership, attendees elected 張富美 first 會長 and 吳秀惠 副會長 (自動接任第二屆), with 呂秀蓮 名譽會長; charter ratified by all 45 members July 1988 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys60|Our Journeys 60]].
- Guest at the 第十屆台灣人夏令會 (1988-06-23, Converse College, SC; theme 台灣社會改革與婦女運動) alongside 艾琳達、黃美惠 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys245|Our Journeys 245]]; speaker at a 北加州聯合會 鄉情座談會 with 陳芳明、艾琳達 (1994, per the internal-dispute memoir) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys142|Our Journeys 142]].
