## Role in the Community
- Recruited into the 全美台灣獨立聯盟 (Formosan League of American Community) during its 1966-67 "自由長征" campus tours, which consolidated Taiwanese student organizations and brought in members such as Harvard's 張富美 and Yale's 陳隆志 and 陳東壁 [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys81|Our Journeys 81]].
- Through 康寧祥's 1974 visit to the U.S., she became one of the Bay Area community leaders (with 艾琳達) who maintained long-term ties with the island's tang-wai (outside) opposition movement [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-357|Our Journeys 357 (協志會)]].
- In April 1978, the Bay Area churches, 協志會 and the 同鄉聯合會 formed the "Joint Committee of Taiwanese American for 1980 U.S. Census" and elected 張富美 as one of seven commissioners, urging Taiwanese to write in "Taiwanese American" on the census — a landmark in Taiwanese American identity-building [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-350|Our Journeys 350]].
- In 1993 she is recorded as National Assembly Member (張富美, legislator Annette Lu同期) attending the "China: A Turning Point in History" seminar at the University of Minnesota and speaking for the Taiwanese American community [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys123-eng|Our Journeys 123 (EN)]].
