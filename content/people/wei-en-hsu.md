# Wei-En Hsu (徐惟恩)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Wei-En Hsu
- **Chinese:** 徐惟恩
- **Era:** Birth Place
- **Geography:** 台灣高雄市
- **Core roles:** professor, artist, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

A recent graduate of the Juilliard School, Mr Wei-En Hsu, pianist, organist, conductor, répétiteur and composer, is now having a busy performing career throughout the States, Europe, and Asia. A native of Taiwan, he received his BFA degree in Piano at Taipei National University of the Arts, and was pleased to be the only candidate at The Juilliard School, New York selected to Royal Academy of Music, London as an exchange student, studying with Malcolm Martineau and Aaron Shorr during 2004-05. While at Juilliard, he was a student of Jonathan Feldman and Margo Garrett.

## Timeline
- **原生台灣高雄市** — Pianist, organist, conductor, répétiteur and composer; active performing career across the US, Europe, and Asia
- **2003** — BFA/B.S. in Music (Piano), 國立台北藝術大學 (Taipei National University of the Arts)
- **2004–2005** — Exchange student at the Royal Academy of Music, London — the only Juilliard candidate selected; studied with Malcolm Martineau and Aaron Shorr
- **2006** — M.S. in Music, 紐約茱莉亞學院 (The Juilliard School, New York); student of Jonathan Feldman and Margo Garrett
- **2009** — Founded 紐約口袋歌劇團 (NY Pocket Opera), Music Director
- **2011** — Asia professional advisor, 卡內基海外音樂教育諮詢中心 (Carnegie overseas music-education consultancy, founder)
- **2012** — Music Director, 紐約大都會國際音樂節; Voice professor, 香港演藝學院音樂學院 (HKAPA School of Music)
- **Present** — Board director, Vital Opera, New York City
