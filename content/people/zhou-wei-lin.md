# Zhou Weilin (周威霖)

**Zhou Weilin** (周威霖) is a Taiwanese American community figure who served as Secretary General (秘書長) of the Taiwanese American Historical Society (TAHS) and contributed multiple profiles to the 2017 TAHS Publication (台美菁英錄).

## Role in TAHS
- **Secretary General (秘書長):** Served as TAHS Secretary General (self-identified in profile bylines)
- **Preparatory member:** Attended the first TAHS preparatory meeting on August 28, 2013 in Fullerton, CA

## Role in 2017 TAHS Publication
- **Profile author:** Authored 徐新宏 (Hsu Hsin-hung) profile (Part 1, L1405–1584)
- **Profile author:** Compiled 王桂榮 (Wang Gui-rong) profile (Part 1, L1879–2396)
- **Co-author:** Co-authored 王能祥 (Wang Neng-xiang) profile with 楊嘉猷 (founding president) (Part 1, L2494–2559)
- **Editor/Compiler:** Listed among the publication's editorial team

## Notes
- Listed separately from 周威廉 (Zhou Weiliang), who also served as an editor/compiler and wrote the 緣起與展望 founding essay. Both names appear in the 2017 publication's editor list; they are distinct individuals.
