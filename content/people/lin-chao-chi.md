# Lin Chao-chi (林朝棨)

**Lin Chao-chi** (林朝棨) was a renowned Taiwanese geologist, best known for discovering the 十三行遺址 (Shihping Archaeological Site), one of Taiwan's most significant archaeological sites. He was the third uncle (三舅) of biochemist 廖述宗 (Liao Shu-zong).

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Lin Chao-chi
- **Chinese:** 林朝棨
- **Profession:** Geologist
- **Geography:** Taiwan
- **Core roles:** Prominent Taiwanese geologist; 十三行遺址 (Shihping site) discoverer
- **Relation:** 三舅 (third uncle) of 廖述宗 (Liao Shu-zong)

## Scientific Contributions

Lin Chao-chi was a famous geologist whose most notable achievement was the discovery of the 十三行遺址 (Shihping Archaeological Site), an important prehistoric archaeological site in Taiwan. This discovery contributed significantly to the understanding of Taiwan's ancient cultures and history.

His reputation as a geologist made him well-known enough to be specifically mentioned in biographical accounts of his nephew 廖述宗, reflecting the prominence of the Lin family in Taiwanese science.

## Family Connections

As the third uncle (三舅) of 廖述宗, Lin Chao-chi was part of the extended family network that included 廖述宗's mother [[people/lin-qiong-xian||林瓊仙]] (Lin Qiong-xian) and second uncle 林朝權 (Lin Chao-quan). The memorial account by [[people/yang-jia-you||楊嘉猷]] (Charles Yang) specifically notes Lin Chao-chi's fame for discovering the 十三行遺址.

## Fengyuan Family & Network (absorbed from [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-350||張瑞雄口述 (Our Journeys #350)]])

- Father **林尚** was the first local gentry in 豐原 (Fengyuan) to own a bicycle.
- Elder brother **林朝權** (see [[people/lin-chao-quan||林朝權]]) went to China after the war; younger brother **林朝業** was a dentist.
- Held a professorship in the **geology department of National Taiwan University (台大地質系)**.
- In their hometown, 林朝棨, 張炳松, and 廖五湖 were known as the **「豐原三傑」** (Three Worthies of Fengyuan); 廖五湖 later served as principal of 員林中學校.
- Painter [[people/liao-ji-chun||廖繼春 (Liao Ji-chun)]]'s wife was 林朝棨's sister; their son is [[people/liao-shu-zong||廖述宗 (Liao Shu-zong)]], founding president of the North American Taiwanese Professors Association — making Lin the third maternal uncle (三舅) on his mother [[people/lin-qiong-xian||林瓊仙]]'s side.
