## Role in the Community
- **1976** — In the Los Angeles area, co-founded the 台灣人權及文化協會 (Taiwan Human Rights & Culture Association) with 許世楷、許千惠、郭清江、張綺石、王廷宜(泰和) and other human-rights-minded Taiwanese Americans; the group agreed to cooperate on human-rights work under the umbrella of the Formosan Association for Human Rights — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys75|75. 台灣人權協會的開始與現況 / 張丁蘭 / 2014-12]], [[organizations/taiwan-human-rights-and-culture-association|Taiwan Human Rights & Culture Association]], [[organizations/formosan-association-for-human-rights|FAHR]]. (Per 張丁蘭's memoir: 「同年」 refers to the same year she describes the 1976 founding of FAHR's predecessor in New York.)
- **1982-01-21** — Attended the 16-person meeting convened by 許英智 (then vice president of the Southern California Taiwanese Association for Interculture, SCTAI) that resolved to form the Taiwanese American Citizens League (TACL); 鄭德和 is named among the attendees in 周實's founding memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys272|272. 台美公民協會的成長：從孕育到發芽 / 周實 / 2017-01]] — [[organizations/taiwanese-american-citizens-league|TACL]].
- **1985-04-15** — Named among the signatories of the 台美公民協會簡介 (TACL introduction) published in 台灣公論報 issue 369, p.9; preserved as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys186|186. 台美公民協會簡介 / 台美公民協會籌備委員會 / 2015-11]].
