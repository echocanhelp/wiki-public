## Role in the Community
- Established the first Taiwan Center in the United States, in Flushing, New York — see [[organizations/new-york-taiwan-center|New York Taiwan Center]].
- Community author: contributed the essay [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/28-e7-b4-90-e7-b4-84-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e6-9c-83-e9-a4-a8-e7-9a-84-e6-88-90-e7-ab|28. 紐約台灣會館的成立與展望]] (2014-09-05) to the TAH story corpus — first-person account of the Taiwan Center's founding and outlook.
- Co-purchaser of the New York Taiwan Center: per the community memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys304|304. 心心念念一間厝 紐約台灣會館起家 (黃兆平, 2017-07)]], 樊豐美, 李汝城 and 陳隆豐 were among 37 股東 who registered 「Taitower公司」 (50 shares at US$12,500 each) to buy the 會館 property, then leased it to the Taiwan Center for a symbolic US$1/year; the Center opened 1986-07-19.
- 1991–92 survival-drive organizer: named in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys27|Our Journeys 27]] among the four 有志 (陳隆豐、樊豐美、黃再添、賴弘典) who kept fundraising for the debt-ridden 會館 through its存亡關鍵 period, alongside [[organizations/new-york-taiwan-center|New York Taiwan Center]].
- Encyclopedia profile: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-56-fon-may-fan|56. Fon-May Fan 樊豐美]] (2014-10-04, band B — bibliographic record; full text stays in the vault).
- Named subject in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys27-eng|Our Journeys 27 (ENG)]] alongside [[people/patrick-huang|Patrick Huang]], [[people/dr-lung-fong-chen|Dr. Lung-Fong Chen]], and [[organizations/new-york-taiwan-center|New York Taiwan Center]].
- HOLD: memoir names the 理事長 as 陳隆豐 while the OJ27 Subjects block links [[people/lung-chen|陳隆]] — same-person identity not auto-merged.
