## Role in the Community

Per the church's own history in the TAHS *Our Journeys* corpus ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys257|洛杉磯台福基督教會沿革 / 台福傳播中心, 2016]]):

- **1975-10** — Called as senior pastor (主任牧師) of 台語福音教會 (Evangelical Formosan Church, Los Angeles), succeeding 高集樂 (Samuel Kau), who had returned to Taiwan to teach. The English account in the same record states: "In 1975 Pastor Felix Liu was installed as the senior pastor. He has continued to serve in this position to this present date."
- Under his leadership the church grew through four migrations: El Sereno (1973) → Highland Park (1977) → El Monte (爱滿地, 1996 — a 3.8-acre former warehouse site). The church multiplied daughter congregations (東安, 南灣, 聖谷, 柑縣, 聖安, 聖喜, 聖達, and the English ministry Harvest San Gabriel Valley, 1997) and joined the 台語福音教會總會 formed 1982-10.
- The El Monte campus became a community hub: the sub-hall was rented to a community senior center on weekdays, and joint Chinese-American evangelistic, spiritual-training, and music conferences were regularly held in the main hall; the hall was also lent cheaply to an Indonesian congregation.
- Profile piece by 劉孟儒: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/268-e5-8a-89-e5-af-8c-e7-90-86-e5-a4-ab-e5-94-b1-e5-a9-a6-e9-9a-a8-e7-82-ba-e7-a|268. 劉富理 夫唱婦隨 為神謀事 (2015/01)]] — a community feature on his and 王仁美's joint ministry ("husband and wife singing in chorus, working for God").

The corpus record is consistent with the TAH Who's Who entry ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/214-felix-liu-e5-8a-89-e5-af-8c-e7-90-86201501|TAH Who's Who #214]]) — Pasadena/El Monte 台福 ministry — and adds the primary-source pastorate timeline above.
