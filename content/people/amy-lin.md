# Amy Lin (林惠美)

**Amy Lin (林惠美)** is a Taipei-born pianist and pharmacist. TAH Who’s Who tags her artist, musician, painter, pharmacist, and pianist. The music trail in the archive is **accompanist / church pianist / trio**, not a concert-career bio.

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Amy Lin
- **Chinese:** 林惠美
- **Born:** year not on TAH card; birthplace 台北市
- **Mother (piano teacher):** 黃蕊花老師 (Chinese TAH bio)
- **Husband:** 賴玉森醫師 (married 1992; no Echopedia page)
- **Geography:** Taipei → California (1981) → Ohio (1992)
- **Core roles:** choir accompanist; church pianist; Ariel Trio pianist; hospital pharmacy director (Taiwan); US pharmacist
- **Tyzen link:** TAH: attended the **same church** as Prof. [[people/tyzen-hsiao||Tyzen Hsiao]] and served as 教會司琴. **Named congregation is not given** — do not invent a SoCal pew (Tyzen page: named SoCal home pew still missing)

## Overview

English TAH blurb: she learned piano from her mother; at National Taiwan University she accompanied the NTU choir and the Medical School Choir (Sing-Lin / 杏林); as Pharmacy Director at Mackay Memorial Hospital (TAH English also spells **Mackey**) she accompanied the hospital choir directed by **Rev. Gin-Rong Cheng**; later she and friends formed **Ariel Trio** (clarinet, soprano, piano) and “play for meals.”

Chinese TAH bio (same card), credited **摘自北美洲台灣婦女會第十三屆年會音樂會手冊**:

- 台北市人。自幼隨母（黃蕊花老師）學琴
- 台大藥學系：台大合唱團及杏林合唱團伴奏
- 馬偕醫院聖歌隊伴奏
- **1981** 移民加州；取得碩士文憑及藥師執照
- **與蕭泰然教授同教會，並為教會司琴**
- **1992** 與賴玉森醫師結婚，移居 Ohio
- 中西部 NATMA 朋友的熱誠…使生活充滿平和與歡樂
- 已由職場退休，專心做農婦，畫水彩，參與 Ariel Trio

Hobby on the card: 畫水彩. TAH family table on the English form is blank; the Chinese paragraph is the family source. **Do not publish** the email address that appears on the TAH card.
