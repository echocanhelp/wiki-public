## Timeline
- 1948 — born, Tainan, Taiwan — per TAH Who's Who profile (record id 73319, `modified` 2026-06-19T16:38:00), archived in [[sources/tah-whoswho-prof-wentai-liu-capture|the profile capture]]
- 1971 — B.S., 國立交通大學電子工程系 (National Chiao Tung University, electronic engineering) — year and major printed by 交大電機學院 系友專訪 「電子眼之父劉文泰：台灣有實力進軍國際醫材」 2018-01-24, https://iee.nycu.edu.tw/tw/news/p1.php?num=111 ; TAH left the degree year blank
- 1975 (reported) — 教育部公費留學 exam, the sole 資訊科學 representative among 12 scholars — per 遠見雜誌 2007-07-01 「32年前」 retrospective (https://www.gvm.com.tw/article/11848, archived 2026-11-17); `alleged`, a computed year from a 2007 article, no MOE roster archived
- M.S., 國立台灣大學電機工程所 (National Taiwan University, electrical engineering) — per [[sources/tah-whoswho-prof-wentai-liu-capture|the profile capture]] and the UCLA official site 官網 Education block
- 1983 — Ph.D., Computer Engineering, University of Michigan, Ann Arbor — TAH record (`Education`) plus the UCSC new-faculty profile https://currents.ucsc.edu/02-03/05-12/newfac.html (major stated) and NCKU research-output profile `https://researchoutput.ncku.edu.tw/en/persons/wen-tai-liu/`
- 1983 — joined North Carolina State University, Dept. of Electrical and Computer Engineering — per UCSC emeriti 簡歷 https://emeriti.ucsc.edu/abstracts/Wentai%20Liu.html
- 1986 — IEEE Outstanding Paper Award (the 1986 instance; the UCLA official site lists 1986 and 2010) — per https://samueli.ucla.edu/people/wentai-liu/
- 1988 — joined the Artificial Retina Project, recruited by Dr. Mark Humayun (then Duke) while a professor of computer and electrical engineering at NC State; had just received tenure in integrated-chip design — per UCLA Newsroom 2013-03-21 https://newsroom.ucla.edu/stories/wentai-liu-artificial-retina-244393
- 1992 (published) — led the wave-pipelining research group at NC State; team CMOS chips demonstrated at the equivalent of 250 MHz, one circuit at 1 GHz — Elisabeth Geake, New Scientist 1992-02-22 https://www.newscientist.com/article/1825236-technology-unclogging-the-chips-electronic-snarl-ups/
- 1994 (published) — matched-delay technique IC designs (digital sampler, data generator, 622 Mbps clock/data recovery, clock waveform shaper) with R. Cavin, G. Moyer, M. Clements, T. Schaffer, J. Kang, J. Medero at NCSU — NCSU ERL paper `archives.ece.ncsu.edu/erl/html2/papers/wentai/1994/NCSU-ERL-WENTAI-94-05.pdf` (contact e-mail `wentai@eos.ncsu.edu`)
- 1997 (documented) — held the rank associate professor of electrical engineering at NC State and the Alcoa Chair Professorship; artificial-retina component-chip work with UNC-CH's Elliot McGucken and Johns Hopkins' Humayun / de Juan, funded by NSF and Fight for Sight — NCSU College of Engineering News, Summer 1997, `knowledge/web-archives/prof-wentai-liu-thirdparty-20261117/ncsu-news1997.pdf`
- 2003 (May) — joined UC Santa Cruz, Baskin School of Engineering, Professor of Electrical Engineering — https://currents.ucsc.edu/02-03/05-12/newfac.html
- 2003–2011 — Professor of Electronic Engineering, Univ. of California, Santa Cruz — per the same capture (`Employment` field)
- 2004 (Oct) — led UCSC's participation in the DOE Artificial Retina partnership signed in Chicago with five DOE national labs, Second Sight Medical Products, USC Doheny and NC State; campus director of the NSF Engineering Research Center on Biomimetic MicroElectronic Systems (BMES) — https://news.ucsc.edu/2004/10/ucsc-researchers-join-in-new-partnership-to-speed-development-of-an-artificial-retina-to-restore-sight/
- 2007 (Jun) — announced the second-generation 64-pixel retinal implant and the plan to start surgery in Taiwan (台北榮總, 台大醫院, 長庚); NCTU named him director of its new 智慧型仿生裝置研究中心 (Intelligent Prosthesis Research Center) — 遠見雜誌 2007-07-01, https://www.gvm.com.tw/article/11848
- 2008 (Jun) — chip of his design implanted in a blind patient; 10 subjects enrolled in the Phase I Argus II trial at four US ophthalmic centers, with trials also in Mexico, Switzerland, France and the UK — https://news.ucsc.edu/2008/06/microchip-developed-by-ucsc-engineer-is-helping-restore-vision-to-the-blind/
- 2009 — R&D 100 Award; Alcoa Foundation Distinguished Engineering Research Award; NASA Group Achievement Award — each printed with its year on the UCLA official site awards list https://samueli.ucla.edu/people/wentai-liu/ (R&D 100 dated 2009 there and in the UCLA Samueli 2012 new-faculty release)
- 2010 — Popular Mechanics Breakthrough Award — corroborated by the UCLA official site awards list and https://samueli.ucla.edu/new-faculty-join-ucla-engineering/ (2012-10-02), no longer only `alleged`
- 2011 (near end of) — joined the UCLA Henry Samueli School of Engineering and Applied Science — per UCLA Newsroom 2013-03-21; the TAH `Employment` row `2011-` is the same move
- 2011 (CE Mark) / 2013 (US FDA market approval) — Argus II Retinal Prosthesis System cleared, its core implant chip designed by Liu — per the official site / 官網 https://samueli.ucla.edu/people/wentai-liu/ and https://newsroom.ucla.edu/stories/wentai-liu-artificial-retina-244393
- 2012 (Winter term) — formal UCLA faculty start as Professor of Bioengineering — https://samueli.ucla.edu/new-faculty-join-ucla-engineering/ (2012-10-02); the 2011/2012 discrepancy is kept as a dated conflict, see GAPS
- 2015 — co-founded ANEUVO with Yi-Kai Lo (PhD, UCLA Bioengineering) for neuromodulation; first product ExaStim® Stimulation System later received CE Mark in Europe and US FDA clearance, with broader rollout planned in 2026 — company page https://aneuvo.com/us/about/
- 2015 — developed with UCLA colleagues the flexible multi-electrode array for epidural spinal-cord stimulation that restored walking in a rat SCI model — UC Technology Transfer disclosure `https://techtransfer.universityofcalifornia.edu/NCD/28941.html` (US 10,773,074) and https://newsroom.ucla.edu/stories/from-brain-to-gut-wentai-liu-engineers-hope-through-bioelectronic-medicine (2025-03-25)
- 2016-11-29 — elected IEEE Fellow «for contributions to electronically-aided neural prosthetic devices», effective 2017-01-01; announced by his own department https://www.bioeng.ucla.edu/prof-wentai-liu-has-been-named-ieee-fellow/ and by https://samueli.ucla.edu/four-ucla-engineering-faculty-named-ieee-fellows/ — the self-reported `IEEE Fellow, 2016` is now third-party corroborated
- 2017 (Apr) — reported 19 paralysis patients able to stand after 12 weeks of rehabilitation in his lab's trials; Argus II then implanted in more than 200 patients in the US and Europe; he had licensed the bionic-eye technology to Second Sight three years earlier (≈2014) — 今周刊 2017-04-20 https://www.businesstoday.com.tw/article/category/80394/post/201704200013/ and its English twin Taiwan Today 2017-06-16 https://taiwantoday.tw/economics/top-news/116963/
- 2018 (Jan) — returned to Taiwan to seek investors for the spinal-cord technology and to establish a medical-equipment company — 交大電機學院 系友專訪 2018-01-24, https://iee.nycu.edu.tw/tw/news/p1.php?num=111
- 2024–2025 (reported) — Visiting Distinguished Chair Professor, Department of Biomedical Engineering, 國立成功大學 (NCKU), also described as 成功大學玉山學者 — `https://researchoutput.ncku.edu.tw/en/persons/wen-tai-liu/` and 醫智亮加速器 MitoBit Club 講座 MB16 2025-01-03 (演講 2024-12-18) https://glintmed.com/news/2328
