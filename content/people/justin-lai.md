## From the record (third-party, web pass 2026-11-16)

Per the third-party capture page [[sources/justin-lai-thirdparty-capture|Justin Lai 賴正雄 — third-party record capture]] (HTML archived at `knowledge/web-archives/justin-lai-thirdparty/`, 2026-11-16; search budget 6/8):

- **嘉中 teaching years (documented).** 國立嘉義高中百週年校慶紀念特刊 carries two independent student memoirs naming him as his 高一導師 respectively 三年級導師: 「高一導師賴正雄，畢業於臺大外文系，他教授英文口說、文法，帶讀課外讀物，還找美國傳教士到班上進行英文授課，當時還因此驚動了教官。」(p293) and 「三年級導師賴正雄老師…耐心引導徬徨的我們思考未來方向」 (p295) — `library.cysh.cy.edu.tw/100-Anniversary`, institution-published.
- **Book #1 publication data (documented).** 指南書局 (Compass Publishing) product post 2008-11-19 prints for 英文字詞研究 (Concise English Usage): 「ISBN13: 978-986-824994-3｜300 pages｜Paperback｜©2007｜ 作者 賴正雄 - Golden State University 教育學博士」; 三民網路書店 lists 「出版日：2007-09-01 作者：賴正雄 出版社：指南書局」 — the self-reported degree credential and the book's publisher/ISBN/date are independently published.
- **南加州嘉中校友會 co-founder (documented, with conflicts).** 母校官網 CYHSASC introduction: 「在沈建成、楊茂森及賴正雄三位校友的發起下，南加州嘉中校友會1999年成立於洛杉磯」; 嘉中百週年特刊 p190 (撰稿 沈毓賢會長): 「南加州嘉中校友會在 1999 年 9 月 25 日南加州蒙特利公園召開成立大會。與會者選出沈建成為首任會長」. The subject's own 2016/2018 posts read 「美國南加州嘉中校友會創會人之一」 and date the founding 「早在 2000年」 with co-founders 「陳茂森、沈建成」.
- **Alumni-community publisher (sourced — second self-report venue).** He founded 台大外文1963 (ntu1963.blogspot.com, ~2015; 100,285 visits announced 2020-10-25) and 嘉中校友全聯會 AACHW2013 (chiayihi.blogspot.com, founded 2013; 102,000+ visits announced 2018-09-22), and runs the 英語漫談 series where 字詞研究/散文 pieces are dated and bylined 「Justin Lai (賴正雄) 編/編譯」.
- **Class of 1959 嘉中 → 台大外文 1959–1963 (sourced).** His own 2017-07-09 obituary of classmate 許文正: 「自 1953年在嘉義中學首次相識」「1959年汝我同時考上台大外文系，同班者尚有羅正展、范板雄、賴國雄(歿)、陳吉雄、賴政孝、暨本人共柒名」「大學畢業後，兄、國雄、板雄和本人共肆人，同時回到母校-嘉義高中任教英文」 — matches the TAH form (NTU 外文 1963) and the yearbook's 「畢業於臺大外文系」.
- **Residence (sourced).** 2014–2024 datelines from his own posts read 「寫於美國南加州」 (latest 2024-03-08).

## Timeline

- 1953 — first met classmate 許文正 on the 嘉義中學 campus (per his own 2017-07-09 obituary; `sourced`).
- 1959 — graduated 嘉義中學; entered 台大外文系 the same year with 許文正 and five other 嘉中 alumni (`sourced`).
- 1959–1963 — 台大外文系; founded a senior-year Discussion Club and staged a one-act play 「the Dear Departed」 directed by 郝繼隆神父 (Fr. O'Hara), per his 2020-10-25 post (`sourced`).
- 1963 — B.A. 外國語文學系, 台大 (TAH form; corroborated by the yearbook 「畢業於臺大外文系」).
- 1964–1965 (民國53–54) — returned to 母校 嘉義中學 to teach English, hired straight into 高三 per his 2024-03-19 memoir (`sourced`); yearbook memoirs independently name him 高一 and 三年級 導師 (`documented`).
- 1986 — M.A. / 教育學博士, Golden State University; Professor, Golden State University (TAH self-report; the 指南書局 product page independently prints 「Golden State University 教育學博士」).
- 1991 — Professor, Univ. of CA, Riverside (TAH self-report; no independent faculty record found — see GAPS).
- 1999 — co-founded 南加州嘉中校友會 per 母校官網 (「1999年成立於洛杉磯」) and 特刊 p190 (成立大會 1999-09-25, 蒙特利公園) (`documented`); his own posts say 2000 (`sourced`, HOLD conflict).
- 2007 — 英文字詞研究 (Concise English Usage) published by 指南書局: ISBN13 978-986-824994-3, 300 pages, paperback (`documented`).
- 2013 — founded 嘉中校友全聯會 blog AACHW2013 (per his 2018-09-22 retrospective; `sourced`).
- ~2015 — founded 台大外文1963 blog ntu1963.blogspot.com (「about 5 years ago」 per 2020-10-25 post; `sourced`).
- 2017-01-01 — 新年感言 written 2016-12-31 「於美國南加州」, prints 「虛度光陰，七十有六」 (`sourced`).
- 2020-10-25 — announced 100,285 visits to ntu1963.blogspot.com (`sourced`).
- 2024-03-08 — dated essay in 懷念嘉中恩師（2）-100, signed 「賴正雄（Justin Lai) 寫於美國…時年八十有五」 (`sourced`).
