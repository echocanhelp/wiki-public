## Timeline
- **1935** — Born in Taichung, Taiwan
- **1961** — M.D., National Taiwan University College of Medicine
- **1966–1972** — Instructor of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Lecturer, NTU College of Medicine
- **1969–1971** — Genetics researcher, New York State Univ. Southern California Medical Center
- **1974–1976** — Researcher (Taiwan Society of Perinatology), New York Einstein Medical Center
- **1976–2001** — Professor and Director of the High-Risk Obstetrics Clinic, OB-GYN Dept., University of Chicago
- **1979** — First certificate holder of Maternal & Fetal Medicine (per TAH #120 encyclopedia record held in vault)
- **1980-02-16** — One of sixteen professors at the first NATPA founding-preparation meeting, University of Chicago International House (per [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys69|Our Journeys #69]] memoir)
- **2001–** — Lifetime Honorary Professor (Professor Emeritus for Life), University of Chicago

## Community Leadership
- President, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/brief-history-of-natpa||North American Taiwanese Professors' Association (NATPA) 北美洲台灣人教授協會]] — see also [[organizations/north-america-taiwanese-professors-sc||NATPA Southern California chapter]]
- Board Member and Chair of the Education Committee, [[organizations/natma-southern-california||North American Taiwanese Medical Association (NATMA) 北美洲台灣人醫師協會]]

## Role in the Community (corpus-absorbed)

- NATMA's founding memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys74|Our Journeys #74]] names him（芝加哥的林靜竹）as one of the trusted, like-minded doctors who responded enthusiastically to [[people/dr-suy-ming-sam-chou|周烒明]]'s city-by-city recruitment calls for Taiwanese physicians to join the association — direct documented evidence of his Chicago-area NATMA organizing role, consistent with the Board Member / Education Committee chair record above (English version: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys74-eng|Our Journeys #74 (EN)]]).
- NATPA founding: he is named among the sixteen professors who attended the first NATPA preparation meeting on **1980-02-16** at the University of Chicago International House (with 張錦哲, 張旭成, 陳炳杞, 黃金來, 賴義雄, 伍焜玉 and others), where the draft constitution was debated and the decision made that the association should hold meetings in Taiwan and give research back to the homeland — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys69|Our Journeys #69]]; he is listed in the association's early roster as 芝加哥大学婦產科敎授 in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys79|Our Journeys #79]].
- He authored [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys47|Our Journeys #47]] on the historical significance of NATPA's first-ever annual meeting held in Taiwan (2004) — written by him in 2014, corroborating his NATPA presidency era.
- Peace Church network (和平契友): in the 第一屆夏令會 memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys268|Our Journeys #268]] he is listed among the New York-area contacts of the 台北和平教會 covenant-friend network used to recruit summer-conference attendees — pre-NATPA movement-organizing context alongside 許登龍, 李豐明, 黃賢理 and others.
- He is also a published essayist/memoirist in the corpus: obituary memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-654|TAH #654: 懷念許世真（10/2018）]] and his literature collection [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1130|TAH #1130: 愛心集——林靜竹教授文集 第二集（08/2017, 文學）]].
