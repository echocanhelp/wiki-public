## Role in the Community
- Keynote speaker at the Intercollegiate Taiwanese American Students Association (ITASA) East Coast Conference in New York City; in the 2013 TaiwaneseAmerican.org interview she spoke about how her Taiwanese roots shaped her labor organizing, crediting a nanny's story as her motivation — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/ai-jen-poo-inspiring-across-generations|Ai-Jen Poo: Inspiring Across Generations (TA online, 2013)]].
- The TA community documented her as a model of Taiwanese Americans "having a role in helping the community beyond themselves"; her Time 100 (2012) listing in the Identity Snapshot matches the interview's account, which adds Newsweek's 150 Fearless Women list.
- Her own encyclopedia record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-2040-ai-jen-poo|TAH #2040 Ai-Jen Poo 蒲艾真]].
