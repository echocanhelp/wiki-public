## Timeline
- 1955 — B.S. in Geology, National Taiwan Univ.; 1961 — M.S. in Earth Science, National Taiwan Univ.
- 1958–1959 — Shihmen Dam Construction, Taiwan
- 1961–1964 — Tsengwen Dam Construction, Taiwan Water Conservancy Bureau; 1961–1962 Engineering Geological Training, US Bureau of Reclamation, Denver
- 1971 — Ph.D. in Earth Science, Washington Univ., St Louis; Assistant Professor there 1971–1973, during which he participated in Apollo 11 & 12 lunar samples studies
- 1973 — Union Dam Project, MO, US Corps of Engineers
- 1973–1975 — Senior Geologist, Geological Investigation, Bechtel Inc., San Francisco
- 1975–1983 — US Geological Survey, Menlo Park — Geothermal Resources Investigation
- 1983–1988 — Bureau of Land Management, Sacramento — Geothermal Resources Investigation
- 1988–2002 — CA Environmental Protection, Department of Toxic Substances Control
- 2005–2020 — Docent, Hornet Aircraft Carrier Museum, Alameda, CA
