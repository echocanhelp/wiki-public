# Lisa Liu (劉雅薇)

**Lisa Liu (劉雅薇)** is **財務長** on the official board of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館)]]. In 2021–2022 Taiwan Justice community reporting she is also named **會長** of 洛杉磯台美商會 (Taiwanese American Chamber of Commerce of Greater Los Angeles), speaking for chamber visits and Orange County political events. This page absorbs those **already-cited vault facts only**. A full personal biography is **not** in the Taiwan Center board source set (Content priority C).

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Lisa Liu
- **Chinese:** 劉雅薇
- **Era:** Contemporary (documented 2021–present on vault sources)
- **Geography:** Greater Los Angeles / Orange County (柑縣) community events

- **Organization:** [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center (TWCLA)]]

## Overview

On the archived taiwancenter.org board roster, Liu is the foundation’s **財務長**, sitting with officers including 董事長 [[people/alan-thian||Alan Thian (田詒鴻)]], 執行長 [[people/simon-lin||Simon Lin (林榮松)]], 秘書長 [[people/ken-wu||Ken Wu (吳兆峯)]], 副秘書長 [[people/ray-jan||Ray Jan (冉瑞宜)]], and several 副董事長. The same Chinese name appears on later 台灣會館 director lists for gala / 年會 ticket contacts (Pacific Palms Resort copy preserved on sibling board pages).

Separately, 台灣公論報 / 中央社 community copy names **洛杉磯台美商會會長劉雅薇** at a 2021 factory visit in Chino (樂立杯 / Karat Packaging) and at a 2022 Orange County fundraiser for U.S. Rep. Michelle Steel (朴銀珠), appearing with 台灣會館董事長 [[people/paul-chen||Paul Chen (陳柏宇)]]. Those articles do **not** spell the English given name; Echopedia keeps the Taiwan Center romanization **Lisa Liu** for the same 劉雅薇 string. No Echopedia organization page currently exists for 洛杉磯台美商會; the chamber is named in prose only.

Do not merge this person with Linda Liu / 劉玲華 (副董事長) or invent education, birth year, employer, or family. Those fields are absent from the board archive and the two Justice articles.
