# C. M Yang (楊啓明)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** C. M Yang
- **Chinese:** 楊啓明
- **Era:** 1928
- **Geography:** 屏東高樹鄉
- **Core roles:** Taiwanese American (TAH Who’s Who)
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Founded the Formosa Club of Eastern US (美東福爾摩莎俱樂部), the predecessor organization to the Taiwanese American Association of New York (TAA NY)

## Role in the Community

- His 2019 TAH Who's Who encyclopedia record — the community's own account of his founding of the Formosa Club of Eastern US, precursor to TAA NY — is held in the corpus at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-2220|2220. C. M Yang 楊啓明/11/2019 (2019-11-04)]].
- The Formosa Club that Yang's record describes as the seed of the Taiwanese American Association appears repeatedly in the TAH "Our Journeys" community memoir corpus, e.g. the TAA/Columbus chapter newsletter line "Formosa Club is our big family" (Newsletters of TAA/Columbus Chapter, 12/11/1972): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-354|354. What did you do when you were young (年輕時做的活動)]], and club-or-association taggings in member memoirs such as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys247|247. 我準備「台灣之夜」的經過 / 廖郭珠真 /09/2016]]. These corroborate the Formosa Club → TAA organizational lineage his record claims.
