## Role in the Community

- **Indianapolis chapter, founder and second president.** The 2023 chapter memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-389|389. 印城台灣同鄉會紀要]] names him among the founders of 印城台灣同鄉會 (Taiwanese-American Association of Indianapolis, 1978 — 吳群也, 吳良也, 楊水森, 余金榜 and him) and lists him as its **second 會長 (1979–1980)**, after founding president 吳群也. He lived in 綠野市 (Greenfield), Indiana 1976–1982 and later wrote that grassroots-America experience up as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/79-e6-9c-b1-e8-80-80-e6-ba-90-e8-aa-8d-e8-ad-98-e7-be-8e-e5-9c-8b-e7-9a-84-e8-8d|#79 認識美國的草根文化: 美國.印第安那州.綠野市.1976-1982 (2014)]].
- **NATPA vice president.** The NATPA ten-year memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys47|Our Journeys #47 (NATPA 十年的回顧 1980–1990)]] records him as the association's Taiwan-based 副會長 who negotiated the blacklist question with KMT overseas-affairs officials (章孝嚴, 鄭心雄) ahead of the 1990 tenth-annual conference, and as a donor (三千元) to that conference.
- **Essayist in the community press.** Two of his essays appear in the Our Journeys collection index [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys107|Our Journeys #107]]: 認識花卉 and 瞭解台灣,台美人能為台灣做什麼. On taiwanjustice.net he is tagged as author of the column series 疫情中的省思 [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2022/20220929020337_tag_朱耀源_ef99f8e56443e579|taiwanjustice.net 朱耀源 tag]].

## Career Notes

Absorbed from his TAH records (#17, #87, #99, #284) already in the vault:

- Born 1937 in Hiroshima, Japan; educated at National Chung Hsing Univ. (B.S.) and the University of Tokyo (Doctor of Agriculture).
- In 1986 he was the first president (not owner) of Twylord Plant Laboratories, Ventura, CA — per TAH #87, which frames him as the first president of a business enterprise founded by Taiwanese Americans.
- Career in horticulture and biotech: Vice President of Ball Horticulture Inc.; President of Twyford International Inc.; founder and president of Taiwan Flower Biotechnology Co., LTD (台灣花卉生物技術股份有限公司) and of A-Turn Biotech Company (艾騰生技股份有限公司).
- Community: founding member of the Taiwanese American Association / Indiana Chapter — the network held in the vault as [[organizations/taiwanese-association-of-indiana|Taiwanese Association of Indiana]]; see Role in the Community for the 1978 founding and his 1979–1980 term as its second president.
- TAH #284 records his forward-looking column writing (前瞻未來, 專欄寫作, by 吳瑞惠, 2015-06).
