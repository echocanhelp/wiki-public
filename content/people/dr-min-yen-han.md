# Dr. Min-Yen Han (韓明元醫師)

This page is about Dr. Min-Yen Han (韓明元醫師), a physician (internist; cardiology
training) recorded in the TAH Foundation Who's Who (record #2010), who completed his M.D. at
National Taiwan Univ. College of Medicine in 1969 and his residency at French Polyclinic
Hospital, practiced in Vernon, NJ (residence of record Hamburg, NJ per the NTUMCAANA 2024
roster), served as Chairman of the Board of 北美台大醫學院校友會 (NTUMCAA-NA) in 2009, and is a
member of Tzu Chi International Medical Association, TIMA (國際慈濟人醫會成員).
