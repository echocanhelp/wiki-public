# Dr. Yunching Yeh Davis (葉芸青)

**Dr. Yunching Yeh Davis (葉芸青)** served as **Moderator of the National Taiwanese Presbyterian Council (NTPC)** of the Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.) and authored the NTPC foreword **"Half a Century of Faith and Mission"** for the 2025 republication of Dr. Albert S. Lai's 1971 dissertation *Toward A Community of Hope*.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Dr. Yunching Yeh Davis
- **Chinese:** 葉芸青
- **Era:** Contemporary (documented as NTPC Moderator at 2025 republication)
- **Geography:** United States (NTPC / Taiwanese American Presbyterian network)
- **Core roles:** NTPC Moderator; historical-ministry advocate; foreword author

## Role in the 2025 Republication

In the NTPC foreword, Dr. Yeh Davis framed Lai's work as both theological and prophetic: at a time when Taiwanese immigrant churches in North America were still in their infancy—especially FPCLA, the first of its kind on the continent—Lai called the church to become a **missional community**, not merely a haven for immigrants.

Key themes in her foreword:

- **Spiritual call amid cultural transition** — linguistic barriers, cultural alienation, identity uncertainty for early Taiwanese in North America
- **From ethnic church to missional community** — FPCLA as a “mission base” in Los Angeles
- **Hopeful community theology** — not optimism slogan but biblical lived imagination
- **Unfinished mission** — intergenerational and multicultural transitions still tested by the 1971 question: *Are we still a hopeful community?*
- **Honor the pioneers** — commemorative reprint as a fresh call to mission for a new generation
