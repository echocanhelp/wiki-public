# Ching Yung Lee (李慶榮)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Ching Yung Lee
- **Chinese:** 李慶榮
- **Era:** 1935
- **Geography:** 台灣 屏東
- **Core roles:** scientist, physician, professor, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who


<!-- tah-tables:start -->
## Education
- National Taiwan Univ. — 1961 — M.D.
- Univ. of Michigan — 1970 — Ph.D., Physiology

## Employment
- Univ. of Minnesota — 1978 — Assistant Professor
- Albany Veterans Affairs Hospital — 1983 — Pathologist
- Albany Medical College — 1994 — Professor of Pathology
<!-- tah-tables:end -->

## Role in the Community
- Research: his discovery of human LH receptors made him internationally known;黄哲陽's TAHS physician-history essay notes the regret that he took his lecturing tours to China rather than Taiwan ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys291|291. 台美人醫師 overview / 黃哲陽]]).
- Community leadership: served two terms (2012–2013) as president — including as joint 聯合理事會主席/兩會共同會長 — of the 紐約首府區台灣同鄉會 and 台美文化促進會, per the officer lists in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys311|311. 紐約首府區台灣同鄉會及台美文化促進會簡介]] and the society's 20th-anniversary retrospective [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-391|391. 紐約州首府區台美文化促進會二十週年回顧]].
- Author: memoir 從基礎醫學到病理，留美四十年回顧 (From Basic Medicine to Pathology: Reflections on 40 Years in the United States), 2016/02 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories397|397. 從基礎醫學到病理，留美四十年回顧]] (also catalogued as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/397-e5-be-9e-e5-9f-ba-e7-a4-8e-e9-86-ab-e5-ad-b8-e5-88-b0-e7-97-85-e7-90-86-ef-b|397 (EN-titled record)]].
- 2015-10-18 — TAH encyclopedia profile: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/658-ching-yung-lee-e6-9d-8e-e6-85-b6-e6-a6-ae-201510|658. Ching Yung Lee 李慶榮]].
