# 戴正德 (Rev. Tai Cheng-te)

戴正德 wrote a recommendation for the reprint of *Toward A Community of Hope*. Pastor [[people/albert-s-lai|賴信雄]] supplied the text on 2026-10-23 and asked that it be included in the reprint and the audiobook. English form Tai Cheng-te is the form in that text.

## Identity Snapshot

- Era: Tainan Theological College alumnus; recommendation written for the 2025/2026 edition
- Geography: Taiwan; the essay concerns the Los Angeles Formosan church
- Core roles: pastor and professor, as identified in Albert's reprint note; medical ethics and bioethics, as stated in that note

## Reprint essay

The piece is titled 〈介紹你「邁向希望的社群」——實踐神學的宣教使命〉. It treats the book as mission theology as well as a history of the first North American Taiwanese church. It names early Los Angeles coworkers [[people/rev-r-h-chang|張瑞雄]], 李宗派, and 許文彬, and [[people/chaolong-kao|高昭龍]] as organizing pastor. It also explains the choice of the name Formosan.

Albert's note in the same text calls 戴正德 a Tainan Theological College alumnus, a PCT pastor, and a medical-ethics scholar, and says he was listed in Stanford University's top 2% scientists in 2024. That Stanford line is his note, not a fact checked here.
