# 戴正德 (Rev. Tai Cheng-te)

戴正德 wrote a recommendation for the reprint of *Toward A Community of Hope*. Pastor [[people/albert-s-lai|賴信雄]] supplied the text on 2026-10-23 and asked that it be included in the reprint and the audiobook. English form Tai Cheng-te is the form in that text.

## Identity Snapshot

- Era: Tainan Theological College alumnus; recommendation written for the 2025/2026 edition
- Geography: Taiwan; the essay concerns the Los Angeles Formosan church
- Core roles: pastor and professor, as identified in Albert's reprint note; medical ethics and bioethics, as stated in that note

## Reprint essay

The piece is titled 〈介紹你「邁向希望的社群」——實踐神學的宣教使命〉. It treats the book as mission theology as well as a history of the first North American Taiwanese church. It names early Los Angeles coworkers [[people/rev-r-h-chang|張瑞雄]], 李宗派, and 許文彬, and [[people/chaolong-kao|高昭龍]] as organizing pastor. It also explains the choice of the name Formosan.

Additional detail carried in the full manuscript (`audiobook-albert-lai/01_AMT_scripts/zh-TW/AMT_v1.0/companion_reprint_tai_recommendation.md`), all attributed to 戴正德's account:

- **UPCUSA special project:** the dissertation was sponsored as SFTS's **first missiology D.Min. research project**; the UPCUSA General Assembly created a **special project with a five-year grant** for the forming church — the vault's FPCLA founding account should be cross-checked against this.
- **高昭龍's credentials per this source:** 哈佛神學院博士, 原台南神學院教授 — 戴正德's own teacher at 南神 before leaving Taiwan.
- **Founding goal:** join the UPCUSA so the church could, as a member church, **speak for Taiwan on the world stage** — the political theology motive stated plainly.
- **Module paradigm:** FPCLA became the 「模塊範式」 for Taiwanese Presbyterian churches nationwide, and the UPCUSA's first concrete care of an Asian immigrant church.
- **On Albert:** 賴炳烔's son; D.Min. SFTS; created the 華商年鑑電話簿 ("另類的文字傳道"); China work incl. **SIAS College in 河南** with the campus-adjacent church (7 stories, ~40 full-time staff after 20+ years) — realizes 黃彰輝's 「職域傳道」 theology. Closing arc: 從「希望的神學」到「台灣人的革命神學」.
- **Terminology:** essay writes 「美國聯合長老教會」 = UPCUSA; in audio spell letters U-P-C-U-S-A (Albert's 10/14 ruling).

Albert's note in the same text calls 戴正德 a Tainan Theological College alumnus, a PCT pastor, and a medical-ethics scholar, and says he was listed in Stanford University's top 2% scientists in 2024. That Stanford line is his note, not a fact checked here.
