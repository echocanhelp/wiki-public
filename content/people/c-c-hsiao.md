## Role in the Community

- His story is recorded in the TAHS community archive: "1498. C. C. Hsiao 蕭忠正" was published 2017-02-05 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/1498-c-c-hsiao-e8-95-ad-e5-bf-a0-e6-ad-a3-201702|TAHS #1498]]).
- **Founding story of 蜀湘園 (Empire Szechuan Gourmet):** 郭忠豪's community-history essay [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories550|550. 曼哈頓的辣椒味道：蜀湘園集團的故事]] (2017-05) tells it in full: born 屏東, after NTU he joined the 1960s wave of graduate students to the US, earning an M.S. in nuclear engineering at New Mexico University (1969). He and his wife 曹淑蓉 decided their next move by flipping a silver dollar — heads meant New York. The couple settled on Manhattan's Upper West Side and he worked in the nuclear-medicine section at Albert Einstein College of Medicine. Through friends 張亞鳳 (Misa Chang) and her brother 翁英俊 — both from Sanchong with food-service experience — the four co-founded a restaurant, named by 蕭太太 after New York's "Empire State": **蜀湘園 / Empire Szechuan Gourmet**, opened 1976 at 97th St & Broadway. Business boomed and by late 1976 蕭忠正 left his research post at the medical school to run the restaurant full-time; the group grew into the 蜀湘園集團, operating nine Manhattan locations by 1992 (see also [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/buiness12|蜀湘園集團]]). This confirms and enriches the "蜀湘園 — Funder" line in the Employment table.
- **Civic service in New York:** listed among the 主席團副主席 in the 紐約蔡英文後援會成立大會 invitation roster (2019, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/e7-b4-90-e7-b4-84-e8-94-a1-e8-8b-b1-e6-96-87-e5-be-8c-e6-8f-b4-e6-9c-83-e6-88-90|紐約蔡英文後援會成立大會邀請函/2019]]) and as a 理事 on the Taiwan Center-New York (台灣會館) 2019–2020 council roster ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwan-center-new-york-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e6-9c-83-e9-a4-a8-e7-b4-90-e7-b4-84-2|台灣會館-紐約]]) — the community record places him in NY Taiwanese civic leadership alongside his restaurant career.
