## Role in the Community
- Long-documented figure in Taiwanese American community media. Ho Chie Tsai's 2008 interview records his UCLA Film/Theatre/TV, Goldsmiths (London) and Carter Thor Studio training and early credits — Popular, The Shield, Without a Trace, ER, Studio 60, Grandma's Boy, Elizabethtown, Milk ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/a-chat-with-actor-kelvin-yu|A Chat with Actor Kelvin Yu]], 2008-11-19)).
- 2016 community interview at his Bento Box studio: played Taiwanese American Brian Cheng on Netflix's Master of None (character shaped by TA producer Alan Yang); writer/producer on FOX's Bob's Burgers (《開心漢堡店》), which won the 2014 Primetime Emmy for Outstanding Animated Program; in 2016 won an Annie Award for Outstanding Achievement in Writing in an Animated TV/Broadcast Production with co-writer Steven Davis ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/kelvin-yu|Meet Jack of All Trades: Actor, Writer, Producer Kelvin Yu]], 2016-02-18)).
