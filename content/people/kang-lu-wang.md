## Role in the Community
- **TAA founding chapter president (Manhattan).** The English TAA founding record lists the 28 founding chapters and their presidents; Kang Lu Wang was president of the Manhattan chapter at the founding of the Taiwanese Association of America — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76-eng|76. Taiwanese Association of America / Mu-Sheng Wu]].
- **台獨聯盟秘书长; farewell 2018.** Community record of his 秘書長告别式及骨灰佈撒行程 (陳宏達, 2018) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys334|334. 故王康陸秘書長告別式及骨灰佈撒行程記事]]. Community remembrance 台美人沒有忘記王康陸 (陳如媜, 2017) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys302|302. 台美人沒有忘記王康陸]]. Memorial photo album «In memory of Dr. Kang-Lu Wang 王康陸博士生命禮讚» (2014) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/photo-albums-activities-26|26. In memory of Dr. Kang-Lu Wang]].
- **1992 fishing-boat return to Taiwan.** 陳榮芳's memoir: autumn 1992 the two flew via Hong Kong, wrote wills with a lawyer, evaded China Airlines staff spotted at their hotel, and crossed by fishing boat to attend the 臺灣獨立聯盟臺灣本部成立大會 (1992-10-18, 海霸王餐廳, 中山北路, 臺北); Wang was badly seasick on the two-day crossing — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys315|315. 我要回家-黑名單人士的返鄉之行 / 陳榮芳]].
- **《台灣公論報》 editorial stance.** In the 張月英 oral history of the paper's founding, when 洪哲勝 replied to critics under the «本報» name, 王康陸 objected («這個不要登») — evidence of his stance on editorial accountability — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys49|49. 《台灣公論報》創刊 / 口述:張月英]].
