# Dr. Shoki Coe (黃彰輝)

**Dr. Shoki Coe (黃彰輝)** is a prominent Taiwanese-American theologian and ecumenical leader whose work bridged Taiwan's church history with global theological discourse.

## Identity Snapshot
- Era: 20th–21st century theological influence
- Geography: Taiwan and global ecumenical movement
- Core roles: theologian, ecumenical leader, church historian

## Historical Context
Dr. Coe is recognized as a key figure in Taiwan's Christian ecumenical movement and has contributed significantly to contextual theology and cross-cultural church dialogue.

## Contributions and Legacy
1. Pioneering work in Taiwanese ecumenical cooperation
2. Bridging Taiwanese church history with global theological discourse
3. Influence on diaspora identity and mission debates
