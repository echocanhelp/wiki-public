## Role in the Community

- Introduced to the Taiwanese American Center of Northern California (TACNC) project in early March (year of the Center's founding search, ~2003) via 吳銀鏤醫師 (Dr. Henry Y. Wu); Wu sponsored the effort by converting the first floor of his own clinic (~3,000 sq ft, nine rooms) into the Center's home at a sponsored price, on a two-year lease starting April 1 — the space included a meeting room, reading room, computer classroom, music classroom, office, a literature/archive room shared with the Federation and other Taiwanese associations, storage, and kitchen. Corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys24|Our Journeys #24 (台獨運動的回顧與前瞻)]] / [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys24-eng|EN]].
- Listed among the TACNC board members who joined over the years and "gave their money and time without expecting any rewards" alongside 吳振和 (Chen-Ho Wu), 林錦堂 (Chin-tang Lin), 吳銀鏤 (Henry Wu), 李信弘 (Hsin-hung Li), 莊純純 (Chun-chun Chuang), 張世昌 (Shih-chang Chang), and 賴明堂 (Ming Tang Lai), during the era of the Center's large annual concerts (SEMISCON 2006, Dream of Formosa 2007, Chai Found 2008). Corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys53|Our Journeys #53]] / [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys53-eng|EN]] (黃美星 memoir).
- Own TAH Who's Who entry recorded at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whoswho1184|TAH #1184]] (2016/07).
