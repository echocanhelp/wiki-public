# TAHS Member Onboarding

**TAHS Member Onboarding** is the lightweight induction pathway for people who are being introduced into the Taiwanese American Historical Society ([[organizations/taiwanese-american-historical-society||台美人歷史協會 (TAHS)]]) and the Echopedia historical-record workflow.

This page exists to give public wiki links a stable destination. It does not publish private rosters, internal spreadsheets, personal contact details, or administrative credentials.

## Public-Facing Purpose

TAHS onboarding should help a new member understand:

- the mission of preserving Taiwanese American historical memory;
- the role of Echopedia as a living community archive;
- how identity, names, titles, and contribution records are documented carefully;
- privacy expectations for personal information, family records, photos, and testimony;
- how to contribute corrections, memories, documents, and source leads.

## Basic Onboarding Flow

1. **Introduction** — a current TAHS/Echopedia contact introduces the person and their historical or volunteer context.
2. **Identity confirmation** — names are recorded with Chinese characters, romanized forms, and role context where available.
3. **Consent boundary** — the person should understand what can be public, what should remain private, and what needs explicit permission before publication.
4. **Role or title note** — if a formal TAHS role is assigned, record the title and scope in the appropriate society or person page only after confirmation.
5. **Contribution path** — direct the person to the community contribution hub for memories, photos, documents, corrections, and source leads.
6. **Review loop** — published records remain correctable; community members may submit updates as new evidence appears.

## Privacy and Safety Rules

- Do not publish private phone numbers, private email addresses, home addresses, private spreadsheets, or internal administration links.
- Treat family history, immigration stories, political history, church records, and elder testimony with consent-aware care.
- Prefer public source links, official documents, and contributor-approved material.
- If a person asks for a correction or removal of sensitive information, route it through the Echopedia correction process.
