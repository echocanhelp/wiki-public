## Role in the Community
- Co-founder of the 印城台灣同鄉會 (Taiwanese-American Association of Indianapolis) in 1978 with 吳群也, 吳良也, 楊水森 and 朱耀源; served as its third president (1980–1981). See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-389|印城台灣同鄉會紀要 (2023/12)]].
- Served as president of the North American Taiwanese Engineering & Science Association, listed among the TAI-trained movement leaders in the [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys37-eng|台灣協志會成立與大事記]]; the record's Chinese edition [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys37|37. 台灣協志會成立與大事記 (紀哲嘉, 2014/09)]] names him as 北美台灣工程師協會會長 余金榜 in the roster of alliance alumni who went on to lead Taiwanese community organizations. Corpus re-grep 2026-09-21 (slice 09201500-27): hit set unchanged (own TAH record + TAI memoirs + works index) — all hits now linked above.
- Amateur stage actor in the 蓬萊歌劇團 (Penglai Opera Troupe): he was one of two "名角" the memoir says the troupe unearthed with 《火燒法統記》 — rehearsals began with the script still unfinished two weeks before curtain, and the playwright's final draft note read only "演員自由發揮" (improvise freely); his performance made his reputation. In 《新相命記》 his wife read the script before casting and knew which role suited him ("知夫莫若妻"); his 院長 was "與眞的院長一樣惹人討厭…眞是一流的演技." The troupe's satire finales anchored the Northern California Lunar New Year banquets, and the troupe toured to Seattle and Los Angeles to "宣慰僑胞." See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys39|蓬萊歌劇團 (林瑞惠, 2014/10)]].
