## Role in the Community
- Co-founder of the 印城台灣同鄉會 (Taiwanese-American Association of Indianapolis) in 1978 with 吳群也, 吳良也, 楊水森 and 朱耀源; served as its third president (1980–1981). See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-389|印城台灣同鄉會紀要 (2023/12)]].
- Served as president of the North American Taiwanese Engineering & Science Association, listed among the TAI-trained movement leaders in the [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys37-eng|台灣協志會成立與大事記]].
- Amateur stage actor in the 蓬萊歌劇團 (Penglai Opera Troupe): his role as the 院長 in 《新相命記》 was singled out as first-rate acting; the troupe's satire finales anchored the Northern California Lunar New Year banquets. See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys39|蓬萊歌劇團 (林瑞惠, 2014/10)]].
