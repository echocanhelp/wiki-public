# Wang Yao-ting & Xie Xiulan (王耀廷、謝秀緞)

**Wang Yao-ting** (王耀廷) and **Xie Xiulan** (謝秀緞) are a Taiwanese American couple profiled together in both the 2017 and 2023 TAHS Publications. Wang is a founding member of the Taiwanese American Historical Society (TAHS).

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Wang Yao-ting & Xie Xiulan
- **Chinese:** 王耀廷、謝秀緞
- **Core roles:** Community leaders
- **Relationship:** Married couple
- **TAHS:** Founding members (2013)
- **Geography:** Southern California

## Profile

Wang Yao-ting and Xie Xiulan are profiled together in the 2023 TAHS Publication, highlighting their joint community contributions. Wang is a founding member of TAHS and served as vice president during the founding era. Both are active in the Taiwanese American community.
