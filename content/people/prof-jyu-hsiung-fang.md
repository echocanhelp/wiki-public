# Prof. Jyu-Hsiung Fang (方菊雄教授)

## Identity Snapshot

- **Era:** 1938

Prof. Jyu-Hsiung Fang 方菊雄教授 – History of Taiwanese American (T.A. Archives) 台美史料中心

## Absorbed Facts (from TAH cites, 2026-09-10)
- Born **1938** in Kaohsiung, Taiwan
- Biology/botany scientist who moved from academic medicine (Columbia) to medical-education leadership at Tzu Chi University (慈濟大學) — first as Director of the Institute of Human Genetics, then as University President
- Community leadership: President of the TAA/New York Chapter (大紐約區台灣同鄉會), Coordinator of the Taiwanese American Conference/East Coast (美東台灣人夏令會), and President of the [[organizations/professor-chen-wen-chen-memorial-foundation||Professor Chen Wen-Chen Memorial Foundation]]

## Timeline
- **1962** — B.S. Botany, National Taiwan Univ.
- **Ph.D.** — Biology, Kansas State Univ.
- **1972–1992** — Assistant Professor, Columbia Univ. College of Physicians and Surgeons
- **2000–** — Director of the Institute of Human Genetics, Tzu Chi Univ.
- **2002–2005** — President, Tzu Chi Univ.

## Community roles
- President — TAA/New York Chapter (大紐約區台灣同鄉會)
- Coordinator — Taiwanese American Conference/East Coast (美東台灣人夏令會)
- President — Professor Chen Wen-Chen Memorial Foundation (陳文成基金會)

## Vault Cross-References (ourjourneys corpus, absorbed 2026-09-10)
- **Kansas State years:** [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys33|Our Journeys #33]] records that in 1964–1965 Fang, then a Kansas State graduate student, met regularly with 莊秋雄, 蔡一, 王能祥, 黃石定, 呂天明 and 陳希寬 as the "Kansas Suite Group," and that the group ran 228-advertisement campaigns in the Kansas State Collegian during the 1966–1968 campus unification/independence debate — corroborating the Ph.D. timeline above.
- **Peace Church network:** [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys268|Our Journeys #268]] names Fang a 和平契友 (Taipei Peace Presbyterian Church fellowship member) and records that he hosted the 22nd 台灣人夏令會 (Taiwanese American Conference) in 1991.
- **Taiwanese House building campaign:** [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys09|Our Journeys #09]] lists Fang as head of the finance group (財務組) in the first 1983 executive-committee meeting of the 台灣會館 building drive under 陳隆豐.
- **Chen Wen-cheng Scholarship:** [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys8|Our Journeys #08]] records Fang, as a standing director, liaising with 北美洲台灣人教授協會 on the 陳文成獎學金 — the first Taiwanese-American scholarship in North America.
