## Role in the Community
- 1971 會長 of the Taiwanese American Association (TAA), in the founding chain of the DC/Baltimore chapter recorded in 陳淮崇's memoir 「華府與巴城TAA的誕生」 (2015-05): 1967 陳淮崇 → 1969 王穎裕 → **1971 賴經都** → 1972 張學賢 → 1974 江應鐘 → 1978 陳文源. See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys-138|華府與巴城TAA的誕生]].
- Named among the 創會先鋒獎得主 (founding-pioneer award recipients) in the memoir's photo caption (source: 陳淮崇).
