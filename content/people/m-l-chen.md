## Role in the Community

Primary corpus material (memoirs) records 陳夢蘭 as a founding figure of Southern California Taiwanese community institutions:

- **南加州台灣長輩會 (elderly association) founding president.** Founded 1979-05-05 as 「南加州台灣老人會」, using the Los Angeles Presbyterian Gospel Church (洛杉磯福音教會) as its first address, ~100 members; 陳夢蘭 served as the first president, with his eldest son [[people/c-j-chen|陳銓仁]] (pharmacist) as secretary. His attempted re-election drew protest from the Presbyterian-church faction (蕭華銓 et al.); the compromise was rotating the presidency between church factions, and 蕭華銓 became the second president. His son 陳隆's memoir treats the Northern California elders association as a transplant of his father's organization. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys50|50. 「南加州台灣長輩會」的成立 / 曾輝光]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-350|350. 美國舊金山灣區台灣基督教會史料簡介 / 何義麟]].
- **SCTFCU credit union consultant.** 陳夢蘭 joined the second preparation meeting of 南加州台灣人聯邦信用合作社 (SCTFCU) in August (1980s), introduced how cooperatives operated in Taiwan (nonprofit, member-service oriented, able to issue cheques), and later served on the board as Consultant (顧問) alongside 絲建東. Before emigrating he had worked in Taiwan's finance industry — consistent with the TAH profile listing 第一銀行. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys244|244. 曇花一現的南加信用合作社 / 周實]].
- Mentioned in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys54|54. 海外台灣人第一所老人公寓一鶴園 / 王桂榮]].

HOLD: TAH #1031 profile lists wife 陳石溫愛; memoirs identify sons 陳銓仁 and 陳隆 as active in LA/Bay Area community leadership — no conflict, but presidency-rotation dates (1979 vs 1980 rename) are per memoir accounts only.
