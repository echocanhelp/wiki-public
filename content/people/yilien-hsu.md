# Yilien Hsu

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Yilien Hsu
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** -
- **Geography:** 台灣台中
- **Core roles:** professor, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

A native of Taiwan, Dr. Hsu holds a Doctoral of Musical Arts degree in flute performance from the City University of New York. Dr. Hsu was previously a member of the Silicon Valley Symphony, and freelances with the Saratoga Symphony, the Redwood Symphony, and the Nova Vista symphony. Currently she is the member of the Palo Alto Philharmonic.
