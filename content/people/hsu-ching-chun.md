# Hsu Ching-chun (許景淳)

**Hsu Ching-chun** (許景淳; English stage name **Christine Hsu**; nicknames 阿淳、耶莉雅; b. 21 November 1963, Wufeng, Taichung County) is a Taiwanese singer, songwriter, musical-theatre performer, radio producer/host, and teacher. She studied Western music at Taipei Hwa Kang Arts School and was a student of Li Tai-hsiang (李泰祥). Active from 1984. Wikipedia lists her faith as **Christian**.

She was raised in a **[[organizations/presbyterian-church-in-taiwan||Presbyterian Church in Taiwan (PCT / 台灣基督長老教會)]] household**. The church connection is not only a faith label: hymn-singing, a Christian children’s choir, her father’s church-music posts (ORTV / 天韻; later 台福 choir), and SoCal church-consortium concerts are the documented mechanisms.

She is recorded on Penghu’s clan note as an 11th-generation descendant of Watong Hsu ancestor **許靖樂**. That is **the same village Hsu clan** as [[people/hsu-ling-yun||Hsu Ling-yun (許凌雲)]], not a published pedigree that she is the *xiucai*’s granddaughter.

## Identity Snapshot

- **Salutation / common form:** 許景淳 / Christine Hsu
- **Born:** 1963-11-21, 臺中縣霧峰鄉
- **Faith:** Christian (Wikipedia infobox). Raised **長老教會** (TAH #258; Fount 2021)
- **Roles:** singer (Mandarin + Taiwanese); Golden Melody winner; radio host (*種子之歌*, National Education Radio); adjunct professor (Tainan National University of the Arts; Ming Chuan); formerly full-time at Chungyu Institute of Technology
- **Childhood choir:** 台北基督教兒童合唱團 (林福裕), lead singer — later alumni include 黃韻玲、李欣芸、趙詠華 (Fount)
- **School:** 華岡藝術學校 西樂科
- **Labels:** 全美 (1987–93); 音樂田／滾石 (1994–97); 種子之歌音樂文化工作坊 (2004–)
- **Geography:** Taichung origin; career Taipei / Taiwan national; **Watong ancestral memory**; father later in the U.S. (see Family)
- **TAH archive:** [#196 singer profile](https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/196-christine-hsu%E8%A8%B1%E6%99%AF%E6%B7%B3-singer201505/) and [#258 father–daughter](https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/258-%E8%A8%B1%E4%B8%95%E9%BE%8D%E8%88%87%E8%A8%B1%E6%99%AF%E6%B7%B3-%E5%85%A9%E4%BB%A3%E9%9F%B3%E6%A8%82%E5%82%B3%E5%A5%87%E6%9E%97%E8%93%AE%E8%8F%AF201505/) (2015/05; #258 excerpted from 美洲台灣日報 / 林蓮華)

## Timeline (career)

- **c. 1965** — Church Christmas: 〈馬槽歌〉 (age ~1–2)
- **Elementary** — 台北基督教兒童合唱團 lead; overseas tours
- **c. 1978** — 金韻獎 四小合唱團優勝 (age 15)
- **1982–83** — 《重唱台灣歌謠》I–II (張弘毅 / 新格)
- **1984** — Debut solo 《流星》(四海)
- **1986** — 《相遇》合輯 (李泰祥 / 滾石) with 唐曉詩、錢懷琪
- **1987.05** — 《玫瑰人生》(全美／點將; 張弘毅). Title song = 台視《玫瑰人生》opening; she wrote 〈睡吧！我的愛〉 (Fount). TAH #196: 金鼎獎最佳演唱 1987
- **1988** — 《別來無恙》; 《精選集1》
- **1990** — 《台灣的囝仔歌》series; 《地球真美麗》benefit (李泰祥 / 樹仁殘障福利)
- **1991** — 《真想要飛》台語; Golden Melody **best dialect female singer** (3rd). Album also won 最佳方言作詞 (林央敏〈嘸通嫌台灣〉) and 最佳編曲
- **1992** — 《你來自何方》 wins **best vocal album** (4th Golden Melody). TAH #196: 金鼎獎最佳演唱 1992
- **1993** — Shared **best instrumental album producer** for 《戀戀風塵》 with 陳揚、陳明章 (5th GMA). Nantes 3 Continents: best film score (TAH #196/#258). TAH #196: 金鼎獎最佳演唱 1993
- **1994** — 《路長情更長》; San Diego film festival: best vocal, 《梅珍》 title song (TAH #196/#258)
- **1995** — 《春天的花蕊》合輯; 《女人哪》(自製)
- **1996–97** — 《天頂的月娘啊》; 1997 Golden Melody **best dialect female singer** again (8th). Same year also nominated **best Mandarin female singer** for 《芬芳》 — Fount: only GMA case of simultaneous 國／台語歌后 nominations
- **1997–98** — 《碩果景淳》; 《純．景淳》(9th GMA 國語女演唱人 nominee)
- **2000** — 21世紀青年百傑, only music-category winner (TAH #196/#258)
- **2003** — 《真感情》(華納)
- **2005** — 《愛河月光》(喜瑪拉雅; includes indigenous-language tracks)
- **2008–09** — 種子之歌 releases; Golden Horse noms: 〈香香〉(*漂浪青春*), 〈流雲調〉(*青春歌仔*). GIO original-song contest: Holo + indigenous tracks 〈Lubi Lubi〉 / 〈香香〉 (wiki infobox: 冠 / 亞). TAH #196: 2009 臺灣原創流行歌曲首獎
- **abt. 2008** — On stage with 許丕龍 at 台福《傳揚》30th (TAH #258; TJ video title)
- **2015/05** — TAH posts #196 and #258

Radio: UFO, EBC network, IC之音; now *種子之歌* on National Education Radio. TAH #258: 500+ interviews with scholars, writers, artists. Judge on 民視《明日之星》.

Stage (Wikipedia): 《大鼻子情聖西哈諾》, 《吻我吧！娜娜》, 《世紀末動物園故事》, 《美麗的錯誤》, 無垢《觀》《潮》.

TAH #196/#258: 20+ albums; 1,000+ concerts; Japan Pony Canyon Mandarin release + tour; critics’ line 「跨世紀臺灣最美麗的聲音」.

Signature titles: 《相遇》《真想要飛》《玫瑰人生》《天頂的月娘啊》《睡吧！我的愛》《路長情更長》.
