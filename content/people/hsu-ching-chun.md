# Hsu Ching-chun (許景淳)

**Hsu Ching-chun** (許景淳; English stage name **Christine Hsu**; nicknames 阿淳、耶莉雅; b. 21 November 1963, Wufeng, Taichung County) is a Taiwanese singer, songwriter, musical-theatre performer, radio producer/host, and teacher. She studied Western music at Taipei Hwa Kang Arts School and was a student of Li Tai-hsiang (李泰祥). Active from 1984. Wikipedia infobox lists her faith as **Christian**.

She is recorded on Penghu’s clan note as an 11th-generation descendant of Watong Hsu ancestor **許靖樂**. That is **the same village Hsu clan** as [[people/hsu-ling-yun|Hsu Ling-yun (許凌雲)]], not a published pedigree that she is the *xiucai*’s granddaughter.

## Identity Snapshot

- **Salutation / common form:** 許景淳 / Christine Hsu
- **Born:** 1963-11-21, 臺中縣霧峰鄉
- **Roles:** singer (Mandarin + Taiwanese); Golden Melody winner; radio host (*種子之歌*, National Education Radio); adjunct professor (Tainan National University of the Arts; Ming Chuan); formerly full-time at Chungyu Institute of Technology
- **School:** 華岡藝術學校 西樂科
- **Labels:** 全美 (1987–93); 音樂田／滾石 (1994–97); 種子之歌音樂文化工作坊 (2004–)
- **Geography:** Taichung origin; career Taipei / Taiwan national; **Watong ancestral memory**; father later in the U.S. (see Family)

## Timeline (career)

- **1982–83** — 《重唱台灣歌謠》I–II (張弘毅 / 新格)
- **1984** — Debut solo 《流星》(四海)
- **1986** — 《相遇》合輯 (李泰祥 / 滾石)
- **1987** — 《玫瑰人生》(全美／點將)
- **1991** — 《真想要飛》台語; Golden Melody **best dialect female singer** (3rd)
- **1992** — 《你來自何方》 wins **best vocal album** (4th Golden Melody)
- **1993** — Shared **best instrumental album producer** for 《戀戀風塵》 with 陳揚、陳明章 (5th)
- **1996** — 《天頂的月娘啊》; 1997 Golden Melody **best dialect female singer** again (8th)
- **2003** — 《真感情》(華納)
- **2005** — 《愛河月光》(喜瑪拉雅; includes indigenous-language tracks)
- **2008–09** — 種子之歌 releases; Golden Horse noms: 〈香香〉(*漂浪青春*), 〈流雲調〉(*青春歌仔*)
- **2009** — GIO original-song contest: Holo + indigenous tracks 〈Lubi Lubi〉 / 〈香香〉 (wiki infobox: 冠 / 亞)
- Infobox also lists: 金鼎獎最佳女演唱; 21世紀百傑; **San Diego** film-song best vocal; Nantes best film score credit on 〈戀戀風塵〉

Radio: UFO, EBC network, IC之音; now *種子之歌* on National Education Radio. Judge on 民視《明日之星》.

Stage: 《大鼻子情聖西哈諾》, 《吻我吧！娜娜》, 《世紀末動物園故事》, 《美麗的錯誤》, 無垢《觀》《潮》.

Signature titles on wiki: 《相遇》《真想要飛》《玫瑰人生》《天頂的月娘啊》.
