## Role in the Community
- 2026-11-19 — AALLC (Asian American Law Librarians Caucus) contemporaneous caucus records of her national AALL leadership: appointed by incoming AALL President Frank Houdek as **Chair of the AALL Committee on Diversity 1996–97** (AALLC newsletter v.2 no.1, Summer 1996); designated by AALL President Bob Oakley as AALL representative to the **Pre-Conference of the Asian-Pacific American Librarians, 2001-06-13–15** (San Francisco, ALA 2001 Annual Meeting) while serving as Executive Board liaison to the Diversity Committee; **Alameda–Taoyuan sister-county agreement established 1998**, and she joined the Alameda County Board of Supervisors / County Administrator / Fremont & Dublin mayors / UC delegation to Taiwan **2001-03-12–16** as President of the Alameda-Taoyuan Sister County Association; AALLC Nominations Committee Chair 2003–04; the Alameda County Law Library moved into the preserved 1924 landmark building in **1995** — [[sources/cossette-sun-frontier-11180842-12-2026-11-18|frontier ledger (AALLC newsletters v.2/1996, v.6/2001, v.9/2004)]].

- Countersigned the 2018 台大校友 open letter as 吳春紅(法律) — see From the record.
- Countersigned the Pew Research Center community statement on Taiwanese Americans as a **North America Taiwanese Women's Association (NATWA)** representative — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/pew-research-center-taiwanese-american-statement|Pew Research Center: Taiwanese American statement]].
- Law-librarian career (AALL Hall of Fame inductee page, fetched 2026-11-18): **Marian Gould Gallagher Distinguished Service Award 2007**, **Bethany J. Ochal Award (SCCLL SIS) 2009**, **AALL Hall of Fame inaugural-class inductee 2010**; AALL Executive Board **1999–2002**; **President, California Council of County Law Libraries 1982–84**; **Alameda County Bar Association Distinguished Services Award 1997**; co-author with Francis R. Doyle of "A Bibliography: The Published Works of Albert A. Ehrenzweig," *California Law Review* 62 (1974): 1080–83 — [[sources/cossette-sun-frontier-11180842-12-2026-11-18|frontier ledger]].
- 2015-06-12 — honored as one of four individuals plus one group from CA AD-10 recognized in the Union City 市政府會議室 by State Senator Bob Wieckowski as 亞太裔傑出領導人物 (NATWA 聯誼通訊 第 53 期 2015-08, 陳碧雲 tribute) — [[sources/cossette-sun-frontier-11180842-12-2026-11-18|frontier ledger]].
- Castro Valley Library campaign: **self-donated US$10,000** to seed the fund as fundraising co-chair, then raised >US$60,000 from 10+ NorCal Taiwanese organizations (≥US$1,000 each), so the library's entrance lobby is named as donated by "The Taiwanese Americans of Northern California"; the library opened 2009 (NATWA 53, 2015-08) — complements her own history [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/172-brief-history-of-castro-valley-library-cossette-sun-201509|172 台美人參與Castro Valley Library]].
- **NATWA-NC president 2012–2013** — foreword bylined 「吳春紅 / Cossette Tsung-hung Sun, President, NATWA-NC (2012-2013)」 in the NATWA-NC 2013 cookbook post #835; elected **NATWA 西北區理事** with **341 of 360 returned ballots** (寄出 978 張選票，收回 360 張) at the 第 27 屆會員大會 (NATWA 聯誼通訊 第 53 期, 2015-08), corroborated by the IRS-derived officer table 「Cossette Sun (Nw Regional Director)」 in NATWA Inc's Form 990 filed 2018-02-19 (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, EIN 11-3048589).
- Educated at Taipei Junior and Senior High Schools; first M.A. (University of Houston) was in political science with a UN-voting-blocs thesis 「A Study of Voting Blocs in the General Asembly」 (taiwaneseamerican.org 100 People self-interview, 2010-05-11) — [[sources/taiwaneseamerican-org|taiwaneseamerican.org]].
- Also served on the Castro Valley Municipal Advisory Council and the board of Women Lawyers of Alameda County; League of Women Voters Eden Chapter 理事; president, Alameda-Taoyuan Sister County Association (100 People profile 2010-05-11; NATWA 53, 2015-08).
