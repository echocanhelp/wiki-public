## Role in the Community

From the community record (our memoirs, primary material):

- Served as principal (校長) of the 台灣語文學校 (Taiwanese Language School); remembered in a parent memoir as 「前任校長黃智舜先生」.
- Language-teaching stance recorded in the memoir: 學語言應該先學音再學字 — sound first, characters after — which the memoirist endorsed from raising three children bilingually.
- Led textbook production: as a 台文班 teacher proficient in 台文 (精通台文), he organized a separate team to edit the Taiwanese-language class materials ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys36|Our Journeys 36]]).
- Co-editor of the school's Chinese readers published 1991/10 and 1992/09, working with 沈郁芳 (convener), 蔡淑媛 (editor-in-chief), 謝清實, 張美枝, 蔡鈴玉, 包方明, 潘美苓, 陳德通 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys227|Our Journeys 227]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys263|Our Journeys 263]]).
