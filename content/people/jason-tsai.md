## Role in the Community

- Co-director (with Brian Tseng) of the revived TACL Leadership in Development (LID) camp near Los Angeles, brought back after a five-year hiatus; both were former campers/counselors from the original TACL camp era. Recruited and interviewed the counselor crew himself. Corpus account: [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/the-return-of-tacls-leadership-in-development-camp-a-glimpse-behind-the-scenes|The Return of TACL's LID Camp]].
- National JTASA Advisor; together with JTASA co-founders Monica Chen and John Wang he created the JTASA handbook that breaks down the club-building process step-by-step for high-school chapters, coordinated with TACL volunteer/networking, scholarship, and internship programs. Corpus account: [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/introducing-the-next-chapter-of-the-taiwanese-american-student-movement-jtasa|Introducing the Next Chapter of the Taiwanese American Student Movement — JTASA]].
