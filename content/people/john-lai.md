# Elder John Lai (賴永祥)

## Identity Snapshot
- **TAH English Name:** John Lai
- **漢名:** 賴永祥 · TAH also 賴永祥長老
- **Era:** born **1922-09-21** (official [生平](http://www.laijohn.com/life/biog.htm)); TAH Birth Year 1922
- **Geography:** 台南後壁嘉田村 · 基隆 / 臺北 · 東京 · 臺大 · Harvard / Boston MA (TAH first US **1972**)
- **Core roles:** historian of Taiwan Christianity; librarian (臺大、哈佛燕京); 《教會史話》作者; [[organizations/elder-john-lai-archives|史料庫]] founder
- **Not:** PCT sitting GA officer; not TAH Foundation staff

Official biog: 父**盧明** 母**賴葉**；長男**從母姓賴**；弟**盧焜熙**.  
TAH Family: wife **劉慶理**; daughter/son `--` (ghost — no pages).

TAH card: Vanderbilt Univ. Boulder College — **conflict**. Official biog: **畢包地（Peabody）師範大學**圖書館學碩士. Both kept; do not merge into Vanderbilt.
