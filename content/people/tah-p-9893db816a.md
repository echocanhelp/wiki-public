## Role in the Community

Cellist and music educator from Taichung, Taiwan. The TAH Foundation encyclopedia profiled her twice in March 2015: as a cellist in the community-story series ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/172-e8-98-87-e5-bf-83-e4-b8-80cellist-201503|172. 蘇心一, Cellist]]) and in the directory encyclopedia ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/334-e8-98-87-e5-bf-83-e4-b8-80|334. 蘇心一]]).
