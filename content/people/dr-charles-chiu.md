## Role in the Community
- Led the UCSF team study of early Northern California COVID-19 cases (29 cases, 2020-02-03 to 2020-03-15): found 8 distinct viral lineages and that more than half of early US cases had travel history, showing the pathogen had already entered communities quietly at the pandemic's outset — the basis for the then-controversial view that suspending unnecessary travel might be necessary (中央社記者舊金山報導, carried by 台灣正義網絡): [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20251207132943_病毒如火花攻進加州_專家_有必要暫停旅行_ab0236187989a427|病毒如火花攻進加州：專家認為有必要暫停旅行]].
- Told The Mercury News the virus entered California "如火花從不同的來源進入加州，造成了多次野火" (like sparks from different sources starting multiple wildfires).
