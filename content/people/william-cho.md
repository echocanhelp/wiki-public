## From the record

- 2021-06-22 — 楊遠薰's TAF history describes Cho (Bill Cho, 卓甫良) as co-founder of the Midwest Formosan Christian Foundation (MFCF, 1973) and fourth president (from 1987) of its successor, the Taiwanese American Foundation 台灣人協進會 (TAF); after 42 years as an engineer — ending as the State of Illinois nuclear-safety certification expert — he retired from state government at the end of 2002 and later settled in Irvine ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20211205041636_2021_06_22_卓甫良與taf的故事-_-楊遠薰_f06677a469620539|楊遠薰 TAF history, TJJ 2021-06-22]]).
- 2015-02 — Dedicated biographical record in the TAH corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories205|205. TAF的傳承故事(1) TAF的創立─卓甫良的故事 / 楊遠薰 / 2015-02]].
- 2021-10 — A camper-parent memoir of the first TAF youth summer camp (1981, Calvin College, Michigan) names Cho as a camp founder and records his daughter Elsa 卓慧麗 co-instructing the Junior Camp alongside Bob, son of 楊忠正 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-377|377. 回憶一九八一年TAF青少年夏令營 / 2021-10]], value band A). The memoir lists the presidential line as 莊明哲 (1980–82), 張信義 (1983–85), 鄭天助 (1986–88), 卓甫良 (1989–91) — HOLD: fourth-term start-year conflict, 1987 (楊遠薰 2021 article) vs 1989 (camp memoir); not auto-merged.
- Obituary note: the 2021 camp memoir records Cho passing away ("安息主懷") in May 2021, announced in 太平洋時報 2021-06-21.
