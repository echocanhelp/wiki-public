## Role in the Community (corpus-absorbed, deepen-x 2026-09-14)
- **Pen Club presidency (corpus-dated):** 賴弘典's ten-year anniversary memoir records that Kuo succeeded 林興隆 as the **third president** of the Great New York Region Oversea Taiwanese Pen Club at end of **2012** (succeeded in turn by 李正三 in early 2015): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys12||Our Journey #12 — 筆會十週年特刊 memoir, 2014-09-05]]. This pins the undated "Pen Club — President" line in the TAH employment table.
- **2014 Pen Club annual meeting host:** 賴弘典 was invited by then-president 郭正昭 to give the 2014 annual-meeting report on the 20-year Taiwan UN-membership movement — Kuo used the Pen Club platform for Taiwan-status civic education: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys104||Our Journey #104, 2015-02-17]].
- **Community entrepreneurship link:** 李正三's memoir of founding the NJ 將軍 (General) Japanese restaurant records inviting 元祿 shareholder 郭正昭先生夫婦 to join the venture — Kuo was a stakeholder in the early Taiwanese immigrant restaurant network in NJ: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys17||Our Journey #17, 2014-09-05]].
- **Speaker, US East Coast Summer Conference:** listed among the scholar-speakers (with 張繼昭, 王文隆) at the 45th 美東夏令會 in 2016: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys260||Our Journey #260, 2016-10-24]].
- **林一洋醫師記念演講 lecturer, 2018:** [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/69-e6-9e-97-e4-b8-80-e6-b4-8b-e9-86-ab-e5-b8-ab-e8-a8-98-e5-bf-b5-e6-bc-94-e8-ac||TAH #69 record, 2018-10-03]].
- **Author, 海外對話錄 series (2018):** a five-part dialogue column in his own name — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publication1272||(一)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publication1273||(二)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publication1274||(三)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publication1275||(四)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publication1276||(五)]].
- HOLD: conflict in honorific — 郭正昭 **博士** (TAH Who's Who, his Univ. of Pittsburgh Ph.D.) vs 郭正昭 **教授** (Pen Club memoirs). Both usages preserved; not merged.
