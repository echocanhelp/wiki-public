## Timeline
- **1931** — Born in Tainan, Taiwan.
- **1953** — B.S., Agricultural Chemistry, National Taiwan Univ.
- **1956** — M.S., Agricultural Chemistry, National Taiwan Univ.
- **1961** — Ph.D., Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, Univ. of Chicago; joined the Institute of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology there as Professor.
- **1980** — Founding President, North America Taiwanese Professors' Association (北美洲台灣人教授協會).
- **1970s (pre-NATPA)** — His University of Chicago home served as the meeting place for Chicago Taiwanese community events before the 台灣同鄉會 had a hall or funds; the university's International House hosted the annual meetings instead ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-378|Our Journeys #378]]).
- **1989** — As a past president, joined the NATPA election-monitoring delegation (觀選團) to Taiwan with 陳文彥、張旭成、林宗光; the delegation issued a statement criticizing KMT abuse of public power during the 立委/縣市長 elections ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys47|Our Journeys #47]]).
- **2015** — Died. NATPA later established the [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/12-natpa-professor-liao-shutsung-memorial-award|Professor Liao Shutsung Memorial Award]] in his honor.
