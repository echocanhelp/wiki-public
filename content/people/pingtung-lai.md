# Pastor Pingtung Lai (賴炳烔)

**Pastor Pingtung Lai (賴炳烔, Lai Ping-tung)** was a Taiwanese Presbyterian pastor, Bible Society pioneer, and interdenominational evangelist whose three-stage ministry career spanned nearly 90 years. He was a student of Dr. Barclay at the Tainan Theological College and Seminary (TTCS), built the Beigang Church, founded the United Bible Societies in Taiwan, and later established the Good Friend Mission — a legacy continued by the second generation. His son, Dr. Albert S. Lai (賴信雄), documented his life and ministry in the self-preface of his 1971 dissertation *Toward A Community of Hope*, reprinted in 2025.

## Historical Context

Pastor Lai's life bridged the Japanese colonial era, the post-war KMT period, and the early diaspora Taiwanese church movement in North America. He was a direct student of Dr. James W. Barclay, the American missionary whose 150th anniversary of mission work in Taiwan was commemorated in 2025 — the same year his son's dissertation was reprinted. Albert S. Lai described his father's ministry as a three-stage journey that evolved from Dr. Barclay's legacy, always maintaining the same spirit of evangelism.

## Life & Ministry

### Stage 1: Pastoral Ministry (Shepherding the Church)

Pastor Lai served as a Presbyterian pastor in multiple congregations across Taiwan:

- **Huwei (虎尾)** — Early pastoral assignment
- **Chingliao (菁寮)** — Pastoral ministry
- **Wu-niu-lan / Ailan Church (烏牛欄 / 愛蘭教會)** — Pastoral ministry
- **Beigang Church (北港教會)** — Missionary assigned from Amoy/Xiamen (廈門); built the Beigang Church

During this period, he pursued further theological studies at **Nanking Theological Seminary (金陵神學院)**. He also served as an evangelistic officer for the **"Centennial Doubling Campaign" (百週年倍加運動)**, assisting in the establishment of Taiwanese churches in the United States.

### Stage 2: Bible Missions (United Bible Societies Period)

While still serving at Beigang Church, Pastor Lai founded the **United Bible Societies in Taiwan** — beginning as a solo operation. His son Albert recalled:

> "I remember when I was in the third year of junior high, I rode a small tricycle with a youth from the church to the post office to pick up dozens of heavy packages containing Bibles sent from Hong Kong, which then returned to the pastor's residence."

A few months later, the operation moved from Beigang Church to **Taichung**, where a formal office and warehouse were established with hired staff.

The United Bible Societies work included:

- Coordinating the selection of talents from various Christian denominations to collaboratively translate the Bible for different ethnic groups (Peh̍-ōe-jī, Romanized script, indigenous languages, Chinese)
- Managing printing (many early beautiful prints done in Hong Kong) and shipping to Taiwan
- Navigating government relations with customs and the Ministry of Information; establishing an advisory committee that included KMT officials such as **張群 (Qun Chang)**, **周聯華牧師 (Dr. Lienhwa Chow)**, and **馬壽華 (Shouhua Ma)**
- Distribution of Bibles to churches, bookstores, and various distribution channels

### Stage 3: Evangelism (Post-Retirement Period)

After retiring from the United Bible Societies, Pastor Lai established **"Taiwan International Missions"** and launched various mission projects:

- **Good Friend Mission (台北市基督教勵友中心)** — A center for youth care and development; the legacy continues to be carried on by the second generation
- **International Evangelical Association**
- Mission projects serving Japanese churches, senior universities, and grandparent choirs

## Legacy & Significance

1. **Three-stage ministry career** — Spanning c. 1936 to the 1990s, covering pastoral ministry, Bible translation/distribution, and post-retirement evangelism
2. **United Bible Societies founder in Taiwan** — Built an interdenominational Bible translation and distribution infrastructure from scratch
3. **Good Friend Mission pioneer** — Established youth and community care programs whose legacy continues
4. **Barclay legacy bearer** — Direct student of Dr. Barclay; his ministry (pastoral, educational, publishing, chapel-building) mirrored his mentor's cross-century work
5. **Father of Dr. Albert S. Lai** — His faith and ministry shaped his son's theological vision and lifelong mission to Formosan communities
