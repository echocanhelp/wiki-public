# Sam Chang (張善良)

**Sam Chang** (張善良, born 1960) is a Taiwanese American real estate developer known as the **"旅館開發大王" (Hotel Development King)** in New York's Taiwanese American community. He is the founder of **McSam Hotel Group**, a Great Neck-based hotel development, construction, management, and investment company.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Sam Chang / Sam (Shen Leong) Chang

- **Notable achievement:** Completed 25 hotels in the five boroughs of New York City over the past decades

## McSam Hotel Group

Sam Chang founded **McSam Hotel Group**, a comprehensive hotel development, construction, management, and investment company based in Great Neck, New York. The company has completed **25 hotels** across the five boroughs of New York City over the past decades.

His portfolio includes hotels associated with major brands, including properties linked to **Trump Entertainment Resorts**.

## Background

Chang was born in Taiwan in 1960 and came to the United States, initially spending time in Japan before settling in New York. He built his career in real estate development, focusing on the hospitality industry.

His profile in the 2017 TAHS publication (菁英錄) was compiled by 周威廉, presenting him as a model of Taiwanese American entrepreneurial success on the East Coast.
