## Timeline
- 1963 — B.A. in Finance and tax, National Chengchi Univ.
- 1969 — M.A. in Accounting, Utah State Univ.; Accounting and Auditing Officer at Main La Frentz and Co. (1969–1971)
- 1971–1975 — Accounting and Auditing Officer at Austin CPA and Geber CPA; 1974 M.A. in Economics, Wayne State Univ.
- 1975–1988 — Financial Officer, Services Management Company
- 1977 — President, Taiwanese American Association / Ann Arbor Chapter (密西根州安雅堡台灣同鄉會)
- 1988–2011 — Private practice as Yung Hwa Hsu CPA
- Community leadership: President (理事長) of the Midwest Taiwanese Summer Conference Foundation (中西部夏令營基金會); Chief Secretary (秘書長) of the World Federation of Taiwanese Associations (世界台灣同鄉會); Co-Founder of the Professor Wen-Cheng Chen Foundation in the U.S.
