## Role in the Community

- **First president of the Chicago Taiwanese hometown club (1954-1956).** Under the name Frank Hsu he was the founding president of the Formosan Club Association (芝加哥台灣同鄉會, 1954), clubhouse at the Magnolia Club House in Chicago; membership spanned Illinois, Michigan, Wisconsin, Missouri, Kansas and Texas. Successors: Bill Hsieh (1957-58), C.H. Lu (1959-60); in late 1960 the East/Mid/West clubs merged into the National Taiwan Club Association (全美台灣人同鄉會). Sources: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ff335|TAH #335 Frank Hsu 許子津 profile]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys255|TAH #255 芝加哥同鄉會簡史]].
- **Co-initiator of the Taiwan Economic Research Institution (TEDS, 1975).** His own memoir with 杜新茂 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys282|TAH #282 台美人籌設「台灣經濟研究所」軼事]] (band A, primary material) — recounts that after 大同公司 came to the US seeking technology in 1975, the two drafted the 「台灣經濟研究所籌設綱領」, won the backing of Finance Minister 李國鼎, and pitched industrial leaders: 吳三連 pledged US$1 per ton of exported cement, 王永慶 offered 3-5 hectares of land, and 林挺生, 辜振甫, 吳金川 endorsed the plan. Fund-raising fell short and the institute was ultimately funded by 辜振甫's 台泥; TEDS became one of Taiwan's three economic-research institutions alongside 中央研究院經濟所 and 中華經濟研究院.
- **Corporate career:** management science and industrial research; 23 years at Mobil Oil Company (美浮石油) in the US, described in his own memoir as Long Term Planning Manager (operations research) in HQ planning. HOLD: TAH Who's Who table lists 美浮石油公司 副總經理 vs the memoir's 企劃部經理.
