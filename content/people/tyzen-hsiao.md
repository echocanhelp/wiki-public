# Tyzen Hsiao (蕭泰然)

**Tyzen Hsiao** (蕭泰然, POJ: Siau Thài-jiân, 1 January 1938 – 24 February 2015) was a Taiwanese neo-Romantic composer, pianist, and conductor. Raised in a [[organizations/presbyterian-church-in-taiwan|PCT]] family in Fengshan, Kaohsiung. Lived and worked in Southern California from 1977; blacklisted by the KMT after 〈出頭天進行曲〉 (1980) until 1995 (ZH Wikipedia). Nicknamed **台灣拉赫曼尼諾夫**. 〈台灣翠青〉 / “Taiwan the Green” is widely treated as an unofficial anthem; it also appears in the **1947 Overture**.

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Tyzen Hsiao
- **Chinese:** 蕭泰然
- **Born:** 1 January 1938, Fengshan, Kaohsiung
- **Died:** 24 February 2015, Los Angeles (EN Wikipedia / TAH). ZH Wikipedia: 25 Feb 2015 13:10 **Taipei time**, same LA home
- **Wife:** 高仁慈 (TAH; ZH Wikipedia: 高長 line, Presbyterian)
- **Parents (ZH Wikipedia):** father 蕭育安 (dentist, PCT elder); mother 蕭林雪雲 (Japan-trained pianist). Grandfather 蕭基源, 台南神學院 preacher
- **Core roles:** composer · pianist · conductor · teacher

## Timeline
- **1938-01-01** — Born Fengshan; piano from mother at age 5 (ZH Wikipedia)
- **長榮中學** — High school, Tainan (ZH Wikipedia)
- **1956–1963** — 師範學院 / 師大音樂. ZH Wikipedia: 專修科 1956–58, 師大音樂系 1959–63 (許常惠 steered him to composition). TAH table: NTNU 1960–63 B.S. Music
- **1963** — Married 高仁慈; taught music in Kaohsiung (ZH Wikipedia)
- **1965–1967** — 武藏野音樂大學, piano (中根伸也); composition with 藤本秀夫
- **1967–1977** — Taught 文藻、高雄女師、台南家專、**台南神學院** music; 1973 NTNU lecturer; 1975 first 蕭泰然樂展, Taipei 中山堂
- **1977** — Family to Atlanta (wife’s business failure); TAH: self-employed arts/crafts shop
- **1980** — 〈出頭天進行曲〉 → KMT blacklist until 1995 (ZH Wikipedia)
- **1982** — With [[people/ahhee-hsu|許丕龍]] founded 南加州台灣音樂社; Oct Thanksgiving concert, Crystal Cathedral (TAH #258 on 許景淳 page)
- **1985** — 北美台灣人文藝協會室內樂團 (TAH employment)
- **1986–1987** — Master’s in composition. **Conflict:** TAH = UCLA Herb Alpert; ZH Wikipedia = **CSULA**, advisor Dr. B. K. Kim. Do not flatten
- **1988–1992** — Three concertos (vn D op.50, vc C op.52, pf c op.53)
- **1993–1994** — *1947 Overture*; aortic aneurysm surgery, then completed 1994
- **1995** — Returned to live in Taiwan; blacklist lifted
- **2009-01** — 行政院文化獎
- **2010 / 2011** — Golden Melody 最佳作曲人 / 特別貢獻獎 (ZH Wikipedia)
- **2015-02** — Died at home in Los Angeles

Art songs (EN Wikipedia): 〈台灣翠青〉 (“Taiwan the Formosa/Green”, lyrics often credited to 鄭兒玉 — TAH #514; no person page yet); “The Fairest Flower”; “Eternal Hometown”; Psalm 23 in Taiwanese; “I Love Taiwan”; folk settings including 望春風 etc. after 1977.

ZH Wikipedia also: oratorio 《耶穌基督》(1971, text by father); 1967 first choral collection; 〈嘸通嫌台灣〉; 〈蕃薯不驚落土爛〉 with [[people/ahhee-hsu|許丕龍]] after hearing [[people/kao-chun-ming|高俊明]] (TAH #258 — do not add 許景淳 as co-writer).

Most of his output is hymns / sacred song, then Taiwanese folk arrangements (ZH Wikipedia).
