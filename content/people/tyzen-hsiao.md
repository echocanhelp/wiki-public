# Tyzen Hsiao (蕭泰然)

**Tyzen Hsiao** (蕭泰然, POJ: Siau Thài-jiân, 1 January 1938 – 24 February 2015) was a Taiwanese neo-Romantic composer, pianist, and conductor. Raised in a [[organizations/presbyterian-church-in-taiwan||PCT]] family in Fengshan, Kaohsiung. Lived and worked in Southern California from 1977; blacklisted by the KMT after 〈出頭天進行曲〉 (1980) until 1995 (ZH Wikipedia / TAH). Nicknamed **台灣拉赫曼尼諾夫**. 〈台灣翠青〉 / “Taiwan the Green” is widely treated as an unofficial anthem; it also appears in the **1947 Overture**.

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Tyzen Hsiao
- **Chinese:** 蕭泰然
- **Born:** 1 January 1938, Fengshan, Kaohsiung (Japanese-era Hōzan)
- **Died:** 24 February 2015, Los Angeles area (EN Wikipedia / TAH / Taipei Times). ZH Wikipedia: 25 Feb 2015 13:10 **Taipei time**, same house. Son Joseph 蕭傑仁 Facebook: **9:10am** 24 Feb, “now in heaven” (Taipei Times / NTNU). Do not flatten the clock
- **Cause:** lung cancer ~2 years after a 2012 Taiwan visit; Taipei Times also: multiple organ failure
- **Wife:** 高仁慈 / 蕭高仁慈 (TAH; ZH Wikipedia: 高長 line, Presbyterian)
- **Children (ZH Wikipedia):** 蕭雅心; 蕭傑夫; 蕭傑文; 蕭傑仁 (Joseph)
- **Parents:** father dentist + PCT **elder** — ZH body **蕭育安**, ZH infobox / laijohn / NTNU **蕭瑞安** (laijohn: 1908–1986). Mother 蕭林雪雲 / 林雪雲, Japan-trained church pianist (laijohn: 林文明長女; 《教會史話》374). Grandfather 蕭基源, 台南神學院 **傳道師** (ZH / laijohn). Touchlife inflates grandfather to 牧師 — do not follow
- **Core roles:** composer · pianist · conductor · teacher

## Family

- **Wife** 高仁慈. Married 1963 after NTNU (ZH). Relatives arranged the match (Touchlife). 1977 US move followed her business failure (ZH / EN). Touchlife: unused green card via **妹妹** (1976 application) then used 1977; Orange County gift shop with a rented piano
- **Children (4):** ZH Wikipedia names 蕭雅心, 蕭傑夫, 蕭傑文, 蕭傑仁. Press: Joseph 蕭傑仁 (death post); 蕭傑文 + 蕭雅心 at 2015 El Monte memorial briefing; 2020 台灣音樂館 opening with 蕭高仁慈, 蕭雅心, 蕭傑文, 媳婦 何文瑩 (OCAC / 自由時報). Touchlife quotes eldest son **傑夫**: father’s rule of thumb 榮神益人
- **2020 pledge:** 蕭傑文 said the US **Tyzen Hsiao Foundation** (founded by 傑文夫婦 after the death) would donate manuscripts / objects to Taiwan when the collection was ready — “and when this country is independent” — as the father’s wish (OCAC). ## Timeline
- **1938-01-01** — Born Fengshan; piano from mother at age 5; public performance at 7 (ZH)
- **長榮中學** — High school, Tainan. Father wanted medicine; principal 戴明福 persuaded him to allow music (ZH / NTNU)
- **高三** — Piano with 高錦花 (ZH)
- **1956–1963** — 師範學院 / 師大音樂. ZH Wikipedia: 專修科 1956–58, 師大音樂系 1959–63 (許常惠 steered him to composition). TAH table: NTNU 1960–63 B.S. Music. EN: mid-course leave as music teacher at Pai-Sha Junior High, Penghu
- **1963** — Married 高仁慈; taught music at 高雄二中初中部 / 前金國中 (ZH)
- **1965–1967** — 武藏野音樂大學, piano (中根伸也); composition with 藤本秀夫
- **1967–1977** — Taught 文藻、高雄女師、台南家專、**台南神學院** music; 1973 NTNU lecturer; 1975 first 蕭泰然樂展, Taipei 中山堂. Also studied with Canadian missionary Isabel Taylor and Austrian pianist Robert Scholz (EN / ZH)
- **1971** — Oratorio 《耶穌基督》, father’s text
- **1972** — 東南亞聖樂會議, Manila; Beethoven Piano Concerto No. 3 with 高雄市交 / 陳澄雄 (ZH)
- **1977** — Family to Atlanta (wife’s business failure); TAH: self-employed arts/crafts shop. EN: an elderly listener in the shop asked why he was there; Touchlife: three women professors in Orange County — **do not flatten**
- **1978** — Moves toward Los Angeles Taiwanese-community work (EN)
- **1980** — 〈出頭天進行曲〉 → KMT blacklist until 1995 (ZH / TAH). Taipei Times / some obituaries also name 〈嘸通嫌台灣〉. Keep both claims
- **1982** — With [[people/ahhee-hsu]]]] founded 南加州台灣音樂社; Oct Thanksgiving concert, Crystal Cathedral (TAH 258 on 許景淳 page)
- **1985** — 北美台灣人文藝協會室內樂團 (TAH employment)
- **1986–1987** — Master’s in composition. **Conflict:** TAH = UCLA Herb Alpert; ZH Wikipedia / Cosmic Light = **CSULA**, advisor Dr. B. K. Kim. NTNU page cites both in different pasted obituaries. Do not flatten
- **1988–1992** — Three concertos (vn D op.50, vc C op.52, pf c op.53)
- **1989** — 臺美文教基金會人文科學獎; 臺美關懷基金會臺灣文化貢獻獎; 川流基金會傑出成就獎 (TAH 157 / ZH)
- **1989–1993** — 許丕龍 phones 鄭兒玉 for 〈遊子回鄉〉 (1989). 1991 LA week (stolen bag): dinner with 許丕龍, 陳黃義敏牧師, 蔡長宗, 黃祖德, 吳西面 — Hsiao recalls 1984 LA Olympics and asks for a national song. 鄭兒玉 12th lyric draft + Hsiao setting **1992** (TAH 514 / laijohn). EN Wikipedia / Taipei Times still print **1988** for 〈台灣翠青〉. **Do not flatten the year**
- **1993–1994** — *1947 Overture*; aortic aneurysm surgery, then completed 1994
- **1995** — Returned to live in Taiwan (Tamsui, EN); blacklist lifted
- **1997** — Tyzen Hsiao Music Association formed in Taiwan (EN)
- **2000** — 〈玉山頌〉 at presidential inauguration (NTNU)
- **2001** — *Ilha Formosa: Requiem* (poetry 李敏勇, EN); US premiere Lincoln Center
- **2002** — Stroke while writing unfinished *Love River Symphony*; moved back to Los Angeles (EN)
- **2004 / 2005 / 2006 / 2009** — 國家文藝獎; 吳三連獎; 高雄市文藝獎; 行政院文化獎
- **2010 / 2011** — Golden Melody 最佳作曲人 / 特別貢獻獎 (ZH)
- **2012-01** — Cancer found on a Taiwan trip (NTNU / Taipei Times). Still appeared at US concerts; helped Taiwan Center of Greater Los Angeles fundraising (Taipei Times)
- **2015-02-24** — Died at home in the Los Angeles area. Touchlife: 羅蘭崗寓所. Viewing 13 Mar, Rose Hills, Whittier. 14 Mar memorial: **牧谷 / Shepherds of the Valley** (Taipei Times, NTNU funeral committee) **and/or** **台福 El Monte** (Chinese Daily address). Presidential 褒揚令 via TECO LA (Chinese Daily)

## Quotes
- On the household piano: 「我媽媽是留日鋼琴家，我還在她肚子裡時就開始聽音樂了。那時日據時代全高雄只有兩台鋼琴，其中一台就在我家…」(ZH Wikipedia, citing 陳宏略 2004)
- Oratorio preface (1971): 「這部神劇是我對家父永遠的尊敬和思念；同時也是我對上帝的信仰告白，及感謝的禮物。」(ZH / NTNU / Cosmic Light)
- Talent parable: 「如果貝多芬是受託擁有五千兩才能的僕人，蕭邦有兩千兩，那我也許就是那個只有一千兩的僕人，我無論如何不敢將這一千兩埋藏起來」(ZH, 陳宏略 2004)
- Kim / style line (often quoted as his later credo): 以「台灣民謠的精神為主體，融入西方古典、浪漫、印象及現代…技巧，來培育現代的台灣新音樂」(ZH 《樂苑》1999; NTNU)
- On folk handling: 整理民謠「就好比摘草藥或蕃薯葉…抽出好的部份」(NTNU / Storm paste)
- 1984 Olympics (鄭兒玉, TAH 514): 「何日何時咱台灣選手…大會就唱咱的國歌，又升咱的國旗咧？」
- Joseph 蕭傑仁, 24 Feb 2015: “My father, is now in heaven today as of 9:10am. No more pain and suffering.” (Taipei Times)
- 高仁慈, after his death (Touchlife): 「不捨。萬分的不捨。泰然，對不起，我沒有照顧好你…」
- 蕭傑夫 (Touchlife): father told him, if it can 榮神益人, do it
