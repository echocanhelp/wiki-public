# Ho Chie Tsai (蔡和杰)

**Dr. Ho Chie Tsai** (蔡和杰) is a Taiwanese American physician and media entrepreneur. He is the founder of **TaiwaneseAmerican.org**, an online platform dedicated to documenting Taiwanese American stories and community history.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Dr. Ho Chie Tsai

- **Role:** Founder, TaiwaneseAmerican.org
- **Profession:** Board-certified pediatrician
- **Affiliation:** UCSF Benioff Children's Hospital
- **Organizations:** [[organizations/taiwan-american-foundation||Taiwanese American Foundation]] (TAF); Taiwanese American Professionals in San Francisco
- **Geography:** San Francisco Bay Area / Northern California
- **Heritage:** Second-generation Taiwanese American

## TaiwaneseAmerican.org

Ho Chie Tsai founded **TaiwaneseAmerican.org**, a website dedicated to documenting and sharing the stories of Asian and Taiwanese American communities. The platform serves as a digital archive and community resource for Taiwanese American history and culture.

The website has become an important tool for the Taiwanese American Historical Society (TAHS) and other community organizations in preserving and sharing the community's stories.

## Professional Career

Ho Chie is a **board-certified pediatrician** on staff at the **UCSF Benioff Children's Hospital**. He has served the medical community in the San Francisco Bay Area for many years.
