# Bob J. Wu (吳瑞椿)

**Bob J. Wu, MD, FACS** (吳瑞椿) is a Taiwanese American general surgeon serving the communities around Monterey Park and Rowland Heights, California. He is known for his minimally invasive surgical techniques and his ability to serve the Taiwanese American immigrant community with cultural understanding.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Bob J. Wu, MD, FACS
- **Chinese:** 吳瑞椿
- **Born:** Taipei, Taiwan
- **Lived in Taiwan:** First 8 years of life
- **Immigrated to US:** Age 8, California
- **Education:** UCLA (undergraduate); Loma Linda School of Medicine
- **Specialty:** General surgery, minimally invasive surgery
- **Geography:** Monterey Park / Rowland Heights, California
- **Languages:** Mandarin, Taiwanese, Cantonese, Spanish, English
- **Credentials:** FACS (Fellow of the American College of Surgeons)

## Early Life

Wu was born in Taipei, Taiwan, and lived there for the first 8 years of his life. As a child, he was content living in the major urban center of Taipei. His father had aspirations for a new possibility — life somewhere with more open spaces, green lawns, swimming pools, and an educational system where children could learn and expand their horizons more freely, rather than face grueling hours of after-school tutoring 6 days a week.

Moving to California was the fulfillment of all these things. Being the youngest member of the family, Wu was the biggest immediate beneficiary of the bold move. He was able to learn English and catch up academically in about 2 years.

### Cultural Transition

Transitioning to a new culture was not completely smooth-sailing for Wu. On one hand, he was acutely aware of the sacrifices his parents sustained to make the American dream come true. They had given up stable and well-paying careers to become new immigrants with little job or financial security. On the other hand, during his crucial formative teenage years, he was fortunate to have a church community who sincerely accepted him for who he was.

Being a youth group leader cultivated confidence and allowed him to discover skills and talents he would otherwise not have developed, eventually earning the respect of his peers even in high school. He has always loved music and sports, but even in high school he realized his strongest suit was in science and medicine. Surprisingly, he also did well in Spanish, acquiring his third language.

### Education

Knowing education would be his key to success and being aware of the sacrifices his family had made, Wu did not take his grades lightly. He worked hard to ensure a perfect GPA through high school and was fortunate to receive the Regent's scholarship, which covered his fees through all 4 years of UCLA.

At UCLA, he briefly considered the humanities but realized his strength had always been in science and medicine. He took the unusual route of being a Spanish major, knowing that being fluent in a third language would always be useful, whether he became a doctor, missionary, or even staying in Southern California.

## Medical Training

Wu attended **Loma Linda School of Medicine**, where he was inspired by the school's motto and mission to "carry on the teaching and healing ministry of Jesus Christ." The altruistic mission of the school was what inspired him to become a surgeon — a person with immediate life-saving skills that transcend any language barrier and are needed all over the world, especially in underprivileged places.

His training path included:
- 4 years in medical school
- 5 years in residency
- 1 year in fellowship

Along the way, he was blessed to find his soul mate, a wife who shared all of his deepest values, also being an Asian American and a physician herself.

## Practice

For the last 5 years (at time of profile), Wu has worked as a general surgeon serving the communities around Monterey Park and Rowland Heights — the exact same neighborhoods where he began his life in the US.

In serving patients, he uses Mandarin, Taiwanese, Cantonese, and even Spanish more than English. Many of his patients are first-generation Taiwanese American immigrants just like his parents, and not infrequently, some of them even know them personally.

### Minimally Invasive Surgery

Wu is known for his expertise in minimally invasive surgical techniques. This approach uses 1 to 3 small incisions, performed through a television monitor, as opposed to traditional surgery's large incisions. The wounds are significantly smaller, and patients recover more quickly.

## Personal American Dream

Wu's personal American dream has come full circle. He works in the same neighborhoods where he grew up, serving the same community that helped him. Though his dreams of being a missionary surgeon going to far-off places have yet to be fulfilled, there is satisfaction in knowing that his skills, training, and languages are being used to their full extent, serving a community with trusted surgical care along with cultural understanding that few others can provide.
