## Role in the Community

- Community record held in the corpus as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/204-clyde-kiang-e6-b1-9f-e9-81-8b-e8-b2-b4201501|TAH encyclopedia record #204 (2015-01-19)]].
- Author of the movement history 客家志 The Hakka Search for a Homeland (1991): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/19-e5-ae-a2-e5-ae-b6-e5-bf-97the-hakka-search-for-a-homeland-e6-b1-9f-e9-81-8b-e|客家志 / The Hakka Search for a Homeland]] — scholarship serving the Hakka-Taiwanese community record.
- Also credited in the corpus as author of THE HAKKA ODYSSEY & THEIR TAIWAN HOMELAND 客家與台灣 (1992): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications487-the-hakka-odyssey-their-taiwan-homeland|客家與台灣 / The Hakka Odyssey]].
- Community-organization leadership recorded in the corpus: 會長 of the Taiwan Hakka Association for Public Affairs in North America 2000–2002, per the successive-presidents roster in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys290|Our Journeys record #290]] (會訓: 「客家人是臺灣人，有關臺灣權益的事務，我們絕對不能缺席」). The same presidency is corroborated by the founding memoir of 北美客協, which records the association's elite-membership model (48 charter members) and lists 江運貴 among the successive 會長 who「都出錢出力，無怨無悔」— [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys252|Our Journeys record #252]].
- Early movement support: listed as a North Dakota contributor to the first 《全美台灣同鄉通訊錄》 compiled by 陳希寬 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys58|Our Journeys #58, 回憶編印第一本《全美台灣同鄉通訊錄》的甘苦經過]]. The same record also lists him among the 熱心捐助同鄉及代表人 donors (19 copies, alongside 洪宏謨, 張燦鍙 and others).
- 1992 — 政治 (politics) track speaker at the 14th 美東南區台灣人夏令會 (July 10 ff., Methodist College, Spartanburg, S.C.) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys245|Our Journeys #245, 美東南區台灣人夏令會簡史]].
- Educator for the community: lectured 「客家文化 I, II」 at the 長青教室 adult-education program of Irvine台灣長老教會 (ITPC) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys107|Our Journeys #107, 長青教室]].
