## Role in the Community

- Named in the TAH Foundation Who's Who record #1884 (09/2017), held in the corpus as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whoswho1884|1884. Kenneth N. Lee 李乃恭 / 09/2017]] (published 2017-09-07, band B).
- Community service beyond his practice: invited main-session speaker at the 台大醫學院北美校友會 40th national annual convention (2022-08-13, Flushing NY) on implant dentistry, and third speaker at the 2017 大紐約區海外台灣人筆會 annual meeting (「再談植牙」) — two separate alumni/community bodies booked him on the same subject, his implant-dentistry expertise.
- Community-recorded distinction: awarded for **Excellence in Orthodontics** by Columbia University College of Dental Medicine; in private dental practice in Flushing, New York, with teaching appointments at NYU College (Clinical Assistant Professor), Columbia (Clinical Instructor), and oral surgery at Bellevue Hospital.
- Third-party community footprint (was wrongly recorded as absent before 2026-11-13): he was the **third speaker at the 2017 annual meeting of 大紐約區海外台灣人筆會** (Greater New York Region Overseas Taiwanese Pen Club) with the talk 「再談植牙」, introduced by 會長 陳欽明 as 「李乃恭牙醫博士，台大醫學院牙科畢業，哥倫比亞大學口腔外科牙醫博士，全能的牙醫，大紐約區很多臺美人都受他照顧」 — per the club's 2017 yearbook, 會長的話 dated Dec. 12 2016, in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1059|大紐約區海外台灣人筆會2017年筆會年刊 (TAH #1059)]].
