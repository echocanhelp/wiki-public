## Role in the Community
- The community documented his memoir years as a formative Taiwanese American narrative: a 2013 TaiwaneseAmerican.org event record preserves his San Francisco interview (Feb 4, 2013, Omnivore Bookstore, with Adam Mansbach) describing him as the proprietor of Baohaus and "one of the food world's brightest and most controversial young stars" — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/sf-interview-with-celebrity-chef-eddie-huang-author-of-fresh-off-the-boat-a-memo|SF interview with Eddie Huang (TA online, 2013)]].
- The TAH story corpus holds a 2014 press record of him discussing the memoir and growing up Taiwanese American — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/97-eddie-huang-discusses-memoir-growing-up-taiwanese-american|TAH #97 Eddie Huang discusses memoir]] — plus his encyclopedia entry [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-160-edwyn-charles-huang|TAH #160 Edwyn Charles (Eddie) Huang 黄颐銘]] and a later 2019 OTA record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ota-231|TAH OTA-231 Edwyn Charles "Eddie" Huang]].
- Community essayists placed him among the second-generation Asian Americans widening representation in the arts: an online editorial naming Jason Wu, Jeremy Lin and Eddie Huang as the figures "who draw more ethnic diversity into a variety of arenas" for coming generations — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/fashion-trending-with-designer-jason-wu|Fashion trending with designer Jason Wu (TA online)]]; cited alongside Vera Wang and Alexander Wang as a notable Asian-descent creative figure in that framing.
- His memoir became community-reaching pop culture with the ABC sitcom adaptation, and the community press recorded his role behind it: a 2015 TaiwaneseAmerican.org profile of child star Hudson Yang notes it was Eddie himself who encouraged Hudson to try out for the role of young Eddie after seeing his audition tape, and frames Huang as "a highly-educated lawyer" beneath the hip-hop image who "challenges preconceived notions of what constitutes quality cuisine and the issues of cultural commodification" — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/hudson-yang|Hudson Yang (TA online, 2015/02)]].
- He is recorded as an opener of doors for the next generation of Taiwanese food entrepreneurs: a TaiwaneseAmerican.org profile of chef Erik of Toki Underground credits Huang's Baohaus (opened New York, 2009) as part of what inspired him, notes the two became friends with Huang doing a pop-up at Toki, and quotes Erik: "He's definitely a guy who paved the way and he's not afraid to speak his mind and have an opinion. It's not the path our parents wanted us to take, so it's nice to see people take the leap." — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/rediscovering-ramen-toki-underground|Rediscovering Ramen: Toki Underground (TA online)]].
