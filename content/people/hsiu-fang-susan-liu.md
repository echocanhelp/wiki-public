## Role in the Community (Deepen-X10080951-2, 2026-10-09)

**Credited artist across the TAH "Footsteps" painting series.** A fresh ZH+EN grep across the corpus shows Liu is the named artist on at least 17 image records in the taiwaneseamericanhistory.org "Footsteps / Our Footsteps" series, each titled "by Artist Susan Hsiu-fang Liu (陳秀芳)" — this is the single largest footprint of any Tier1 artist in the corpus and was previously only partially absorbed (Bob Cheng portrait only). The works, by depicted subject/year: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/footsteps-1||Taiwanese American Museum 台美博物館 (2013)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/footsteps-2||Festival of Nations – Minnesota 萬國節-明尼蘇達 (2012)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/footsteps-5||Race for Cure 療愈之跑 (2013)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/footsteps-11||Arriving in America 到達美國 (2013)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/footsteps-12||Departing from Taiwan 要離開台灣 (2013)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/footsteps-14||Student Life 學生生活 (2014)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/footsteps-16||Little League Tournament 少棒賽 (2013)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/footsteps-17||NATWA Annual Conference 北美洲台灣婦女會年會 (2015)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/footsteps-18||Weddings of Second Generation 第二代的婚禮 (2014)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/footstep-20||A Tribute to Jeremy Lin 向林書豪致敬 (2015)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/footsteps-21||Tsai Ing-wen's US Visit 蔡英文訪美 (2015)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/footsteps-22||Remembering Prof. Wen-Chen Chen 紀念陳文成教授 (2018)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/footsteps-26||Charlene Chen with Farmers in Kenya (2019)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourfootsteps-23||Prof. Yuan Tseh Lee Receiving the Nobel Prize 李遠哲博士領取諾貝爾獎 (2018)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourfootsteps-25||Prof. Ming Min Peng 彭明敏教授 (2019)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-footsteps-31||Andrew Yang in 2020 U.S. Presidential Election (2020)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/footsteps-35||Dr. Bob Cheng 鄭寶鼎博士, Founder of T.A. Archives (2020)]].

**Festival of Nations cultural exhibition — core "死黨" team member.** 曾啟明's [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys123||明州台美人百年大事 (2015)]] and 吳朱實's [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys15||點滴回顧明州文化展 (2014)]] place her inside the Minnesota Taiwanese Association's Festival-of-Nations Taiwan booth design team from **1990** onward, alongside 楊日信/陳玲琇, 蔡友仁/吳朱實, 賴瑞宏, 張麗惠 and 王明基. 吳朱實 credits her directly: "如果沒有秀芳，台灣文化展不會有今日的成果" — she began painting for the cultural exhibition in **1992**, moved to murals, and built the Taiwan booth's winning entries. Her own account is [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys286||台灣同鄉會在明州萬國節的文化展 (2017)]].

**Commissioned muralist in Minnesota public/campus space.** 曾啟明's chronology records two large murals she was commissioned to paint and unveiled: the Asian- nations mural for the University of Minnesota Asian Students Association at Coffman Memorial Union (March year, per the chronology), and a world-landmarks mural at Century College's多元文化中心 (August year, per the chronology). Both events are dated only by month within the chronology — HOLD: exact years not stated in the source.

**Community award and writing.** Received the Minnesota Taiwanese Association's first **傑出貢獻獎 (Outstanding Contribution Award, Taiwan culture / community welfare) in January 2006**, cited for her community service and Festival-of-Nations cultural exhibitions ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys123||明州台美人百年大事]]). Her essays in the corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories15||談繪畫 — 我與台美人 (2014)]] on painting and the Taiwanese American community, and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-750||承先啟後 – 繼續Bob的遺志 (2020)]] continuing Dr. Bob Cheng's work after his death. She is also a listed participant in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys218||明州「城市探險到台灣」(2016)]] and appears in the people index of [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys322||花轎記 (2017)]] — the flower sedan chair (花轎) her garage housed, per 吳朱實's account.
