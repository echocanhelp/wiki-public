# Joshepine Chuang (莊靜怡)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Joshepine Chuang
- **Chinese:** 莊靜怡
- **Era:** Birth Place
- **Geography:** 台灣
- **Core roles:** professor, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Joshepine Chuang is a graduate of Fu-Jen Catholic University in Taiwan, majoring in Vocal, minoring in piano. In 1993, she received her master degree in education from USC. She has studied vocal under Professor C. Kim and Professor Y.C. Lin since she was in high school. Mr. Chuang has been featured as soloist with several chorales in Los Angeles. Recently, she has been involved in conducting the children’s choir at Grace Taiwanese Presbyterian Church, North Orange County, and Taiwanese American School in South Bay.
