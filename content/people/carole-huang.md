## Role in the Community

- **1988** — Listed among the people connected to the founding of the Taiwanese Association of America Greater Washington Chapter, in Chen Huaichong's memoir of the chapter's birth ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys-138|138. 華府與巴城TAA的誕生]]); she appears there as "Carol Huang 陳春華" alongside Jason Huang 黃仁宗.
- **1992–94** — First Taiwanese American to serve as chair of the national Asian Pacific American advisory committee, per the TAH encyclopedia profile ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ff306|306. Carol Huang 陳春華 / 第一位台美人擔任全美亞太裔顧問會主席]]).
- **2008/11** — Profile drawn from the Taiwanese Association of America Greater Washington Chapter special edition; re-listed in the 2016 Who's Who ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos1383-carole-huang|1383. Carole Huang 陳春華]]).

Name-variant: corpus uses both "Carole Huang" and "Carol Huang" for her. HOLD: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys263|ourjourneys263]] memorialises a different "Carol Huang" (黃欣怡), a Taiwan Language School student killed in a 1988 car accident (Carol Huang Scholarship) — likely a distinct person; not merged.
