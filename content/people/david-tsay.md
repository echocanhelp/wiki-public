## Role in the Community
- **NATPA 教授協會 founding-era board:** Elected as a Regional Director at the founding meeting of the North American Taiwanese Professors Association, alongside 張旭成 (East), 張富美 (West), and 黃金來, 吳得民, 蔡嘉寅, 陳炳杞, 賴義雄 as general directors — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys69|69. 北美洲台灣人教授協會 / 廖述宗]]. - **Tahs profiles:** Also recorded in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whoswho2113|2113. David Tsay 蔡丁財 / 05/2018]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-2160-david-tsay|2160. Dr. David Tsay 蔡丁財博士]].
