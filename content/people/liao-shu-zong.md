# Liao Shu-zong (廖述宗)

## Identity Snapshot
- **Born:** 1931, Tainan, Taiwan
- **Era:** First-generation Taiwanese American
- **Geography:** Chicago, Illinois (UC Chicago professor)
- **Core roles:** Biochemist, NATPA founder, democratic movement supporter

## Overview
- 廖述宗 (1931–2017) was a renowned biochemist born in Tainan, son of famous painter 廖繼春
- He published a series of outstanding papers on male hormones that earned him international recognition in biochemistry
- In 1980, he founded the North America Taiwanese Professors Association (NATPA), which supported Taiwan's democratic movement and served as a bridge between overseas intellectuals and mainland Taiwan dissidents
- After Taiwan's democratization, he worked to introduce high-tech industries to Taiwan, hoping to build Taiwan into a high-tech nation alongside like-minded individuals
- He was a typical early Taiwanese student — wise, diligent, thoughtful, and passionate, especially about Taiwan
- He emphasized that "the meaning of success lies not in the destination, but in the journey"
- He passed away in 2017; a memorial service was held, and a memorial tribute was written by 楊嘉猷
