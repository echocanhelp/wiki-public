# Dr. William L. Ko (柯威霖博士)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Dr. William L. Ko
- **Chinese:** 柯威霖博士
- **Era:** 1927
- **Geography:** Danshui, Taiwan
- **Core roles:** engineer, scientist, artist, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

LBJ Ranch in Spring Time, Watercolor. A permanent collection of the Lyndon Baines Johnson Library, 1975. At a Mossy Well, Watercolor, 21 x 29. For more bio information, please click link in Who’s Who: 360. William L. Ko 柯威霖

## Education
- National Taiwan Univ. — 1950 — B.A., Mechanical Engineering
- California Institute of Technology — 1959 — M.S., Aerospace Engineering
- California Institute of Technology — 1963 — Ph.D., Aerospace Engineering

## Employment
- Taiwan Railways Administration — Driver of CT250 steam train — (1941-1954) — Taipei Tamkang High School — Mathematics, Physics Teacher — (1954-1958) — California Institute of Technology — Research Fellow — (1963-1964) — NASA — Senior researcher and senior technical consultant of spacecraft, supersonic aircraft high thermal structure — (1977-) — Texas Fine Arts Association — Examiner — (1976-) — Ko Watercolor Research Center — Founder — Southwest Research Institute, Texas — Researcher/Staff — Texas Art Association — Juror (審查委員)
