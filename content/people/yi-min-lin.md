# 林逸民

Taiwanese physician and movement figure; current political commentator for 美洲台灣日報.

## Story

林逸民 is a physician whose record runs from the founding circles of the Taiwanese Christian community in St. Louis in the early 1970s — where he, with 張世聰, 翁秀惠, 高銘憲 and others, began the discussions that formed the believers' community [TAH](https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org) — to the present, writing political commentary for 美洲台灣日報 on Taiwan's defense and identity. He served as an elder of the St. Louis church and chaired its first building-committee meeting.

- Era: 1970s–2020s
- Geography: St. Louis (1970s–80s), later US (2020s commentary)
