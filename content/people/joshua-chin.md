## What the record shows

**2010-07-19 —** Selected from hundreds of statewide competitors for the
inaugural California Cyber Camp (U.S. Cyber Challenge) at Cal Poly Pomona,
July 19–23, 2010, all expenses paid. He was then a CPP student. His own words
in the campus announcement: *"From my freshman year in high school, I sought to
find a way to break into the computer security world and to develop valuable
skills to aid in my quest to defend cyberspace."* The article's photo credits
the CPP team at the Western Regional Collegiate Cyber Defense Competition,
March 26, 2010.
