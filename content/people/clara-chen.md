## Role in the Community

- **Seattle Taiwan Center (西雅圖台灣會館), founding couple:** In April 2003 Clara Chen and her husband 陳紹紀, together with 林正南 and 陳淳美, pooled funds to purchase the property that became the Seattle Taiwan Center; the transfer completed 2003-04-30. The ~5,000 sq-ft building near the UW campus was renovated over about a year (event hall, kitchen, meeting room, library, classrooms) and the first two floors leased to the Greater Seattle Taiwanese American Foundation for a symbolic $1/year rent; the renovated lower level is rented to students to offset the mortgage. Clara Chen, 陳淳美 and 李卓芬 are credited as the three Taiwanese women whose labor drove the renovation. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys30|30. 西雅圖台灣會館 (Our Journeys, 2014-09)]] (English account: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys30-eng|ourjourneys30-eng]]). This matches the TAH press-kit line "Community — Seattle Taiwan Center — Main contributor."
- **Christian student movement, Madison WI:** 劉真真 and 陳紹紀 (with 鄭學禮) organized the first prayer/Bible-study summer retreat at Wisconsin Dells for Taiwanese students in Madison in the 1960s; the effort grew into the Midwest "基督徒夏令會" held for several years across Midwestern states. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys-369|369. 懷憶六十年代前期的明市 (謝伯芳, 2021-03)]].
- **Own memoir:** Her first-person account of her Taiwan-independence journey, 「我的台獨路程」 (2015-12), is held as community historical record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories383|383. 我的台獨路程 / 劉真真]]. A later Christie Park / U-District community-space account appears in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-362|362. Recent Christie Park renovation (2020-12)]], consistent with the press-kit "Christie Park, WA — Main contributor" entry.
