# George Leslie Mackay (馬偕)

**George Leslie Mackay** (偕叡理 / 馬偕, 21 March 1844 – 2 June 1901) was a Canadian Presbyterian missionary, the first Presbyterian missionary to **northern** Taiwan (Tamsui, 1872). In Taiwan he is **馬偕博士**. Not [[people/james-laidlaw-maxwell||James Laidlaw Maxwell (馬雅各)]].

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** George Leslie Mackay
- **Chinese:** 馬偕 / 偕叡理
- **Born:** 21 March 1844, Ontario, Canada
- **Died:** 2 June 1901, Tamsui, Taiwan
- **Sent by:** Canadian Presbyterian Mission
- **Field:** Northern Taiwan, 1872–1901
- **Not:** Macdonald · Maxwell · 馬雅各

## Timeline
- **1872** — Arrived Tamsui; first Presbyterian missionary in the north
- **1872** — Maxwell (already in the south) advised him to start near Tamsui (EN Wikipedia)
- **1901-06-02** — Died in Tamsui
- **2022** — 馬偕博士台灣宣教150週年; [[organizations/good-shepherd-taiwanese-presbyterian-church||GSTPC]] joined a SoCal TPC mark (6 Mar)

## Works / institutions (named for him)
- 馬偕紀念醫院 (Mackay Memorial Hospital)
- Northern mission that became part of [[organizations/presbyterian-church-in-taiwan||PCT]]
