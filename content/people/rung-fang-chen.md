## Role in the Community
- Seventh President of the Taiwanese American Federation of Northern California (TAFNC, 北加州台灣同鄉聯合會), from the East Bay Taiwanese Association. He took office around the time of the Formosa Incident and the Lin Family Massacre, when the KMT blacklist made the presidency dangerous; his guarantee that the East Bay Association would keep supplying presidents steadied the Federation. Over those "most difficult five years" four consecutive TAFNC Presidents came from the East Bay Association — 陳榮芳, Tsun-liang Chang (張傳良), Shih-ming Huang (黃世明) and I-ren Kan (簡義仁) — per the federation's own history in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys38-eng|Our Journeys 38 (TAFNC, English)]].
- Author of the blacklist memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys315|315. 我要回家-黑名單人士的返鄉之行 / 陳榮芳 /10/2017]] (band A): arrested by the 警備總部 in 1976, forced to act as a 警总 agent inside the 臺獨聯盟, defected on arrival in the US and won political asylum; as a blacklisted person he attempted a fishing-boat return to Taiwan in autumn 1992 and, with 王康陸, reached the 臺灣獨立聯盟臺灣本部 founding convention in Taipei on 1992-10-18.
- Predecessor's testimony in the federation history: the 2nd President 彭傳良 (Liang Chuan Peng) records that in fall 1978 no South Bay member would take the presidency, and that 陳榮芳「generously agreed」to become the 7th President; after his term nine more Presidents (張傳良、黃世明、黃清盛、簡義仁、何文亮、Andrew Lee、連日昌、陳明春、廖萬福) took the burden, and 16 of TAFNC's 30 Presidents came from the South Bay Taiwanese Association — per [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys38-eng|Our Journeys 38 (TAFNC, English)]].
