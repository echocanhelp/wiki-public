## Role in the Community

- **1983 — New York Taiwan Center building campaign:** at the first Executive Committee meeting of the Taiwan Center Building Committee (1983-11-23, held in Dr. Hong-Tien Lai's office), Morisan Lu was elected head of the publicity group (宣傳組呂明森) under Chairman Lung-Fong Chen, alongside the financial (Fang J. S. Fang), planning (A-Huo Chen), fundraising (Martin Tsai), engineering (Cheng-Bin Ku), and secretary (Patrick Huang) groups. The committee's launch plan was to raise $500,000 within six months. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys9-eng|Our Journeys 9 — Taiwan Center building memoir (EN)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys09|Our Journeys 9 (華文)]]. See also [[organizations/new-york-taiwan-center|New York Taiwan Center]].
- His own TAH encyclopedia record (907, 2016/03) is held at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-m-s-lu|907. Morisan Lu 呂明森]].
- **1983–84 — 泛美建築公司 designed 將軍日本餐廳 (Tokyo Japanese Restaurant, NJ):** in 李正三's memoir of founding the New Jersey 將軍 restaurants, 泛美建築公司的呂明森 is described as a 舊識 — 泛美 had already renovated his 元祿壽司 — and 「多才多藝，確是台美中不可多得的人才」; by the 80s 泛美 had designed or renovated over a hundred Japanese restaurants. Lu designed and built the Garden State Parkway location (Japanese garden, tatami rooms, lighting, sushi bar; broke ground 1983-11-01, completed 1984-03-07), and later also worked with David 施 on the design of 將軍27 (Kendall Park, opened 1988-09-24). Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys17|Our Journeys 17 — 紐澤西的將軍日本餐廳 (李正三 memoir)]].
