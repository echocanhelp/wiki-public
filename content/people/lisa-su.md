# Lisa T. Su (蘇姿丰)

**Lisa T. Su** (蘇姿丰, born 1969) is the CEO and President of **Advanced Micro Devices (AMD)**, a Sunnyvale, California-based semiconductor giant. She is one of the most powerful women in technology and a prominent figure in the Taiwanese American community.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Lisa T. Su
- **Chinese:** 蘇姿丰
- **Born:** 1969, Taiwan
- **Education:** BS MIT EE 1990, MS MIT EE 1991, PhD MIT EE 1994
- **Role:** CEO and President, AMD (Advanced Micro Devices)
- **Geography:** Sunnyvale, California / Silicon Valley
- **Heritage:** Second-generation Taiwanese American
- **Family:** Father 蘇春槐 (Su Chun-huai) profiled in 2017 TAHS Publication

## Education

Su was born in Taiwan in 1969 and immigrated with her family to the United States. She attended the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) for all of her degrees:

- **B.S. Electrical Engineering**, MIT (1990)
- **M.S. Electrical Engineering**, MIT (1991)
- **Ph.D. Electrical Engineering**, MIT (1994)

Her PhD focused on semiconductor technology, specifically copper interconnect process technology at 0.22 micron nodes — a critical advancement in semiconductor manufacturing.

## Career at IBM

Before joining AMD, Su developed copper interconnect process technology at **IBM** while working at 0.22 micron nodes. This work was critical to advancing semiconductor manufacturing and was a key area of research at IBM's research laboratories.

## AMD Leadership

Su joined AMD and rose through the ranks to become CEO and President of the company. Under her leadership, AMD has become a major competitor in the semiconductor industry, particularly in processors and graphics cards.

Her appointment as CEO of AMD was a landmark moment for both the technology industry and for Asian American women in leadership positions.

## Recognition

Su has been recognized with numerous honors:
- **IEEE Board of Directors**
- **MIT EE Advisory Board**
- Recognition from the semiconductor industry for her technical leadership

## Community Significance

Su's father, **蘇春槐 (Su Chun-huai)**, was profiled in the same 2017 TAHS Publication as a "tiger father" proud of his daughter's achievements. The profile "虎爸談虎女" (Tiger Father Talks About Tiger Daughter) highlighted Su Chun-huai's pride in his daughter's accomplishments as CEO of AMD.

Su's story is emblematic of the second-generation Taiwanese American experience — children of immigrants who achieve the highest levels of success in mainstream American society.
