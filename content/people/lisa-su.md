# Lisa T. Su (蘇姿丰)

## Identity Snapshot
- **Era:** Second-generation Taiwanese American
- **Geography:** Silicon Valley / New Jersey
- **Core roles:** AMD CEO, MIT EE PhD, IBM copper interconnect developer

## Overview
- 蘇姿丰 (Lisa T. Su) is the CEO of Advanced Micro Devices (AMD), a semiconductor company
- She earned her PhD in Electrical Engineering from MIT in 1994
- Before AMD, she developed copper interconnect process technology at IBM while working at 0.22 micron nodes
- She was elected to the IEEE Board of Directors and MIT EE Advisory Board
- Her father, 蘇春槐, was profiled in the same 2017 TAHS Publication as a "tiger father" proud of his daughter's achievements
- She was awarded the IEEE Board of Directors and MIT EE Advisory Board recognition
- Her educational background includes: BS MIT EE 1990, MS MIT EE 1991, PhD MIT EE 1994
