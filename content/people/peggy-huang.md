In her account to CNA she relayed eyewitness details — the receptionist greeted the shooter with 「歡迎你第一次來」 and he replied 「這不是我第一次，我已經來這裡兩三次了」; he attended service, produced the gun at lunch, chained the church doors, and shoved a questioning member with 「不關你的事」 before the 鄭姓醫師 who rushed him died beside his 90+-year-old mother he had come to accompany. - 2020 campaign, from the community's own press: a 台灣公義報 promoter introduced her at the 台美傳統週 circuit — 「幾乎每年都來參加我的台美傳統週活動」 — stressing that unlike other Taiwanese American politicians she had lived in Taiwan and understood 台灣民情, as she sought the Republican nomination for the 2020 U.S. House race (FB: Peggy For Congress) [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250617074648_黃瑞雅_peggy-huang_希望能代表共和黨角逐2020聯邦眾議院_ab91fdd76a1f4c9c|台灣公義報 2019-12]].
