## Role in the Community
- Cited within the Taiwanese American community record as the leading figure of the Taiwanese-American medical field, part of the community's case that Taiwanese Americans are "small in population but outstanding in many fields" — see [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240620194633_root_1f6d7e8310d96d08|台美人公民社會觀察 (Taiwan Justice)]].
- Public-health messaging during the 2020 coronavirus panic (how the public should overcome panic and cope) was carried in the community press — see Tag: 何大一 archive stub in [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250914235723_tag_何大一_649c1cdd33b5ea41|Taiwan Justice tag page]].
- In the 2012 presidential-election U-Chan (宇昌案) controversy, he was attacked alongside Tsai Ing-wen and 翁啟惠 by the KMT investigative push; the case was later closed with no wrongdoing — see [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240719210735_root_75897dd051edbd73|宇昌案社論 (Taiwan Justice)]].
- TAH honors him as the first Taiwanese American on the cover of Time magazine (Person of the Year 1996, AIDS research).
- Corpus records: his own TAH encyclopedia entries exist in the corpus as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/118-dr-david-ho|TAH #118 Dr. David Ho 何大一醫師]] (2018) and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-196-david-ho|TAH #196 Dr. David Ho 何大一醫師]] (2015), and the community's own tribute series profiles him as the 7th entry of 台美人的榮耀 (Pride of Taiwanese American): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/228-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e4-ba-ba-e7-9a-84-e6-a6-ae-e8-80-80-ef-bc-88pride-of-taiwa|TAH #228 台美人的榮耀：7, 何大一博士 (2015/04)]], with an additional profile at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/32-dr-da-yi-david-ho-e4-bd-95-e5-a4-a7-e4-b8-80-e5-8d-9a-e5-a3-ab-the-first-fron|TAH #32 Dr. Da-yi, David Ho 何大一博士]]. The community record frames his Time cover explicitly as *Taiwanese American* pride, not merely personal achievement.
