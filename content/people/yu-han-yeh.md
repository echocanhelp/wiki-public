# Yu-Han Yeh (葉宇涵)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Yu-Han Yeh
- **Chinese:** 葉宇涵
- **Era:** -
- **Geography:** 台灣
- **Core roles:** artist
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Born in Tainan, Taiwan, Miss Yu-Han Yeh began her piano studies at the age of five. In her hometown, she was under the guidance of Grace Chung and I-Ting Chen in Tainan National Girls’ Senior High School (TNGS), where she graduated with the Top Prize honor for the outstanding musical and academic performance in the Music Concentration Program.
