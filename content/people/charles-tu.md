## Role in the Community

- Held in the TAH corpus as a community figure across three records: profile ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-charles-tu|TAH #601]], 2015-09-19) and Who's Who entry ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ota-247|TAH #247]], 2019-02-15).
- Co-initiator (共同發起人) of the 2021 南加州僑界 joint statement supporting Taiwan's participation in WHA, alongside TAHS among the initiating organizations; his name also appears in the corpus republication of the statement: [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20251108183438_2021年南加州僑界支持台灣參與世界衛生大會wha聯合聲_80c0a825a7a661b6|2025 republication of the WHA 聯合聲明]].
