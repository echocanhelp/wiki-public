# Alvina Ling (林佑珊)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Alvina Ling
- **Chinese:** 林佑珊
- **Era:** 1974
- **Geography:** Atlanta, Georgia
- **Core roles:** entrepreneur
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Accomplishment
1. Won Newbery and Caldecott Honors, the Boston Globe-Horn Book Award, the Coretta Scott King Medal, the E.B. White Read-Aloud Award, the Golden Kite, and several that have made the New York Times Best Illustrated list

## Role in the Community

- 多次擔任台灣美国人語文協會（TA.org）創作獎評審：2023年（與 Charles Yu、Shawna Yang Ryan，見 [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/2023-creative-writing-prize-winners|2023 Creative Writing Prize]]）、2025年（見 [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/charles-yu-grace-loh-prasad-alvina-ling-select-2025-creative-writing-prize-winne|2025 Creative Writing Prize]]）、2026年（與 Charles Yu、Grace Loh Prasad、Shawna Yang Ryan、Averylin Cummins，見 [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/2026-creative-writing-prize-winners|2026 Creative Writing Prize]]）。
- 育成台裔作家：作者 Julia 回憶 Alvina 與 Connie Hsu 是「把台裔創作者與有色人種創作者的繪書放上書架」的兩位重要編輯，並經兒時因緣引介她签下第一本合約（Jenny Han 的經紀人 Emily Van Beek，見 [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/when-love-is-more-than-words|When Love Is More Than Words]]）。
- 家族：父親為 [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-1679-evan-ling|林富文博士（Dr. Evan Ling，TAH #1679）]]；本人 TAH 檔案見 [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-1680-alvina-ling|TAH #1680]]。
