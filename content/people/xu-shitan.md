# Hsu Shitan (徐士坦)

**Hsu Shitan (徐士坦)** — Computer class instructor at Taiwan Center Taiwan School.

## Identity Snapshot

## Teaching at Taiwan Center

Hsu Shitan is listed on Taiwan Center's class-information page as the instructor of the school's computer class (電腦). The course runs on weekends at a published fee of $40, alongside the school's 華文, 日文, 合唱, and other adult/community classes (see [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center]] — Taiwan School courses & teachers table). He is one of the school's named instructor cohort, alongside [[people/april-lin||林淑卿 April Lin]] and [[people/wang-rongyi||王榮義]].

No further biography is published on the site; no details are invented here.

Source: 課程資訊｜大洛杉磯台灣會館, [https://www.taiwancenter.org/class-information](https://www.taiwancenter.org/class-information), fetched 2026-07-16, `per knowledge/web-archives/taiwancenter-org-class-information.md`.

- English-site rendering of the same record names him "Teacher Hsu Shih-Tan" and repeats the demand/small-class facts: "These popular classes fill up fast, and small class sizes ensure personal attention." Source: [https://www.taiwancenter.org/en/class-information](https://www.taiwancenter.org/en/class-information), fetched 2026-07-16, `per knowledge/web-archives/taiwancenter-org-en-class-information.md`.
