# Alan Thian (田詒鴻)

**Alan Thian** (田詒鴻) is a Taiwanese American banker and business leader. He serves as **董事長 (Chairman)** on the official board of the **Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館)** and as President of **皇佳銀行 (Royal Business Bank / Huangjia Bank)**.

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Alan Thian
- **Chinese:** 田詒鴻
- **Born:** 1952, Singapore
- **Education:** Bachelor's degree in business administration (Singapore); MBA, University of Southern California (1982)
- **Immigrated to US:** 1976 with parents
- **Role:** 董事長 (Taiwan Center); 皇佳銀行總裁
- **Geography:** Los Angeles, California
- **Organizations:** Taiwan Center Foundation; Royal Business Bank

## Early Life and Education

Alan Thian was born in Singapore in 1952, where he received a bachelor's degree in business administration. In 1976, he migrated to America with his parents. Six years later, in 1982, he graduated from the University of Southern California (USC) with an MBA degree.

## Royal Business Bank

Thian started his first Royal Business Bank with $71 million in initial capital in Downtown Los Angeles. He serves as the bank's President, leading the institution's operations and strategic direction.

## Taiwan Center

Alan Thian serves as 董事長 (Chairman) on the official board of the Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles, a key organization in the Taiwanese American community that serves as a cultural and community hub.

## Profile

His profile in the 2017 TAHS Publication was titled "皇佳銀行總裁田詒鴻 (Alan Thian) 台灣的奇妙因緣" (Alan Thian of Royal Business Bank — Taiwan's Wonderful Destiny). The profile was conducted as an interview by the Taiwanese American Historical Society (本會專訪) and edited by Edda Huang.
