# Alan Thian (田詒鴻)

**Alan Thian (田詒鴻)** is listed as **董事長** on the official board of the [[organizations/taiwan-center|Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館)]]. He is also President of 皇佳銀行 (Huangjia Bank).

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Alan Thian
- **Chinese:** 田詒鴻
- **Role:** 董事長 (Taiwan Center); 皇佳銀行總裁
- **Organizations:** [[organizations/taiwan-center|Taiwan Center (TWCLA)]], 皇佳銀行
