Full column re-anchored with canonical URL 2024-07-06: signing himself 「美國紐約市大學經濟系教授」, he argues (his opinion, reported) that before the November 2024 U.S. election Taiwan should push a bilateral trade agreement (BTA) rather than an FTA — citing IPEF's stalled trade pillar, Sherrod Brown's and Elizabeth Warren's opposition, a possible Trump pledge to cancel IPEF, CPTPP re-entry as the best-but-uncertain path, the Chip-4 alliance leverage, and Kurt Tong's view of a possible U.S.–Japan bilateral FTA — [美洲台灣日報](https://taiwandaily.net/政治評論/26561/).
- NATPA service, third-party attested: at the 38th annual conference on August 4–5, 2018 in Arlington, VA he chaired the 「台灣政情開講」 pre-conference session and was listed among the directors stepping down in October 2018 — per `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/activities-of-natpa.md`.
- 大紐約區海外台灣人筆會 speaker circuit: keynote 「超越黨閥政治的失落，深化台灣民主的民主」 at the annual dinner (posted 2015-01) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/484-2015-e5-b9-b4-e7-ad-86-e6-9c-83-e5-b9-b4-e5-88-8a-e5-a4-a7-e7-b4-90-e7-b4-84|484. 2015 年筆會年刊大紐約區海外台灣人筆會 2015/01]]; forum 「兩岸經濟關係分析」 at the 筆會 2014 十週年 forums — `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/386-大紐約區海外台灣人筆會2014-十週年特刊大紐約區海.md`.
