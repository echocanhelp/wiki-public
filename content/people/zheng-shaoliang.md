# Zheng Shaoliang (鄭紹良)

**Zheng Shaoliang** (鄭紹良) is a Taiwanese American pharmacist and radio host. He ran "十全藥局" (Shiquan Pharmacy) in San Francisco for 30 years, serving the local community not just as a pharmacist but as a trusted advisor and friend.

## Pharmacy Career

Zheng ran Shiquan Pharmacy in San Francisco for 30 years. His pharmacy was more than just a place to fill prescriptions — it became a community gathering point where patients came not only for medication but to talk with him. Customers from diverse backgrounds, ages, and genders would line up not just to ask about health issues and complex conditions, but also to seek his advice on personal love and life struggles.

## Radio Program

Zheng hosted a two-hour radio program where he discussed life philosophy with his audience. His show was popular with the community, and listeners appreciated his optimistic outlook on life despite his physical disabilities. He maintained an optimistic view of his own condition, stating: "I rarely think of myself as disabled; I just think of myself as a person with a hunchback and lame leg, and everything else is worth being grateful for."

## Philosophy on Disability

Despite his physical challenges, Zheng approached life with optimism. He reflected: "Fortunately, because of my physical condition, most of the time is spent on things I love — literature, music. Otherwise, with my lively personality and decent looks, I probably wouldn't be able to live a steady life."
