## Role in the Community

- **大會總幹事, 2007 美南夏令會 (Dallas):** chaired the 台美人檔案座談會 (Taiwanese American Archives forum, 2007-07-07 evening, Holiday Inn Select North Dallas, ~100 attendees) and opened the discussion on why Taiwanese Americans must archive their own history and raise awareness that Taiwanese are not Chinese — the meeting record (band A, 2007美南夏令會 minutes, reporter 張仕英) also documents 張仁裕's report on archiving 台灣人傳統基金會/台灣會館 records. Recorded in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys155|155. 台美人檔案座談會 in 2007美南夏令會]] — he helped launch the archival drive that became this corpus.
