# Shun Dar Lin (林舜達)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Shun Dar Lin
- **Chinese:** 林舜達
- **Era:** 1935
- **Geography:** Taiwan Tainan
- **Core roles:** engineer, scientist, professor, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

1. Water Quality Division Best Paper Award for “Giardia lamblia and Water Supply” from the American Water Works Association, 1986

## Education
- National Taiwan Univ. — 1958 — B.S., Civil Engineering
- Univ. of Cincinnati — 1964 — M.S., Sanitary Engineering
- Syracuse Univ. — 1967 — Ph. D., Sanitary Engineering

## Employment
- Illinois Pollution Control Board — 2008 - 2010 — One of 5 board members
- University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign — 1967 - 2002 — Professor Emeritus

## Role in the Community

- **Memoirist in the TAHS story corpus.** He wrote his own life story 「一個鄉下孩子由台灣台南到芝加哥」 (*A Country Boy from Tainan, Taiwan to Chicago*, writing as "Stan Lin 林舜達", 2015/11) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories366|366 (Chinese)]] / [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories366-eng|366-eng (English)]]. It is the primary first-person record of his journey from a rural Tainan childhood to Chicago.
- **Author, community health writing.** 「長生操」, a piece in the TAHS *Life/生活* publications column (2004/12) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1042|1042. 長生操]].
- **Archival donor.** The TAHS holds "Collection of Prof. Shun Dar Lin 林舜達教授的收藏" — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/collection-of-prof-shun-dar-lin|42. Collection of Prof. Shun Dar Lin]] — evidence his papers/collection were entrusted to the movement record itself.
- Biographical encyclopedia entry: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-shun-dar-lin|597. Shun Dar Lin 林舜達]] (2015/09).
