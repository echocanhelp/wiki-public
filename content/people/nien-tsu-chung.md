# Nien-Tsu Chung (莊念祖)

## Story

When NASA handed 莊念祖 (Nien-Tsu Chung) its highest honor in the summer of 2024, the citation pointed at something very specific: rocket fuel, and what it makes possible for the exploration of space. The 台美史料中心 (TA Archives) told the story to the LA community in its August 2024 newsletter, and its own story corpus had run it a month earlier as 「907. 台裔莊念祖研究火箭燃料助探索火星 NASA授最高榮譽」.

The work itself is unforgiving. Chung's experiments have to be completed inside the roughly twenty seconds of weightlessness a plane can create by flying a parabola — a window too short for most lab routines, which is exactly why the research is hard and why the award mattered. What the newsletter foregrounded was where he came from: Chung thanked 清華大學 (National Tsing Hua University) for his education, the university that sent a young man on to a life of American aerospace research.

His path, as TA Archives recorded it, is one the community knows well — Taiwan-born, and settled in the United States as a young man.
