# Dr. Taitzer Wang (王泰澤博士)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Dr. Taitzer Wang
- **Chinese:** 王泰澤博士
- **Era:** 1939
- **Geography:** Pingtung, Taiwan(屏東萬丹)
- **Core roles:** scientist, professor, artist
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

我小時住鄉間，喜歡看木工作木具，看竹工作竹具，雖然無親手工作經驗，但是無意識中，模仿了匠工手藝。留學美國學成工作時，因為 DIY 流行，受環境影響，閒暇時，自然養成木工嗜好。

## Family

- **Wife:** Judy(張喜久)


## Role in the Community
- With wife Judy (張喜久), the first Taiwanese couple to co-own an American retail bookstore — Little Professor Book Center, Cincinnati, 1991–1999 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/150-e7-8e-8b-e6-b3-b0-e6-be-a4-e5-a4-ab-e5-a9-a6-taitzer-and-judy-wang-the-first|150. 王泰澤夫婦 / Little Professor Book Center]]).
- Credited as one of the founding leaders of the first Taiwanese American-association clubhouse (辛城台灣同鄉會會館, Cincinnati) in 鄭憲章 et al.'s memoir ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys293|293. 第一個台灣同鄉會的會館]], citing Taiwan Tribune 915, 1990-11-29).
- Community author: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-161|161. 台語七十年－日、漢、英語無法取代的福佬話]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-162|162. 我的木雕情趣：親情、友情、政情 / 2014-12]].
- His memorabilia are held as an archival collection: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/private-collections-95-taitzer-wang|95. 王泰澤博士的收藏]].
- Appears (左一) among the singers at a wedding banquet in the Houston Taiwanese community record ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-359|359. Houston 台灣人合作社記憶]], 1960s).
- HOLD: conflict in store name — employment table says "Little Professor's Bookstore (小教授書局)", the TAH work record titles it "Little Professor Book Center".
