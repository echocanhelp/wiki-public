## Role in the Community

- **2007– — YouTube 世代 :** Under the handle KevJumba he became one of YouTube's most-subscribed comedians at age 19, posting 60+ videos on high school, racism, and stereotypes — a touchpoint the Taiwanese American community explicitly claimed as one of its own.
- **2009-10 — B HERE 肝病運動 :** Interviewed by TaiwaneseAmerican.org staff at the B HERE Hepatitis B campaign stop at UC Davis, where he talked about his parents' support and his JumbaFund giving-back project — an example of the community steering its young online celebrities toward Asian-American health advocacy.
- **2010-10 — 主流能見度 :** Covered by TaiwaneseAmerican.org when he and his father Michael Wu appeared on CBS's Amazing Race.
- **2012 — 「other」論述中的台美人 :** Cited alongside Jeremy Lin and Justin Lin in the TaiwaneseAmerican.org Blacklava-20th-anniversary essay as visible Taiwanese Americans who counter the model-minority stereotype.
