# Prof. Lily Yeh Ja (葉公杼教授)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Prof. Lily Yeh Ja
- **Chinese:** 葉公杼教授
- **Era:** 1947
- **Geography:** Fuzhou, China (福州)
- **Core roles:** scientist, professor, educator
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who's Who

Best known for completing the molecular selection of potassium channels (完成鉀離子通道的分子選殖工作而最為知名).
