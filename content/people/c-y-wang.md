## Role in the Community

- 2011 — US-side publicity coordinator for the《好國好民》Dear Taiwan eight-city screening-and-talk circuit: the TJJ movement record carries notices for each stop — 波士頓 (2011-10-15, [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240718223218_root_b5e568ec43e787f1|TJJ 2011-10-15]]) and 洛杉磯・舊金山・芝加哥 (2011-11-08, [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240719002430_root_1f001897255f71a7|TJJ 2011-11-08]]) — naming 黃泰郎、周明宏、王振源 as US coordinators and LK Chen／陳麗貴、CY Wang／王振源 as producers alongside director 陳麗貴.
- 2011 — Co-producer (with director 陳麗貴) of the documentary《好國好民》Dear Taiwan —完整版 81 分鐘、剪短版 43 分鐘，記錄范姜、魚仔林、林昶佐、大支等各領域台灣青年世代 — and US coordinator (with 黃泰郎、周明宏) for its nationwide eight-city screening-and-talk circuit（[[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whoswho1577|TAH #1577]]）.
