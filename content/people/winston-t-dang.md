## Role in the Community
- 1974 — 三壘手 for the New York team at the Toronto Taiwanese softball tournament, a founding memory of NY Taiwanese community sports ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/349-our-journeys|349. 1974年(45年前) 多倫多壘球賽追憶]]).
- c. 1991–1992 — 公關主任 (public-relations director) of CTIR. With director 蔡武雄 and 總幹事 王羨茹 he helped organize the 1992-02-14 meeting with 立委 許添財 and Clinton White House aides 魏得曼/蘇德柏 on the passport place-of-birth issue, and fed documentation to Rep. Ackerman's office — a key thread of the TAIWANGATE-era lobbying record ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1056|1056. TAIWANGATE / Winston T. Dang / 07/1991]]).
- The campaign's own victory memo — CTIR's account in 台灣公論報 1250 (1994-04-23), preserved as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys254|Our Journeys #254]] — names 陳重信 alongside 蔡武雄 and 王羨茹 as the CTIR officers whose White House and congressional lobbying (alongside FAPA's 佩爾 amendment track) led to the 1994-04-19 Senate–House Foreign Affairs joint conference adopting the passport place-of-birth "台灣" amendment without challenge.
- His own TAH Foundation biography record is held at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/661-winston-t-dang-e9-99-b3-e9-87-8d-e4-bf-a1-201510|661. Winston T. Dang 陳重信 / 2015/10]].
