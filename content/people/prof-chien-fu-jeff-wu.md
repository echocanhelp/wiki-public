## Story

吳建福教授 was born in 1949 in Hsinchu, Taiwan, and the TAH Foundation Who's Who lists him as a scientist, professor, and educator. He studied mathematics at National Taiwan Univ, graduating in 1971, and took the Berkeley doctorate in statistics in 1976 under advisor Peter Bickel, writing on optimal design of experiments. His posts ran from instructor at Berkeley, 1976–1977, through the Univ. of Wisconsin statistics department, 1977–1988, rising from assistant to full Professor; he then held the GM/NSERC Chair in Quality and Productivity at the Univ. of Waterloo, 1988–1993, the Carver chair at Michigan, Ann Arbor, 1993–2003, and the Coca-Cola Chair in Engineering Statistics and Professor at Georgia Institute of Technology, where the faculty page now lists him as Professor Emeritus. His 1983 paper on the convergence properties of the EM algorithm is his most cited, and the page credits him with the first use of the term Data Science in a 1986 lecture in Beijing and with the 1997 Carver Chair lecture "Statistics = Data Science". Honors on this page include Academician of Academia Sinica, 2000, the US National Academy of Engineering election, 2004, the 1987 COPSS Presidents' Award, the 2020 Class of 1934 Distinguished Professor Award — Georgia Tech's highest faculty award — and fellowships of the IMS, ASA, ASQ, and INFORMS. His TAH Who's Who record numbered 1792 was published in July 2017.
