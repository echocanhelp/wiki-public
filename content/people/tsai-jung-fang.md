# 蔡榮芳 (Tsai Jung-fang)

蔡榮芳 is a historian and author. Pastor [[people/albert-s-lai|賴信雄]] supplied his essay 〈憶往事〉 on 2026-10-23 for the reprint of *Toward A Community of Hope* and for the audiobook. Romanization Tsai Jung-fang is provisional until he confirms the English form.

## Identity Snapshot

- Era: 1950s Beigang; Los Angeles about 1969–1973; writing in 2020s
- Geography: 雲林北港; Los Angeles; later 台中
- Core roles: historian; UCLA history doctoral student in the late 1960s–early 1970s; author of books on 黃彰輝 and 林攀龍

## Reprint essay

〈憶往事〉 is dated 2026-06-02, 台中. He describes himself as not a Christian, but as someone who attended church school, knew pastors, and often joined the early Los Angeles Taiwanese Christian gatherings as a student. The essay remembers the Beigang church built under [[people/pingtung-lai|賴炳烔]], meeting 賴美玲 and 賴信雄 around 1969–1970, and losing touch in 1973 until a 2023 letter about his book on 黃彰輝.

Full manuscript stays with the audiobook production files. This page does not replace the forthcoming print text.
