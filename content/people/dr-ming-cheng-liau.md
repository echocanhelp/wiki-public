## Role in the Community
- **UFAI founding (1966):** Listed as a Houston delegate at the 1966-07-04 founding of the United Formosans in America for Independence (全美台灣獨立聯盟), per the movement memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys81|ourjourneys81]]; the parallel account [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys234|ourjourneys234]] names him among the Houston delegates (with 張燦鍙) at the 1966-06-18 Philadelphia UFI–FASG merger talks that resolved to found UFAI on 7/4, whose members were sworn secret members except those who had to appear publicly.
- **望春風 editor:** Part of the Houston team (with 林振榮、吳維煌、郭鐘樑、陳淮崇) that edited 《望春風》 issues 29–47 (1971-12 – 1973-06), per [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys307|ourjourneys307]].
- **Houston community center:** One of the five Taiwanese Language School principals (with 郭珠貞 — his wife Grace — 沈郁芳、謝清實、林秋成) who filed the 1988-09 application that made the Taiwanese Heritage Society of Houston (THSH) a tax-exempt non-profit, a prerequisite for buying the Taiwanese Community Center (IRS approval came mid-January 1991). He joined the language-school side (with 吳連山) in inspecting the candidate building's interior on 5/18. At the founding general meeting on 1991-06-01 the purchase motion passed and he was elected **secretary (秘書)** of the seven-director board — 莊勝義理事長、楊朝諭副理事長、莊承業財務 — formally empowered to negotiate the purchase, per [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys233|ourjourneys233]] (EN: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys233-eng|ourjourneys233-eng]]).
- **His own memoirs in the corpus:** [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories460-eng|460. 告別休士頓台灣同鄉 / Farewell to TAA Houston / 2016-09]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-712|712. 一個黑名單的自白 / 2019-12]].
