# John Hsu (許惠欽)

> Not to be confused with [[people/dr-john-hsu|Dr. John Hsu (徐新宏)]], nuclear scientist — same English name, different person.

## Identity Snapshot
- **Era:** Contemporary (real-estate career from 1988; firm leadership 1985–present per LinkedIn)
- **Geography:** Whittier / Rowland Heights, Los Angeles area, Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** business leader (real-estate investment & management)
- **Firm:** Officer/CEO & Managing Member, [[organizations/stc-management|STC Management]]

## Record

- **Chinese name 許惠欽** — from Chinese-language press: 《大紀元》2013-12-27 interview ("STC 許惠欽 (John Hsu)：指標顯示 2014 榮景可期", Rowland Heights bureau), cross-referenced by Sing Tao USA interview carried on STC's own news page.
- **Trade leadership:** **2010 President, CCIM Greater Los Angeles Chapter** (installed 2009-11-03, Beverly Hills Country Club — announced on STC's blog); first Chinese-American selected to the CCIM Institute's Jay W. Levine Leadership Development Academy (2009); CCIM Institute China Liaison, International Committee; IREM Greater LA membership committee.
- **Firm under his leadership:** first Asian firm in the US to earn the AMO (Accredited Management Organization) designation; portfolio >$1.5B commercial; ~60 commercial centers in SoCal (OCBJ); LinkedIn-era company stats: founded 1985, revenue $20–30M range.
- **Deals in press:** acquired the former Bank of America building (Nixon Plaza site, National Historic Landmark — Nixon's first law office), Uptown Whittier, Oct 2014, $4.75M, with restoration plans incl. the rooftop flag; STC affiliate purchased **Anaheim GardenWalk** for $80M (closed Dec 21 [2024], OCBJ) — he is Managing Member of GardenWalk and quoted "if our tenants succeed, we succeed… this is a long-term holding, we are not planning to flip."
- **Community-economy interface (company news page, his tenure):** RHCA (Rowland Heights Chinese Association) installation gala at STC Center 2012; Million Stars Singing Contest and ACDA dance festival hosted at STC Center; AIR mixer "to bridge the gap between mainstream and Asian realty communities" (2011); Ed Royce fundraiser attendance (2012); Yes Plaza New Year countdown (夜市廣場); Nov 2011 luncheon with ROC/PRC-consular note: met "Consul General of China in Los Angeles" Qiu Shaofang — title as STC wrote it.
- **Biography (Chinese press, Sept 2026):** raised in Taiwan; emigrated to the US ~43 years before 2026 (so ~1983); founder of **STC 顺天联合基金会 (STC Foundation)**, which presents 電音三太子 and cultural shows at the firm's community events; on the Nov 2025 acquisition of the 13-acre Colima Rd. mall now renamed **[[organizations/stc-rowland-legacy|STC Rowland Legacy]]**: name = 傳承, and with **Taste of Taiwan 2026** (Taiwan night market + Double Ten, Oct 2–11, 2026): "這個夢想終於實現了" — bringing the Taiwan night market of his memory to America.
- **Shaped SGV commercial landscape:** credited (Chinese press) with shaping 四季廣場 (Four Seasons Plaza), 夜市 (Yes Plaza night market), 羅蘭廣場 commercial districts.
- Education: UCLA B.A. Business & Economics; Pepperdine (Graziadio) M.B.A. Management & Investment. Designations CPM, CCIM. Languages per own bio: English, Mandarin, **Taiwanese**.
