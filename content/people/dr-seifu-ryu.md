## Role in the Community
- The memoir "台美醫師的歷史" (Our Journeys) places 劉清風 as the cautionary pioneer of the whole pre-war generation: even he, having graduated from an American medical school in 1926, could not obtain U.S. residence under the era's Chinese-exclusion/Japanese-exclusion immigration regime — Taiwanese were classified only as Japanese or Chinese — and was forced to take a teaching post at 復旦大學 in Shanghai instead (source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys285|Our Journeys 285, 台美醫師歷史]]). The essay uses him to show that no Taiwanese-American physicians could exist before WWII.
- Family memory recorded in the same memoir series: his sister 劉聰慧醫師 (b. 1917, 台北帝大醫學博士 1945, 杜聰明's snake-venom lab) trained in the U.S. 1947–49, later Okinawa and Japan, and emigrated to Seattle from 1980; their niece 劉姃姃 (Seifu Ryu's eldest daughter) supplied the 1974 résumé behind that account (source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys270|Our Journeys 270]]).
- Community-record profiles: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-1847-seifu-ryu|1847. Dr. Seifu Ryu 劉清風醫師]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/firstfamous-391|391.
