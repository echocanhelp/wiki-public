# Yea-Hwang Uang (汪雅煌博士)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Yea-Hwang Uang
- **Chinese:** 汪雅煌博士
- **Era:** 1949
- **Geography:** 台灣 台南 關廟
- **Core roles:** engineer, scientist
- **Source:** TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Founding Member and President of East Tennessee Taiwanese American Association (1975; 1976-1977)

## Role in the Community
- Founding member of the East Tennessee Taiwanese American Association (東田納西台灣同鄉會, later TAA East Tennessee chapter). According to the chapter's founding memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys313|313. 東田納西台灣同鄉會成立簡史]] (張輝雄), he was one of the five UT students — 高民環、汪雅煌、陳耀宗、林正惠、李明輝 — who organized the chapter in 1974–1975, free of the Nationalist student-union surveillance; the founding convention was held at the UT Baptist Student Center on Thanksgiving Day 1975, attended by 50–60 people (English version: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys313-eng|Brief Introduction of TAA East Tennessee Chapter]]).

One detail newly absorbed from 張輝雄's founding memoir: during the 1974–75 organizing phase, it was either 高民環 or 汪雅煌 (the author could not recall which) who first approached 張輝雄 to serve as founding president — evidence Uang was in the innermost organizing circle. The chapter was originally founded as 「諾誠台灣同鄉會」 (Knoxville Taiwanese Association) and only later renamed 東田納西台灣同鄉會 at the request of nearby chapters.
