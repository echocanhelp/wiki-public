## Role in the Community

- **1965** — Attended the 1965-10-29~30 Taiwanese students' nation-building congress at the University of Wisconsin as foreign-affairs representative of the 台灣共和國臨時政府 (Provisional Government of the Republic of Taiwan), alongside Chin Man-li (Tokyo 台灣青年社), Hwang I-ming (Canada 台灣人權委員會), Chen I-te (U.S. 台灣獨立聯盟) and others. Recorded in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys81|81. 周烒明: 早期威大台灣學生在台灣建國運動所扮演的角色]].
- **1982** — One of fifteen attendees at the 1982-02-13 Los Angeles meeting (convened after 張燦鍙's 1982-01-10 letter) that set aside the radio-station plan and founded the body that became FAPA, adopting its three-point charter on democracy, self-determination and overseas Taiwanese rights. Recorded in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys65|65. 蔡同榮: FAPA的誕生]].
- **Who's Who record** — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/934-c-a-chou-e5-91-a8-e6-98-8e-e5-93-b2-e5-ae-89-201604|934. C. A. Chou 周明(哲)安 / 2016/04]] (TAH spells the name 周明(哲)安; matches this page's 哲安 form).
