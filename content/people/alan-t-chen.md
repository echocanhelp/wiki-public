## Role in the Community

- **NATPA editor:** As 編輯 of 北美洲台灣人教授協會 (NATPA), 陳清風 published the quarterly Newsletter and edited the commemorative volume 「NATPA十年的回顧 1980–1990」 during the run-up to NATPA's first return-to-Taiwan annual meeting (1990) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys47|47. 北美洲台灣人敎授協會首度回台召開年會的經緯和歷史意義]].
- **Chicago cultural supporter:** thanked by name as 陳清風 of 芝加哥台灣人文化促進會 among supporters of the 《世界台灣人美術展覽會》 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys256|256. 寫在畫展之前 / 黃根深 / 10/2016]]. This corroborates the Chicago base in the employment table above.
- **Own community record:** [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/713-alan-t-chen-e9-99-b3-e6-b8-85-e9-a2-a8-201511|713. ALAN T. CHEN 陳清風 / 2015/11]].
- **Disambiguation:** TAH also holds a profile for a *different* "Alan T. Chen" — 陳天令, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whoswho1457|1457. Alan T. Chen 陳天令 / 2017/01]]. Do not merge the two.
- HOLD: conflict — the puppet-theatre memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys277|277. 威斯康辛大學「台灣布袋戲團」的故事 / 朱靜女 / 02/2017]] credits a 陳清風 professor (described as 已不在人世 by 2017, active at UW-Madison 台灣同學會, supplied 布袋戲 scripts/puppets, directed the 1997 「虎姑婆」 debut at Milwaukee's Moon Festival) with founding the troupe; the memoir places him in Wisconsin and records his death, while this page's TAH profile (published 2015/11, Chicago-based) does not establish whether this is the same person. Not merged.
