## Role in the Community

- **NATPA editor:** As 編輯 of 北美洲台灣人教授協會 (NATPA), 陳清風 published the quarterly Newsletter and edited the commemorative volume 「NATPA十年的回顧 1980–1990」 during the run-up to NATPA's first return-to-Taiwan annual meeting (1990) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys47|47. 北美洲台灣人敎授協會首度回台召開年會的經緯和歷史意義]].
- **Chicago cultural supporter:** thanked by name as 陳清風 of 芝加哥台灣人文化促進會 among supporters of the 《世界台灣人美術展覽會》 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys256|256. 寫在畫展之前 / 黃根深 / 10/2016]]. This corroborates the Chicago base in the employment table above.
- **台語劇團 (theatre):** 鄭炳全's memoir records that as a 台美公民協會洛杉磯分會 (UFAI/LA) board member he booked the 「芝加哥陳清風台語劇團」 to stage 台語劇《林投姐》 at the chapter's annual banquet — consistent with the 影劇編導 training and Chicago base on this profile — [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250209211009_旅美五十周年-_-鄭炳全_438dc69b91722b16|旅美五十周年 / 鄭炳全]].
- **Own community record:** [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/713-alan-t-chen-e9-99-b3-e6-b8-85-e9-a2-a8-201511|713. ALAN T. CHEN 陳清風 / 2015/11]].
