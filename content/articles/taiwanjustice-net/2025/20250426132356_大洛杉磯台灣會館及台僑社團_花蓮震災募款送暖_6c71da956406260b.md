2024-04-05
2024年4月2日花蓮地牛翻身，震撼台灣之餘也讓全球關切，在第一時間不僅無數海內外的臺僑同胞想幫助台灣，也有不少國際朋友希望能用行動來協助台灣。
為求把台灣救災需要的資金經由正確的管道送達相關單位，大洛杉磯台灣會館與洛杉磯台美社團請台美鄉親一起響應這項募款送暖行動，本會館將從即日起募款至4月25日截止，所募得善款將全數捐給台灣衛福部0403花蓮震災援助專案捐款專案，協助罹難者遺屬、傷家及傷亡家庭的生活醫療重建，願受災地區早日重建、受災民眾獲得更多支援。
支票捐款抬頭請寫給Taiwan Center ，並於memo處註明2024台灣0403地震，由於台灣會館是501c3非營利組織，辦公室將在收到您的善款後核發抵稅收據。

2024 Hualien Earthquake Disaster Relief Fundraising Action
On April 2, 2024, the Hualien earthquake shook Taiwan, capturing global attention. Not only did countless Taiwanese nationals at home and abroad wish to assist Taiwan, but also many international friends hoped to lend much needed support.
To channel the necessary funds for Taiwan’s disaster relief through the proper channels, the Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles calls on Taiwanese American organizations and community members to join in supporting this fundraising initiative.
We will be accepting donations from now until April 25th, and all proceeds will be donated to the 0403 Hualien Earthquake Fundraising Program under Taiwan’s Ministry of Health and Welfare affiliated Taiwan Foundation for Disaster Relief. This will aid in assisting the families of the deceased, as well as providing support for the injured and families affected by the disaster, with the hope of facilitating the reconstruction of the affected areas and providing further assistance to those affected.
Please make checks payable to “Taiwan Center” and indicate “2024 Taiwan 0403 Earthquake” in the memo section. As the Taiwan Center is a 501(C) nonprofit organization, tax-compliant donation receipts will be issued by the Taiwan Center office upon receipt of your donation.
