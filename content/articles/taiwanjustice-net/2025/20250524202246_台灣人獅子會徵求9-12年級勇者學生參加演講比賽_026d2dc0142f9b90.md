台灣人獅子會將在3/1/21-3/15/21 舉行學生演講比賽。參賽資格為本地9到12年級的高中生。比賽的題目是：“Distance on Online Learning: Pros and Cons” 。參賽者講5-10分鐘，前2-3名可以獲得$100獎金，然後再代表台灣人獅子會和其它區的代表比， 獎金高達$4500-$11000。 （詳情請看附件）。請有興趣者，踴躍報名參加！
Taiwanese Lions Club’s “Student Speech Contest” program is seeking local high school students (grade 9-12) to participate in presenting their 5 to 10-minute speech to be judged for the winner at the local level who will move up to further participate in other zone and regional level for more scholarship prize awards. The local club level speech will occur between March 1 to March 15 and the winner will receive $100 at that level and move up to other levels for eventual district level winner prizes of $4,500 to $11,000 (see Student Contest Booklet pg 10 for prize detail).
The topic for this year’s speech is “Distance on Online Learning: Pros and Cons”. The attached file contains the event flyer, participant signup form, and detail booklet. Please share this information with students interested. Thanks!
