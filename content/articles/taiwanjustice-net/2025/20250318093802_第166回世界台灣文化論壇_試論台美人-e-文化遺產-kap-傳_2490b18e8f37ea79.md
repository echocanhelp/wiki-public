AI assisted English Translation: The Taiwanese American Archives Center invited representatives from Taiwanese summer camps, hometown associations, Taiwan Centers, Taiwanese cultural organizations, and second-generation Taiwanese American groups across the US to attend the 2-night, 3-day 2nd Taiwanese American Conference from 1/10-1/12 this year.
The main topic was the preservation of Taiwanese American cultural heritage. Professor Peng-hui Liao from UC San Diego’s Taiwan Studies Center and the “Lessons from Ah-má and Ah-kong” documentary team shared about preserving Taiwanese American historical materials, including collection, preservation, digitization, and dissemination methods. Organizations shared their histories and current status.
In this Forum Mr. Simon Cheng, aka Tēⁿ Liông-kng President of the Taiwanese United Fund (TUF), and his TUF colleage will present a more detailed discussion based on his conference summary report.
