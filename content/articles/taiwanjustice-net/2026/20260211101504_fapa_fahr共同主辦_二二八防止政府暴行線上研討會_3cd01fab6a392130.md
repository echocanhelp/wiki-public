2022大洛杉磯二二八追思會系列活動之一的第六屆跨族裔「二二八防止政府暴行研討會」將由台灣人公共事務會（FAPA）洛杉磯分會及全美台灣人權協會（FAHR）共同主辦。
今年參加的族群代表有台灣、香港、緬甸、泰國、維吾爾族、羅興亞民族（Rohingya)，古巴、多明尼加和波多黎各共八位族群代表，來藉著了解並追思台灣228事件的同時，供各族裔講述各民族過去經歷政府暴行實例。詳情請參閱活動傳單。
希望大家能踴躍參加並解各族群的故事，活動全程以英文發表交談，各講者發表後會有Q&A時間供鄉親反應或分享。請大家千萬不要錯過！

Taiwan 228 Atrocities Prevention Online Seminar
The 6th annual “Taiwan 228 Government Atrocities Prevention Seminar” will be co-organized by the Formosan Association for Public Affairs (FAPA) Los Angeles Chapter and the Formosan Association for Human Rights (FAHR).
We are fortunate and honored to have organization representatives from the following communities to join us this year and share: Taiwan, Hong Kong, Burma, Thailand, Uyghur, Rohingya, Cuba, Dominican and Puerto Rico. The speakers shall describe past examples of government atrocities experienced by each ethnic group, and we will have the opportunity to learn more about the stories and what we can do to support each other, stop the ongoing crisis and prevent the tragedies from happening again. See the event flyer for details.We hope those who are interested can attend this meaningful event. After each speakers’ presentations, there will be a Q&A session for the audiences to learn more from our speakers. Please don’t miss it!
