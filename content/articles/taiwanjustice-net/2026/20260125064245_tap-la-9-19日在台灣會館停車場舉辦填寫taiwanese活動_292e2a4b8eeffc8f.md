美國2020人口普查
台美菁英協會洛杉磯分會舉辦填寫 Taiwanese(台灣人)活動
歡迎鄉親一起來參加
大洛杉磯台灣會館響應2020人口普查，邀請大家來參加TAP-LA台美菁英協會洛杉磯分會舉辦的填入Taiwanese活動。
活動於9月19日下午2點台灣會館停車場，一起來創意車輛裝飾比賽，請攜帶可水洗顏料、氣球、貼紙和任何物品來裝飾您的愛車。最佳創意獎可獲得free boba card to 7 Leaves.請台美洛杉磯鄉親們一起來共襄盛舉！
溫馨提醒，活動現場請記得戴口罩、勤用乾洗手液及保持安全社交距離喔
2020 Census Write in Taiwanese
Saturday, September 19th 2PM
Taiwan Center Parking Lot, 3001 Walnut Grove Ave, Rosemead, CA 91770
Get creative and have fun! Bring out your washable window markers, balloons, and anything to decorate your car! Car Decoration Contest: the best decorated car will receive a free boba card to 7 Leaves! TAP-LA board members will be the judge, so show off your talent!
For everyone’s safety, please bring face mask, hand sanitizers. keep at least 6 feet away from others.
