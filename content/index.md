# Echopedia

**Echopedia** is the public knowledge base of the Taiwanese American Historical Society (**TAHS** / 台美人歷史協會). It documents people, congregations, and organizations that shape Taiwanese American life — with bilingual names, careful verification notes, and links across a living archive.

## What Echopedia is (and isn't)

**Is**

- A public, linkable encyclopedia of Taiwanese American people and organizations
- Bilingual where evidence allows (Chinese names + romanization)
- Conservative about verification — pending pages are labeled as such
- Maintained as part of the Echo / TAHS community systems stack

**Isn't**

- A social network or private member directory
- An automated dump of every message or contact
- A substitute for primary archives, interviews, or academic monographs

## Contribute & correct

Community memory is stronger when many hands review it.

1. **Browse** existing pages and note errors (names, roles, chronology).
2. **Prefer sources** you can point to: publications, public records, consented oral history.
3. **Reach TAHS / Echo maintainers** via the community channels that already use Echopedia for onboarding and documentation.
4. **Respect privacy** — do not publish private contact data, keys, or non-consented personal details.
