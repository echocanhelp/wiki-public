# Taiwanese American Organization at UC Irvine

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Organization at UC Irvine
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Organization (TAO) at the University of California, Irvine is a student organization founded in April 2009. It serves as UCI’s primary community for Taiwanese Americans and students with an interest in Taiwanese culture, and is listed among the Asian American organizations recognized by the UCI School of Humanities.
