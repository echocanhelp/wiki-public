# Taiwanese American Organization at UC Irvine

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Organization at UC Irvine
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Organization (TAO) at the University of California, Irvine is a student organization founded in April 2009. It serves as UCI’s primary community for Taiwanese Americans and students with an interest in Taiwanese culture, and is listed among the Asian American organizations recognized by the UCI School of Humanities.

## Role in the Community

- 2012-10: UCI Taiwanese American club joined the inter-campus Mini Olympics & Social with UCLA: [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/mini-olympics-social-with-uci-ucla-taiwanese-american-clubs|Mini Olympics & Social with UCI + UCLA Taiwanese American Clubs, 2012]] (record names "UCI … Taiwanese American Clubs" generically; attributed to TAO on name-match only — HOLD if other UCI clubs existed on campus at the time).
- 2014-04: hosted a Lantern Festival and Night Market event at UCI, recorded in the community press: [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/lantern-festival-and-night-market-with-tao-at-uci|Lantern Festival and Night Market with TAO at UCI, 2014]].
- 2010-11: named by full name — "Taiwanese American Organization (TAO) at UC Irvine" — in Ho Chie's retrospective of the dozen-plus campuses and conferences he visited that year: [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/on-discovering-passion-purpose|On Discovering Passion & Purpose, 2010]]. This is the corpus's earliest explicit TAO-at-UCI naming; it supersedes the earlier note that the 2014 Lantern Festival record was the first explicit naming, and confirms the club was already active by late 2010, consistent with the April 2009 founding.
- 2012-01: recruitment/party notice in the community press: [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/college-students-party-with-uc-irvines-taiwanese-american-organization|College Students! Party with UC Irvine's Taiwanese American Organization, 2012]].
