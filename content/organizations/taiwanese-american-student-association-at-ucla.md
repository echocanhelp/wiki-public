# Taiwanese American Student Association at UCLA

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Student Association at UCLA
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Student Association (TASA) at UCLA is a student organization established on October 31, 2011. Its mission, as stated in the UCLA Community club directory, is to spread Taiwanese awareness and culture through community involvement and to build and strengthen the Taiwanese American community on campus. TASA describes itself as open to Taiwanese students, Taiwanese Americans, and anyone with an interest in Taiwanese culture.
