# Taiwanese Graduate Student Association at UC Davis

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Graduate Student Association at UC Davis
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Graduate Student Association (TGSA) at UC Davis is a community organization for Taiwanese graduate students at the university. According to its Google Sites page, TGSA serves primarily Taiwanese graduate students, providing a platform for sharing life information, and exchanging experiences related to TA and RA roles, among other topics. The site notes that as of fall 2016, UC Davis enrolled more than 70 Taiwanese graduate students.

## Role in the Community
UC Davis has a documented place in the Taiwanese American movement in Northern California before TGSA's founding. The corpus "Our Journeys" records that during the tenth president Ching-sheng Huang's (黃景生) term, the World Federation of Taiwanese Associations (世台會) held its tenth annual meeting at UC Davis (July 1–4) — the first time the host federation brought the WFTA annual meeting to its own city ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-357|Our Journeys 357]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys38|Our Journeys 38]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys38-eng|English]]). The corpus also documents other Taiwanese student groups active on the Davis campus in the 2010s: an ITASA mixer in 2013 ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/itasa-mixer-at-uc-davis|ITASA Mixer at UC Davis]]), a TAO Tang Yuan Night gathering on 2013-11-12 ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/tao-tang-yuan-night-at-uc-davis|TAO Tang Yuan Night at UC Davis]]), and a TAO's Got Talent performance in 2014 ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/taos-got-talent-performance-at-uc-davis|TAO's Got Talent at UC Davis]]). Davis also hosted the 2009 B HERE campaign's final stop, recorded in a band-A oral interview with vlogger KevJumba at the campus ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/we-b-here-with-kevjumba-he-e2-80-99s-taiwanese|We B HERE with KevJumba, 2009-10-30]]) — an early marker of the Taiwanese American identity wave that TGSA's generation later institutionalized on campus. TGSA's reported platform — life information sharing and TA/RA experience exchange for Taiwanese graduate students (70+ enrolled as of fall 2016) — sits within this longer student-activist ecosystem at Davis.
