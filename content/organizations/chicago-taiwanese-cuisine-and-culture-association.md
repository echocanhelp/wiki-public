# Chicago Taiwanese Cuisine and Culture Association (芝加哥台灣美食文化交流協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Chicago Taiwanese Cuisine and Culture Association
- **Chinese:** 芝加哥台灣美食文化交流協會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Chicago Taiwanese Cuisine and Culture Association promotes Taiwanese traditional and contemporary cuisine and cultural exchange among the local Taiwanese diaspora and broader community. The organization offers cooking classes taught by Teacher Chen, who charges only a modest materials fee of $15 per session with no tuition. Each session of eight classes culminates in a shared meal of the dishes prepared, with participants often taking home additional food to share with family. Interested students may enroll in either Friday or Saturday class sessions by contacting the association directly.
