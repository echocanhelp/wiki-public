# Taiwanese American Organization at UC Davis

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Organization at UC Davis
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Organization (TAO) at UC Davis is a cultural and social student organization focused on serving Taiwanese Americans and others interested in Taiwanese culture on the UC Davis campus. TAO was founded in approximately 2011 — the name TAO was chosen because “TASA” was already in use at Davis by the Thai American Student Association. The organization adopted the TAO name, a variant used at other UC campuses such as UC Irvine, where the model originated. TAO’s founding at Davis followed the involvement of a student leader with the West Coast ITASA network in 2011.
