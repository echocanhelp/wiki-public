# Taiwanese Association of America East Tennessee (東田納西)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of America East Tennessee
- **Chinese:** 東田納西
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America East Tennessee (東田納西 台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization of Taiwanese American community associations. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the East Tennessee area, centered in Knoxville, and is affiliated with the TAA’s Eastern Southern (美東南) regional division.

## History & Role in the Community
- **Founding (1975):** Organized at the University of Tennessee Knoxville amid the 1970s wave of Taiwanese student organizing against KMT campus surveillance. Five UT students led the organizing: 高民環、汪雅煌、陳耀宗、林正惠、李明輝. 陳耀宗 persuaded recently-immigrated 陳玉明 (co-owner of the L & N Restaurant) to serve as organizing-committee head and founding president; the founding convention drew 50–60 people. Primary account: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys313|313. 東田納西台灣同鄉會成立簡史 / 張惠雄]] (30th-anniversary history, originally in the chapter newsletter 大煙山麓 vol.2 no.5, 1995-11-05).
- **First charter name:** founded on Thanksgiving Day 1975 at the UT Baptist Student Center as 「諾城台灣同鄉會」 (Knoxville); renamed 東田納西台灣同鄉會 at the request of members in nearby towns.
- **Founding officers:** 陳玉明, first president — his 20th-anniversary essay (orig. 大煙山麓 vol.1 no.7, 1994-11-05; Japanese original trans. 周佑蓮) is held at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys314|314. 東田納西台灣同鄉會創會二十週年感言 / 陳玉明]]. 呂錦文, newly arrived in Knoxville, spoke at the founding convention and was elected vice president — see [[people/jinwun-winston-lue|呂錦文]].
- **Newsletter:** the chapter published the community newsletter 大煙山麓 (Big Smokies) from at least 1994 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/newsletter-taa-east-tennessee|大煙山麓 record]].
- **Southeast camp host:** the chapter hosted the first 美東南區台灣人夏令會 on 1979-06-08 at Harrison Bay State Park, Chattanooga (120 attendees from Atlanta/Athens/Knoxville; organizer 李豐隆; the chapter won the softball tournament), again in 1982 at Tusculum College, Johnson City (convener 林永樂; site of the 黨外四人行's first overseas collective appearance at an overseas camp), and in 1986 (convener 呂錦文) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys245|245. 美東南區台灣人夏令會簡史 / 劉格正]]. At the 1982 Tusculum camp the 黨外四人行 (尤清、康寧祥、張德銘、黃煌雄) appeared as the tour's first overseas stop, drawing 400+ attendees; the大会 renamed campus buildings for the occasion — Fine Art Building 「美麗島大樓」、Union Hall 「明德大樓」、Katherine Hall 「秀蓮大樓」, and the Gymnasium 「文成館」 in memory of 陳文成 — with one dormitory dubbed 「台灣村」 (same record).
