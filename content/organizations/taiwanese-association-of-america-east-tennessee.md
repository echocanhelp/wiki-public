# Taiwanese Association of America East Tennessee (東田納西)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of America East Tennessee
- **Chinese:** 東田納西
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America East Tennessee (東田納西 台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization of Taiwanese American community associations. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the East Tennessee area, centered in Knoxville, and is affiliated with the TAA’s Eastern Southern (美東南) regional division.
