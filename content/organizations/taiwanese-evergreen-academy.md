# Taiwanese Evergreen Academy (矽谷迦南長青學院)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Evergreen Academy
- **Chinese:** 矽谷迦南長青學院
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Evergreen Academy is a federally and state-registered nonprofit educational institution founded in February 2002 in Fremont, California, serving Taiwanese American seniors in the San Francisco Bay Area. The academy offers a wide range of courses and activities including practical English, health and medicine, arts and crafts, cooking, photography, computer skills, karaoke, fitness, and music appreciation. A branch campus was established in Mountain View in June 2006 to serve the Peninsula and South Bay communities, and relocated to San Jose in February 2009 under the name ‘Canaan Campus’ at 4405 Fortran Ct. The academy aims to enrich the retirement lives of Taiwanese American elders through learning, socializing, recreation, and physical activities. Seasonal programs include leisure travel, mid-term activities, and a graduation ceremony with awards.
