# Penghu.info 平台（澎湖知識服務／澎湖記憶庫）

**Penghu.info（澎湖知識服務平台／澎湖記憶庫）** is a live knowledge-service
platform about Penghu County — a cultural-memory archive rather than a
commercial or single-organization site. Its own homepage states the platform
totals **8,946 則知識**, organized along three axes: **空間** (空間: 馬公市／西嶼鄉／
望安鄉／七美鄉／白沙鄉／湖西鄉), **類別** (文化、宗教、歷史、地理、交通、產業、治理、生態),
and **時間** (史前時代、宋代、元代、明代、荷治時期、明鄭時期、清領時期、日治時期、戰後時期).

It is the authoritative local source cited by [[people/hsu-ling-yun||Hsu Ling-yun (許凌雲)]],
[[people/hsu-ching-chun||Hsu Ching-chun (許景淳)]], and
[[organizations/hsu-ling-yun-xiucai-memorial-hall||Huu Ling-yun Xiucai Memorial Hall]]
for Qing-era Penghu biography, the 凌雲秀才 memorial, and the 瓦硐 Xuu clan.

**Class:** live-small knowledge platform (no WordPress REST; the 2017-era
`sitemap.xml` lists 10 anchors, so the watch loop discovers the corpus by
same-site href crawl). **Full corpus (8,946 則) is a separate large ingest** —
this record captures only the historically-anchored subset linked from Tier1.
