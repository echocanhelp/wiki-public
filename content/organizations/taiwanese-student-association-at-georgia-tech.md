# Taiwanese Student Association at Georgia Tech

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Student Association at Georgia Tech
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Student Association at Georgia Tech (GT TSA) is a registered student organization at the Georgia Institute of Technology in Atlanta, Georgia. It is listed in Georgia Tech’s official Engage club directory (gatech.campuslabs.com) as well as in the OMED Student Resource Hub, confirming its status as a recognized campus organization. The organization maintains an Instagram presence at @gt.tsa and a Facebook group under the handle gttsa.

## Role in the Community
The TA.org story corpus documents campus activity at Georgia Tech within the intercollegiate Taiwanese American student movement:

- **2012-02-16** — Georgia Tech hosted the ITASA (Intercollegiate Taiwanese American Student Association) East Coast Conference in Atlanta, recorded in the corpus as [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/itasa-east-coast-conference-at-georgia-tech-in-atlanta|ITASA East Coast Conference at Georgia Tech in Atlanta]]. Hosting a regional ITASA conference indicates an active campus TSA chapter connected to the national network (see also [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/history-of-itasa|History of ITASA]]).
- **2014-04-11** — A Taiwanese Night Market was held at Georgia Tech, recorded as [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/taiwanese-night-market-at-georgia-tech|Taiwanese Night Market at Georgia Tech]] — a cultural-outreach event of the type campus TSAs organize.

HOLD: the corpus records place events at Georgia Tech but does not name the organizing chapter; attribution of these events specifically to GT TSA (vs. ad-hoc student groups) is inferred, not stated. Kept as campus activity evidence only.
