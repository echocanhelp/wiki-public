# Taiwanese Association of America Hawaii (夏威夷)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of America Hawaii
- **Chinese:** 夏威夷
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America Hawaii (夏威夷台灣同鄉會) is the Hawaii chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA), the national umbrella organization serving Taiwanese American communities across the United States. The chapter is based in Honolulu, Hawaii, and serves the Taiwanese American community in the state.
