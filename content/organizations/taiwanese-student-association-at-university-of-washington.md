# Taiwanese Student Association at University of Washington

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Student Association at University of Washington
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Student Association at the University of Washington (TSAUW), also known by its Chinese name 華盛頓大學台灣學生會, is a registered student organization dedicated to improving Taiwanese cultural awareness on campus and in the Greater Seattle area. The organization’s website and UW career center listing both confirm it is open to students of all backgrounds — not just those of Taiwanese heritage — and that its programming spans both cultural and social events throughout the academic year.
