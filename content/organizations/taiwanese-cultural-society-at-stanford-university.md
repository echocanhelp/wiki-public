# Taiwanese Cultural Society at Stanford University

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Cultural Society at Stanford University
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Cultural Society at Stanford University (TCS) is an undergraduate student organization that celebrates and promotes Taiwanese culture at Stanford and in the greater San Francisco Bay Area. The organization was formerly known as the “Taiwan Club” until 1995, when it was renamed the Taiwanese Cultural Society. It is housed within Stanford’s Asian American Activities Center (A³C) and is a member of both Stanford’s Asian American Student Association (AASA) and the Intercollegiate Taiwanese American Students Association (ITASA).
