# Taiwan Care Inc. (關懷台灣基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwan Care Inc.
- **Chinese:** 關懷台灣基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Taiwan Care Inc. is a Taiwanese American nonprofit organization rooted in the New Jersey Taiwanese community, which itself grew out of the broader wave of Taiwanese student immigration to the United States beginning in the late 1950s. The organization has its origins in the New Jersey Taiwanese Association, which formed in the late 1960s to early 1970s around New Brunswick and served as a social and cultural hub for Taiwanese immigrants in the region. As Taiwan's democratic movement intensified following the Formosa Incident (Meilidao), the New Jersey community became a key stop on North American tours by Taiwan's democracy activists, with members providing hospitality and financial support. Taiwan Care Inc. was established to formalize and sustain this support for democratic and civic causes in Taiwan in a more organized and ongoing manner.
