# Fri. Philo (哲學星期五)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Fri. Philo
- **Chinese:** 哲學星期五
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Café Philo (哲學星期五, literally ‘Philosophy Friday’) is a civic forum series that began on August 13, 2010, originally as part of the Youth Platform (青平台) initiative in Taiwan, becoming independently operated through small-scale fundraising in 2012. The forum holds regular and ad hoc citizen dialogues on topics spanning politics, human rights, education, and the arts. A companion series, ‘Philosophy Non-Friday’ (哲學「非」星期五), was established in August 2012 to explore unconventional civic and imaginative topics. The format has expanded to multiple U.S. cities — New York, Boston, Chicago, Pittsburgh, and New Jersey — as well as universities in Taiwan. The New Jersey chapter (NJ Café Philo) focuses on connecting young immigrant families through discussions of parenting, health, and education.
