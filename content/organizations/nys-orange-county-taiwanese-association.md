# NYS Orange County Taiwanese Association (紐約橙縣台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** NYS Orange County Taiwanese Association
- **Chinese:** 紐約橙縣台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The NYS Orange County Taiwanese Association (紐約橙縣台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in Orange County, New York, and is listed under the TAA’s East Northern (美東北) regional grouping.
