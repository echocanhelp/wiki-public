# Los Angeles Life Forum

**Los Angeles Life Forum** (洛杉磯生活座談會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** 洛杉磯生活座談會
- **English (as listed):** Los Angeles Life Forum
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
- **Source:** https://www.taiwancenter.org/group-members · `taiwancenter-org-group-members.md`

## Role in the Community

- The TAH Foundation story corpus preserves a group profile of the forum: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/shingwa-society-of-los-angeles|洛杉磯生活座談會]] (published 2016-03-30), which also appears alongside the sister record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/southern-shingwa-society|南區生活座談會]] — indicating a southern-California network of 生活座談會 chapters.
- Corpus record of a forum picnic event: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/los-angeles-life-picnic-forum|30. 洛杉磯生活座談會野餐]] (published 2015-11-27), filed among TAHS records that also document 南加州暑假青年工作計劃 and 東南區台灣人夏令會 — the forum's activities sit within the broader SoCal TA community-program circle.
- The 生活座談會 format was a cross-region institution, not a single club: the corpus preserves sibling records for the LA-area [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/south-bay-shingwa-society|南灣生活座談會]], the San Diego chapters [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/es-shingwa-society|聖東生活座談會]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/sd-shingwa-society|聖地牙哥台灣人生活座談會]], further chapter records [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/easy-shingwa-society|輕鬆生活座談會]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/central-shingwa-society|中區生活座談會]], and a coordinating body record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/shingwa-society|生活座談會理事會]].
- The model is described in 黃正源's Taiwan Center of San Diego memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys26|26. 聖地牙哥台灣中心的前半生與轉型]]: monthly 生活座談會 where 同鄉 share life and business experience, hosted at the community center alongside 同鄉會, 網球會, 台灣商會 and TPA events — the LA forum's monthly-meeting character matches this documented pattern. The format also appears in the East Coast record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys292|292. 早年匹茲堡台灣同鄉會簡介]] as a standard 同鄉會 program type.

- A founding-history record exists for the format itself: 劉天良's memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys212|212. 生活座談會簡史 / 劉天良 / 03/2016]] — the 生活座談會 grew out of an earlier 讀書會 when 劉天良 was 同鄉會長 and proposed that members share their professional expertise; the format later evolved into 「台美人論壇」. Per the companion memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys240|240]], the SoCal 生活座談會 circuit cultivated speakers who later contributed in Taiwan (郭清江、謝清志、王秋森、蘇育德、蔡滄波、江昭儀 et al.), and 李木通 credits it with his public-speaking experience — context for the LA forum's role in the community's leadership pipeline.
- A feature report on chapter life: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/44-e3-80-8e-e5-ae-b6-e6-9c-89-e5-96-9c-e4-ba-8b-e3-80-8f-e7-94-9f-e6-b4-bb-e5-ba|44.『家有喜事』-生活座談會報導 / 劉美娥 / 2014-10]] (bibliographic record only).
- The monthly life-talk format persists at 大洛杉磯台灣會館: a pandemic-era online series covering 園藝、地產、稅務、法律、科技、醫療 opened 2021-03 with 林榮松醫師 on 武漢肺炎一年回顧 ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20210415114826_2021_03_01_大洛杉磯台灣會館線上生活座談-武漢肺炎_一年_3990aedba169afdf|線上生活座談-武漢肺炎一年]]); by 2021-10 the 12th session hosted 駐洛經文處林暉程副處長 on 疫情期間僑民領務服務 ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20211020141135_2021_10_12_大洛杉磯台灣會館生活座談會_駐洛經文處林暉程_96088ab998c9f16a|生活座談會-經文處僑民領務服務]]); the series was still running in 2025 with 居家有機園藝入門 at session 九 ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250323065458_大洛杉磯台灣會館生活座談會_九_居家有機園_ea599dafe6e60f6e|生活座談會九 居家有機園藝]]) and 手機攝影入門 at session 10 ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250916155805_大洛杉磯台灣會館生活座談會_10_手機攝影入門_efb12460bf98e244|生活座談會10 手機攝影入門]]), contact tcgla@taiwancenterla.org.
