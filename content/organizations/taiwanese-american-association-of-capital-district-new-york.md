# Taiwanese American Association Of Capital District, New York (紐約州首府區台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Association Of Capital District, New York
- **Chinese:** 紐約州首府區台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Association of Capital District, New York (TAA-CDNY; 紐約州首府區台灣同鄉會) is a chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA), the national umbrella organization of Taiwanese American community associations. The chapter serves the Capital District region of New York State, including Albany, Troy, Rensselaer, Schenectady, Colonie, Latham, Malta, Clifton Park, and Saratoga Springs.
