# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Ohio/Kentucky Chapter (北美洲台灣婦女會俄亥俄)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Ohio/Kentucky Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣婦女會俄亥俄
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Ohio/Kentucky Chapter is a local chapter of the North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA), serving the Ohio and Kentucky area.

## Role in the Community

The corpus preserves concrete chapter activity: NATWA Ohio's community-service project making hats and neck pillows is documented in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/7-hat-neck-pillow-by-natwa-ohio|7. 社區的服務 製作帽子與Neck Pillow by NATWA Ohio]].
