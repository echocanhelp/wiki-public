# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Ohio/Kentucky Chapter (北美洲台灣婦女會俄亥俄)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Ohio/Kentucky Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣婦女會俄亥俄
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Ohio/Kentucky Chapter is a local chapter of the North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA), serving the Ohio and Kentucky area.

## Role in the Community

The corpus preserves concrete chapter activity: NATWA Ohio's community-service project making hats and neck pillows is documented in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/7-hat-neck-pillow-by-natwa-ohio|7. 社區的服務 製作帽子與Neck Pillow by NATWA Ohio]]. Ohio-area Taiwanese community organizing also surfaced around the Akron concert of Hsiao Tai-jen's D-major violin concerto (see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys269|ourjourneys 269]]; year not stated in the memoir, post-2002 by internal reference), where hundreds of同鄉 gathered at E. J. Thomas Performing Arts Hall. In Kentucky, adjacent movement organizing is recorded for FAPA's Kentucky chapter ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/25-kentucky-chapter-fapa|25. Kentucky Chapter / FAPA 台灣人公共事務會肯塔基州分會]]).

Parent-organization infrastructure the chapter operated within: NATWA's central 聯誼通訊 newsletter is preserved across decades in the corpus — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/663-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|663. 北美洲台灣婦女會聯誼通訊 第七期 (1992/06)]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/679-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|679. 聯誼通訊 第二十三期 (2000/08)]] — and its care-information network is recorded in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/columns17|17. 關懷網 by NATWA北美洲台灣婦女會關懷資訊中心 (2016)]]. Corpus re-grep 2026-09-21 (slice 09201500-27): no Ohio- or Kentucky-specific passage in the newsletters themselves; chapter-level facts remain the hat/neck-pillow service record and the Akron concert gathering above.

Other NATWA chapters with their own records: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-san-diego-chapter|San Diego]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-oregonsouth-west-chapter|Oregon South-West]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-st-louis-chapter|St. Louis]]; see also [[organizations/natwa-southern-california|NATWA Southern California]].
