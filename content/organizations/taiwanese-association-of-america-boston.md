# Taiwanese Association of America Boston (波士頓台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of America Boston
- **Chinese:** 波士頓台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America Boston (波士頓台灣同鄉會) is the Boston-area chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization serving Taiwanese American communities across the United States. The chapter serves the greater Boston, Massachusetts region.
