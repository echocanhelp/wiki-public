# Houston Taiwan Institute for Senior Citizens (休士頓台灣松年學院)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Houston Taiwan Institute for Senior Citizens
- **Chinese:** 休士頓台灣松年學院
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Houston Taiwan Institute for Senior Citizens is a nonprofit educational institution registered with the State of Texas, offering lifelong learning opportunities for older adults. The institute provides a welcoming, multilingual (Mandarin, Taiwanese, English) learning environment with courses in history, literature, art, health and wellness, athletics, life philosophy, science, leisure, and language. Operating on a two-semester academic year with classes held Monday through Thursday, the institute uses an elective course system covering intellectual, interest-based, and practical categories. The institute welcomes seniors of all ethnicities who share its mission, and offers scholarship incentives to encourage enrollment and participation. Donations to the organization are tax-deductible.
