# Taiwanese American Historical Society (TAHS)

The **Taiwanese American Historical Society (TAHS)** (台美人歷史協會) is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization dedicated to documenting, preserving, and promoting Taiwanese American history, culture, and heritage.

## Identity Snapshot
- **Type:** Historical and cultural organization, 501(c)(3) non-profit
- **Geography:** Southern California (Los Angeles area)
- **Founded:** December 11, 2013 (founding conference at Hilton Hotel, San Gabriel)
- **First President:** 楊嘉猷 (Charles Yang) — founding president
- **Second President:** Franklin Ping Cheng (2014–2017)
- **Mission:** Historical preservation, cultural education, community building

## Mission and Activities
TAHS works to:
1. Document and preserve Taiwanese American historical records
2. Build community connections across generations
3. Support research and education about Taiwanese American experiences
4. Maintain digital archives and knowledge bases (Echopedia)

### Founding Narrative (from 緣起與展望 essay)
- **June 2013:** Dr. 鄭炳全 (Pacific Times) began convening community leaders, notably 楊嘉猷
- **August 28, 2013:** First preparatory meeting in Fullerton, CA
- **December 11, 2013:** Founding conference at Hilton Hotel, San Gabriel, with press conference
- **Key founders:** 鄭炳全 (first convener), 楊嘉猷 (founding president), 周威霖 (secretary), 王耀廷 (vice president)
- **Initial preparatory members:** 鄭炳全, 楊嘉猷, 周威霖, 王耀廷, 黃樹人, 黃根深, 林衡哲, 謝清志, 王廷宜, 李成奎, 鄭英松, 洪珠美, 楊子清, 吳光明, 周實, 陳隆, 曾俊嘉, 楊平猷, 楊文顯, 魏博基, 林小慧
- **Motivation:** First-generation Taiwanese Americans who participated in Taiwan's democratization movement were aging; their stories needed to be recorded before they were lost

### Major Activities (2013–2017)
1. **Exhibitions:** 228 Historical Materials Exhibition, pre-WWII Formosan students in the US, Taiwan puppet theater exhibition
2. **Oral history interviews:** Recorded first-generation members, produced video archives
3. **Book launches:** Supported publications by Taiwanese American authors including Shawna Yang Ryan
4. **Community events:** Intergenerational gatherings, lectures, 228 memorial services
5. **Digital archives:** USC East Asian Library Taiwanese American historical materials digitization project
6. **Political engagement:** Supported pro-Taiwan grassroots diplomacy (e.g., TPP lobbying trip to Washington DC, 2013)

### Publications
- **2017 TAHS Publication (台美菁英錄):** Yearbook profiling first- and second-generation Taiwanese Americans (pp. 1–118)
- **Donor lists:** 2015, 2016 annual donor recognition
- **Activity summaries:** Annual society activity reports

### Future Plans (as stated by founding president)
1. Improve website and operations
2. Collect and preserve individual, group, and ethnic historical materials
3. Join organizations such as Immigration and Ethnic History Society, AASLH, AANI
4. Increase participation and identification among younger generation Taiwanese Americans
