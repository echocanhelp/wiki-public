# Hakka Foundation (客家基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Hakka Foundation
- **Chinese:** 客家基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Hakka Foundation is a Taiwanese American nonprofit organization serving the Hakka community. The organization promotes Hakka language, culture, and heritage while fostering community integration, civic participation, and mutual support among Hakka people in the United States. Its mission spans education, cultural preservation, senior services, social welfare, civil rights advocacy, and charitable activities.
