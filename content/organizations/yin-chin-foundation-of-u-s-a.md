# YIN CHIN FOUNDATION OF U.S.A. (美國殷勤文教公益基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** YIN CHIN FOUNDATION OF U.S.A.
- **Chinese:** 美國殷勤文教公益基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Yin Chin Foundation of U.S.A. is a nonprofit charitable organization registered in the United States and recognized by the federal government as a 501(c)(3) entity. It was founded by Mr. C. L. Yin (殷清隆) and is chaired by Ms. Min-Kuan Yin (殷敏寬). The foundation focuses on promoting public welfare and giving back to society by consolidating and sharing charitable resources across communities. Through fostering mutual aid among allied organizations and rigorous grant review processes, it supports charitable relief, cultural education, environmental protection, and public health initiatives worldwide.
