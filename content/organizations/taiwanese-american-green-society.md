# Taiwanese American Green Society (台美人生活綠化促進會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Green Society
- **Chinese:** 台美人生活綠化促進會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Green Society is a nonprofit organization founded by Taiwanese American professionals in Southern California, including academics, engineers, lawyers, and business people. The organization was established after approximately one year of planning, with the goal of promoting environmental awareness, energy conservation, carbon reduction, and sustainable living within the Taiwanese American community and broader society. The founding president is Lin Yong-yuan, a former president of the Los Angeles Taiwanese American Chamber of Commerce. The organization emphasizes that change begins at the individual lifestyle level, advocating green energy, renewable resources, and reduced overconsumption.

- 「從台美人作起」 — per the archived TAH story corpus record (`knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/taiwanese-american-green-society.md`, source: https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/taiwanese-american-green-society/), the society was formed after 一年 (one year) of preparation as a 非營利綠化協會 (nonprofit greening association) by Taiwanese American 學術、工程、法律與企業人士 (academia, engineering, legal, and business professionals), aiming to raise awareness of 節能、減碳、抑制過度消費 (energy saving, carbon reduction, curbing overconsumption).
- 創會副會長吳和甫 — per the same archived record, founding vice president Dr. 吳和甫 (Hofu Wu), 波莫那工藝大學建築系教授 (professor of architecture at Cal Poly Pomona), said at the founding event that although governments actively promote green building, everyday lifestyle energy saving is truly important, and he presented 「綠色建築的最新發展」 (the latest developments in green building) to the audience; see [[people/hofu-wu|Dr. Hofu Wu 吳和甫]].
- Prominent founding board — per the same archived record, the society's 理事 (board directors) included 前台大公共衛生學院院長王秋森 ([[people/chiu-sen-wang|Chiu-Sen Wang]], former dean of the NTU College of Public Health), 台灣會館董事長徐新宏 ([[people/hsu-hsin-hung|Hsu Hsin-hung]], chairman of the Taiwanese Community Center), 前台灣人獅子會會長余忠村, 波莫那工藝大學電機系教授林洪權, Sun Green System 總經理徐利亨, and 前台美律師協會會長張天駿.
- Founding-president statement — per the same archived record, 創會會長林永源 stressed that green energy and environmental living are inseparable: beyond cutting-edge technology and efficient energy use, renewable energy is essential, and changing daily lifestyle will greatly affect energy conservation; his Chamber presidency is corroborated by the 2007 LA Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce yearbook (會長的話 bylined 林永源, `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/`, posted 2015-07).
