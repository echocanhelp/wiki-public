# Taiwanese American Green Society (台美人生活綠化促進會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Green Society
- **Chinese:** 台美人生活綠化促進會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Green Society is a nonprofit organization founded by Taiwanese American professionals in Southern California, including academics, engineers, lawyers, and business people. The organization was established after approximately one year of planning, with the goal of promoting environmental awareness, energy conservation, carbon reduction, and sustainable living within the Taiwanese American community and broader society. The founding president is Lin Yong-yuan, a former president of the Los Angeles Taiwanese American Chamber of Commerce. The organization emphasizes that change begins at the individual lifestyle level, advocating green energy, renewable resources, and reduced overconsumption.
