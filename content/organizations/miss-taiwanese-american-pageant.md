# Miss Taiwanese American Pageant Foundation

**Miss Taiwanese American Pageant Foundation** (台灣小姐選拔基金會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** 台灣小姐選拔基金會
- **English (as listed):** Miss Taiwanese American Pageant Foundation
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
- **Source:** https://www.taiwancenter.org/group-members · `taiwancenter-org-group-members.md`

## Role in the Community (corpus record)

The pageant has been a recurring Taiwan Center community event since at least 2010, documented in the TAH story corpus:

- 2010 台美親善小姐 pageant at 大洛杉磯台灣會館 (magazine coverage): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/744-2010-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e8-a6-aa-e5-96-84-e5-b0-8f-e5-a7-90-miss-taiwanese-am||744. 2010 台美親善小姐 Pageant]]
- 2012 edition: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/745-2012-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e8-a6-aa-e5-96-84-e5-b0-8f-e5-a7-90-miss-taiwanese-am||745. 2012 台美親善小姐 Pageant]]
- 2016-08-07 pageant hosted by the Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/7-08072016-miss-taiwanese-american-pageant||7. Miss Taiwanese American Pageant 2016]]
- 2018-08-04 pageant in San Gabriel, sponsored by Taiwan Center of Greater LA: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/19-miss-taiwanese-american-2018-sponsored-by-taiwan-center-greater-los-angeles-i||19. Miss Taiwanese American 2018]]
