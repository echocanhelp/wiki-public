# Modern Taiwanese Language Association (台語現代文協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Modern Taiwanese Language Association
- **Chinese:** 台語現代文協會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Modern Taiwanese Language Association (MTLA) was founded in 2003 by teachers, scholars, doctors, and engineers to promote the study and teaching of Taiwanese language and literature. MTLA collaborates with the Washington D.C. Taiwanese School and the Taiwanese Youth Arts Foundation in its educational outreach. The association maintains a website dedicated to Modern Taiwanese Language (MTL), a romanized writing system for Hokkien/Taiwanese. The site serves as a self-learning resource, a community space for practitioners to exchange experiences and publish works, and a news hub for MTL-related developments.
