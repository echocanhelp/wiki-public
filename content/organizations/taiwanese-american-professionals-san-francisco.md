# Taiwanese American Professionals San Francisco

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Professionals San Francisco (TAP-SF, 舊金山台美菁英協會)
- **Chinese:** 台美菁英協會 (chapter name from corpus record #24)
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Taiwanese American Professionals San Francisco (TAP-SF) is a chapter of the Taiwanese American Citizens League (TACL), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit run entirely by volunteers. One of the largest TAP chapters, TAP-SF includes over 2,500 young professionals in the Bay Area. The chapter organizes programming centered on professional development, cultural engagement, and community service. TAP-SF also produces the Taiwanese American Cultural Festival (TACF), the West Coast’s largest Taiwanese American cultural celebration, which has been held annually since 1993 and draws over 10,000 visitors each year to San Francisco.
