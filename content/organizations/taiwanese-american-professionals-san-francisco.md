# Taiwanese American Professionals San Francisco

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Professionals San Francisco (TAP-SF, 舊金山台美菁英協會)
- **Chinese:** 台美菁英協會 (chapter name from corpus record #24)
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Taiwanese American Professionals San Francisco (TAP-SF) is a chapter of the Taiwanese American Citizens League (TACL), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit run entirely by volunteers. One of the largest TAP chapters, TAP-SF includes over 2,500 young professionals in the Bay Area. The chapter organizes programming centered on professional development, cultural engagement, and community service. TAP-SF also produces the Taiwanese American Cultural Festival (TACF), the West Coast’s largest Taiwanese American cultural celebration, which has been held annually since 1993 and draws over 10,000 visitors each year to San Francisco.

## Role in the Community

Corpus records place TAP-SF inside the documented TAP (台美菁英協會) chapter network of the Taiwanese American movement:

- The parent TAP network is itself recorded in the corpus as 「1. Taiwanese American Professionals (TAP) 台美菁英協會」, 2014-10-12 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-professionals-tap|TAH TAP overview]]; bibliographic record only).
- 2015-07-24 — the chapter's own community-investment record 「24. 舊金山台美菁英協會獎學金 TAP-SF Scholarship Award」 documents its scholarship program for the next generation ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/24-taiwanese-american-professionals-san-francisco-chapter-tap-sf-scholarship|TAH #24]]; bibliographic record only).
- The chapter's own directory entry is held as a corpus work: 「10. San Francisco Chapter / TAP 台美菁英協會舊金山分會」, 2014-10-12 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/san-francisco-chapter-tap|TAH #10, SF chapter]]; bibliographic record only).
- Sibling chapters appear in the same corpus — its own chapter record sits alongside dedicated records for Boston ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/boston-chapter-tap|TAH #4]]), D.C. ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/d-c-chapter-tap|TAH #5]]), Seattle ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/seattle-chapter-tap|Seattle chapter]]), Orange County ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/orange-county-chapter-tap|OC chapter]]; [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/activities-of-orange-county-chapter-tap|OC TAP activities]]), Los Angeles ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/los-angeles-chapter-tap|LA chapter]]), New York ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/new-york-chapter-tap|NY chapter]]), Philadelphia ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/philadelphia-chapter-tap|Philadelphia chapter]]), San Diego ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/san-diego-chapter-tap|San Diego chapter]]), Austin ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/austin-chapter-tap|Austin chapter]]) and Atlanta ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/atlanta-chapter-tap|Atlanta chapter]]) — situating TAP-SF as one node of a national professional-chapter movement, with the national coordination documented in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tacl-tap-national-quarterly-newsletter|TACL/TAP national quarterly newsletter]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-professionals-tap|the TAP overview]].
