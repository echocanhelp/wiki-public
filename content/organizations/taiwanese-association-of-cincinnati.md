# Taiwanese Association of Cincinnati (辛辛那提)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of Cincinnati
- **Chinese:** 辛辛那提
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of Cincinnati (辛辛那提 台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations across the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in Cincinnati, Ohio.
