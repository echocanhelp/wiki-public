# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – New Jersey Chapter (北美洲台灣婦女會紐澤西分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – New Jersey Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣婦女會紐澤西分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – New Jersey Chapter is a local chapter of the North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA), serving the New Jersey area.

## Role in the Community

Corpus records document the chapter's activity within the NATWA network:

- Dedicated chapter record in the TAH encyclopedia: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-new-jersey-chapter|TAH #9 New Jersey Chapter / NATWA 北美洲台灣婦女會紐澤西分會 (2015/12)]].
- Co-hosted a 讀書研討會 (reading/study seminar) with the New York branch of the Taiwan PEN Writers Association, recorded 2018/05: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/study-group-nj|讀書研討會 by 紐約區台灣人筆會、北美洲台灣婦女會紐澤西分會]].
