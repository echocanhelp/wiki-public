# Taiwanese Overseas Student Association at the University of Washington

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Overseas Student Association at the University of Washington
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Overseas Student Association at the University of Washington (TOSAUW, commonly referred to as TOSA UW) is a nonprofit, student-run registered student organization at the University of Washington. According to the organization’s own website, its primary goal is to promote Taiwanese culture and to serve both undergraduate and graduate students at UW.

## Role in the Community

- **2012-05-19** — TOSA co-participated with TSA (University of Washington) at a night market in Seattle, WA: a community recruiting/outreach event recorded in the TaiwaneseAmerican.org corpus — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/join-tsa-and-tosa-at-a-night-market-in-seattle-wa|Join TSA and TOSA at a Night Market in Seattle, WA]].
- **2014-10-12** — TAH Foundation recorded the organization under its Chinese name 華大海外同學會 in the community historical record — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tosa-taiwnanese-overseas-students-association|Taiwanese Overseas Students Association (TOSA) 華大海外同學會]].
