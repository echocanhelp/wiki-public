# Taiwanese Overseas Student Association at the University of Washington

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Overseas Student Association at the University of Washington
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Overseas Student Association at the University of Washington (TOSAUW, commonly referred to as TOSA UW) is a nonprofit, student-run registered student organization at the University of Washington. According to the organization’s own website, its primary goal is to promote Taiwanese culture and to serve both undergraduate and graduate students at UW.
