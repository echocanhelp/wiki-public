# Yes Plaza (夜市廣場)

## Identity Snapshot
- **Era:** 2008–2020s (countdown events documented 2008–2014; "seventh year" stated for Dec 31, 2012 → first countdown ~2006)
- **Geography:** 1715–1747 Fullerton Rd. / Colima Rd., Rowland Heights, CA — the intersection at the heart of the east San Gabriel Valley Taiwanese district
- **Core roles:** community venue (taiwanese American) — commercial plaza whose night-market format and New Year countdowns became an institution

## Record

- **Yes Plaza 夜市廣場 New Year's Eve Countdown**: annual countdown concert + night market run by [[organizations/stc-management|STC Management]]; 5th annual Dec 31, 2011 ("**an iconic tradition for Chinese Americans in Southern California**" — STC's own words, thousands attending); 8th annual Dec 31, 2013 featured **電音三太子** (electronic-tridance) presented by **STC顺天联合基金会 (STC Foundation)** and free shuttles from Seasons Place. Blog record goes back to Dec 2008.
- Format: Taiwanese night-market food, live concert, karaoke, raffles — explicitly modeled on **Taiwan night markets**; Lunar New Year lantern festivals at STC's Seasons Place drew "crowds of thousands" (2013).
- **Legacy:** the countdown institution migrated to **STC Rowland Legacy** (18991 Colima Rd., acquired Nov 2025 — see [[organizations/stc-management|STC Management]]), where the **OCLA STC Night Market** runs weekends from July 31, 2026, and **"Taste of Taiwan 2026"** (Oct 2–11) brought 15 Taiwan night-market vendors to Rowland Heights for Double Ten — founder [[people/john-hsu|John Hsu (許惠欽)]], raised in Taiwan, "移民美國43年," quoted: "這個夢想終於實現了" (this dream finally came true). Co-organizers: AI教育基金會, 台美餐飲產業經貿發展協會 (TUFBA).
- Rowland Heights itself: the east-SGV "Little Taipei" — dominant immigrant group from Taiwan from the late 1980s (LA Times 2007: "the dominant culture here is from Taiwan"; LA Times 1992 already documents the Colima Rd. Taiwanese commercial strip).

## Historical Significance

The Yes Plaza countdown is a case study in how a **property manager became a community institution-builder**: STC didn't just manage a plaza, it invented a civic ritual (New Year's Eve, thousands strong) that made Rowland Heights' Taiwanese identity publicly visible — night-market food, 電音三太子, and all — and the same operator is now staging a literal Taiwan night market (Taste of Taiwan 2026, ROC-115 Double Ten framing, balloons for "台灣及台美友誼") on a 13-acre site it re-imagined as a night-market venue. Business infrastructure as nation-building infrastructure, in the SGV style.
