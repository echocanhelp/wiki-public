# Taiwanese Student Association for Public Affairs (台灣公共事務學生會華大分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Student Association for Public Affairs
- **Chinese:** 台灣公共事務學生會華大分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

TSAPA is the University of Washington chapter of the Taiwanese Student Association for Public Affairs, a student organization dedicated to advocating for Taiwan’s international recognition and amplifying Taiwanese voices abroad. The chapter was founded in response to China’s obstruction of international aid to Taiwan during the 2003 SARS outbreak, which galvanized the founding president to establish a student organization that could speak up for Taiwan on the world stage. TSAPA aims to counter stereotyped or politically distorted perceptions of Taiwan — whether originating from Beijing or from KMT-era propaganda — by providing international audiences with a genuine understanding of contemporary Taiwanese identity and aspirations. The organization hosts events such as the ‘228 Hand-in-Hand Protect Taiwan’ commemoration and social outings, and serves as a civic and cultural platform for Taiwanese students at UW.
