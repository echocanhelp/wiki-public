# Rowland Heights Chinese Association (羅蘭崗華人協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **Era:** founded ~1990 (20th anniversary Sept 19, 2010; 22nd gala May 19, 2012) — born with the east-SGV Chinese/Taiwanese community of the late 1980s
- **Geography:** Rowland Heights / Hacienda Heights / City of Industry, Los Angeles County
- **Core roles:** community association (chinese american) — civic, school-funding, safety advocacy

## Record

- **Founding generation of the "east district":** the east-SGV Chinese community (Diamond Bar, Walnut, Hacienda Heights, Rowland Heights) formed in the late 1980s, with Taiwanese immigrants as the dominant cohort (LA Times 2007: "the dominant culture here is from Taiwan"); RHCA is its contemporaneous association, founded ~1990.
- **Annual Installation & Fundraiser Gala**: proceeds fund community programs — 2012 (22nd) gala at STC Center directed part of proceeds to the **FORUS Foundation** (Friends of Rowland Unified Schools); performances by Rowland High Show Choir "Infusion," Teacher Ma Dance, Shifu Gao. 2010 20th-anniversary event displayed "a 20 year history of community involvement."
- **Under president John Hsu (elected ~2011–2012, STC CEO):** resolved the decades-long Alvarado St. / Desire Ave. parent-dropoff parking dilemma by working with the LA County Sheriff (No Parking signs installed 2012-05-02, alongside Rowland Unified board VP **Cary Chen**); his stated goal: the association as "a role model … a harmonious community with prosperity … best quality of education, business environment."
- **Joint community events:** Chinese New Year party with the Rowland Senior Club (Feb 2011, STC Center — contacts SueAnn Tai, Charles Liu); Buckboard Days Parade participation ("Rowland Heights, the best community for kids," 2011).

## Historical Significance

The association layer of **Little Taipei's formation**: created by the same first wave that built the Colima Rd. commercial strip, and its gala-school-funding model shows the community self-provisioning public goods (education supplements, safety advocacy) in an **unincorporated area with no city government** to do it. Its 2011–12 presidency by the STC CEO is the seam where the business network (this archive's [[organizations/yes-plaza|Yes Plaza]] thread) and the civic network are literally the same person.
