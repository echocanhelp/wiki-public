# Taiwanese American Student Association at Georgia Tech

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Student Association at Georgia Tech
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Student Association at Georgia Tech (GT TASA) is a registered student organization at the Georgia Institute of Technology in Atlanta, Georgia. It is listed in both the Georgia Tech OMED Student Resource Hub and the Georgia Tech SGA’s JacketPages organization directory, confirming its standing as a recognized campus organization. The group maintains a Facebook page at facebook.com/gttasa and an Instagram account at @gt_tasa.

## Role in the Community
The Georgia Tech campus appears in the Taiwanese American movement record as a venue for community events covered by TAHS:
