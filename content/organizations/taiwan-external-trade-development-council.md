## Role in the Community

Outside its Taiwan Center group-membership listing, the corpus records the council (commonly 外貿協會/貿協, TAITRA) as an actor in Taiwanese American community exchange programs:

- Per the Indianapolis chapter memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-389|Our Journeys 389]], from 2004 to 2007 the council selected and sent Taiwanese eMBA (企業管理碩士班) students — 6 in 2004, 12 in 2005, 16 in 2006, 20 in 2007 — for internships at companies in Indianapolis. The program was locally coordinated by Prof. Shao-Ching Liu 劉少青 of Butler University, with students hosted by Taiwanese American host families to learn American business customs; it ended when Prof. Liu moved to Quinnipiac University.
