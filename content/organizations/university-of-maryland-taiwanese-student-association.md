# University of Maryland Taiwanese Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** University of Maryland Taiwanese Student Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The University of Maryland Taiwanese Student Association (TSA) is a student organization at the University of Maryland, College Park. It is listed on TerpLink, UMD’s official club directory, under the name “Taiwanese Student Organization” at the handle umdtsa, and maintains an Instagram account at @umd_tsa and a Facebook group. A founding year and formal mission statement could not be confirmed from available first-party sources.
