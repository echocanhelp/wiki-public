# Taiwanese American Student Association at Cornell

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Student Association at Cornell
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Student Association at Cornell (TASA) is a registered student organization at Cornell University in Ithaca, New York. It is listed in Cornell’s official CampusGroups directory and recognized under the Cornell Asian Pacific Islander Student Union (CAPSU) umbrella, which coordinates Asian and Asian American student organizations on campus.

## Role in the Community
- Predecessor record: a community memoir of the Ithaca Taiwanese Association describes Taiwanese students at Cornell gathering from the mid-1960s (Joseph H. Chen arrived for doctoral study in 1965), formally renaming to the "Cornell University Taiwanese Student's Association" during the 1970s and reaching nearly 300 members including families at its height [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys157|Our Journeys 157]]. HOLD: continuity between that association and today's registered TASA is asserted by locality but not documented in the corpus.
- The memoir's English edition corroborates the above and adds texture: the founding circle included earlier Taiwanese doctoral students (Chao-hsiung Yeh, later NTU civil-engineering professor; Shu-ho Wang; Yang-yang Wu), the group's function was homesickness relief, Taiwanese food, and student information plus welcoming new arrivals and sending off graduates; the Taiwanese association and the "Chinese student's association" co-existed for a period before merging under the Taiwanese name after Mainland China opened up and Chinese students auto-joined the CSA; in the 60s–70s the association communicated in Taiwanese, later shifting to mixed Mandarin as younger members grew up more fluent — some members said they joined specifically to learn Taiwanese [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys310-eng|Our Journeys 310 (EN)]].
- Cornell also served as a national-movement venue: THA-USA's 3rd-term president Yung-pin Liu is recorded as based in "Cornell, NY" in the organization's own presidents' timeline [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys110-eng|110 (EN). Introduction to the Taiwanese Hakka Organizations of America]].
- Cornell also figures in the wider movement record: 李登輝 returned to his alma mater on 1995-06-12 as president and gave the landmark 「民之所欲，長在我心」 speech (recalled in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-375|Our Journeys 375]], alongside his 1983 Albany episode), and the 羅徹斯特臺灣同鄉會愛鄕合唱團 was invited to perform at Cornell [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/15-e7-be-85-e5-be-b9-e6-96-af-e7-89-b9-e8-87-ba-e7-81-a3-e5-90-8c-e9-84-95-e6-9c|15. Taiwanese Choral Society of Rochester (2014-12)]] — Cornell as a node in the Taiwanese American community circuit.
- Institutional lineage (deepen-x slice 09220900-8, 2026-09-22): the Ithaca chapter was one of the 28 founding chapters of 全美台灣同鄉會 at its 1970 formation, with 蔡武雄 as its founding chapter president — the earliest documented organizational ancestor of today's Cornell TASA, predating the memoir-only account above ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76|Our Journeys 76: 全美會分會紀錄]]). Related Ithaca/Cornell community records now linked: the 美東台灣人夏令會 concert by TAC/EC at Ithaca NY 1995-07-02 「台灣人音樂—蕭泰然作品演奏會」 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts14|Concerts 14]]) and the 2005 王康陸博士紀念音樂會 also held at Ithaca ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts23|Concerts 23]]) — Cornell/Ithaca as a recurring venue in the eastern Taiwanese American community circuit. The Chinese edition of the association memoir exists at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys310|310. 伊薩卡臺灣同鄉會的簡介 / 程美希]] alongside the English edition already cited.
- Cornell also appears as a waypoint in individual movement biographies: 黃介山 (樹林人,长老教会信徒) is recalled in a Bay Area Taiwanese church history as having first gone to the US for study at 康乃爾大學 (Cornell) before transferring to Stanford, later gathering in Bay Area Taiwanese Presbyterian circles ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-350|350. 美國舊金山灣區台灣基督教會史料簡介 / 何義麟 2019-11]]). Peripheral to TASA itself — Cornell as study destination in the sojourner generation, noted for completeness.
- Disambiguation: in the corpus "TASA" also denotes the Intercollegiate Taiwanese American Student Association network ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/history-of-itasa|46. History of ITASA]]) and the Taiwanese American Seniors Association of Southern California — distinct organizations.
