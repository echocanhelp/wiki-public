# Taiwanese American Student Association at Cornell

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Student Association at Cornell
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Student Association at Cornell (TASA) is a registered student organization at Cornell University in Ithaca, New York. It is listed in Cornell’s official CampusGroups directory and recognized under the Cornell Asian Pacific Islander Student Union (CAPSU) umbrella, which coordinates Asian and Asian American student organizations on campus.

## Role in the Community
- Predecessor record: a community memoir of the Ithaca Taiwanese Association describes Taiwanese students at Cornell gathering from the mid-1960s (Joseph H. Chen arrived for doctoral study in 1965), formally renaming to the "Cornell University Taiwanese Student's Association" during the 1970s and reaching nearly 300 members including families at its height [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys157|Our Journeys 157]]. HOLD: continuity between that association and today's registered TASA is asserted by locality but not documented in the corpus.
- Disambiguation: in the corpus "TASA" also denotes the Intercollegiate Taiwanese American Student Association network ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/history-of-itasa|46. History of ITASA]]) and the Taiwanese American Seniors Association of Southern California — distinct organizations.
