# Senior Taiwanese Association of Northern California (北加州台灣長輩會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Senior Taiwanese Association of Northern California
- **Chinese:** 北加州台灣長輩會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Senior Taiwanese Association of Northern California is a nonprofit service organization founded in 1981 to serve elderly Taiwanese Americans in the greater Northern California region, including Sacramento, San Francisco, Tracy, Concord, Livermore, and Danville. Membership has grown from a handful of founding members to over 500, making it the largest senior Taiwanese organization in the Bay Area with the most active event calendar. The association provides physical, mental, and spiritual wellness activities for retired seniors, including tai chi, karaoke, hiking, and day trips, as well as major annual events for Mother’s Day, Father’s Day, Double Ninth Festival, and an annual general meeting. It also participates in broader community events such as the 228 Memorial Concert, cultural festivals, National Day celebrations, and the San Jose Veterans Day Parade. The organization publishes a monthly newsletter and an annual directory, and produces health and wellness publications to promote member wellbeing.

## Role in the Community

Absorbed 2026-09-14 from the movement record (our memoirs, not press-kit):

- The Bay Area senior movement grew out of the Taiwanese church's community-service work: 陳隆, hired late 1976 as community-service officer of the 灣區台灣人社區服務中心 (church-affiliated), helped elders from Taiwan settle and organized their first long-distance trip to Southern California with ~20 elders in April 1978, sealing a sister-association tie with the 南加州台灣長輩会 (absorbed from [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-350||350. 美國舊金山灣區台灣基督教會史料簡介 / 何義麟 / 11/2019]]).
- **HOLD: founding date conflict** — the TAH directory states founded **1981**, but our own memoirs give earlier, more specific milestones: 何義麟 records 陳隆 assisting elders to form 台灣長輩會 on **1978-03-11**, while the association's own history by 莊麗華 says the organization 孕育成形 in **1979** as an 老人會 founded at 台灣基督教會 with **陳錦川 first president** and **彭笑生 general secretary**, renamed **北加州台灣長輩會 on 1981-08-08** (president 許基隆, third) and registered with California **1981-10-29**. Not auto-merged: 1978/1979 likely reflect the informal 老人會 stage; 1981 is the incorporation.
- The 南加州長輩會 it sistered with was founded by 陳隆's father 陳夢蘭 — the Northern California association was, in effect, a transplanted replica of the LA founding family's organization (per 何義麟).
- Later institutional record per 莊麗華's own history ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys299||299. 北加州台灣長輩會簡介 / 莊麗華 / 06/2017]]): annual 長輩的生活智慧 publication series (eight volumes by 2010, e.g. [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/334-e9-95-b7-e8-bc-a9-e7-9a-84-e7-94-9f-e6-b4-bb-e6-99-ba-e6-85-a7-1995-e5-8c-97|長輩的生活智慧 1995]]), 30th-anniversary special issue [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/800-e5-8c-97-e5-8a-a0-e5-b7-9e-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e9-95-b7-e8-bc-a9-e6-9c-83-e4-b|800. 北加州台灣長輩會三十週年紀念特刊 (2012)]], own website from 2010, activities centered at the 南灣文教中心 Milpitas site from 2015.
- **陳文雄** served as 名譽會長 from 1988 for nearly thirty years, described as the association's perennial pillar and chief patron (per 莊麗華).
