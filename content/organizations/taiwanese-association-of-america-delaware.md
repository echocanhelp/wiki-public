# Taiwanese Association of America Delaware (德拉瓦州)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of America Delaware
- **Chinese:** 德拉瓦州
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America Delaware (德拉瓦州 台灣同鄉會) is the Delaware chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA), the national umbrella organization serving Taiwanese American communities across the United States. As a TAA chapter, it serves the Taiwanese American community in Delaware and operates under the auspices of the national organization.
