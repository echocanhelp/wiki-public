# Taiwanese American of Dayton (戴頓台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American of Dayton
- **Chinese:** 戴頓台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Taiwanese American of Dayton (戴頓台灣同鄉會) is a local chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the Dayton, Ohio area.
