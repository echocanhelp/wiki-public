# Global HAKKA UN NGO Applying Committee UN NGO (世界客家社團加入)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Global HAKKA UN NGO Applying Committee UN NGO
- **Chinese:** 世界客家社團加入
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Global Hakka UN NGO Applying Committee (協進會) was established on August 16, 2014, during the Global Taiwanese Hakka Kinship Assembly held in Paris by the France Taiwan Hakka Association. Its primary purpose is to encourage and coordinate Hakka community organizations worldwide in applying for UN NGO consultative status, in order to promote the sustainable development of Hakka culture and counter the suppression of Taiwan’s international participation by the People’s Republic of China. The committee provides a platform for member organizations to share application experiences, exchange information, and coordinate strategies for engaging in United Nations NGO activities. Leadership includes a Chairperson from the World Taiwanese Hakka Federation, vice-chairs representing Hakka federations in the Americas, Europe, Asia, and Africa, and an Executive Director responsible for administrative affairs and UN NGO application support. The committee convenes annual meetings in conjunction with the World Hakka Kinship Assembly.
