# Taiwanese American Council (紐約紐澤西台灣人社團聯合會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Council
- **Chinese:** 紐約紐澤西台灣人社團聯合會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Council (紐約紐澤西台灣人社團聯合會) was founded in 1982 by Dr. Sheng-nan Hsu (許盛男), who served as its first president. The organization was established to coordinate activities among Taiwanese community organizations in the greater New York and New Jersey area, strengthening ties and mutual support between member groups. Founding member organizations included the Greater New York Taiwanese Association, the New Jersey Taiwanese Association, the Professor Chen Wen-chen Memorial Foundation, the New York chapter of the Taiwanese Human Rights Association, the New York chapter of the Formosan Association for Public Affairs (FAPA), and the Taiwan Studies Association.
