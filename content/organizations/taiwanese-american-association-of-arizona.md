# Taiwanese American Association of Arizona (亞歷桑那州)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Association of Arizona
- **Chinese:** 亞歷桑那州
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Association of Arizona (TAAA) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (全美台灣同鄉會), serving the Taiwanese American community across the state of Arizona. As a TAA chapter, it operates within the national umbrella organization’s network of hometown associations dedicated to community connection, cultural preservation, and civic engagement among Taiwanese Americans in the United States.
