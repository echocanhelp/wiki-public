# Taiwanese American Association of Arizona (亞歷桑那州)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Association of Arizona
- **Chinese:** 亞歷桑那州
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Association of Arizona (TAAA) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (全美台灣同鄉會), serving the Taiwanese American community across the state of Arizona. As a TAA chapter, it operates within the national umbrella organization's network of hometown associations dedicated to community connection, cultural preservation, and civic engagement among Taiwanese Americans in the United States.

## Role in the Community

The TAH encyclopedia holds a dedicated directory record for the chapter: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/arizona-chapter-taa|Taiwanese American Association of Arizona 亞利桑那州台灣同鄉會]] (catalogued 2015-12-30). The chapter is also credited in the corpus record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-heritage-week-of-arizona|Taiwanese American Heritage Week of Arizona 亞利桑那州台灣同鄉會]] (2018-03-16) — civic-recognition activity under the chapter name. Arizona's Taiwanese associational life also includes a neighboring FAPA presence, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/3-arizona-chapter-fapa|Arizona Chapter / FAPA 台灣人公共事務會亞利桑那州分會]], and a NATWA chapter in the same TAH directory batch, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-arizona-chapter|Arizona Chapter / NATWA 北美洲台灣婦女會亞利桑那分會]].
