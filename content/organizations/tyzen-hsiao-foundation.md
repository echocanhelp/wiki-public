# Tyzen Hsiao Foundation (蕭泰然基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Tyzen Hsiao Foundation
- **Chinese:** 蕭泰然基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Tyzen Hsiao Foundation is a nonprofit organization dedicated to promoting the music legacy of Taiwanese American composer and maestro Tyzen Hsiao to an international audience. The foundation works to preserve and share Hsiao’s musical contributions, which represent a significant part of Taiwanese American cultural heritage.

## Role in the Community
The vault's community record documents the musical legacy this foundation exists to carry, across decades of diaspora performances:
- The D-major Violin Concerto received its world premiere by [[people/cho-liang-lin|Cho-Liang Lin 林昭亮]] with the San Diego Symphony on 1992-11-13; a later Akron, Ohio performance (conducted by [[people/ya-hui-wang|Ya-Hui Wang 王雅蕙]], violin [[people/cho-liang-lin|Cho-Liang Lin]]) drew 400+ community members and was hailed by [[people/dr-khang-loon-ho|Khang-Loon Ho 何康隆]] as a milestone of Taiwanese music overseas — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys269|269. Akron演奏會側記 / 何康隆 /12/2016]].
- 1995 — "台灣人音樂—蕭泰然作品演奏會", a tribute concert organized by TAC/EC (美東台灣人夏令會) in Ithaca, NY: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts14|14. A Musical Tribute to Tyzen Hsiao, Ithaca NY 07/02/1995]].
- Hsiao's own 2016 open letter to Taiwanese expatriates in Minnesota, with the reply by 蔡友仁 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories456|456. 蕭泰然給所思念的明州同鄉的一封信 /08/2016]].
- Broadcast biography feature — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/videos112|112. 【台灣演義】台灣音樂家 蕭泰然傳 03/01/2015]]; the 2018 US Tyzen Hsiao Music Festival — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/videos-152|152. 2018美國蕭泰然音樂節舞台現場最後彩排]]; a ticketed community-organized Formosan Melodies concert — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys327|327. 台灣音樂之夜 (Formosan Melodies) / 台美史料中心 /12/2017]].
