# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Arizona Chapter (北美洲台灣婦女會亞利桑那分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Arizona Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣婦女會亞利桑那分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Arizona Chapter is a local chapter of the North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA), serving Arizona area.

## Role in the Community

- A local chapter of [[organizations/north-america-taiwanese-womens-association|北美洲台灣婦女會 NATWA]], the umbrella Taiwanese women's community organization in North America.
- The chapter is documented in the TAH story corpus as a community historical record published 2015-12-30: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-arizona-chapter|2. Arizona Chapter / NATWA 北美洲台灣婦女會亞利桑那分會]] (bibliographic record; full text stays in the vault).
- Sits within the wider Arizona Taiwanese-American institutional network also recorded in the corpus: FAPA's Arizona chapter ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/3-arizona-chapter-fapa|3. Arizona Chapter / FAPA 台灣人公共事務會亞利桑那州分會]]), alongside [[organizations/taiwanese-american-association-of-arizona|Taiwanese American Association of Arizona]].
- Umbrella-org context absorbed 2026-09-22 (deepen-x slice 09220700-31): the corpus shows NATWA operating long-standing infrastructure the Arizona chapter sits under — the 聯誼通訊 membership newsletter (e.g. [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/663-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|663. 北美洲台灣婦女會聯誼通訊 第七期, 1992/06]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/679-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|679. 第二十三期, 2000/08]]), a 關懷資訊中心 care-information network ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/columns17|17. 關懷網 by NATWA關懷資訊中心]]), and sister chapters documented to the same standard ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-san-diego-chapter|5. Greater San Diego Chapter]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-oregonsouth-west-chapter|12. Oregon/South West Chapter]]).
- The umbrella org's community-aid role is documented in movement memoirs: in the Hurricane Harvey relief effort NATWA jointly donated US$21,090 with the TA community foundation, 洛杉磯台灣會館, 聖地牙哥台灣會館 and 休士頓台聲合唱團 to Houston's relief fund — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys328|ourjourneys328]]. Chapter-level participation in such drives is not separately recorded.
