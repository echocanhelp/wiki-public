# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Arizona Chapter (北美洲台灣婦女會亞利桑那分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Arizona Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣婦女會亞利桑那分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community

North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Arizona Chapter is a local chapter of the North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA), serving Arizona area.

## Role in the Community

- A local chapter of [[organizations/north-america-taiwanese-womens-association|北美洲台灣婦女會 NATWA]], the umbrella Taiwanese women's community organization in North America.
- The chapter is documented in the TAH story corpus as a community historical record published 2015-12-30: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-arizona-chapter|2. Arizona Chapter / NATWA 北美洲台灣婦女會亞利桑那分會]] (bibliographic record; full text stays in the vault).
