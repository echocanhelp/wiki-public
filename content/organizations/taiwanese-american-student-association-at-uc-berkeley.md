# Taiwanese American Student Association at UC Berkeley

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Student Association at UC Berkeley
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Student Association (TASA) at the University of California, Berkeley is a social and cultural student organization founded in 2003. It was established to provide a way for Taiwanese American students at Berkeley to connect with and understand their Taiwanese heritage and identity, and it welcomes students from all backgrounds who share an interest in Taiwanese and Taiwanese American culture.
