# San Diego Taiwanese Cultural Association (南加州)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** San Diego Taiwanese Cultural Association
- **Chinese:** 南加州
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The San Diego Taiwanese Cultural Association (SDTCA, 聖地牙哥台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization serving Taiwanese American communities across the United States. The chapter is based in San Diego, California, and is listed under the TAA Western (美西) region alongside other California and Pacific chapters.

- Its own history is recorded in the corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/project-3-41|41. 聖地牙哥台灣同鄉會簡介]] (2018-01-24), also in English as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/project-3-41-eng|41. - The chapter publishes its own newsletter 鄉訊, archived in the corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/newsletter-sdtca|鄉訊 by SDTCA]] (2015-07-28). A first-person 20-year retrospective of the San Diego Taiwan Center, drawn from the 07/2018 鄉訊, records internal organizing life — fundraising, board rosters, and the naming dispute in which LA's 王桂榮 objected to San Diego using the 台美基金會 name first ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys337|Our Journeys 337]], 2018-07-16).
- Community infrastructure in the San Diego record: the acquisition of the permanent home is told by Jinwun Winston Lue 呂錦文 in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/11-the-birth-of-the-permanent-home-for-the-taiwanese-american-community-center-o|11. The Birth of the Permanent Home for the Taiwanese American Community Center of San Diego]] (2014-09-05), leading to the grand opening of the Taiwanese American Community Center ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys162-eng|162. 聖地牙哥台灣中心開幕典禮與慶祝音樂會]], 2015) and its summer camp program ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tcsd-summer-camp|38. 聖地牙哥台灣中心夏令營]], 2017).

- Protest record: the chapter organized the "China, Hands Off Taiwan" protest rally documented at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/8-china-hands-off-taiwan-protest-rally-by-san-diego-taiwanese-association|8. "China, Hands Off Taiwan" Rally by San Diego Taiwanese Association]] (2015-08-05).
- Band-A institutional memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys26-eng|26. 聖地牙哥台灣中心的前半生與轉型 / Huang Cheng-yuan]] (2014): the San Diego Taiwan Center officially opened 1997-02-28 after a community-wide fundraising drive under 會長 陳秋山 (Chiu-shan Chen); the foundation's site search (99 Ranch Market neighborhood considered, a former church building finally chosen) and the "one big family" identity the drive forged are the chapter's founding-era internal record. The 20-year retrospective [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys337|Our Journeys 337]] continues this account.
- Senior affiliate documented separately: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/seniororg6|6. 聖地牙哥台灣長青會]] (2014).
- 2017 Hurricane Harvey relief: 聖地牙哥台灣會館 contributed US$17,619 to a joint US$87,459 donation (with the TAA Houston Foundation, LA Taiwanese American Cultural Center, 北美洲台灣婦女會, and 休士頓台聲合唱團) to the City of Houston's Harvey Relief Fund — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys328|Our Journeys 328]].
- Foundation-era record: the Taiwanese American Foundation of San Diego / Taiwan Center celebrated its 21st anniversary and found-raising dinner on 2018-03-10 at the San Diego Taiwan Center with 200 attendants ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/52714|26. CEO of SDTC/TAF — 歐春美 Chunmei Ou Lin's Journey]] records the leadership of [[people/carol-ou-lin|歐春美]], CEO of the San Diego Taiwan Center and the [[organizations/taiwanese-american-foundation-of-san-diego|Taiwanese American Foundation of San Diego]] (2024–2025).
