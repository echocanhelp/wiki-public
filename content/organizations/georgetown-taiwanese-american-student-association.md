# Georgetown Taiwanese American Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Georgetown Taiwanese American Student Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Georgetown Taiwanese American Student Association (TASA) is a student organization at Georgetown University in Washington, D.C., dedicated to promoting Taiwanese culture through social, educational, and cultural programming. The organization is officially recognized by the university and is listed in Georgetown’s student organization directory, where it is reachable at tasa@georgetown.edu. It is also a named member of the Students of Color Alliance (SOCA), Georgetown’s umbrella coalition of cultural student organizations.
