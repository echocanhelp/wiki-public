# Family Keepers International /2015/05 (國際真愛家庭協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Family Keepers International /2015/05

- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Family Keepers International is a nonprofit organization founded in 2001 in Los Angeles by Dr. and Mrs. Ye Gaofang, registered with the U.S. federal government as a nonprofit public benefit corporation (EIN 95-4852173). The organization is dedicated to strengthening Chinese-speaking families through professional programs addressing the spiritual, psychological, social, and physical dimensions of family life. It provides services across four domains — education, care, counseling, and healing — and serves individuals across all life stages from children to the elderly, including single-parent and blended families. Its headquarters are in the United States, and it has established eight international chapters worldwide. Key ministry arms include publications (a family magazine and educational materials), online resources, family counseling, volunteer training, and international outreach.
