# Taiwanese Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Student Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Student Association (TSA) at Texas A&M University is a registered student organization serving Taiwanese students at one of the largest public universities in the United States. The organization is listed on the university’s official Get Involved club directory and maintains an active presence on campus. Its Chinese name, 德州農工大學台灣同學會, reflects its identity as a self-governing association formed by students from Taiwan.

## Role in the Community
「台灣同學會」(Taiwanese Student Association) chapters were a backbone of the overseas Taiwanese movement — student-led associations formed to promote Taiwanese identity and culture. Community records linked to this page:
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ff33|33. Taiwanese Student Association – University of Kansas / The First Taiwanese Student Association / 1961]] — the KU chapter (1961) is recorded as the first Taiwanese Student Association.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys81|81. 早期(1960〜1970年)威大台灣學生在台灣建國運動所扮演的角色 / 周烒明 / 2014-12]] — UW students registered 「UW Formosan Student Club 威大台灣同學會」in early 1963 over Nationalist student opposition; after a campus debate won by 利騰俊 (on the Formosa/Taiwan name etymology), the charter was completed 1963-10-02 and 「The Formosan Club of the University of Wisconsin 威大台灣同鄉會」was formally registered 1963-10-19.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys277|277. 他們這樣愛台灣：威斯康辛大學「台灣布袋戲團」的故事 / 朱靜女 / 2017-02]] — by 1997 the UW-M 台灣同學會 staged its first 布袋戲（虎姑婆, conceived with the late 陳清風教授）at a Moon Festival, later spinning off a formal puppetry troupe touring local schools.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/dumplings-usctsa|燒肉粽 by USC Taiwanese Student Association 南加大台灣同學會]] — USC chapter's cultural performance record (2017-05-12).
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys219|219. 留美台灣青年就業座談會 / 柯金寅 / 2016-04]] — NJIT, Stevens, and NYU 台灣同學會 chapters volunteered at the North-Jersey career seminar.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys321|321. 陳文成紀念基金會的成立 (追述)]] — the 密西根大學台灣同學會 (with the Ann Arbor 台灣同鄕會) co-organized a 陳文成 memorial special issue on the 12th anniversary of his death, an early activity record of a Michigan chapter.

Chapter-foundations across the continent (sibling chapters, shared movement lineage — not this chapter's own history):
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys240|240. UCLA 台灣同學會 formation]] — 劉天良 hosted the first UCLA 台灣人 迎新 gathering in 1966 (no 迎新 existed in 1965 when he arrived alone); the reading club of 劉天良, 黃根深, 李木通, 陳嶄新, 王秋森, 陳松楨 grew into the UCLA/USC 台灣同學會 and then the 大洛杉磯台灣同鄉會 — the 「同學會→同鄉會→生活座談會→台美人論壇」 lineage.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys33|33. 陳希寬 / Kansas State]] — 陳希寬 succeeded 張肅 as Kansas State University 台灣同學會 會長 in 1965-09; the same circle (莊秋雄, 蔡一, 王能祥, 黃石定, 呂天明, 方菊雄) formed the Kansas Suite Group in 1964-65 and ran 228 ad campaigns in the Kansas State Collegian through the 1966-68 campus unification-independence debate.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys45|45. UT Austin 台灣同學會 — KMT intervention]] — a KMT official's letter to 「木盛同學」 pressing the University of Texas to block the organizing of a 「台灣同學會」 by Taiwanese students, warning against 「分化中國同學」; the Nationalist state treated a campus student club as a security matter.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys237|237. 北卡海報事件 / NCSU]] — the NCSU 台灣同學會 circle of 郭倍宏 and 林國庆 faced campus KMT agents who tore down 中國同學會 posters to frame them; the resulting lawsuit ended with the two Taiwanese students winning in court. See also [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tsa-north-carolina-state-university-raleigh-nc|TSA North Carolina State University, Raleigh, NC]].
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-381|381. 北卡 — NCSU 台灣學生會 profiles]] — 林國慶 described as the 「國父」 of the NCSU 台灣學生會 (activity library, regular reading groups), 李應元 as first publisher of 「台灣學生」 (4,000+ copies) while at UNC.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys205|205. UW Madison — passport retaliation]] — a second, independent account of the 1963 UW Formosan Students Club founding: the Nationalist mission pressured the university to refuse the charter, and when that failed, 周烒明's ROC passport was revoked, making him stateless — the first Taiwanese student association on a U.S. campus cost its founder the right to return.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tsa-taiwanese-student-association-at-the-university-of-washington|TSA at the University of Washington (華大台灣學生會)]] — UW chapter record; see also [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/uw-taiwanese-student-association-orientation-meeting|UW TSA orientation meeting]] and [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/taiwanese-student-association-orientation-at-uw-seattle|TSA orientation at UW Seattle]].
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/osu-taiwanese-student-association|Newsletter 會訊 by OSU Taiwanese Student Association 哥城台灣同學會]] — chapter newsletter record.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys238|238. 郭倍宏 / 「台灣學生」 launch]] — 郭倍宏 and his 台灣同學會 friends launched the first issue of 「台灣學生」 in 1983-09.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwan-s-by-n-a-t-s-a|台灣同學 by N.A.T.S.A. 全美台灣同學會]] — the national federation's publication; the 全美台灣同學會 was itself born at the 1985-06 seventh 夏令會 (274 attendees, 55 visiting students — the largest cohort in 20 years) per [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys245|245. 夏令會 roster]].
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tsapa-taiwanese-student-association-for-public-affairs|16. Taiwanese Student Association for Public Affairs 台灣公共事務學生會華大分會]] — UW chapter public-affairs offshoot.
- [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/taiwanese-student-association-at-rice-university-first-general-meeting|TSA at Rice University — first general meeting]] — Texas chapter record.

Texas A&M-linked community records in our corpus:
