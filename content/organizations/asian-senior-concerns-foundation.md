# Asian Senior Concerns Foundation (亞裔銀髮族關懷基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Asian Senior Concerns Foundation
- **Chinese:** 亞裔銀髮族關懷基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Asian Senior Concerns Foundation is a nonprofit organization established in 2001 in Seattle, Washington, founded by physicians, lawyers, accountants, engineers, and scholars from Taiwan. The foundation works to plan and provide resources for Asian Americans approaching or in retirement, helping them enjoy a secure, comfortable, healthy, and happy later life. It combines governmental and community resources to serve the senior Asian American population. Members volunteer their time outside of busy professional careers to research and share information on financial security, suitable housing, physical health, and peace of mind for elderly Asian Americans. The foundation’s long-term vision includes building a service network and retirement community suited to the needs of Asian seniors.

- Growth recorded in the same founder memoir: by 2004 volunteers and participants kept increasing, members donated to lease a fixed gathering venue and donated furniture themselves, and the Saturday program standardized into themed weeks (birthday/经验分享, medical, social-welfare, travel/personal topics, financial planning) alongside full-day classes (computer, line dance, tai-chi, qigong, ballroom, calligraphy, ink painting, photography, chess, mahjong). The foundation also ran structured health self-management courses — chronic-disease and diabetes self-management, fall-prevention/balance, emergency preparedness, and senior safe-driving — coordinated with local social-welfare and public-health agencies, and grew into a respected local Asian-American and diaspora community group.
