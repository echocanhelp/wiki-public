# TACL Toastmaster Club (議會學社)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** TACL Toastmaster Club
- **Chinese:** 議會學社
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

TACL Toastmasters Club was founded in September 1992 by board members and members of the Taiwanese American Citizens League (TACL), with the primary purpose of providing members opportunities to practice English communication skills. The club is affiliated with Toastmasters International (TI), a global organization founded in 1924 by Dr. Ralph C. Smedley in Los Angeles. The club focuses on three core areas: communication skills, leadership skills developed through parliamentary procedure, and self-confidence and personal growth. Meetings are held every Monday evening from 7:30 to 9:00 PM in Room 203 of the Taiwanese Community Center, and include agenda discussions, impromptu responses, formal speeches, evaluations, and joke sharing. Club officers are elected every six months.
