# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Boston Chapter (北美洲台灣婦女會波士頓分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Boston Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣婦女會波士頓分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Boston Chapter is a local chapter of the North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA), serving the Boston area.

## Role in the Community
The Boston chapter is documented in the TAH story corpus: its chapter profile 「3. Boston Chapter / NATWA 北美洲台灣婦女會波士頓分會」 is held at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-boston-chapter|NATWA Boston Chapter profile]] (published 2015-12-29, band B), which records the chapter as an organized Taiwanese women's community presence in the Boston area.

The chapter sits within the NATWA (北美洲台灣婦女會) network ([[organizations/north-america-taiwanese-womens-association|North America Taiwanese Women's Association]]), whose coordination is visible in the corpus through its 聯誼通訊 newsletter — e.g. [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/663-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|聯誼通訊 第七期 (1992-06)]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/679-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|聯誼通訊 第二十三期 (2000-08)]] — and through network-wide initiatives such as the NATWA 關懷資訊中心 column [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/columns17|關懷網 (2016-10-27)]].
