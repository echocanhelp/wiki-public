# Taiwanese Association of America – New Jersey (紐澤西)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of America – New Jersey
- **Chinese:** 紐澤西
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America – New Jersey (TAA-NJ, 紐澤西台灣同鄉會) is a chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations. The chapter serves Taiwanese Americans in central New Jersey and the surrounding vicinity, and is listed under the TAA’s Eastern Northern (美東北) regional grouping.
