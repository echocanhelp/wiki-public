# Cornell Taiwanese Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Cornell Taiwanese Student Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Cornell Taiwanese Student Association (CTSA; 康乃爾臺灣同學會) is a registered student organization at Cornell University in Ithaca, New York. It is a non-profit, non-religious, and non-political group primarily oriented toward students from Taiwan, helping them navigate life and academics at Cornell.
