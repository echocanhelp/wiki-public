# Texas College Station Taiwan Study Club (德州卡城台灣研究社)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Texas College Station Taiwan Study Club
- **Chinese:** 德州卡城台灣研究社
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Texas College Station Taiwan Study Club is a student organization based in College Station, Texas, focused on the study of Taiwanese history, culture, politics, economics, and society. The club promotes Taiwanese identity and consciousness, and advocates for Taiwan’s democracy and human rights. Members gather regularly for study sessions and discussions to understand Taiwan’s past, present, and future. The club was founded by Professor 黃界清 and a group of founding members, and has maintained an active leadership structure since its inception.

## Role in the Community
- 2015 — the club is recorded in the TAH story corpus as directory entry #34: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/enews-texas-college-station-taiwan-study-club|TAH record #34 — 德州卡城台灣研究社]].
- 2017 — the club organized a 228 commemorative event (228週年紀年活動) in Texas, documented in the corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tx-228-70th|德州卡城台灣研究社舉辦 228週年紀年活動]]. This memorial activism complements the study-session mission recorded in the TAH directory.
- Founder Professor 黃界清 (Je-Chin Han) is documented independently in the corpus: TAH encyclopedia record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-604-je-chin-han|#604 Prof. Je-Chin Han 黃界清教授]], the 2020 ASME Honorary Member Award record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/award-77|#77 (2020)]], and as 召集人 of the NATPA 廖述宗教授紀念獎委員會 in the 2015 award announcement [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys129|129. 台美人顧台灣：NATPA 廖述宗教授紀念獎 / 2015-04]] — confirming the club's founder as an active figure in the Taiwanese American academic community.
- The College Station base itself has deep movement roots: the corpus records a TAA 全美台灣同鄉會卡城分會 chapter [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/college-station-chapter-taa|College Station, TX Chapter / TAA 卡城分會 (2015-12-21)]], and TAA founding history lists College Station (會長 廖大林 Ta-lin Liao) among the 28 founding chapters [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76|TAA founding memoir 全美會歷史]]. The study club emerged in a town with a documented Taiwanese community since TAA's founding era.
