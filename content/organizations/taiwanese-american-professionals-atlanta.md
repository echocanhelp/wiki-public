# Taiwanese American Professionals Atlanta (台美菁英協會亞特蘭大分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Professionals Atlanta
- **Chinese:** 台美菁英協會亞特蘭大分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Taiwanese American Professionals Atlanta (TAP-ATL) is a chapter of the Taiwanese American Citizens League (TACL), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit run entirely by volunteers. TAP-ATL serves the Taiwanese American community in the greater Atlanta area by organizing monthly TAPpy Hours and larger quarterly events focused on social networking, professional development, and cultural celebration. The chapter welcomes people of all cultures and backgrounds.

## Role in the Community

TAP-ATL is the Atlanta chapter of [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-professionals-tap|TAP 台美菁英協會]], documented in the TAH story corpus as its own chapter record ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/atlanta-chapter-tap|Atlanta Chapter / TAP 台美菁英協會亞特蘭大分會]], published 2014-10-12).

Corpus event records trace the chapter's activity before and after its formal chapter listing:

- 2013-12-11 — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/tappy-hour-in-atlanta|TAPpy Hour in Atlanta]] — earliest Atlanta-area TAP gathering in the corpus, predating the announcement party
- 2014-01-16 — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/tap-atlanta-announcement-party|TAP Atlanta – Announcement Party]] — chapter launch event
- 2014-05-15 — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/taiwanese-heritage-week-tappy-hour-in-atlanta|Taiwanese Heritage Week TAPpy Hour in Atlanta]]
- 2014-08-14 — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/tappy-hour-in-atlanta-ga|TAPpy Hour in Atlanta, GA]]
- 2014-08-27 — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/mandarin-dinner-series-with-tap-in-atlanta|Mandarin Dinner Series with TAP in Atlanta]] — language/culture programming alongside social networking

The monthly TAPpy Hours described in the TAH directory listing are corroborated by these contemporaneous event records; the Mandarin Dinner Series shows the chapter also ran cultural-education programming, not only social networking.
