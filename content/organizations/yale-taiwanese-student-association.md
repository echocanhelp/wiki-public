# Yale Taiwanese Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Yale Taiwanese Student Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Yale Taiwanese Student Association (YTSA, 耶魯臺灣同學會) is a graduate-level student organization at Yale University in New Haven, Connecticut. Its membership is drawn primarily from Taiwanese graduate students and postdoctoral researchers across Yale’s various schools and programs. The organization is registered through Yale’s official GSAS (Graduate School of Arts and Sciences) chapter platform, Yale Connect.
