# Greater Washington Taiwan Culture Center (華府台灣文化中心)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Greater Washington Taiwan Culture Center
- **Chinese:** 華府台灣文化中心
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Greater Washington Taiwan Culture Center (TCC) is a non-profit 501(c)(3) organization established by Taiwanese Americans in the greater Washington, D.C. area. Located in Rockville, Maryland, near the Shady Grove Metro station, the center serves as a fixed venue for Taiwanese American cultural, civic, and community activities in the mid-Atlantic region.
