# Living Well Club of New Jersey (紐澤西州)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Living Well Club of New Jersey
- **Chinese:** 紐澤西州
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Living Well Club of New Jersey is a Taiwanese American senior social organization providing weekly gatherings in central New Jersey. Members meet every Wednesday from 10am to 3pm at the Taiwanese American Fellowship Presbyterian Church (TAFPC) at 100 Livingston Avenue, New Brunswick, NJ 08901, with a shared lunch provided. Activities span recreational, educational, cultural, social, and spiritual programs including bridge, chess, karaoke, Tai-Chi, Ping-Pong, arts and crafts, and occasional seminars and field trips. A second meeting location is Somerset Presbyterian Church, 100 John F Kennedy Blvd, Somerset, NJ 08873. Summer luncheons are held July–August at East Grand Buffet, East Brunswick, NJ.
