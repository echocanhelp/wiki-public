# Texas Formosa Fishing Association (德州台灣釣魚協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Texas Formosa Fishing Association
- **Chinese:** 德州台灣釣魚協會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Texas Formosa Fishing Association is a nonprofit, nonpolitical organization based at the Taiwanese Activity Center in Houston, Texas. It is dedicated to promoting recreational fishing as a healthy leisure activity and cultivating personal character among its members. The association works to foster cooperation and harmony among anglers, improve fishing skills, and advocate for member welfare. Activities include annual fishing competitions, fishing technique seminars, camping and outdoor events, and family-oriented gatherings to strengthen bonds among members and their families.
