# STC Rowland Legacy

## Identity Snapshot
- **Era:** 2025–present (property acquired Nov 2025)
- **Geography:** 18991/18993 Colima Rd., Rowland Heights, CA — ~13 acres, ~700 parking spaces
- **Core roles:** community venue (taiwanese American) — event plaza explicitly modeled on Taiwan night markets

## Record

- Former shopping center **acquired by STC in Nov 2025**; name "Legacy" = 傳承 (heritage/transmission) per founder [[people/john-hsu|John Hsu (許惠欽)]] — World Journal / China Times / Epoch Times, Sept 2026.
- **OCLA STC Night Market**: Fri–Sun night market from July 31, 2026 (free admission; movie nights, karaoke, cultural shows — stcrowlandlegacy.com).
- **"Taste of Taiwan 2026"** (Oct 2–11, 2026): 15 Taiwan vendor teams, 60+ Taiwan dishes, 50+ local vendors; Double Ten (ROC 115) framing — opening ceremony with balloon-blessing (天燈 tradition, balloons standing in), 電音三太子, "為台灣及台美友誼祈福." Co-hosts: **STC顺天联合基金会** (STC Foundation), **[[organizations/ai-education-foundation|AI教育基金會]]** (president [[people/pan-yi-ling|潘意玲]], 僑務委員), **台美餐飲產業經貿发展協會 TUFBA**.
- Hsu (raised in Taiwan; "immigrated 43 years" per China Times): wants the market as an **incubator** — vendors test the SoCal market here and open physical stores; multi-ethnic roster (Taiwanese, Mexican, Korean, Chinese vendors) framed as exchange.

## Historical Significance

Third-generation SGV Taiwanese civic infrastructure: first generation built churches and associations, second built festivals; this is a **business owner converting a dead mall into a nation-flavored public square** — the Taiwan night market, Double Ten, and 台美 friendship performed as commerce. Also a data point for the "unincorporated SGV" pattern: this civic life happens without a city government.
