# The Chuan Lyu Foundation (川流基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** The Chuan Lyu Foundation
- **Chinese:** 川流基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Chuan Lyu Foundation is a private philanthropy dedicated to preserving the culture and history of the Taiwanese people, founded in 1986 and based in Palo Alto, California. The Foundation sponsors programs worldwide, presenting the richness and diversity of Taiwanese culture through seminars, lectureships, fellowships, scholarships, film projects, film festivals, and a wide array of cultural events. Its goal is to nurture Taiwanese culture after nearly 100 years of suppression. The Foundation serves as a catalyst for Taiwanese expression by supporting existing programs and new projects that bring Taiwanese culture to public consciousness.
