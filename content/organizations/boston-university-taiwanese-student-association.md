# Boston University Taiwanese Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Boston University Taiwanese Student Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Boston University Taiwanese Student Association (BUTSA, 波士頓大學台灣同學會) is a student organization dedicated to promoting Taiwanese culture and history at Boston University. Its stated purpose is to unite students with an interest in Taiwanese backgrounds through a variety of events. BUTSA is open to all students regardless of background, welcoming anyone with an interest in Taiwan. The org’s WordPress site (butsaa.wordpress.com) lists a contact email of butsaa@bu.edu and links to its Facebook Group.
