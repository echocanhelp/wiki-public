# Taiwanese Association of Syracuse (雪城台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of Syracuse
- **Chinese:** 雪城台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of Syracuse (雪城台灣同鄉會) is a local chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the Syracuse, New York area. The chapter appears in corpus records under both English renderings "Taiwanese Association of Syracuse" and "Syracuse Taiwanese Association" for the same 雪城台灣同鄉會.
