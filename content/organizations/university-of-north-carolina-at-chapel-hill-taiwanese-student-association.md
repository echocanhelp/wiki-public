# University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill Taiwanese Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill Taiwanese Student Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill Taiwanese Student Association (UNC TSA / UNCTSA), known in Chinese as 北卡羅萊納大學教堂山分校台灣同學會, is a registered student organization at UNC Chapel Hill serving Taiwanese students at the university. The organization is listed in the university’s official Heel Life club directory at heellife.unc.edu and maintains an Instagram account (@uncch_tsa), a Facebook group, and a Blogspot-based website that has served as its primary web presence.
