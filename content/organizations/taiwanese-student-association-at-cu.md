# Taiwanese Student Association at CU

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Student Association at CU
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Student Association at CU (TSA) is a recognized student organization at the University of Colorado Boulder serving students with ties to Taiwan. The organization’s Chinese name, 科羅拉多大學波德分校台灣學生會, appears on its Instagram profile (@cuboulder.tsa), confirming its Taiwanese-language identity and community focus.
