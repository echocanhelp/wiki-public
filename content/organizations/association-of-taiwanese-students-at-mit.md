# Association of Taiwanese Students at MIT

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Association of Taiwanese Students at MIT
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Association of Taiwanese Students (ATS) at MIT is an undergraduate student organization founded in 1991 to celebrate and explore Taiwanese culture on campus. According to the organization’s own history, ATS was established that year when a student had the idea of forming a group devoted to activities related to Taiwanese culture, at a time when MIT’s existing Chinese student organizations were seen as serving a different function. The club is listed among MIT’s officially recognized multicultural student organizations and maintains an active website at mitats.org.
