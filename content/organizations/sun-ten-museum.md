# Sun Ten Museum (順天美術館)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Sun Ten Museum
- **Chinese:** 順天美術館
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Sun Ten Museum is a nonprofit fine arts museum located in Irvine, California, founded in 1996 in commemoration of Dr. Hong-yen Hsu, known as the “Father of Scientific Chinese Herbal Medicine.” The museum holds the “Sun Ten Collection,” a documentarily significant collection of Taiwanese artists’ works spanning approximately 100 years of art movements on Taiwan. Sun Ten Museum aims to preserve Taiwanese cultural treasures, serve as an open cultural resource for the community, and act as a bridge for cultural interchange in the United States. The museum presents regular rotating exhibitions from the Sun Ten Collection, annual large-scale themed exhibitions, and is developing a Taiwan Artists Information Center and a Taiwan Artists Works Lending Center.
