# Taiwanese Students Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Students Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Students Association (TSA) at Carnegie Mellon University is a student organization serving the Taiwanese and Taiwanese American undergraduate community in Pittsburgh. The organization is registered with CMU’s official club platform, TartanConnect, and maintains an active presence across Facebook and Instagram, where it operates under the handle @cmu_tsa with over 1,000 followers and more than 450 posts as of 2025.

## Role in the Community
- The CMU TSA is one campus chapter under the "Taiwanese Students Association" name, which the TAH/TAHS story corpus documents at multiple universities as separate organizations. It is listed in the TAH organization directory alongside sibling student groups.
- Corpus records for other-campus TSAs (context, not this chapter): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tsa-taiwanese-student-association-at-the-university-of-washington|華大台灣學生會 TSA story]] (published 2014-10-12) and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/rutgerstsa-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e7-a0-94-e7-a9-b6-e7-a4-be-e5-be-9e-sars-e5-88-b0-c|Rutgers TSA 台灣研究社 — 從 SARS 到 COVID-19]] (published 2020-12-04).
- Corpus re-grep 2026-09-21 (slice 09201500-19): the only CMU-naming memoir in the corpus is the 吳文遠 memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys8-eng|Our Journeys #8 (EN)]], which names CMU president Richard Cyert and professor Morris DeGroot as congressional-testimony supporters of the 台獨 movement — CMU-community ties to the movement, but neither man is a TSA member and no CMU TSA memoir exists. Broader same-name student-network context: the Taiwanese Collegian/台灣學生社 movement lineage ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts114|Landscape of Formosa 音樂會 2000]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-collegian-irvine-ca|台灣學生季刊]]), the UW-Madison 台灣同學會 at the Taiwanese Collegian Conference ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys277|Our Journeys #277]]), NCSU 台灣學生會 founding figures ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-381|Our Journeys #381]]), and the University of Wisconsin 台灣問題研究會 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys81|Our Journeys #81]]). No CMU-chapter-specific biography invented.
- The TAH directory roster ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/first-136|first 136 organizations]]) enumerates peer student associations including PTASA (Princeton) and 羅格斯大學台灣研究社 RTSA (Rutgers), situating the CMU TSA within a nationwide network of Taiwanese student organizations.
- 複核吸收（deepen-x slice-09211400-6, 2026-09-22）: new corpus hit [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys356-eng|Our Journeys #356 (EN)]] supplies the Pittsburgh movement environment the CMU chapter later sat in — the 1969 Pittsburgh Taiwanese Association co-founded by Fu-Chen Lo, Edward Huang, Daniel Ko, Chiung-hui Ko; activities hosted at universities/churches; the 1979-12-15 Formosa Incident symposium at Pitt's Lawrence Hall; and the 1981 killing of CMU math professor 陳文成 Chen Wen-Chen, which sparked street demonstrations around the Oakland campus. Context for the Pittsburgh/CMU Taiwanese student milieu, not a CMU TSA chapter memoir.
