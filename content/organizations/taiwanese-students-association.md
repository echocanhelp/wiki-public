# Taiwanese Students Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Students Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Students Association (TSA) at Carnegie Mellon University is a student organization serving the Taiwanese and Taiwanese American undergraduate community in Pittsburgh. The organization is registered with CMU’s official club platform, TartanConnect, and maintains an active presence across Facebook and Instagram, where it operates under the handle @cmu_tsa with over 1,000 followers and more than 450 posts as of 2025.
