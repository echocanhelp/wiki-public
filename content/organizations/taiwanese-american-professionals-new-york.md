# Taiwanese American Professionals New York (台美菁英協會紐約分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Professionals New York
- **Chinese:** 台美菁英協會紐約分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Taiwanese American Professionals New York (TAP-NY) is a chapter of the Taiwanese American Citizens League (TACL), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit run entirely by volunteers. TAP-NY serves the Taiwanese American community in the greater New York City area through programming centered on social enrichment, community involvement, and personal and professional growth. The chapter’s events are open to all regardless of background.
