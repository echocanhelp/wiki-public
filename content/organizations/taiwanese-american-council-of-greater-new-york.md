# Taiwanese American Council of Greater New York (大紐約區台灣人社團聯合會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Council of Greater New York
- **Chinese:** 大紐約區台灣人社團聯合會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Council of Greater New York (TAC-GNY) is an umbrella organization coordinating Taiwanese American organizations and clubs in the Greater New York area. It promotes Taiwanese culture and heritage, and educates the broader community about Taiwanese American history. TAC-GNY serves as a coordinating apparatus to promote the well-being and betterment of the Taiwanese American community in the region.
