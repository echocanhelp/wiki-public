# Princeton Taiwanese American Students Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Princeton Taiwanese American Students Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Princeton Taiwanese American Students Association (TASA) is an undergraduate affinity group at Princeton University dedicated to promoting Taiwanese culture and heritage on campus. The organization was revived in 2022 after a prior iteration became inactive around 2018, and has grown into one of the more visible cultural organizations on campus. It is recognized and supported by Princeton’s Office of the Dean of Undergraduate Students (ODUS), which provides funding for student organizations to host events.
