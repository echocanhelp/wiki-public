# 美洲台灣日報 (Taiwan Daily)

Los Angeles–based Chinese-language daily serving the Taiwanese-American community, publishing since the 1990s. The paper's WordPress corpus (sequential post IDs past 125,000; ~162,000 posts) carries news, political commentary, columns, and the person-dense **台美人物** (Taiwanese-American notables) category that makes it one of the densest single sources of 台美人 community records on the web.
