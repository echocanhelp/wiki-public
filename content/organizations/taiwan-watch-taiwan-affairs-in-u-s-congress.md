# Taiwan Watch – Taiwan Affairs in U. S. Congress (美國國會台灣觀測站)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwan Watch – Taiwan Affairs in U. S. Congress
- **Chinese:** 美國國會台灣觀測站
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Taiwan Watch is an organization based in Houston, Texas that monitors and tracks Taiwan-related legislation and affairs in the U.S. Congress. Operating in the spirit of open data and open government, it collects and organizes congressional bills pertaining to Taiwan to make them accessible to a broader audience. The organization builds websites and tools to observe Taiwan-related legislation in the U.S. Congress. Its work aims to strengthen U.S.-Taiwan relations and raise awareness among Americans about the importance of those ties.
