# North America Taiwanese Professors' Association Southern California

**North America Taiwanese Professors' Association Southern California** (北美洲台灣人教授協會南加分會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣人教授協會南加分會
- **English (as listed):** North America Taiwanese Professors' Association Southern California
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
- **Source:** https://www.taiwancenter.org/group-members · `taiwancenter-org-group-members.md`

## Role in the Community

NATPA Southern California sits within the wider NATPA network documented in the TAH story corpus:

- NATPA (北美洲台灣人教授協會) was co-founded by 廖述宗, a旅美學者 and its first chapter-era 創會會長, per the oral-history memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-350||Our Journeys #350]].
- The association's annual meeting has been held jointly with the 美南台灣人夏令會, recorded in the 2012 joint special issue [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/919-e7-be-8e-e5-8d-97-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e4-ba-ba-e5-a4-8f-e4-bb-a4-e6-9c-83-e6-9||919. 美南台灣人夏令會暨北美洲台灣人教授協會年會特刊 2012]] — the Southern California chapter operates in this 美南 community milieu.
- NATPA publishes its own newsletter, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natpa-forum-by-north-america-taiwanese-professors-association-natpa||NATPA FORUM]].
- The headquarters documents a serial of 年會特刊 spanning at least 1998–2008: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/909-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e4-ba-ba-e6-95-99-e6-8e-88-e5-8|909. 第十八屆年會特刊 1998]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/911-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e4-ba-ba-e6-95-99-e6-8e-88-e5-8|911. 第二十二屆年會特刊 2002]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications918|918. 年會特刊 2008]] — the annual-meeting record the Southern California chapter plugs into.
- Parent-org movement footprint: 周實's 1990 census campaign memoir records NATPA (教授協會) as one of twelve Taiwanese community organizations that jointly wrote to ~12,000 Taiwanese households nationwide in July 1990 urging them to register "Taiwanese" on the census — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys253|253. 1990年美國人口普查-TACL的角色 / 周實 / 09-2016]].
- Sibling chapters are individually documented in the corpus — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natpa-michigan-chapter|6. 密西根分會]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natpa-new-england-chapter|7. 新英格蘭分會]] (both 2015) — showing the chapter structure the 南加分會 belongs to; NATPA's scholarly mission is recorded in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natpa-professor-liao-shutsung-research-award|13. 廖述宗教授研究獎金]].

- 2019 — the TAH story corpus holds a dedicated activity record for the chapter: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/north-american-taiwanese-professors-association-napta-south-ca-chapter|北美洲台灣人教授協會南加州分會的活動 (NATPA S CAL, 2019-02-26)]] — bibliographic record only; full text remains in the vault.

NATMA 南加分會暨總會年會特刊 2007]] and the 2017 傳統月 牙科義診/CPR 記者會 record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/videos59|大洛杉磯台美人傳統月 NATMA 南加分會記者會 20170503]]) belong to the sibling physicians' association NATMA (北美洲台灣人醫師協會), not NATPA — do not conflate the two 南加分會.
