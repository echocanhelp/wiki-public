# North America Taiwanese Professors' Association Southern California

**North America Taiwanese Professors' Association Southern California** (北美洲台灣人教授協會南加分會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣人教授協會南加分會
- **English (as listed):** North America Taiwanese Professors' Association Southern California
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
- **Source:** https://www.taiwancenter.org/group-members · `taiwancenter-org-group-members.md`

## Role in the Community

NATPA Southern California sits within the wider NATPA network documented in the TAH story corpus:

- NATPA (北美洲台灣人教授協會) was co-founded by 廖述宗, a旅美學者 and its first chapter-era 創會會長, per the oral-history memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-350||Our Journeys #350]].
- The association's annual meeting has been held jointly with the 美南台灣人夏令會, recorded in the 2012 joint special issue [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/919-e7-be-8e-e5-8d-97-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e4-ba-ba-e5-a4-8f-e4-bb-a4-e6-9c-83-e6-9||919. 美南台灣人夏令會暨北美洲台灣人教授協會年會特刊 2012]] — the Southern California chapter operates in this 美南 community milieu.
- NATPA publishes its own newsletter, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natpa-forum-by-north-america-taiwanese-professors-association-natpa||NATPA FORUM]].
