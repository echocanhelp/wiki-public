# Taiwanese American Association Milwaukee (密爾瓦基台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Association Milwaukee
- **Chinese:** 密爾瓦基台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Association Milwaukee (TAAM, 密爾瓦基台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the Milwaukee, Wisconsin area.
