# ASU Taiwanese International Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** ASU Taiwanese International Student Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The ASU Taiwanese International Student Association (TISA), known in Chinese as 亞利桑那州立大學台灣國際學生會, is a student organization at Arizona State University in Tempe, Arizona. It is listed as a member organization of ASU’s Coalition of International Students, an official university-recognized grouping of international student orgs hosted on the ASU Educational Outreach and Student Services website. TISA is distinct from the broader TSA at ASU and explicitly positions itself as a community for Taiwanese international students.
