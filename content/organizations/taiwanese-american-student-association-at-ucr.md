# Taiwanese American Student Association at UCR

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Student Association at UCR
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Student Association at UCR (TASA @ UCR) is a student cultural organization at the University of California, Riverside. It is listed as an officially recognized cultural organization under the umbrella of UCR’s Asian Pacific Student Programs (APSP), which coordinates Asian American and Pacific Islander student groups on campus. TASA @ UCR maintains an Instagram presence at @ucr.tasa and a Facebook page, and has a listing in UCR’s official student organization directory, HighlanderLink.
