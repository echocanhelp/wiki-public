- Context from movement memoirs: 台北工專 appears as a formative institution in Taiwanese American movement lives — e.g. 楊宗昌 (Taipei工專土木工程科, later 29-year central committee member of the UFAI 台灣獨立聯盟) studied there before moving to the U.S. in 1962; see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys33|33. 一家人做伙來打拼／張炎憲]]. A second corpus memoir names 王文隆 (b. 1944, 宜蘭), 台北工專五年制畢業 then MIT 核子工程博士, then Bell Labs in Chicago — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys256|256. 寫在畫展之前／黃根深, 2016-10-10]]. (Alumni-association membership of such individuals is not asserted by the corpus.)
