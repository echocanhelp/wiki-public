# The Great New York Region-Oversea Taiwanese Pen Club (大紐約區海外台灣人筆會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** The Great New York Region-Oversea Taiwanese Pen Club
- **Chinese:** 大紐約區海外台灣人筆會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Great New York Region-Oversea Taiwanese Pen Club is a literary and civic organization founded in 2003 by a group of writing enthusiasts from the Taiwanese American community in the New York and New Jersey area. The club encourages members to write and publish, with the stated goal of cultivating Taiwanese identity, a shared sense of community destiny, and active engagement in social justice and the welfare of Taiwan and Taiwanese Americans. Within its first decade it grew from 12 founding lifetime members and 60 annual members to over 100 members total. The club has hosted 56 events including 10 annual conferences and lectures spanning topics such as politics, economics, literature, music, environmental issues, technology, and film.
