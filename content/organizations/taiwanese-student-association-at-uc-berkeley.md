# Taiwanese Student Association at UC Berkeley

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Student Association at UC Berkeley
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Student Association (TSA) at the University of California, Berkeley is a non-profit, non-political student organization dedicated to Taiwanese culture and community on campus. The organization maintains an official website at tsa.berkeley.edu, a CalLink directory listing, an Instagram account (@berkeleytsa) with over 1,900 followers, and a Facebook page under the name “UC Berkeley TSA 柏克萊加大台灣同學會.” Its Chinese name, 柏克萊加大台灣同學會, appears consistently across its social media presence.

## Role in the Community

- 2012年2月在校內辦理 WorldVision Taiwan 救濟募款活動，顯示社團活動從校園文化延伸到台灣 humanitarian relief（[[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/worldvision-taiwan-fundraiser-by-tsa-at-uc-berkeley|WorldVision Taiwan Fundraiser by TSA at UC Berkeley]]，taiwaneseamerican.org，2012-02-14）。
