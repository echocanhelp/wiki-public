# Asia Democracy Foundation (民主亞洲基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Asia Democracy Foundation
- **Chinese:** 民主亞洲基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Asia Democracy Foundation is dedicated to promoting democratization in Asia, with a particular focus on China. Operating through modern internet and email platforms, the foundation publishes the online journal ‘Democracy Forum’ and the electronic newsletter ‘Democracy Newsletter.’ It has built a global network of individuals concerned with Chinese democratization and human rights issues, with readers and contributors reaching deep into mainland China. The foundation serves as a bridge for the exchange of experiences between the Taiwanese democratic movement and democratic movements in mainland China.
