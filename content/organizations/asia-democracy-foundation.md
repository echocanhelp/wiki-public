# Asia Democracy Foundation (民主亞洲基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Asia Democracy Foundation
- **Chinese:** 民主亞洲基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Asia Democracy Foundation is dedicated to promoting democratization in Asia, with a particular focus on China. Operating through modern internet and email platforms, the foundation publishes the online journal ‘Democracy Forum’ and the electronic newsletter ‘Democracy Newsletter.’ It has built a global network of individuals concerned with Chinese democratization and human rights issues, with readers and contributors reaching deep into mainland China. The foundation serves as a bridge for the exchange of experiences between the Taiwanese democratic movement and democratic movements in mainland China.

## Role in the Community
- Founded in **1998 in New York** by the Taiwanese-American independence veteran **洪哲勝 (Hung Cheh-sheng)**, former vice-chairman of the Formosan Union Government (台獨聯盟) US headquarters, after Taiwan's democratic transition turned his attention to Chinese democratization.
- Published the electronic newsletter **《民主通訊》Democracy Newsletter** and created the **《民主論壇》Democracy Forum** website; funded mainland Chinese dissidents through royalties and built a network of readers and contributors reaching deep into China. Held that if China democratized, Taiwan's democracy and security would be more secure.
- 洪哲勝 led the foundation as chairman until his death of lymphoma complications in New York on **2020-12-18**, aged 81; the online farewell was attended by over a hundred overseas figures, with eulogies from 駐紐約辦事處長李光章 and remarks from 王丹 and 陳破空 honoring the foundation's work with the overseas Chinese democracy movement.
