# Silicon Valley Taiwanese American Association (北加州)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Silicon Valley Taiwanese American Association
- **Chinese:** 北加州
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Silicon Valley Taiwanese American Association (矽谷台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization serving Taiwanese American communities across the United States. The chapter serves the Silicon Valley area of California and is listed under the TAA’s Western region (美西).
