# Taiwanese American Organization at the University of Southern California

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Organization at the University of Southern California
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Organization (TAO) at the University of Southern California is a student cultural and social organization founded in 1995. It was established as a club welcoming all USC students regardless of cultural background, with a particular focus on promoting awareness and exploration of Taiwanese and Taiwanese American culture. TAO maintains an official website at usctao.org and a Wix events site, and is a member organization of the USC Asian Pacific American Student Assembly (APASA).
