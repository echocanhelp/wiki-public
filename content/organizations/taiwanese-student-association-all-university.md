# Taiwanese Student Association – All-University

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Student Association – All-University
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Student Association – All-University (NYU TSA) is a student organization at New York University registered through NYU Engage, the university’s official club and events platform. The organization’s Chinese name, 紐約大學台灣同學會, appears on its Instagram profile, where the account describes itself as the “NYU Taiwanese Student Association” and invites members to direct-message for inquiries. As of mid-2026, the Instagram account (@nyu.tsa) has 934 followers and 48 posts, indicating an active but relatively modest web presence. No dedicated website, Facebook page, or Linktree has been identified for this organization.
