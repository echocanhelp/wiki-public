# Penn Taiwanese Society

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Penn Taiwanese Society
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Penn Taiwanese Society (PTS) is the undergraduate Taiwanese student organization at the University of Pennsylvania, founded in 1990 according to the organization’s own Instagram bio. Listed on both Penn’s Pan-Asian American Community House (PAACH) directory and the official Penn Clubs platform, PTS describes itself as “the destination for all undergraduate students interested in Taiwanese affairs.” The organization is open to students of all backgrounds and emphasizes cultural community-building on campus.
