# Taiwanese-America Seniors Association of Southern California TASA (南加州台灣人長輩會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese-America Seniors Association of Southern California TASA
- **Chinese:** 南加州台灣人長輩會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese-America Seniors Association of Southern California (TASA) is a community organization founded in 1979 to serve Taiwanese American senior citizens in the greater Los Angeles area. Originally established as the ‘Southern California Taiwanese Elderly Association,’ the organization grew from approximately 100 founding members to over 1,400 members by 1989. TASA developed physical infrastructure for its community, including the construction of the Crane Garden Apartments (鶴園公寓) in El Monte, California, which broke ground in 1987. The organization provides monthly social gatherings, birthday celebrations, recreational activities, and inter-community exchanges for its senior members. It has also pursued elder care initiatives including a proposed day health care center with nutrition services.
