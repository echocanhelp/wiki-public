# Formosar Chriotion for Self Determination (臺灣人民自決運動)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Formosar Chriotion for Self Determination
- **Chinese:** 臺灣人民自決運動
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Formosan Christians for Self Determination was a movement founded in 1973 to mobilize overseas Taiwanese to advocate for the right of the Taiwanese people to determine their own destiny. On March 19, 1973, four founders — including Reverend Huang Chang-hui — convened 21 representatives from across the United States and Canada in Washington, D.C. The following day, a press conference was held at the National Press Club, where the ‘Declaration of the Formosan People’s Self-Determination Movement’ was formally read aloud. The movement publicly declared its intent to unite overseas Taiwanese compatriots in pursuit of the sacred right and freedom of self-determination for the people of Taiwan.
