# Taiwan Environmental Action Network TEAN (台灣環境行動網)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwan Environmental Action Network TEAN
- **Chinese:** 台灣環境行動網
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Taiwan Environmental Action Network (TEAN) is an action-oriented environmental advocacy organization composed primarily of Taiwanese international students, with members concentrated at UC Berkeley and additional members on the East Coast. The organization addresses a wide range of environmental issues relevant to Taiwan, including conservation of the Black-faced Spoonbill, anti-nuclear and nuclear waste concerns, coastal wetlands, river and dam issues, carbon emissions, energy policy, environmental justice, and animal rights. TEAN exists in response to Taiwan’s inability to fully participate in global environmental diplomacy due to its constrained international status, and works to bridge that gap from abroad. Members are organized into seven working groups covering research, external relations, communications, web design and systems, finance, publishing, and event planning. TEAN has become a recognized overseas environmental organization and serves as an overseas representative for numerous civic groups in Taiwan.
