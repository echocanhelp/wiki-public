# Rice Taiwanese Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Rice Taiwanese Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Rice Taiwanese Association (RTA) is a non-partisan cultural and social organization at Rice University dedicated to celebrating and sharing Taiwanese culture and heritage with the broader campus community. It is recognized by Rice’s Student Center and Office of Multicultural Affairs, and holds membership in the Intercollegiate Taiwanese American Students Association (ITASA). Its founding year is not documented in available first-party sources.
