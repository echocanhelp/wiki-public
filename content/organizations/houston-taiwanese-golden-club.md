# Houston Taiwanese Golden Club 樂部 (休士頓台灣清閒俱)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Houston Taiwanese Golden Club 樂部
- **Chinese:** 休士頓台灣清閒俱
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Houston Taiwanese Golden Club is a nonprofit, nonpolitical, and nonreligious autonomous organization founded on December 12, 1998. It operates on the principle of ‘from the members, for the members,’ with the goal of benefiting members and fostering fellowship among Taiwanese compatriots in the Houston area. Membership is open to individuals aged 50 and above who are originally from Taiwan, with annual dues of $30 for couples and $15 for individuals. Activities include travel, outings, sports, dancing, music appreciation, karaoke, cuisine, gardening, and lectures on health, medicine, finance, and technology. The club is based at the Taiwanese Community Center.
