# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Hawaii Chapter (北美洲台灣婦女會夏威夷分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Hawaii Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣婦女會夏威夷分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

North America Taiwanese Women's Association – Hawaii Chapter is a local chapter of the North America Taiwanese Women's Association (NATWA), serving Hawaii area.

## Role in the Community

- The chapter is documented in the TAH story corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-hawaii-chapter|6. Hawaii Chapter / NATWA 北美洲台灣婦女會夏威夷分會]] (recorded 2015-12-26; bibliographic record only, full text in the TAH vault).
- Josephine Wu Vuylsteke signed the Pew Research Center Taiwanese American statement on behalf of NATWA Hawaii Chapter: [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/pew-research-center-taiwanese-american-statement|Pew Research Center Taiwanese American statement]].
- Community ecosystem (corpus record): the Hawaii Taiwanese community maintained parallel chapter organizations documented alongside NATWA Hawaii in the TAH story corpus — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/hawaii-chapter-taa|美國台灣同鄉會夏威夷分會 (TAA Hawaii Chapter)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/21-hawaii-chapter-fapa|台灣人公共事務會夏威夷州分會 (FAPA Hawaii, recorded 2015-12-11)]], and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natma-hawaii-chapter|北美洲台灣人醫師協會夏威夷分會 (NATMA Hawaii Chapter, recorded 2015-12-25)]]. TAA Hawaii also published [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/formosan-quarterly-by-taahawaii-chapter|Formosan Quarterly 台僑]] and co-recorded the [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/honolulu-festival|Honolulu Festival]] with the community.
