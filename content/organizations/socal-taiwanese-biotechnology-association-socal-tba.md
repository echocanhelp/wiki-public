# SoCal Taiwanese Biotechnology Association SoCal TBA (南加台灣生物科技協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** SoCal Taiwanese Biotechnology Association SoCal TBA
- **Chinese:** 南加台灣生物科技協會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

SoCal Taiwanese Biotechnology Association (SoCal TBA) is a non-profit organization established in 2016 by Taiwanese professionals in bio-related fields, serving members from San Diego, Orange County, and Los Angeles areas. The organization connects Taiwanese biotechnology professionals in both academia and industry, and builds bridges between biotech communities in Southern California and Taiwan. It holds annual symposiums and workshops to promote local biotech communities, provide networking opportunities for small to medium start-ups, and foster connections between large firms and emerging companies. SoCal TBA also maintains close relationships with sister organizations including the Boston Taiwanese Biotechnology Association (BTBA) and Texas Taiwanese Biotechnology Association (TTBA).
