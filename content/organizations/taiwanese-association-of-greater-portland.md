# Taiwanese Association of Greater Portland (大波特蘭)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of Greater Portland
- **Chinese:** 大波特蘭
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of Greater Portland (TAGP) is a chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), serving the Taiwanese American community in the greater Portland, Oregon area. The chapter was founded in 1992 and became the 63rd chapter to join the national TAA organization. TAGP holds regular community events including Lunar New Year luncheons, summer gatherings, and annual participation in Taiwanese American Heritage Week.
