# Taiwanese American Professionals Austin

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Professionals Austin
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Taiwanese American Professionals Austin (TAP-ATX) is a chapter of the Taiwanese American Citizens League (TACL), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit run entirely by volunteers. Founded around 2015, TAP-ATX organizes social, cultural, and professional events for the Taiwanese American community in Austin, Texas. The chapter hosted the 2025 TACL National Convention, which also marked approximately ten years of the TAP program in Austin.
