# Taiwanese Student Business Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Student Business Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Student Business Association (TSBA) is a student organization at the UCLA Anderson School of Management. It is dedicated to enhancing the study experience of students with a Taiwanese background or those interested in Taiwanese culture, with a mission to serve as a cultural exchange hub and to promote meaningful dialogue between cultures. TSBA is listed as an active identity club on the Anderson school’s official clubs and associations directory.
