# Taiwanese Association of America Northern New Jersey (北澤西)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of America Northern New Jersey
- **Chinese:** 北澤西
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America Northern New Jersey (TAA-NNJ, 北澤西 台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations across the United States. The chapter serves Taiwanese Americans in the northern New Jersey area and is listed under the TAA’s East Northern (美東北) regional grouping.
