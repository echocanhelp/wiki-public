# Taiwanese Association of America Northern New Jersey (北澤西)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of America Northern New Jersey
- **Chinese:** 北澤西
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America Northern New Jersey (TAA-NNJ, 北澤西台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations across the United States. The chapter serves Taiwanese Americans in the northern New Jersey area and is listed under the TAA’s East Northern (美東北) regional grouping.

## Role in the Community

The corpus holds four TAA/NNJ community records:

- Chapter history: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/history-taannj|43. 北澤西台灣同鄉會簡史]] (2018-02-05) — the chapter's own concise history, held as community historical record.
- Activity record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/activities-of-taannj|北澤西台灣同鄉會的活動]] (2019-01-14).
- Education: hosted the 台灣語文教師研習會 (Taiwanese-language teachers' training seminar), recorded in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/projects-5-15|15. 台灣語文教師研習會 by 北澤西台灣同鄉會]] (2016-08-30), and ran the Pine Green Institutes 松青學院, recorded in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/pine-green-institutes|25. 松青學院 (北澤西)]] (2016-03-14).
- Youth employment: held its annual 留美台灣青年就業座談會 on 2016-04-16 at Courtyard Jersey City Newport for the greater-New-York-area Taiwanese students and young professionals. Panelists: 郭念慈律師（法律/移民法）、魏桂女會計師（商業界）、陳志明（科技）、柯金寅工程師（工業界）; over 40 Taiwanese students attended, with volunteer support from the NJIT, Stevens IT, and NYU Taiwanese student associations and coverage by 新唐人電視台 reporters led by 王姿懿; 紐約經文處 文教中心副主任張希賢 and 組長黃耀良 spoke. Recorded in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys219|Our Journeys #219]].
- Inter-community support: a Taiwan Pen (美洲台灣筆會) memoir recalls TAA/NNJ's 年會 and 台語教會郊外聯合禮拜/野宴 events being covered (photos and reports) by a pen-club member — evidence of chapter ties to the local language-church and literary circles. Recorded in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys12|Our Journeys #12]] (2014-09-05).
