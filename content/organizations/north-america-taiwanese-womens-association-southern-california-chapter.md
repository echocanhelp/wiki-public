# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Southern California Chapter (北美洲台灣婦女會南加分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Southern California Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣婦女會南加分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Southern California Chapter is a local chapter of the North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA), serving the Southern California area.

## Role in the Community
- The TAH directory corpus carries a dedicated chapter record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-southern-california-chapter|14. Southern California Chapter / NATWA 北美洲台灣婦女會南加分會]] (catalogued 2015-12-18, bibliographic record only).
- Parent body [[organizations/north-america-taiwanese-womens-association|NATWA]] was founded March 1988 in Los Angeles, so the Southern California chapter sits in NATWA's founding region; nationally NATWA is recorded as the Taiwanese American group with the most activity projects and the most members ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/239-natwa-e6-b4-bb-e5-8b-95-e9-a0-85-e7-9b-ae-e6-9c-80-e5-a4-9a-e7-9a-84-e5-85-a|239. NATWA 活動項目最多的全國性台美團體]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/240-natwa-e6-9c-83-e5-93-a1-e6-9c-80-e5-a4-9a-e7-9a-84-e5-85-a8-e5-9c-8b-e6-80-a|240. NATWA 會員最多的全國性台美團體]]). Chapter networking ran through the 聯誼通訊 newsletter ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/663-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|第七期, 1992/06]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/678-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|第二十二期, 2000/01]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/679-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|第二十三期, 2000/08]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/695-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|第三十九期, 2008/08]]).
- A separately catalogued listing page records this chapter as a group member of the Taiwan Center of Greater Los Angeles: [[organizations/natwa-southern-california|NATWA-SC (Taiwan Center group member)]].
- Chapter-hosted event (band A attribution in the record title): 牽手50年音樂會, held by the chapter itself in El Monte, CA on 06/10/2017 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts99|99. 牽手50年音樂會 by 北美洲臺灣婦女會南加分會]].
- Named chapter voice in national advocacy: Teresa Huang spoke for the chapter in the community response to Pew Research reports downplaying Taiwanese identity — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/pew-research-center-taiwanese-american-statement|Response to Pew Research Reports Hiding Taiwanese Identity]].
- Greater-LA coalition membership: the chapter is listed as a co-organizer/co-initiator of the Taiwan Center's 228 memorial series ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2026/20260209105852_大洛杉磯台灣會館二二八紀念會轉型正義研討會暨_27ac84aea15e3211|228紀念會轉型正義研討會協辦單位]], and [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2026/20260210173508_2021大洛杉磯台灣會館228事件線上追思紀念會與系列活_ac0c255ef64908a5|2021 線上追思紀念會友情宣傳名單]]) and of the 2021 South California 僑界 WHA joint statement ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20210616072940_2021_05_17_2021年南加州僑界支持台灣參與世界衛生大會wha聯合聲_b1d58af16c0a5e5b|2021 WHA 聯合聲明共同發起單位名單]])
- Joint community-service event at the Taiwan Center (Rosemead): on **2013-01-12** the chapter co-hosted "Taiwanese American Women Standing Up: A Community Service Event" with **NATWA II** at the Taiwan Center, 3001 Walnut Grove Ave., Rosemead, CA — with a keynote from the Asian Pacific Women's Center (APWC) on domestic violence in the API community and **Deana Chuang** of NATWA II on prosecuting domestic-violence crimes; documented in the community record ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/community-service-event-with-natwa-ii-in-socal|Community Service Event with NATWA II in SoCal, 2013-01, taiwaneseamerican.org band A]]).
- HOLD: the 2017 Hurricane Harvey relief donation of $21,090 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys328|Our Journeys 328, 2017/12]]) is credited to national NATWA, not this chapter.
