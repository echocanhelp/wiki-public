# Global Education Fund (美國環球教育基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Global Education Fund
- **Chinese:** 美國環球教育基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Global Education Fund (美國環球教育基金會) was established in April 1999. The organization promotes educational activities aimed at advancing global peace, including establishing academic grants to encourage scholarly research on international conflict and peaceful resolution methods. It provides scholarships and academic mentorship to support international students studying in the United States. The foundation also promotes global educational travel to foster mutual understanding among peoples, and seeks to establish a global research institute focused on the social, economic, cultural, and political integration of the world.
