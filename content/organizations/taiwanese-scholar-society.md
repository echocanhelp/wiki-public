# Taiwanese Scholar Society

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Scholar Society
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Scholar Society (TSS) at Carnegie Mellon University is a student organization registered on CMU’s TartanConnect platform and focused primarily on graduate students and scholars from Taiwan. Its stated goals include helping incoming Taiwanese students adapt to life in the United States, connecting the Taiwanese community at CMU, and promoting Taiwanese culture within the university. The organization maintains a Facebook page under the name “CMU Taiwanese Scholar Society” with approximately 249 followers.
