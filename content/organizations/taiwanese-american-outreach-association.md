# Taiwanese American Outreach Association (台美人交流協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Outreach Association
- **Chinese:** 台美人交流協會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Outreach Association (TAOA) is a volunteer-driven, not-for-profit Taiwanese American organization registered in the State of Maryland on October 29, 2004. TAOA is dedicated to promoting interaction and integration of Taiwanese Americans with mainstream America, encouraging volunteerism, and fostering understanding of Taiwanese culture and heritage. The organization sponsors and manages the annual Taiwanese American Independence Day Parade (TAIDP), originally initiated in May 2002, as well as the TA Cross-Community Volunteer (TACV) Program. TAOA also participates in cross-community events such as the National Cherry Blossom Parade in Washington, D.C. and the Montgomery County Fair, alongside other cultural, educational, and charitable activities.

## Role in the Community
- Sponsors the DC-area 『台美親善大使』Taiwanese American Friendship Ambassador selection contest, documented in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/dc-taiwanese-american-friendship-ambassador|30. 台美親善大使選拔賽]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/136-e8-8f-af-e5-ba-9c-e3-80-8c-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e4-ba-ba-e4-ba-a4-e6-b5-81-e5-8|136. 華府台美親善大使選拔賽 / 2015-05]].
- Per 謝榮春's memoir of the 2016 July 4 parade ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys232|232. 華府台美人參加2016年度獨立紀念日遊行記]]), the parade permit was secured by 台美人交流協會, the entry was co-organized with 費郡舞蹈藝術學院 (founder 李清秀, daughter 蔡玉慧) with logistics support from 華府台灣同鄉會 (TAA-GWC); the Taiwanese American Independence Day Parade Committee handled actual operations, with supporters incl. Charles Kuo, Gilbert Wang, Beatrice Tsai (蔡碧珠), 王能祥 (fundraising), 黃興貫 ([[people/sam-huang|黃興貫]]), and 陳明賢 (truck transport).
- HOLD: conflict in acronym — the 2016 memoir names the permit holder 台美人交流協會 as "Taiwanese American Interaction Association, TAIA", while the directory record says "Taiwanese American Outreach Association, TAOA".
- The TAH directory entry itself is held in the vault as its own work record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-outreach-association|Taiwanese American Outreach Association 台美人交流協會]] (matches the TAOA naming above; does not resolve the HOLD).
- The DC parade itself is documented in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/independence-day-parade-washington-dc|17. 華府美國獨立紀念日遊行 / 台美人獨立紀念日遊行團隊 (TAIDP Group)]] and in 陳美麗's memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys150|150. 驕傲的在遊行隊裡 (陳美麗, 2015-07)]]: the TAIDP Group was founded by 謝榮春 (former president of 華府台灣同鄉會) who secured participation for eight consecutive years (recounting the 2009 entry, "美國233歲的生日"); when the entry passed the reviewing stand the announcer read out 「Taiwanese American Interaction Association」 over the microphone, and the dance team was led by 李清秀 — corroborating the TAIA naming in the memoir above (HOLD stands; both names appear in primary material).
