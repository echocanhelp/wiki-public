# New York Taiwan Center (紐約台灣會館)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** New York Taiwan Center
- **Chinese:** 紐約台灣會館
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

New York Taiwan Center (紐約台灣會館) is a non-profit 501(c)(3) organization established in 1987 in Flushing, Queens, by donations from Taiwanese Americans in the Greater New York area. It is recognized as the first community center established by Taiwanese immigrants in the United States and is one of the largest Taiwanese American non-profit organizations in the country. The center serves as a hub connecting the Taiwanese diaspora with both the broader New York community and Taiwan, and is home to the Greater New York Taiwanese American Organizations Alliance, which links nearly thirty Taiwanese American organizations across New York and New Jersey.
