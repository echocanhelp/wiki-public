# Taiwanese American Ma Ma Class DC (大華府區台美人媽媽教室)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Ma Ma Class DC
- **Chinese:** 大華府區台美人媽媽教室
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Taiwanese American Ma Ma Class is a Washington DC-area organization founded by Taiwanese immigrant mothers to promote Taiwanese cuisine and culture within the local community. The group holds monthly gatherings featuring cooking and baking instruction, and with support from the Republic of China Overseas Community Affairs Council, annually invites renowned chefs from Taiwan for demonstration classes. Membership has remained at approximately 100 members annually, spanning first-generation immigrants as well as second-generation Taiwanese Americans. Beyond culinary arts, the organization has expanded its programming to include knitting workshops and medical lecture series. The group celebrated its 10th anniversary at the Washington DC Overseas Chinese Education Center, receiving congratulatory messages from Maryland Governor Larry Hogan and the OCAC Commissioner General.
