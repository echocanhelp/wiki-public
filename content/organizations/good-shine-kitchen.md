# Good Shine Kitchen (故鄉台灣料理)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Good Shine Kitchen · **Chinese:** 故鄉台灣料理 ("Good Shine" = transliteration of Hokkien *gū-shang*, motherland; motto "be of good cheer")
- **Era:** 1984/85 – present (LAT review 1985-06-06; CA SoS successor corp "Good Shine Kitchen, Inc" filed 2018-02-09)
- **Geography:** 235 S. Garfield Ave, Monterey Park, CA
- **Core roles:** Taiwanese-cuisine restaurant (+ founder's bakery and food shop); named hub of the 1980s LA Taiwanese community

## History
- Founded by [[people/ahhee-hsu||許丕龍 (Ahhee Hsu)]] — in LAT print as **"Leonard Hsu, poet, musician, conductor, entrepreneur and Taiwanese patriot"** ([LAT 1985-06-06](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1985-06-06-fo-7155-story.html)); restaurant described as activist gathering spot ([LAT 1985-07-21](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1985-07-21-mn-6906-story.html)). Local evidence archive: `knowledge/web-archives/latimes/leonard-hsu-monterey-park-trail.md`.
- **Sold to Jiping Sun** (owner-stated 2026-09-20; he is registered agent of the 2018 successor corporation at the same address). No family relation.
- Related founder business: **The Rainbow Group** LP (CA SoS filed 1984-07-25, 940 E. Garvey Ave, Monterey Park) — GPs Leonard Hsu + James Hsu; agent Judy Hsu. **Owner-stated 2026-09-20: James and Judy Hsu were business partners of 許丕龍, family name coincidental — NOT relatives.**

## Role in the Community
- Founder 許丕龍 (Ahhee Hsu) is recorded in the TAH Who's Who as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-2267-ahhee-hsu|2267. Ahhee Hsu 許丕龍]] (2020-05-04).
- He served on the 顧問 (advisor) list of the 「台美公民協會」 (TACR — Taiwanese American Citizens League/Association) Preparatory Committee, whose 顧問會議 was held in Cerritos (Apr 1985), alongside 吳澧培 (convener), 賴明詔, 蕭泰然 and other movement leaders — corroborating the restaurant's role as a movement gathering node within the broader LA-area network. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys186|Our Journeys 186 台美公民協會籌備報導]] (from 台灣公論報 369, 1985-04-15); English roster in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys186-eng|Our Journeys 186 (EN)]] — the TACR Preparatory Committee document itself dates the 籌備會 to April (1985), not "April 6" as printed in our vault copy — HOLD: "April 6" vs the 1985-04-15 公論報 issue date; never auto-merged.
