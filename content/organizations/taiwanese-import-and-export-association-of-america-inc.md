# Taiwanese Import and Export Association of America, Inc. (美國台灣人進出口公會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Import and Export Association of America, Inc.
- **Chinese:** 美國台灣人進出口公會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Import and Export Association of America is a business association founded by Taiwanese entrepreneurs in New York. The organization was informally active in the early 1980s before being formally established in 1991. It provides scholarships to encourage the children of Taiwanese Americans in their educational pursuits.
