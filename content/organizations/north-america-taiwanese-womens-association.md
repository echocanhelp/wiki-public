# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (北美洲臺灣婦女會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association
- **Chinese:** 北美洲臺灣婦女會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA) is a nonprofit organization founded in March 1988 in Los Angeles. Approximately 40 founding members elected 張富美 and 吳秀惠 as the first president and vice president. The organization is divided into five geographic regions with sixteen local chapters across the United States and Canada. NATWA holds an annual convention each April and a mid-year board meeting and leadership training camp each October. Since 1991, NATWA has published an annual calendar introducing Taiwanese history, geography, culture, and customs to an international audience. The organization also publishes a membership directory and a semi-annual magazine.

- Scholarship & founding record: NATWA is named as one of the host bodies of the community-wide Taiwanese American Community Scholarship Awards for local high-school seniors, run alongside 台灣同鄉會, 台灣會館, TAC and TAYA — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys303|T.A. - Note on naming: corpus pages use both 北美洲台灣婦女會 and 北美洲臺灣婦女會 for the same body (typography variance, not a conflict).
- Publishing record: the 聯誼通訊 magazine series is catalogued item-by-item in the corpus — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/663-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|聯誼通訊 第七期, 1992-06]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/678-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|第二十二期, 2000-01]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/679-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|第二十三期, 2000-08]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/695-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|第三十九期, 2008-08]] — consistent with the directory's statement that NATWA publishes a magazine (semi-annual).
- Military-family care (Kansas chapter, first-hand): NATWA-Kansas ran long-running "Care for soldiers" and "Care for wounded veterans" programs — monthly parcels to troops overseas, Christmas gifts for 10–15 wounded veterans' families, and emergency aid (rent, utilities, car loans) bridging wounded veterans until government benefits kicked in; the chapter received a U.S. flag that had flown over Iraq (2008-05), an Operation Iraqi Freedom award (2009), and a US Army Freedom Team Salute certificate from the Army Chief of Staff and Secretary of the Army (2010) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys137|Our Journeys 137 NATWA-堪薩斯愛心計劃]].
- Community health & visibility: NATWA was among the founding Taiwanese American groups of "Team Taiwan" at the Susan G. Komen Race for the Cure breast-cancer fund raiser; the NYC/NJ expansion was registered and captained by NATWA NYC chapter president 邱瑞惠 alongside TAA, WUFI, FAPA and FAHUR — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys6|Our Journeys 6「Team Taiwan」]].
- Book-donation service: NATWA's 贈書台灣計劃 shipped books to Taiwanese schools and libraries, documented by press coverage such as《書中包有台灣心》《春風化雨》 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys12|Our Journeys 12 筆會與台美社團]].
- Calendar holding: the corpus catalogue preserves the annual 北美洲台灣婦女會月曆 run for 1993–2018 (dozens of bound issues), corroborating the directory's "annual calendar since 1991" claim — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications555|555. 北美洲台灣婦女會月曆, 1993–2018]].
- Movement context: a community historian's survey places NATWA's 1988 founding in the wave of Taiwanese-American "ethnic politicization" after the 1979 高雄事件, alongside NATPA (1980), FAPA (1982) and the 全美臺灣客家會 (1988) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-357|Our Journeys 357 臺美人認同的形成]].
- Joint action with other groups: after Hurricane Harvey (2017) NATWA joined the TAH Foundation, 洛杉磯台灣會館, 聖地牙哥台灣會館 and 休士頓台聲合唱團 in donating ~$87,459 total to the Houston city Hurricane Harvey Relief Fund, NATWA's share $21,090 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys328|Our Journeys 328]]. NATWA was also one of the twelve groups (TAA, TACL, FAPA, TCCCNA, 人權會, NATMA, NATPA, 旅館公會, 客家會, 文藝協會, 學生會) that jointly canvassed ~12,000 Taiwanese households for the US census campaign in 1989-90 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys253|Our Journeys 253]].
