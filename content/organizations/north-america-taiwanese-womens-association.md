# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (北美洲臺灣婦女會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association
- **Chinese:** 北美洲臺灣婦女會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA) is a nonprofit organization founded in March 1988 in Los Angeles. Approximately 40 founding members elected 張富美 and 吳秀惠 as the first president and vice president. The organization is divided into five geographic regions with sixteen local chapters across the United States and Canada. NATWA holds an annual convention each April and a mid-year board meeting and leadership training camp each October. Since 1991, NATWA has published an annual calendar introducing Taiwanese history, geography, culture, and customs to an international audience. The organization also publishes a membership directory and a semi-annual magazine.

- Founding history (primary, NATWA's own account): 「咱的故事——NATWA的歷史」 by 吳秀惠 and 黃美惠 (2014-11) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys60|Our Journeys 60]] — records that NATWA grew out of 全美會 president 楊黃美幸's plan to add a women's department, with 呂秀蓮 (freshly released from imprisonment and brought to the U.S. in 1987) invited to lead the planning. At the 全美會 annual convention in Los Angeles, ~30 women from across North America met on 1988-03-13 and resolved to form an independent women's organization — electing 張富美 first president, 吳秀惠 vice president (and second president), with 呂秀蓮 as 名譽會長; the charter was ratified by 45 members in July 1988. The vice-president-succeeds-president rule and the 1992 mid-year board meeting/leadership-training practice (introduced under president 黃美惠, formalized by 陳麗英) both trace to this account. The annual 月曆 began when 呂秀莲 urged the idea at the 1990-03 convention; 郭榮桔 donated US$10,000 as seed money, the first calendar appeared 1990-11, and the first (1991) theme 「臺灣傳統建築之美」 was curated by 林郁子 — corroborating the directory's "annual calendar since 1991" with an earlier first-issue date. HOLD: the directory says five geographic regions / sixteen chapters; this founding account describes seven regions under the 1988 charter (美東、美西、美南、美平原、美東南、美中西、加拿大) — likely reorganization over time, not auto-merged.
- Named-president program record (from the same history): 方惠音 (first post-blacklist return delegation, 1992), 陳麗英 (institutionalized roles; 1994 世界婦女高峰會議), 葉錦如 (fund system, 「婦女與性」), 李素秋 (EQ lectures), 林郁子 (開源組/基金組, NATWA 會歌), 吳美芬 (選罷法, 獎學金組 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/29-natwa-scholarship-awards|29.
