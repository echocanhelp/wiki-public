# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (北美洲臺灣婦女會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association
- **Chinese:** 北美洲臺灣婦女會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA) is a nonprofit organization founded in March 1988 in Los Angeles. Approximately 40 founding members elected 張富美 and 吳秀惠 as the first president and vice president. The organization is divided into five geographic regions with sixteen local chapters across the United States and Canada. NATWA holds an annual convention each April and a mid-year board meeting and leadership training camp each October. Since 1991, NATWA has published an annual calendar introducing Taiwanese history, geography, culture, and customs to an international audience. The organization also publishes a membership directory and a semi-annual magazine.
