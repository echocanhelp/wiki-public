# Clear Lake Taiwanese Association (明湖台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Clear Lake Taiwanese Association
- **Chinese:** 明湖台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Clear Lake Taiwanese Association (明湖台灣同鄉會) is a local chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the Clear Lake area of Texas, a region southeast of Houston near NASA’s Johnson Space Center, and is listed under the TAA’s Southern (美南) regional grouping.
