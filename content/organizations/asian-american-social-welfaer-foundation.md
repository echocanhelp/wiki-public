# Asian American Social Welfaer Foundation

**Asian American Social Welfaer Foundation** (亞美社會福利基金會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** 亞美社會福利基金會
- **English (as listed):** Asian American Social Welfaer Foundation
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
