# East Bay Taiwanese Americans Community Service Center EBTACSC (東灣台美人社區服務中心)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** East Bay Taiwanese Americans Community Service Center EBTACSC
- **Chinese:** 東灣台美人社區服務中心
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The East Bay Taiwanese Americans Community Service Center (EBTACSC) is a nonprofit organization established in 2002 to provide diverse services to Taiwanese immigrants and the broader community in the San Francisco East Bay area. The center hosts educational seminars on medicine, health, professional expertise, and life wisdom, as well as Japanese language classes, interpersonal skills training, and mental health consultations. It also runs a popular gardening sharing program. Activities are held approximately twice a month and are free to attend, supported by voluntary donations from community members. The center is located at 1755 Sunnyvale Avenue, Walnut Creek, CA 94597.
