# 〈TASS-EB〉 (北加州東灣台美人長樂會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** 〈TASS-EB〉
- **Chinese:** 北加州東灣台美人長樂會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Senior Society – East Bay (TASS-EB) is a nonprofit, informal, membership-based private organization serving Taiwanese Americans aged 50 and above in the East Bay area of Northern California. Founded in October 2014 in Walnut Creek, the organization meets at the East Bay Taiwanese Christian Church and holds gatherings approximately four times per month. It operates through Special Interest Groups (SIGs), each led by a Coordinator and Assistant Coordinator, and publishes a monthly newsletter. The organization is governed by a board of seven directors elected by the membership, with a rotating leadership structure established beginning in 2018.
