# Happy Dancers Club

**Happy Dancers Club** (快樂排舞班) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** 快樂排舞班
- **English (as listed):** Happy Dancers Club
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
- **Activity (as listed):** line-dance class — 排舞 (line dance) in the Chinese name; two Taiwan Center class sections appear on the Center's own Clubs listing as 「Happy Line Dance Club / 快樂排舞班 (A)/(B)」 (alleged identity match, see GAPS)
