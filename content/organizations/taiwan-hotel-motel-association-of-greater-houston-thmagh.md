# Taiwan Hotel-Motel Association of Greater Houston THMAGH (休士頓美南台灣旅館公會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwan Hotel-Motel Association of Greater Houston THMAGH
- **Chinese:** 休士頓美南台灣旅館公會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwan Hotel-Motel Association of Greater Houston (THMAGH) was founded in 1991 by area Taiwanese hotel and motel owners. The association promotes cooperation among members through the sharing of information and experiences, and provides assistance on all aspects of the hospitality industry. Over the years, THMAGH has expanded beyond hotel/motel owners to include other area business associates, extending its influence into the broader business and Chinese-American community through networking, community service, and charitable activities. THMAGH has established sister associations with the Taiwan Hotel & Motel Association of Southern California, the Kaohsiung Hotel Association, and the Taipei Hotel Association.

- Sister ties to the Kaohsiung and Taipei hotel associations are asserted only in the TAH directory blurb; no corroborating corpus record found in the vault — treat as press-kit claim pending a source.
- Memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys29-eng|Our Journeys #29 (EN)]] credits community leader Kenjohn Wang with first founding the Taiwan Hotel-Motel Association and the Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce, then endowing the Taiwanese American Foundation with US$1M — the memoir's context (Rosemead estate donation to the Taiwan Center) suggests this refers to the Southern California association [[organizations/taiwan-hotel-motel-association-of-southern-california|THMASC]], not the 1991 Houston body; recorded as movement-lineage context only.
