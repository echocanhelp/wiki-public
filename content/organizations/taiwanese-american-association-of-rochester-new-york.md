# Taiwanese American Association Of Rochester, New York (羅徹斯特台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Association Of Rochester, New York
- **Chinese:** 羅徹斯特台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Association of Rochester, New York (羅徹斯特台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the greater Rochester, New York area and is classified under the TAA’s East Northern (美東北) regional grouping.

## Role in the Community

- Chapter history in the corpus: Rochester joined the Taiwanese Association of America during the term of TAA's second president Yi-Ho Cheng (1970-07-01 to 1972-06-30), one of six local associations (Minneapolis, Baton Rouge, Dallas, Lawrence, Buffalo, Rochester) that affiliated during that term; TAA counted 34 chapters by its end. The Rochester organizer is named in the record as Cheng-hsiao Wu: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76-eng|Our Journeys (EN) — TAA chapter history]].
- Own TAH directory record preserved in the corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/rochester-chapter-taa|Taiwanese Association of Rochester (TAR) 羅徹斯特台灣同鄉會]] (published 2015-11-09).
- Affiliated community group: the 愛鄉合唱團 (Taiwanese Choral Society of Rochester), formed 1993 by Taiwanese singers, rehearsed biweekly, performed at 同鄉會 events and in the wider community (Toronto, Cornell University, Rochester sister-city celebrations; shared stage with the Rochester Chamber Orchestra in May 2014 alongside a ~150-child chorus), typically conducted and accompanied by Eastman School of Music Taiwanese students — corpus record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/15-e7-be-85-e5-be-b9-e6-96-af-e7-89-b9-e8-87-ba-e7-81-a3-e5-90-8c-e9-84-95-e6-9c|15. 羅徹斯特臺灣同鄕會愛鄉合唱團]] (band A, 2014-12-22).
- Founding backdrop in the student-migration wave: Our Journeys 185 records that in the US-study boom of the late 1960s (六十年代後期), East-coast cities — Boston, New York, Albany, **Rochester**, Syracuse, Ithaca — were the most sought-after destinations, and local 台灣同鄉會相繼成立 as the regular gathering places for new students ("留學生的大家庭"), with ping-pong and baseball as the familiar ways to meet 鄉親: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys185|Our Journeys 185]]. This situates the Rochester chapter's origin in the same late-1960s/early-1970s chapter-building wave as its 1970–72 affiliation under 鄭義和 above.
- Rochester-adjacent community record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/firstfamous-389-prof-mei-chu-wang-hsiao|389. Prof. 王美珠教授 — first Taiwanese American woman Ph.D. in Economics (Univ. of Rochester 1967)]].
