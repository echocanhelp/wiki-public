# Coalition of Taiwan Independence’s (台灣建國聯合陣線)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Coalition of Taiwan Independence’s
- **Chinese:** 台灣建國聯合陣線
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Coalition of Taiwan Independence (台灣建國聯合陣線) was a broad united front organization established on December 15, 1979, bringing together multiple Taiwanese independence and democracy organizations from around the world. Member organizations included the World United Formosans for Independence, the Formosan Association, the Overseas Alliance of Taiwan Democratic Movement, the Taiwan Christian Self-Determination Movement, the Independent Taiwan Association, the Formosa Magazine, and the Chao Liu Magazine. Representatives included prominent figures such as Peng Ming-min, Kuo Yu-hsin, Huang Chang-hui, Shih Ming, Hsu Hsin-liang, and Chen Wan-jen. The coalition represented a significant expansion and consolidation of the Taiwanese democratic movement to Taiwanese communities worldwide.
