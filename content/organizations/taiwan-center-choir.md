# Taiwan Center Choir

**Taiwan Center Choir** (台灣會館合唱團) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** 台灣會館合唱團
- **English (as listed):** Taiwan Center Choir
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
