# United Formosans for Independence UFI (台獨聯盟)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** United Formosans for Independence UFI
- **Chinese:** 台獨聯盟
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

World United Formosans for Independence (WUFI) is a global advocacy organization dedicated to the establishment of a free, democratic, and independent Republic of Taiwan based on the principle of self-determination. It was formed in 1970 through the merger of four overseas independence movement groups: the Taiwan Youth Independence League (Japan, est. 1960), the Taiwanese Human Rights Committee of Canada (est. 1964), the United Formosans for Independence in America (est. 1966), and the European Taiwanese Independence League (est. 1967), along with the Taiwan Freedom League on the island. In 1987, the organization was renamed to its current form and expanded its headquarters structure to include chapters in the United States, Japan, Canada, Europe, and South America. WUFI is committed to the fundamental freedoms and human rights embodied in the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and repudiates all forms of foreign dominance and interventions contrary to the interests of the Taiwanese people.
