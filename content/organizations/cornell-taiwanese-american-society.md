# Cornell Taiwanese American Society

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Cornell Taiwanese American Society
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Cornell Taiwanese American Society (CTAS) is a registered student organization at Cornell University in Ithaca, New York. It describes itself as a non-political social club that aims to celebrate Taiwanese culture within the Cornell community, providing a welcoming environment and support network while sharing Taiwanese culture through events and activities.
