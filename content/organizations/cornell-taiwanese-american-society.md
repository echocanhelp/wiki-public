# Cornell Taiwanese American Society

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Cornell Taiwanese American Society
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Cornell Taiwanese American Society (CTAS) is a registered student organization at Cornell University in Ithaca, New York. It describes itself as a non-political social club that aims to celebrate Taiwanese culture within the Cornell community, providing a welcoming environment and support network while sharing Taiwanese culture through events and activities.

## Role in the Community
- Context from the Ithaca community memoir (band A, Mei-Hsi Chen, published 2017-08-25): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys310-eng|310. 伊薩卡臺灣同鄉會的簡介 / Brief History of the Taiwanese Association of America, Ithaca, NY]] records the earlier Taiwanese student movement at Cornell — from the 1960s gatherings around Taiwanese students (Joseph H. Chen, Ph.D., arrived 1965) to the "Cornell University Taiwanese Student's Association," which at its height counted nearly 300 members with families.
- Related Cornell, NY record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys110-eng|110 (Our Journeys) — Cornell, NY entry]] notes the Nov. 1991 founding of the Taiwan Hakka Association for Public Affairs in North America in connection with Cornell, NY.
- HOLD: CTAS (self-described non-political social club, contemporary) vs the historic "Cornell University Taiwanese Student's Association" / TAA Ithaca chapter in the memoir — distinct-era organizations on the same campus; not auto-merged as one lineage.
