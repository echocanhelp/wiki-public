# Tzu Chi USA Southern Region (慈濟美國南部區)

The Southern Region of **Tzu Chi USA** (Buddhist Tzu Chi Foundation's US branch), a Buddhist
humanitarian organization. Its Southern Region Office with **Jing Si Hall (靜思堂)** is at
**6200 Corporate Dr., Houston, TX 77036**.

> Source for all facts on this page until otherwise noted: Tzu Chi USA official event page
> https://tzuchi.us/houston/concert (fetched 2026-11-04), archived at
> `knowledge/web-archives/tzuchi-us-houston-concert.md`.

## Identity Snapshot

- **Org:** Tzu Chi USA Southern Region (region of Tzu Chi USA / Buddhist Tzu Chi Foundation)
- **Office / hall:** Southern Region Office, Jing Si Hall (靜思堂)
- **Address:** 6200 Corporate Dr., Houston, TX 77036
- **Mission line (as published):** "From horizon to horizon, Tzu Chi volunteers strive to
