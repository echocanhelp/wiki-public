Status: sourced (third-party news). Source: https://www.thelantern.com/2024/04/taiwanese-american-student-association-night-market-to-highlight-asian-american-cultures/

Quoted officers: Sharon Yeh, Alex Pham. Status: sourced (third-party news). Source: https://www.thelantern.com/2018/02/traditional-taiwanese-night-market-gets-daytime-makeover/

Status: alleged (first-party self-report, live site). Source: https://tasaohiostate.weebly.com/archive.html
