# Wellbrook Foundation (美國美溪慈善基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Wellbrook Foundation
- **Chinese:** 美國美溪慈善基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Wellbrook Foundation is a charitable foundation established in the United States in 1989 by Dr. Chung-Chi Chou, who serves as its president. The foundation focuses on supporting educational development and talent cultivation, with a particular emphasis on developing nations.
