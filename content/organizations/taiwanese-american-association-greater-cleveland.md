# Taiwanese American Association Greater Cleveland (克里夫蘭)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Association Greater Cleveland
- **Chinese:** 克里夫蘭
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Association Greater Cleveland (克里夫蘭 台灣同鄉會) is the Greater Cleveland chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations across the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the greater Cleveland, Ohio area.

## Role in the Community
The Cleveland chapter was one of the **28 founding chapters** of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), with 翁讀龍 (Tu-lung Weng) listed as its founding president in the TAA founding record ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/cleveland-chapter-taa|Cleveland Chapter / TAA 全美台灣同鄉會克里夫蘭分會]]; ZH counterpart in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/22-e5-85-8b-e9-87-8c-e5-a4-ab-e8-98-ad-e4-ba-9e-e5-a4-aa-e7-af-80-e6-b4-bb-e5-8b|克里夫蘭亞太節活動]]). TAA's founding mission — promoting friendship, inter-chapter coordination, and advocacy on Taiwanese social, economic, cultural, legal, and human-rights issues — frames the chapter's role in Greater Cleveland.

Cleveland's Taiwanese institutional ecosystem also includes parallel organizations documented in the corpus: NATMA's Cleveland chapter ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natma-cleveland-chapter|NATMA 北美洲台灣人醫師協會克里夫蘭分會]] — described as a birthplace of NATMA's early 1983–1990 activity), the Formosan Music Fund of Cleveland ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/34-formosan-music-fund-of-cleveland-e5-85-8b-e5-9f-8e-e5-bf-83-e8-81-b2-e5-90-88|克里夫蘭心聲合唱團]]), and the Taiwanese Foundation of Greater Cleveland ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-foundation-of-greater-cleveland|Taiwanese Foundation of Greater Cleveland]]).
