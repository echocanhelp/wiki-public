# TPC (disambiguation)

**TPC** on GSTPC/ITPC sites is **not** [[organizations/presbyterian-church-in-taiwan|PCT]].

| Label | Meaning | Polity |
|---|---|---|
| **PCT** | 台灣基督長老教會 — Taiwan General Assembly | Taiwan |
| **TPC** | 南加州 / North American **Taiwanese Presbyterian Churches** fellowship (GSTPC menu: TPC 主日直播, TPC50週年, 南加州台灣人教會聯合禱告會) | Fellowship, not a denomination |
| **NTPC** | [[organizations/national-taiwanese-presbyterian-council\|National Taiwanese Presbyterian Council]] | PC(USA) council of US Taiwanese churches |
| **PC(USA)** | US denomination of record for FPCLA / GSTPC / ITPC | Los Ranchos and other presbyteries |

## Established PCT → TPC links (what we actually have)

Strong: people and books (黃德利 台神; seven PCT pastors at GSTPC plant; 劉炳熹 / East Gate; 潘恩盛 PCT moderator on Lai reprint; STM Taiwan; 台北重新姊妹).

Weak: **no shared polity**. US churches are PC(USA). Do not write “GSTPC is a PCT congregation.”
