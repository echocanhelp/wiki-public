# TPC — Taiwanese Presbyterian Churches (U.S., not PCT)

## How to read the letters

## Identity Snapshot
- **Type:** disambiguation
- **Rule:** PC + place = denomination in that country. T+PC = Taiwanese church in the U.S.

**PC + place** = the denomination *in that country*.  
**T + PC** = *Taiwanese* Presbyterian Church(es) — the T is the people/language, so the building is in the **U.S.** (otherwise you would not need the T).

| Initials | Read as | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **PCT** | **P**resbyterian **C**hurch in **T**aiwan | Taiwan |
| **PC(USA)** | **P**resbyterian **C**hurch **(U.S.A.)** | United States |
| **TPC** | **T**aiwanese **P**resbyterian **C**hurches | United States (fellowship) |
| **NTPC** | **N**ational **T**aiwanese **P**resbyterian **C**ouncil | United States (PC(USA) council) |
| **GSTPC** | **G**ood **S**hepherd **T**aiwanese **P**resbyterian **C**hurch | Monterey Park, U.S. |
| **ITPC** | **I**rvine **T**aiwanese **P**resbyterian **C**hurch | Orange County, U.S. |
| **FPCLA** | **F**ormosan **P**resbyterian **C**hurch in **L**os **A**ngeles | Los Angeles, U.S. (1970 first) |

**TPC 50週年 (2020)** = fifty years of Taiwanese Presbyterian Churches **in North America** (from FPCLA 1970). Not fifty years of the Presbyterian Church in Taiwan (1865 / 1951).

**台福 / EFC** (Evangelical Formosan Church) is another U.S. family. Do not merge with FPCLA / TPC.

## Link we actually have

**Strong:** people and books — 黃德利 at 台灣神學院; seven Presbyterian Church in Taiwan pastors at the Good Shepherd plant; 劉炳熹 / East Gate Barclay; 潘恩盛 (Presbyterian Church in Taiwan moderator) on the Lai reprint; STM Taiwan (short-term mission); sister tie with 台北重新教會.

**Weak:** **no shared polity.** U.S. churches are Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.). Do not write “Good Shepherd is a Presbyterian Church in Taiwan congregation.”
