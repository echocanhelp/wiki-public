# TPC — Taiwanese Presbyterian Churches (not PCT)

## Identity Snapshot
- **Type:** disambiguation of initials
- **Do not flatten:** Presbyterian Church in Taiwan ≠ Taiwanese Presbyterian Churches (U.S. fellowship) ≠ National Taiwanese Presbyterian Council ≠ Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.)

Same three letters do **not** mean the same church.

| Initials | Spell-out | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **PCT** | **P**resbyterian **C**hurch in **T**aiwan · 台灣基督長老教會 | Denomination **in Taiwan**. General Assembly in Taiwan. [pct.org.tw](https://www.pct.org.tw/) |
| **TPC** | **T**aiwanese **P**resbyterian **C**hurches · 南加州 / 北美台灣人長老教會 | **U.S. fellowship** of Taiwanese-language Presbyterian congregations. GSTPC menu: TPC 主日直播, TPC 50週年, 南加州台灣人教會聯合禱告會. **Not a denomination.** |
| **NTPC** | **N**ational **T**aiwanese **P**resbyterian **C**ouncil · 美國基督長老教會全國台灣人教會聯合會 | **U.S. council** of those churches under Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.). [ntpc-usa.org](https://www.ntpc-usa.org/). **Not** New Taipei City. |
| **PC(USA)** | **P**resbyterian **C**hurch **(U.S.A.)** | The **U.S. denomination** that actually holds GSTPC / ITPC / FPCLA (e.g. Los Ranchos Presbytery). |
| **GSTPC** | **G**ood **S**hepherd **T**aiwanese **P**resbyterian **C**hurch · 好牧者台灣基督長老教會 | Monterey Park congregation. |
| **ITPC** | **I**rvine **T**aiwanese **P**resbyterian **C**hurch · 爾灣台灣基督長老教會 | Orange County congregation (chapel at Geneva Presbyterian Church). |
| **FPCLA** | **F**ormosan **P**resbyterian **C**hurch in **L**os **A**ngeles · 洛杉磯台灣基督長老教會 | First North American Taiwanese Presbyterian church (1970). |
| **EFC / 台福** | **E**vangelical **F**ormosan **C**hurch · 台福基督教會 | **Different family.** Do not merge with FPCLA / TPC. |

**TPC 50週年 (2020)** = fifty years of **Taiwanese Presbyterian Churches in North America** from FPCLA (1970).  
**Not** fifty years of the Presbyterian Church in Taiwan (that clock is 1865 / 1951).

## Link we actually have

**Strong:** people and books — 黃德利 at 台灣神學院; seven Presbyterian Church in Taiwan pastors at the Good Shepherd plant; 劉炳熹 / East Gate Barclay; 潘恩盛 (Presbyterian Church in Taiwan moderator) on the Lai reprint; STM Taiwan (short-term mission); sister tie with 台北重新教會.

**Weak:** **no shared polity.** U.S. churches are Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.). Do not write “Good Shepherd is a Presbyterian Church in Taiwan congregation.”
