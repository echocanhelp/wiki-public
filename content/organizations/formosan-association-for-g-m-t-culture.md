# FORMOSAN ASSOCIATION FOR G.M.T. CULTURE (台灣人牛罵頭文史拹會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** FORMOSAN ASSOCIATION FOR G.M.T. CULTURE
- **Chinese:** 台灣人牛罵頭文史拹會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Formosan Association for G.M.T. Culture is a Los Angeles-based organization founded by immigrants from the Qingshui (Cingshuei) district of Taiwan, historically known as ‘Gu-mah-thau’ (牛罵頭, G.M.T.), a name derived from the Papora indigenous community of Gomach. The organization was established on May 20, 2016, to promote awareness of Qingshui’s cultural heritage, history, and tourism. ‘G.M.T.’ stands for ‘Good Mar Town,’ an English rendering meaning ‘a settlement with good water,’ reflecting the region’s historic identity as a water-rich and culturally vibrant community. The association aims to document and share the rich historical, cultural, and civic legacy of the Qingshui area, including its indigenous roots dating back 4,500–3,400 years, its agricultural development, and its democratic and artistic traditions.
