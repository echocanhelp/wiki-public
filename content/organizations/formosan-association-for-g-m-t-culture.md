# FORMOSAN ASSOCIATION FOR G.M.T. CULTURE (台灣人牛罵頭文史拹會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** FORMOSAN ASSOCIATION FOR G.M.T. CULTURE
- **Chinese:** 台灣人牛罵頭文史拹會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Formosan Association for G.M.T. Culture is a Los Angeles-based organization founded by immigrants from the Qingshui (Cingshuei) district of Taiwan, historically known as ‘Gu-mah-thau’ (牛罵頭, G.M.T.), a name derived from the Papora indigenous community of Gomach. The organization was established on May 20, 2016, to promote awareness of Qingshui’s cultural heritage, history, and tourism. ‘G.M.T.’ stands for ‘Good Mar Town,’ an English rendering meaning ‘a settlement with good water,’ reflecting the region’s historic identity as a water-rich and culturally vibrant community. The association aims to document and share the rich historical, cultural, and civic legacy of the Qingshui area, including its indigenous roots dating back 4,500–3,400 years, its agricultural development, and its democratic and artistic traditions.

- Founding purpose (self-stated): 让世界认识清水文化及历史、宣導清水觀光旅遊維新 — `alleged` per the same ZH post.
- Name etymology (self-stated): 清水昔稱「牛罵頭」，原為平埔族拍瀑拉族（Papora）牛罵社（Gomach）之社域，「牛罵」即 Gomach 音譯；G.M.T. = Good Mar Town，Mar 是「水」的意思，指有好水的聚落 — `alleged` per the same ZH post.
- 1920 (大正九年): 因鰲峰山麓下有湧泉「埤仔口水」清澈可鑑，牛罵頭改名「清水」 — `alleged` per the same ZH post.
- 4500~3400 年前: 漢移民入墾前，清水海岸平原與大肚山緩坡一帶已有原住民農業漁獵生活，形成牛罵頭文化 — `alleged` per the same ZH post.
- Pre-war development (self-stated): 地方仕紳楊澄若等開發埤子潭、掘建五福圳引大甲溪水創豐腴水利；塭子寮海事通航；以觀音亭、媽祖間寺廟為中心的南、北管子弟戲與藝術學風興盛 — `alleged` per the same ZH post.
- 1960s self-image: 清水人以台灣文化重鎮自許，民主思潮啟蒙較早 — `alleged` per the same ZH post.
- Post-1960s grievance list (self-stated): 賣掉埤子口水源、放棄梧棲港開發商機、假都市計畫之名把楊肇嘉祖宅切半開出百餘公尺無意義的路、無意識污染水源 — `alleged` per the same ZH post.
- Planned program (self-stated): 對故鄉扮演「後視鏡」、提示本土意識、推動返鄉聯誼、鼓勵協助故鄉社團、舉辦論壇、培養下一代在地關懷、研究故鄉文物 — `alleged` per the same ZH post.
- Topics slated for research (self-stated): 義渡碑、五福圳、各族姓發展史、越戰油管史跡、宗教師、民間信仰，成果結集發表向海外公佈推展 — `alleged` per the same ZH post.
