# The Life Enrichment Club (暢樂人生社)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** The Life Enrichment Club
- **Chinese:** 暢樂人生社
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Life Enrichment Club is a discussion group organized on June 22, 2009, by Sung-Peng Hsu and others who shared an interest in talks about life, death, and Eastern philosophies including Taoism, Buddhism, and Confucianism. The club brought together members to explore a wide range of intellectual topics including philosophies of life, chaos theory, genetics, DNA and human migrations, Mahayana Buddhism, astronomy, geology, and the arts. Sung-Peng Hsu served as coordinator from the club’s founding through the end of 2014. Five summary articles documenting club activities from 2009 through 2014 were produced and made available online.
