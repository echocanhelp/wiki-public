# Shee Highland Musée in IL (施哲三海嵐美術舘)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Shee Highland Musée in IL
- **Chinese:** 施哲三海嵐美術舘
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Shee Highland Musée is the artistic institution associated with international artist Shee, who specializes in oil painting and sculpture. His work is created across three cultural geographic locations: Paris, Chicago, and Taipei. The musée showcases art that spans a range of forms and styles, resonating with art enthusiasts worldwide. Dr. Gloria Groom of the Art Institute of Chicago has praised his work for communicating joy through color, shape, and a deep appreciation of the human body. The institution reflects a philosophy that culture is foundational to civilization and human existence.
