# Taiwanese American Science and Technology Association TASTA (華府台灣產業科技協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Science and Technology Association TASTA
- **Chinese:** 華府台灣產業科技協會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

TASTA is a civil and non-profit organization founded in May 2002 by Taiwanese American professionals in the Greater Washington, D.C. area. Its primary focus is promoting cooperation between Americans and the people of Taiwan in science, technology, and industry through education, research, communication, and networking activities. Members include professionals from high-tech companies, entrepreneurial ventures, government and defense research labs, and universities, most holding advanced degrees in science and engineering. TASTA is also affiliated with the U.S.-Taiwan Business Council.
