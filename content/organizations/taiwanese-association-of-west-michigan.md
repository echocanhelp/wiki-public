# Taiwanese Association of West Michigan (西密西根台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of West Michigan
- **Chinese:** 西密西根台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of West Michigan (西密西根台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization serving Taiwanese American communities across the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the West Michigan area and is listed under the TAA’s Mid-Western (美中西) regional grouping.

## Role in the Community
The corpus does not yet hold chapter-level records (officers, events) for this chapter; its TAH directory listing remains the primary attribution. Regional context: the West Michigan Taiwanese community dates at least to the 1980s — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys74|Our Journeys #74]] records that the North American Taiwanese Medical Association (NATMA, 北美洲台灣人醫師協會) was founded on 1984-07-14 at Western Michigan University in Ypsilanti, Michigan, by ~40 physicians including local members 黃昭聲 and 陳英三, with keynote talks by 林宗義 and 林成德. The chapter's later formation sits within this established West Michigan community base.

HOLD: no corpus source dates the chapter's own founding; do not infer it from the 1984 NATMA event.
