# Taiwanese American Professionals Orange County (台美菁英協會橙縣分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Professionals Orange County
- **Chinese:** 台美菁英協會橙縣分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Taiwanese American Professionals Orange County (TAP-OC) is a chapter of the Taiwanese American Citizens League (TACL), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit run entirely by volunteers. Founded in January 2018, TAP-OC serves the Taiwanese American community in Orange County, California through social events, community service, cultural celebrations, and wellness activities. The chapter is inclusive, welcoming people of all cultures and backgrounds.
