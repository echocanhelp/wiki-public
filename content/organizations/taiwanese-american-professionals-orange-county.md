# Taiwanese American Professionals Orange County (台美菁英協會橙縣分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Professionals Orange County
- **Chinese:** 台美菁英協會橙縣分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Taiwanese American Professionals Orange County (TAP-OC) is a chapter of the Taiwanese American Citizens League (TACL), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit run entirely by volunteers. Founded in January 2018, TAP-OC serves the Taiwanese American community in Orange County, California through social events, community service, cultural celebrations, and wellness activities. The chapter is inclusive, welcoming people of all cultures and backgrounds.

## Role in the Community
- TAP-OC is the Orange County chapter of Taiwanese American Professionals (TAP) 台美菁英協會, the national TACL-affiliated professional network documented in the TAH community record ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-professionals-tap|TAP 台美菁英協會]], 2014).
- Sibling TAP chapters recorded in the same corpus: Boston ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/boston-chapter-tap|波士頓分會]]), Austin ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/austin-chapter-tap|奧斯丁(德州)分會]]), D.C. ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/d-c-chapter-tap|華府分會]]); TAP chapters also run scholarship programs (e.g. [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/35-taiwanese-american-professionals-of-seattle-scholarship-award|西雅圖台美菁英協會獎學金]], 2015).
- The chapter's own community activities are held as a primary community record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/activities-of-orange-county-chapter-tap|台美菁英協會橙縣分會的活動]] (published 2019-01-24).
