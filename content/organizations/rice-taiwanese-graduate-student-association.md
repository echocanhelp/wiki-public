# Rice Taiwanese Graduate Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Rice Taiwanese Graduate Student Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Rice Taiwanese Graduate Student Association (RTGSA) is a student organization at Rice University serving master’s, doctoral, and postdoctoral Taiwanese students. Its stated purpose centers on professional support — helping members prepare for job recruiting — as well as fostering connections with local Taiwanese community groups in the Houston area. The organization is registered on OwlNest, Rice’s official student engagement platform, which serves as its primary public listing. No dedicated website, Instagram, or Facebook page was identified through available first-party sources.

## Role in the Community
- No direct RTGSA mention found in the memoir/work corpus (grep for 萊斯/賴斯/Rice targets the older Houston student generation, not this club). Contextual record: Taiwanese student organizing at Rice goes back at least to the 2012 re-launch documented in [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/taiwanese-student-association-at-rice-university-first-general-meeting|Taiwanese Student Association at Rice University First General Meeting]] (2012-08-30).
- Sister campus club: [[organizations/rice-taiwanese-association|Rice Taiwanese Association]] (undergraduate cultural/social, ITASA member). RTGSA is the graduate-level counterpart focused on career support and Houston community ties.
