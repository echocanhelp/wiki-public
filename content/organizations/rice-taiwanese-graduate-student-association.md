# Rice Taiwanese Graduate Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Rice Taiwanese Graduate Student Association
- **Chinese:** Rice 台灣研究生學生會 (per PTT posts 2024-03-18 / 2025-03-29); constitution self-name: 「Rice Taiwanese Student Association (Rice TSA)」 (2015-09-28 revision)
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Rice Taiwanese Graduate Student Association (RTGSA) is a student organization at Rice University serving master’s, doctoral, and postdoctoral Taiwanese students. Its stated purpose centers on professional support — helping members prepare for job recruiting — as well as fostering connections with local Taiwanese community groups in the Houston area. The organization is registered on OwlNest, Rice’s official student engagement platform, which serves as its primary public listing. No dedicated website, Instagram, or Facebook page was identified through available first-party sources.

A second paragraph of the same directory record adds that RTGSA is listed among Rice's diversity-focused student organizations in the university's library research guide and is recognized by the Rice University Community of Asian Alumni (RUCAA) as part of the campus Asian student organization ecosystem, and states that its founding year is not documented in available first-party sources. Full verbatim capture: [[sources/rice-taiwanese-graduate-student-association-tah-organization-capture|TAH org directory capture — RTGSA]].

The club's own constitution, hosted on the Rice OwlNest/Campus Labs platform, names it 「Rice Taiwanese Student Association (Rice TSA)」 and describes it as the official cultural club on Rice campus for Taiwanese and Taiwanese-American graduate students, with study breaks (mahjong, dumplings, brush-painting, shaved ice, red bean soup, mooncakes), Houston Chinatown restaurant trips, travel to the Texas A&M Taiwanese association, and ITASA conferences. See [[sources/rtgsa-rice-thirdparty-captures-2026-11-16|RTGSA @ Rice — third-party captures (2026-11-16)]].

- The club's constitution places it in the intercollegiate Taiwanese student circuit: travel to the Texas A&M Taiwanese association and ITASA conferences.
- No direct RTGSA mention found in the memoir/work corpus (grep for 萊斯/賴斯/Rice targets the older Houston student generation, not this club). Contextual record: Taiwanese student organizing at Rice goes back at least to the 2012 re-launch documented in [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/taiwanese-student-association-at-rice-university-first-general-meeting|Taiwanese Student Association at Rice University First General Meeting]] (2012-08-30).
- Sister campus club: [[organizations/rice-taiwanese-association|Rice Taiwanese Association]] (undergraduate cultural/social, ITASA member). RTGSA is the graduate-level counterpart focused on career support and Houston community ties.
- HOLD: relationship between RTGSA, the RTA 「Rice TSA」 constitution self-name, and the 2012-era TSA at Rice is not settled in the corpus; not merged. Naming flag: PTT titles alternate 「Rice TGSA」/「Rice TGSS」 while the body always says RTGSA.
