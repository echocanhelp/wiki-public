# Westchester Taiwanese Women’s Association WTWA (威郡台灣婦女會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Westchester Taiwanese Women’s Association WTWA
- **Chinese:** 威郡台灣婦女會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Westchester Taiwanese Women’s Association (WTWA) is a nonprofit, non-political organization founded in the fall of 1974 by Taiwanese women in the Westchester County area of New York, approximately 30 miles northeast of Manhattan. The organization holds monthly gatherings from September through June at rotating host family homes, featuring programs on topics such as family life, children’s education, finance, arts, health, and Taiwanese cuisine. WTWA has actively engaged in cultural exchange with local organizations such as the Scarsdale Woman’s Club, presenting Taiwanese music, folk songs, and puppet theater performances. The association also participates in broader community events including Taiwanese American Heritage Week activities, the Passport to Taiwan event in Manhattan, and the Susan G. Komen Race for the Cure as part of Team Taiwan. In 1999, to celebrate its 25th anniversary, WTWA organized a charity piano concert that raised funds for the Taiwan Center’s Steinway Concert Grand Piano and the Music Conservatory of Westchester Scholarship Fund.
