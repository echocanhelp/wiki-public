The Taiwanese Graduate Student Association (TGSA) at UC Irvine is a graduate student organization serving Taiwanese graduate students at the university. Known in Chinese as UCI台灣研究生學生會, TGSA operates a Google Sites page and a Facebook Group (facebook.com/groups/ucitgsa), both of which have minimal current public-facing content.
