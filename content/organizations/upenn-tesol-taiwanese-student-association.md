# UPenn TESOL Taiwanese Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** UPenn TESOL Taiwanese Student Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The UPenn TESOL Taiwanese Student Association (UTTSA) is a Taiwanese student organization housed within the University of Pennsylvania’s Graduate School of Education, listed in the PAACH Student Communities directory. Its focus is specific to students in the TESOL (Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages) program. According to the PAACH listing, UTTSA provides a forum for students to discuss combining TESOL theory with practice, improving English skills, and managing coursework — while also coordinating networking opportunities with alumni around career experiences and doctoral application processes.
