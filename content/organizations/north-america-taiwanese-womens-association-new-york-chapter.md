# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – New York Chapter (北美洲台灣婦女會紐約分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – New York Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣婦女會紐約分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – New York Chapter is a local chapter of the North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA), serving the New York area.

## Role in the Community
Corpus evidence covers the parent body NATWA (北美洲台灣婦女會); no New York–chapter-specific record found in the vault corpus yet.
