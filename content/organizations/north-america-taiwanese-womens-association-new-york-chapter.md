# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – New York Chapter (北美洲台灣婦女會紐約分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – New York Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣婦女會紐約分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – New York Chapter is a local chapter of the North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA), serving the New York area.

## Role in the Community
The New York Chapter has its own records in the TAH corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-new-york-chapter|10. New York Chapter / NATWA 北美洲台灣婦女會紐約分會]] (2015-12-22). The chapter is known for annual member art exhibitions — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/art-show-16|16. 詩情(琴)畫藝畫展 by NATWA/NY (04/2017)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/art-show-28|28. 紐約分會會員畫展, Flushing, NY (05/2018)]], and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/36-art-exhibition|36. 婦女會紐約分會第九屆年度藝術展&講座 (2019-05-04)]]. In electoral/political organizing, 邱瑞惠 (NATWA NYC chapter president) registered and captained the expanded New York "Team Taiwan" team for the NYC Race for the Cure, co-working with NATWA II, TAA, WUFI, FAPA and FAHUR: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys6|6. 加入 Team Taiwan 為臺灣加油 (葉寶桂, 2014-09)]].
Corpus evidence for the parent body NATWA (北美洲台灣婦女會):
- NATWA published the 聯誼通訊 newsletter (issues archived in the corpus from 1992 to 2000): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/663-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|663. 北美洲台灣婦女會聯誼通訊 第七期 (1992-06)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/679-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e8-8|679. 北美洲台灣婦女會聯誼通訊 第二十三期 (2000-08)]].
- NATWA ran a 關懷資訊中心 "關懷網" information/care column: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/columns17|17. 關懷網 by NATWA關懷資訊中心 (2016-10-27)]].
- **2017** — NATWA contributed $21,090 to the Houston Hurricane Harvey Relief Fund in a joint community donation: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys328|328. 休士頓台灣人傳統基金會社區服務參與 (2017-12)]].
- Sibling chapters with their own records: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-san-diego-chapter|NATWA San Diego Chapter]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-oregonsouth-west-chapter|NATWA Oregon/South-West Chapter]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-st-louis-chapter|NATWA St. Louis Chapter]]; the NJ chapter's concert record is [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts118|118. 「與莫札特有緣」音樂會 by NATWA-NJ (2006-02-19)]].
- NATWA's first president was 張富美 (Dr. Fu-Mei Chang), 1988: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/19-dr-fu-mei-chang-e5-bc-b5-e5-af-8c-e7-be-8e-e5-8d-9a-e5-a3-ab-the-first-presid|19. Dr. Fu-Mei Chang 張富美博士 / The First President of NATWA / 1988]].
- NATWA was one of twelve organizations (with TAA, TACL, FAPA, TCCCNA, NATMA, NATPA and others) in the joint project committee that wrote to ~12,000 Taiwanese-American households in the census campaign; the Kansas chapter later proposed a Taiwanese American community scholarship: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys253|Our Journeys 253 (memoir)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys303|Our Journeys 303]].
- **2021-05-13** — NATWA and its SoCal chapter co-initiated the 南加州僑界支持台灣參與WHA聯合聲明: [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20210616072940_2021_05_17_2021年南加州僑界支持台灣參與世界衛生大會wha聯合聲_b1d58af16c0a5e5b|record]].
