# Taiwan National Treasure Foundation (國家寶藏基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwan National Treasure Foundation
- **Chinese:** 國家寶藏基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwan National Treasure Foundation is a US 501(c)(3) public charity nonprofit organization dedicated to recovering and digitizing historical records about Taiwan held in foreign archives. Its first project site is the National Archives and Records Administration (NARA II) near Washington, D.C., where volunteers use a mobile app to photograph and catalog documents. Materials gathered — including intelligence records, geographic surveys, and biographical documentation compiled by countries such as the US and UK over the past century — are automatically entered into a searchable database. The foundation aims to bring these historical resources back into public view to help Taiwanese people rediscover and reinterpret their own history.
