# Taiwanese American CPA Association, TACPA (台美會計師協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American CPA Association, TACPA
- **Chinese:** 台美會計師協會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American CPA Association (TACPA) is a professional organization founded in 1992 in Los Angeles by Charles Chiang, CPA, and a group of Taiwanese American CPAs. It encourages members to share professional tax knowledge and provide cross-state tax services to mutual clients. The TACPA holds at least one annual tax forum and periodically hosts interactive forums in Taiwan with the Taiwan CPA Association and Taiwan Government to offer constructive suggestions on Taiwan’s tax systems. In 2011, Taiwanese Canadian CPAs joined the organization, expanding its membership across North America to address cross-border tax issues between the USA and Canada. The TACPA currently has approximately 60 active members across various states and provinces.
