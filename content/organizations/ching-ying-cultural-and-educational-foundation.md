# Ching Ying Cultural and Educational Foundation

**Ching Ying Cultural and Educational Foundation** (慶瑛文教基金會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]]. A separate third-party record names [[people/hsu-hsin-hung||Hsu Hsin-hung (徐新宏)]] as its 董事長 (chairman) and identifies the foundation's namesake as his late wife, 張慶瑛 — see GAPS for the identity caveat.
