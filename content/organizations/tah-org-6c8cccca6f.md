The Greater Kansas Acupuncture and Oriental Medicine Association is a community organization based in the Kansas City area dedicated to promoting Oriental medicine and raising health awareness among its members. The association holds events approximately twice per year, featuring lectures and demonstrations by practitioners of traditional Chinese medicine and acupuncture. As of 2014, the organization had been active for over twenty years. A 2014 event at a member’s home featured Dr. Yang Li-Dian, a Taiwan-born doctor of traditional Chinese medicine, who lectured on foundational TCM concepts and demonstrated qigong exercises for attendees.
