# San Diego Taiwanese Tennis Association (聖地牙哥台灣人網球協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** San Diego Taiwanese Tennis Association
- **Chinese:** 聖地牙哥台灣人網球協會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The San Diego Taiwanese Tennis Association (SDTTA) is a grassroots nonprofit organization founded in 1986 that combines health, sport, and recreation for the Taiwanese American community in San Diego. Membership has historically hovered around 100 people, with a peak of 165 members in 1992. The association organizes singles and doubles seasonal tournaments, quarterly group doubles matches, and an annual family team tournament called the ‘Qinqing Cup.’ Members also enjoy an annual year-end banquet with free meals and raffle prizes. All operations are managed by a volunteer board of directors, with communications conducted electronically through a dedicated webpage and email.
