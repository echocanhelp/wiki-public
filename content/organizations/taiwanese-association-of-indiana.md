# Taiwanese Association of Indiana (印州台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of Indiana
- **Chinese:** 印州台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of Indiana (印州台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization serving Taiwanese American communities across the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the state of Indiana and is listed under the TAA’s Mid-Western regional grouping on the national chapter directory.
