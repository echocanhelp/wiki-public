# Chicago Taiwanese Architecture Institute, CTAI (芝加哥台灣建築學會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Chicago Taiwanese Architecture Institute, CTAI
- **Chinese:** 芝加哥台灣建築學會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Chicago Taiwanese Architecture Institute (CTAI) is an Illinois 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based in Chicago. It serves as a networking platform for Mandarin-speaking professionals and architects worldwide. The organization provides architecture, art, and design related educational events, lectures, and workshops for the public. Its membership includes Chicago-area architects and young professionals from a variety of fields.
