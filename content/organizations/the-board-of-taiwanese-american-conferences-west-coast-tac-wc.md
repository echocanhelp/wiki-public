# THE BOARD OF TAIWANESE AMERICAN CONFERENCES – WEST COAST TAC-WC (美西台灣人夏令會理事會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** THE BOARD OF TAIWANESE AMERICAN CONFERENCES – WEST COAST TAC-WC
- **Chinese:** 美西台灣人夏令會理事會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Conferences – West Coast (TAC-WC) is a nonprofit corporation formally incorporated in March 2017 to coordinate annual summer conferences for Taiwanese Americans living on the West Coast of the United States. The organization grew out of more than 30 years of informal TAC-WC conferences held across the region, with the formal organizational structure proposed by Ken S. Huang (黃根深) in 2012. TAC-WC is governed by a five-member Board of Directors representing regional chapters spanning Las Vegas/Salt Lake City, Los Angeles, San Diego, San Francisco/Sacramento, and Seattle/Portland. The organization was in the process of applying for 501(c)(3) nonprofit status at the time of its founding announcement, and its inaugural conference as a formal corporation was scheduled for July 7–9, 2017 in the San Diego area.
