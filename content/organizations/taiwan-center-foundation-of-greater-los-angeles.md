# Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles
- **Chinese:** 大洛杉磯台灣會館
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館) is a non-profit organization established in 1998 to promote Taiwanese culture and heritage among Taiwanese Americans in Southern California. It operates the Taiwan Center, a community facility in Alhambra that serves as a gathering place for cultural events, civic meetings, educational programs, and organizational activities.
