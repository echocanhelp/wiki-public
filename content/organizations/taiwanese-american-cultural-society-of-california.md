# Taiwanese American Cultural Society of California (台美文化生活協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Cultural Society of California
- **Chinese:** 台美文化生活協會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Cultural Society of California is a cultural organization dedicated to promoting exchange and mutual understanding between Taiwanese and American cultures. It serves as a platform for Taiwanese Americans to celebrate both their Taiwanese heritage and their life in the United States. The society seeks to help Americans appreciate modern Taiwanese culture and to help Taiwanese people better understand American culture. It also aims to strengthen cohesion within the Taiwanese American community.
