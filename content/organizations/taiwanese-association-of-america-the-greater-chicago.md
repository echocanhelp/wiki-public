# Taiwanese Association of America the Greater Chicago (大芝加哥台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of America the Greater Chicago
- **Chinese:** 大芝加哥台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Taiwanese Association of America the Greater Chicago (大芝加哥台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations across the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the greater Chicago, Illinois area.
