# Taiwanese Association of America Greater Philadelphia (大費城)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of America Greater Philadelphia
- **Chinese:** 大費城
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America Greater Philadelphia (大費城 台灣同鄉會, TAAGP) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the greater Philadelphia, Pennsylvania area.

## Role in the Community

- The Philadelphia chapter was one of the 28 founding chapters of the national TAA (全美台灣同鄉會), with 李儒英 listed as its founding chapter president — see the TAA founding history memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76|76. 全美台灣同鄉會/吳木盛/12/2014]] (published 2014-12-21).
- Long-running community service record: the chapter participates annually in the International Spring Festival in Philadelphia — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/30-international-spring-festival|30. International Spring Festival 大費城台灣同鄉會參與國際春季園遊會]] (2016-05-25) and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/international-spring-festival-philadelphia|43. International Spring Festival 國際春季園遊會 / TAA Greater Philadelphia Chapter 大費城臺灣同鄉會]] (2018-05-09), the latter paired with a Friendship Concert at Lansdale, PA ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts125|125. International Spring Festival placard and Friendship Concert by TAA/Greater Philadelphia Chapter]]).
- Chapter co-hosts the Taiwanese American Community Scholarship Awards for the Greater Philadelphia area ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/2-taiwanese-american-community-scholarship-awards-greater-philadelphia|2. 台美人社區獎學金/大費城區]], 2015-07-21); the award was already running by 2013, recorded in the nationwide scholarship round-up [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys303|303. 台美人社區獎學金專輯]] alongside chapters from Houston and the Kansas NATWA chapter.
- Oral-history interviews in the TAH series document recent leadership: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/video-185|185. President of TAAGPC – 謝己 James Jii Shieh's Journey]] (2024 chapter president; see [[people/dr-jii-james-shieh|謝己]]) and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/video-187|187. Advisor of TAAGPC – 王博文 Powen Wang's Journey]] (chapter advisor; see [[people/powen-wang|王博文]]).
- Inside the community the chapter is also remembered for its language-practice debates: Hakka member 李常吉 recalls twenty-plus years attending TAA Greater Philadelphia meetings and summer camps, and argues for multilingual gatherings rather than Hokkien-only ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys202|202. 台灣人集會的語言問題 / 李常吉 / 03/2016]], band A memoir).
- The chapter's **own brief history** is held in the corpus as a dedicated work: 17. 大費城台灣同鄉會的簡介 / Brief History of TAAGP ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/project-3-17-eng|project-3-17-eng, 2017-10-18]]); a bibliography-only duplicate exists at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/project-3-17|project-3-17]].
- The chapter's own activities and commemorations are held as dedicated corpus records: a chapter activities record ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/greater-philadelphia-chapter-taa|TAAGP 大費城台灣同鄉會, 2015-12-10]]), [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/activities-of-taagp|大費城台灣同鄉會的活動 / 2019-01-20]], and the chapter's **228 commemoration** ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taagp-228|大費城台灣同鄉會228紀念會 / 2018-03-05]]).
- **Neighboring chapter-family orgs in the corpus:** the area's wider Taiwanese organizational fabric includes 大費城台灣客家同鄉會 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-hakka-assoc-of-greater-philadelphia|2015-12-23]]), 台美工商大費城會 (Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce of Greater Philadelphia, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-chamber-of-commerce-of-greater-philadelphia|record]]), NATWA's greater-Philadelphia chapter ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natma-greater-philadelphia-chapter|record]]), and the NTU alumni chapter whose first president was Dr. 李彥輝 Franklin Lee ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/230-dr-franklin-lee-e6-9d-8e-e5-bd-a5-e8-bc-9d-e9-86-ab-e5-b8-ab-the-first-presi|230. Dr. Franklin Lee / NTUAA Greater Philadelphia / 1986-04]]) — consistent with 李常吉's account of Hakka participation in chapter gatherings; organizational relationships to TAAGP not stated in the record, not merged.
