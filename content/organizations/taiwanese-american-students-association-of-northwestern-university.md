# Taiwanese American Students Association of Northwestern University

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Students Association of Northwestern University
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Students Association (TASA) at Northwestern University is a student organization dedicated to celebrating Taiwanese and Taiwanese American culture on campus. Founded in 1992 under the name Taiwanese American Students Club (TASC), it was formally recognized by the Associated Student Government in January 1994. The organization has since grown from an original group of eight members into an ASG A-status organization welcoming students of all backgrounds.
