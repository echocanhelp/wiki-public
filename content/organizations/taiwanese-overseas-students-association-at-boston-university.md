# Taiwanese Overseas Students Association at Boston University

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Overseas Students Association at Boston University
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Overseas Students Association at Boston University (TOSA, 波士頓大學台灣同學會) is a student organization focused on connecting Taiwanese international students with one another and with the broader BU campus community. TOSA is listed in BU’s official Terrier Central club directory and also appeared in BU’s 2018 club directory PDF alongside the Taiwanese American Student Association. The organization maintains an Instagram account (@tosa_bu) with over 1,300 followers and 540 posts, as well as a Facebook page at facebook.com/TOSAatBU.
