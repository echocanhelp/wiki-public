# Taiwan Human Rights & Culture Association

**Taiwan Human Rights & Culture Association** (台灣人權文化協會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** 台灣人權文化協會
- **English (as listed):** Taiwan Human Rights & Culture Association
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
- **Source:** https://www.taiwancenter.org/group-members · `taiwancenter-org-group-members.md`

## Role in the Community
- Founding (from community memoir material): in 1976, in the Los Angeles area, 許世楷、許千惠、郭清江、張綺石、鄭德和、王廷宜（泰和）and other Taiwan-human-rights-minded community members founded the 台灣人權及文化協會 (Taiwan Human Rights & Culture Association). Because the New York-area 台灣人權協會 (Formosan Association for Human Rights, grown out of 張丁蘭's 台灣婦女維護人權委員會) had the same aims, the two groups agreed to cooperate on human-rights work under the FAHR umbrella. Source: 張丁蘭的故事 (2000), preserved in the TAHS story corpus as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwan-cultural-association-for-human-rights|14. 台灣人權文化協會 (2014)]].
- HOLD: naming conflict — this page and the Taiwan Center listing use 台灣人權文化協會; the 張丁蘭 memoir writes 台灣人權及文化協會. Recorded as-is, not merged.
- Later relation: listed as a group member of the [[organizations/taiwan-center|Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]]; related umbrella body: [[organizations/formosan-association-for-human-rights|Formosan Association for Human Rights]].
