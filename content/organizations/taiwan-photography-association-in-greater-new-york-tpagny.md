# Taiwan Photography Association in Greater New York TPAGNY (大紐約區台灣攝影學會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwan Photography Association in Greater New York TPAGNY
- **Chinese:** 大紐約區台灣攝影學會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwan Photography Association in Greater New York (TPAGNY) was founded in 2005 by Taiwanese-American photography enthusiasts under the leadership of Professor Ching-tse Lee. The organization’s guiding philosophy is ‘Photography is Mindography,’ emphasizing the capture of images that reflect the heart, soul, and mind, and that resonate emotionally, intellectually, and spiritually. TPAGNY seeks to honor the multicultural and multilingual heritage of Taiwan through photographic art while also pursuing universal imagery. The association fosters community and friendship among members who share a passion for photography and a connection to Taiwanese culture. It welcomes all who share its vision to join and collaborate.
