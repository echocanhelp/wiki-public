# Good Shepherd Taiwanese Presbyterian Church

**Good Shepherd Taiwanese Presbyterian Church** (好牧者臺灣基督長老教會, GSTPC) is a Taiwanese-language Presbyterian congregation in Monterey Park, Southern California. The church describes itself as a **Gospel-Sharing, Truth-Practicing Community**—a fellowship that shares the gospel of Christ and practices biblical teaching. Members include immigrants and students from Taiwan who gather in the familiar warmth of Taiwanese (臺語) worship, with ministries for English-speaking youth and children as well.

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese name:** 好牧者臺灣基督長老教會
- **Type:** Taiwanese Presbyterian congregation (PC(USA) network context)
- **Location:** 606 South Atlantic Blvd., Monterey Park, CA 91754
- **Denomination / language:** Presbyterian; primary worship in Taiwanese; integrated English ministry (Noah's Ark) for youth and children
- **Leadership:** [[people/mingyuan-hsu|Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu (許明遠牧師)]] (full-time pastor, July 2019–present per official profile)
- **Official site:** [GSTPC Official Site](https://gstpc.org/home/)

## Overview

GSTPC sits in Monterey Park, a hub for Chinese and Taiwanese communities in the San Gabriel Valley. The congregation formed around brothers and sisters who immigrated or studied in Southern California from Taiwan. Worship emphasizes prayer, praise, restoration of relationship with God, the Spirit's presence, spiritual character, and equipping members to share and witness to the gospel in daily life.

The church welcomes new immigrants, students, visitors, seekers, and believers looking for a Bible-centered church family.

## History

### Founding

**設教:** 7 December 1980 (GSTPC40 許明遠前言). [[people/david-huang|黃德利]]’s 40th essay: planted in Monterey Park for 台語 worship during the 1980s immigrant wave; first year grew from **three families and seven youth** to 70+ in worship and 37 youth. Seven PCT pastors then in SoCal helped the plant (among them [[people/rev-j-m-hsu|許錦銘]]; 邱以正、許文將、余裕義 later deceased; 陳獻平 later in Taiwan).

潘淑姃 (GSTPC40): 1985 fifth-anniversary — 黃德利 **就任首任牧師**; 1988 the congregation bought the Monterey Park building (bid vs a Korean congregation after the white host church offered the property); 1990 tenth-anniversary worship; fifteenth-anniversary 獻堂; sister-church tie with 台北重新教會.

### Connection to Monterey Park Community

Monterey Park is widely recognized as one of the earliest and most established Chinese suburban communities in the United States. GSTPC's presence in this community provides a spiritual anchor for Taiwanese and Chinese immigrants, students, and families navigating life in Southern California while maintaining connections to their Taiwanese heritage and faith.

## Ministries

GSTPC offers a range of ministries serving different age groups and needs within the congregation:

- **Noah's Ark English ministry** — Truth and life teaching for second-generation youth and children growing up in the U.S. This program was added in recent years to more deeply pass on the Christian faith to the immigrant second generation.
- **Adult Sunday school** — Weekly Bible study and instruction for adults.
- **Choir** — Regular gathering and practice for the church choir.
- **活泉 (Living Spring) fellowship** — Senior member fellowship for older adults.
- **Sisters' fellowship** — Women's ministry and small group gatherings.
- **Area home small groups** — Weekday neighborhood-based small group Bible study and fellowship.
- **Discipleship training (門徒訓練)** — Discipleship equipment program (listed as paused through March 2026; times subject to change).
- **Student fellowship / QT Bible study** — Friday evening student fellowship and Quiet Time Bible study (listed as paused through March 2026; times subject to change).

## Worship and Programs

- **Sunday worship (in person / live stream):** Sundays **10:00 AM–11:00 AM** — unified family worship with English ministry and children's ministry during the same block ([worship live](https://gstpc.org/home/worship-live/)).
- **Wednesday prayer meeting:** Weekly midweek prayer gathering ([prayer meeting](https://gstpc.org/home/prayer-meeting/)).
- **Sunday school (children):** Sundays **10:00 AM–11:30 AM** — Bible time, crafts, music, and activities ([children's ministry](https://gstpc.org/home/childrens-ministry/)).
- **Digital bulletins:** Weekly bulletins and sermon listings on the [bulletin archive](https://gstpc.org/home/bulletin-archive/).
