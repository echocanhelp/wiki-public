# Taiwanese American Student Association @ Ohio State University

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Student Association @ Ohio State University
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Student Association (TASA) at The Ohio State University is a registered student organization focused on exploring Taiwanese American identity and culture on the Columbus campus. Its founding year is not stated on available first-party sources, though the organization’s official website is hosted on OSU’s org.osu.edu platform and its 2026–2027 constitution is on file with OSU Student Activities. TASA is open to all students regardless of identity or background.
