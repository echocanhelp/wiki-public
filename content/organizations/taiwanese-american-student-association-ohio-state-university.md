# Taiwanese American Student Association @ Ohio State University

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Student Association @ Ohio State University
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Student Association (TASA) at The Ohio State University is a registered student organization focused on exploring Taiwanese American identity and culture on the Columbus campus. Its founding year is not stated on available first-party sources, though the organization’s official website is hosted on OSU’s org.osu.edu platform and its 2026–2027 constitution is on file with OSU Student Activities. TASA is open to all students regardless of identity or background.

## Role in the Community
- Community memoirs in the archive recall the **Ohio State University Taiwanese Association** as one of "the two most typical examples" (with the University of Texas at Austin Taiwanese Association) of the ~1970 campus Taiwanese-association wave: students broke away from Chinese Student Associations they saw as controlled by the consulate and the Kuomintang. See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76-eng|Our Journeys 76 (EN)]].
- An OSU team competed in the first Midwest Taiwanese softball tournament (1973-06-16/17, Purdue), among 11 teams — evidence of an organized OSU Taiwanese student community by 1973. See [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-376|Our Journeys 376]].
- The archive also holds a 2016 newsletter record (會訊) by **OSU Taiwanese Student Association 哥城台灣同學會**, showing the group active under that name in the mid-2010s — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/osu-taiwanese-student-association|OSU Taiwanese Student Association 會訊]].
- **2013-04** — OSU hosted the **2013 ITASA Midwest Conference**, documented in the Taiwanese American Press archive: [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/2013-itasa-midwest-conference-at-the-ohio-state-university|2013 ITASA Midwest Conference at The Ohio State University]]. This places the campus group within the Midwest intercollegiate Taiwanese student network (ITASA) in the contemporary era, independent of the TAH directory record.
- **2010-12** — a Taiwanese American movement speaker's year-end recap records the **Midwest Asian American Student Union (MAASU) conference at OSU** as one of the campus stops on a nationwide tour that also included ITASA conferences (MIT, UT Austin, UCSD), TANG camps, and the TAF conference — corroborating OSU's place in the Midwest Taiwanese/Asian-American student circuit in the contemporary era: [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/on-discovering-passion-purpose|On Discovering Passion & Purpose (Taiwanese American Press)]].
- HOLD: whether today's registered TASA continues that 1970-era organization is not established on first-party sources; the founding year remains unstated. The corpus records the campus Taiwanese student presence under three names across eras (Taiwanese Association ~1970s, 哥城台灣同學會 2016, TASA present) without a first-party lineage statement.
