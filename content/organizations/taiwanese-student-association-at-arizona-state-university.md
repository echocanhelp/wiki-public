# Taiwanese Student Association at Arizona State University

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Student Association at Arizona State University
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Student Association at Arizona State University (TSA @ ASU) is a student organization based in Tempe, Arizona, that serves Taiwanese students at ASU. Its official Facebook page identifies it as the official presence of the Taiwanese Student Association at ASU (亞利桑那州立大學台灣同學會), and the organization maintains an active Instagram account under the handle @asu.tsa. A legacy university-hosted website at asu.edu/studentprgms/orgs/taiwan documented early activity including new student orientations and Lunar New Year celebrations, though that site has not been substantially updated in recent years.
