The Taiwanese Student Association at Arizona State University (TSA @ ASU) is a student organization based in Tempe, Arizona, that serves Taiwanese students at ASU. It is one of several campus Taiwanese student clubs in North America, alongside the [[organizations/taiwanese-student-association-tsa|Taiwanese Student Association at UC Santa Cruz]] and the [[organizations/the-north-america-taiwanese-students-association-tsa-na|North America Taiwanese Students' Association (TSA-NA)]]; on campus it sits in the same Arizona Taiwanese community as the [[organizations/arizona-taiwanese-student-association|Arizona Taiwanese Student Association]], the [[organizations/taiwanese-american-association-of-arizona|Taiwanese American Association of Arizona]], and the [[organizations/north-america-taiwanese-womens-association-arizona-chapter|North America Taiwanese Women's Association – Arizona Chapter]]. Its official Facebook page identifies it as the official presence of the Taiwanese Student Association at ASU (亞利桑那州立大學台灣同學會), and the organization maintains an active Instagram account under the handle @asu.tsa. A legacy university-hosted website at asu.edu/studentprgms/orgs/taiwan documented early activity including new student orientations and Lunar New Year celebrations, though that site has not been substantially updated in recent years.
