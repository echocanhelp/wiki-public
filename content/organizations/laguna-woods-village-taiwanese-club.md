# Laguna Woods Village Taiwanese Club (南加州)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Laguna Woods Village Taiwanese Club
- **Chinese:** 南加州
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Laguna Woods Village Taiwanese Club (拉古拿木屋台灣同鄉會) is a chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization of Taiwanese American community associations. The chapter is listed under the TAA’s Western (美西) regional grouping and serves Taiwanese Americans residing in Laguna Woods Village, a senior community in Orange County, California.
