# Laguna Woods Village Taiwanese Club (南加州)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Laguna Woods Village Taiwanese Club
- **Chinese:** 南加州
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Laguna Woods Village Taiwanese Club (拉古拿木屋台灣同鄉會) is a chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization of Taiwanese American community associations. The chapter is listed under the TAA’s Western (美西) regional grouping and serves Taiwanese Americans residing in Laguna Woods Village, a senior community in Orange County, California.

The village's Geneva Presbyterian Church is also a concert venue — the 洛杉磯福爾摩沙聖樂團 performed Elijah there ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts69|69. - On 2022-05-15 a gunman attacked the Taiwanese Presbyterian congregation gathered at Geneva Presbyterian Church in Laguna Woods (1死5傷) — an event that struck the village's 台美人 community directly; memoir at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-380|Our Journeys 380 (05/2022)]].
- Context for the club's membership milieu: retired 台美人 moving to Laguna Woods Village gathered at the nearby ITPC 長青教室 (Evergreen Class, Geneva Church; weekly from 2009, ~200 classes, ~100 attendees per session, taught in Taiwan mother tongue) — the club's 10th-anniversary record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/22-10th-anniversary-of-evergreen-class-e9-95-b7-e9-9d-92-e6-95-99-e5-ae-a4-by-ir|22. - Corpus re-grep 2026-09-21: no further club-specific material beyond the records linked above (remaining Laguna Woods hits are the 2022 church-shooting news cluster and unrelated chorale/event pages).

A 2017-04-03 community profile characterizes the club as the most active chapter with the smallest member residential footprint and the highest average member age in Taiwanese American association history (最活耀、會員分布區域最小及平均年齡最大的台美同鄉會): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ff320|320. Taiwanese Club Laguna Woods Village]].

A memoir in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys239|Our Journeys 239]] recalls ~20 Laguna Woods Village residents joining the 美西台灣同鄉夏令會 (~400 attendees), noting the club as a significant feeder to 「蕃薯藤」-generation events.
