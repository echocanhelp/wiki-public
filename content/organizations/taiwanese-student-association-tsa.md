# Taiwanese Student Association TSA

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Student Association TSA
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Student Association (TSA) at UC Santa Cruz — known in Chinese as 臺灣同學會 — is a non-profit cultural student club at the University of California, Santa Cruz. The organization is dedicated to providing a welcoming environment for overseas Taiwanese students as well as any student interested in Taiwanese culture, and is officially registered in the UCSC Get Involved club directory under the Cultural Identity category.

## Role in the Community
- "Taiwanese Student Association" is a generative name shared across campuses; the corpus preserves several distinct chapters, each its own record.   - [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/dumplings-usctsa|燒肉粽 by USC Taiwanese Student Association 南加大台灣同學會]] — USC chapter's cultural performance record
  - [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/osu-taiwanese-student-association|Newsletter 會訊 by OSU Taiwanese Student Association 哥城台灣同學會]] — Stillwater/Oklahoma chapter newsletter

  - [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tsapa-taiwanese-student-association-for-public-affairs|16. Taiwanese Student Association for Public Affairs 台灣公共事務學生會華大分會]] (2014) — TSAPA, the University of Washington chapter oriented to public affairs, showing the TSA lineage extending into advocacy-focused student orgs
- Early-chapter organizing detail preserved in memoirs: at the University of Wisconsin, 周烒明's account records the chapter registering in early 1963 as "UW Formosan Student Club 威大台灣同學會", blocked by the ROC embassy's professional students until the students won a public Student Senate debate (利騰俊 arguing the Formosa/台灣 name's origin), then formally chartered 1963-10-19 as "The Formosan Club of the University of Wisconsin 威大台灣同鄉會" with $1/semester dues and 周烒明's father 周试明醫師 as faculty advisor — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys81|81. 早期威大台灣學生在台灣建國運動所扮演的角色]]
- Cultural-organizing branch of a chapter: the UW-Milwaukee 台灣同學會 (founded c. 1996) staged 陳清風 professor-scripted puppet shows (虎姑婆) at the 1997 Moon Festival using pre-recorded 台詞 ("卡拉OK布袋戲"), spinning off a standing 台灣布袋戲團 in 1999 that toured local schools — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys277|277. 威斯康辛大學「台灣布袋戲團」的故事]] (2017-02)
- Context from the movement-history essay [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-357|Our Journeys 357]]: campus 臺灣同學會 grew out of late-1960s Berkeley/Stanford student gatherings; because chapters bearing "臺灣" in their names carried explicit Taiwanese consciousness, the KMT government pressured them and organized rival groups to split the overseas community — student associations were among the most common organs of community life alongside 同鄉會 and 臺灣教會.
