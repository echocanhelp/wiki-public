# Taiwanese Undergraduate Student Organization

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Undergraduate Student Organization
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Undergraduate Student Organization (TUSO) at the University of Wisconsin–Madison is a student organization focused on supporting Taiwanese undergraduate students and promoting Taiwanese culture to the broader campus community. Its founding year is not documented in available first-party sources. TUSO is listed as an affiliated student organization with UW–Madison’s International Student Services (ISS) office and is registered in the Wisconsin Involvement Network (WIN) directory.
