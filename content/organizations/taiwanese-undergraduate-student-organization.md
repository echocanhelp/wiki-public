# Taiwanese Undergraduate Student Organization

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Undergraduate Student Organization
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Undergraduate Student Organization (TUSO) at the University of Wisconsin–Madison is a student organization focused on supporting Taiwanese undergraduate students and promoting Taiwanese culture to the broader campus community. Its founding year is not documented in available first-party sources. TUSO is listed as an affiliated student organization with UW–Madison’s International Student Services (ISS) office and is registered in the Wisconsin Involvement Network (WIN) directory.

## Role in the Community
TUSO itself is not named in our memoir corpus, but it stands on a documented UW–Madison Taiwanese student-organizing lineage that the corpus preserves:
- The UW–Madison Madison chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America was among the 28 founding chapters (first president Li-yuan Huang) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76-eng|Taiwanese Association of America / Mu-Sheng Wu]].
- Early (1960s–70s) UW Taiwanese students played a leading role in the Taiwanese independence movement, including the "UW Taiwanese independence movement" — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys81-eng|Early Years of the UW Taiwanese Independence Movement / Suy-Ming Sam Chou]].
- The UW-M 台灣布袋戲團 (founded 1997 out of the UW-M 台灣同學會, with professor 陳清風's scripts and puppets) performed 虎姑婆 at Milwaukee's Moon Festival and annually at the Celebrate Madison International Festival from 2003 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys277|威斯康辛大學「台灣布袋戲團」的故事 / 朱靜女]].
