# New Orleans Taiwanese Association (紐奧良)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** New Orleans Taiwanese Association
- **Chinese:** 紐奧良
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The New Orleans Taiwanese Association (紐奧良 台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the New Orleans, Louisiana area and is listed under the TAA’s Southern (美南) regional grouping.
