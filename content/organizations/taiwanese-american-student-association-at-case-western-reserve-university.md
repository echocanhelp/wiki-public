# Taiwanese American Student Association at Case Western Reserve University

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Student Association at Case Western Reserve University
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Student Association at Case Western Reserve University (CWRU TASA) is a student organization based in Cleveland, Ohio, focused on the Taiwanese American community on campus. The organization's mission, as stated on its official university portal, is to empower and inspire the Taiwanese American community, to work with other cultural groups at Case to promote diversity, and to help develop personal friendships and professional networking through events and resources that explore Taiwanese American identity.
