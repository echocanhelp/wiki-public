# Taiwanese Association of Oklahoma (奧克拉荷馬台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of Oklahoma
- **Chinese:** 奧克拉荷馬台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of Oklahoma (奧克拉荷馬台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization serving Taiwanese American communities across the United States. The chapter serves Taiwanese Americans residing in the state of Oklahoma.
