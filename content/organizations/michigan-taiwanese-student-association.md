# Michigan Taiwanese Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Michigan Taiwanese Student Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Michigan Taiwanese Student Association (MTSA) is a Taiwanese student organization at the University of Michigan–Ann Arbor. Its university-hosted website describes it as a portal for Michigan Taiwanese students, offering resources and community for those studying and living in Ann Arbor. MTSA maintains a companion Google Sites wiki (MTSA 知識庫) that serves as a collaborative resource for Taiwanese students navigating academic and daily life at UMich, covering topics such as coursework, housing, and life in Ann Arbor. The organization is listed in the University of Michigan’s official Maize Pages club directory and is recognized by the LSA Asian Languages and Cultures department’s student organization listing.
