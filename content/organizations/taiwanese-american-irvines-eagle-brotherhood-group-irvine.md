# Taiwanese-American Irvine’s Eagle Brotherhood Group -Irvine (台美人)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese-American Irvine’s Eagle Brotherhood Group -Irvine
- **Chinese:** 台美人
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese-American Irvine’s Eagle Brotherhood Group is a fraternity organization within the Taiwanese American community in Irvine, California, founded on August 8, 2012. The group gathers for a Lunch & Forum Luncheon on the second Friday of each month and publishes a monthly newsletter covering member activities and inspirational content. Its central theme is promoting lasting bonds of fraternity so that older members may sustain their spirit and vitality. The group draws inspiration from Isaiah 40:31, using the eagle as a symbol of endurance, faith, and perspective. Members share updates on current events, family, and children at each gathering.
