# Taiwanese Association of South Florida (南佛州台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of South Florida
- **Chinese:** 南佛州台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of South Florida (南佛州台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community across the South Florida region.
