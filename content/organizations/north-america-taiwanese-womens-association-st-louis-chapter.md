# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – St. Louis Chapter (北美洲台灣婦女會聖路易分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – St. Louis Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣婦女會聖路易分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – St. Louis Chapter is a local chapter of the North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA), serving the St. Louis area.

## Role in the Community
The chapter's own activities are documented first-hand in the TAH story corpus (2015):

- 2015-09-21 — took part in the St. Louis Festival of Nations (聖路易市國際節), presenting the Taiwanese community at a city-wide multicultural event — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/3-festival-of-nation-by-natwa-stl|聖路易市國際節 Festival of Nation / NATWA-St. Louis Chapter]]
- 2015-11-30 — held its 年終慈善晚會 (year-end charity dinner) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/19-2015-e5-b9-b4-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-|2015年聖路易分會年終慈善晚會]]
- 2015-12-17 — chapter profiled in the NATWA directory record — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-st-louis-chapter|15. St. Louis Chapter / NATWA 北美洲台灣婦女會聖路易分會]]
- 2015-12-28 — served a Christmas dinner (聖誕晚餐) at HavenHouse, a St. Louis shelter for homeless mothers and children — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-serves-havenhouse|北美洲台灣婦女會聖路易分會 Serves HavenHouse 聖誕晚餐]]

Context note: St. Louis's older Taiwanese professional organizing (NATMA's St. Louis chapter, per the 周烒明 memoir) predates this women's chapter; the NATWA chapter continues that civic tradition focused on service and cultural presence.
