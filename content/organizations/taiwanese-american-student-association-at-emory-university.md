# Taiwanese American Student Association at Emory University

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Student Association at Emory University
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Student Association at Emory University (Emory TASA) is a student organization founded in 2003 on Emory’s Atlanta campus. According to its About page, the organization’s goal has been to promote awareness of the culture and heritage of Taiwan and Taiwanese Americans through social and cultural events aimed at the entire Emory community. The organization welcomes all interested students, not only those of Taiwanese descent.
