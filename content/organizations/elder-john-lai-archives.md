# Elder John Lai's Archives (賴永祥長老史料庫)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Elder John Lai's Archives
- **漢名:** 賴永祥長老史料庫
- **Official site:** http://www.laijohn.com/
- **Era:** site live as John Lai’s online 蒐藏; 《教會史話》欄 1988–
- **Geography:** Taiwan church history (stage = Taiwan, also 涉外); operator later US/Boston
- **Core roles:** digital archive of PCT / Taiwan Christianity sources; 《教會史話》全文；剪報與信徒檔
- **Not:** PCT official website ([[organizations/presbyterian-church-in-taiwan|pct.org.tw]]); not TAH Foundation

## History

Official Index (1990-04 識, archived 2026-08-18): the site studies **history of Christianity in Taiwan**, especially the Presbyterian Church in Taiwan. Three parts:

1. Full text of John Lai’s **《教會史話》** (Kau-hoe-su-oe)
2. Scrapbook files and related sources
3. Personal biography, works, and family history

Materials: 中日文、台文、西文；刊物、檔案、各教會紀念刊、受洗名冊、家譜、回憶錄、見證、信函. 蒐藏經過見講演〈[台灣教會史料研究回顧與展望](http://www.laijohn.com/works/lectures/Twkhsl.htm)〉（1998-05 中原／宇宙光）.

《教會史話》應《台灣教會公報》總編輯**盧俊義**之邀。約定一題一話約千字。自第 **1871** 期（**1988-01-10**）至第 **1985** 期（**1990-03-18**）共百篇，結為初輯。結集時文儘量照原，加圖。續編仍在公報刊登。賴寫：不作長篇大論，要「上窮碧落下黃泉，動手動腳找東西」（傅斯年）。範圍不限狹義傳道史、不限 PCT；要寫西洋宣教師，也不忽略本地傳教者與信徒。

English home: prepared mainly for Chinese readers; also English, Japanese, and romanized Taiwanese. Academic/private use; [Contact](http://www.laijohn.com/contact.htm).
