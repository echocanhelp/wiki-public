# NY Taiwanese Golf Club NYTGC (紐約台灣人高爾夫球俱樂部)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** NY Taiwanese Golf Club NYTGC
- **Chinese:** 紐約台灣人高爾夫球俱樂部
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

NY Taiwanese Golf Club is a non-profit organization serving the Taiwanese American community in the greater New York metropolitan area, including upstate NY, Long Island, Connecticut, and New Jersey. The club promotes its members’ interest in golf by organizing monthly tournaments held on Sundays between April and October, followed by a dinner and prize award ceremony. It maintains a uniform handicapping system in accordance with USGA Rules of Golf and encourages fair play and sportsmanship. The club also organizes social and community events, including an annual year-end evening party, and fosters exchanges with other organizations sharing similar interests. The club does not have a home course and rotates among various golf courses across the metro NY area.
