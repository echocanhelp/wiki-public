# NEW WORLD ART CENTER – (紐約市陳錦芳文化館)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** NEW WORLD ART CENTER –
- **Chinese:** 紐約市陳錦芳文化館
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The New World Art Center (NWAC) is an international art gallery and management company based in SoHo, NYC, with branches in Beijing’s 798 art district and Taipei. It engages in exhibition, publishing, licensing, print production, collecting, and art investment consulting. Under the leadership of Lucia Chen, NWAC has organized approximately 200 exhibitions for Dr. T.F. Chen and other international artists over 30 years. It has been a continuous sponsor of the T.F. Chen Cultural Center and collaborates with galleries and organizations worldwide. NWAC International assists artists in entering the art history narrative through customized career planning and market strategy while delivering returns for investors and collectors.
