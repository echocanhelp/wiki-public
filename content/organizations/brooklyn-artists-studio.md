# Brooklyn Artists Studio (布魯克林藝站)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Brooklyn Artists Studio
- **Chinese:** 布魯克林藝站
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Brooklyn Artists Studio (BAS) is a nonprofit residential and activity space in Brooklyn, New York that provides free short-term housing to Taiwanese artists visiting New York City. Originally named the Taiwanese Artists Center when it began operations in 2003, it was renamed BAS in the summer of 2007 at the suggestion of director Huang Ming-chuan. The facility is a two-story townhouse with a backyard built in 1919, with physical infrastructure provided by the couple Huang Tsai-tian and Yang Shu-ching. Financial support is provided annually by the Oasis Foundation (founded 2009) and the Cheng Yin-cheng Memorial Fund (founded 2012). The space operates on a DIY, self-sufficient model to give visiting artists maximum freedom.

- **1919 / 1938:** the facility is a two-story townhouse with a backyard built in 1919; the 1F rear extension was built in 1938 (一樓後面的延伸建於1938年). Sources: self-report Capture 1 (1919) and Capture 2 Chinese long-form dump, `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/brooklyn-artists-studio.md` (1938).
- **2003 purchase:** bought from a German widow whose husband ran a machine shop on site and whose son later used it for storage. Source: Capture 2 Chinese long-form dump (`knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/brooklyn-artists-studio.md`).
- **Donors:** physical infrastructure provided by the couple 黃再添 Patrick Huang / 楊淑卿 S. C. Yang ("硬體設備由黃再添楊淑卿夫婦提供", printed twice in the dump — one restatement, not two sources); annual financial support from the Oasis Foundation 綠洲基金會 (founded 2009) and the Cheng Yin-cheng Memorial Fund 鄭胤成紀念基金 (founded 2012). Source: self-report Captures 1–2, [[sources/brooklyn-artists-studio-tah-org-capture|capture page]]; donor rosters have no independent record on this box.
- **2017-08-10 (CNA report):** 黃再添 served as 站長; by then 14 years and 「免費接待上千名台灣藝術工作者」; capacity 至多 12 人, 寒暑假常常滿檔. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories564|564. 紐約爸爸 / 黃兆平 / 中央通訊社 2017-08-10]].
- **Application rule (per the 2017 CNA article):** apply 90 天前檢附書面計畫, 免費入住以 30 天為限, 每天 5 美元清潔費; 黃再添's 「我從不說NO！」. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories564|mystories564]] — third-party, `reported`.
- **Resident artists (per the 2017 CNA article):** 拷秋勤樂團, 滅火器, 晨曦光廊, MR.OGAY, 中央公園「台灣之夜 (Taiwanese Waves)」, 裝置藝術家徐瑞憲、張力山及方偉文; 林煌迪、王婉婷 twice resident with children's paintings left for the couple; 黃浩庭 coined 「紐約爸爸」; Mr.OGAY 黑雞先生 door-wall graffiti became a Wyckoff Avenue landmark. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories564|mystories564]] — single article, `reported`, not independently verified.
- **Location:** an old building on Wyckoff Avenue (魏克夫街), Brooklyn, adjacent to 黃再添's real-estate management company; the article notes the Soho/East Village → DUMBO/Williamsburg arts-geography shift and Wyckoff Avenue price doubling. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories564|mystories564]].
- **Self-report context (submitted 08/2017 by 黃再添):** the couple's 台獨 stance (台灣中國成為兄弟之邦), 18-year KMT blacklist under martial law, 黃再添's present DPP overseas membership; identification lines 黃再添(台南市、台大社會系1971)、楊淑卿(台北市、台大動物系1974); the DIY 自給自足自住自治 rule (不打擾訪客，讓訪客有充分自由). Source: Capture 2, `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/brooklyn-artists-studio.md` — `sourced` self-report.
- **Website:** the org's own stated site `http://bas-ny.org/index.html` — not archived on this box (see capture page, "What this capture does NOT establish").
