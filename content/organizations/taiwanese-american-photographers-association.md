# Taiwanese American Photographers Association(TAPA)

**Taiwanese American Photographers Association(TAPA)** (台美攝影學會 TAPA) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** 台美攝影學會 TAPA
- **English (as listed):** Taiwanese American Photographers Association(TAPA)

## Role in the Community

- **Taiwan Center LA programming:** As of 2023 the association (台美攝影學會) ran a beginner photography class (攝影初級班) at the Greater Los Angeles Taiwan Center's Taiwan School, advertised alongside other center programs — an active skill-sharing group within the Taiwan Center ecosystem. Source: [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2023/20231208130833_root_9e4671c2c3322639|Taiwan Justice 大洛杉磯台灣會館 program listing, 2023-06]]; the class kept its own archive tag page into 2025 ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250328033145_tag_台美攝影學會攝影初級班_75053198a320e220|Tag: 台美攝影學會攝影初級班]]).
- **Member exhibition:** 台美攝影學會 held a members' works exhibition (會員作品聯展) on 2023-09-09~10, announced in the Taiwan Justice 藝文 section — evidence of an active membership showing its work publicly. Source: [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2023/20231201230251_root_3ab5b8dfba8018b5|Taiwan Justice 藝文 listing, archived 2023-08-29]].
- **TAH story record:** A community story "21. Taiwanese American Photographers Association 台美攝影協會" (2015-07-31) is held in the TAH story corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-photographers-association|21. Taiwanese American Photographers Association]].
- HOLD: conflict in the association's Chinese name — this page's source listing says 台美攝影**學會**; the TAH story title says 台美攝影**協會**. Not merged pending owner confirmation.
- Note (disambiguation): the "TAPA" acronym in the FAPA founding memoir ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys65|Our Journeys 65]]) refers to a proposed name for FAPA ("Taiwanese Association for Public Affairs" → TAFPA/TAPA), not this photographers' association.
