# FAPA North Texas (TX-N)

**FAPA North Texas (TX-N)** is a local chapter of the [[organizations/formosan-association-for-public-affairs|Formosan Association for Public Affairs (FAPA)]], the US-based 501(c)(3) grassroots advocacy organization for Taiwan founded in 1982. FAPA reports 44 chapters with 2,700+ active members; this roster entry is from FAPA's official chapter page.
