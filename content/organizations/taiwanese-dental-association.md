- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Dental Association (TDA) is a Taiwanese student organization based at the University of Pennsylvania’s School of Dental Medicine. It is listed in the PAACH Student Communities graduate organizations directory. TDA’s stated mission focuses on promoting Taiwanese cultural interest within the dental school setting and helping students with shared backgrounds build community and ease the transition to life in Philadelphia.
