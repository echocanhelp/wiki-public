# Taiwanese Youth Arts Foundation / Washington DC (華府台灣人青少年才藝基金會)

The Taiwanese Youth Arts Foundation, Inc. (TYAF), established in 1982 and IRS-recognized as a nonprofit, is the primary funding resource for the Washington DC Taiwanese School (WDCTS). WDCTS offers Mandarin, Taiwanese, and Japanese language classes for students from pre-school through 12th grade, as well as extracurricular activities including chess, Erhu, violin, art, Chinese Yoyo, tennis, aerobic dance, and Tai-Chi. TYAF sponsors the Outstanding Taiwanese American Youth Awards for students in the Greater Washington DC and Baltimore area, the Summer Taiwanese Teachers Workshop, and annual cultural events including Lunar New Year, Taiwanese American Heritage Week, and the Harvest Moon Festival. The foundation also participates in charitable activities such as disaster relief fundraising.
