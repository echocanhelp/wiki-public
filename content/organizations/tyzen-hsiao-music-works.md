# TYZEN HSIAO MUSIC WORKS (蕭泰然音樂基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** TYZEN HSIAO MUSIC WORKS
- **Chinese:** 蕭泰然音樂基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Tyzen Hsiao Music Works (蕭泰然音樂基金會) is a foundation dedicated to honoring the legacy of Taiwanese American composer Tyzen Hsiao and preserving and promoting his musical works. Maestro Hsiao trained in Taiwan, Japan, and the United States, developing a unique compositional style that blended international influences with a deep love of Taiwanese folk music. His compositions captured the spirit and resilience of the Taiwanese people and continue to be performed in churches and symphony halls worldwide. The foundation carries forward his memory and artistic legacy, inspiring poets, artists, and musicians across generations.

## Role in the Community

- Directory record in the TAHS corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tyzen-hsiao-music-works|23. TYZEN HSIAO MUSIC WORKS 蕭泰然音樂基金會]] (2015-10-05), which also links the composer page [[people/tyzen-hsiao|Tyzen Hsiao (蕭泰然)]].
- The foundation's namesake composer was a touchstone of community cultural organizing decades before the foundation existed. The 美東台灣人夏令會 (TAC/EC) staged "A Musical Tribute to Tyzen Hsiao" (台灣人音樂-蕭泰然作品演奏會) in Ithaca, NY on 1995-07-02: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts14|14. A Musical Tribute to Tyzen Hsiao, Ithaca 07/02/1995]] — the program paired his symphonic works with Chiang Wen-yeh (江文也) dances and Taiwanese folk songs, sung by local choirs including the 拾音合唱團.
- The Akron, Ohio concert of 2016-02-10 (E. J. Thomas Performing Arts Hall, ~400 attendees, 王雅蕙 conducting, 林昭亮 violin) performing Hsiao's D-major Violin Concerto — world-premiered 1992-11-13 in San Diego by 林昭亮 — is remembered in 何康隆's memoir as a milestone of Taiwanese music overseas ("首次在海外的音樂會上，指揮是台灣人，演奏者是台灣人，作曲者是台灣人"): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys269|269. Akron演奏會側記 今晚我們照亮了這個角落 / 何康隆 /12/2016]]. 林衡哲, who drove from Michigan to witness it, is described there as the dreamer behind the 1983 essay 「我有一個夢」.
- Hsiao himself engaged the diaspora directly: his 2016-08 open letter to fellow Taiwanese in Minnesota, with 蔡友仁's reply, is preserved at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/mystories456|456. 蕭泰然給所思念的明州同鄉的一封信 + 蔡友仁先生的回信]].
- Media legacy records: TV documentary portrait 【台灣演義】台灣音樂家 蕭泰然傳 (2015-03-01) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/videos112|112 Tyzen Hsiao History 台灣演義 蕭泰然傳]]; and the 2018 美國蕭泰然音樂節 final stage rehearsal — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/videos-152|152. 2018美國蕭泰然音樂節舞台現場最後彩排]] — evidence the music-festival activities the foundation carries forward.
