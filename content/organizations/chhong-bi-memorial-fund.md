# Chhong-bi Memorial Fund (聰美姐紀念基會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Chhong-bi Memorial Fund
- **Chinese:** 聰美姐紀念基會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Chhong-bi Memorial Fund was established in April 1993 in memory of a selfless Taiwanese woman who, along with her physician husband, served the poor in Nepal for over a decade as part of a Japanese overseas medical mission, and anonymously donated monthly to the Taiwan Independence League’s publication ‘Taiwan Youth’ for over ten years. The fund is dedicated to carrying on her spirit of devotion to Taiwan and promoting Taiwanese language and culture. Its major projects include managing the Taiwanese-language newsletter ‘Tai-bun Thong-sin’ (issues 25–142, November 1993 to January 2006), digitizing all 500 issues of ‘Taiwan Youth’ magazine, and publishing a Taiwanese-language translation anthology including coverage of the 228 Incident. The foundation also operates a website with four major sections: Tai-bun Thong-sin, Taiwan Youth, a Taiwan forum, and a Taiwanese-language living seminar. The fund views the written form of the Taiwanese language as the core of new Taiwanese culture and has worked for over twenty years to cultivate, promote, and sustain it against the pressure of dominant language hegemonies.
