# L.A. Study Group (大洛杉磯台灣讀書會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** L.A. Study Group
- **Chinese:** 大洛杉磯台灣讀書會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The L.A. Study Group is a Taiwanese American reading and discussion group based in the Greater Los Angeles area, modeled after the Berlin Study Group (柏林讀書會). The group provides a forum for members to express their individual perspectives and opinions on social and political issues related to Taiwan. Its goal is to strengthen independent critical thinking among participants and to keep the community informed about current events in Taiwan. Members come from diverse professional backgrounds, and the group values that diversity as its greatest resource for balanced, multifaceted discussion. The group complements the existing lecture programming offered by the local FAPA chapter by providing a space for active community expression and engagement.
