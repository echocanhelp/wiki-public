# American Citizens for Taiwan (西雅圖美台會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** American Citizens for Taiwan
- **Chinese:** 西雅圖美台會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

American Citizens for Taiwan (ACT) is a U.S. nonprofit organization composed of United States citizens who support freedom and democracy both at home and abroad. ACT works to spur the U.S. Government to be fully supportive of Taiwan’s right to self-determination, recognizing Taiwan’s democracy as important to U.S. security and economy. The organization monitors U.S. government and Congressional actions regarding Taiwan, keeps the public informed via email, website, and social media, and provides online tools for constituents to contact their members of Congress. ACT advocates for strong U.S.-Taiwan relations and works to counter efforts that would weaken the freedoms and security of both nations.
