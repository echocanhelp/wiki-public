# Taiwan National Organization at UCI

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwan National Organization at UCI
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwan National Organization (TNO) at UC Irvine is a student organization founded in 2017 and registered through UCI’s official campus engagement platform, ZotSpot. Known in Chinese as UCI台灣學生會, TNO focuses primarily on serving students from Taiwan, distinguishing it from the Taiwanese American-oriented TAO also active on campus.
