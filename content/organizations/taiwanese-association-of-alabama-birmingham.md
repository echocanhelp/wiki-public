# Taiwanese Association of Alabama Birmingham (伯明罕臺灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of Alabama Birmingham
- **Chinese:** 伯明罕臺灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of Alabama Birmingham (伯明罕臺灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the Birmingham, Alabama area and is classified under the TAA’s East Southern (美東南) regional grouping.
