# Greater Sacramento Taiwanese Association GSTA (大沙加偭度台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Greater Sacramento Taiwanese Association GSTA
- **Chinese:** 大沙加偭度台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Greater Sacramento Taiwanese Association (GSTA, 大沙加緬度台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. GSTA serves the Taiwanese American community in the greater Sacramento, California area and is classified under the TAA Western (美西) region.
