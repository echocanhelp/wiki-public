# Taiwanese Association of America Colorado (科羅拉多)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of America Colorado
- **Chinese:** 科羅拉多
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America Colorado (TAA-CO, 科羅拉多 台灣同鄉會) is the Colorado chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization serving Taiwanese American communities across the United States. The chapter serves Taiwanese Americans in Colorado, with its primary base in the Denver metropolitan area.

## Role in the Community
- Chapter records exist in the TAH corpus: directory entries for the chapter in 2015 and a 1980 Denver chapter record, plus a chapter newsletter record from 2019 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/colorado-chapter-taa|TAA Colorado (2015 directory entry)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-association-of-america-colardo-chapter-taa-co|TAA/Colorado Chapter Denver in 1980]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/newsletter-of-taiwanese-association-of-american-colorado|TAA/Colorado Chapter newsletter (2019)]].
- Colorado's earliest role in the movement predates the chapter itself: Cary S. Huang and others began publishing the movement journal 五月花 (May Flower) in 1969 in Ft. Collins, Colorado; it later became the official TAA journal (1973–1980) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76-eng|Our Journeys (EN), May Flower history]].
- Colorado is named in the 台灣政論 North America regional representative roster (issue 5, 1975.12): 科羅拉多地區代表 蕭廣志 (the publication misprinted him as 蕭應志), who later became head of 科州台灣之音 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-357|Our Journeys 357, notes]].
- Colorado appears as a listener reach-area for the Bay Area 「台灣之音」 phone-news line (from 1978), showing Colorado members' tie into the island tangwai news network — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys37|Our Journeys 37]].
- A chapter-authored institutional history exists in the corpus (2017-10-04, band B): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/history-of-taiwanese-association-of-america-colorado|科州台灣同鄉會簡介與感言]] — bibliographic record only in the vault; full text stays in the source archive.
- Mutual-aid role in the present day: in 2024 the chapter, together with 科羅拉多台灣人緊急救助會, ran the GoFundMe relief drive (target US$250k, exceeded at US$326,200 by 2024-06-08) for Air Force Academy cadet 徐皓明 after his mother and grandmother were critically injured in a car accident near Colorado Springs, with 駐丹佛辦事處長黃世昌 also assisting — [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240622131108_root_c4078f60c12e24af|TJJ report via 大洛杉磯台灣會館, 2024-06-07]], [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240622133059_root_77fe74fe6d54efd6|TJJ / 中央社記者 report]], [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250324131146_母親外婆來美參加畢業典禮卻遇嚴重車禍_請慷慨_0e8044bd2c9f261f|TJJ follow-up, 2025-03-24]], [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250815064510_美軍校台生徐皓明家人來美遇車禍重傷-台僑援助募_eeb9974330046d04|TJJ 中央社 report, 2025-08-15]].
