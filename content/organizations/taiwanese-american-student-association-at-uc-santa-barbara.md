# Taiwanese American Student Association at UC Santa Barbara

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Student Association at UC Santa Barbara
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Student Association (TASA) at UC Santa Barbara is a student-led social and cultural organization that celebrates and explores Taiwanese American culture. Officially recognized on UCSB’s Shoreline platform and listed by the university’s Asian Resource Center and Undergraduate Admissions office, TASA is open to students of all cultural backgrounds and describes its mission as fostering community, friendship, and cultural awareness through events, workshops, and service initiatives.
