# TSUNAH FOUNDATION (慈林教育基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** TSUNAH FOUNDATION
- **Chinese:** 慈林教育基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Tsunah Foundation is a U.S. IRS-registered nonprofit organization based in Laguna Niguel, California. The foundation supports scholars and students engaged in study, research, and publications related to Taiwan’s arts, culture, education, history, and political and social movements. It works to establish and assist nonprofit institutions that advance understanding of Taiwan, and runs programs aimed at younger generations of Taiwanese Americans. The foundation also promotes peace, freedom, and democracy in Taiwan.

## Role in the Community

Corpus record (memoirs are primary material):

- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tsunah-foundation|1. TSUNAH FOUNDATION 慈林教育基金會]] — the foundation's own TAH directory entry (published 2014-10-12, band B), the base record for this page.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys301|301. 南加州臺灣人聯合基金會（Taiwanese United Fund）的誔生、回顧與展望（1986-1996）/ 林衡哲]] — memoir (band A). Records that when author Lin Heng-cherh returned to Taiwan to serve Menlo Hospital in 1997, Ng Li-pei (吳澧培) donated US$500,000 to Lin I-hsiung's 《慈林基金會》 (alongside $500k to TUF); Ng later donated a further US$1,000,000 split between TUF and 慈林基金會 while chairing the 台灣會館筹备会.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys29|29. 台灣會館籌建回憶]] — memoir (band A). Records 吳澧培 donating US$1,000,000 to 台灣人聯合基金會與慈林基金會 during the 台灣會館 drive, corroborating the ourjourneys301 account of Ng's gifts to 慈林基金會.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-379|379. 十年東南夏令憶感 | 01/2022]] — memoir (band A). Records Lin I-hsiung presenting 《慈林文教基金會》's "台灣社會運動史料中心" (Taiwan social-movement archives center) at an overseas community lecture, with attendees donating on the spot — showing the foundation's archives mission active in the diaspora community.
- [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/tsunah-foundations-youth-culture-tour-of-taiwan|Tsunah Foundation's Youth Culture Tour of Taiwan]] — TA.org report (2011-10-31) of the foundation's Youth Culture Tour of Taiwan, corroborating its programs for younger generations of Taiwanese Americans.

- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys301|301. 南加州臺灣人聯合基金會（1986-1996）/ 林衡哲]] additionally records that the 《慈林之夜》 events Lin I-hsiung later held in Taiwan were the extension of the overseas 《台灣文化之夜》 series run with the United Fund's sponsorship — the cultural-program milieu out of which the foundation's culture mission grew (re-grepped slice 09190130-5, 2026-09-20).
- Press record (taiwanjustice.net harvest): 「撕開黑幕的光：海內外人權救援暨北美台灣之音聯展」, held by 慈林教育基金會 from 2024-06-08 through year-end — reviewing 1960s–1990s human-rights rescue (彭明敏 escape / 宗像隆幸's forged Japanese passport) and paying tribute to 北美台灣之音, the phone-answering-machine "The Voice of Taiwan" founded in New York in 1977 by the married couple [[people/morgan-chang|張富雄]] and [[people/eileen-chang|楊宜宜]]. Source: [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250814164746_海內外人權救援聯展-慈林教育基金會登場_向北美_c082b03671e7c8ee|海內外人權救援聯展 慈林教育基金會登場，向北美台灣之音致敬 (CNA, 2024-06-08)]] — the foundation's diaspora-connected human-rights archives mission in practice.
- The foundation has held the 228事件暨林家祖孫受難 memorial at 慈林紀念林園 (the Lin family cemetery) every year since 2006; in 2021 (41st anniversary) it cancelled public events for pandemic reasons and 林義雄 instead visited the grave privately with family — corroborated, with the foundation's coordination role toward 義光教會, by [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250428212842_賴清德出席義光教會活動_林義雄赴林家墓園追思_39fe85e3f70eb4ac|賴清德出席義光教會活動，林義雄赴林家墓園追思 (2021-02-28)]]. A 2018 call for registration confirms the annual series: [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240713160318_root_c43f4dc5772fdbd4|228事件暨林家祖孫受難2018年追思紀念活動報名 (慈林教育基金會, 2018-02-02)]].
