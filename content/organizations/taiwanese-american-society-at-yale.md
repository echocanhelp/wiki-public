# Taiwanese American Society at Yale

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Society at Yale
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Society at Yale (TAS) is an undergraduate student organization at Yale University dedicated to promoting awareness of the cultural, political, and social aspects of Taiwanese American culture and fostering the broader Asian American community on campus. Founded in 1991 as the Taiwanese Club, the organization was later renamed the Yale College Taiwanese American Society to reflect the Taiwanese American identity of its membership. TAS is a member of Yale’s Pan-Asian Council and is listed among the internal organizations of the Asian Network at Yale.

## Role in the Community
- Corpus scan found no memoir mentions of the club itself (founded 1991). Pre-history of the Taiwanese student community at Yale it grew out of: during the 台獨聯盟 "自由長征" campus tours (1967), Yale's 陳隆志 and 陳東壁 were recruited as盟員 — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys81|Our Journeys 81]]; Yale-affiliated theology students 鄭義勇 and 林天民 also featured in the 美東夏令會 network — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys268|Our Journeys 268]].
- 1991 — Yale sent representatives among the ~twenty schools (Harvard, Columbia, U Penn, Smith, Rutgers, NYU) at the founding ITASA "seeds" meeting at Columbia, the intercollegiate Taiwanese network that campus clubs like TAS joined — see [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/inspire-empower-and-activate-itasas-coming-of-age|Inspire, Empower, and Activate: ITASA's Coming of Age]] (Winston Yang's recollection).
- 1999/02 — A Yale University chapter entry appears in the 台美大學生跨校際協會 (ITASA) 1999 handbook, documenting the Yale campus chapter within the intercollegiate network — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/556-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e5-a4-a7-e5-ad-b8-e7-94-9f-e8-b7-a8-e6-a0-a1-e9-9a-9b-e5-8|556. 台美大學生跨校際協會1999手冊/Yale University]].
- 2014 — ITASA alumni events were hosted at Yale, evidencing an active alumni layer around the campus club — see [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/itasa-2014-alumni-events-at-yale|ITASA 2014 Alumni Events at Yale]].
