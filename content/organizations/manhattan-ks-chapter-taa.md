# Manhattan, KS Chapter / TAA (全美台灣同鄉會曼哈頓分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Manhattan, KS Chapter / TAA
- **Chinese:** 全美台灣同鄉會曼哈頓分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America Manhattan Chapter was founded in January 1985 in Manhattan, Kansas, with Chen Shu-Mei serving as its first president. The chapter has approximately eighteen member families, many of whom are also honorary and supporting members of the Kansas State University Taiwanese Student Association. The organization hosts four to five events annually, including Taiwanese New Year gatherings, spring barbecues and picnics, softball and tennis tournaments, and a Christmas social. The chapter has also co-organized Plains Region Taiwanese conferences and camping events drawing nearly two hundred attendees. It maintains close ties with the KSU Taiwanese student community and actively supports cultural preservation efforts for the next generation.

### Founding host of the Plains Region Camp Meeting (平原區台灣人秋令會)
Per 童海南's memoir excerpt from 平原區台灣人夏令會 1996 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys296|Our Journeys 296]]):

- The camp meeting was conceived in 1983 by Manhattan, Kansas chapter pioneers 范良正、楊景文、刁明華、黃金來, pressed forward by 徐福棟's proposal, and organized/convened by 林啓東.
- The **first Plains Region Taiwanese camp meeting was founded in 1985 at Rock Spring**, the 4-H Club training grounds near Manhattan, Kansas, covering six Plains states (Colorado, Iowa, Kansas, Missouri, Oklahoma — as listed); 林啓東 set the first theme 「大家來，來做堆」, deliberately distinguishing the Taiwanese identity from a Chinese one.
- The Manhattan, KS chapter served as host again in **1992** (chair 張洋銘, theme 「Love Taiwan 愛心連愛心，痛惜咱台灣」) and **1997** (chair 吳志行, theme 「誰來救台灣」), per the host-chair roster in the same memoir.
