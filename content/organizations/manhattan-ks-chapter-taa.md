# Manhattan, KS Chapter / TAA (全美台灣同鄉會曼哈頓分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Manhattan, KS Chapter / TAA
- **Chinese:** 全美台灣同鄉會曼哈頓分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America Manhattan Chapter was founded in January 1985 in Manhattan, Kansas, with Chen Shu-Mei serving as its first president. The chapter has approximately eighteen member families, many of whom are also honorary and supporting members of the Kansas State University Taiwanese Student Association. The organization hosts four to five events annually, including Taiwanese New Year gatherings, spring barbecues and picnics, softball and tennis tournaments, and a Christmas social. The chapter has also co-organized Plains Region Taiwanese conferences and camping events drawing nearly two hundred attendees. It maintains close ties with the KSU Taiwanese student community and actively supports cultural preservation efforts for the next generation.
