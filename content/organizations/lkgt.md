# LKGT 「」 (老康健)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** LKGT 「」
- **Chinese:** 老康健
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

LKGT (老康健) is an informal senior social group of Taiwanese Americans in the San Leandro, California area who share common interests including Taiwan politics, travel, and other hobbies. The group has been established for more than ten years and consists of approximately twenty families, with regular Monday gatherings from noon to three o’clock held either indoors or outdoors. Most members hold PhDs in various fields, and every other month a member delivers a speech at a Taiwanese church in San Leandro. LKGT is not officially registered and operates without a formal president, instead selecting one member each year to coordinate group activities.
