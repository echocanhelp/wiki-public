# Bliss & Wisdom Foundation of North America (北美福智基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Bliss & Wisdom Foundation of North America
- **Chinese:** 北美福智基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Bliss & Wisdom Foundation of North America is the North American arm of the Bliss & Wisdom organization, a Buddhist community rooted in the teachings of Lama Tsongkhapa’s Lamrim Chenmo (The Great Treatise on the Stages of the Path to Enlightenment). The broader Bliss & Wisdom organization encompasses a monastic community, Buddhist educational enterprises, cultural and educational initiatives, compassionate agriculture (Cixin), and virtuous business endeavors. Founded under the guidance of Master Jih-Chang, the organization emphasizes spiritual cultivation as the foundation for life education and the development of human potential. Following Master Jih-Chang’s passing in 2004, disciples have continued his mission of fostering a healthy, harmonious world where all beings attain genuine well-being.
