# South Bay Taiwanese Association of N. California (北加州)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** South Bay Taiwanese Association of N. California
- **Chinese:** 北加州
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The South Bay Taiwanese Association of N. California (北加州 南灣台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the South Bay area of Northern California, centered on the Silicon Valley and Santa Clara County region near San Jose. As a TAA chapter, it operates within the national TAA network to support community connection, cultural preservation, and civic engagement among Taiwanese Americans in its local area.

## Role in the Community

- 1971 — Founded as 北加州南灣台灣同鄉會, the first-era regional 同鄉會 of the Taiwanese community concentrated around San Jose/South Bay; the founding president was 謝鐐暲, then employed at IBM (per 何義麟's Bay Area community-history study, band A: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-357|Our Journeys #357, 2020-08]]; the same dating appears in the timeline of [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys106|Our Journeys #106]]).
- 1973-09-22 — The South Bay association was one of the four founding 同鄉會 (with 東南灣, 東灣, and 舊金山) that joined with two Taiwanese Christian fellowships and 台灣協志會 to create the 北加州臺灣同鄉組織聯合會, the Bay Area umbrella body ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-357|Our Journeys #357]]).
- 1993–1995 — Chapter published its own print record: the 1993年刊 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/585-e5-8c-97-e5-8a-a0-e5-b7-9e-e5-8d-97-e7-81-a3-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-90-8c-e9-8|TAH #585, 1993年刊]]) and the 1994會員名錄 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/11851|TAH #586, 1994會員名錄]]) — evidence of an established, documented membership by the mid-1990s.
- 2014 / 2019 — Chapter activity profiled in the TAH story corpus ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/south-bay-taiwanese-association|TAH story, 2014-10-12]]; [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/activities-sbta|TAH story, 2019-02-21]]; [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/enews-northern-california-south-bay-taiwanese-association|TAH eNews, 2019-03-15]]).
