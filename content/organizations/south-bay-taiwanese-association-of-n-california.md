# South Bay Taiwanese Association of N. California (北加州)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** South Bay Taiwanese Association of N. California
- **Chinese:** 北加州
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The South Bay Taiwanese Association of N. California (北加州 南灣台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the South Bay area of Northern California, centered on the Silicon Valley and Santa Clara County region near San Jose. As a TAA chapter, it operates within the national TAA network to support community connection, cultural preservation, and civic engagement among Taiwanese Americans in its local area.
