# Bald Eagle Society (白首老鷹社)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Bald Eagle Society
- **Chinese:** 白首老鷹社
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Bald Eagle Society is a community organization founded in 2014 in Southern California by a group of like-minded individuals passionate about life. The organization provides a platform for mutual learning and community enrichment through monthly lectures held on the second Friday of each month. These gatherings aim to broaden members’ knowledge in the humanities, history, arts, science, culture, and other aspects of daily life, with the goal of enriching community members’ lives and deepening their appreciation for living.
