# North America Taiwanese Engineering & Science Association(NATEA)

**North America Taiwanese Engineering & Science Association(NATEA)** (北美洲台灣工程師協會南加分會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣工程師協會南加分會
- **English (as listed):** North America Taiwanese Engineering & Science Association(NATEA)
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
- **Source:** https://www.taiwancenter.org/group-members · `taiwancenter-org-group-members.md`

## Role in the Community

Corpus check 2026-09-18 (NATEA material in the vault concerns the parent association, not the Southern California chapter specifically — kept as org-level context):

- **Association activity record.** A dedicated TAH record documents NATEA's activities (北美台灣工程師協會的活動) ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/activities-of-natea|Activities of NATEA]]); a parallel record covers the Illinois chapter, confirming NATEA was a multi-chapter North American organization of which this LA chapter is one branch ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natea-illinois-chapter|NATEA Illinois Chapter]]).
- **Taiwan Center co-sponsor.** NATEA is listed among the sponsoring/attending associations in Taiwan Center of Greater Los Angeles event records — the Asian Choral Festival video, concert records, and photo albums ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/video-asian-choral-festival|Asian Choral Festival video]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts26|concerts26]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/photo-albums-activities-19|photo albums: activities 19]]) — consistent with its group-member listing above.
- **Movement directory entry (re-check 2026-09-18).** NATEA appears under "Professional Organizations" in the community movement record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys247|Our Journeys, record 247]], listed alongside NATPA — another instance of org-level (not chapter-level) recognition in the corpus.

- **Dedicated chapter record (re-check 2026-09-20).** The corpus carries a dedicated bibliographic record for this chapter — record 11, published 2015-12-22 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natea-southern-california-chapter|11. Southern California Chapter / NATEA 北美台灣工程師協會南加分會]]); bibliographic record only (full text stays in the TAH vault), so it confirms the chapter's existence and dating but adds no narrative detail.
- **Chapter network.** Parallel dedicated records exist for sibling chapters — Illinois, Florida, Silicon Valley, Dallas, Seattle, Greater New York, Chicago, St. Louis, Austin, North Carolina, Upper State New York (e.g. [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/florida-chapter-natea|Florida chapter]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natea-silicon-valley-chapter|Silicon Valley chapter]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/business-by-natea-dallas|Dallas]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natea-seattle-chapter|Seattle]]) — plus parent-association records: the yearbook ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/annual-yearbook-of-natea|NATEA Annual Yearbook]]), an e-newsletter ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natea-enewsletter|NATEA eNewsletter]]), and a Taiwan homecoming delegation ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natea-tw|北美台灣工程師協會回國訪問團]]).
- **Movement circuit.** A Bay Area community memoir counts NATEA among the associations its alumni led, naming 余金榜 and 施天墩 as NATEA 會長 within the wider movement network ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys37|Our Journeys, record 37]]); a New Jersey community profile likewise notes NATEA chapters holding professional activities and annual meetings alongside NATMA and NATPA ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys356-eng|Our Journeys, record 356]]).

- **Chapter-tagged LA event records (re-check 2026-10-10).** Three Los Angeles-area works in the corpus carry this chapter directly as a Subject — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/2018-6|Asian Choral Festival 2018 (Taiwan Center, Arcadia CA)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/musical-concerts-150|Los Angeles Formosan Master Chorale (2019)]], and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/footsteps-27|Interview artist at latwtvfinearts by Nami Yang (2019)]] — the strongest chapter-level attribution in the corpus to date: the SoCal chapter is indexed as a participant/subject in Taiwan Center arts programming, consistent with its group-member status. Additional movement-directory appearances (same Professional-Organizations listing as record 247 above): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys248|Our Journeys 248]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys251|Our Journeys 251]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwan-oral-history-research-office|台灣口述歷史研究室]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/e4-ba-8c-e3-80-81-e6-88-91-e7-9a-84-e6-95-85-e4-ba-8b|二、我的故事 My Stories]], and concert/photo records [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts98|concerts98]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts69|concerts69]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts18|concerts18]].
