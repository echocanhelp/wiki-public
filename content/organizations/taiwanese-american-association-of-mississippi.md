# Taiwanese American Association of Mississippi (密西西比臺灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Association of Mississippi
- **Chinese:** 密西西比臺灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Association of Mississippi (密西西比臺灣同鄉會) is a chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization serving Taiwanese American communities across the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in Mississippi and is listed in the TAA national chapter directory.
