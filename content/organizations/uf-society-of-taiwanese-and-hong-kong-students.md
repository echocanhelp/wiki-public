# UF Society of Taiwanese and Hong Kong Students

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** UF Society of Taiwanese and Hong Kong Students
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The UF Society of Taiwanese and Hong Kong Students (STAHKS) is a student organization at the University of Florida in Gainesville, Florida. As indicated by its name and LinkedIn presence, STAHKS is distinct from the UF TWSA and focuses specifically on students of Taiwanese and Hong Kong heritage and background. The organization maintains an active Instagram account under the handle @ufstahks and has a LinkedIn company profile under the name “UF Society of Taiwanese and Hong Kong Students.”
