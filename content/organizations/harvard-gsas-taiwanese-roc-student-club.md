# Harvard GSAS Taiwanese ROC Student Club

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Harvard GSAS Taiwanese ROC Student Club
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Harvard GSAS Taiwanese (ROC) Student Club (HTROCSC) was a graduate student organization at Harvard University serving students from Taiwan (Republic of China) and those interested in Taiwanese culture. It is listed in Harvard’s Worldwide international student organization directory with the stated purpose of promoting “culture, academic exchange and mutual understanding between people from Taiwan (ROC) and people in the Harvard community through social, intellectual and cultural activities.” The club maintained a website hosted on the Harvard Computer Society server (hcs.harvard.edu/~htrocsc) and a separate blog (harvardrocc.blogspot.com), both of which documented events including Lunar New Year celebrations, joint activities with the Federation of Taiwanese Student Associations in New England (FTSANE), and new student orientation materials. The most recent content found on the hcs-hosted website references a 2012 new student handbook, and the blog’s last documented posts are from 2009.
