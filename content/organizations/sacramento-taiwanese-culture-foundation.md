# Sacramento Taiwanese Culture Foundation (美國加州首府沙加緬度台灣文化基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Sacramento Taiwanese Culture Foundation
- **Chinese:** 美國加州首府沙加緬度台灣文化基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Sacramento Taiwanese Culture Foundation is a nonprofit organization based in Sacramento, California, dedicated to promoting Taiwanese culture within the Taiwanese American community and beyond. It sponsors cultural activities including performances, exhibitions, and lectures, and organizes field trips and overseas travel for cultural learning experiences. The foundation awards scholarships to Taiwanese American youth who demonstrate community service, and provides grants to those who study and publish materials related to Taiwanese culture. It also promotes Taiwanese language education through a language school, summer camps, and studying tours, and works to establish a Taiwanese Cultural Resources Center to collect and preserve documents, books, articles, and videos related to Taiwanese culture.
