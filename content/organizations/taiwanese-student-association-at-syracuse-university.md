# Taiwanese Student Association at Syracuse University

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Student Association at Syracuse University
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Taiwan Student Association at Syracuse University is a cultural organization of people interested in Taiwanese culture. Our group is not restricted to only Taiwanese students; we welcome people from any race and ethnicity. Our objectives are to promote Taiwan and Taiwanese Culture across campus to students and faculty as well as increase awareness of Taiwan’s position in the International Arena.
