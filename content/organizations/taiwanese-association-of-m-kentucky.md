# Taiwanese Association of M. Kentucky (中肯德基)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of M. Kentucky
- **Chinese:** 中肯德基
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of Middle Kentucky (中肯德基 台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA), the national umbrella organization serving Taiwanese American communities across the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the middle Kentucky area.

## Role in the Community
- The chapter's TAA identity is corroborated in the TAHS story corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/kentucky-central-chapter-taa|Kentucky-Central Chapter / TAA 全美台灣同鄉會肯德基中部分會]] (record published 2015-12-01), whose Chinese title 肯德基中部分會 matches this chapter's 中肯德基.
- Neighboring-organization context: Kentucky also hosted a FAPA chapter, recorded at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/25-kentucky-chapter-fapa|25. Kentucky Chapter / FAPA 台灣人公共事務會肯塔基州分會]] (era 2015).
- Early community scale: the state-by-state survey in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys58|Our Journeys 名錄]] names one Kentucky respondent, 吳連四, in its 1967 founding roster, and the directory's Kentucky appendix count for that same year is 4 names (肯德基州 4名) — small but long-established.

Two deeper checks were run to see whether anything new could be pulled in: (a) 吳連四 — the name recurs in the 2018 台大校友會 roster [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2018/20180721091303_2018_07_20_抗議-南加州台大校友會邀管中閔來年會做專題演講_a428dcebbceacf38|2018 UCLA 台大校友會 dinner]], but there as one of roughly 170 台大 departmental alumni seated at a Los Angeles dinner, with no state and no chapter attached. Nothing in the corpus puts him in Kentucky after 1967, so the 1967 contact → 1972 chapter link stays uncorroborated. (b) The four Kentucky appendix names in the 1967 edition — 吳連四, 林茂生, 張炎憲, 黃奇俊 — were grepped as a set with Kentucky and Kentucky-Central; all four hit only in the appendix line, and none names or joins a Kentucky chapter, so no founding membership can be lifted from it.
- HOLD (three items): (i) founding-era status — the chapter is recorded as "Existed pre-1972 · Active until post-2015", which is only an era band, not a founding date; do not date the chapter. (ii) The 1967 roster lists 吳連四 with no K-State asterisk (the asterisk marks K-State alumni), so he is a Kentucky **resident** respondent, not a Kansas alumnus — do not carry the roster asterisk onto him. (iii) The two Kentucky sources answer different questions — the 1967 directory counts **where people were**, the 2015 chapter record records **an organization** — so the roster is context, not proof of chapter membership, and must not be written as such.
