# Taiwanese Association of M. Kentucky (中肯德基)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of M. Kentucky
- **Chinese:** 中肯德基
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of Middle Kentucky (中肯德基 台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA), the national umbrella organization serving Taiwanese American communities across the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the middle Kentucky area.

## Role in the Community
- The chapter's TAA identity is corroborated in the TAHS story corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/kentucky-central-chapter-taa|Kentucky-Central Chapter / TAA 全美台灣同鄉會肯德基中部分會]] (record published 2015-12-01), whose Chinese title 肯德基中部分會 matches this chapter's 中肯德基.
- Neighboring-organization context: Kentucky also hosted a FAPA chapter, recorded at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/25-kentucky-chapter-fapa|25. Kentucky Chapter / FAPA 台灣人公共事務會肯塔基州分會]] (era 2015).
- Early community scale: the state-by-state survey in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys58|Our Journeys 名錄]] lists one Kentucky name — 吳連四 — among the 94 respondents who answered the 1967 founding circulars, and its Kentucky appendix count for that year is 4 names (肯德基州 4名). The 4-name appendix total and the 1-name founding roster are two different layers of the same 1967 directory and must not be collapsed into one figure.
- Continuity gap (open, not evidence either way): 吳連四 recurs in the 2018 南加州台大校友會 protest roster [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2018/20180721091303_2018_07_20_抗議-南加州台大校友會邀管中閔來年會做專題演講_a428dcebbceacf38|2018 南加州台大校友會年會 protest]], where the same name appears among the 台大 外文系 alumni signatories (in the 外文 group, 吳連四 is the only name listed). No source in the corpus names a Kentucky chapter member or officer, so nothing ties that 2018 signature to the chapter.
