# Taiwanese American Student Association at Penn State

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Student Association at Penn State
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Student Association at Penn State (TASA) is a student organization at Pennsylvania State University’s University Park campus dedicated to connecting students with Taiwanese backgrounds and serving as a cultural and social home for Taiwanese and Taiwanese American students. Known on social media as TASA @ Penn State, the organization operates under its Chinese name 賓州州立大學台灣同學會 and maintains an active presence on both Instagram and Facebook.
