# Tainan East Gate Barclay Memorial Church (東門巴克禮紀念教會)

A [[organizations/presbyterian-church-in-taiwan||PCT]] congregation in **台南中會** (presbytery of Tainan, Taiwan). **Not** a U.S. TPC church. Founded May 1903; renamed 台南東門巴克禮紀念教會 in 2003.

## Identity Snapshot
- **PCT org no.:** C08007
- **Presbytery:** 台南中會 (PCT)
- **Address:** 台南市東區東門路一段187號
- **Founded:** 1903-05-01 (PCT); 福音堂 1903; 太平境支會 1906-06-12; 堂會 1921
- **Website:** [eastgatebarclay.org](https://eastgatebarclay.org/)

- **2003** — Centennial; renamed 巴克禮紀念教會; 18號公園 → 巴克禮紀念公園
- **2020-09-14** — [[people/pinghsi-liu||劉炳熹]] installed as **第十任** pastor (church history)
