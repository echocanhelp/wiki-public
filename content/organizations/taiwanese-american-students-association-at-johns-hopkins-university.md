# Taiwanese American Students Association at Johns Hopkins University

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Students Association at Johns Hopkins University
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Students Association at Johns Hopkins University (jhuTASA) is a student organization at Johns Hopkins University in Baltimore, Maryland. Its founding year is not documented in available first-party sources, though the organization’s official mascot, Tubs — a Taiwanese black bear — was created in 2019. jhuTASA primarily serves Taiwanese American students while welcoming all who wish to connect with Taiwanese culture and community.
