# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Wisconsin/Illinois Chapter (北美洲台灣婦女會威斯康辛)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Wisconsin/Illinois Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣婦女會威斯康辛
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Wisconsin/Illinois Chapter is a local chapter of the North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA), serving the Wisconsin and Illinois area.

## Role in the Community

This chapter is one of the local chapters of [[organizations/north-america-taiwanese-womens-association|北美洲臺灣婦女會 (NATWA)]], the nationwide nonprofit founded in March 1988 in Los Angeles, organised into geographic regions with local chapters across the US and Canada, an annual convention each April, and since 1991 an annual calendar introducing Taiwanese history and culture.

No Wisconsin/Illinois-specific corpus record exists yet in works/ or articles/ — the chapter appears in the TAH directory only, so the facts above are organization-level context, not chapter-specific claims. The chapter sits in the same Midwest region documented by neighbouring chapter and community records: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-st-louis-chapter|NATWA St. Louis Chapter]], [[organizations/north-america-taiwanese-womens-association-kansas-chapter|Kansas Chapter]], and the Chicago-area 台灣人同鄉會 history in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys255|255. 芝加哥同鄉會簡史 (出外人, 10/2016)]], which records how Taiwanese community clubs in the Midwest (including members from Wisconsin) grew into the 1980s wave of 基金會、人權會、婦女會 organizations.

NATWA's organization-wide publications that reach all chapters are held in the corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications555|555. 北美洲台灣婦女會月曆 (1993–2018)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/554-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e7-a|554. 聯誼通訊 (2000–2014)]], and its service work via [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/columns17|17. 關懷網 by NATWA 關懷資訊中心]].
