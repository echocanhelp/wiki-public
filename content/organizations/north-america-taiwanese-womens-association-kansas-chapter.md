# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Kansas Chapter (北美洲台灣婦女會堪薩斯分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Kansas Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣婦女會堪薩斯分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Kansas Chapter is a local chapter of the North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA), serving the Kansas area.

## Role in the Community
- Regional chapter of [[organizations/north-america-taiwanese-womens-association|北美洲台灣婦女會 (NATWA)]], alongside sister chapters such as [[organizations/north-america-taiwanese-womens-association-greater-san-diego-chapter|Greater San Diego]] and [[organizations/north-america-taiwanese-womens-association-oregon-southwest-washington-chapter|Oregon/Southwest Washington]].
- The parent association documented its chapter network in the TAH archive (e.g. [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-san-diego-chapter|Greater San Diego Chapter record]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-oregonsouth-west-chapter|Oregon/South West Chapter record]]), showing the Kansas chapter as part of a multi-chapter network spanning the US.
- NATWA published a membership newsletter across the 1990s: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/663-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|北美洲台灣婦女會聯誼通訊 第七期 (1992-06)]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/679-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|第二十三期 (2000-08)]].
- Parent-level community action is recorded in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys328|Our Journeys 328]]: NATWA jointly donated $21,090 to Houston's Hurricane Harvey Relief Fund with the TFN Foundation, Los Angeles and San Diego Taiwanese Community Centers, and the Houston Tainuan Singers — an example of the relief-giving culture chapters like Kansas participated in.
