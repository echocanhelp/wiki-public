# Wellesley Taiwanese Cultural Organization

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Wellesley Taiwanese Cultural Organization
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Wellesley Taiwanese Cultural Organization (TCO) is a student organization at Wellesley College dedicated to celebrating Taiwanese culture and fostering community among students with ties to Taiwan. The organization is formally recognized through the college’s official WEngage student engagement platform, where it holds regular weekly meetings and hosts a range of cultural and social events throughout the academic year. Its founding year is not documented in available first-party sources.
