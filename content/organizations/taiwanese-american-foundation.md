# Taiwanese-American Foundation

**Taiwanese-American Foundation** (台美基金會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** 台美基金會
- **English (as listed):** Taiwanese-American Foundation
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
- **Source:** https://www.taiwancenter.org/group-members · `taiwancenter-org-group-members.md`

## Role in the Community

The corpus shows an active 台美基金會 publishing and giving awards from the 1980s onward:

- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications784|784. 台美基金會1986季刊]] — quarterly publication, 1986
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/785-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e5-9f-ba-e9-87-91-e6-9c-831995-e9-a0-92-e7-8d-8e-e7-89-b9-|785. 台美基金會1995頒獎特刊]] — award-giving program by 1995
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/787-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e5-9f-ba-e9-87-91-e6-9c-831999-e5-b9-b4-e5-88-8a-e5-8f-b0-|787. 台美基金會1999年刊]]

Regional chapters appear in the record:

- **大西雅圖區台美基金會** — 501(c)(3) founded with 蕭勝雄 as first 理事長; operates the Seattle 台灣會館 (opened 2004-02-28, housed in space rented for US$1/yr from the 陳、林 families; 33-member board; see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys30|Our Journeys 30]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/547-e8-a5-bf-e9-9b-85-e5-9c-96-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e6-9c-83-e9-a4-a8-e5-89-b5-e5-8|547. 西雅圖台灣會館創刊號 2005]])
- **聖地牙哥台美基金會** — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-foundation-of-san-diego|7. Taiwanese American Foundation of San Diego]]
- **波士頓台美基金會** — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-foundation-of-boston|17. Taiwanese-American Foundation of Boston 波士頓台美基金會]]; its scholarship giving has its own corpus record ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-foundation-of-boston-scholarship-award|Taiwanese American Foundation of Boston scholarship award]])

Founding account preserved in the Taiwan Center opening memoir ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys29-eng|29. 南加州台灣會館開創的史料]]): **王桂榮 Kenjohn Wang donated US$1,000,000 to establish the Foundation**, which "awards yearly scholarships to extraordinary talents both in the US and overseas"; the Wang family subsequently donated its 20,000+ sq ft Rosemead estate to the Taiwan Center. **李培吾 Li Pei Wu** — also general convener of the Taiwan Center preparatory committee — was elected **Chairman of the Foundation** and worked with Wang on the Taiwan Center (see also [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/153-kenjohn-wang|153. Kenjohn Wang 王桂榮]]).

Further corpus records:

- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/573-taiwanese-american-foundation-2005-2006tafmagazines-e9-9b-9c-e8-aa-8c|573. Taiwanese American Foundation 2005-2006 TAF雜誌]] — magazine run 2005–2006, extending the publication series 1986季刊 → 1995頒獎特刊 → 1999年刊 → 2005–2006.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/49-taiwanese-american-foundation-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e5-9f-ba-e9-87-91-e6-9c-83-th|49. Taiwanese American Foundation 台美基金會]] — community record describing TAF as **the first Taiwanese American non-profit foundation, 1982**.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/788-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e5-9f-ba-e9-87-91-e6-9c-831982-2000-e9-a0-92-e7-8d-8e-e7-8|788. 台美基金會1982-2000頒獎特刊 (2000)]] — retrospective award special issue covering 1982–2000, consistent with the 1982 founding date in record #49.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/786-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e5-9f-ba-e9-87-91-e6-9c-831997-e5-b9-b4-e5-88-8a-e5-8f-b0-|786. 台美基金會1997年刊]] — fills the publication series between 1995特刊 and 1999年刊.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/88-e6-9d-8e-e6-96-87-e6-9e-9d-1995-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e5-9f-ba-e9-87-91-e6-9c-83-|88. 1995 台美基金會家庭音樂會: 蕭泰然的抒情音樂 / 李文枝]] — the Foundation also sponsored cultural events (family concert of composer 蕭泰然's lyrical music, 1995).
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys337|337. 致我們過去的青春-憶台灣中心台美基金會1998 / 楊禮朱 / 2018-07]] — A-band memoir: the author kept the original signed attendee documents of the Taiwan Center's **first formal meeting in 1998** (台美基金會 context); recalled 20 years later.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/52714|26. TAF of San Diego/Taiwan Center 21st Anniversary (2018-03-10)]] — the San Diego chapter's 21st anniversary & fundraising dinner at San Diego Taiwan Center with 200 attendants, implying the chapter's own 1997 origin.
- Listed among the self-organized, independent overseas Taiwanese groups of the 1980s in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys206|206. 多彩多姿的社團活動 / 台灣公論報 / 2016-03]], and in 黃武東's overview of Taiwanese in North America ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys266|266. 台灣人在北美洲 / 黃武東 / 2016-11]]) — community primary testimony that 台美基金會 was counted among the movement's core bodies by the 1980s.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys162-eng|Our Journeys 162 (EN)]] — grand-opening report excerpted from the *San Diego Taiwanese American Foundation and Taiwan Center YAM Quarterly* (Vol. 11, Issue 3, Autumn): remodeling plaques presented to Dr. Ming Wu and Dr. Winston Lue, opening concert by pianist Julie Wong 翁致理 and soprano Irene Lee 李智惠 (Chinese names per the corpus record's subject links) — the San Diego foundation's own quarterly is corpus primary material.

- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taf-summer-conference|7. Taiwanese American Foundation Summer Camp]] (2015-07-16) — family/youth summer camp; filed in that record under the style 台美協進會 / [[organizations/taiwan-american-foundation|Taiwan American Foundation]]
