# Columbus Taiwanese Association of America (哥倫布)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Columbus Taiwanese Association of America
- **Chinese:** 哥倫布
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Columbus Taiwanese Association of America (TAACOLS), also known in Chinese as 哥倫布台灣同鄉會, is a local chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in Columbus, Ohio, and is classified under the TAA’s Midwest Western (美中西) regional grouping alongside other Ohio, Indiana, Illinois, and neighboring state chapters.
