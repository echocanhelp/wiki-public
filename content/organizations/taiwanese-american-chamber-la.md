# Taiwanese American Chamber of Commerce of Greater Los Angeles

**Taiwanese American Chamber of Commerce of Greater Los Angeles** (洛杉磯台美商會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** 洛杉磯台美商會
- **English (as listed):** Taiwanese American Chamber of Commerce of Greater Los Angeles
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
- **Source:** https://www.taiwancenter.org/group-members · `taiwancenter-org-group-members.md`

## Role in the Community

- Listed as a group member of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]] (official group-members listing).
- TAHS holds the chamber's own yearbook (年刊) run spanning **1991 through 2013** — near-continuous publications evidencing active operation across more than two decades: from the earliest [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/806-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e5-95-86-e6-9c-831991-e5-b9-b4-e5-88-8a-e6-b4-9b-e6-9d-89-|1991年刊]] through [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/813-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e5-95-86-e6-9c-831999-e5-b9-b4-e5-88-8a-e6-b4-9b-e6-9d-89-|1999年刊]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/818-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e5-95-86-e6-9c-832004-e5-b9-b4-e5-88-8a-e6-b4-9b-e6-9d-89-|2004年刊]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/821-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e5-95-86-e6-9c-832007-e5-b9-b4-e5-88-8a-e6-b4-9b-e6-9d-89-|2007年刊]], up to the [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/828-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e5-95-86-e6-9c-832013-e5-b9-b4-e5-88-8a-e6-b4-9b-e6-9d-89-|2013年刊]] (plus a 2010 特刊); the corpus also holds its e-newsletter record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/enewsletter-taccla|eNewsletter TACCLA]] (2017-10-15).
- **TAHS encyclopedia records:** [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/23-e6-b4-9b-e6-9d-89-e7-a3-af-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e5-95-86-e6-9c-83|23. 洛杉磯台美商會/2014/09]] (2014-09) and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-chamber-of-commerce-of-greater-los-angeles|13. Taiwanese American Chamber of Commerce of Greater Los Angeles 洛杉磯台美商會]] (2016-05-06).
- Organizes annual galas with major political engagement: US House Foreign Affairs Chair Ed Royce spoke at the chamber's 年會 on 2018-01-13 — his first appearance after announcing retirement ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240614153408_root_844c315cf7351428|台灣公義報 recording, 2018-01]]). The chamber hosted the 2016-07-24 「2016台灣愛飛揚」晚會 at the Sanctuary (聖蓋博劇院) ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240522043914_root_cbdc98ada329c042|TJJ, 2016-07-10]]).
- Leadership named in the corpus: 會長 **何巧玲** (wife of 世界台灣人商會聯合總會前總會長 楊信; co-convener of the 2016-07 Young Kim for Congress fundraiser, [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240725002611_root_ee76a40eec7c0012|TJJ, 2016]]), later 會長 **劉雅薇** and 副會長 **陳玲華** (2021, [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20210927005058_2021_09_11_靠波霸奶茶發跡洛杉磯_飲料小店做到美股上市_7570d10402d2fc4c|TJJ/CNA, 2021-09-11]]); **陳慶恩** represented the chamber at the 2025-04-20 donation of PPE to the LA County Sheriff's Department ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250420000557_南加州台灣僑團捐醫護用品-助洛杉磯郡警察_5724d4f358a0d5f5|TJJ, 2025-04-20]]). Note: 劉雅薇 vs 陳慶恩 tenure order undated in corpus — HOLD: officer-sequence inferred, not merged.
- Economic-community bridge role: the chamber organized a 2021-09-08 visit of Taiwanese businessmen to the Chino HQ of boba-supplier-turned-NYSE-listed Karat Packaging/樂立杯, with chamber officers hosting ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20210927005058_2021_09_11_靠波霸奶茶發跡洛杉磯_飲料小店做到美股上市_7570d10402d2fc4c|TJJ/CNA, 2021-09-11]]).
- Longstanding pillar of 大洛杉磯台美人傳統週: supported and sponsored Heritage Week continuously for 22+ years as of 2021 ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20210608030909_2021_05_25_2021年台美人傳統週及系列活動5月29日起跑_8531ec4cf23144a1|TJJ, 2021-05-25]]), and again a named sponsor of the 2025 Heritage Week series ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250518185808_2025大洛杉磯台美人傳統週隆重登場-文化融合綻放台_3ea94b0c1ab03c3e|TJJ, 2025-05-18]]).
- Sibling bodies in the vault (distinct orgs, not this chapter): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-chamber-of-commerce-of-oklahoma|奧克拉荷馬台灣商會]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taccnc|北加州台灣工商會]].
- Co-initiating organization (共同發起單位) of the 2021-05-13 Southern California Chinese-community joint statement supporting Taiwan's participation in the World Health Assembly, press conference at 洛杉磯華僑文教服務中心 — corpus record [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20210616072940_2021_05_17_2021年南加州僑界支持台灣參與世界衛生大會wha聯合聲_b1d58af16c0a5e5b|2021年南加州僑界支持台灣參與WHA聯合聲明]]. Listed alongside [[organizations/taiwanese-american-historical-society|台美人歷史協會]] among the 40+ initiating groups.
