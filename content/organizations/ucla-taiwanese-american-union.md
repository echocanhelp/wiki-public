# UCLA Taiwanese American Union

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** UCLA Taiwanese American Union
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The UCLA Taiwanese American Union (TAU) was an undergraduate Taiwanese student organization at UCLA. Its Facebook page describes it as focused on Taiwanese Culture Night and a cappella programming, and a archived UCLA directory listing states its purpose was to educate the campus about Taiwanese culture and to create a bridge between students and the Taiwanese American community. TAU maintained a Facebook page at facebook.com/uclatau, where the most recent identifiable post dates to March 2022.
