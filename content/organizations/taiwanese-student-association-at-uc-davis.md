# Taiwanese Student Association at UC Davis

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Student Association at UC Davis
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Student Association (TSA) at UC Davis — known in Chinese as 台灣同學會 — is a registered student organization at the University of California, Davis. The organization brings together students who share an interest in Taiwanese culture, welcoming both Taiwanese students and those from around the world. According to its description on the UC Davis East Asian Languages & Cultures department club directory, TSA provides social and cultural opportunities for members throughout the year.

## Role in the Community

- **Heir to the TSA movement of the 1960s:** campus 台灣同學會 chapters descend from the first Taiwanese Student Association, founded at the University of Kansas in 1961 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ff33|33. Taiwanese Student Association – University of Kansas / 1961]]. The UC Davis chapter is part of this continental lineage documented across the TAH corpus.
- **UC Davis in movement history:** the Davis campus hosted the 10屆世界台灣同鄉聯合會年會 (1983-07-01–04), the first 世臺會 annual conference organized by the 北加州台灣同鄉聯合會 (then under 第十屆會長黃景生) — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys38|38. 北加州台灣同鄉聯合會三十年回顧 / 石清正 / 2014/10]]; the same event appears in the Bay Area community-formation chronology — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-357|357. 舊金山灣區臺灣人的社團活動與家國意識之形成 / 何義麟 / 2020/08]].
- HOLD: the 1983 世臺會 hosting is a UC Davis *campus* fact from 北加州同鄉聯合會 memoirs, not a record of this registered student organization itself (its Era is Contemporary); no corpus record ties the present-day TSA chapter to that event. Not merged into the org's own timeline.
- **2013 — campus activity record:** UC Davis hosted an ITASA (台美學生社) mixer on 2013-04-27, catalogued in the taiwaneseamerican.org corpus ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/itasa-mixer-at-uc-davis|ITASA Mixer at UC Davis, 2013-04-27]]) — a contemporary record of the campus Taiwanese student circle in the chapter's own era.
- **Sibling TSA chapters in the corpus:** USC 南加大台灣同學會 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/dumplings-usctsa|燒肉粽 by USC Taiwanese Student Association]]; OSU 哥城台灣同學會 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/osu-taiwanese-student-association|Newsletter 會訊 by OSU Taiwanese Student Association]].
