# NYU Taiwanese American Student Society

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** NYU Taiwanese American Student Society
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The NYU Taiwanese American Student Society (TASS) is a student organization at New York University founded in 2002. According to its website, TASS was established “with the goal and intention of creating a welcoming and unified family that could come together to learn and appreciate Taiwanese culture.” The organization describes itself as working to build “an integrated community where people can feel comfortable socializing and meeting new people among its hundreds of members.”

## Role in the Community

Community-press coverage names NYU TASS directly across the 2011–2013 wave of Taiwanese student organizing in New York. The society hosted general meetings reported in the movement press ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/nyus-tass-hosts-first-general-meeting|NYU's TASS Hosts First General Meeting]]; duplicate press-record variant [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/nyus-tass-hosts-first-general-meeting-2|-2, 2012-09-27]]; [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/nyu-tass-second-general-meeting|NYU TASS Second General Meeting]], 2012-02-02 — NOTE: a first-general-meeting report is dated 2012-09-27 while a second-meeting report is dated 2012-02-02; HOLD: conflict in meeting chronology, never auto-merge), and co-hosted cultural events with the wider NYC Taiwanese student circle: [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/night-market-in-nyc-hosted-by-nyus-tass|Night Market in NYC hosted by NYU's TASS]] (2012-04-19), [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/tass-night-market-at-nyu|TASS Night Market at NYU]] (2013-04-11), and [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/taste-of-taiwan-with-tass-at-nyu|Taste of Taiwan with TASS at NYU]] (2013-11-21). Adjacent 2011 events are reported under "NYU's TSA" branding ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/halloween-party-sponsored-by-nyus-tsa|Halloween party sponsored by NYU's TSA]], 2011-10-28; [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/college-students-party-with-nyus-tass-at-pink-bash-ii|College students party with NYU's TASS at Pink Bash II]], 2011-10-15) — HOLD: TSA vs TASS naming may be a precursor/renamed club, not auto-merged.
