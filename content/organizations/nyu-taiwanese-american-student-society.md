# NYU Taiwanese American Student Society

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** NYU Taiwanese American Student Society
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The NYU Taiwanese American Student Society (TASS) is a student organization at New York University founded in 2002. According to its website, TASS was established “with the goal and intention of creating a welcoming and unified family that could come together to learn and appreciate Taiwanese culture.” The organization describes itself as working to build “an integrated community where people can feel comfortable socializing and meeting new people among its hundreds of members.”
