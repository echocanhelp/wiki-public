# Taiwanese American Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Student Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Student Association (TASA) at Vanderbilt University is a registered student organization serving the Taiwanese and Taiwanese American community on campus. The organization is formally recognized by Vanderbilt’s Student Center for Belonging and Communities (SCBC), which lists TASA among its advised cultural organizations, and holds an official registration on the university’s Anchor Link platform.
