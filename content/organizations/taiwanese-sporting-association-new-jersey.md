# Taiwanese Sporting Association, New Jersey (紐澤西台灣人運動協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Sporting Association, New Jersey
- **Chinese:** 紐澤西台灣人運動協會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Sporting Association of New Jersey is a community organization centered on bringing people together through sports and recreation. The association hosts a wide variety of weekly sporting competitions and activities, including table tennis, softball, tennis, swimming, and yoga. In addition to athletic events, the group regularly organizes themed lectures and outdoor excursions. Activity levels tend to be higher in the summer months and lighter during winter.
