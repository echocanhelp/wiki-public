# Taiwanese American Association of Pharmaceutics (台美藥劑協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Association of Pharmaceutics
- **Chinese:** 台美藥劑協會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Association of Pharmaceutics (TAAP) is a professional organization with members located primarily in the United States and Taiwan. Its membership consists largely of individuals with advanced degrees in life science-related fields, including academic researchers, senior scientists, and professionals in managerial positions. Members work across various areas of biopharmaceutical discovery and development. The organization serves as a network bridging Taiwanese American pharmaceutical and biotech professionals across both countries.
