# Stanford Taiwanese Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Stanford Taiwanese Student Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Stanford Taiwanese Student Association (STSA) is a non-political, non-religious, and non-profit voluntary student organization serving the community of Taiwanese students and staff at Stanford University. The organization is listed as an affiliate of Stanford’s Asian American Activities Center (A³C) and primarily serves graduate students and staff from Taiwan, helping newcomers acclimate to academic and social life at Stanford.

## Role in the Community
- **2022 — record of the Bay Area tech layoff wave's impact on Taiwanese students.** A Taiwan Justice report on the Silicon Valley layoffs describes roughly 100 Taiwanese graduate students at Stanford, about 80% in tech-related fields, and quotes STSA president 林柏廷 on how the job fair that year offered mostly internships rather than full-time positions — software students felt the cutbacks most acutely ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2022/20221205052636_2022_11_21_加州矽谷掀裁員潮_台灣留學生_畢業工作難找_8679d1279a2cceec|TJJ report, 2022-11-21]]).
