# United Taiwanese Association at UC San Diego

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** United Taiwanese Association at UC San Diego
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The United Taiwanese Association (UTA) at UC San Diego — known in Chinese as 台灣學友會 — is a registered, non-profit student-run sociocultural organization founded in 1992. It is one of the longer-established Taiwanese student organizations at a UC campus and describes itself as one of the largest Asian-interest student organizations at UCSD, with over 200 members and more than 100 events per year as noted on its official website.

## Role in the Community
- The association has its own TAH story-corpus record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ucsd-united-taiwanese-association|UCSD United Taiwanese Association 台灣學友會]] (published 2017-02-16), confirming the Chinese name 台灣學友會.
- 2015: the Chuan Lyu Endowed Chair in Taiwan Studies 川流台灣研究講座基金 was established at UCSD [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/30-chuan-lyu-endowed-chair-in-taiwan-studies|30. Chuan Lyu Endowed Chair in Taiwan Studies, UCSD（07/2015）]] — an earlier layer of the campus Taiwan-studies ecosystem around UTA, predating the 2023 center gift below.
- UCSD's Taiwan-studies ecosystem around the association grew sharply in the 2020s: a US$5 million gift established the interdisciplinary Center of Taiwan Studies at UCSD [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-386|UCSD獲500萬成立台灣研究中心跨學科合作（05/2023）]], with the founding director's first-year review recorded at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-390|390. 2023 in Review — Founding Director's Year-end Message from UCSD's Center of Taiwan Studies（12/2023）]].
- 2011: the revived 美西夏令會 (US West Taiwanese summer camp) contracted with UCSD as venue on 1/28 per 黃根深's retrospective [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys265|265. 2011年美西夏令會的回顧（黃根深 11/2016）]]; new volunteer 賴慧娜 joined through the camp and took charge of the 台灣之夜 program — the camp movement and campus groups like UTA shared the same student pipeline (connection inferred, not stated in source).
