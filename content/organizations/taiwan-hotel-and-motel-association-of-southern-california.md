# Taiwan Hotel &Motel Association of Southern California (THMASC)

**Taiwan Hotel &Motel Association of Southern California (THMASC)** ([[organizations/laguna-woods-village-taiwanese-club||南加州]]台灣旅館業同業公會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** [[organizations/san-diego-taiwanese-cultural-association||南加州]]台灣旅館業同業公會
- **English (as listed):** Taiwan Hotel &Motel Association of Southern California (THMASC)
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
- **Source:** https://www.taiwancenter.org/group-members · `taiwancenter-org-group-members.md`

## Role in the Community

- Recorded in the TAH story corpus as **the first Taiwanese American organization in the business field** (第一個台美人商業團體), per the 08/1974 record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ff261|261. 第一個台美人商業團體 / 08/1974]]; a dedicated corpus record also exists: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwan-hotel-motel-association-of-southern-california|Taiwan Hotel & Motel Association of Southern California 南加州台灣旅館業同業公會]].
- Community memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys29-eng|Our Journeys 29 (ENG)]] credits **Kenjohn Wang** (then OCACC commissioner) with first founding the Taiwan Hotel-Motel Association (alongside the Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce) to protect the welfare of local Taiwanese associations.
- The association organizes community activities, incl. a **Charity Golf Tournament 慈善高爾夫球友誼賽** ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/golf-thmasc|record, 2018]]) and hosted **台灣餐旅系學員美國暑假在職訓練營**, an in-service summer training camp for hotel/restaurant-management students from Taiwan ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/12-63|63. record, 2016]]).
- Locally it is a group member of [[organizations/taiwan-center|Taiwan Center Foundation]]; at the continental level it relates to the umbrella body 北美洲台灣旅館公會聯合總會 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwan-hotel-motel-association-of-north-america|record, 2017]]).
