# Taiwanese American Citizens League

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Citizens League
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Citizens League (TACL) is a national 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization established on July 13, 1985 by Li-Pei Wu. Founded to promote the rights and welfare of Taiwanese Americans and foster active participation in American civic life, TACL modeled its approach on the Japanese American Citizens League (JACL), which had demonstrated how a minority community could build institutional credibility and political voice. TACL is run entirely by unpaid volunteers.

## Role in the Community
The corpus documents TACL's founding leadership and chapter network across the Taiwanese American movement. Its first president was Li-Pei Wu (吳澧培), serving from 1985, recorded in the TAH community history ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/26-li-pei-wu-the-first-president-of-taiwanese-american-citizen-league-tacl-1985|Li-Pei Wu, first TACL president]]). Chapter-level records include the Houston chapter ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tacl-houston-chapter|TACL Houston]], 2014) and Northern California ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tacl-northern-california|TACL NorCal]]), a TACL Toastmaster Club ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tacl-toastmaster-club|TACL Toastmaster Club]]), and the Leadership Identity Development camp that seeded the next generation ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-citizens-league-leadership-identity-development-tacl-lid-camp|TACL-LID Camp]], 2015/07). TACL also co-published a national quarterly newsletter with TAP ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/tacl-tap-national-quarterly-newsletter|TACL/TAP national quarterly newsletter]], 2017).

- **Second president 周實 (1986-11)** turned TACL into the census-advocacy engine of the movement: at the 1986-12-17 board meeting he moved the creation of a Census Committee (周實、鍾茂智、黃森元、楊子清), which became the 「1990年美國人口普查台美專案委員會」; TACL then had an office in Rosemead and 總幹事 鄭金德. His own band-A memoir is preserved in the corpus — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys253|253. 1990年美國人口普查-TACL的角色 / 周實 /09/2016]].
- **1993-08-07** — TACL-NJ, together with WATAA, sponsored the first Taiwanese-American softball tournament for the second generation at Woodlot Park, South Brunswick, N.J. — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ff347|347. Taiwanese-American Softball Tournament – New Jersey Invitational]].
- Youth leadership pipeline beyond the LID Camp: the TACL Summer Conference 台美青少年夏令營 is held in the corpus as a bibliographic record — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/33-tacl-summer-conference|33. TACL Summer Conference 台美青少年夏令營]].
