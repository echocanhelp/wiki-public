# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Greater San Diego Chapter (北美洲台灣婦女會聖地牙哥分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Greater San Diego Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣婦女會聖地牙哥分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Greater San Diego Chapter is a local chapter of the North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA), serving the Greater San Diego area.

## Role in the Community
- The chapter has its own dedicated story record in the TAH story corpus: «5. Greater San Diego Chapter / NATWA 北美洲台灣婦女會聖地牙哥分會», published 2015-12-28 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-san-diego-chapter|NATWA Greater San Diego Chapter story]]). Text stays in the vault; the page is a bibliographic record.
- A second chapter-specific record documents its activities: 北美洲台灣婦女會聖地牙哥分會的活動, published 2019-02-24 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/activities-of-natwa-gsd|NATWA GSD chapter activities]]), bringing the chapter's own documentary footprint to two dated records (2015, 2019).
- The parent body [[organizations/north-america-taiwanese-womens-association|NATWA]] published the membership newsletter 北美洲台灣婦女會聯誼通訊 (corpus records e.g. 第七期 1992-06, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/663-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|record 663]]; 第二十三期 2000-08, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/679-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|record 679]]); NATWA also ran a 關懷資訊中心 network ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/columns17|關懷網]]).
- **2015 — San Diego hosted NATWA's national convention.** The 2015 NATWA Convention was held at the San Diego Marriott Mission Valley, 2015-04-16–19, themed "Telling Our History Through Prose, Poetry and Art," with a NATWA II collegiate/young-professional track (2015-04-17–19) and a 2nd-generation panel ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/natwa2015|National Taiwanese American Women's Conference in San Diego, 2015-03-11]]). The announcement places the chapter's city at the center of NATWA's 80s-founded, 1st-through-3rd-generation community that year.
- Corpus mentions of NATWA disaster relief (e.g. the 2017 Hurricane Harvey Relief Fund joint donation of $21,090) concern NATWA as a whole / the Houston chapter, not the San Diego chapter specifically.
