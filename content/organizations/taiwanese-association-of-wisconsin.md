# Taiwanese Association of Wisconsin (威斯康辛)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of Wisconsin
- **Chinese:** 威斯康辛
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of Wisconsin (威斯康辛 台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization serving Taiwanese American communities across the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the state of Wisconsin.
