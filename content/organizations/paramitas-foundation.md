# Paramitas Foundation

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Paramitas Foundation
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Paramitas Foundation was founded by Winston Chen in 1992. The Foundation supports universities, environmental and community service organizations. Since its founding, it has established numerous scholarships and research projects at Stanford University, Harvard University, Santa Clara University, California State University, University of Vermont, and National Cheng Kung University.

## Role in the Community
