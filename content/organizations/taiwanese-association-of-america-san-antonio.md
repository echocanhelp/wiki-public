# Taiwanese Association of America San Antonio (聖安東尼)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of America San Antonio
- **Chinese:** 聖安東尼
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America San Antonio (TAASA) is a chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization of Taiwanese American community associations. TAASA serves the Taiwanese American community in the San Antonio, Texas area and is listed under the TAA’s Southern region (美南).
