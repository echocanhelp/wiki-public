# Youth Orchestra, CYCNY (紐約幼獅青少年管弦樂團)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Youth Orchestra, CYCNY
- **Chinese:** 紐約幼獅青少年管弦樂團
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The CYCNY Youth Orchestra is a Queens-based youth orchestra founded in 1996 and incorporated as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in 2002. It provides orchestra training and performance opportunities for students aged 10–18, with a repertory spanning Classical, Popular, Jazz, Broadway, Film, and Asian music. The orchestra premieres and commissions works by American and Asian composers, and has organized multiple concert tours to Asia, Europe, and across the United States. Weekly rehearsals are held at Middle School 158 in Bayside, New York, with two formal annual concerts: a free Holiday Concert in Queens and an Annual Concert at Lincoln Center. The orchestra also serves the broader Queens community through performances at senior centers, community centers, and nursing homes.
