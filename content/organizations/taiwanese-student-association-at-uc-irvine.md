# Taiwanese Student Association at UC Irvine

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Student Association at UC Irvine
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Student Association (TSA) at the University of California, Irvine is a student organization listed among the recognized Asian American campus organizations by the UCI School of Humanities. The organization maintains a Facebook page (facebook.com/tsauci) and an Instagram account (@uci.tsa), though neither carries a formal mission statement or founding year in their public-facing content.
