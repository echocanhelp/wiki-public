# UW iTaiwan Graduate Students and Professional Organization

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** UW iTaiwan Graduate Students and Professional Organization
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The UW iTaiwan Graduate Students and Professional Organization (Chinese name: 西雅圖華大台灣研究生社) is listed as a Taiwanese student organization at the University of Washington on the UW Jackson School of International Studies Taiwan Studies Program community page. It is categorized alongside TSAUW and TOSAUW as one of three UW student organizations oriented toward the Taiwanese community on campus.
