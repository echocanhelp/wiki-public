# Taiwanese American Association Baltimore (巴爾地摩)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Association Baltimore
- **Chinese:** 巴爾地摩
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Association Baltimore (巴爾地摩 台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations across the United States. The chapter is listed under the TAA’s East Northern (美東北) regional grouping and serves the Greater Baltimore and Columbia area of Maryland, with its presence centered in Ellicott City, Maryland.

## Role in the Community
The chapter's origin is recorded first-hand in the memoir 華府與巴城TAA的誕生 by 陳淮崇 (Norman Chen), [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys-138|TAH #138 (band A, 2015/05)]]:

- 1965 — FMC chemical engineer 陳淮崇, newly arrived in Baltimore, compiled the 「Greater Baltimore/Washington台灣人名錄」 by working through the local phone book for Chinese-sounding surnames.
- 1966 (農曆新年) — he convened the first Taiwanese gathering in Baltimore and was elected its first 會長; the program included 台灣民謠 singing and a satirical skit mocking the 蔣 regime's surveillance of students abroad. Activities were deliberately held midway between Baltimore and Washington DC, with Chesapeake Bay steam-crab picnics.
- Watch era: the group took no commemorative photos to protect exchange-visa doctors from KMT informant reporting.
- 1968–1970 — while 陳淮崇 was posted to Houston (where he helped found the 休士頓台灣同鄉會), the Washington DC members split off; in 1969 the DC Taiwanese formed their own 同鄉會, leaving the Baltimore chapter's lineage distinct.
- Related Baltimore-area civic bodies documented in TAH: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natma-baltimore-chapter|NATMA 巴爾地摩分會]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natpa-baltimoredc-chapter|NATPA 巴爾的摩/華府分會]].

The chapter itself is documented in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/baltimore-columbia-chapter-taa|TAH: Baltimore & Columbia Chapter / TAA 全美台灣同鄉會巴爾的摩分會]].

HOLD: founding year — 陳淮崇's memoir places the first gathering at 農曆新年 1966, while his own TAH bio counts "1967 Baltimore/Washington同鄉會" as the founding; not merged. Also note 陳淮崇 record: he insisted the name avoid 「台灣同鄉」 (implying Taiwan is not a country), yet the directory's 同鄉會 naming persisted.
