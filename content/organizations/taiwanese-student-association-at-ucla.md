# Taiwanese Student Association at UCLA

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Student Association at UCLA
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Student Association (TSA) at UCLA is a student organization dedicated to uniting the Taiwanese community on campus. Founded in 1988, TSA is one of the longer-standing Taiwanese student groups at the university. Its stated mission is to gather Taiwanese people, host events related to Taiwanese cultures and traditions, and help Taiwanese students integrate into UCLA life across both cultural and academic dimensions. The organization’s email address (ucla.tsa.1988@gmail.com) and a 2025 ASUCLA publication both reference the 1988 founding year.

## Role in the Community

The UCLA campus was one of the earliest hubs of organized Taiwanese student activity in the US. Decades before TSA's 1988 founding, a 1960s UCLA-centered study group (陳嶄新, 李木通, 黃根深, 王秋森, 陳松貞 and others) met weekly for talks and reading reports — the embryo of the 生活座談會 movement, per 劉天良's memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys212|生活座談會簡史]], and recalled directly in 李木通/黃樹人's memoir of UCLA in that era [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys240|半世紀前 UCLA的那些日子]]. TSA (founded 1988) is the contemporary continuation of this student organizing lineage on campus.
