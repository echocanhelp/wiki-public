# Taiwanese Association Of America – Detroit TAGD (底特律台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association Of America – Detroit TAGD
- **Chinese:** 底特律台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America – Detroit (TAGD), known in Chinese as 底特律台灣同鄉會, is the Detroit-area chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. TAGD serves the Taiwanese American community in the greater Detroit, Michigan region and is listed under the TAA Mid-West (美中西) regional grouping.

## Role in the Community
The TAH story corpus holds primary records of Detroit-area Taiwanese community activity that document the chapter's milieu:

- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwan-monitor|Taiwan Monitor 台風眼]] — community newspaper published jointly by the TAA Ann Arbor, Detroit, and Lansing chapters: the chapter's own press organ, corroborating TAGD as an active 美中西 chapter ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76-eng|Our Journeys #76]] also lists the Ann Arbor/Detroit/East Lansing chapter newsletter).
- 1981 — in the wake of the 陳文成 case, Detroit (with Pittsburgh, Ann Arbor, Lansing) formed the 底特律台灣人人權會 initiative that opened public fundraising for the 陳文成紀念基金會; the second-drive donors and memorial-service workers included Detroit-area names Bernie Huang, Chao-fang Chuang, I-hsiung Lin, Kuo-nan Hsu, Chin-ti Lin, Hui-hui Huang, and Chang-Jang Hsieh ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys321-eng|Our Journeys #321 (ENG)]])。
- 2014 — companion Detroit record: 底特律婦女會 2014 台灣美食小吃盛會 (NATWA) ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/photo-albums-activities-24|24. 底特律婦女會2014年台灣美食小吃盛會]]).
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-association-of-america-detroit-chapter-activity|Taiwanese Association of America Detroit Chapter (底特律台灣同鄉會的活動)]] — a 2019 community record of Detroit chapter activity (published 2019-01-21), the corpus's direct documentation of TAGD.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/9291970-prof-peng-ming-min-arrived-at-detroit-mi|9/29/1970 — Prof. Peng Ming-Min arrived at Detroit, MI]] — photographic record of Peng Ming-min's 1970 arrival in Detroit, early Detroit Taiwanese community history (TAGD's involvement in this event is not stated in the record).
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-chambers-of-commerce-of-greater-detroit|9. Taiwanese Chambers of Commerce of Greater Detroit 底特律台灣商會]] — companion Detroit-area Taiwanese organization record.
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/enews-taiwanese-association-of-america-detroit-chapter|TAA/Detroit Chapter (底特律台灣同鄉會)]] — 2019-10-15 e-news bibliographic record of the chapter (published later than the January 2019 activity record above).
- [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys33|33. 一家人做伙來打拼 (張炎憲, 2014/09)]] — band-A family memoir naming Detroit chapter leadership: 張月英 (Zhang family fifth daughter, Soochow/Fu-Jen philosophy grad, former teacher in Keelung) served as **vice president of 底特律台灣同鄉會 in 1997** after taking over (and eventually closing, 1997) the family business left by her husband 張伯寬; she then chaired 底特律婦女會 (1999), served as secretary of the North America Taiwanese Women's Association (2002), and as secretary-general of 全美台灣同鄉聯合會 (2005–2006). Primary memoir documentation of TAGD officer activity.
