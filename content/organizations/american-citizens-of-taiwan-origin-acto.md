# American Citizens of Taiwan Origin ACTO (美台公民協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** American Citizens of Taiwan Origin ACTO
- **Chinese:** 美台公民協會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

American Citizens of Taiwan Origin (ACTO) is a non-profit 501(c)(3) organization founded in 1988 to serve the Taiwanese American community in the Northern Virginia area. ACTO promotes active participation of American citizens of Taiwan origin in the political process and advocates for equal opportunity and justice for Asian Americans. The organization contributes to American society through educational and community services, and works to preserve the cultural heritage of its members. ACTO operates affiliated organizations including the Northern Virginia Chinese School and the Investment Information Exchange Community (I2EC), and runs an annual Outstanding Student Award recognizing academic and public service achievement.
