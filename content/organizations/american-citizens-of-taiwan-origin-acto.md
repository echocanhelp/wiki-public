# American Citizens of Taiwan Origin ACTO (美台公民協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** American Citizens of Taiwan Origin ACTO
- **Chinese:** 美台公民協會
- **Era:** Contemporary (founded 1988, per self-report)
- **Geography:** Northern Virginia / Washington DC metro, Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory; site taxonomy `organization_type-social`)

American Citizens of Taiwan Origin (ACTO) is a non-profit 501(c)(3) organization founded in 1988 to serve the Taiwanese American community in the Northern Virginia area. ACTO promotes active participation of American citizens of Taiwan origin in the political process and advocates for equal opportunity and justice for Asian Americans. The organization contributes to American society through educational and community services, and works to preserve the cultural heritage of its members. ACTO operates affiliated organizations including the Northern Virginia Chinese School and the Investment Information Exchange Community (I2EC), and runs an annual Outstanding Student Award recognizing academic and public service achievement.

## Record (self-reported detail, from the 2015-08-05 corpus dump)
- **Stated objectives (alleged, per `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/american-citizens-of-taiwan-origin-acto.md`):** Responsible Citizenship (active participation of American citizens of Taiwan origin in the American political process); Community Service (educational and other community services); Cultural Heritage (preserve the cultural heritage of American citizens of Taiwan origin, work with other Asian American organizations); Equal opportunity (advocate for justice and fairness toward American citizens of Taiwan origin; oppose prejudice and discrimination against Asian Americans).
- **Affiliates (alleged, same dump):** Northern Virginia Chinese School — 「a non-profit language school based in Marshall High School in Falls Church, Virginia」; Investment Information Exchange Community (I2EC) — 「a non-profit organization for educating the community on the basics of investment」, meetings in Falls Church, Virginia.
- **Activities (alleged, same dump):** ACTO Outstanding Student Award — annual, 「given to a student based on academic record and public service」; Senior Citizens Foundation — services for senior citizens 「including transportation during elections」.
- **Scholarship terms (alleged, per `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/40-american-citizens-of-taiwan-origin-acto-realco-second-spring-scholarship-award.md`):** ACTO Realco Second Spring Scholarship Award — college-board seniors in local high schools, enrolled in their first or second year at a U.S. college/university, of Taiwan origin; USA territory, up to 10 awards, $1000 per recipient plus an engraved plaque; sponsor ACTO 美台公民協會; status Active; website `http://www.actous.org/`.
