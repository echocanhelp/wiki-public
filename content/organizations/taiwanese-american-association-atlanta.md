# Taiwanese American Association Atlanta (亞特蘭大)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Association Atlanta
- **Chinese:** 亞特蘭大
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Association Atlanta (ATAA; 亞特蘭大 台灣同鄉會) is the Atlanta-area chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA; 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization of Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the greater Atlanta, Georgia area.

## Role in the Community
Corpus records showing the chapter's activity:
- Founding — the Atlanta chapter was organized under the leadership of Evan Ling during TAA president Ho Ru Hsu's term, one of 9 new chapters added onto the 34 that existed at the end of Yi-Ho Cheng's term (bringing TAA to 43 chapters; only ~10 kept regular contact with the national body). Per the TAA memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76-eng|Our Journeys #76 (TAA history, Eng.)]]; Evan Ling profiled at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whos-who-1679-evan-ling|TAH #1679 Evan Ling]].
- 1991–1992 — published the 美東南區台灣人名錄 (Southeast US Taiwanese directory/yearbook) [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1330|1330. 美東南區台灣人名錄 1991-1992]].
- 2014 — chapter profile in the TAH encyclopedia [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/atlanta-taiwanese-association-of-america|Atlanta Taiwanese Association of America (ATAA) 亞特蘭大台灣同鄉會]].
- 2015 — awarded outstanding-youth scholarships to local students [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/25-atlanta-taiwanese-american-association-scholarship-award|25. 亞特蘭大台灣同鄉會優秀青年獎學金]].
