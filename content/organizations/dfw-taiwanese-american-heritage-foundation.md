# DFW Taiwanese American Heritage Foundation (達福台美人傳統文化基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** DFW Taiwanese American Heritage Foundation
- **Chinese:** 達福台美人傳統文化基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Heritage Foundation (TAHF) is a community organization based in the Dallas/Fort Worth, Texas area dedicated to promoting Taiwanese culture and heritage. Since 2003, TAHF has sponsored the Taiwan Exhibit at Mayfest, an annual event held at Trinity Park in Fort Worth during the first week of May. Through displays, games, demonstrations, and brochures, the event offers local communities an opportunity to learn about Taiwan and Taiwanese culture and heritage.
