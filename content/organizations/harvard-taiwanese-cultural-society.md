# Harvard Taiwanese Cultural Society

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Harvard Taiwanese Cultural Society
- **Chinese:** 哈佛大學台灣文化學會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Harvard Taiwanese Cultural Society (TCS) is a student-run organization at Harvard College dedicated to promoting the culture and heritage of Taiwan and Taiwanese Americans. Alumni records on the organization’s archived website document members as far back as the Class of 1995, placing the group’s founding in the early-to-mid 1990s, though an exact founding year is not confirmed in first-party sources. TCS describes its vision as fostering “a community in which people of all backgrounds can share, explore, and further develop an interest in Taiwanese culture,” and is recognized by Harvard College as one of the university’s student affinity groups.

## Programming & structure
- Weekly Wednesday dinners, cultural events such as dumpling-making, social events like karaoke — `per` TAH organization capture `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/organization/harvard-taiwanese-cultural-society.json` (alleged: first-party self-report).
- Signature annual **Night Market** in the fall semester, replicating Taiwanese night markets with food from Taiwanese caterers, student performances, and carnival-style games — `per` the same organization capture (alleged). Corroborated in kind (different campus context) by the Boston Taiwanese Night Market story `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamerican-org/taiwanese-night-market-in-boston.md`.
- **"sib-fam"** mentorship structure pairing members into smaller family-like groups; motto **"Small Island, Big Family"** — `per` the same organization capture (alleged).
- Annual **Winterfest** traditionally organized by four freshmen, feeding ~300 attendees with help from local "aunties" — Maggie Hsu testimony, [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/maggie-hsu-mochimag|TAH interview, 2015-05-29]].

## Affiliations
- Longstanding participation in the **Boston Intercollegiate Taiwanese Students Association (BITSA)**, with documented involvement dating to BITSA's founding era in the **late 1990s** — `per` TAH organization capture (alleged).
- Co-host of **哈佛臺灣英文領導營 / Harvard Undergraduate Taiwan Leadership Conference** with 國立臺北大學 (National Taipei University): all teachers are current Harvard students; peer-teaching through debate and communication training; the **2008** edition drew ~400 applicants nationwide and selected 52; 20 NTU students joined as 生活輔導團隊; free for high-school participants, funded by NTU alumni fundraising (`zh-htlc.weebly.com`) — `per` TAH story #26 `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/26-harvard-undergraduate-taiwan-leadership-conference.md`, work page [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/26-harvard-undergraduate-taiwan-leadership-conference]] (alleged: single TAH editorial record).
