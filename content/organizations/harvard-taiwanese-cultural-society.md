# Harvard Taiwanese Cultural Society

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Harvard Taiwanese Cultural Society
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Harvard Taiwanese Cultural Society (TCS) is a student-run organization at Harvard College dedicated to promoting the culture and heritage of Taiwan and Taiwanese Americans. Alumni records on the organization’s archived website document members as far back as the Class of 1995, placing the group’s founding in the early-to-mid 1990s, though an exact founding year is not confirmed in first-party sources. TCS describes its vision as fostering “a community in which people of all backgrounds can share, explore, and further develop an interest in Taiwanese culture,” and is recognized by Harvard College as one of the university’s student affinity groups.
