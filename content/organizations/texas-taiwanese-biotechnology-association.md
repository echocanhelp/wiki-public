# Texas Taiwanese Biotechnology Association (德州台灣生物科技協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Texas Taiwanese Biotechnology Association
- **Chinese:** 德州台灣生物科技協會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Texas Taiwanese Biotechnology Association is a young organization established by graduate students, postdoctoral fellows, and researchers affiliated with major Texas research institutions including UT Southwestern, Baylor College of Medicine, UT MD Anderson Cancer Center, UT HSC at Houston, Rice University, UT Austin, and Texas A&M University. The organization serves as a professional and intellectual network for Taiwanese biomedical scientists in Texas. It focuses on facilitating career development for its members both in the United States and Taiwan.
