The Student Association of Taiwan (SAT) at the University of Wisconsin–Madison is a student organization serving Taiwanese students, local students, and international students at the university. Its founding year is not documented in available first-party sources. The organization is registered in the university’s Wisconsin Involvement Network (WIN) club directory under the slug “taiwanesestudent.”
