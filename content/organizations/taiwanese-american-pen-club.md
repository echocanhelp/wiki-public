# Taiwanese American Pen Club

**Taiwanese American Pen Club** ([[organizations/taiwanese-american-irvines-eagle-brotherhood-group-irvine||台美人]]筆會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** [[organizations/taiwanese-american-pen-club-la||台美人筆會]]
- **English (as listed):** Taiwanese American Pen Club
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
- **Source:** https://www.taiwancenter.org/group-members · `taiwancenter-org-group-members.md`

## Role in the Community

- Dr. Franklin Cheng (鄭炳全博士) is recorded in the TAH story corpus as **the first president of the Taiwanese American Pen Club** (profile published 2016-02-10, dating to 1998): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/216-dr-franklin-cheng-e9-84-ad-e7-82-b3-e5-85-a8-e5-8d-9a-e5-a3-ab-the-first-pre|216. Dr. Franklin Cheng 鄭炳全博士 / The first president of Taiwanese American Pen Club / 1998]].
- The club was part of a wider overseas Taiwanese writers' movement with sibling chapters, e.g. the Great New York region chapter ([[organizations/the-great-new-york-region-oversea-taiwanese-pen-club|大紐約區海外台灣人筆會]], annual 年刊 recorded at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/491-e5-a4-a7-e7-b4-90-e7-b4-84-e5-8d-80-e6-b5-b7-e5-a4-96-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e4-b|491. 大紐約區海外台灣人筆會第六屆年刊]], 2010) and the SF Bay Area/Silicon Valley chapter ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/sf-oversea-taiwanese-pen-club|15. 舊金山灣區矽谷海外台灣人筆會]]). Whether the LA-based club listed at Taiwan Center is the same body as these regional chapters or a separate LA chapter: HOLD, unresolved in corpus.
