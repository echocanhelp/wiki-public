# Taiwanese American Pen Club

**Taiwanese American Pen Club** ([[organizations/taiwanese-american-irvines-eagle-brotherhood-group-irvine||台美人]]筆會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** [[organizations/taiwanese-american-pen-club-la||台美人筆會]]
- **English (as listed):** Taiwanese American Pen Club
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
- **Source:** https://www.taiwancenter.org/group-members · `taiwancenter-org-group-members.md`

## Role in the Community

- Dr. Franklin Cheng (鄭炳全博士) is recorded in the TAH story corpus as **the first president of the Taiwanese American Pen Club** (profile published 2016-02-10, dating to 1998): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/216-dr-franklin-cheng-e9-84-ad-e7-82-b3-e5-85-a8-e5-8d-9a-e5-a3-ab-the-first-pre|216. Dr. Franklin Cheng 鄭炳全博士 / The first president of Taiwanese American Pen Club / 1998]]. His own 2025 memoir 旅美五十周年 corroborates and sharpens this: the club was founded at the 洛杉磯台灣會館 in 1998 and he was pushed into the founding-presidency role (possibly prompted by his decade of popular medical writing — 醫藥與生活 ×3 and 實用天然藥物); he records the club then spanning 五六十位作家分布美國各地 ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250209211009_旅美五十周年-_-鄭炳全_438dc69b91722b16|鄭炳全 旅美五十周年, TJJ 2025-02-09]]).
- The club's own history of itself is held as a corpus record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/history-taiwanese-american-pen-club|3. Brief History of Taiwanese American Pen Club 台美人筆會的簡介]] (published 2017-06-12), and the club runs its own newsletter record ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/newsletter-tapc|Taiwanese American Pen Club (TAPC) newsletter]]); a 2015-05 photo-album record documents its activities ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/photo-albums-activities-73|73. 台美人筆會 / 2015/05]]).
- **2021-12-21 — documented leadership transition**: after two years as president, 賴慧娜 handed over by member vote to 王淑芬 (Atlanta), herself a former 北美洲台灣婦女會總會長 and the 辣媽 column essayist; 李芬芬 was appointed 副會長兼秘書 and 林文政 財務; the ceremony drew 15 member families at Laguna Woods Village, with 賴慧娜 reporting that despite the pandemic the dispersed membership kept creating via group chat and the biennial 台美文藝 was about to publish ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2022/20220517053449_2021_12_26_台美人筆會會長交接_賴慧娜交棒王淑芬_70a8a32d4cc3ca7a|台美人筆會會長交接，賴慧娜交棒王淑芬, TJJ 2021-12-26]]). 王淑芬's own book-launch and 2022 memoir records are also in the corpus ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2022/20220127023112_2022_01_09_回家_好難-_-王淑芬_d26807f04426a0e8|回家，好難 — 王淑芬]]).
- The club's writing appeared in the movement's literary venue across a sustained run of club-attributed 台美文藝 issues in the TAH corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/79-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e6-96-87-e8-97-9d-2004-e9-99-b3-e5-9e-a3-e4-b8-89-2004-03-e|79. 2004]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/80-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e6-96-87-e8-97-9d-2005-e9-99-b3-e5-9e-a3-e4-b8-89-2005-01-e|80. 2005]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/82-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e6-96-87-e8-97-9d-2007-e9-99-b3-e5-9e-a3-e4-b8-89-2008-05-e|82. 2007]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/333-taac-201209-magazine|333. 2012/09]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/106-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e6-96-87-e8-97-9d-2011-e9-84-ad-e7-82-b3-e5-85-a8-e4-b8-bb|106. 2011 (edited by 鄭炳全)]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/633-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e6-96-87-e8-97-9d-2015-e5-8f-b0-e7-be-8e-e4-ba-ba-e7-ad-86|633. 2015]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1117|1117. 2017]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/literature-1337|1337. 2019]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publicatioins-1365|1365. 2021]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications-1366|1366. 浩劫餘生錄 / 2022]] — a club-attributed run spanning 2004–2022.
- The club has its own entry in the TAH encyclopedia corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-pen-club-la|7. Taiwanese American Pen Club (LA) 台美人筆會]].
- The club was part of a wider overseas Taiwanese writers' movement with sibling chapters — e.g. the Great New York chapter's first president was Dr. Long Tsung Tsai 蔡榮聰醫師 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/215-dr-long-tsung-tsai-e8-94-a1-e6-a6-ae-e8-81-b0-e9-86-ab-e5-b8-ab-the-first-pr|215. Dr. Long Tsung Tsai 蔡榮聰醫師 / The first president of Great New York Pen Club / 2005]]), mirroring Dr. Franklin Cheng's role in LA, and the New York chapter's 6th annual convention and New Year banquet took place in Morris Plains/NJ on 01/01/2020 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/2020-important-events-activities-and-news-4|4. The 6th Annual Convention & New Year Banquet of Oversea Taiwanese Pen Club/New York, 2020-01-01]]). Sibling chapters include the Great New York region chapter ([[organizations/the-great-new-york-region-oversea-taiwanese-pen-club|大紐約區海外台灣人筆會]], annual 年刊 recorded at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/491-e5-a4-a7-e7-b4-90-e7-b4-84-e5-8d-80-e6-b5-b7-e5-a4-96-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e4-b|491. 大紐約區海外台灣人筆會第六屆年刊]], 2010) and the SF Bay Area/Silicon Valley chapter ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/sf-oversea-taiwanese-pen-club|15. 舊金山灣區矽谷海外台灣人筆會]]). Whether the LA-based club listed at Taiwan Center is the same body as these regional chapters or a separate LA chapter: HOLD, unresolved in corpus.
