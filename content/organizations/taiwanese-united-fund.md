## Role in the Community

- Southern California organizing record: 吳西面 (Sei-Mi Wu) is memorialized as the **first 南加州台灣人聯合基金會會長 in 1985** ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/270-e5-90-b3-e8-a5-bf-e9-9d-a2-sei-mi-wu-e7-ac-ac-e4-b8-80-e4-bb-bb-e5-8d-97-e5-|270. 吳西面 Sei-Mi Wu / 第一任南加州台灣人聯合基金會會長 / 1985]]).
  - HOLD: conflict on founding year — the 1985 first-president record vs the 2017 "30 Anniversary" issue (implying 1987) vs the 簡介's stated 1986. Not merged.
- Flagship event 台灣文化之夜: annual program books held as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/photo-albums-activities-80|80. 台灣人聯合基金會-台灣文化之夜年刊]]; 30th-anniversary issue [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1134|1134. 30 Anniversary 2017 Cultural Night / Taiwanese United Fund]] (2017-09).
- 228 commemorative work: held the 228 68th-commemorative "Spiritual Day of Taiwan" concert with 優社 (Taiwan Elite Alliance) in Arcadia, CA on 2015-02-28 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts3|3. The Spiritual Day of Taiwan 2-28 68th Commemorative Concert]]).
- Long-running cultural ministry: the 台灣名家演奏系列 (Great Performers From Taiwan) concert series ran under TUF (南加洲台灣人聯合基金會) from 1991 through 2013 across many seasons ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts1|1. Great Performers From Taiwan by TUF, 1991–2013]]).
- Philanthropic standing: the Taiwan Center opening memoir records 吳佩利 (Li Pei Wu) donating one million dollars to the Taiwanese United Fund and the Chilin Foundation before taking the Taiwan Center preparatory-committee convener role ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys29-eng|29. 南加州台灣會館開創的史料 / The Opening of the S. CA Taiwan Center]]).
- Founding record (self-published 簡介, 2017-06): TUF was founded in **1986** by 吳西面 (Sei-Mi Wu) and [[people/jer-shung-lin|林衡哲]], "LA-area nonprofit" dedicated to preserving and promoting Taiwanese culture in the US ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys294|294. 南加州台灣人聯合基金會的簡介]]).
  - HOLD: three-way founding-year conflict — the 1985 first-president record (270), the 簡介's stated 1986, and the 2017 "30 Anniversary" issue (implying 1987). Not merged.
- 林衡哲 served as **TUF's second president** (做TUF第二任會長), during which he commissioned the birth of 蕭泰然's three immortal concertos ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys70|70. 各位敬愛的股東同仁平安 / 林衡哲]]); 蕭泰然's C小調鋼琴協奏曲 was completed in 1992 at TUF's invitation ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys339|339]]).
- Cultural Night flagship (Montebello, CA): program runs documented for 1992–2002, 04, 06, 11–12, 17 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwan-cultural-night|33. Taiwan Cultural Night by TUF]]) — extends the annual-program record beyond the 年刊/anniversary issues already cited.
- 台灣名家演奏系列 opening: the series began 1992 with 胡乃元's violin recital under 吳澧培's sponsorship; later artists include 林昭亮, 陳毓襄, 陳麗嬋, 劉孟捷, and the 2016 三十周年 曾宇謙 violin recital ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys294|294 簡介]]).
- 228 commemoration continuity: the "Spiritual Day of Taiwan" 2-28 concert with 優社 ran 2015, 2016, and 2017 in Arcadia ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts68|68]]) — corroborates the 2024 "17屆" account; also 鄭智仁醫師 concert/lecture in El Monte 2003-02-18 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts63|63]]).
- Other TUF-presented programs: 台灣作家演講系列 (2015, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-writer-lecture-series|35]]); 台灣之美—亞洲之心國際巡迴畫展 (2017, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/art-show-14|14]]); community service: Los Angeles River Clean Up with 優社 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/5-los-angeles-river-clean-up-e6-b8-85-e6-8e-83-e6-b4-9b-e6-9d-89-e7-a3-af-e6-b2-|5]]).
- Sister chapter: 芝加哥台灣人聯合基金會 (Taiwanese United Fund, Chicago, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-united-fund-chicago|22]]) contributed special funding to the 世界台灣人美術展覽 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys256|256]]).
- Also appears in corpus records as 美國台灣人聯合基金會 (co-host credit, 第161回世界台灣文化論壇 2024-12, already cited above).
- Wider concert-programme corpus: TUF also presented 楊呈偉「尋找心家園 / Finding Home」 in Los Angeles on 2005-07-09 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts24|24. Welly Yang: Finding Home by TUF, 07/09/2005]]) and placed a Culture Night advertisement in San Gabriel on 2017-09-09 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/an-advertisement-in-culture-night-by-taiwanese-united-fund-in-san-gabriel-on-090|36. Culture Night advertisement, San Gabriel 09/09/2017]]) — both bibliographic records, no narrative detail.
