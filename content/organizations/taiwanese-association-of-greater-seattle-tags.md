# Taiwanese Association of Greater Seattle TAGS (大西雅圖區台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of Greater Seattle TAGS
- **Chinese:** 大西雅圖區台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of Greater Seattle (TAGS, 大西雅圖台灣同鄉會) is a chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization of Taiwanese American community associations. TAGS serves the Taiwanese American community in the greater Seattle area and the state of Washington. The chapter is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization.
