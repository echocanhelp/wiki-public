# Taiwanese Association of Greater Seattle TAGS (大西雅圖區台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of Greater Seattle TAGS
- **Chinese:** 大西雅圖區台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of Greater Seattle (TAGS, 大西雅圖台灣同鄉會) is a chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization of Taiwanese American community associations. TAGS serves the Taiwanese American community in the greater Seattle area and the state of Washington. The chapter is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization.

## Role in the Community
- TAGS is documented in TAH's community story corpus as the Greater Seattle chapter of TAA — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/greater-seattle-chapter-taa|TAIWANESE ASSOCIATION OF GREATER SEATTLE (TAGS)]], a community record dated 2015-12-09.
- A dedicated chapter history, written as community history by the community itself, is held at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/history-of-taiwanese-association-of-greater-seattle|Brief History of TAGS 大西雅圖區台灣同鄉會簡介]] (published 2017-10-04).
- An affiliated seniors' group, the Senior Taiwanese Association of Greater Seattle (大西雅圖台灣同鄉松年會), is recorded at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/senior-taiwanese-association-of-greater-seattle|大西雅圖台灣同鄉松年會]] (record dated 2014-10-12), indicating an established elder-membership wing by the mid-2010s.
- Cross-community recognition: community-activity leader David Chen credits TAGS, alongside the UW Taiwanese Student Association and Seattle Formosan Christian Church, as foundational to his Taiwanese-language learning — see [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/david-chen-taiwanese-handbook|David Chen Taiwanese handbook interview]].
