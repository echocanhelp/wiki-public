# Taiwanese American Lawyers Association (TALA)

**Taiwanese American Lawyers Association (TALA)** ([[organizations/taiwanese-american-lawyers-association-tala||台美律師協會]]) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Role in the Community

Corpus records place TALA among the movement's institutional builders in Southern California:

- **Founding president:** 黃茂清 (Mao-ching "David" Huang), per TAH Who's Who #318 ("First President of Taiwanese American Lawyers Association (TALA) 台美律師協會 / 1994") — see [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ff318|TAH #318 record]].
- **台灣會館 (Taiwan Haus) foundation work.** 1998: with the 台灣會館籌備會 formed 1998-02-19, "台美律師協會的律師團正著手擬訂基金會的章程" — TALA's lawyers drafted the charter of the 台灣會館基金會 that would hold the Haus's title. Source: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys29|29. 南加州台灣會館開創的史料 / 黃樹人]].
- **TAHS story-corpus records:** [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-lawyers-association|1. TALA 台美律師協會 (2014)]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/activities-of-taiwanese-american-lawyers-association|台美律師協會的活動 (2019)]] — bibliographic records; full text stays in the vault.
- Mentioned in a 2021 WHA joint statement: [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20210616072940_2021_05_17_2021年南加州僑界支持台灣參與世界衛生大會wha聯合聲_b1d58af16c0a5e5b|2021年南加州僑界支持台灣參與世界衛生大會WHA聯合聲明]].
