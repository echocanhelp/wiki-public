# Taiwan Benevolent Association of California

**Taiwan Benevolent Association of California** (加州台灣同鄉聯誼會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** 加州台灣同鄉聯誼會
- **English (as listed):** Taiwan Benevolent Association of California
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
- **Source:** https://www.taiwancenter.org/group-members · `taiwancenter-org-group-members.md`
