# East Bay Taiwanese Association (北加州)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** East Bay Taiwanese Association
- **Chinese:** 北加州
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The East Bay Taiwanese Association (EBTA, 東灣台灣同鄉會) is a chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations. EBTA serves the Taiwanese American community in the East Bay region of the San Francisco Bay Area, California.
