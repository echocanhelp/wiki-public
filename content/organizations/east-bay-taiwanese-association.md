# East Bay Taiwanese Association (北加州)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** East Bay Taiwanese Association
- **Chinese:** 北加州
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The East Bay Taiwanese Association (EBTA, 東灣台灣同鄉會) is a chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations. EBTA serves the Taiwanese American community in the East Bay region of the San Francisco Bay Area, California.

- Founding member of the North America Taiwan Association of Northern California Federation (TAFNC, 北加州台灣同鄉聯合會): per 石清正's 30-year federation history, EBTA was one of the five founding chapters (東灣、東南灣、南灣、舊金山同鄉會、協志會) behind the federation formed 1973-08-17 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys38|38. 北加州台灣同鄉聯合會三十年回顧 / 石清正 /2014/10]] (English: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys38-eng|TAFNC Thirty Year Review]]).
- During the Formosa Incident / Lin Family Massacre years (post-1979), four EBTA members served as TAFNC President — Rung-fang Chen, Tsun-liang Chang, Shih-ming Huang, I-ren Kan — carrying the federation through its most difficult period (same source, ourjourneys38/-eng).
- 1979-09-29: EBTA played the first Bay Area Taiwanese tennis tournament in Fremont vs. 協志會 (Taiwanese Alliance for Interculture); tennis/softball became a staple Bay Area community summer event (same source).
