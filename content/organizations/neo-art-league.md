# Neo Art League (加州新藝畫會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Neo Art League
- **Chinese:** 加州新藝畫會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Neo Art League is a professional artists association founded in 2006 by Taiwanese artists based in the United States, with its primary activities centered in Southern California. The association exhibits annually at the Taiwanese American Heritage Week in Orange County and has participated in multicultural events such as Art Walk Day in Los Angeles. Members work across a wide range of media including oil, acrylic, mixed media, glass, digital, ink, and sculpture, with an emphasis on innovation and contemporary expression. The association has been invited to exhibit at venues across the United States and internationally, including in San Francisco, North Carolina, Arizona, New York, Paris, Taiwan, Japan, and Italy. Members are also active in art competitions, lectures, and painting instruction.
