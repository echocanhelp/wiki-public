# UCR United Taiwanese Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** UCR United Taiwanese Student Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The UCR United Taiwanese Student Association (UTSA) is a student organization at the University of California, Riverside oriented toward students from Taiwan. According to its Facebook page, the organization aims to give opportunities for Taiwanese students studying at UCR to communicate and make friends. It maintains a Facebook page at @UCRUTSA and an Instagram account at @ucr_utsa.
