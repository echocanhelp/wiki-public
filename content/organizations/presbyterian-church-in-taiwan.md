# Presbyterian Church in Taiwan (PCT)

The **Presbyterian Church in Taiwan (PCT)** is one of the oldest Christian denominations in Taiwan with deep historical roots and significant influence on Taiwanese society.

## Identity Snapshot
- Type: Christian denomination
- Geography: Taiwan
- History: Established in the 19th century, significant role in Taiwanese social and political history

## Historical Significance
The PCT has played a crucial role in:
1. Education and healthcare in Taiwan
2. Social justice movements
3. Taiwanese identity and democratic development
4. Diaspora church connections worldwide
