# Taiwanese American Students Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Students Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Students Association (TASA) at Texas A&M University is a social and cultural student organization founded in 2018 and registered with the university’s Department of Student Activities. It is listed on both the university’s Get Involved portal and StuAct Online, and maintains a dedicated website at tamutasa.com. TASA is distinct from the campus TSA in that it is oriented primarily toward Taiwanese Americans and others interested in Taiwanese American culture, rather than international students from Taiwan.
