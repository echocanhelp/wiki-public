# Taiwanese American Federation of N. California (北加州)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Federation of N. California
- **Chinese:** 北加州
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Federation of N. California (TAFNC, 北加州台灣同鄉聯合會) is a regional umbrella organization serving the Taiwanese American community across Northern California, with a particular focus on the San Francisco Bay Area. It is listed as a chapter affiliate on the national Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會) website.
