## Activities (as co-organizer)

- **2006-02-25 — co-organizer (協辦) of the SoCal 228 commemoration lecture** 「二二八、白色恐怖與轉型期的正義」 by Taiwan columnist 林世煜, hosted 6:30 PM at 大洛杉磯台灣會館; organized by 南加州台灣人權協會, with 台灣會館、聖東台灣同鄉會、加州二二八平反促進會、台灣建國促進會、橙縣台灣同鄉會、台灣之友會 as co-organizers. The program also screened the documentary 「白色見證」 with an evening candle-lighting; 林世煜 gave a follow-up lecture in Dallas on 2006-02-26. Source: Epoch Times (大紀元) b5, 2006-02-25, https://www.epochtimes.com/b5/6/2/25/n1236760.htm (searched `"加州二二八平反促進會"`, 2026-11-14).
- The 2006 record shows the association (listed in the shortened form 加州二二八平反促進會) active in the LA 台美 civic coalition around 228 commemoration in the 2000s, alongside the Taiwan Center itself.
