# Austin Taiwanese Association (奧斯丁)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Austin Taiwanese Association
- **Chinese:** 奧斯丁
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Austin Taiwanese Association (ATA) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the Austin, Texas area and is listed under the TAA Southern (美南) regional division.

## Role in the Community
- The chapter's own history is held in the TAH story corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/austin-tx-taiwanese-association|Austin Taiwanese Association 奧斯汀台灣同鄉會 (德州)]] (record published 2014-10-12).
- Community publication: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/newsletter-of-austin-taiwanese-association|Newsletter of Austin Taiwanese Association]] (2016).
- Material culture: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/t-shirt-of-austin-taiwanese-association|31. T-Shirt of Austin Taiwanese Association]] (2017 artifact).
- Austin's wider Taiwanese civic ecosystem also includes TAP 台美菁英協會奧斯丁(德州)分會 and 奧斯丁台灣商會, each documented as separate records in the corpus ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/austin-chapter-tap|Austin Chapter / TAP]], 2014; [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/austin-chapter-of-taiwanese-chambers-of-commerce|Austin Chapter of Taiwanese Chambers of Commerce]], 2016).

## Timeline
- The chapter's own community history (in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourney-343|Our Journey — Austin Taiwanese Association]]) describes the chapter growing out of two graduate-student groups at UT Austin — a "Chinese National" group attending the UT Chinese Students Association, and Taiwanese students who formed their own community — the movement-record origin of the chapter.
- 1987-05-09 — hosted the chapter's 第一次母親節音樂會 (first Mother's Day Concert) at West Lake Hills, TX: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/concerts54|54. Mother's Day Concert by TAA of Austin, Texas]].
- 1991–1993 — Peter Wu 吳宗憲 (b. 1947 台南; UT Austin Ph.D. in pharmaceutical science, arrived Austin 1974) served as chapter president, per the chapter's Our Journey history ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourney-343|Our Journey]]).
- 2017 — photo-activity display panels documenting chapter activities 1970–2004 entered the corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/artifacts34|34. Display Panel for the Photo Activities of Austin Taiwanese Association]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/photo-albums-activities-122|122. Display Panel for Photo Activities of Austin Taiwanese Association 1970~2004]].
