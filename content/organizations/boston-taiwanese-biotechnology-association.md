# Boston Taiwanese Biotechnology Association (波士頓台灣人生物科技協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Boston Taiwanese Biotechnology Association
- **Chinese:** 波士頓台灣人生物科技協會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Boston Taiwanese Biotechnology Association (BTBA) is an organization founded in 2012 by Taiwanese graduate students, postdocs, and young professionals in the greater Boston area. Its goals are to enhance scientific collaborations, foster individual career development, and strengthen networking among academic and industrial bioscience communities in Taiwan and the US. BTBA holds annual symposiums attracting more than 350 young Taiwanese professionals from biology-related fields across the US, featuring keynote speakers, academic and industry panels, career development sessions, oral presentations, and poster sessions. In addition to the annual symposium, BTBA hosts seminars and workshops throughout the year to serve as a platform for young scientists to share research, exchange ideas, and explore career opportunities.
