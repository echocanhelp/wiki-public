# Taiwanese American Association Great Richmond (大雷城)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Association Great Richmond
- **Chinese:** 大雷城
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Association Great Richmond (大雷城 台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in the Greater Richmond, Virginia area.
