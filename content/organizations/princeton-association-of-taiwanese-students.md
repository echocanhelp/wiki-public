# Princeton Association of Taiwanese Students

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Princeton Association of Taiwanese Students
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Princeton Association of Taiwanese Students (PATS; 普林斯頓台灣同學會) is a Taiwanese student organization at Princeton University with a dedicated website hosted on the princeton.edu domain. The organization’s Chinese name, rendered in traditional characters, translates literally as the “Princeton Taiwan Fellow Students Association,” a naming convention common among Taiwanese student groups that draw primarily from students from Taiwan rather than Taiwanese Americans. The group maintains a Facebook group and lists links to affiliated regional organizations, including the Federation of Taiwanese Students Associations in New York (FTSANY) and the Taipei Economic and Cultural Office’s education division in New York.
