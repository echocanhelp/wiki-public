# TAH Foundation (台美史料中心)

**TAH Foundation / History of Taiwanese American (T.A. Archives) — 台美史料中心** runs [taiwaneseamericanhistory.org](https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/). It is a **sibling archive** to the [[organizations/taiwanese-american-historical-society||Taiwanese American Historical Society (TAHS / 台美人歷史協會)]] — same Taiwanese American history mission, **different organization**. TAH is **not** TAHS, and this site is **not** the 2017/2023 TAHS yearbook PDFs.

Official About: [About Us](https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/about-us/) (archived 2026-08-21).

## History

In early 2013, six first-generation Taiwanese Americans in different parts of the United States agreed by phone to collect, record, and preserve Taiwanese American historical documents and artifacts from the 1950s (English About also dates the arrival horizon to 1949) to the present. First tasks: a history website, office space near Irvine, staff and volunteers.

The first website launched **May 2013**, later moved platforms; a dedicated staffer was hired after about a year; the current site design dates from **2015**. Address: www.TaiwaneseAmericanHistory.org.

The first Archives Center opened **February 2014** in an Irvine industrial park (~3,800–4,000 sq ft). In **July 2016** it moved to a two-story Irvine office-park building (~19,000 sq ft).

Two review committees (each with an operation manual) gate what is published: **Article Committee** (「我的故事」 / My Stories; 「我們的旅程」 / Our Journeys) and **Art Committee** (Taiwanese American artists and works). Majority committee acceptance, not a single editor.

By the 2017/2020 About, the center had uploaded about ten thousand items, plus on-site libraries and artifacts. Monthly newsletter began **May 2015**. Since **2015** the site also posts current community events and invites a year-end vote for the year’s top ten Taiwanese American events.

**Do not invent:** the six 2013 conveners are **not named** on the official About. The 50th/Who’s Who card English name is not a merge key (TAH slug ≠ Echopedia identity).

## Mission

> TAH’s mission is to celebrate the lives and achievements of Taiwanese people who are contributing to American society. Together, we aim to preserve and promote our shared heritage.
> — site home, 2026-08-21

Chinese About (same page): the site’s materials come from the Taiwanese American community and belong to that community; the aim is a usable public archive for scholars and the public.

Stated future goals (About, prepared 2017-09, updated 2020-06): an *Encyclopedia of Taiwanese American*; a permanent museum/history center; durable staff and funding; more young-generation collecting; English website and English history books; film; expanded Who’s Who.
