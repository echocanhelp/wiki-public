# Taiwanese Student Association at the University of Virginia

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Student Association at the University of Virginia
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Student Association at the University of Virginia (TSA at UVA) is a student organization dedicated to promoting Taiwanese culture and fostering community among those interested in Taiwan’s cultural heritage. The organization maintains an active presence both on campus and online, with a dedicated website at tsaatuva.org, an Instagram account with over 1,200 followers, and a Facebook group.
