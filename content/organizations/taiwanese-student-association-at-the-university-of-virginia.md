# Taiwanese Student Association at the University of Virginia

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Student Association at the University of Virginia
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Student Association at the University of Virginia (TSA at UVA) is a student organization dedicated to promoting Taiwanese culture and fostering community among those interested in Taiwan’s cultural heritage. The organization maintains an active presence both on campus and online, with a dedicated website at tsaatuva.org, an Instagram account with over 1,200 followers, and a Facebook group.

## Role in the Community

- A founding member of TSA at UVA — Christina Hu, later TACL National Director of Civic Engagement for the Census 2020 Initiative and 2012 Virginia Regional GOTV Director — is named in the TaiwaneseAmerican.org Census 2020 essay — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/my-signature-represents-me-writing-in-taiwanese-on-the-united-states-census|My Signature Represents Me: Writing in Taiwanese on the United States Census]].
- Charlottesville/UVA Taiwanese community activity is also attested in the 2009 Typhoon Morakot relief era: ethnomusicologist Wendy Hsu (UVA PhD) organized a benefit concert in Charlottesville bringing together the University of Virginia and the local Taiwanese community — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/wendy-hsu-on-issues-of-invisibility-and-asian-american-identities|Wendy Hsu on Issues of Invisibility and Asian American Identities]].
- Taiwan-oriented student activity at UVA is further attested by a TaiwaneseAmerican.org STEP Taiwan essay written by Anna Wang, a second-year UVA nursing major — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/step-taiwan-the-taiwanese-american-experience|STEP: The Taiwanese American Experience]] (STEP itself: volunteer-run 501(c)(3) founded 2016).
- The founding-member named in the Census essay is **Christina Hu**: her documentary bio confirms a B.A. in Foreign Affairs from UVA, returned U.S. Peace Corps Volunteer (where she helped found the Minority Awareness Group supporting American minority volunteers serving in Ukraine), a Harvard Kennedy School master's, chair of the National Asian Pacific American Conference on Law and Public Policy, 2012 Virginia Regional GOTV Director for Obama's re-election campaign, and 2019 TACL National Director of Civic Engagement for the Census 2020 Initiative ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/blacklistfilm|Blacklist]]). Her second documentary, following Taiwan's democratization through KMT-blacklisted activists, is itself a UVA-connected Taiwanese American cultural record.
- Wider Virginia Taiwanese civic infrastructure around UVA is attested by the 2015 FAPA directory: the FAPA Virginia chapter lists 黃文旭 as the state contact, and Virginia counted 19 Taiwanese students/recorded residents in the directory's state distribution — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/51-virginia-chapter-chapter-fapa|51. Virginia Chapter / FAPA 台灣人公共事務會維吉尼亞州分會]].
- Corpus re-checked 2026-09-23: remaining UVA hits are unrelated to TSA — an orthopedics-residency memoir mentioning the UVA orthopedics program ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys176|Our Journeys 176]]) and UVA business professor 陳朝暉 quoted in a 2026 press article.
