# Taiwanese American Industrial Technology Association TAITA-East Coast (台美產業科技協會美東分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Industrial Technology Association TAITA-East Coast
- **Chinese:** 台美產業科技協會美東分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

TAITA-East Coast is the East Coast chapter of the Taiwanese American Industrial Technology Association, founded to connect Taiwanese American science and technology professionals in the United States with Taiwan’s emerging industries. The association was established with the recognition that Taiwan’s traditional industries were shifting to China, creating an urgent need to support new technology sectors, and that experienced Taiwanese American professionals could help fill the talent gap. Beginning around 2005, the organization concentrated its efforts on authoring and publishing articles introducing cutting-edge technologies through a monthly column in ‘Chemical Monthly’ (化工資訊與商情), accumulating over 45 articles spanning chemistry, energy, materials, pharmaceuticals, process engineering, quality control, and environmental technology. The chapter regularly hosts seminars and joint events with other professional associations, such as a 2008 joint conference with the Happy Club (精精俱樂部) in Saddle Brook, New Jersey, featuring entrepreneurship and technology talks.
