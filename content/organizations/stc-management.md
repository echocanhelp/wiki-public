# STC Management (順天 · Sung Tien Collaboration)

Real-estate investment, property-management, and leasing firm in the San Gabriel Valley, named for **順天 (Sung Tien, "following the heavens")** — the site explains the Chinese saying as the company's guiding idea. A community-economy node: Taiwanese-American professional staffing and Taiwan-linked capital, inside mainstream US commercial real-estate institutions.

## Identity Snapshot
- **Era:** Contemporary (company yearbooks on-site run 2009–2025)
- **Geography:** Whittier / Los Angeles area, Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (real estate management firm)
- **Address (on-site):** 10722 Beverly Blvd., Whittier, CA 90601
- **Official site:** https://stcmanagement.com (Tier 2: `knowledge/web-archives/stcmanagement-com-home.md`)

## Record

- **Accredited Management Organization (AMO)**; affiliations listed on-site: CCIM, ICSC, AIR, IREM, AOA, MRMLS, NAR, CoStar, LoopNet, CAR.
- **CEO John Hsu:** CPM + CCIM; President, Greater Los Angeles Chapter of CCIM; **first Chinese-American selected (2009) to the CCIM Institute's Jay W. Levine Leadership Development Academy**; UCLA B.A. Business & Economics, Pepperdine M.B.A.; bio lists fluency in English, Mandarin and **Taiwanese**.
- **Taiwan capital nexus (bios, on-site):** staff member Benjamin previously Director of Asset Management for a publicly traded company **based in Taiwan**, acquiring $450M+ of Southern California real estate (5,000+ residential units, 3M+ SF commercial); Susan spent two years with an overseas affiliated PR company **in Taiwan**.
- **Community-identity marker:** four separate staff bios state fluency in "English, Mandarin and **Taiwanese**" — the SGV professional-world usage of "Taiwanese" as a language name, worth noting for the language-identity record.

## Historical Significance

A documented example of Taiwanese-American presence in a sector (commercial real estate/asset management) where presence is usually invisible, and of Taiwan-based investment capital flowing into the SGV housing stock since the 2000s.
