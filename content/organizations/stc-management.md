# STC Management (順天 · Sung Tien Collaboration)

Real-estate investment, property-management, and leasing firm in the San Gabriel Valley, named for **順天 (Sung Tien, "following the heavens")** — the site explains the Chinese saying as the company's guiding idea. A community-economy node: Taiwanese-American professional staffing and Taiwan-linked capital, inside mainstream US commercial real-estate institutions.

## Identity Snapshot
- **Era:** Contemporary (company yearbooks on-site run 2009–2025)
- **Geography:** Whittier / Los Angeles area, Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (real estate management firm)
- **Address (on-site):** 10722 Beverly Blvd., Whittier, CA 90601
- **Official site:** https://stcmanagement.com (Tier 2: `knowledge/web-archives/stcmanagement-com-home.md`)

## Record

- **Accredited Management Organization (AMO)** — per CEO bio, **first Asian firm in the country** to receive the designation; affiliations listed on-site: CCIM, ICSC, AIR, IREM, AOA, MRMLS, NAR, CoStar, LoopNet, CAR.
- **Scale (web-sourced, 2026-09-26):** founded **1985** (LinkedIn company data); ~60 commercial centers in Southern California, portfolio valued ~**$1.5B** (OCBJ); annual revenue $20–30M, 10–20 employees per LinkedIn profile era (OCBJ 2024-era story cites ~100 including center staff). Venues/ritual record: see [[organizations/yes-plaza|Yes Plaza]] and [[organizations/stc-rowland-legacy|STC Rowland Legacy]]. Second office in **City of Industry** ("STC Center" — venue for community events per the firm's news page).
- **Notable holdings in press:** former Bank of America / **Nixon Plaza** building, Uptown Whittier (acquired Oct 2014, $4.75M; National Historic Landmark — Nixon's first law office; rooftop-flag restoration planned, Whittier Daily News); **Anaheim GardenWalk** — affiliate purchase closed Dec 21 [2024] at **$80M** (OCBJ; "long-term holding… not planning to flip" — John Hsu).
- **Community-economy venue role:** STC Center (City of Industry) hosted RHCA installation gala (2012), Million Stars Singing Contest, ACDA dance festival, AIR mainstream/Asian realty mixer (2011), F5 Financial Forum (2011); firm attended Ed Royce fundraiser (2012); news page records a 2011 luncheon with "Consul General of China" Qiu Shaofang (title as STC wrote it). Chinese press credits STC/Hsu with shaping 四季廣場, 夜市 (Yes Plaza) and 羅蘭廣場 commercial districts.
