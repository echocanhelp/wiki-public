# Taiwanese Association – NC Chapter (北卡)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association – NC Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北卡
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association – NC Chapter (北卡 台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA), the national umbrella organization serving Taiwanese American communities across the United States. The NC Chapter serves Taiwanese Americans in North Carolina and is listed under the TAA’s Eastern Southern regional grouping.

## History and Community Role

Absorbed from the chapter's 50-year retrospective by 謝金朱 — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-381|381. 我的青春在北卡 / 北卡台灣同鄉會50年回顧 / 2022-06]] (band A oral history):

- **Founded 1971**, in the same wave as other TAA chapters; founding president 張振盛, early core of STEM graduate students from UNC, NCSU and Duke in the Research Triangle area.
- **Advocacy:** hosted touring Taiwan-movement speakers (陳隆志, 史明, 洪哲勝, 許榮淑, 陳唐山, 蔡同榮 and others); the chapter's early years include the Triangle-poster incident, in which FBI interviewed member 劉格正 about Nationalist surveillance of students.
- **Publications:** issued the newsletter 台訊 roughly monthly, more than 100 issues; in the 1980s 李宗敬 ran a daily call-in tape line of Taiwan news.
- **Community calendar:** lunar New Year gatherings once drew 300+; 肉粽節, 中秋節 and Southeast-region summer conferences; a 2019 pilot Taiwan Night market drew 400–500; in 2020 the chapter raised funds and donated masks, and gave to a domestic-violence center and food bank. Membership today is around 100 as student numbers have shrunk.
- **Notable early members:** 林國慶 (NCSU), 郭倍宏, 李應元 (UNC, first publisher of 《台灣學生》), 高龍榮, 吳焜裕.
