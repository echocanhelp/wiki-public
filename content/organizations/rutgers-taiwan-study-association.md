# Rutgers Taiwan Study Association (羅格斯大學台灣研究社)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Rutgers Taiwan Study Association
- **Chinese:** 羅格斯大學台灣研究社
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Rutgers Taiwan Study Association is a student and youth-run nonprofit organization based at Rutgers University in New Jersey. The organization focuses on Taiwanese social and cultural issues, screening high-quality documentaries to promote academic discussion of social topics relevant to Taiwan. It also organizes forums and cultural events to connect young Taiwanese studying and working in New Jersey, and works to foster cultural exchange and resource sharing between Taiwan and the United States.
