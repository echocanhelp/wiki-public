# Hsinchu High School Alumni Association of North America (北美新竹中學校友會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Hsinchu High School Alumni Association of North America
- **Chinese:** 北美新竹中學校友會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Hsinchu High School Alumni Association of North America is an alumni organization connecting graduates of Hsinchu High School (founded 1922 in Hsinchu, Taiwan) living across North America. The association was formally established on June 12, 2004 in Cincinnati, Ohio under the leadership of Dr. Franklin Y. H. Lee (Class 1956). It holds biennial conferences rotating among its regional chapters, with inaugural and subsequent meetings held in Cincinnati (2004), Chicago (2006), and Foster City, California (2008). The association includes chapters in Southern California, Chicago, Cleveland, Cincinnati, Dayton, Houston, New England, Philadelphia, Washington D.C., Vancouver, Toronto, and Ontario. Notable alumni include Nobel Laureate in Chemistry Professor Yuan T. Lee (Class 1955).
