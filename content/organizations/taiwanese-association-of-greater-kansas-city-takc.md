# Taiwanese Association of Greater Kansas City TAKC (大堪薩斯)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of Greater Kansas City TAKC
- **Chinese:** 大堪薩斯
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of Greater Kansas City (TAKC), also known in Chinese as 大堪薩斯 台灣同鄉會, is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA). It serves the Taiwanese American community in the greater Kansas City metropolitan area, which spans both Kansas and Missouri. TAKC is listed under the TAA Mid-West regional grouping (美中西) on the national TAA directory.
