# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Northern California Chapter (北美洲台灣婦女會北加分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Northern California Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣婦女會北加分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Northern California Chapter is a local chapter of the North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA), serving the Northern California area.

## Role in the Community
- The TAH directory corpus carries a dedicated chapter record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-northern-california-chapter|11. Northern California Chapter / NATWA 北美洲台灣婦女會北加分會]] (catalogued 2015-12-21, bibliographic record only).
- Chapter-authored publication: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/624-e6-87-b7-e5-bf-b5-e7-9a-84-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e8-8f-9c-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b|624. 懷念的台灣菜 / 北美洲台灣婦女會北加州分會 / 1995]] — a 1995 Taiwanese-cuisine collection produced by the chapter itself (catalogued 2015-04-19), its earliest chapter-attributed activity record in the corpus.
- Parent body [[organizations/north-america-taiwanese-womens-association|NATWA]] (founded March 1988, Los Angeles) is organised into five geographic regions with sixteen chapters across the US and Canada; nationally it is recorded as the Taiwanese American group with the most activity projects and the most members ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/239-natwa-e6-b4-bb-e5-8b-95-e9-a0-85-e7-9b-ae-e6-9c-80-e5-a4-9a-e7-9a-84-e5-85-a|239. NATWA 活動項目最多的全國性台美團體]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/240-natwa-e6-9c-83-e5-93-a1-e6-9c-80-e5-a4-9a-e7-9a-84-e5-85-a8-e5-9c-8b-e6-80-a|240. NATWA 會員最多的全國性台美團體]]). Chapter networking ran through the 聯誼通訊 newsletter ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/663-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|第七期, 1992/06]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/678-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|第二十二期, 2000/01]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/679-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|第二十三期, 2000/08]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/695-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e5-a9-a6-e5-a5-b3-e6-9c-83-e8-8|第三十九期, 2008/08]]).
