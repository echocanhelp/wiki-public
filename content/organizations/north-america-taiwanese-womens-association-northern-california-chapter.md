# North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Northern California Chapter (北美洲台灣婦女會北加分會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Northern California Chapter
- **Chinese:** 北美洲台灣婦女會北加分會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Northern California Chapter is a local chapter of the North America Taiwanese Women’s Association (NATWA), serving the Northern California area.

## Role in the Community
- The TAH directory corpus carries a dedicated chapter record: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/natwa-northern-california-chapter|11. Northern California Chapter / NATWA 北美洲台灣婦女會北加分會]] (catalogued 2015-12-21, bibliographic record only).
