# Taiwanese American Student Association at the University of Maryland, College Park

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Student Association at the University of Maryland, College Park
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Student Association (TASA) at the University of Maryland, College Park is a social and cultural student organization dedicated to celebrating Taiwanese culture on campus. Open to students of all backgrounds, TASA welcomes anyone with curiosity or passion for Taiwanese culture and holds general body meetings weekly during the academic year. The organization is registered on UMD’s official club platform, TerpLink, and is recognized as part of the university’s APIDA student organizations.

## Role in the Community
- **Founding identity:** UMD TASA is on record as the place where members discovered their Taiwanese identity — computer-science graduate Jonathan Chen (later FiscalNote co-founder) credits joining UMD's TASA for that turn and notes most of his friends today came through TASA — see [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/jonathan-chen|Interview: Jonathan Chen (TaiwaneseAmerican.org)]].
- **Community events:** co-hosted a Taiwanese Night Market event on the University of Maryland campus, documented 2012-04-13 — see [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/taiwanese-night-market-with-university-of-marylands-tasa|Taiwanese Night Market with University of Maryland's TASA]].
- Broader Taiwanese-campus activity at UMD predates/parallels TASA's current chapter: a Night Market at University of Maryland was documented 2011-04-29 ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/night-market-at-university-of-maryland|record]]), a year before the 2012 TASA-attributed event; also Taiwanese Movie Festival 2011 — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/taiwanese-movie-festival-at-university-of-maryland|record]]; Asian American Literature Symposium 2010 — [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/asian-american-literature-symposium-at-university-of-maryland|record]]. Authorship of these events is not attributed to TASA in the records — no merge made.
- **Founding memory (first-hand):** in 楊遠薰's 2017-09-16 essay 「台美人的未來」, co-founder Steve Hsu (許智恆) recalls returning to Taiwan the summer after his freshman year, coming back with a passion for Taiwan, and co-founding 「台美學生會」(TASA) at UMD (馬大) with classmates in his sophomore year, recruiting eagerly — and immediately hitting a wall when a Taiwan-born, US-raised classmate refused to join any "TA" organization, insisting he was Chinese American. Hsu also recounts that the club's "TA" name was itself stigmatized as 綠派/台獨 and boycotted during the sharp 藍綠 polarization of the mid-2000s (「十餘年前」 per the 2017 article — founding era inferred mid-2000s, no exact date in the record); he later co-founded Taiwanese American Professionals (TAP) in the DC area ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20211205043442_2017_09_16_台美人的未來上-_-楊遠薰-2017-09-16_2ea7bed1b6d2e2ce|台美人的未來(上)／楊遠薰 (TWJ)]], record also mirrored 2024-05-27 ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240527043449_root_2c122cd9a2bbcda7|TWJ reprint]])). This memoir predates and supplements the directory's contemporary-chapter snapshot above.
