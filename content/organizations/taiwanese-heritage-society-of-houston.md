# Taiwanese Heritage Society of Houston (台灣人傳統基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Heritage Society of Houston
- **Chinese:** 台灣人傳統基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Heritage Society of Houston (THSH) is an IRS 501(c)(3) non-profit organization based in southwest Houston, Texas, dedicated to providing community services and promoting Taiwanese culture and heritage in the greater Houston area. It owns and operates the Taiwanese Community Center (TCC), a 15,000-square-foot facility on a two-acre lot at 5885 Point West Drive that has served as the home of Houston’s Taiwanese American community since opening in 1992.

## Role in the Community

- **1988-09** — The five principals of the Taiwanese Language School — Chu-chen Kuo, Yu-fang Shen, Ching-shi Hsieh, Chiu-cheng Lin, Ming Cheng Liau — with consultant Kuei-fen Hsieh, applied to register THSH as a tax-exempt non-profit, the organizational vehicle for purchasing the community center.
- **1991-01** — IRS approved the tax-exempt application. At the first general meeting (June 1st) the property purchase was approved and a seven-member negotiating committee was elected, with Chao-Yuh Yang as President and Sheng-Yi Chuang as Vice-president, switching after a few months. The parent Taiwanese Association of America granted a $30,000 no-interest loan repayable over ten years for the purchase.
- THSH's founding is inseparable from the Taiwanese Community Center (TCC): without THSH "it's hard to say how long it would have taken to open a community center" ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys233-eng|233. 回顧休士頓台灣人活動中心的成立 / Cheng Y. Eddie Chuang]]).
- The TCC complex has co-housed the Taiwanese American Federal Credit Union, the Houston Chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America, and the Taiwanese Language School; THSH's community-service role is documented in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys328|328. 休士頓台灣人傳統基金會對大休士頓地區社區服務的參與 / 蕭文源 / 12/2017]].
- **2018-11-03** — THSH celebrated its 30th anniversary with an annual fund-raising dinner, corroborating a 1988 founding ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/16-taiwanese-heritage-society-of-houston-celebrated-30th-year-anniversary-and-an|16. THSH Celebrated 30th Year Anniversary and Annual Fund Raising 11/3/2018]]).
