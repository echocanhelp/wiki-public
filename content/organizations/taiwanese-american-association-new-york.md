# Taiwanese American Association New York (大紐約區)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Association New York
- **Chinese:** 大紐約區
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Association New York (TAANY, 大紐約區 台灣同鄉會) is a chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations. TAANY serves the Taiwanese American community in the Greater New York area and is listed under the TAA’s East Northern (美東北) regional grouping.

## Role in the Community
Per the chapter's own history [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys300|大紐約區台灣同鄕會沿革 (2017)]]:
- **1960s roots:** East-coast Taiwan students gathered as the "East Coast Formosan Club," meeting each Christmas season at International House (I House) near Columbia and Riverside Church in Manhattan; many community marriages trace to these gatherings.
- **1970-06-01:** 美東台灣同鄕會, 芝加哥台灣同鄕會 and 洛杉磯台灣同鄕會 founded 全美台灣同鄉會 (TAA), registered in Washington DC as "Formosan Club of America, Inc." 美東同鄕會 reorganized as the TAA 大紐約分會; its former satellites (波士頓、康州、綺色佳、費城、巴爾的摩、華府) joined TAA directly.
- **Movement stage:** 1971 民衆大會 in NYC (~2,000 attendees); a follow-up event in Washington DC in 1972; hosted the 世界台灣同鄕會聯合會 second annual conference in 1975.
- **Venues:** I House → Hunter College High School (~1973–74) → Senator Robert Wagner High School (1975) → Queens/Flushing as immigration patterns shifted; 台灣人會館 later became the hub for TAANY and other groups.
- **Mission (per charter):** 聯絡鄉親促進合作維護在美台人福利 · 關心台灣維護台灣人之人權及利益 · 宣揚台灣文化促進台美文化交流.
- **Publications:** [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publicationmagazines975|紐約台灣 1999-11 創刊號]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/791-e7-b4-90-e7-b4-84-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a32000-e5-b9-b41-e6-9c-88-e8-99-9f-e6-9c-83|紐約台灣 2000-01 會刊]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/792-e7-b4-90-e7-b4-84-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a32000-e5-b9-b47-e6-9c-88-e8-99-9f-e6-9c-83|2000-07]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/793-e7-b4-90-e7-b4-84-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a32001-e5-b9-b43-e3-80-815-e6-9c-88-e8-99-9|2001-03/05]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/794-e7-b4-90-e7-b4-84-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a32007-e5-b9-b41-e6-9c-88-e6-9c-83-e5-88-8a|2007-01]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/795-e7-b4-90-e7-b4-84-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a32007-e5-b9-b4-e6-9c-83-e5-88-8a-e5-a4-a7-|2007 年會刊]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/798-e7-b4-90-e7-b4-84-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a32010-11-e6-9c-83-e5-88-8a-e5-a4-a7-e7-b4-|紐約台灣 2010-11會刊]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/annual-yearbook-of-taiwanese-american-association-of-new-york|年刊 Yearbook]], [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/newsletter-taany|chapter newsletter]].
- **Commemorations:** annual 二二八 activities — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ny-228|大紐約區台灣同鄉會二二八紀念活動]] (2018-02-14).
- **Movement actions (from TAA & memoir corpus):** 1973 — coordinated with the national TAA and 台灣人民自決會 (宋泉盛牧師) against KMT bullying of Taiwanese spectators at the Williamsport Little League finals; the NY chapter (per 吳澧培's TAA memoir [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys76-eng|76. 全美台灣同鄉會 / Mu-Sheng Wu]]) worked with 方菊雄 and letter campaigns to Williamsport citizens and press ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys182|182. 少棒賽場外記 / 許民能]] recalls riding the NY chapter's chartered bus and the "Team of Taiwan, Not Rep of China" banner stand-off). 1983 — chapter president 楊黃美幸 and ~20–30 members formally applied to visit Taiwan; the KMT refused except for 楊黃美幸 and 一位美國小姐, an early probe of the 回台 ban that LA's 1984 delegation followed ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys66|66. 南加州同鄉會組團回台 / 謝清志]]).
- **Cultural work:** hosted the Passport to Taiwan Festival 台灣巡禮文化藝術節 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/passport-to-taiwan-festival|record, 2017-05-02]]).
- **Chapter history record:** the chapter's own brief history is held in the TAH corpus as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/history-of-taany|22. Brief History of TAA / New York Chapter 大紐約區台灣同鄕會簡介]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taany|Taiwanese American Association of New York 大紐約區台灣同鄉會]]; paper newsletters catalogued at [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taany-paper-newsletters|TAA/New York Chapter newsletters]].
- **2020-12 WHO 連署:** among the 31 Greater-New York Taiwanese groups (with 大紐約區台灣人社團聯合會, 紐約台灣會館, 紐約台灣商會) co-signing the 大紐約地區支持台灣參與WHO共同宣言 ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20251110223518_大紐約地區31個僑團挺台參與who_台灣駐紐約辦事處_4c312d1526674bdf|TJJ record, webinar statement]]).
- **2025 罷韓連署:** chapter president 劉佩芸 (Jennifer Liu), returning to her US job, completed her 罷韓二階連署 at the 光復高雄罷韓總部 before boarding her flight; the group's stated reason: 韓國瑜「落跑」選總統 shortly after taking office as mayor ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20251209224432_堅持要罷韓_大紐約區台灣同鄉會會長完成連署再_72d7230f290816d0|TJJ, 2025-12-09]]).
