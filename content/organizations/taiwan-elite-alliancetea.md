# Taiwan Elite Alliance TEA (優社)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwan Elite Alliance TEA
- **Chinese:** 優社
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Taiwan Elite Alliance (TEA) is a California-based nonprofit organization established in July 2000 by Josephine Pan and officially incorporated with the State of California in 2007. It holds 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status and works to promote Taiwanese and Taiwanese American arts and literature while protecting and enhancing human rights, freedom, and democracy for the people of Taiwan. TEA supports scholars, artists, and other community leaders in fostering understanding of Taiwanese culture and heritage among Taiwanese Americans. The organization orchestrates events, raises funds, and sponsors or co-sponsors other associations’ events, mobilizing volunteers on a per-event basis. Its programming spans regular educational programs, volunteer programs, cultural events, and political support events.

## Role in the Community
- TEA (優社) co-held the annual **2-28 "Spiritual Day of Taiwan" commemorative concerts** in Arcadia, CA with the Taiwanese United Fund (台灣人聯合基金會) — documented in the TAH concert archive for **2015, 2016 and 2017**, showing a recurring civic-cultural partnership rather than a one-off event.
