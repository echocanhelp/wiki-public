# Pine Green Institutes (松青學院)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Pine Green Institutes
- **Chinese:** 松青學院
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Pine Green Institute (松青學院) is a Taiwanese American community learning program based in northern New Jersey, operating out of Fair Lawn Community Church. It offers continuing education and enrichment courses for Taiwanese American adults, with a focus on cultural, recreational, and wellness activities. Spring semester courses include vocal music, karaoke, Japanese language, iPad skills, tai chi, watercolor and Chinese painting, table tennis, ocarina, English conversation, and stretch exercise. Classes meet on Saturdays over approximately 12 sessions, with lunch and social time included. Registration fees cover meals, and the program is coordinated by community volunteers.
