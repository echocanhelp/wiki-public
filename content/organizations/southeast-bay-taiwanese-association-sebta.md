# Southeast Bay Taiwanese Association SEBTA (北加州)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Southeast Bay Taiwanese Association SEBTA
- **Chinese:** 北加州
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Southeast Bay Taiwanese Association (SEBTA, 東南灣台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. SEBTA serves the Taiwanese American community in the southeast San Francisco Bay Area of California and is listed under the TAA Western Region (美西). The chapter is also a member association of the Taiwanese American Federation of Northern California (TAFNC), a coalition of major Northern California Taiwanese American organizations.
