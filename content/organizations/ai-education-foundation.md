# AI Education Foundation (AI教育基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **Era:** ~2024–present (first documented course Feb 2025)
- **Geography:** Los Angeles (Chinatown OCAC center, SGV venues)
- **Core roles:** community education organization (chinese american)

## Record

- **Purpose (stated by president [[people/pan-yi-ling|潘意玲]]):** popularize technology education, close the digital divide (縮短數位落差), "協助更多民眾接觸並善用數位工具."
- **Programs:** AI手機功能教學課程 (first cohort **2025-02-12**; 2026-02-12 second run; instructor 謝逸琦); LINE/Google Maps/iPhone courses co-hosted with **洛杉磯華僑文教服務中心** at the Chinatown library (2025-06-25); co-organizer of the **Taichung VGH health-lecture** LA/OC tour press event.
- **Civic reach:** co-host (with [[organizations/stc-rowland-legacy|STC顺天联合基金会]] and TUFBA 台美餐飲產業經貿发展協會) of **Taste of Taiwan 2026** — the Taiwan night-market + Double Ten event at STC Rowland Heights, Oct 2–11, 2026.
- Self-described civic philosophy: in-person teaching as community support network; digital literacy as the overseas community's link to the world; AI as a bridge displaying **Taiwan's soft power**.

## Historical Significance

A data point on **first-adopters of AI-era community organizing in the Chinese diaspora**: an LA foundation whose entire program is teaching elders smartphone AI — anti-fraud, voice assistants — as elder-care-as-civic-work, operated hand-in-glove with the ROC consular apparatus (OCAC LA, 僑務委員 system) while co-fronting a Taiwan-framed public festival. The layer of SGV/Chinatown civil society that rarely reaches English-language histories.
