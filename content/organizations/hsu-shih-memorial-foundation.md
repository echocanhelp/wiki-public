# Hsu Shih Memorial Foundation

**Hsu Shih Memorial Foundation** (許石基金會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** 許石基金會
- **English (as listed):** Hsu Shih Memorial Foundation
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
- **Source:** https://www.taiwancenter.org/group-members · `taiwancenter-org-group-members.md`

## Role in the Community

- The foundation's namesake, composer 許石 (1919–1980, 台南), is remembered in the corpus via the Tainan municipal 許石音樂圖書館, which in 2021 opened the 信雅古典音樂珍藏专区 from a donation of 10,000+ records/CDs by 旅美醫師葉思雅・張信惠夫婦 — see [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20211207113948_2021_10_15_台南許石音樂圖書館信雅古典音樂珍藏專區啟用_4e3b2180b83318fa|台南許石音樂圖書館信雅专区啟用 (2021)]] and the 百歲冥誕 memorial coverage [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20210210230609_2019_01_11_167433_8a371f5ef1c30ecf|作曲家許石百歲冥誕紀念特展 (2019)]].
- HOLD: no corpus material yet ties the Los Angeles 許石基金會 (Taiwan Center group member) directly to the Tainan 許石音樂圖書館 or the memorial foundation's own activities — the two 許石-memorial entities above must not be conflated.
