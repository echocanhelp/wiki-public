# Taiwanese Graduate Student Association at UVa

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Graduate Student Association at UVa
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Graduate Student Association at UVa (TGSA) is a graduate-focused student organization registered in UVA’s Presence platform. It is distinct from the undergraduate-oriented TSA at UVA ([[organizations/taiwanese-student-association-at-the-university-of-virginia|Taiwanese Student Association at the University of Virginia]]) and specifically serves graduate students with an interest in Taiwanese culture and community.

## Role in the Community
