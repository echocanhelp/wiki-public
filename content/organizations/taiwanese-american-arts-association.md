# Taiwanese American Arts Association

**Taiwanese American Arts Association** (台美藝術協會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Role in the Community

TAAA is an arts association in the Taiwanese American community of Southern California. Member memoirs describe it as a group with twice-yearly (spring and autumn) seasonal exhibitions (春秋二次季展) that encourage members' continued practice — the physician/woodcarver 鄭炳全 records joining after four years of 木雕 study in his 2025 memoir ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250209211009_旅美五十周年-_-鄭炳全_438dc69b91722b16|鄭炳全《旅美五十周年》]], Taiwan Justice Net]).

Beyond the exhibition circuit, TAAA sat on the institutional side of the Los Angeles Taiwanese American community, not only its artistic side:

- At the 大洛杉磯台灣會館 (Taiwan Center) 會員大會 of **2018-06-17** the association held one of the ten 團體董事 (group-director) seats on the newly elected 11th board, represented by **郭曉玗** — alongside 聖東同鄉會, 彰化同鄉會, 台灣婦女會, FAPA, 亞美社福, 台灣人醫師協會, 台美律師會 and 台美商會 ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2020/20201118162555_2018_06_27_大洛杉磯台灣會館會訊_2018-年六月-_大洛杉磯台灣_79291dad808ef7f7|台美會訊 2018-06-27]], via the 2020 Wayback capture). The page's "Taiwan Center group member" status is therefore corroborated by a governance record, not only a directory listing.
- In **2024** the association mounted a 夏季畫展 (summer painting exhibition) at the 洛僑中心 on 5/18–19, one of the exhibitions folded into that year's 大洛杉磯台美人傳統週 (Taiwanese American Heritage Week) series ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240429065124_root_55dbc6c8f41b2ad0|2024 傳統週 series listing]]); the same event surfaces as a headline in a 2025 category-page snapshot ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250420093558_category_taiwan-culture_page_2_b0f55e35c6448ed6|台灣人文藝術 category page]]).
- 鄭炳全's memoir ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250209211009_旅美五十周年-_-鄭炳全_438dc69b91722b16|旅美五十周年]]) additionally records that most of the community's clubs 後繼無人 — few new-generation members joined. Whether that generalisation extends to TAAA is **HOLD**: the corpus has no TAAA membership continuity record.
