# National Taiwanese Presbyterian Council (NTPC)

**National Taiwanese Presbyterian Council (NTPC)** is an organization of Taiwanese Presbyterian churches in the United States, operating under the Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.) [PC(USA)] system. The NTPC plays a key role in preserving Taiwanese Presbyterian heritage and supporting diaspora church communities.

## Identity Snapshot

- **Type:** Presbyterian church council/organization
- **Geography:** United States
- **Denomination:** Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.) [PC(USA)]
- **Focus:** Taiwanese-American Presbyterian ministry, church heritage preservation, diaspora support

## Historical Role

### FPCLA Founding Connection

The NTPC traces its roots to the founding of the first Taiwanese Presbyterian church in North America — the Formosan Presbyterian Church in Los Angeles (FPCLA) — in 1970. FPCLA was established under the United Presbyterian Church in the USA (UPCUSA, now PC(USA)) system, and the NTPC grew from this foundation to become a national council of Taiwanese Presbyterian churches.

### 50th Anniversary Republication (2025)

The NTPC played a central role in the 2025 republication of Dr. Albert S. Lai's 1971 dissertation *Toward A Community of Hope — A Mission to Formosan Community in Los Angeles*. The republication included a foreword by NTPC Moderator Dr. Yunching Yeh Davis (葉芸青), signaling the council's commitment to preserving and promoting Taiwanese Presbyterian heritage.

## Mission and Vision

The NTPC's work is guided by the theological vision articulated in Dr. Lai's dissertation — that the church should transcend being merely a haven for immigrants and become a missional community, a beacon of hope and witness to the gospel in the larger society.

## NTPC Historical Committee (2025–Present)

### Establishment

In 2025, the Annual Conference of the NTPC convened under the theme **"Preserving and Passing On the Faith History of NTPC."** Following the 50th anniversary celebration in 2024 (during which Rev. David Huang edited a commemorative publication), the NTPC took a significant step: the official establishment of the **NTPC Historical Committee**.

**Rev. Ching Fang Chen (陳清芳)** was appointed as its inaugural chairperson, stating:

> "The history of the Church is a testament to the grace of God—a record not only of divine providence but of the fellowship, labor, and witness of God's people through the ages. Half a century has passed in the blink of an eye. Many of the pioneering stories and tear-stained testimonies of those who sowed in faith are at risk of fading as the generations who lived them grow old and pass on. Without timely preservation, these precious memories may be lost to time."

### Mission and Purpose

The NTPC Historical Committee's work includes:

- **Preserving faith history:** Gathering, organizing, and documenting the spiritual heritage of Taiwanese American Presbyterian churches
- **Publishing historical resources:** The 2025 republication of Dr. Lai's dissertation was the first publication of the NTPC Historical Committee
- **Encouraging church archives:** Calling on more churches to actively document and preserve their own testimonies of faith
- **Connecting generations:** Creating a clear and enduring trail of witness for the Church of Christ

### Dr. Lai's Dissertation as Cornerstone Resource

The NTPC Historical Committee identified Dr. Albert S. Lai's 1971 dissertation as a cornerstone resource for understanding the development of Taiwanese Presbyterian Churches in the United States:

> "Particular gratitude is due to Dr. Albert S. Lai, whose 1971 Doctor of Ministry dissertation, 'Toward a Community of Hope — A Mission to Formosan Community in Los Angeles,' remains a rare and invaluable document. It offers a detailed and moving account of the founding of the very first Presbyterian Church in Taiwan in Southern California, as well as the missionary vision that inspired its formation."

The NTPC Executive Committee formally endorsed and supported the publication, signaling the Council's affirmation of historical ministry as a vital calling.

## 50th Anniversary Republication (2025)

The NTPC played a central role in the 2025 republication of Dr. Albert S. Lai's 1971 dissertation *Toward A Community of Hope — A Mission to Formosan Community in Los Angeles*. The republication included:

- **Foreword by NTPC Moderator Dr. Yunching Yeh Davis (葉芸青):** "Half a Century of Faith and Mission"
- **Foreword by PCT Moderator Rev. En Sheng Pan (潘恩盛):** "Praise and Recommendation for the Book"
- **Foreword by NTPC Historical Committee Chair Rev. Ching Fang Chen (陳清芳):** "Preserving and Passing On the Faith History of NTPC"
- **Foreword by Rev. Mingyuan Hsu (許明遠):** "Let History Ignite a New Flame of Mission"
- **Foreword by Rev. Pinghsi Liu (劉炳熹):** "The Mission of Connection: A Faith Journey Across the Ocean"
- **Self-Preface by Dr. Albert S. Lai (賴信雄):** Written May 2025, Monterey Park, California

The republication was published by **Taiwan Church Press** and the **Incorporated Association of Tainan East Gate Barclay History Education Promotion**.
