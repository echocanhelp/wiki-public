# National Taiwanese Presbyterian Council (NTPC)

**National Taiwanese Presbyterian Council (NTPC)** is an organization of Taiwanese Presbyterian churches in the United States, operating under the Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.) [PC(USA)] system. The NTPC plays a key role in preserving Taiwanese Presbyterian heritage and supporting diaspora church communities.

## Identity Snapshot

- **Type:** Presbyterian church council/organization
- **Geography:** United States
- **Denomination:** Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.) [PC(USA)]
- **Focus:** Taiwanese-American Presbyterian ministry, church heritage preservation, diaspora support

## Historical Role

### FPCLA Founding Connection

The NTPC traces its roots to the founding of the first Taiwanese Presbyterian church in North America — the [[organizations/formosan-presbyterian-church-in-los-angeles|Formosan Presbyterian Church in Los Angeles (FPCLA)]] — in 1970. FPCLA was established under the United Presbyterian Church in the USA (UPCUSA, now PC(USA)) system, and the NTPC grew from this foundation to become a national council of Taiwanese Presbyterian churches.

### 50th Anniversary Context (2024–2025)

In 2024, NTPC celebrated its 50th anniversary; Rev. David Huang edited a commemorative publication. In 2025 the Annual Conference theme was **"Preserving and Passing On the Faith History of NTPC,"** and the council established the NTPC Historical Committee.

## Mission and Vision

The NTPC's work is guided by the theological vision articulated in [[people/albert-s-lai|Dr. Albert S. Lai]]'s dissertation [[sources/toward-a-community-of-hope|Toward A Community of Hope]] — that the church should transcend being merely a haven for immigrants and become a missional community, a beacon of hope and witness to the gospel in the larger society.

## NTPC Historical Committee (2025–Present)

### Establishment

**[[people/ching-fang-chen|Rev. Ching Fang Chen (陳清芳)]]** was appointed inaugural chairperson of the NTPC Historical Committee in 2025, stating:

> "The history of the Church is a testament to the grace of God—a record not only of divine providence but of the fellowship, labor, and witness of God's people through the ages. Half a century has passed in the blink of an eye. Many of the pioneering stories and tear-stained testimonies of those who sowed in faith are at risk of fading as the generations who lived them grow old and pass on. Without timely preservation, these precious memories may be lost to time."

### Mission and Purpose

- **Preserving faith history** of Taiwanese American Presbyterian churches
- **Publishing historical resources** (first publication: 2025 republication of Lai's dissertation)
- **Encouraging church archives** across congregations
- **Connecting generations** through documented witness
