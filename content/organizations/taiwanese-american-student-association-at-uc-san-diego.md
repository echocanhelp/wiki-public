# Taiwanese American Student Association at UC San Diego

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Student Association at UC San Diego
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Student Association (TASA) at the University of California, San Diego is a registered student organization that provides a community for Taiwanese Americans and those interested in Taiwanese culture. The organization is listed in the UCSD Center for Student Involvement’s official student org directory and maintains an active constitution last revised in September 2024.

## Role in the Community
No corpus record documents this chapter's own founding or activities (none absorbable beyond the directory listing). What the corpus does establish is the UCSD campus as a node of Taiwanese-American institutional life: UCSD received a $5M gift to found an interdisciplinary Center of Taiwan Studies in 2023 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-386|386. UCSD獲500萬成立台灣研究中心跨學科合作]]; founding director's year-end review in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-390|390. 2023 in Review: UCSD Center of Taiwan Studies]]), and the Chuan Lyu Endowed Chair in Taiwan Studies (川流台灣研究講座基金) is recorded at UCSD ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/30-chuan-lyu-endowed-chair-in-taiwan-studies|Chuan Lyu Endowed Chair]]). UCSD also hosted early TACF (Taiwanese Christian Fellowship) summer-camp site contracts per the movement memoir ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys265|Our Journeys 265]]). These contextual anchors frame the environment the TASA chapter operates in; they are not attributed to the club itself.
