# Taiwanese American Student Association at UC San Diego

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Student Association at UC San Diego
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Student Association (TASA) at the University of California, San Diego is a registered student organization that provides a community for Taiwanese Americans and those interested in Taiwanese culture. The organization is listed in the UCSD Center for Student Involvement’s official student org directory and maintains an active constitution last revised in September 2024.

## Role in the Community
- **2010 — UCSD campus documented as an active Taiwanese student node.** Ho Chie's year-in-review lists the ITASA conference at UCSD among his 2010 campus stops, and separately records a southern-California TACL-LID summer camp comeback organized by UCSD-area student leaders preparing for the next West Coast ITASA conference at UC San Diego ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/on-discovering-passion-purpose|On Discovering Passion & Purpose, 2010-11-11]]).
- **2012-05-05 — the chapter's own documented activity:** a "Mr. & Ms. Formosa Culture Pageant Presented by UCSD TASA" is recorded in the community corpus ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/mr-and-ms-formosa-culture-pageant-presented-by-ucsd-tasa|Mr. & Ms. Formosa Culture Pageant, 2012-05-05]]) — a cultural pageant presented directly by UCSD TASA.
- Beyond the chapter itself, the corpus establishes UCSD as a node of Taiwanese-American institutional life: UCSD received a $5M gift to found an interdisciplinary Center of Taiwan Studies in 2023 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-386|386. UCSD獲500萬成立台灣研究中心跨學科合作]]; founding director's year-end review in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/our-journeys-390|390. 2023 in Review: UCSD Center of Taiwan Studies]]), and the Chuan Lyu Endowed Chair in Taiwan Studies (川流台灣研究講座基金) is recorded at UCSD ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/30-chuan-lyu-endowed-chair-in-taiwan-studies|Chuan Lyu Endowed Chair]]). UCSD also hosted early TACF (Taiwanese Christian Fellowship) summer-camp site contracts per the movement memoir ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys265|Our Journeys 265]]).
