# Chinese Christian Church of New Jersey (新澤西主恩堂台語堂)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Chinese Christian Church of New Jersey
- **Chinese:** 新澤西主恩堂台語堂
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Chinese Christian Church of New Jersey (CCCNJ) is a multi-congregation Christian church in New Jersey that includes both Mandarin-speaking and Taiwanese (Hokkien)-speaking congregations. The Taiwanese-language congregation (台語堂) grew out of small groups and fellowships that began in 1989–1990, formally becoming a fellowship in 1991 and an official congregation in 1998. The church is noted as a pioneering model in North America for integrating Mandarin and Taiwanese congregations under one roof. Activities have included Taiwanese-language Bible study, Sunday school, evangelistic outreach, and instruction in Pe̍h-ōe-jī (romanized Taiwanese script).

The memoir documents the 台語堂's several concurrent 查經小組 and the generational-tech adaptation challenge of its aging membership.

## From the record
- 1989–1990 — 台語堂 predecessor: 主恩堂 regional small groups reorganized and named after the fruit of the Spirit; the 節制組 (mixed Hokkien/non-Hokkien members) is the seed, and in 1990 it merged the Hokkien speakers of another group to form the 台語小組 (per the church's own 台語堂發展史, `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/32-%e6%96%b0%e6%be%a4%e8%a5%bf%e4%b8%bb%e6%81%a9%e5%a0%82%e5%8f%b0%e8%aa%9e%e5%a0%82201506.md`) — `alleged` (first-party).
- 1991 — 台語團契 formally founded; from its first year it taught 白話字 (Pe̍h-ōe-jī) and opened the first 台語主日學 class 「基要信仰」, drawing on the century-plus of 台語宣教 in Taiwan (per the same 2015 development history) — `alleged`.
- 1992–1998 — between 1992 and the official founding in 1998, the 台語團契 ran internal 查經分享/靈命成長/聯誼互助 plus external 台語佈道會 (per the same record) — `alleged`.
- 1997 — planning for 台語崇拜 began: after the weekly Wednesday prayer meeting, group workers repeatedly stayed to deliberate, completed a proposal submitted to the 長老團, and a 午堂主日崇拜委員會 was formed (per the same record) — `alleged`.
- 1998-03-01 — CCCNJ congregation assembly (會友大會) voted to approve the 午堂台語禮拜; first worship service held 1998-04-05; adult Sunday school opened in June 1998; the 台語禮拜同工會 was formally organized (per the same record) — `alleged`.
- c. 1999 — the 台語堂 had no settled pastor at founding; the following year the congregation voted to call a 台語部牧師, the 台語事工部 was established, and the 〈台語事工組織規章〉 was adopted (per the same record) — `alleged`.
- 2000 — in October the 同工會 resolved to create the 台語事工策劃委員會 (「策委會」) as the 台語堂's internal decision-making body; earlier that year a 聘牧委員會 was formed (per the same record) — `alleged`.
- 2001 — the 長執董聯合會 approved the 聘牧委員會's nominee, the 會友大會 voted in April, and the first pastor was installed in August — completing the 台語堂's organizational structure (per the same record) — `alleged`.
