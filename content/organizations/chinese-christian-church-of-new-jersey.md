# Chinese Christian Church of New Jersey (新澤西主恩堂台語堂)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Chinese Christian Church of New Jersey
- **Chinese:** 新澤西主恩堂台語堂
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Chinese Christian Church of New Jersey (CCCNJ) is a multi-congregation Christian church in New Jersey that includes both Mandarin-speaking and Taiwanese (Hokkien)-speaking congregations. The Taiwanese-language congregation (台語堂) grew out of small groups and fellowships that began in 1989–1990, formally becoming a fellowship in 1991 and an official congregation in 1998. The church is noted as a pioneering model in North America for integrating Mandarin and Taiwanese congregations under one roof. Activities have included Taiwanese-language Bible study, Sunday school, evangelistic outreach, and instruction in Pe̍h-ōe-jī (romanized Taiwanese script).
