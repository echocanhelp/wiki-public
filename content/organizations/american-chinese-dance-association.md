# American Chinese Dance Association (美國華裔舞蹈協會, ACDA)

## Identity Snapshot
- **Era:** founded ~1987–89 (25th anniversary party Feb 11, 2012 → 1987; 34th annual gala Feb 5, 2023 → 1989; "cultivating 20+ years" already by 2009) — first-draft SGV institution
- **Geography:** San Gabriel Valley / greater Los Angeles
- **Core roles:** performing-arts federation (taiwanese american community institution) — an association *of* dance schools, not one school

## Record

- **Model:** a member association of independent SoChinese dance studios — at the 2026 **中華舞篇 (Chinese Dance Chapter)** showcase (37th edition, Apr 18, 2026, San Gabriel Mission Playhouse, ~1,300 audience): **13 troupes, 300+ teachers/dancers/crew** in one co-produced gala. Troupes named on the program include 宇清藝術工作坊, 金鈴舞蹈學校, 陳進舞蹈學校, 王馨悅舞蹈學校, 和韻舞團, 核桃土風舞社.
- **Repertoire as identity data:** the 2009 showcase programmed Han, Manchu, Yunnan, Mongolian, **Taiwanese (山地/aboriginal)**, and **Hakka** dances — and in 2026: 《我們的田野》, 客家採茶舞, 白紵舞. The 2009 show opened right after a performance slot at the local **台灣文化節 (Taiwan Cultural Festival)**; 2023: ROC 經文處 紀欽耀's remarks — "台灣是個自由民主的國家，所以我們有中華傳統文化、原住民文化的舞蹈，也有現代舞蹈."
- **Leadership lineage (multi-sourced):** presidents **賴泱璇** (first term **1996**, re-took office at the 2023-02-05 handover gala; Cultural University (TW) dance faculty 16 years, then East LA College Chinese-dance instructor), **吳玉玲** (2009 era), past presidents **陳西園**, **易天羽**.
- **Institution moves:** founded **ACDA舞劇場 (dance theater)** 2004 to train the next generation of choreographers (members later at UC Riverside dance PhD, NYC MFA); annual **dance competition** with age brackets toddlers→70s, record turnout 2023 with scholarships added; the gala's free-ticket-for-seniors (65+) tradition; OCAC/經文處 consistently attends.
- **SGV venue history:** galas at San Gabriel Theater/Mission Playhouse; 25th anniversary (2012) at STC Center, City of Industry; annual competitions at the LA OCAC center (洛僑中心).

## Historical Significance

The **arts pillar of the SGV first generation** — same vintage as RHCA and TAALA East SGV, and its record quietly documents the identity negotiation of that class: Chinese-classical canon as the respectable shell, with **Taiwan aboriginal and Hakka dances programmed inside it**, funded by 僑委會, cheered on by the 經文處, and run by teachers trained in Taipei. Thirty-seven years of one-night-only intergenerational showcases (ages 4–70s, seniors in the front row) is also the cleanest longitudinal record of the community's children growing up.
