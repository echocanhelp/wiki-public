# Taiwanese International Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese International Student Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese International Student Association (TiSA) at the University of Texas at Austin is a student organization focused on serving Taiwanese and Mandarin-speaking undergraduate students at UT Austin, as well as others interested in Taiwanese culture. TiSA is registered on UT Austin’s official HornsLink student organization directory and carries the Chinese name 德州大學台灣國際學生會. The organization maintains a website at uttisa.com, a Facebook page under the handle TiSAatUT (221 likes), and an Instagram account (@tisa_utaustin).
