# MIT Republic of China Taiwan Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** MIT Republic of China Taiwan Student Association
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The MIT Republic of China (Taiwan) Student Association, known as MIT ROCSA, is an MIT-sponsored, student-run organization serving Taiwanese students, researchers, faculty, and their families at MIT and in the greater Boston area. The organization describes itself on its website as a community for students who come from the Republic of China (Taiwan), with a mission focused on connection, mutual support, and cultural introduction to the wider MIT community.
