# MIT Republic of China Taiwan Student Association

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** MIT Republic of China (Taiwan) Student Association (MIT ROCSA)
- **Chinese:** MIT臺灣同學會 per TAH directory infobox, https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/organization/mit-republic-of-china-taiwan-student-association/ (2026-11-14); the organization has also used 麻省理工學院中華民國(台灣)同學會 (own old homepage, http://rocsa.mit.edu/old/index.html) and 麻省理工學院台灣學生會 (press usage)
- **Era:** 1983 claim (alleged, single source) — Contemporary
- **Geography:** Cambridge, Massachusetts (MIT campus) / greater Boston
- **Core roles:** Student organization (MIT-sponsored, student-run); TAH directory taxonomy `organization_type-student` per `knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/organization/mit-republic-of-china-taiwan-student-association.json`

The MIT Republic of China (Taiwan) Student Association, known as MIT ROCSA, is an MIT-sponsored, student-run organization serving Taiwanese students, researchers, faculty, and their families at MIT and in the greater Boston area. The organization describes itself on its website as a community for students who come from the Republic of China (Taiwan), with a mission focused on connection, mutual support, and cultural introduction to the wider MIT community.
