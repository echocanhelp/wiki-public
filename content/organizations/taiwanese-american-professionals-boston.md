# Taiwanese American Professionals Boston

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Professionals Boston
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

Taiwanese American Professionals Boston (TAP-BOS) is a chapter of the Taiwanese American Citizens League (TACL), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit run entirely by volunteers. TAP-BOS serves the Taiwanese American community in the greater Boston area by providing a supportive environment where members can reflect, grow, and build meaningful relationships. The chapter runs programming centered on leadership development and cultural engagement, including the EDGE Program.

## Role in the Community
The corpus records a broader Taiwanese American Professionals (TAP, 台美菁英協會) network with chapters documented across the US: the national profile ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-professionals-tap|TAP 台美菁英協會]], 2014), Orange County chapter activities ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/activities-of-orange-county-chapter-tap|TAP-OC]]), the TAP-LA e-newsletter ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/enewsletter-tapla|TAP-LA eNewsletter]]), and scholarship programs in Seattle ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/35-taiwanese-american-professionals-of-seattle-scholarship-award|Seattle TAP scholarship]]) and San Francisco ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/24-taiwanese-american-professionals-san-francisco-chapter-tap-sf-scholarship|TAP-SF scholarship]]). TAP-BOS extends this chapter model to Boston with leadership-development and cultural programming, including the EDGE Program.
