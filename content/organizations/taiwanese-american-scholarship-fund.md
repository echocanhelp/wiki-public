# Taiwanese American Scholarship Fund (台美獎學金基金會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Scholarship Fund
- **Chinese:** 台美獎學金基金會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Scholarship Fund (TASF) was created in 2014 to support economically challenged students of Taiwanese descent residing in the United States in their pursuit of higher education. Through a competitive application process, scholarship funds are applied toward tuition for selected students in their first or second year of college. TASF serves as a financial resource for Taiwanese American students facing economic barriers to higher education.

## Timeline

- 2014 — TASF created amid a broader community-scholarship wave: 黃東昇's Our Journeys 303 retrospective records that between 2003 and 2017 fourteen regions across the US ran 台美社區高中生獎學金 programs by local Taiwanese groups, with nearly a thousand high-school recipients before most programs faded due to leadership and funding turnover; TASF continued that mission at national scale ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys303|303. 台美社區高中生獎學金方案的簡介 / T.A. Archives /07/2017]]).
- 2015 — first campaign covered by TaiwaneseAmerican.org and the TAH directory; TASF ran as a project of the Asian Pacific Community Fund; the 2015 drive advertised $35,000 in awards ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/the-2015-taiwanese-american-scholarship-fund|TA.org 2015 drive]]; [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-american-scholarship-fund|TAH #15]]). The fund is described in the corpus as made possible by a group of Taiwanese American philanthropists spearheaded by Sean Yu (余崇孝).
- 2016 — drive of $50,000: twenty $2,500 scholarships for high-school seniors and 1st-year students who are U.S. citizens of direct Taiwanese descent, deadline 2016-02-29 ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/the-2016-taiwanese-american-scholarship-fund|TA.org 2016 drive]]).
- 2017-02-16 — the annual drive again ran as a project of the Asian Pacific Community Fund, targeted at economically-challenged students of Taiwanese descent in their first or second year of college, covered by TA.org as community record ([[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/2017-tasf|TA.org 2017 drive]]).
- 2017-01-04 — scholarship awards notice ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/awards-taiwanese-american-scholarship-fund|TAH #73]]).
- 2020-03-07 — TASF Scholarship record ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/50-tasf-scholarship-2020|TAH #50]]).
