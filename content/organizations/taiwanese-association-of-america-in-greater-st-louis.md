# Taiwanese Association of America in Greater St. Louis (聖路易)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of America in Greater St. Louis
- **Chinese:** 聖路易
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America in Greater St. Louis (TAA-STL; 聖路易 台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community across the Greater St. Louis metropolitan area of Missouri.
