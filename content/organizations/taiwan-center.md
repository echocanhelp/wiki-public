# Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles

Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles is a cultural and community hub serving the Taiwanese-American community in the Greater LA area. The center hosts events such as the annual 228 Memorial Commemoration and supports the new Taiwan Center building construction project.
