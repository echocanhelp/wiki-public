# Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館)

**Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles** (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會; also **Taiwan Center** / TWCLA) is a nonprofit cultural and community institution for Taiwanese Americans in Southern California. Official site: [taiwancenter.org](https://taiwancenter.org/).

## Identity Snapshot

- **English:** Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles
- **Chinese:** 大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會 / 台灣會館
- **Type:** Nonprofit cultural foundation; community hub
- **Geography:** Greater Los Angeles (property historically associated with Rosemead / LA County area per org history)
- **Founded:** 12 June 1998 as 南加州台灣會館基金會; renamed 大洛杉磯台灣會館 in 2001
- **Core roles:** Taiwanese cultural promotion; community education (Taiwan School); venue rental; library; major annual events (TAHW, 228 memorial, annual gala)
- **EIN / charity:** Charity Navigator lists EIN **95-4679702** (linked from official donate path)

## Historical Context

In 1998, overseas commissioner **王桂榮 (Wang Kuei-jung / K.C. Wang)** donated property in Rosemead (Los Angeles County). **吳澧培 (Wu Li-pei / former head of General Bank / 萬通銀行)** served as fundraising convener. With support from **40+ Taiwanese organizations** and about **1,000 community donors**, roughly **US$1 million** was raised. The foundation was established **12 June 1998** as a nonprofit holding the Taiwan Center and charged with promoting Taiwanese culture and mutual understanding with other communities. Renamed **大洛杉磯台灣會館** in **2001**.

Source: official introduction page archive (`knowledge/web-archives/taiwancenter-org-introduction.md`).

## Mission (official themes)

- Public-interest, cultural, and educational programs that promote Taiwanese cultural tradition  
- Exchange with mainstream society; raise Taiwanese American visibility  
- Cultivate second-generation Taiwanese Americans and cultural transmission  
- Encourage community participation  

## Organization

Per official introduction:

- **Board:** 36 directors; chairperson also serves as Center president  
- Officers include chief supervisor / deputy, CFO / deputy, secretary-general / deputy; six vice presidents under the president  
- **10 committees** and **14 working groups** (building, activities, membership, next-gen, office, mainstream, historical materials, business services, bylaws, social media, Taiwan School, interior design, nominations, annual gala, Miss Taiwanese American, TAHW, 228, major events, etc.)  
- **Membership:** 800+ permanent individual members; 50+ group members; described as a leading LA Taiwanese American community hub  

## Programs & facilities

### Taiwan School (台灣學校)
Courses include Chinese language, Japanese, computer, vegetarian cooking, choir, sewing, beginner photography, and related community classes.

### Facilities / venue
Spaces described for multi-use events (e.g. auditorium capacity cited ~250, meeting rooms, classrooms, media room, exhibition space, rooftop garden). Forms and rental process published on the site.

### Wang Kuei-jung Memorial Library (王桂榮紀念圖書館)
Collection described as **10,000+ volumes** (art, history, fiction, music, language, humanities).
