# Taiwanese Association of America – South Jersey Chapter (南澤西台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of America – South Jersey Chapter
- **Chinese:** 南澤西台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America – South Jersey Chapter (南澤西台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in southern New Jersey and is classified under the TAA’s Eastern Northern (美東北) regional grouping.

## History & Community Role

- Founded in the **1980s** by local Taiwanese Americans in the **Cherry Hill area of southwest New Jersey** (east of Philadelphia), where many Taiwanese American families were settled. ([corpus record](https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/organization/taiwanese-association-of-america-south-jersey-chapter/) held as [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/south-jersey-chapter-taa|South Jersey Chapter / TAA 全美台灣同鄉會南澤西分會]], published 2015-11-03, band B)
- Early activities combined **fellowship events and softball tournaments** with civic work: members engaged local Congressmen and government officials on issues beneficial to Taiwan; TAH classifies the chapter as a **政治社團 (political organization)**.
- Bilingual corpus account (EN/ZH) also mirrored in [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys356|Our Journeys 356]] and its English counterpart [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys356-eng|Our Journeys 356 (EN)]].
