# Taiwanese Association of America – South Jersey Chapter (南澤西台灣同鄉會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Association of America – South Jersey Chapter
- **Chinese:** 南澤西台灣同鄉會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese Association of America – South Jersey Chapter (南澤西台灣同鄉會) is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations in the United States. The chapter serves the Taiwanese American community in southern New Jersey and is classified under the TAA’s Eastern Northern (美東北) regional grouping.
