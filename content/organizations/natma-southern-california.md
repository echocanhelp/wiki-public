# North American Taiwanese Medical Association - Southern California Chapter（NATMA-SCC）

**North American Taiwanese Medical Association - Southern California Chapter（NATMA-SCC）** (南加州台灣醫學會) is listed as a **group member** of the [[organizations/taiwan-center||Taiwan Center Foundation of the Greater Los Angeles]].

## Identity Snapshot

- **Chinese:** 南加州台灣醫學會
- **English (as listed):** North American Taiwanese Medical Association - Southern California Chapter（NATMA-SCC）
- **Relation:** Taiwan Center group member (official listing)
- **Source:** https://www.taiwancenter.org/group-members · `taiwancenter-org-group-members.md`

## Role in the Community

- The chapter published its own yearbook: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/847-e5-8c-97-e7-be-8e-e6-b4-b2-e5-8f-b0-e7-81-a3-e4-ba-ba-e9-86-ab-e5-b8-ab-e5-8|TAH #847 北美洲台灣人醫師協會南加州分會2005年刊]]
- Organized the 台美醫學交流會 in 2016: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/12-70|TAH #70 台美醫學交流會 by NATMA / S. California]]
- Took part in the 2017 NATMA delegates' Taiwan Medical Progress Exchange Tour (11/05–12/2017): [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/2017-28|TAH #28 NATMA Delegates Exchange Tour, 2017]]
- NATMA's international medical-service tradition is documented in member memoirs: from 2005 members joined NATMA 國際義診團 to Taiwan's Central American allies six times (family medicine, internal medicine/pediatrics, OB-GYN, surgery, dentistry, acupuncture, donated medicines and equipment); the March 2015 Guatemala mission fielded 56 people, served 3,058 patients, and set a record of 53 surgeries at the Amatitlan public hospital: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys274|Our Journeys — NATMA義診團 memoir]]
- An earlier-generation memoir lists NATMA (醫師協會) among the twelve Taiwanese American organizations that jointly ran the US census advocacy campaign alongside TAA, TACL, and FAPA: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys253|Our Journeys — 人口普查專案 memoir]]
- 2021: 北美洲台灣人醫師協會南加州分會 (and its foundation chapter) were co-initiating organizations of the Southern California joint statement supporting Taiwan's WHO/WHA participation: [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2021/20210616072940_2021_05_17_2021年南加州僑界支持台灣參與世界衛生大會wha聯合聲_b1d58af16c0a5e5b|2021年南加州僑界支持台灣參與WHA聯合聲明]] — the initiator list names all four NATMA entities side by side (協會、協會南加州分會、協會基金會、協會基金會南加州分會), documenting the chapter as part of a four-body organizational structure
- The chapter operates under the parent national association 北美洲台灣人醫師協會 (NATMA/USA), whose own TAH directory record and member bulletin are held in the corpus: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/north-american-taiwanese-medical-association-foundation|TAH — NATMA/USA 北美洲台灣人醫師協會]] and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/enewsletter-natma|TAH — NATMA eNews letter record]]
- 2010: the chapter's annual meeting and banquet is documented in its own TAHS corpus record: [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/natma-scc-2010-annual-meeting-and-banquet|NATMA-SCC 2010 Annual Meeting and Banquet]]
- 2025-06-18: NATMA-SCC and WHO Committee issued a three-point statement of full support for Taiwan For WHO during the pandemic — arguing Taiwan must not be excluded from WHO and that NATMA's founding mission, like WHO's, is Health For All regardless of politics: [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20250618180803_natma-scc-and-who-committee-全力支持taiwan-for-who的三點聲明及呼籲_0d62c671f819ee47|TJJ 2025-06-18 三點聲明及呼籲]]; ongoing TJJ coverage is archived under the tag page [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2025/20251010065053_tag_natma-南加州分會_c289b7415d1ae041|Tag: NATMA 南加州分會]]
- A republished Southern California community event calendar lists the chapter's 2016-09-10 annual meeting at Pacific Palms Resort alongside 台灣會馆 events — evidence of the chapter's place in the regular SoCal community calendar: [[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2024/20240721101942_root_04f32b14d8cf8f12|TJJ archive — 南加州活動日曆]]

- 2021/02 — 北美洲台灣人醫師協會南加州分會 is listed among the 友情宣傳 community supporters of the 台灣人聯合基金會 / 台灣優社 228 事件線上追思紀念會 series, alongside 洛杉磯聖東台灣同鄉會 and other SoCal groups ([[articles/taiwanjustice-net/2026/20260210173508_2021大洛杉磯台灣會館228事件線上追思紀念會與系列活_ac0c255ef64908a5|TJJ 2021-02-24 紀念會系列活動]])
