# Taiwanese American Center of Northern California (北加州台灣會館)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Center of Northern California
- **Chinese:** 北加州台灣會館
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Center of Northern California (北加州台灣會館, TAC) is a non-profit community organization founded in 2003 to serve as a gathering place and resource center for Taiwanese Americans in the San Francisco Bay Area. Based in San Jose, the center operates out of an 8,600-square-foot facility in the Silicon Valley area and provides a wide range of educational, cultural, and civic programs.
