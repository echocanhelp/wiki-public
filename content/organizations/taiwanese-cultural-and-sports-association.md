# Taiwanese Cultural and Sports Association (台灣文化體育協會)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese Cultural and Sports Association
- **Chinese:** 台灣文化體育協會
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

TCSA is a non-profit organization based in Northern California that promotes Taiwanese culture and facilitates intercultural exchange between Taiwan, the United States, and other countries. It encourages young Taiwanese Americans to participate in cultural and sports activities. The organization strengthens coordination between Taiwanese communities and other communities, and provides entertainment, educational, and voluntary events such as festivals and sports events.
