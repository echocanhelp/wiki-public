# AJ WANG FOUNDATION – SCHOLARSHIP FUND

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** AJ WANG FOUNDATION – SCHOLARSHIP FUND
- **Chinese:** —
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The AJ Wang Foundation is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization established in November 2008 by immigrant entrepreneurs committed to giving back to the communities that helped them succeed. The Foundation provides funding to educational institutions and supports students with the greatest financial need. Its Scholarship Fund, established in 2013, targets students attending community colleges in the Los Angeles and Ventura County areas. Scholarships may cover tuition, fees, books, housing, transportation, and food, and are renewable for up to two or three years based on academic progress. The Foundation is entirely volunteer-supported and also accepts donations from others wishing to participate in its mission.
