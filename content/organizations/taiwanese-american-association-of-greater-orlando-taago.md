# Taiwanese American Association of Greater Orlando TAAGO (大奧蘭多)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Association of Greater Orlando TAAGO
- **Chinese:** 大奧蘭多
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Association of Greater Orlando (TAAGO), known in Chinese as 大奧蘭多 台灣同鄉會, is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations. TAAGO serves the Taiwanese American community in the greater Orlando, Florida area and is listed under the TAA’s Eastern Southern (美東南) regional grouping.

## Role in the Community

The corpus holds a dedicated TAH record of the chapter itself: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/orlando-fl-chapter-taa|Taiwanese American Association, Greater Orlando Chapter 大奧蘭多台灣同鄉會]] (published 2015-11-14), which confirms the 大奧蘭多台灣同鄉會 name and its status as a TAA (全美台灣同鄉會) chapter.

Orlando's Taiwanese community is small but institutionally layered: TAAGO sits alongside [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-chamber-of-commerce-of-greater-orlando|大奧蘭多台灣商會]] (Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce of Greater Orlando, 2016 record) and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/evangelical-formosan-church-of-orlando|奧蘭多台福基督教會]] (Evangelical Formosan Church of Orlando, 2016 record), forming the local civic–commercial–church triangle typical of regional Taiwanese American communities.

TAAGO representation appears in the community's public advocacy record: HueiTong Huang was listed as the Taiwanese American Association of Greater Orlando signatory in the Pew-statement response signed by Taiwanese American leaders [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/pew-research-center-taiwanese-american-statement|Pew Research Center Taiwanese American statement response]].
