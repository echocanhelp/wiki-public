# Taiwanese American Association of Greater Orlando TAAGO (大奧蘭多)

## Identity Snapshot
- **English:** Taiwanese American Association of Greater Orlando TAAGO
- **Chinese:** 大奧蘭多
- **Era:** Contemporary
- **Geography:** Taiwanese American community
- **Core roles:** Community organization (TAH directory)

The Taiwanese American Association of Greater Orlando (TAAGO), known in Chinese as 大奧蘭多 台灣同鄉會, is a regional chapter of the Taiwanese Association of America (TAA, 全美台灣同鄉會), the national umbrella organization for Taiwanese American community associations. TAAGO serves the Taiwanese American community in the greater Orlando, Florida area and is listed under the TAA’s Eastern Southern (美東南) regional grouping.

## Role in the Community

The corpus holds a dedicated TAH record of the chapter itself: [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/orlando-fl-chapter-taa|Taiwanese American Association, Greater Orlando Chapter 大奧蘭多台灣同鄉會]] (published 2015-11-14), which confirms the 大奧蘭多台灣同鄉會 name and its status as a TAA (全美台灣同鄉會) chapter.

Orlando's Taiwanese community is small but institutionally layered: TAAGO sits alongside [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/taiwanese-chamber-of-commerce-of-greater-orlando|大奧蘭多台灣商會]] (Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce of Greater Orlando, 2016 record) and [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/evangelical-formosan-church-of-orlando|奧蘭多台福基督教會]] (Evangelical Formosan Church of Orlando, 2016 record), forming the local civic–commercial–church triangle typical of regional Taiwanese American communities.

TAAGO representation appears in the community's public advocacy record: HueiTong Huang was listed as the Taiwanese American Association of Greater Orlando signatory in the Pew-statement response signed by Taiwanese American leaders [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/pew-research-center-taiwanese-american-statement|Pew Research Center Taiwanese American statement response]].

## From the record

Chapter leadership and activity attested in the TAH archive (full quote-verified capture: [[sources/taiwanese-american-association-of-greater-orlando-taago-capture]]):

- 2017: 蕭俊吉 signed the 海外台灣人聲援民視聯署書 (書署 2017年4月26日於台北) as 「佛州大奧蘭多台灣同鄉會會長」 — per knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/related-tw-81.md (vault [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/related-tw-81]]).
- 2018: the 1350-name overseas endorsement list for 《喜樂島聯盟》 (成立大會 2018-04-07, 名單 2018年4月9日) lists 「林瓊華博士：佛州大奧蘭多台灣同鄉會前會長」 and 「蕭俊吉：前奧蘭多台灣同鄉會會長」 — per knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/2018-1.md (vault [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/2018-1]]).
- Same 2018 list records Orlando membership: 「黃會順：奧蘭多台灣同鄉會及FAPA 會員」、「廖蕙澄：奧蘭多台灣同鄉會及FAPA 會員」、「黄明毅、施曙芬：佛羅里達州奧蘭多同鄉會員」 — per knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/2018-1.md.
- 2005: at the 2005 SETAA 美東南區夏令會 (Furman University, SC, 2005-07-07–10), 奧蘭多同鄉會蕭俊吉 performed 臺語雙簧 with Jacksonville 曾博義 and won a 最佳幽默獎; 奧蘭多隊 took 亞軍 in 台語辯論 — per knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/setaa-2005.md (摘自 南佛州台灣同鄉會會訊 05/2005; vault [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/setaa-2005]]).
- 2016: the SETAA 2017 handbook (TAH #1118, 07/2017) credits 「IN 2016, Mr. Shiau (蕭俊吉) worked so hard to have our summer conference」 — Orlando hosted the 2016 美東南區台灣人夏令會 — per knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/setaa-2017.md (vault [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1118]]).
- Directory era: the TAA chapter roster newsletter lists row 33 — FL, Greater Orlando Chapter 「大奧蘭多同鄉會」, status Active, website http://orlandoformosans.blogspot.com/ — per knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/30-newsletter-of-t-a-archives-to-taapresidentlocal-and-usa.md.
