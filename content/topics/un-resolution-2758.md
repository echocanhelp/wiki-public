Resolution 2758 seated the People's Republic of China as China's representative at the 26th UN General Assembly and expelled "the representatives of Chiang Kai-shek." It never mentioned, let alone disposed of, Taiwan's political status. Later readings converge: the US State Department's 1972 legal position, the European Parliament, and the Netherlands' 2021 statement all hold that 2758 does not imply Taiwan is PRC territory. UN practice — the label "Taiwan, Province of China" — follows member consensus, not law; Taiwan has never applied for UN membership. Counter stack: [[topics/counter-ccp-narrative]] TP-3/TP-4.
