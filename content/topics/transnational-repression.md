# 跨國鎮壓 / Transnational Repression

**Tier E event record.** The community is a party to this: people of Taiwanese and Hong Kong
origin living in the United States, Canada and Europe are surveilled, coerced, or attacked on
behalf of the PRC state. This page is not commentary on the news — it is the archive's own
record of a pattern we have documented since 2021, assembled from our own holdings so that any
future story lands on a spine we already have.

## Why this belongs in Echopedia

Per CONTROL §1b, the Tier D↔E discriminator is whether **the community is a party**, not
whether the story is famous. It is:

- The operations occur on soil where our members live: Los Angeles, the San Gabriel Valley,
  Toronto, university towns across the U.S.
- Our archive carried the evidence **before** the current cycle made it a headline.

Labeling standard: every claim below carries its status. **Sourced** = a document in this
archive. **Reported** = press assertion we have not independently verified. **Alleged** =
accusation in a filing or statement, recorded with attribution and without a finding of fact.
Nothing is omitted for being uncomfortable; nothing is asserted beyond what the record holds.
