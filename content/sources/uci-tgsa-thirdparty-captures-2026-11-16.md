## Identity ruling
Both Wayback captures sit on URLs the TAH record itself names (clubs.uci.edu/tgsa, the Google
Sites page), carry the same abbreviation TGSA and matching Chinese self-names, and cross-link each
other (the Google Sites page points to clubs.uci.edu/tgsa as the 新版官方網站). The GitHub org
declares the same blog URL. Treat as one organization; sibling-campus TGSA pages (UC Davis, UCSD,
UCR, Columbia, Brown/RISD, Rice, UVA) remain separate — do not merge.
