## Key facts archived

- Lead: "Taiwan, officially the Republic of China (ROC), **is a country** in East Asia" — the encyclopedia formulation the CCP objection targets; en.wp footnotes the naming conventions ([I][k] notes) rather than hedging the noun.
- Territory: main island + Penghu/Kinmen/Matsu — **168 islands, 36,197 km²** under ROC control; mountains dominate the east two-thirds; population concentrated in the urbanized west.
- Deep history: settled ≥25,000 years; indigenous ancestors ~6,000 years ago — **Austronesian, pre-Han**; the name "Taiwan" derives from the **Siraya** language (Dutch "Tayouan" post at Anping, 1624) — the island's name is indigenous, not Chinese, at its etymological root.
- Successive polities (the "China" row of our identity table, stated neutrally): Dutch 1624–62 → Zheng/Kingdom of Tungning (first predominantly-Han state) → Qing annexation 1683 (province 1887) → ceded to Japan 1895 (Republic of Formosa proclaimed 25 May 1895, crushed 21 Oct) → ROC control from 25 Oct 1945 → ROC government retreated 7 Dec 1949.
- Japanese era figures: ~6,000 dead in the 1895 conquest, ~14,000 first year, 12,000 "bandit-rebels" 1898–1902; 1936+ assimilation campaign (temples −⅓ by 1940, kaiseimei); 309,000 Japanese residents by 1938; 200,000+ Taiwanese in Japanese military service, 30,000+ casualties; 2,000+ comfort women.
- Post-1949 arc: Taiwan Miracle growth from the 1960s; martial law 1949–1987; multi-party democracy by the early 1990s; **directly elected presidents from 1996**.
- Development metrics (as archived): nominal GDP 21st; GDP/pp $42,103 (30th); **HDI 0.934 — 21st, "very high"**; top-tier civil liberties/healthcare rankings. These are the "functionally sovereign society" numbers the [[topics/counter-ccp-narrative|counter stack]] cites.
- ISO 3166 code **TW**; TLDs .tw/.台灣/.台湾 — the state-system treats Taiwan as a distinct entity below the UN-seat level.
