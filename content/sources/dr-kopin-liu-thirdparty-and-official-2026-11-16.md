## What this capture establishes (independent of the TAH self-report)
- **Born 25 January 1949**, grew up in Hsinchu; Hsinchu High School three years (NSTC official profile).
- NTHU chemistry admission was **免試保送** (no entrance exam); two years military service before the U.S. (NSTC).
- **Ph.D. is Ohio State University (1977)** — the TAH form's `Ohio Univ.` is corrected by the academy CV,
  the NSTC profile, and the NSYU chemistry dept. page.
- **Postdocs:** U. Toronto (1977–1979, with J. C. Polanyi) and U. Minnesota (1979–1980, 1981–1982, with W. R. Gentry).
- **Georgia Tech, Experimental Engineering Station, Scientist II (1981)** — a career row absent from the TAH form.
- Returned to Taiwan at the invitation of Academia Sinica president **Yuan T. Lee (李遠哲)**; became IAMS
  **director in August 2001** (NSTC).
- **The 2001 Presidential Science Prize was the inaugural (1st) prize; he was one of three recipients** (TWAS
  Taiwan Chapter roster; NSTC lists him under 2001 Awardees).
- Honors beyond the five self-reported TAH rows, per the academy CV + IAMS page: NTHU Distinguished Alumni
  (2002), Academia Sinica Investigator Award (2008–2012), NSC-NRC Eminent Researchers Award (2009–2010),
  Honorary Chair Professor NTHU (2010–2012), Humboldt Research Award (2011), CUSO Lectureship (2012),
  Fellow RSC UK (2013), C. T. Chang Memorial Lectureship (2014), Richard B. Bernstein Award (2014),
  European Academy of Sciences member (2018), Dudley R. Herschbach Medal (2019).
- **NTU Distinguished Research Professor, Physics Dept.: academy CV says 2010–2018** — fixes the
  `2110-present` typo in both TAH artifacts to a 2010 start (end date from the academy CV only; IAMS/NSYU
  pages still say `2010~present`, an as-of difference, both kept).
- Current status: **Fellow Emeritus / Director Emeritus, IAMS** (academy CV + IAMS emeriti page).
- A 2000 quote about taking a pay cut to return to Academia Sinica is attributed to him in
  Cyranoski, "Beating the odds", **Nature 407:422–423 (2000)** (via en.wp references; not read on this box).
- IAMS 官網 faculty page additionally lists a **Scholarly Chair, Lin Jun-San Foundation, 1993–1996** where the
  TAH form and the NSTC profile both render **Lin Rong-shan Foundation** — two spellings of the chaired-chair
  foundation, preserved, not silently corrected.
