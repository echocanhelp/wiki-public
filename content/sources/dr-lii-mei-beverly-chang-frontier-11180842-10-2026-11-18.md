## Dated facts (from her own writings held in the vault corpus)
- 1978 — registered the 福爾摩斯基督徒團契 (Formosan Christian Fellowship, later Taiwanese
  Presbyterian Church of Greater St. Louis) with the State of Missouri — her own church
  history, [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys305|TAH #305]] (作者 張理美, 07/2017),
  `https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/ourjourneys305/`
- 1982-05 — FCF incorporated as a nonprofit religious corporation (federal + MO state) — same source.
- 1984-11 — as 幹事 she was the delegate sent to Taiwan to interview and invite Rev. 賴俊明
  (David Chun-Ming Lai, 台北雙連教會) as organizing pastor; he arrived in St. Louis 1984-11 — same source.
- 1989-05-07 — installed among the first six elders (長老) at the first pastor installation:
  高銘憲, 林逸民, 張理美, 陳克充, 鄭紀昭, 蘇希三 — same source (matches the elder row already
  on [[people/yi-min-lin|林逸民]]).
- 1991 — as 青少年事工長老 she led youth ministry after Rev. Lai's departure; later chaired the
  second associate-pastor searching committee that called Rev. Wesley M. Cummins (installed
  2005-08-14), and served as 英語事工長老 — same source.
- Her church-building design input is recorded in #305: she proposed the fan-shaped sanctuary
  + rectangular fellowship hall layout whose aerial view reads the character 台.
- 2014, 2015 — President, 聖路易台灣同鄉會 (TAA/STL), independently confirmed by the
  chapter-presidents roster `https://taiwan.ucsd.edu/gallery/TAA-50_Chapter-E_part7.pdf`
  (matches TAH profile and her own 01/2018 TAA-STL history).
- 2017-11 — member of the NATMA 2017-11-05~12 回台醫學交流訪問團 (delegation roster lists
  蔡承昌醫師 and 張理美醫師), and listed as Medical School Coordinator Committee member —
  per NATMA 總會年刊 2017 `https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/1149_北美洲台灣人醫師協會總會年刊-2017.pdf`
- 2018-08 — elected **Outstanding Taiwanese American by T.A. Archives (O.T.A. #31, category
  Community Services)** — per TAH author page Accomplishment row and the O.T.A. book
  `http://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/O.TA-Book-2019_1115.pdf`
