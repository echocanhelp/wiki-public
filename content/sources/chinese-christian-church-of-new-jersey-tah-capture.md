- **Verbatim `content_html` (single paragraph, self-reported):** CCCNJ is a multi-congregation
  Christian church in New Jersey with both Mandarin-speaking and Taiwanese (Hokkien)-speaking
  congregations; the 台語堂 grew out of small groups and fellowships begun 1989–1990, became a
  fellowship in 1991 and an official congregation in 1998; noted as a pioneering model in North
  America for integrating Mandarin and Taiwanese congregations under one roof; activities include
  Taiwanese-language Bible study, Sunday school, evangelistic outreach, and instruction in
  Pe̍h-ōe-jī (romanized Taiwanese script).
