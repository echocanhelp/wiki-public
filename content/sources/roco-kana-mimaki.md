# ROCO — Kana Mimaki artist bio

機構自述（ROCO 官網藝術家頁），擷取 2026-11-06。逐字要點：

> Acclaimed pianist Kana Mimaki is a versatile artist, recognized internationally for her distinguished career as a soloist, collaborator, and educator. A first-prize laureate of the Los Angeles Liszt Competition, International Russian Music competition, the Catania International Rachmaninoff Piano Concerto Competition, and the Bradshaw and Buono International Piano Competition, she has performed on prestigious stages and with orchestras worldwide. Her concert career has taken her from Carnegie Hall and the Kennedy Center in the United States to major venues across Europe and Asia, including London, Paris, Tokyo, Sicily, and Budapest.
>
> Dr. Mimaki's global training began at Tokyo National University of Fine Arts and Music and continued in the United States, where she earned a Master's from the San Francisco Conservatory and a Doctor of Musical Arts from Rice University. She has enjoyed mentorships with internationally renowned pianists including André Watts, Gilbert Kalish, Menahem Pressler, Anton Kuerti, and Alain Lefèvre.
>
> Deeply committed to the future of classical music, she serves as Concert Director for the Houston Young Artists' Concert since 2007 and joined the Houston Ballet as a pianist in 2023.
