the profile was submitted in the roster batch of the 28th TACEC (美東台灣人夏令會), 07/1997.

or staffed that 1997 session. Independent TACEC speaking/hosting records for 1987 and 2002 are
separate artifacts (`posts/tacec-1987.md`, `posts/tacec-2002.md`, both indexed 2019-06-11) and are
