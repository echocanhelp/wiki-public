## Record 1 — Densho ordination program (primary, dated)

- **Object:** ddr-densho-446-382 「Service of Ai Chih Tsai Ordination Program」
  (https://ddr.densho.org/ddr-densho-446-382/)
- **Collection:** Ai Chih and Ryo (Morikawa) Tsai Family Collection, Densho Digital Repository
  (ddr-densho-446, inclusive 1900–2010, bulk 1938–1961; 457 objects)
- **Dated:** November 8, 1942 — Chicago, Illinois
- **Densho description:** ordained as a Congregational minister and recognized as pastor of
  The Church of Christ (Japanese), at the Fourth Presbyterian Church
- **Named participants (Densho index):** 蔡愛智, Japanese Church of Christ, Fourth Presbyterian
  Church, Uratani Michizo, David Barry, Robert Cashman, George M. Gibson, Harrison R. Anderson,
  Cyrus A. Osborne, T.F. Rutledge Beale, Fred Neal, Albert W. Palmer
- **Why it matters:** documentary primary source for the children's-memoir "ordained November
  1942 at Chicago Church of Christ (Japanese)" row — pins the exact day (1942-11-08) and venue
  (Fourth Presbyterian Church). Family-held primary; single-document, not multi-source.

## Record 2 — PCUSA Historical Society blog (third-party, institutional)

- **Page:** 「Painting the Picture: Rev. Seido Ogawa」, McKenna Britton, PCUSA Historical
  Society blog, 2025-05-21 (https://pcusa.org/news-storytelling/blogs/historical-society-blog/painting-picture-rev-seido-ogawa)
- **Local snapshot:** `pcusa-ogawa-blog.html` (217 KB, fetched 2026-11-19)
- **Rows it corroborates independently of the family/TAH cluster:**
  - Lifespan "(1913-1998)"; "Born in Taiwan in November 1913" (matches zh-wp 1913-11)
  - Traveled to Chicago 1938 to continue education (matches TAH card 1938/Chicago/IL)
  - "closing out 1942 as an ordained minister and married man" (matches ordination 1942-11 and
    marriage to Ryo Morikawa December 1942)
  - "research analyst, instructor, and translator for the U.S. Navy from 1944 to 1947"
    (matches zh-wp 1944/1945 roles)
  - **Retirement-year HOLD:** "in 1948, Ai Chih Tsai took up the pastorate of the Seattle
    Japanese Congregational Church, where he served until his retirement in 1978" — a third
    independent source for 1978 (with TAH card + zh-wp) against 1979 (Seattle Times obit,
    children's memoir "31 years", 陳顯貴 memorial)
- **New material (sourced, single-source = these cited letters):** 1947 U.S. immigration quota
  episode — Ryo Tsai's June 24, 1947 letter to Rev. Seido Ogawa reports an immigration officer
  saying a quota-channel visa (150/year) "might take 10 years"; Ogawa's search for a ministerial
  position shortened it to one year, with a Pasadena Japanese Union Church option floated in his
  October 6, 1947 letter; 1948 Seattle pastorate followed.

## Provenance note

Both records ultimately draw on the same family papers (Densho Tsai Family Collection); the
PCUSA blog is an independent institutional reading of those letters, not an independent event
record. The retirement-year agreement with the TAH card and zh-wp is a genuine source-cluster
alignment (PCUSA narrative is written against the letters, and its 1978 matches the card/wp).
