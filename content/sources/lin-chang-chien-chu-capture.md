## Memoir — posts/my-stories-716.md (third-party, bylined daughter)
- 「前言」: 家母林張簡菊生於一九二一年的台灣，於二〇一九年三月在美國紐澤西辭世 — death dated
  2019-03, New Jersey, US. English version published first for Western friends; Chinese version
  Google-translated then author-revised.
- She worked unpaid as pharmacist for her husband's clinic while raising four children; after the
  children became independent the family moved to Japan — father took a physician position in
  rural Ibaraki, later Okayama, where a hospital pharmacist post was offered to the mother.
- Japanese government pharmacist license issued 1984; photo preserved by daughter 淑華 (Suhua) in
  the family storage room in Fangshan. She graduated Showa Women's Pharmacy College 1943 and
  "finally worked and got paid as a pharmacist at the age of 63".
- Botany class notebook from the 1940s (pharmacy studies in Japan) preserved in the same storage
  room, with teacher's approval seals and annotations (e.g. a plant used as diuretic).
- Retired from the Japan hospital 1986; parents then split time between Taiwan and the U.S.
  Father died 1994; mother's Shea Stadium (NY) MLB habit drew a radio-station coverage request
  via her English teacher, which she declined.
- Maternal origin: 張簡 family of Daliao (大寮), now Kaohsiung; maternal grandfather (張簡 side)
  was village chief (村長) of Daliao during Japanese rule; family thrived in farming and sugar.
  The 張簡 surname became well-known in southern Taiwan from the many physicians the family
  produced since the 1940s; grandfather let daughters study as far as ability/will took them.
- 2012-01: daughter 林淑麗 saw a Daliao 張簡 family-history exhibition at the Kaohsiung Historic
  Museum shortly after the wedding of her sister's second son; mother attended a granddaughter's
  wedding in 2018 (photo caption).
- Byline/provenance: `Source from Shwu Lih Lin`, `Posted in 01/2020`.
