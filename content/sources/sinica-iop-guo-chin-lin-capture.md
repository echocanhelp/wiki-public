## Ambiguity — the 經歷 line
`2001. Assistant Professor, 2006-14; Caltech, 2014` is not parseable as clean prose. Two readings
are open and **neither is adopted as fact**:
1. Assistant Professor **2001**–2006 (i.e. at some institution), then **Caltech 2006–2014** — which
   would mean the Caltech years in the TAH self-report (`2006-2014`) disagree with the institution.
2. Assistant Professor **2006–14** at Caltech, with `2001` and `2014` stray year fields from a
   truncated export — which would **agree** with the TAH self-report on Caltech.
The `;` placement is closer to reading 1, but the string is too damaged to decide. Recorded as an
open ambiguity on the person page rather than resolved by inference. Note also that the institutional
record shows **no Caltech post before 2001 and none after 2014**, and its `經歷` carries no entry at
all for the 2001–2006 window under reading 2.
