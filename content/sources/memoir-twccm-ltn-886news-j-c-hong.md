# 洪濬正 first-person memoir + 瓊斯盃 brother coverage + 2023 金門 camp

本頁彙整四項關於洪濬正（[[people/j-c-hong]]）的來源：他在《中信月刊》【真實故事】欄目的第一人称口述回憶（同稿三連載：GOOD TV《真情部落格》→ 雜誌 → 網頁），自由時報 2015 年對洪濬正、洪濬哲兄弟瓊斯盃賽事的報導，台灣新聞雲 2023 年金門神射手雙語籃球營報導，以及 NTU 機構典藏保存的 1998 年大成報瓊斯盃報導書目。內容涵蓋其球員年表（20 歲入選瓊斯盃、亞青盃 48 分）、留美與惠德學院／Whittier 線索、婚姻家庭，以及 44 分與 42 分、三屆與五屆等尚未調處的記載分歧。

## 1. 中信月刊 【真實故事】應聲入網 — 文/ 洪濬正 口述

- URL: <https://twccm.eoffering.org.tw/publications/twccm_ct?id=274>
- Publisher: 財團法人基督教台灣中國信徒佈道會 (TWCCM) 《中信月刊》, 【真實故事】column.
- Byline form: `文/ 洪濬正 口述` — **dictated first person**, credited to him; the body ends
  `（整理/李菽霓/選自GOOD TV真情部落格）`, i.e. edited by 李菽霓 from the GOOD TV
  《真情部落格》 programme. So this is **one story in three serializations** (TV interview →
  magazine dictation → this web page). Treat the set as **one self-report, not corroboration**.
- Dated? **No issue date on the page.** Only the URL query id `274`. Treated as undated
  self-report; the internal content must post-date 洪其安's graduation/decision to quit work.
- First-person facts recovered here that are **absent from every other artifact on this box**:
  - `我成為瓊斯盃國手時才二十歲` — he was **20** when first a 瓊斯盃 national-team player.
    With birth `1957-05-16` ([[sources/zhwp-j-c-hong|zh.wp]]) that pins the first 瓊斯盃 squad
    to **c. 1977**, the only age anchor anywhere for the playing chronology.
  - 兄弟差六歲 (`雖然兩人差六歲`), and 主播常認錯、球迷也搞不清楚 — the twins-confusion
    detail, plus his own statement that both appearance and 球風 were near-identical.
  - Basketball came from **both** his brother and his father (`還有父親也喜歡打球。小時候他
    常帶著我和哥哥，在籃球場上消磨時間`) — first-person corroboration of the father's role,
    though the father is **still unnamed** here.
  - 高一: brother already in the 軍方飛駝隊, he was 校隊 and benched for lack of height,
    `曾經一度想放棄`; 高二 he joined **歸主籃球隊** — the only source that puts 歸主隊 in
    高二 and ties it to staying in the sport.
  - 周神助牧師 privately read the Bible with him (this account names **only 周神助**; the
    論壇報 pair names 周聯華 and 周神助 as 培靈講員).
  - 亞青盃 in the **Philippines**, 高三: 水土不服「幾乎癱瘓」, prayed, recovered next day,
    scored **48 分** (`破了當時的紀錄`, 轟動台菲兩地), no 三分球 era, 上半場 over 20, an
    **arena power cut of over an hour** mid-game; baptized immediately on return to Taiwan.
    Matches the 論壇報 account → two serializations of the **same** self-report.
  - `打完瓊斯盃第二年，我得到獎學金出國念書，但生活費要自理` — the scholarship came the
    **year after** the 瓊斯盃 breakout, and he was **半工半讀** with a strict coach. The 論壇報
    version omits the self-funded part.
  - First-person account of **種族歧視** limiting his playing time in the U.S. (`因種族歧視，
    根本沒有表現和發揮空間`).
  - `在美國時認識妻子康汝華，結婚、生下老大後，我剛好畢業` — he **graduated from Whittier
    around the birth of his first child**, and many teams then recruited him back to Taiwan;
    this is the first anchor tying the Whittier graduation to a life event.
  - 太太高中時就移民到美國; later 遠距婚姻 period (大女兒抱爸爸照片睡覺, 小女兒看到飛機就哭),
    he held **at least four jobs** while playing, and had a **highway car crash** from
    falling asleep — total loss of the car, the hymn player the only thing still working. He
    then quit the Taiwan jobs and returned to the U.S. to be with family.
  - **洪其安** (小女兒): 畢業於名校, good job, **chose to quit to seek a serving path** —
    first-person confirmation of who 洪其安 is in the family, independent of the
    [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/15-e7-be-85-e5-be-b9-e6-96-af-e7-89-b9-e8-87-ba-e7-81-a3-e5-90-8c-e9-84-95-e6-9c|Rochester choir]]
    name coincidence (which stays HOLD on its own merits).
- **Label discipline:** every row above is `alleged`-grade **self-report** (one person, three
  serializations). Not `sourced` in the sense of third-party confirmation.

## 2. 自由時報 2015-08-29 — 瓊斯盃》台灣歷代球星（上，系列六）

- URL: <https://sports.ltn.com.tw/news/breakingnews/1427579>
- Byline: 記者林宗偉／綜合報導, `2015-08-29 07:31`. **Press of record, third party.**
- Recovered:
  - 洪濬哲 and [[people/j-c-hong|洪濬正]] were **主力 of 飛駝籃球隊** and **同是第一屆、第二屆瓊斯盃明星球員**.
    First third-party dating of two of his All-Star seasons (第1屆 1977, 第2屆 1978) — the
    GAPS line on 2026-11-12 asked for exactly this: "any 瓊斯盃 / 亞青盃 roster".
  - **1978 第二屆瓊斯盃**: the 惠德學院 head coach, representing the USA side, publicly said
    「絕對不會讓洪氏兄弟得超過30分」; final scores **洪濬正 44 分, 洪濬哲 33 分**, and the
    惠德學院 coach **led his players to line up and salute** the brothers afterwards.
    This is the **third-party** version of the 44-point game; the on-box
    [[sources/ct-org-tw-hung-jun-cheng-2023|論壇報]] piece has the coach's name (傑柯巴) and a
    **42-point** figure in a different framing.
  - 哥哥洪濬哲 is described as `約6歲` older — matches the memoir's `差六歲`.
  - `現為SBL台銀主帥` — 2015 contemporary confirmation of the 台銀 tenure (see also
    [[sources/ettoday-tsna-news586-j-c-hong-sbl|the SBL coaching coverage]]).
