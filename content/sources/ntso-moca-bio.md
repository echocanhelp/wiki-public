# NTSO / MOCA artist bio — 陳毓襄 Gwhyneth CHEN

國立臺灣交響樂團 artist profile (MOCA event system). Full Chinese career bio: 1983/1986 MTNA wins, 1986 Bullocks, 1988 Young Keyboard Artists, 1990 Tchaikovsky only-female finalist + 7 Moscow Conservatory recital seasons, state occasions (Lee Teng-hui 1997, Wu Shu-chen 2002, Ma Ying-jeou 2008, Beijing 2008 Olympic concert, 2018 Disney Hall with NTSO), Calgary honorary citizenship, 稻香樂齡音樂學堂（2023，北投稻香日照中心，教失智長者彈鋼琴）。Additional facts in live text: 高中時期跨組大學組國際鋼琴錄音比賽贏首獎（五冠王之一）；2018 於 Dorothy Chandler Pavilion 擔任洛杉磯藝術高中畢業典禮致詞嘉賓；2025 擔任高雄美國學校畢業典禮致詞嘉賓；2021 獲兩廳院表演藝術雜誌「年度人物」；2022/23 起臺灣國中藝術教科書選錄其成就；2024 奇美音樂節四場音樂會演奏 72 首曲子。Cited by [[people/gwhyneth-chen|Gwhyneth Chen]].
