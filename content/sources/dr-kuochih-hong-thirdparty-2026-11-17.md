  specification reports a sealed AA-size nickel-hydride cell with **<12% capacity drop after
  350 cycles** using a Ti-Cr-V-Ni-Li active material — the technical evidence for the
  self-report's bare 「world 1st Commercial Nickel Metal Hydride Battery Materials」 claim:
  https://patents.google.com/patent/US5501917A/en (298,476 B).
