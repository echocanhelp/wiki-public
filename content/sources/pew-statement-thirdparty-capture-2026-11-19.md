## 1. Publisher page (official site, primary)

## 2. Counterpart open letter from the same campaign (official site)
- URL: https://www.taiwaneseamerican.org/2021/09/pew-research-reissues-report-on-asian-americans-after-misrepresenting-taiwanese-americans/
- Posted 2021-09-08 08:29, "Written by Leona". Second open letter to Pew and Neil G. Ruiz
  responding to the correction.
- Dates the target report: 「Key facts about Asian origin groups in the U.S」 published 2021-04-29.
- States the evidence the campaign put to Pew: the 2019 ACS already published disaggregated
  Taiwanese counts in its online 1-year and 5-year tables, while the microdata Pew received did not.
- Quotes Pew's 2021-09-08 footnote: data for 2000, 2010 and 2019 for 「Taiwanese」,
  「Chinese, except Taiwanese」 and 「Okinawan」 was added to the first table.
- Records the campaign's two standing principles (self-reported identities fully counted; write-in
  identities as legitimate as pre-existing checkboxes) and its 2030-census recommendation
  (inclusive race list / drop-down, public education on write-in options, larger answer boxes).
- Adds Chieh-Ting Yeh's second title absent from the May letter: Editor-in-Chief, Ketagalan Media.
- Vault twin already exists: [[works/taiwaneseamerican-org/pew-research-reissues-report-on-asian-americans-after-misrepresenting-taiwanese-]].

## 3. Pew's own companion pieces (counterparty record)
- https://www.pewresearch.org/decoded/2021/09/08/how-many-taiwanese-live-in-the-u-s-its-not-an-easy-question-to-answer/
  — Jeffrey S. Passel, Neil G. Ruiz, Abby Budiman, 2021-09-08. Estimates the U.S. Taiwanese
  population at 195,000 (race alone) / 375,000 (ancestry) / 409,000 (birthplace) / 697,000
  (race+ancestry+birthplace+descendants), 2019 ACS. Explains why: Taiwanese is neither a
  choice nor an example on the ACS race question (ranked 12th among Asian origin groups in 2019),
  and the Census Bureau recognises Taiwanese+Chinese combination only for 2000–2004 microdata.
- https://www.pewresearch.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Taiwan-ACS-data-statement-new.pdf —
  Pew's accompanying ACS-microdata methodology statement (the 「statement」 Pew published in this
  episode). Third-party ZH coverage below calls it a correction, not an apology.
- Later counterparty context (no conflict with the above):
  https://www.pewresearch.org/race-and-ethnicity/2023/07/19/taiwanese-americans-views-of-taiwan-and-other-places/
  (2023-07-19) — Pew separated the Chinese and Taiwanese samples in the 2022-23 survey; 126 raw
  Taiwanese interviews → effective n = 63, MoE ±12.4 pp, subgroup reporting suppressed for size.
