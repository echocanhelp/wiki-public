## Third-party attestation (posts/mystories732.md, non-self-report)
- NY-time 4/7 afternoon, 2020: 伍炯豪牧師 (美國福音信義會迦南教會) posted on Facebook that a
 台裔 believer had died of Covid-19; 當地華文媒體 reported it the next day.
- The article names the deceased as the 41-year-old New York lawyer 莊士晟 (Susan Chuang) and
  identifies her as the 姪女 (niece) of 紐約台灣商會總幹事 莊振輝.
- Per the pastor's post she spoke 英語、西班牙語、台語、華語及廣東話 (five languages), her family
  were devout Christians, and many church members entrusted property matters to her.
- She volunteered as a translator for elders at a 華埠 (Manhattan Chinatown) church; per the
  article she was infected in mid-March 2020, died April 6, 2020, survived by her architect
  husband and young children (children's ages given inconsistently across the two reports).
- Per the 顏嘉瑩 report excerpted from `https://tcnn.org.tw/archives/68243`: she had gone to
  Columbia University for a 心理輔導 (counseling) master's degree for her children's education,
  was scheduled to graduate in May 2020, and did weekend 老人協談 (elder counseling) at a
  西語裔 (Spanish-speaking) church.
