# TAH Who's Who profile capture — 993. James Jean / 2016/05

This page is the source capture for the TAH Foundation Who's Who record of James Jean, the Taiwanese American illustrator and visual artist (born 1979 in Taipei, raised in New Jersey) archived from taiwaneseamericanhistory.org. It preserves three artifacts of his self-reported profile — the crawled person page, the story-corpus dump, and two bibliographic work records — and inventories what the self-report does and does not establish, so every award and honor it feeds to [[people/james-jean|James Jean]]'s subject page stays labeled as self-reported.
