## Capture 3 — NCKU alumni memorial (third party, NOT part of the self-report)
- **Original:** [https://alumni.ncku.edu.tw/p/404-1004-63303.php?Lang=zh-tw](https://alumni.ncku.edu.tw/p/404-1004-63303.php?Lang=zh-tw)
- **Title:** `懷念咱所敬疼e林料總教授 (陳秋官)` · section: `95成大校友之夜` · **發佈日期: 2010-03-01**
- **Author:** 陳秋官, a former student (classmate of 魏景泉, the professor's first EE graduate student); written as a personal memorial after the professor's death.
- **Facts it carries beyond the self-report** (extracted live 2026-11-14 via web_extract):
  - 成大電機系教授 **自1944年至1985年退休**; 「春風化雨四十年」 — corroborates the self-report employment range from an independent author.
  - Named students it lists: 許文龍 (奇美企業創辦人), 劉炯朗 (前台灣清華大學校長), 鄭崇華 (台達電董事長), 王康隆 (奈米電子研發中心 Director).
  - 「他曾應三民書局之邀，**1971年**出版『電機機械』一書，再版多次頗受好評，多所專科、大學指定為教科書。」 — the publication the self-report leaves `--`.
  - 「自**1990年**林教授退休來美定居後…從他在City of Orange買了Condo之後」 — corroborates the self-report's `1990/Orange/CA` arrival field.
  - Childhood: 澎湖出生, **6歲過繼給叔父後搬到屏東**; 公學校、中學、日制高工 (台南工學院前身).
  - Wartime: 高工畢業後到滿州長春架設電台; 患傷寒, 學長 **林含鈴** 找醫生救回一命; 二戰結束前蘇聯侵入滿州, 一批工程師被送到莫斯科架設電台; 戰後回台灣在改制後的工學院 (成大前身) 教書; 曾以交換學者身分到 **普渡大學** 進修.
  - Teaching: taught 交流電機 (required EE course); the Japanese-era campus 機電設備 only he could maintain (發電機馬達); kept forty years of student grade books (成績簿).
  - 2008-11: 五十週年 Reunion held in Taiwan (author absent; classmates reportedly toured campus, preserved generators/motors on display).
  - Family: five children — 四位就讀台大, 一位就讀師大; grandson's 「HAPPY BIRTHDAY GRANDPA」 card anecdote.
  - Death: 「今年一月十二日林教授因病去逝，享年90歲」 (i.e. **2010-01-12**), 安葬於加州 Corona Del Mar 的 **Pacific View Memorial Park**; MeM.com memorial (Last Name: Lin, First Name: Liao-Tsung).
- **Provenance label:** the memoir is a single third-party account by one author restating some facts (employment range, arrival year) that also appear in the editable self-report; where both agree, treat as two independent renderings of overlapping memory, and the memoir's unique anecdotes (childhood, 滿州/莫斯科, grade books, burial) are `documented` only to the extent of this one account.
