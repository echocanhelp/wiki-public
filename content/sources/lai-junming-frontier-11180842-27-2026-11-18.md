## Identity chain (why this is one person)
The page previously knew only two fragments: the GSTPC (SoCal) visiting
preacher 2022–2023 and the St. Louis founding pastor per 張理美's memoir
[[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys305|Our Journeys 305]]. This
pass closes the biography into one documented life and bridges the hanzi to
the English name printed in the memoir's own EN half:

- 張理美's EN memoir text (same page, already in vault) names 「the Rev.
  **David Chun-Ming Lai** of Shuang-Lien Presbyterian Church, Taipei」 as the
  invited organizing pastor, and 「the Rev. **David Lai** was installed as
  the first pastor ... on May 7, 1989」 — the ZH half names 台北雙連教會
  賴俊明牧師 for the same events. This is the **sole co-print of 賴俊明 ↔
  David Chun-Ming Lai** found (TAH corpus, not an independent source).
- 柳嘉信〈台灣基督教聖經事工與教會拓植：賴俊明牧師事蹟研究〉 (第四屆北台文史
  與資產保存學術研討會) independently gives 賴俊明: b. 1940-07 花蓮, 台神
  1965, 鳳林教會, Lexington Theological Seminary M.A. church history +
  Luther Seminary, 玉山神學院, then 雙連教會 associate pastor with 陳溪圳
  1970–1984 (14 years), emigrated 1984, St. Louis Eden Theological Seminary
  D.Min track, founded the St. Louis Taiwanese church, returned to Taiwan
  1991 as 總幹事 of the Bible Society, retired 2009, Osaka 2010. Every node
  matches the #305 memoir (Shuanglian pastor → Nov 1984 arrival → Eden
  Seminary → 1989-05-07 installation → left after 1991-08-17 dedication for
  the International Bible Society Taiwan post). Two independent accounts of
  the same career ladder ⇒ same person; the GSTPC 2022–2023 visiting-preacher
  row remains a **HOLD** (elderly-age-80s Southern California pulpit is
  plausible for a retired 賴俊明, but no bulletin names birthplace, St.
  Louis, or the Bible Society; bridging evidence still absent).

## Dated facts (new this pass)
- 1940-07 — born 花蓮 (Hualien); Hakka family, father 賴彌梯 (first-generation
  believer, 公賣局工友 in 1940, from 新竹新埔), mother 溫明月 (from
  高雄) — 柳嘉信 paper — `third-party` (interview-based academic paper).
- Family — 4 boys + 1 girl: 賴俊明 (牧師), 賴逸明 (長老, 建築工程師,
  Boston Univ. urban-planning Ph.D. student at the time of the 雙連
  building campaign), 賴天明 (長老, 電機工程師), 賴佑明 (長老,
  會計師), 賴春娥 (馬偕醫院); uncle's son 賴允明牧師; mother 溫明月 is
  daughter of 溫阿林, her sister 溫蘭妹 married 蔡連山, father of
  蔡仁理牧師 — 賴俊明's own 1994-10-24 letter to 賴永祥长老, laijohn.com
  family archive — `documented` (self-authored).
- 1965 — graduated 臺灣神學院 (was about to enter Yale Divinity but for the
  two-year service custom; volunteered for 東部 service instead) — 柳嘉信 —
  `third-party`.
- 1965–c.1967 — evangelist (傳道) at 花蓮鳳林教會 (Mackay-founded; 張七郎
  父子 were members) — 柳嘉信 — `third-party`.
- c.1967–1970 — U.S. study: Lexington Theological Seminary (Kentucky),
  M.A. church history, KFC Scholarship Sander; + ~6 months Luther
  Theological Seminary (Minneapolis–St. Paul), Practical Theology —
  柳嘉信 — `third-party`.
- 1970 — returned to Taiwan; briefly taught 「宗教教育原理」 at
  玉山神學院 (Yushan Theological Seminary, 玉里), invited by 高俊明 —
  柳嘉信 — `third-party`.
- 1970–1984 — 副牧師, 台北雙連教會, serving alongside 主任牧師 陳溪圳
  (14 years together; 賴 was 30, 陳 was 77). Led the church-building
  campaign: first Taipei church to worship in a multi-storey building
  (「空中起樓閣」; ~NT$40M project, ~NT$30M bank loan; loans fully repaid
  1983); launched the 家庭小組 movement from 1980, a foundation for
  small-group ministry in the PCT — 柳嘉信; corroborated by 雙連教會's own
  official history page (slpcctaipei history: 「教會前後聘請許錦銘牧師、
  賴俊明牧師作為副牧師」 for the 1960–1970 period) and by the 1984-01-12
  foundation registration at 台北市中山北路二段111號 listing 法人代表/董事長
  賴俊明 (社團財團法人資料站, cited via search snippet) — `documented`
  (church official page + register row) / `third-party` (paper details).
- 1984 — resigned 雙連, emigrated whole family to U.S.; part-time study
  Reformation history in New Jersey, then Eden Theological Seminary
  (St. Louis) D.Min track — 柳嘉信; Eden row corroborated by #305 —
  `documented` (two independent accounts).
- 1984-11 → 1991-08 — organizing/first pastor, 大聖路易台灣人長老基督
  教會 (TPC of Greater St. Louis): arrived Nov 1984, installed 1989-05-07,
  Bible-study movement, membership 60–70; building at 542 **Ries** Rd,
  Ballwin MO dedicated 1991-08-17; left for the Bible Society post right
  after the dedication — #305 + 柳嘉信 + stltpc.org about page (joined
  Giddings-Lovejoy Presbytery 1988, new Ries Road building 1991) —
  `documented`. NOTE: the ZH half of #305 prints 「542 Res Road」 and
  「波溫市」 for Ballwin; the church's own site, PCT churchdata E30319,
  and PCUSA directory all print 542 Ries Rd, Ballwin — `Ries` is correct,
  `Res` is a memoir typo.
- 1991–2009 — 總幹事 (General Secretary / CEO), 中華民國聖經公會 →
  renamed 台灣聖經公會 in 2003 under his tenure (restored name; formal
  name change 2004 per 聖經公會 2016 newsletter) — 柳嘉信 + #305
  (「應國際聖經公會之邀去當台灣地區聖經公會主任」, left Aug 1991) +

  `documented`. Term length check: ETtoday 2017-05-22 calls it 執行長
