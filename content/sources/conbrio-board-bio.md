# Con Brio Examinations — board bio

Fullest English career bio found (mirrors her own site's About/Profile). Key facts beyond the press-kit boilerplate:

- Teachers: Eduardo Delgado, Robert Turner, Aube Tzerko (LA); Martin Canin, Byron Janis, Yin Cheng-Zong (NY/Juilliard).
- 1990 Tchaikovsky: laureate at 19; 1992 Prokofieff laureate; 1999 International Web Concert Audition grand prize; first orchestral performance at age 11 (Prokofiev Third).
- Orchestras: LA Philharmonic, Hong Kong Phil, Moscow Radio Sym, Greenville Sym, Bergen Phil, Kyushu Sym, Taiwan National O, Shanghai Symphonic, Moscow State Philharmonic, Pasadena Sym, Pacific Symphonic, Aspen Music Festival O, Taipei City Sym, Fort Worth Sym, Orchestre Fresnes, Zagreb Phil, Lake Placid Symphonietta.
- Tours: David Atherton/Hong Kong Phil (US+Canada); Fedoseyev/Moscow Radio Sym (North America+Mexico); Vladimir Ponkin/Russian Philharmonic (Taiwan). First soloist, New Colorado Symphony debut — Tchaikovsky 1st, 15,000 at McNichols Stadium.
- 1994 Munich Herkulessaal recital debut, then Croatia/Spain; same year season-opener in the Great Hall of the Moscow Conservatory with Russian National O under Pletnev (Rachmaninoff 3rd). 1999 NY debut, Alice Tully Hall.
- Festivals: Aspen, Montreal, Pogorelich, Bowdoin, Chopin (Majorca, Poland, Hanover, Vienna), Lake Placid. 2010 complete Chopin Nocturnes (Taiwan Chopin Festival); 2011 Liszt Transcendental Études.
- Halls: Kennedy Center, Davies Hall, LA Music Center, Royal Theater (Vancouver), Orpheum (Victoria), National Concert Hall (Taiwan), Instituto Nacional de Bellas Artes (Mexico City), Moscow Conservatory Great Hall, Tchaikovsky Hall, Debussy Hall (Cannes). Estonia Tubin Festival recital tour.
- 2011 Japan disaster relief benefit with Buddhist Tzu Chi Northern California; Fountain Project, American Cancer Society, City of Ten Thousand Buddhas / DRBA branches.
- Jury: Taiwan Chopin 1999/2005, First Taiwan International Piano Competition 2003, Monopoli International Asian division 2003, Taiwan Young Artists 1998. Faculty Perugia 2010.
- Chimei CD "Chopin Favorites" won Best Performance, Golden Melody Awards 2011. Engaged with Miami Symphony six consecutive seasons. Resides Los Angeles and Taipei. Steinway Artist.
