## Headline finding (tier-2 firewall payoff)

All the web evidence resolves to ONE person: the Taiwan-based ophthalmologist 林逸民 (b. 1943 豐原,
台中縣) — 中國醫藥學院醫學系 1969 → 日本北里大學醫學博士 → St. Louis Washington University 眼科訓練
→ F.A.C.S./美國眼科學院院士 1980 → 羅東五福眼科院長 — and NOT a separate US-based physician. The vault
page's open IDENTITY CHECK gap (St. Louis 1972 founding elder vs 美洲台灣日報 政治評論 columnist) closes:
they are the same man. The card's "later US (2020s commentary)" geography guess is superseded — he
returned to Taiwan in 1990 and is 現任宜蘭羅東五福眼科院長 per multiple independent 現職 rosters.
