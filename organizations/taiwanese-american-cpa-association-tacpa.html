The Taiwanese American CPA Association (TACPA) is a professional organization founded in 1992 in Los Angeles by Charles Chiang, CPA, and a group of Taiwanese American CPAs. It encourages members to share professional tax knowledge and provide cross-state tax services to mutual clients. The TACPA holds at least one annual tax forum and periodically hosts interactive forums in Taiwan with the Taiwan CPA Association and Taiwan Government to offer constructive suggestions on Taiwan’s tax systems. In 2011, Taiwanese Canadian CPAs joined the organization, expanding its membership across North America to address cross-border tax issues between the USA and Canada. The TACPA currently has approximately 60 active members across various states and provinces.

Purpose (as self-stated)

The association publishes two stated purposes: to provide and encourage members to share “the resources, information and leadership that enable them to provide valuable services to benefit the public, employers and clients”, and to “strengthen the public’s understanding and trust in TACPAS members, increase the value of member’s services, and contribute to the success of our members” (quoted from the association’s own directory text, per knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/tacpa.md, archived copy of https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/tacpa/).

Officers (2018 election)

At the biennial election held during the 2018 annual meeting, 陳國昌 (Kuo Chang Chen, Los Angeles) was elected president; two vice presidents were named — 莊雅玲 (Houston) and 洪巍 (Los Angeles); CPE continuing-education planning was assigned to 張裕華 and 盧以樂 (San Diego), and website maintenance to 孫光芬 (Canada) (per knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/act-org-cpaa.md, published 2019-01-30, written by the incoming president).

Annual meeting & CPE program

  • 2018-12-14 → 12-16 — three-day annual meeting in Palm Springs, Southern California, hosted by then-president 張裕華 (San Diego); 40+ members from ten states plus spouses attended, including two Canadian members and ten local Southern California members. Per knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/act-org-cpaa.md.
  • Curriculum: eight hours of CPE on the new tax law, centered on the new 20% Qualified Business Income deduction, structures for foreign capital investing in the US, and foreign-gift and estate-tax planning; Canadian members presented US–Canada cross-border rules; the closing theme was 會計師事務所的營運及管理 (client communication, staff training, tax-software comparison, stress relief). Per …/posts/act-org-cpaa.md.
  • Operating model: the association is unusual in the Taiwanese American community — members are dispersed across North America and coordinate by email and LINE, so each service team carries the leadership load; early presidents still actively participate: 李友禮 (Chicago), 梁志宏 (Los Angeles), 張祿生 (Silicon Valley), 魏十洲 (New York), 謝貴分 (Houston). Per …/posts/act-org-cpaa.md.
