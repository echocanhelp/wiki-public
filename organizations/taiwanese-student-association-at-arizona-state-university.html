The Taiwanese Student Association at Arizona State University (TSA @ ASU) is a student organization based in Tempe, Arizona, that serves Taiwanese students at ASU. It is one of several campus Taiwanese student clubs in North America, alongside the Taiwanese Student Association at UC Santa Cruz and the North America Taiwanese Students’ Association (TSA-NA); on campus it sits in the same Arizona Taiwanese community as the Arizona Taiwanese Student Association, the Taiwanese American Association of Arizona, and the North America Taiwanese Women’s Association – Arizona Chapter. Its official Facebook page identifies it as the official presence of the Taiwanese Student Association at ASU (亞利桑那州立大學台灣同學會), and the organization maintains an active Instagram account under the handle @asu.tsa. A legacy university-hosted website at asu.edu/studentprgms/orgs/taiwan documented early activity including new student orientations and Lunar New Year celebrations, though that site has not been substantially updated in recent years.
