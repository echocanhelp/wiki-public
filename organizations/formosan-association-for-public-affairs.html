Formosan Association for Public Affairs (FAPA) 台灣人公共事務會

Formosan Association for Public Affairs (FAPA) (台灣人公共事務會) is a US-based 501(c)(3) grassroots advocacy organization for Taiwan, founded in 1982 and headquartered at 552 7th St SE, Washington, DC 20003. Its stated mission is educational: providing US policymakers, the media, scholars and the general public with information on issues related to Taiwan, and informing Members of Congress and their staff on issues regarding Taiwan.

Mission & Vision (official text, fapa.org/about-us/, retrieved 2026-11-21)

Vision: “We believe a free, democratic, independent, and sovereign Taiwan is essential to peace and security for the United States, Taiwan, and the Indo-Pacific region.”

FAPA seeks:

  1. To promote international support for the right of the people of Taiwan to establish an independent and democratic country and to join the international community;
  2. To promote relations and cooperation between Taiwan and the United States;
  3. To protect the right of self-determination for the people of Taiwan;
  4. To promote peace and security for Taiwan; and
  5. To advance the rights and interests of Taiwanese communities throughout the world.

As a 501(c)(3), FAPA states it does not fundraise for or endorse candidates; it encourages members to be politically active and reports annual DC advocacy cycles: building Hill partnerships, organizing petition letters, drafting talking points for chapters, and publishing weekly news updates for Hill staffers.

History

  • 1982 — FAPA founded; founding president Trong Chai (蔡同榮). The LA chapter’s TAH memoir dates the founding to 1982-02-14 in Los Angeles (Our Journeys 197); FAPA’s own 45th-anniversary gala is set for 2027-02-14 at Marriott Taipei, consistent with a 02-14 founding date.
  • 1982-05-20 — In FAPA’s first year, the House Asian Subcommittee held a hearing on Taiwan’s martial law; founding president Trong Chai and Hamline University professor Richard Kagan testified; more than 35 members of Congress (incl. Senators Kennedy and Pell, Speaker Tip O’Neill) issued a joint statement calling on President Reagan to press the Taiwan authorities to end martial law — per FAPA’s official history fapa.org history 1982–2012, retrieved 2026-11-21.
  • 1999 — FAPA conceived “Taiwanese American Heritage Week”; President Clinton dedicated the week after Mother’s Day that year — per FAPA history 2012–2022 (published 2022-10-10), retrieved 2026-11-21.
  • 2014 — FAPA supported the Sunflower Movement: brought students Wei Yang and Huang Yu-fen to DC, helped place the “Democracy at 4AM” full-page ad in The Hill (2014-04-03), and hosted a second delegation (incl. Huang Kuo-chang, Lin Fei-fan) in August 2014.
  • 2016 — H.Con.Res.88 / S.Con.Res.38 reaffirming the Taiwan Relations Act and Six Assurances as cornerstones of US–Taiwan relations passed both chambers unanimously (House 2016-05-16, Senate 2016-07-06), introduced by Rep. Steve Chabot and Sen. Marco Rubio at FAPA’s advocacy initiative.
  • 2018 — Taiwan Travel Act became Public Law 115-135 (signed 2018-03-16), the culminating legislation of FAPA’s decade-long campaign (H.R.535 / S.1051 lineage documented in FAPA history 2012–2022).
  • 2022 — FAPA marked its 40th anniversary with published decade histories (both retrieved 2026-11-21).
  • 2026 — Active petition campaigns include the Taiwan Representative Office Act (S.974/H.R.3180), PORCUPINE Act (H.R.7146), Six Assurances to Taiwan Act, and U.S.–Taiwan double-taxation relief (S.199) — per fapa.org homepage, retrieved 2026-11-21.

Programs

  • National Advocacy Conference (NAC) — annual fall advocacy weekend (since the Young Professionals Group era; YPG formed 2003, sunset at the 2016 Board Meeting, NAC retained).
  • Emerging Leaders Workshop / Taiwan Trek — brings emerging leaders from Taiwan to DC (first held under president Bob Yang; 2026/2027 edition “Taiwan Trek”).
  • Annual Fundraising Speech Tour — e.g. 2026 tours with Tony Hu (胡振東) and Roy Liu (劉珞亦).
  • The View From Capitol Hill Series — bi-monthly conversations with members of Congress (launched 2021).
  • FAPA Advocacy Captains Program.
  • 45th Anniversary Gala — 2027-02-14, Marriott Taipei.
