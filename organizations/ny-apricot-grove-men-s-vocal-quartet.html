Name note (杏林 = the physician metaphor, not a place)

The corpus article opens by explaining the name: 三國時代吳國名醫董奉 治病不受酬，癒者植杏樹，積年 得杏樹十餘萬株，蔚然成林，後世以「杏林春滿」「譽滿杏林」稱頌醫家 —— 「從此杏林象徵行醫」. So 杏林 here marks the group as a physicians’ ensemble (matching the roster: all named singers are 醫師), not a neighbourhood. The English gloss 「Apricot Grove」 is therefore ours; it appears nowhere in the sources on this box and must not be treated as the group’s registered English name.

Adjacent-name caution: the TAH Who’s Who record of R. F. Huang 許黃瑞鳳 lists her as conductor of 「紐約杏仁 Quartet」 and, in a second field of the same record, 「紐約杏林 Quartet」. Same conductor, same city, 杏仁/杏林 one-character wobble (plus the English-word “Quartet” form) → almost certainly the same ensemble rendered three ways by an editable directory, but no source on this box states the identity. HOLD — not merged as a canonical alias.

Timeline

  • 1991 — founded on Long Island, NY by 八位愛好古典音樂的醫學院校友；their relatives served as 音樂指導、指揮及伴奏 — sourced (single community record).
  • 1990s (record-relative “過去十年間”, record dated 2014-12-22) — regular performances at 同學會、醫學會、同鄉會、教會、婚禮 and other special occasions; repertoire from 懷舊的古典音樂、 藝術歌曲及各國民謠 — sourced.
  • 1995 — invited to perform at Lincoln Center, New York City at a fund-raising concert hosted by 美華防癌協會 (Chinese-language affiliate programming of the American Cancer Society) — sourced (single-source; the ACS/美華防癌協會 gala tradition is independently documented in ZH press from 2003 on, but no 1995 item naming this quartet was found — see frontier capture, arms).
  • 2001-07-02 — invited back to Taiwan to join the 陳文成 memorial concert at the National Concert Hall (國家音樂廳), Taipei, followed by the 「島國之旅」 concerts in 南投、新營、高雄 — sourced; see the 2001 attribution note under Record.
  • 2008-09-05…07 (approx.) — performed at the NATMA (北美洲台灣人醫師協會) 第24屆年會「台灣之夜」banquet at the Newark Airport Marriott, listed as 「由台美人醫師組成的〈杏林男聲四重唱〉」 in 柯金寅’s convention record (source NATMA 10/2016) — first third-party post-2001 event naming the group; documented (published convention record, independent of the story corpus). See frontier pass 2.
  • undated program print — 《鄭智仁專輯》/《福爾摩莎頌》 concert program prints 「紐約杏林男聲四重唱 指導:黃瑞鳳 鋼琴伴奏:孔碧薰 長笛伴奏:李露西」 with a nine-title block incl. 蕭泰然-arranged《微聲盼望》and 許登龍·許丕龍-lyrics 《思念阿母》《感念阿爹》 — sourced, event frame HOLD (no date/venue on page).
  • by 2014-12-22 — 目前由黃瑞鳳指揮，蘇秋莉伴奏 (as printed) — sourced.

Record

The corpus article’s own dating is internally inconsistent: it writes 「2001年又受邀回台灣參與 “陳文成十週年紀念音樂會”」, but the event its program documents — 《島國旋律：陳文成博士殉難20週年 紀念音樂會》, 2001-07-02, 國家音樂廳 — is the 20th-anniversary concert (陳文成 died 1981), and 陳文成博士紀念基金會 was only renamed in 2000. Field-level HOLD on the “十週年” label; the 2001-07-02 / 國家音樂廳 dating is the documented form and is what this page anchors.
