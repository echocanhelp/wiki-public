taiwanjustice.net (台灣公義網)

taiwanjustice.net (台灣公義網) is a Taiwanese-American news and opinion publication founded and published by Freeman Huang (黃樹人). The site covers Taiwan-related social and political issues, with content spanning international news, Taiwan domestic affairs, U.S. politics, opinion columns, and video series. It was archived via the Wayback Machine (59,422 unique URLs) and ingested into Echopedia as a full-domain source.

History

Freeman Huang, a veteran Taiwanese-American journalist and publisher of New Asian Weekly (1994–2010), launched taiwanjustice.net in 2015. The platform focuses on Taiwan-related social and political issues, serving the Taiwanese-American community with news aggregation, opinion columns, and video content.

The site’s content spans from 2011 to 2026, with the bulk of archived material concentrated in 2024–2025 (15,061 + 3,712 files respectively). The archive was captured via the Wayback Machine and converted to markdown using the Trafilatura extraction pipeline.
