## History / 教會歷史 (A)

Source: `knowledge/web-archives/eastgatebarclay-org/eastgatebarclay.org--e6-95-99-e6-9c-83-e6-ad-b7-e5-8f-b2.md` (ZH) + `eastgatebarclay.org-simple-history.md` (EN).

Seven-stage 120-year history. Key milestones:
- **1903-05** — Barclay (巴克禮) & Elizabeth buy house on 東門街 as seminary dorm; 福音堂, evangelism
- **1906-06** — 太平境教会支會
- **1911** — Iron-pipe chapel, ~300 seats; 1914 臺灣大會 venue
- **1914** — Venue of 臺灣大會
- **1921** — Independent 堂會; **1921** 女執事, **1926** 女長老 — earliest known local female believer participation in Presbyterian decision-making
- **1926-06** — New chapel (80×52 ft, 170 benches, 800+ seats); 信望愛 ceiling inscription by 黃俟命
- **1931** — 蘇格蘭式紅磚 Sunday-school building; WWII bullet holes preserved
- **2003** — Centennial; renamed 巴克禮纪念教会; 18號公園 → 巴克禮纪念公園
- **2017-05** — Elizabeth Memorial Chapel (伊莉莎白纪念教会)
- **2018-05** — 伊莉莎白纪念教会 dedication
- **2022** — 東門巴克禮歷史教育推廣協會
- **2024-10** — Tainan municipal historic monument
