## Official self-description (Index, A)
Three parts: (1) full text of 《教會史話》 Kau-hoe-su-oe; (2) scrapbook files; (3) personal bio, works, family. Emphasis: history of Christianity in Taiwan, especially PCT. 1990-04 識：應《台灣教會公報》盧俊義邀約；自 1871 期（1988-01-10）至 1985 期（1990-03-18）百篇結為初輯。傅斯年「上窮碧落下黃泉」.
