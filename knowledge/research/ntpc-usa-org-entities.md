## Mission / 宗旨 (A)

> 本會宗旨是為要結合全美台灣人長老教會及信徵共同宣揚福音，見證上帝的愛和真理，並促進種族、文化、社會和經濟之和諧與平等。  
> — ntpc-usa.org bylaw + home (2025/26 letter)

**B-tier.** PC(USA) Matthew 25 vision: build congregational vitality, dismantle structural racism, eradicate systemic poverty.

## Membership (A)

Bylaws 第三章第二條第一項:

- Every PC(USA) Taiwanese congregation is a member church — no application, no lapse.
- Each church sends its pastor + one lay representative as 大會代表.
- Additional representatives: in-presbytery Taiwanese ministers, 區TPC chairs, NTPW/NTPYC/NTPYAC chairs, committee members.
- 五分之一 (5/12) of church delegates = quorum for Annual Meeting.
