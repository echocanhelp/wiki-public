                  生技巨人林福坤的故事
                                         ◎文 / 楊文顯

   (Editor's Note: Dr. Fu-Kuen Lin, born in Taiwan in 1941, is the inventor of Epogen (Epoetin
Alfa), a DNA engineering medicine, and thus becomes a big name in biomedical science. After Mr.
Lin received a B.S. degree in plant pathology and an M.S. degree in plant pathology at National
Taiwan University, he earned his doctorate in plant pathology at the University of Illinois/Urbana-
Champaign in 1971. In 1981, Dr. Lin joined the Thousand Oaks-based Amgen, now growing as the
world's number one biotech behemoth, and led a research team conducting a recombinant human
erythropoietin project. Two years later, his team successfully established the gene coding for EPO
recombinant human erythropoietin and was finally approved by the FDA in 1989, with the generic
name Epoetin Alfa, tradenamed Epogen. Since it was introduced in the markets, Epogen has
successfully treated millions of anemic persons with chronic renal failure worldwide. His discovery
and invention marked the beginning of the biotech revolution. Dr. Lin is a world-class scientist of
high achievements, who received many acknowledgments and honors, including, among others,
the "1990 Quality of Life Award," the "1995 Discoverers Award" by Pharmaceutical Research
and Manufacturers of America. In 1994, in particular, Amgen was the recipient of the "National
Medal of Technology" at the White House, the honor of which could be attributed to Dr. Lin's
contributions.)

                                                 結語
                                                 成功的人生有很多版本。有的從出生那一片
                                                刻就與眾不同；家庭背景好，加上自我期許高。
                                                這種人生一路走來受衆人注目期待，所以不
                                                「成功」也很難。有一種成功的版本是出生平
                                                凡，天資聰穎，自勵性強，卻為人謙虛、做事
                                                低調。這種人生一路走來不受注目，直到最後
                                                一鳴驚人。這就是林福坤的成功模式。

                                                 林福坤形容自己不是從小立定志向的人，大
                                                約到了初二才懂得用功讀書，考上台大是運氣
                                                好。後來到美國念書研究，到安進公司攻克紅
                                                血球荷爾蒙的基因都是一種人生的機緣，處處
        2002 年攝於中國雲南                            都是給人學習，創造，增加人生歷練和色彩的
                                                機會。

                                                 林福坤的成功不是為自己做大官、賺大錢，
                                                而是發明藥物，造福人類。一個人的「名望」
                                                是別人贈送的，「品格」則要靠自己營造。

                                                 人生的遭遇好壞不是絕對的。試想，如果林
                                                福坤在那段「十年換五個頭家」的人生旅途上，
                                                遇到任何一家欣賞他，而給他一份能「安居樂
                                                業」的職位，林福坤就不會去翻「科學」雜誌
                                                看到安進公司徵人啓事的廣告了。林福坤的故
                                                事給吾人的啟示是，誠如一首台灣名曲所唱的
                                                「一時失志不免怨嘆，一時落魄不免膽寒。…
                                                人生可比是海上的波浪，有時起有時落。好運、
