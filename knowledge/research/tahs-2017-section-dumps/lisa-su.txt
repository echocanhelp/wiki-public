    (Editor's Note: Dr. Lisa T. Su was born in Taiwan in 1969. She is currently the CEO and
 President of the Sunnyvale, Calif.-based Advanced Micro Devices (AMD), a giant in semiconductor
 industry. She immigrated with her family to the U.S. at the age of 2. Encouraged by her parents,
 she attended the Bronx High School of Science (Bronx Science) and then the Massachusetts Institute
 of Technology (MIT), ending up majoring in electrical engineering. She earned her bachelor's,
 master's, and doctoral degrees at MIT. As a doctoral candidate, the MIT Technology reports, Lisa
 was "one of the first researchers to look into silicon-on-insulator technology, a then unproven
 technique for increasing transistors' efficiency by building them atop layers of an insulating
 material." In 1994, Lisa served at Texas Instruments as a member of the technical staff, followed by
 working at IBM as VP of its Semiconductor Research and Development Center. To acknowledge her
 achievement and contribution, MIT Technology Review named her a "Top Innovator Under 35" in
 2001. The following year, she was selected as one of the "Top 100 Young Innovators" by the same
 Review, and, in 2014, was named "Executive of the Year" by the EE Times. During 2012-2014, Dr.
 Su served as Senior VP and General Manager at AMD. She was promoted to President and CEO in
 2014. As a pioneer in semiconductor industry, Dr. Su sits on the Board of the Global Semiconductor
 Alliance and the U.S. Semiconductor Industry Association. In 2016, Lisa was named one of the "50
 Most Powerful Women in Technology" by the National Diversity Council, and, in the same year, was
 named one of the "Outstanding 50 Asian Americans in Business" by the Asian American Business
 Development Center. In 2017, Dr. Su was named one of the "World's Greatest Leaders" by The
 Fortune Magazine.)
