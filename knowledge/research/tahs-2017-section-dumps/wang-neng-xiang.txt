    (Author's Note: Mr. Neng-Hsiang Wang, born in a Christian family of Formosa (Taiwan)and
 spent three years roaming alone on mountains when he was a nine-year old boy after being denied
 schooling by Japanese colonial master, is a lifelong fighter for Taiwan’s international visibility
 and to end Taiwan’s colonial status. Knowing that Washington, DC will become the center of
 international power play since the end of World War II, for the past 43 years (since 1972) he
 established Washington, DC as his home, where he pursued his professional career—he is a CPA by
 profession and owner of a CPA firm -- and security and justice for Taiwan—he is a pioneer lobbying
 from Washington, DC for his motherland - Taiwan. A man of versatility and action, he earned
 the titles of Head of Foreign Affairs’ Department for World United Formosans for Independence
 (1973-1977), and Secretary General for Overseas Alliance for Democratic Movement in Taiwan
 (1977-1985). He succeeded in holding four hearings—(1) Hearing at Kansas State University
 to seek denial for Chinese Student Association’s representation of Taiwan and to seek acceptance
 for Formosan Student Association as its lawful representation (1965), (2) hearing at US House of
 Representatives to investigate KMT’s human rights violations (1977), (3) hearing at US Senate
 and House of Representatives to offer a legal framework for Taiwan which led to the birth of
 Taiwan Relations Act and a new institution called American Institute in Taiwan (1980), and (4)
 hearing at House of Representatives to offer a license to anti-KMT television station (1994) which
 led to the birth of FTV in Taiwan. Now in his eighties and still in excellent health, in addition to
 busily running a CPA firm, Mr. Wang, from the nation's capital, is untiringly pursuing Taiwan's
 international visibility and aspiring to end her colonial sufferings.)
