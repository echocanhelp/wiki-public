                          Gratitude
      After serving one year as the vice-president of the Taiwanese American
Historical Society, I was elected to as President of the Society in 2014. The
term was supposed be 3 years. Due to the physical condition of the founding
president Charles Yang, he handed the big job to me. Thanks to God, in one
year he fully recovered from two surgeries and helped me with the Society.
      Before I finish the term, Mr. Yang wishes the Society to publish a special
yearbook introducing the communities. In the past 4 years, we emphasized the
website and interviewed many senior members before they lost their memory.
We also held a few lectures or forums and posting videos on You Tube and our
website www.TAhistory.org each year.
      This year we introduce four prominent TA entrepreneurs and more
outstanding second generations. I hope next time we can include many others.
You are welcome to recommend the name list for the Society.
      I am glad Mr. Yang is willing and well enough now to lead the Society
next year at least to complete his term. Many thanks to my many friends of the
Historical Society for their support and encouraging words.
  Special thanks to TECO 僑委會 for the sponsor


  									Franklin Ping Cheng
  									November, 2017
