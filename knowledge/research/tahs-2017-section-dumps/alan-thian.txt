                       ◎台美歷史協會專訪 Edda Huang 文字整理

    (Editor's Note: Mr. Alan Thian was born in Singapore in 1952, where he received a bachelor's
 degree in business administration. In 1976, he migrated to America with his parents. Six years later,
 he graduated from the University of Southern California with an MBA degree. After several years
 of working at, and associated with, a couple of banking institutions in southern California, Mr. Thian
 started his first Royal Business Bank with 71 million in initial capital in Downtown L.A. in 2008.
 To date, Royal Business Bank has 14 branches in California and Nevada, which strategically and
 primarily caters to serve the financial needs of Asian American communities, ethnic Asian minority
 groups, and Asian American small businesses. In 2013, Mr. Thian was selected to serve in the FDIC
 Advisory Committee on Community Banking. A great honor, indeed. Partly because he married
 to a lady originally from Taiwan, Mr. Thian has established strong connections and affiliations with
 the Taiwanese American communities throughout the U.S., developed a certain form of "Taiwan
 identity," and got intensely involved in Taiwanese American community affairs and services.)
