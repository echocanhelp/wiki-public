寶爾博物館 (Bowers Museum) 董事長 施劉秀枝 (Anne Shih)
                     ◎台美歷史協會專訪 / 鄭炳全撰稿 黃樹人攝影

    Originally from Chiayi, Taiwan, Anne Shih immigrated to the U.S. in 1978 with two children.
 She started volunteer service at the Bower Museum in 1992. She joined the museum board in 1996
 and created the Junior Ambassador program introducing high school students to art and culture.
    Anne Shih was credited with initiating many rare exhibitions from Taiwan, Tibet and China. She
 became the Board Chairwoman since 2010 and re-elected until 2018. She was honored with Ellis
 Island Medal of Honor in May 2017 for her dedication and achievement in the U.S.
