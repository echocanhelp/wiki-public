    (Editor's Note: Mr. John Chiang was born to Mr. Mudong Chiang and Ms. Yinhsiang Shen,
 immigrants from Taiwan, in New York City in 1962. Like many other politicians on the East
 Coast, he studied law and earned a J.D. degree from Georgetown University Law Center. His
 future path to California State Controller (2007-2015) and California State Treasurer (2015-present)
 started from being a tax law specialist for the IRS, working as an attorney for then-California
 State Controller Gray Davis (37th Governor of California, 1999-2003), and then serving as a staff
 member of Senator Barbara Boxer. Believing John was a political star on the rise, the Taiwanese
 American Historical Society invited him to a party for outstanding first- and second-generation
 Taiwanese Americans in 2015. In 2016, not surprisingly, John announced his intention to run for
 Governor of California in the 2018 race. That marked the first gubernatorial bid by a Taiwanese
 American in American history.)
