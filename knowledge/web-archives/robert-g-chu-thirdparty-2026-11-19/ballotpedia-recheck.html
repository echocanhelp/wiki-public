Robert Chu is a judge of the Superior Court of Los Angeles County in California. Chu assumed office in 2018.

Chu won re-election for judge of the Superior Court of Los Angeles County in California outright without appearing on the ballot. If the elections had not been canceled, the primary would have been held on June 2, 2026, and the general election would have been held on November 3, 2026.

Education

Chu received a bachelor's degree from the University of California, Los Angeles and a J.D. from the Southwestern Law School.[1]

Career

  • 2018-Present: Judge, Superior Court of Los Angeles County, California
  • 2006-2018: Deputy public defender, Los Angeles County Public Defender's Office
  • 2006: Associate, Masry and Vititoe

Elections

2026

See also: Municipal elections in Los Angeles County, California (2026)

General election

The general election scheduled for November 3, 2026, was canceled.

Nonpartisan primary

The primary scheduled for June 2, 2026, was canceled. Incumbent Robert Chu (Nonpartisan) won election in the primary for Superior Court of Los Angeles County without appearing on the ballot.

2020

See also: Municipal elections in Los Angeles County, California (2020)

Incumbent Robert Chu was the only candidate to file and won the position by default when the election was canceled.
