## Directions

自北方往南, 五號高速公路往南, 在 El Toro 出口, 右轉進 El Toro Road, 向西直走, 過 Moulton 路,再駛過二個紅綠燈, 教會便在右側.

From I-5, take El Toro exit west (towards Laguna Hills). Continue about 3 miles up El Toro Road. Turn right on Calle Sonora and turn immediately left into the church parking lot, or continue on El Toro Road past the church and turn right into the parking lot.
