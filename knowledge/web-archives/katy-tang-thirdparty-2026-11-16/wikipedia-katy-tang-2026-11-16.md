# en.wikipedia — Katy Tang 湯凱蒂 (transcription 2026-11-16)
- Infobox: Member of the San Francisco Board of Supervisors from the 4th district,
  in office **February 27, 2013 – January 8, 2019**; Mayor London Breed; preceded by
  Carmen Chu; succeeded by Gordon Mar.
- President of the San Francisco Board of Supervisors, in office **December 1, 2014 –
  January 8, 2015**; Mayor Ed Lee; preceded by David Chiu; succeeded by London Breed.
- Party: Democratic. Education: University of California, Davis (BA, BA); University of
  San Francisco (JD). Occupation: politician.
- Early life: grew up in the Sunset District, **attended Lowell High School**, graduated
  from UC Davis with a double B.A. in 3 years; graduate of USF School of Law.
- District 4 includes Central Sunset, Outer Sunset, Parkside, Outer Parkside, Pine Lake Park.
- Electoral history: appointed February 2013; elected November 2013 and November 2014.
  On **November 18, 2014** the Board elected Tang Interim President after David Chiu's
  election to the State Assembly; succeeded by London Breed after an election on
  **January 8, 2015**.
- Legislation (attributed by the article): HOME-SF (2017, amended 2018); Lactation in the
  Workplace / Family Friendly Ordinance (2017; led to SB 937, Wiener 2018); Zero Emission
  Vehicles fleet mandate (half of city light-duty fleet zero-emission by 2022); Food
  Service Waste Reduction (plastic straws); ADU Program Reform; Flexible Retail; Arts
  Funding initiative (voters, November 2018); **Fur Sale Ban — authored by Tang, SF first
  major U.S. city to ban fur sales**; Exotic Animal Performance & Sale of Non-Rescue Cats
  & Dogs.
- **June 12, 2018** — announced she would not run for reelection; succeeded by Gordon Mar.
- Article's own references include SF Chronicle (Lagos 2014-11-18; Lagos 2015-01-09;
  2018-06-13 no-reelection item), CBS SF 2018-03-20 (fur ban), AP 2018-03-21.
