# Mayor Breed press release 2021-11-10「MAYOR LONDON BREED APPOINTS KATY TANG AS DIRECTOR OF OFFICE OF SMALL BUSINESS」(transcription)
- Appointed Executive Director of the Office of Small Businesses; **begins January 2022**;
  replaces Regina Dick-Endrizzi (13 years).
- "Tang currently serves as the **Director of Public and Governmental Affairs at the San
  Francisco Department of Public Health (SFDPH)**"; prior: District 4 supervisor 2014–2019.
- Legislative record cited: ground-floor space flexibility, small-business permitting
  acceleration, permit-fee waivers; elements incorporated into Prop H Save our Small
  Businesses Initiative (voters, November 2020).
- Small Business Commission president Sharky Laguana nominated her.
