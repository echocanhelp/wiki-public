# SF Business Times 2021-11-10「Former S.F. Supervisor Katy Tang to take over Office of Small Business」(transcription)
- Tang (supervisor 2013–2019, District 4) becomes executive director of the city's
  Office of Small Business in January (2022) after Regina Dick-Endrizzi retires at end of 2021.
- Career between offices: "land use analyst at a local law firm and management roles at the
  San Francisco International Airport and the Department of Public Health" — i.e. Farella
  Braun + Martel LLP land use analyst; a year as **SFO's managing director of strategy and
  social impact**; **director of public and governmental affairs at DPH**.
- Policy legacy named: flexible/multiuse storefront definition (5 districts), streamlined ADU
  permitting, plastic-straw ban, mandated employer breastfeeding accommodations.
- Note: Dick-Endrizzi noted Tang worked closely with her as a legislative aide for
  then-District 5 Supervisor Ross Mirkarimi (statement; the Tang-side record says Chu office).
