Herbert Defeats Button to Flip House District 112 as Democrats Take the State House

    November 4, 2026

Zach Herbert defeated longtime Republican Rep. Angie Chen Button, while incumbents held other Texas House seats serving Richardson and Garland.

Published November 4, 2026 at 12:43 p.m. • By Ben Vance
Share this story

Democrat Zach Herbert defeated longtime Republican Rep. Angie Chen Button on Tuesday, flipping Texas House District 112 and ending her nearly 18-year hold on a seat representing parts of Richardson and Garland.

Herbert received 43,251 votes, or 53.6%, to Button’s 37,499 votes, or 46.4%, according to unofficial results from the Texas secretary of state. His margin was 5,752 votes, with all polling locations reporting.

Button has served in the House since January 2009 and chairs its Trade, Workforce and Economic Development Committee. Her defeat ends a nine-term tenure which included multiple close victories.

Herbert, a Marine Corps veteran and Richardson attorney, campaigned on public school funding, teacher pay and affordability. He criticized Button’s support for private school vouchers, while Button defended the program as giving families more educational options.

It was a part of a state-wide tsunami of Democratic support in a state which has been a Republican stronghold for decades. Democrats swept all state-wide races and are projected to take back the state house, with Herbert contributing to the new majority.

Elsewhere in House District 102, Democratic Rep. Ana-Maria Rodriguez Ramos won reelection over Republican Bonnie Abadie, receiving 31,468 votes, or 68.9%, to Abadie’s 14,228 votes, or 31.1%.

Democratic Rep. Rhetta Andrews Bowers also retained House District 113, defeating Republican Stephen W. Stanley with 37,611 votes, or 67.3%, to Stanley’s 18,254 votes, or 32.7%. Democratic Rep. Linda Garcia was unopposed in House District 107.

In two districts that include portions of Richardson in Collin County, Democratic Rep. Mihaela Plesa held House District 70 with 58.3% against Republican George Flint. Republican Rep. Katrina Pierson retained House District 33 with 55.4% against Democrat Orlando Lopez.

The results remain unofficial pending canvassing and certification.
