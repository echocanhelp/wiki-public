# About the Center

As one of the largest overseas Taiwanese cultural institutions, we use education, art, and community services to let Taiwanese culture flourish on American soil, building a solid foundation of cultural identity for the next generation of Taiwanese Americans.

Our History

## Establishment History

The Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles is a non-profit organization. In February 1998, the family of Ken John Wang donated the property at the corner of Garvey Avenue and Walnut Grove Avenue in the city of Rosemead to the foundation to to become the future home of the Taiwan Center. Later that year, responding to Mr. Li-Pei Wu's ardent call for action, Southern California Taiwanese Americans held a million dollar fund-raising event to raise money for the establishment of the Taiwan Center. Mr. Li-Pei Wu was then elected to be the first chairperson of the Board of the Foundation.

Our Organization

## Leadership Structure

The Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles is managed by its Board of Directors. There are 36 board members, including one Chairperson, who also serves as the Chief Executive Officer of the Taiwan Center. The Board consists of four Vice Presidents, a Secretary, two Assistant Secretaries, a Treasurer, an Assistant Treasurer, a Controller, and an Assistant Controller. To better plan and implement the projects of the Foundation, there are ten committees and fourteen working groups under the Board of Directors. The Foundation currently has over eight hundred members and fifty affiliated organizations in its membership. The Taiwan Center of Greater Los Angeles is considered by the Taiwanese Americans to be the leader of the Taiwanese American community in Greater Los Angeles.

Organization Structure

## Committees & Teams

1

###### New Building Committee

Leads the planning and construction of the new Taiwan Center, keeping the project on track and on budget.

2

###### Building & Design Team

Handles architectural design, structural planning, and construction oversight to ensure the new building is both functional and culturally meaningful.

3

###### Building Fundraising Team

Drives fundraising efforts for the new building, rallying community support to fund this historic project.

4

###### Events Committee

Plans and runs the Center's year-round cultural events, talks, performances, and community gatherings.

5

###### Membership Committee

Manages membership applications, renewals, and benefits, ensuring members feel valued and connected.

6

###### Next Generation Committee

Engages young Taiwanese Americans through youth programs, building future leaders and bridging generations.

7

###### Office Management Committee

Oversees day-to-day operations, staffing, and internal resources to keep the Center running smoothly.

8

###### Mainstream Outreach Committee

Builds bridges with mainstream American society, raising the profile and influence of Taiwanese Americans through cross-cultural partnerships.

9

###### Archives & History Committee

Collects and preserves historical documents, photos, and records, safeguarding our community's immigration story for future generations.

10

###### Business Services Committee

Supports Taiwanese American businesses with networking opportunities, resources, and a platform for professional collaboration.

11

###### Bylaws Committee

Reviews and updates the Center's bylaws to ensure legal compliance and transparent governance.

12

###### Social Media Committee

Manages the Center's social media channels, promoting events and sharing community stories to grow our digital presence.

13

###### Taiwan School

Offers classes in Mandarin, Japanese, computer skills, cooking, choir, photography, and more—providing lifelong learning for the community.

14

###### Interior Design Committee

Designs the new building's interior spaces, blending modern style with Taiwanese cultural touches for a warm, functional environment.

15

###### Board Nomination & Election Committee

Manages board candidate nominations, vetting, and elections to ensure a fair and transparent process.

16

###### Annual Gala Committee

Organizes the Center's annual gala and fundraising dinner—our biggest community event of the year.

17

###### Miss Taiwanese American Committee

Hosts the annual Miss Taiwanese American pageant, empowering young women with cultural pride and leadership skills.

18

###### Heritage Week Committee

Plans the annual Taiwanese American Heritage Week, showcasing Taiwanese culture through performances, exhibitions, and talks that bring communities together.

19

###### 228 Memorial Committee

Organizes annual February 28th memorial events—honoring history and promoting peace, human rights, and democratic values through ceremonies and educational programs.

20

###### Major Events Committee

Coordinates the Center's major celebrations and special events, bringing committees together to deliver memorable, large-scale programs.
