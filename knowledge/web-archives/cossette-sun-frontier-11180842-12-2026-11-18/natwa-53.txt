會長的祝福
親愛的姐妹們:
又是 NATWA 一個新年度的開始，我在此感謝大家給我這個機會，
為大家服務。NATWA 是一個融洽的大家庭，姐妹們互相幫忙，一起學習和
成長，分享人生的酸甜苦辣，也享有永恆美麗的友誼。世間有很多不幸的人需要幫忙，我們若能付出真誠的關
懷，給他們帶來一點快樂和溫暖，不是很有意義嗎? 看到他們浮出希望的眼光或一絲微笑，會給我們帶來一種
無以形容的成就感與快樂。對退休的人來說，關心別人，付出愛心，是獲得快樂，保持年輕活力的最佳活動。
因此今年的主題是: Compassion; Feeling the pain of others and willingness to help。關心弱勢，將心
比心，愛需即時行動。希望 NATWA 姐妹們及各分會能考慮加入回饋社會，社區服務之行列，給需要幫忙的人
一點協助。我知道有些姐妹，也有分會已經做得相當有成就，我很敬佩她們，也值得我們效法。
今年年中理事會 10/15 – 17/2015，將在美麗的 Salt Lake City，Utah 舉行。年中會是姐妹們互相學習
的一個訓練營，也有機會增加對婦女會運作的瞭解。今年在年前理事會曾討論過 NATWA“何去何從”。潘賢
敬前會長將計劃在今年的年中會撥出一段時間討論這重要的議題。希望所有的理事們都能提供妳們的意見和看
法。使 NATWA 更加茁壯。敬請各位姊妹來參加和遊覧風景綺麗的神祕之州– UTAH。
明年 2016 年 1 月 16 日，台灣總統和立法委員大選。這是台美人的大代誌。很多姐妹們會回台投票，到
時在台灣跟大家見面。
明年的年會 4/21 – 24/2016 在 San Jose 舉行。我們會精心籌劃節目及會後旅遊。我真誠祝福大家一切
順利，健康快樂!!

PS: NATWA II celebrated their 10th anniversary at the Convention in San Diego。It is a milestone。2 new co-chairs
are from Northern California this year，instead of Southern California that is also new。Let’s all make concerted effort
to help them and work with them。If you see a NATWA II member who has special talent or interest，please get them
involved in our respective committees。
Thank you again，looking forward to a Joyous and challenging year。
Yamei from Northern California

歐春美會長的歡迎詞
歡迎來到聖地牙哥！今年的年會有幾位姊妹因種種因素臨時取消行
程，原本邀請的講員吳念真導演也意外受傷，被醫生囑咐不能出國，吳導再三
致歉。我們在座的姐妹們，能夠歡聚一堂，表示我們身體健康，家人也都平安，
我們要感恩惜福。感謝今天大家能在此相聚，讓我們一起牽手為 NATWA 再寫
一段喜悅的歷史。
今年我們邀請到葉菊蘭女士來與我們分享她的人生故事；也很高興
請到了太平洋時報林文政社長來和我們分享一些台美人的“武林外史”，分析
台美人對台灣本土及美國社會的影響與貢獻。
為了配合主題，今年的年會有幾項特別展覽，史料組展覽完整的聯誼
通訊、年會手冊、及 NATWA 的所有出版品。另外，各分會也發揮了他們的團
隊精神，將分會的歷史呈現出來。我們有十三個分會參與這項歷史展覽。希望今後，分會都能夠繼續 update 她
們的年鑑，將北美各地台灣婦女的故事傳承下去。
雖然我的年紀也不小了，這一年來還是成長了很多。姐妹們，如果有人對你說：“you can't teach an old
dog a new trick.” 你可以跟他說：“你錯了！無論年紀多少，我們都能接受挑戰，隨時都能學習與成長。”聖地
牙哥團隊希望各位姐妹高高興興地來，歡歡喜喜地玩，平平安安地回家。

長者生命回顧團體」在台灣

文/郭慈安
郭慈安

2008 年，我結束在美國 25 年的生活，回台灣任
教。這六年一路走來，始終有個夢想，希望為台灣的長
輩留下更多的歷史，分享他們的生命風華，讓未來的子
孫可以學習他們的精神。於是，我開始推動“老人生命
回顧”，研發教材，帶領社區的長輩進行 10 週的生命
回顧課程。起初，我將我的老師 James Birren 教授研發
的“引導式生命回顧”翻譯成中文，以為如法泡製就可
以在台進行。但是，後來發現事情並不是這麼簡單，因
為文化與社會結構(如教育和經濟)的差異，以及不同
的老化生理與社會心理，台灣長輩需要另類的生命回
顧引導工具。
因此，我與另外一位老師合作，共同研究如何以
“生命回顧”為理論基礎，讓台灣多元的長者與社區經
由我們帶領，實習屬於長輩自己的生命故事書。很幸運
的，第一年邀約了 7 個團體(與弘道基金會合作)，分別
在台北、台中、和高雄展開。這七個團體接受了三天的
帶領者訓練，也實際練習與感受“生命回顧”帶給長輩
的期待與想像。第一年下來，我看到長者從生活無望到
興致勃勃的表情。於是，我將過去四年來在台灣演講的
車馬費集結起來，研發生命回顧帶領的課程。就這樣，
“生命回顧團體”在台灣開始發展了。

什麼是「長者生命回顧團體」?
「長者生命回顧團體」是一項有結構性，10 週
的團體式活動。長者每個星期聚集一次，每次大約兩小
時，帶領者會準備一個主題，帶領長者回顧或敘說此主
題。這些主題包括生命的轉捩點(我的生命線)、我的家
人、一生中的朋友、我的價值觀、誰是我、我最擅長的
事、最快樂的一件事、老照片說故事、我的遺憾、我的
夢想等。生命回顧有別於懷舊活動，因為它不僅讓長者
回顧過去，也給長者以現在的角度，重新詮釋過去的
我。透過自我敘述，重新定義過去的自己，在這過程當
中，發現自己生命的意義，長者往往自我整理後，產生
自信，不再怨嘆，對未來的生活有新的樂趣。這四年來，
台灣各地區已有超過 30 個團體舉辦過「長者生命回
顧」活動，成功的帶領超過 400 位長者製作屬於他們的
生命故事書。這些生命故事書，有人把它當成傳家寶珍
藏，有些把它當成親朋好友拜訪時的話題。有些家庭把
它當成療癒的工具，因為長者已經失智，看到這本他們

44

曾經做過的生命故事書，彷彿回到從前，不僅可以鎮定
情緒，長者還可以進入短暫的背景氛圍，享受快樂的情
境。
對我而言，
「長者生命回顧團體」是一項生命的
傳承，銀髮族智慧的資產。每次帶領長者回顧過去，好
像跟著他們一起走過歷史，從日本時代、世界大戰、國
民政府遷台、228 與白色恐怖、戰後的台灣、家庭代工
的時代、台美斷交、經濟起飛、政黨輪替。對我最大的
啟發，是長者刻苦耐勞的精神，遇到困難不灰心，咬著
牙根往前走，他們的意志力、勇氣都讓我佩服。
今年能在 NATWA 年會分享這幾年在台推廣
「長者生命回顧團體」的經驗，我個人覺得更是一件很
光榮的事，因為我想跟 NATWA 姊妹們說，讓我們為下
一代蒐集更多台灣的精神，婦女勇敢的力量吧!

咱可以講，不同的族群、民族，伊的語言就不同。歷史

文藝、文學，甚至詩歌的流傳、歷史的記載。這樣，才
有法度來將一個民族特別的文化來傳承落去。

Sharing My life

Grace Buie

On my way to Ottawa by bus, I met a man in his mid-thirties. While he was bending looking at his computer, I spotted
on his on a part of his back uncovered by his t-shirt a three-faced god tattoo. He told me it took 45 hours to complete,
and he endured the pain to remind him that his wife went through pain in contractions when giving birth to their daughter. He intrigued me. This inspired me to create my DVD.
“Ghost and Tattoo” is a sound, image and text DVD production, my cultural pearl necklace ( 文化珍珠項鏈) made from
cultural pearls ( 文化珍珠) I collected. With the help of an artist, it was completed in 2007. Most of the photos are from
my garden. I have presented “Ghost and Tattoo” on a few occasions: Ryerson Life Senior course, Diversity Toronto and
Mensa club, Enquiry Night, 2013, and a few other presentations. I never promote it commercially. Maybe I consider any
art endeavor therapy. It is biographical and, due to time, is limited to 20 minutes. I present the six clips, highlights from
the DVD.
The DVD describes the journey of my soul, expressed in writing, in images, and in a special DVD that captures the fragmentation and healing in my quest for meaning.
This is a personal story reflecting the East meeting the West. It represents the clash between an adopted culture and a
traditional culture, it is leaving your homeland, your root culture and loving one not from your own culture. It is a story
discussing love from an academic perspective, it gives insight from intergenerational issues, cultural issues and historical issues.
If you are interested, visit my Facebook: Ghost & Tattoo.

A Chat With Our President

Yamei Lee
By/Jenny Lee
Twenty-seven years ago in March of 1988, Yamei Lee and 19 other Taiwanese women gathered in Los Angeles upon the request of then future vice
president of Taiwan, Annette Lu. Ms. Lu had been on a tour around the
United States to hand-pick women she knew to share her vision of what is
to become NATWA -- an association of Taiwanese American women based
on friendship and common culture. Thus began our current president’s
journey and involvement with NATWA.

I had the pleasure of interviewing Yamei, who shared her history, goals for
the year, and vision for the future generation. Her goals and theme for what NATWA accomplishes this year
come as no surprise when I find that her past careers involve being a psychiatric nurse and teacher; success
in both careers involve a great deal of compassion.
The Taiwanese American community as a whole is successful, but our culture of not getting involved in other
people’s business causes us to look inward and to care only about our own welfare. Many people see very
little of what goes on outside of our community. Yamei feels that since we have benefited by being in the
land of plenty, land of opportunity, and land of freedom, we should be grateful for what we have and consider paying it back. Her vision is for all of us to look outside and start helping other people - either by volunteering or other means. We want to remind ourselves that we can give a little bit of love and concern to
those who are less fortunate and to make their lives a little better. The joy we get from helping others is
priceless, uplifting, and a reward in itself.

Yamei is honored to have been nominated by the 9 members of the nominating committee, and accepted the
post because she believes in its mission and she likes the group very much. Additionally, she feels that she
has something to offer to the group, and that is why she is taking this opportunity to give back what she has
received over the years and to give her input. She hopes to lead the group toward compassionate acts, and to
exert a positive influence on others.
When asked what her favorite thing is about NATWA, she said that the friendship between so many wonderful, outstanding Taiwanese American women who care for and learn from each other is what she cherishes
the most. This is also what she hopes will continue in future generations of NATWA. She hopes that in 10
years, the organization will remain vibrant, although she does feel that the group will need to evolve. Yamei
feels that in order to remain relevant, the vision of NATWA must be re-evaluated to meet the identity of the
emerging NATWA group.
