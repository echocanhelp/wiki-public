"Chen Min-jen (Chinese: 陳明真) is a Taiwanese nuclear scientist and politician who served as a
member of the Legislative Yuan from 2005 to 2008."
Infobox: Member of the Legislative Yuan, In office 1 February 2005 – 31 January 2008,
Constituency: Overseas Chinese, Party: Democratic Progressive.
Education: National Tsing Hua University (BS), Iowa State University (PhD).
Fields: Nuclear engineering. Workplaces: Argonne National Laboratory, General Electric,
Macromicro Technology. Thesis: "Simulation of plenum thermo-hydraulics in a liquid metal fast
breeder reactor under a buoyancy-affected condition". Doctoral advisor: Alfred Franklin Rohach.
PhD earned 1983 (doi:10.31274/rtd-180813-8561). As a legislator he commented on infrastructure
projects led by the National Science Council (cites Taipei Times 2006-10-20, Hirsch).
