title: Inventor Spotlight: Yih-Chyun Jenq

Introduction

Yih-Chyun Jenq is a notable inventor based in Lake Oswego, Oregon, with an impressive portfolio of 10 patents. His contributions to the field of electronics and signal routing have marked significant advancements in technology, particularly in the design and functionality of input/output systems and flexible circuitry.

Latest Patents

Among his latest inventions is the "Input/output (I/O) bidirectional buffer for interfacing I/O ports." This innovative bidirectional buffer features first and second unidirectional buffers that allow for efficient signal retransmission in opposite directions between two buses. The design ensures that when one bus is pulled low, the corresponding unidirectional buffer acts to manage the signals accurately, with features that enable rapid return to high logic levels. Another significant patent is the "Apparatus for flexibly routing signals between pins of electronic devices," which includes a field programmable circuit board equipped with various sockets and connector pins. This design utilizes buffered, multiple-port cross-point switches to allow host computers to dynamically connect electronic components, enhancing flexibility and efficiency in signal routing.

Career Highlights

Yih-Chyun Jenq has made significant contributions during his tenure with leading firms such as Tektronix, Inc. and Icube Corp. His work has been instrumental in pushing the boundaries of electronic circuit design, particularly in response to the growing demand for sophisticated and adaptable electronic solutions.

Collaborations

Throughout his career, Jenq has had the opportunity to collaborate with several talented coworkers, including Wen-Jai Hsieh and Chi-Song Horng. These partnerships have fostered an environment of innovation and creativity, further enriching his contributions to the field.

Conclusion

Yih-Chyun Jenq's inventive spirit and technical prowess have resulted in multiple patents that continue to influence the electronics sector. His work exemplifies the essential role of inventors in pioneering technologies that drive progress in various industries. As he continues to innovate, his contributions promise to shape the future of signal processing and electronic design.
