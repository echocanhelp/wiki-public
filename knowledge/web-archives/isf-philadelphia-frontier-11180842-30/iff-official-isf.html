We plan, organize and host The 35th annual Lansdale International Spring Festival, the largest diversity event in the state.

The festival is a feast for the senses. It is held every year during the month of April at the North Penn High School. The Festival includes international foods, music, entertainment a Health Fair and tons of children’s activities.

You don’t want to miss this year. Family friendly, Admission is free, Parking is free. This year’s festival will be held on Saturday, April 18th, 2026, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM at the North Penn High School.

This will be the 37th year of the International Spring Festival to be held on April 18, 2026 from 10.00 am to 5.00 pm. Every Year the North Penn Area schools exhibit over a 1000 artworks by children.

The Festival also features a children’s arts and education area where children taste international foods, try on ethnic costumes and play various cultural musical instruments. The kids can also participate in the Passport Program where they will learn fun facts about many different countries and earn prizes upon completion.

Children’s Activities

There is so much for children of all ages to engage in at the festival.

There are multicultural art projects such as: sand painting, origami, animal masks, paper bag puppets for the Lunar New Year, and the ever-popular Fetish necklace or bracelets. There are stamps for the Chinese Zodiac and Egyptian Hieroglyphics (write your name in Hieroglyphs). Take a photo riding on a camel in Egypt or climbing the Eiffel Tower!

The passport program is a great learning experience for children. When they complete their “visit” to the various country displays -with a stamp for each country-, they get to pick out a special prize!

Then for all ages: Try out a wide variety of multicultural rhythm instruments; try on a costume from another culture; get a free photo at the green screen (Picture yourself…).

Cultural Exhibits

From our nearest neighbors, to the far reaches of the globe, North Penn residents proudly display cultures based on their own heritage, a deep personal connection, whose culture they admire and respect. There is no right way or wrong way to represent your chosen or native cultural  exhibit. Whether you choose to use personal artifacts or literature from the country’s tourism department, we hope that you will enjoy putting together a fun and representative portrayal of the country. While nationality tables are FREE for anyone who wants to share, we ask that only a country be promoted and that the exhibit not be used in any promote an organization or for sales. For this please see vendor displays below.

Vendor Exhibits

Vendor exhibits are a crucial component of the International Spring Festival. Whether you are interested in sales, awareness of your company’s services, or promote a non-profit that does good in our local or global community, the exhibits section of International Spring festival brings a high volume of residents and visitors every year.

We will give you an early registration DISCOUNT if you sign-up on or before April 6, 2026 for Vendor tables

By request, we can pair a vendor table with a cultural table. Please register for both and indicate the pairing in the “notes and comments” section of both registrations.

ISF – Exhibitor Code of Conduct:

Each exhibitor agrees to the following:

 I will present culture, heritage, arts, traditions, language, or cuisine only and will not include political or geopolitical messaging.

 I will not display political statements, advocacy, disputed territorial claims, or sovereignty-related content.

I will use respectful language and behavior and avoid political debate, confrontation, or harassment.

I agree to modify or remove any content immediately if requested by the organizer.

I understand that violations may result in removal of materials or closure of my exhibit without refund.

Any maps, flags, symbols or clothing used will be neutral, non-provocative, and approved by the organizer.

 Legal Disclaimer: Participation in the exhibition does not constitute endorsement by the organizer of any political position, government, or territorial claim.

To Whom It May Concern

At IFF, we follow the U.S. Department of State’s guidance on recognition of
countries and territories.

As we are not a rights or activist organization, our mission remains focused on
our core objectives of inclusive of all cultures.

 While cultural displays are encouraged, flags of countries & territories not recognized by the U.S. State Department

 https://www.state.gov/independent-states-in-the-world

will not be permitted in the Parade of Nations at the International Spring Festival.

 This policy applies to all groups without exception.

Thank you,
