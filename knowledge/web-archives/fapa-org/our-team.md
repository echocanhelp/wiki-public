## Our Team

## Su-Mei Kao — National President

Su-Mei Kao has been a FAPA member since 2008, dedicated to the advancement of FAPA mission. She is a scientist, a homemaker, a teacher, and a community builder. Su-Mei received her M.S. in Microbiology from North Carolina State University and a PhD in Microbiology from Cornell University.
