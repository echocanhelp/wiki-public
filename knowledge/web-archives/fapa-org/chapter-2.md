## Members are our Strength

Local Chapters: 5 (site counter widget — stale)
Members: 346 (site counter widget)

As a grassroots organization, FAPA has 44 Chapters with more than 2,700 active members around the United States. Each Chapter holds its own events with the local communities. It is a great way to learn more about FAPA and its members’ work.

If you are interested in participating in these events, we strongly encourage you to contact the Local Chapter President near you or contact FAPA HQ (home@fapa.org) if their email is not listed below.
