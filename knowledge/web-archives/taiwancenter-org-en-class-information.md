## Mandarin Classes

Want to chat with your grandparents in Chinese? Reconnect with your Taiwanese roots? Help your kids learn their heritage language? Or just curious about Mandarin? Whether you're a complete beginner or looking to improve, we've got you covered. Three experienced teachers—Lin Shu-Ching (National Taiwan Normal University, 10+ years overseas teaching), Shih Yu-Li (specialist in teaching non-native speakers), and Ho Su-Mei (linguistics expert)—bring Mandarin to life. We use authentic Taiwan textbooks plus seasonal materials to make learning fun and culturally rich.

Feeling lost with technology? Want to email friends and family, browse the web, or master your smartphone? No worries! Teacher Hsu Shih-Tan's beginner-friendly classes cover everything from basics to social media, step by step. These popular classes fill up fast, and small class sizes ensure personal attention. Don't let tech intimidate you—make it work for you!

## Vegetarian Cooking Classes

Want healthy, delicious meals that help the planet too? Nine expert instructors—nutritionists, chefs, and cooking teachers—share 24 amazing plant-based recipes over eight weeks. From everyday dishes to impressive dinner party fare, you'll learn to create delicious food from vegetables and fruits. Plus, enjoy your creations with classmates after each lesson!

## Taiwan Center Choir

Love to sing? Want to sound better and feel more confident? Join our choir and let Teacher Wang Rong-Yi help you improve! Learn proper breathing, vocal techniques, and song interpretation in a relaxed, fun atmosphere. No musical background needed—just bring your enthusiasm! We perform at Center events, giving you a chance to shine on stage while building community through music.

## Photography Basics

Want better photos of family, friends, and travels? More engaging social media posts? Photography is easier than you think! Learn camera basics, composition, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, lighting, and more. Includes outdoor practice sessions. Whether you shoot with a DSLR or smartphone, you'll learn to tell stories through your lens!
