2021-2022 CRB Officers and Board Members

Congratulations to CRB's 2021-2022 officers and board members:

  • President: Salu Ribeiro, M.Sc., TS(ABB), ELS/ALS(AAB)
  • President-Elect: Marlane Angle, Ph.D., HCLD/CC(ABB)
  • Secretary: Shawn Breen, Ph.D., M.Sc., HCLD/TS(ABB)
  • Director Member: Michael Stout, Ph.D., HCLD/ELD/TS/CC/ALD(ABB)
  • CRB Supervisor Member: Eva Schenkman, M.Sc., TS(ABB)*
  • CRB Medical Technologist Member: Elizabeth Williams, ELS/ALS(AAB)*
  • CRB Past President/Chair of the CRB Meeting Committee (2022): Arthur Chang, Ph.D., HCLD/ELD/CC(ABB)

The 2021 officers and board members took office on June 16, 2021.

*Note that Eva Schenkman and Elizabeth Williams are serving the second year of two-year terms of office.

2021-2022 CRB Committee Chairpersons

  • CRB Credentialing and Membership Committee: Michael Stout, Ph.D., HCLD/ELD/TS/CC/ALD(ABB)
  • CRB Legislative Committee: Eva Schenkman, M.Sc., TS(ABB), Chair
  • CRB Meeting Committee: Arthur Chang, Ph.D., HCLD/ELD/CC(ABB), Chair
  • CRB Nominating Committee: Arthur Chang, Ph.D., HCLD/ELD/CC(ABB), Chair
  • CRB Publications Committee: Marlane Angle, Ph.D., HCLD/CC(ABB), Chair
  • CRB Standing Rules Committee: Shawn Breen, Ph.D., M.Sc., HCLD/TS(ABB), Chair

About the CRB

The College of Reproductive Biology, a not-for-profit special interest group of the AAB, provides a focused forum for andrologists and embryologists to communicate with each other, share ideas and knowledge, and foster closer collaborations.
