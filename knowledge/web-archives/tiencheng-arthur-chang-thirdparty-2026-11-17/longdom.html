Tien Cheng Chang

Tien Cheng Chang
Associate Professor and Director, Clinical ART Lab, Reproductive Endocrinology and Infertility, Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology
The University of Texas Health Science Center, USA

Biography

Dr. Tien-cheng Chang is Assistant Professor at the Division of Reproductive Endocrinology and Infertility, Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, University of Texas Health Science Center, San Antonio (UTHSCSA). He also serves as Clinical Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART) Laboratories Director at UT Medicine Fertility Center and Embryology and Stem Cell Research Laboratory Director at UTHSCSA OB/GYN. He received his bachelors degree from National Taiwan University, Taiwan, then Masters and Ph.D. degrees in Endocrinology and Reproductive Physiology at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. Dr. Chang is board certified as clinical laboratory director by the American Board of Bioanalysis (ABB).
