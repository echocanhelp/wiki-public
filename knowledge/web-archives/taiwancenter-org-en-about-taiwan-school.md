# School Introduction

For eight years, the Taiwan School at the Taiwan Center of Greater Los Angeles has been dedicated to providing diverse learning opportunities for our community.

![Image 6](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/693f9fee857d9f8d63d016d9_an%20empty%20modern%20library.jpg)

![Image 7](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/693f9cb11684c5c26f530104_3d%20rendering%20of%20an%20empty.jpg)

![Image 8](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/693f9f5a60cdacc96b7f0b14_a%20photograph%20of%20modern%20%40Variation.jpg)

![Image 9](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/693fb4dde871add4d2bc6ba0_a%20modern%20university.jpg)

![Image 10](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/693fb684df79ecada6bd23ee_photo%20of%20modern%20interior.jpg)

From language and culture to life skills, we believe learning has no age limit, and everyone can find their own space for growth here. Professional teachers, practical courses, and a warm atmosphere make learning a part of life.

[Apply Now](https://www.taiwancenter.org/en/about-taiwan-school#)

[Mandarin Class ![Image 11: Architecture](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/693fb852cbd37d3b81be9a71_asian%20university%20students.jpg) Three professionally trained senior teachers lead practical-oriented Mandarin instruction. Teacher Lin Shu-Ching graduated from NTNU Chinese Department with over ten years of overseas teaching experience; Teacher Shi Yu-Li specializes in teaching Chinese to foreigners; Teacher Ho Su-Mei majored in linguistics with expertise in language pedagogy. Courses use original Taiwanese textbooks supplemented with American university Chinese materials, plus self-developed materials based on seasonal festivals, making learning closer to daily life and more interesting. Through Chinese language study, students can gain a deeper understanding of the richness of Taiwanese culture.](https://www.taiwancenter.org/class-information)[Japanese Class ![Image 12: Architecture](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/693fbaa24727d093a17f9fe3_a%20japanese%20companys.jpg) Teacher Wei Bo-Ji graduated from Tokyo University of Education and has accumulated over twenty years of Japanese teaching experience in Taiwan and American community colleges. Courses start from basic pronunciation, covering reading, grammar, conversation, dictation, and comprehensive training, with two teaching assistants to help. Whether **you want to** travel to Japan, communicate with elders, or **gain a deeper** understanding of this country closely connected to Taiwan, this class can meet your needs.](https://www.taiwancenter.org/class-information)[Computer Class ![Image 13: Architecture](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/693fbccd1b60ee036f7413ba_a%20photograph%20of%20two%20young%20%40Variation.jpg) Teacher Xu Shi-Tan's computer class is very popular, filling up immediately when offered. Practical courses designed for different skill levels, from basic operations to advanced applications, help students master modern digital tools, making technology a helpful companion in daily life.](https://www.taiwancenter.org/class-information)[Vegetarian Cooking Class ![Image 14: Architecture](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/6948d5756c1c5a70b5b4dc7d_A%20high-quality%2C%20candid%20%40Variation.jpg) Promoting the dietary philosophy of "eating well, eating full, healthy and eco-friendly." Nine senior cooking instructors including nutritionists, restaurant chefs, and cooking teachers share secrets of healthy vegetarian cuisine. Eating is not just about filling the stomach or enjoyment, but also a way of health preservation, contributing to personal health and environmental sustainability.](https://www.taiwancenter.org/class-information)

##### Jennifer C., Mandarin Chinese Class

### "As a second-generation Taiwanese American, not learning Chinese well was always a regret. After two years here, I can finally talk to my grandparents in Chinese. The teachers are patient, the materials are engaging, and I've reconnected with my cultural roots."
