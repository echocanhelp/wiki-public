About TGSA in UC-Irvine

Welcome to UC Irvine TGSA!

Taiwanese Graduate Student Association (TGSA) is a student organization at University of California, Irvine. 
Our goals are:

1. Promote understanding of Taiwanese culture;
2. Organize activities in the field of social, educational, academic, cultural and other humanitarian services; 
3. Provide services to the new Taiwanese graduate students.
