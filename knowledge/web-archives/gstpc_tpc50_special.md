# TPC 50th Anniversary Special Program

**Source:** [https://gstpc.org/home/tpc-50/](https://gstpc.org/home/tpc-50/)  
**Archived:** 2026-07-14

## Overview

The TPC 50th Anniversary Special Program (TPC 50 週年特別節目) was a series of broadcast segments celebrating the 50th anniversary of the Presbyterian Church in Taiwan (台灣基督長老教會). The program was hosted and featured **Rev. David Huang (黃德利牧師)** as the main speaker.

## Program Episodes

Three episodes were published in May–June 2020:
