昨晚又稱「小台北」的蒙特略公園（Monterey Park）市發生大規模槍擊案，造成十人死亡和16人受傷，震驚整個大洛杉磯地區。
大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會謹此向此起憾事的所有受害者表達最深切的哀悼，本會也要嚴正地譴責這起在除夕佳節期間發生的殘酷暴力罪行。失去的生命無法挽回，我們期待社區及國家都能有更多的保護來應對未來類似的暴力犯罪。
目前本會對於有無台僑於此槍擊事件受害仍不清楚，但大洛杉磯台灣會館將持續與執法機關及相關單位聯繫，了解案情的同時也盡所能協助被此事件影響的社區成員。我們也呼籲相關單位能夠徹底調查此案，火速將嫌犯逮捕到案。
The news of last night’s shooting in Monterey Park is a tragic shock for the Taiwanese American community in the Greater Los Angeles area.
The Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles wishes to express condolences to all who were affected in this sad incident, and we want to strongly condemn this senseless act of violence on the eve of lunar new year. Nothing will bring back the lives of those needlessly cut short. It is important that we ensure our communities and country are better protected against this sort of violence.
it is not yet clear if any Taiwanese Americans were hurt in the shooting, the Taiwan Center will be in touch with the law enforcement authorities to learn more about the incident and see if we can provide any assistance to members of our community affected by this shooting. We also urge the authorities to thoroughly investigate and swiftly bring the perpetrator responsible to justice.
