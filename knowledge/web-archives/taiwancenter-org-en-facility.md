Facility

From a 10,000-volume library to a 250-seat auditorium, from permanent historical exhibits to a rooftop garden—the Taiwan Center offers versatile spaces for culture, education, and community gathering.

Venue overview

## Center Venues

[![Image 3: Architecture ](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/693f9fee857d9f8d63d016d9_an%20empty%20modern%20library.jpg)](https://www.taiwancenter.org/en/facility#)

#### Kuei-Jung Wang Memorial Library

Home to over 10,000 volumes on Taiwan spanning art, history, literature, music, language, and humanities—the largest overseas Chinese-language library dedicated to Taiwan. Open to all community members.

[![Image 4: Architecture ](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/6964c74503e3cf63950094ad_228%E8%BF%BD%E6%80%9D.jpg)](https://www.taiwancenter.org/en/facility#)

#### 228 Memorial Exhibition Room

A permanent exhibit featuring historical documents, photographs, and artifacts from the February 28th Incident, preserving this pivotal chapter in Taiwan's history and honoring the values of peace, human rights, and democracy.

[![Image 5: Architecture ](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/693f9cb11684c5c26f530104_3d%20rendering%20of%20an%20empty.jpg)](https://www.taiwancenter.org/en/facility#)

#### Main Auditorium

A 250-seat multipurpose venue with professional sound and lighting, ideal for lectures, concerts, award ceremonies, and large-scale community events.

[![Image 6: Architecture ](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/693f9f5a60cdacc96b7f0b14_a%20photograph%20of%20modern%20%40Variation.jpg)](https://www.taiwancenter.org/en/facility#)

#### Conference Room

A meeting space for up to 50 people, well-suited for organizational meetings, workshops, and panel discussions in a comfortable setting.

[![Image 7: Architecture ](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/693fb4dde871add4d2bc6ba0_a%20modern%20university.jpg)](https://www.taiwancenter.org/en/facility#)

#### Classrooms

Well-equipped learning spaces used for Taiwan School classes and available to community groups for educational programs and training sessions.

[![Image 8: Architecture ](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/693fb2db49d5eb915300b232_photo%20of%20a%20dance%20studio.jpg)](https://www.taiwancenter.org/en/facility#)

#### Screening Room

A dedicated space for film screenings, documentaries, and media events, bringing Taiwanese culture and history to life through film.

[![Image 9: Architecture ](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/698b01fdd3b6b3e85e01dd44_gru%CC%88ne%20Dachterrasse%20mit.jpg)](https://www.taiwancenter.org/en/facility#)

#### Rooftop Garden

An open-air green space on the top level of the Center, perfect for outdoor receptions, casual gatherings, and community relaxation.

[![Image 10: Architecture ](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/693fc840da86af791b616feb_presentation%20printing%20in.jpg)](https://www.taiwancenter.org/en/facility#)

#### Exhibition Hall

A rentable gallery space for art shows, photography exhibitions, and cultural displays. Available for booking by community groups and artists.

[![Image 11: Architecture ](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68a69cbd7fc3be5050e05c92/693fc6f1307b501c60622a37_studio%20photo%20of%20a%20stack.jpg)](https://www.taiwancenter.org/en/facility#)

#### Taiwanese American Heritage Gallery

A permanent exhibit documenting the growth and milestones of Taiwanese American organizations in Greater Los Angeles, telling the story of how our community took root and thrived in Southern California.
