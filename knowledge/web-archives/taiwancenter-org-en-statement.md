# Media Usage Statement

## About Our Media Content

Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles is a legally registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Photos and media content displayed on this website were captured at public community events hosted by our Center for the purposes of:

*   Recording and sharing wonderful moments from community events
*   Showcasing the rich achievements of Taiwanese cultural heritage
*   Promoting connection and exchange among community members
*   Demonstrating the Center's services and contributions to the public

## Our Commitment

All media content is presented following principles of positivity, respect, and protection. We commit to:

*   Using only for nonprofit community service and cultural promotion purposes
*   Not using media content for commercial purposes
*   Presenting all participants in positive and appropriate ways
*   Never involving any content that exploits or could harm children

## Privacy Protection

We understand and respect each participant's privacy rights. If you or your family members appear in website photos or media and wish to have the content removed, please contact us at any time. We will process your request as soon as possible after receiving notification.
