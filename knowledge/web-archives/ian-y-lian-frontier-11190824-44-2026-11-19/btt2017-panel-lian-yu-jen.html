    焦點

    Christmas gathering 相約在耶誕

    A Different Christmas Party

     

    Scientists around the globe are flying back to Taiwan during Christmas break. During this period, we invite biotech talents in Taiwan and worldwide to share their experience and vision. We want to inspire next generation scientists in the bio-related fields, to strengthen connections among scientists, and to catalyze collaboration among academic and industrial communities in Taiwan and worldwide.

     

    一場知性的耶誕聚會

     

    很多人在耶誕假期從世界各地回家。我們想藉此，聚集在臺灣與世界各地的生技人，讓臺灣的學生聽一聽科學家的生活點滴、讓不同領域的生技人彼此交流、成為未來合作的開端。​

     

    We thank TBAs for the recruitment of oversea speakers!
    A short introduction of TBAs: Taiwanese Biotechnology Associations (TBAs) are non-profit organizations initiated in 2012 by a group of Taiwanese graduate students, postdocs and young professionals in Boston. In addition to Boston, TBAs have now been started in multiple places including Texas, California (Bay Area, SoCal) and Midwest in the US as well as in other countries including Switzerland, Canada and UK.

    Graduate school 101 研究所的第一堂課

    Can I become a Great Scientist?

     

    Come to meet students and professors to know how they enjoy their lives as scientists and what are the important characters to become a great scientist.

     

    想要成為優秀的科學家嗎？科學家們有哪些特質呢？

     

    來自各地的優秀科學家，跟與會的大學生與研究生們，分享他們在科學研究上的點點滴滴。

    Speed Networking 午間快閃交流

    Building Global Connections

     

    Meet young scientists and leaders in academia, biotech industry and other scientific related fields. Know what are the opportunities after school and how to prepare yourself for the next step!

     

    認識全球生技人

     

    與生技領域的前輩與領袖互動， 多認識他們的工作內容與特質。也可藉此思考自己想走的下一步！

    The future of biotech talents 生技人的未來

    Trends in Biomedical Field

     

    Students and young professionals in the biomedical field will join us from all over the world to share their views on the future of biotech development.

     

    生技的未來

     

    來自臺灣與世界各地的學/業界的生技高手，跟大家一起腦力激盪，思考生技發展與生技人的未來！

    搭乘高鐵
    搭乘台灣高鐵抵台南站者，可轉搭台鐵電車抵達台南火車站，自後站出口走大學→遇勝利路左轉→遇小東路右轉約100公尺，左側即為醫學院成杏校區入口。

     

    自行開車（國道路線）
    南下：
    沿國道一號南下 → 下大灣交流道右轉 → 沿復興路 → 接小東路 → 於小東路口右轉，直走即可抵達本院。【自國道三號南下者，轉國道8號（西向），可接國道一號（南向）】

    北上：
    沿國道一號北上 → 下仁德交流道左轉 → 沿東門路(西向)往台南市區直走 → 遇中華東路右轉(北向) → 於小東路口左轉，直走即可抵達本院。 【自國道三號北上者，轉86號快速道路（西向），可接國道一號（北向）】
