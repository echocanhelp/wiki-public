## Conflict ledger (what the independent sources do NOT agree on)
- Wideman Gold Medal year: **2002** (widemanpianocompetition.com past-winners;
  Wikipedia) vs **2003** (Chapman 2008 press release; Chapman program PDF).
  The organizer's list wins the dating; Chapman renderings kept as variants.
- Christel DeHaan Classical Fellowship: **winner, 2009** (USC Thornton 2011;
  Chapman Newsroom 2015) vs **finalist, 2008-2009** (Wikipedia; TAH record's
  Notable Accomplishments block). The two Chapman/USC institutional renderings
  and the APA-cycle wording ("2008-2009 Classical Fellowship Awards" finalists
  list, per Wikipedia's citation title) are reconcilable: finalist in the
  2008-2009 cycle announcement vs winner named for the 2009 award. Both
  renderings remain on the page; the institutional winner renderings now
  outnumber the single finalist rendering.
- Bakersfield upbringing (Wikipedia) vs Los Angeles birth (TAH record, Chapman
  faculty page "Born in Los Angeles", Chapman 2015 "native Angeleno") —
  birth-vs-raising, not necessarily a conflict; both recorded.
