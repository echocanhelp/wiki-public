# Forums provide Taiwan with soapbox — Taipei Times, 2002-09-13 (snippet capture)

> On Aug. 26, at a session on renewable energy at the World Sustainability Hearing, Lai Wei-chieh (赖伟杰) of the Taiwan-based Green Citizen Action Alliance, gave a presentation about Taiwanese grassroots groups and their fight against the establishment of the nation's Fourth Nuclear Power Plant while Lin Tze-luen (林子伦) of US-based Taiwan Environmental Action Network (TEAN) worked as a translator on the presentation.

> Activists of TEAN, including Lin and another co-chair, Tu Wen-ling (杜文苓), acted as go-betweens during the preparation of the hearing. They introduced four case studies detailing environmental issues in Taiwan.

> Jeff Hou (侯志仁), international coordinator of the Spoonbill Action Voluntary Echo (SAVE) committee in the US, shared his experience of working with Taiwanese groups to halt the Pinnan Industrial Complex's (滨南工业区) proposal of establishing a site where the black-faced spoonbill -- an endangered species with a worldwide population less than 1,000 -- settles down for the winter.

> "Local experience is actually the most useful tool linking Taiwanese NGOs and their counterparts in the rest of the world," Hou told the Taipei Times.
