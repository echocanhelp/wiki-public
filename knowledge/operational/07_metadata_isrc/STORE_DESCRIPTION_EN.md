# Store description — English edition

## Short (≤300 chars)

Unabridged audiobook of Dr. Albert S. Lai’s foundational study of Formosan / Taiwanese immigrant church and community life in Los Angeles — migration, faith, identity, and hope across generations.

## Long

*Toward A Community of Hope: A Mission to Formosan Community in Los Angeles* is Dr. Albert S. Lai’s (賴信雄) landmark 1971 work — reintroduced for new listeners in the era of its 2025 republication conversations.

This unabridged English audiobook traces historical memory, migration pressure, identity tension, and communal mission among Taiwanese (Formosan) Christians in Southern California, including the formative world of the Formosan Presbyterian Church in Los Angeles (FPCLA).
