# Casting brief — English narrator

## Project
Audiobook of Dr. Albert S. Lai, *Toward A Community of Hope* (Taiwanese American church / migration history). Professional retail release (YouTube Music + audiobook stores).

## Voice
- Warm, scholarly-pastoral (not news anchor, not theatrical preacher)
- Mature color welcome (40s–70s)
- Clear American or careful international English; consistent accent
- Comfortable with theological vocabulary and Chinese/Taiwanese proper names (with pronunciation bible)

## Pace
145–160 words/minute; allow silence at section breaks.

## Audition sides (record all three)
1. **90s** cold read from Chapter I opening (producer supplies PDF page)  
2. **60s** names/places paragraph (Formosa, Los Angeles, Presbyterian, FPCLA)  
3. **45s** first-person testament/quote with restrained emotion  

## Deliverables if cast
- Remote or studio 48 kHz/24-bit WAV
- Chapter-by-chapter delivery + pickups within 5 business days of notes
- Work-for-hire / buyout preferred (quote day rate × estimated studio days)
