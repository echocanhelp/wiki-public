# Track 02 — Author’s audiobook preface (English)

**Runtime target:** 90–180 seconds  
**Voice:** Dr. Albert S. Lai (preferred) — else EN narrator reading “in the author’s words” only if Albert approves text  
**Status:** DRAFT for Albert review — not locked

---

## SCRIPT (draft for Albert)

[0.8 s silence]

My name is Albert S. Lai — 賴信雄.

More than fifty years ago, I wrote about the Formosan community in Los Angeles — about migration, faith, identity, and the hope of becoming a true community under God.

I am grateful that a new generation still finds publishing value in this work.  
This audiobook is offered so that listeners — children and grandchildren of that journey, and friends who walk with us — may hear the story with the ear, not only the eye.

If my voice is imperfect, let the meaning be clear:  
we were searching for home, for church, and for hope —  
and we are still called to form a community of hope.

Thank you for listening.

[1.0 s silence]

---

## ALT shorter (60 s)

My name is Albert S. Lai.  
This audiobook presents my work, *Toward A Community of Hope*.  
I am glad it can reach listeners today, across languages and generations.  
May it serve the church and the community. Thank you.
