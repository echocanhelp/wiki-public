Biographical content (verbatim from Capture 1 content_html / Capture 3)

Paragraphs 1–2 are already reflected on the person page. Paragraph 3 is the delta this capture exists for — the prior deepen passes absorbed paragraphs 1–2 and dropped it:

Hanchien Lee was accepted at the Curtis Institute of Music at age eleven where she studied with Claude Frank and Eleanor Sokoloff. Following graduation from Curtis, Ms. Lee continued her studies at Yale University with Claude Frank, earning both a Master’s degree and an Artists’ Diploma. She holds a doctoral degree at the Peabody Conservatory of John Hopkins University where she was under the tutelage of with Boris Slutsky. Ms. Lee has also studied with such esteemed pianists as Gary Graffman, Richard Goode, Meng-Chieh Liu, Jerome Lowenthal and Fou T’song. She currently serves as a piano faculty at the Peabody Preparatory of the Johns Hopkins University. (Information source: http://www.hanchienlee.com/)

Paragraph 1–2 content (already on the page, recorded here so the capture is complete): debut with the Philadelphia Orchestra at age sixteen; performances across North America, Europe and Asia — Steinway Hall (New York), Academy of Music and Kimmel Center (Philadelphia), Joseph Meyerhoff Symphony Hall (Baltimore); recitals at the Philadelphia Chamber Music Society, Muhlenberg College Piano Series, Hillman Performing Arts Center, Texas International Piano Festival, Basilica San Pietro (Italy), Centro Cultural del Antiguo (Spain), Taipei Convention Center (Taiwan); soloist with the American Elite youth orchestra, Plainfield Symphony, Taiwan National Orchestra, Chamber Orchestra of Perugia, Poland’s Capella Cracovienses Chamber Orchestra; Chi-Mei Scholarship and Clara Ascherfeld Award; first prize Russel C. Wonderlic Piano Competition; top prizes Heida Hermanns International Piano Competition and Yamaha Piano Competition; festivals Sarasota, Norfolk Chamber Music Festival, Prussia Cove International Music Seminar, Music Academy of the West; member of the prize-winning quartet that won the Yale School of Music Chamber Music Competition, performed at Stony Brook University, Muhlenberg College and Montana University, where she also conducted a master class.
