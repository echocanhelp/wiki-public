English body (verbatim, content_html)

Grace Fong is a prize-winning American pianist born in Los Angeles to Taiwanese immigrant parents, with an international career as a concerto soloist, recitalist, chamber musician, and contemporary keyboardist. She has performed at major venues including Weill Hall at Carnegie Hall, the Kennedy Center, the Hollywood Bowl, the Liszt Academy in Budapest, and the Konzerthaus Dortmund in Germany, and has appeared with orchestras including the Mariinsky Theater Orchestra under Valery Gergiev, the Hallé Orchestra under Mark Elder, the Indianapolis Symphony, the Phoenix Symphony, and the Pacific Symphony. Radio and television performances have included NPR’s Performance Today, the BBC, and WFMT Chicago.

A gold medalist and prizewinner at numerous international competitions including the Leeds International, the International Liszt, the Bosendorfer International, and the Wideman International, Fong is also the winner of the Christel DeHaan Classical Fellowship of the American Pianists Association, the first female recipient in twelve years. She received the Grand Prize in piano from the National Foundation for the Advancement of the Arts and was named a Presidential Scholar in the Arts, performing at the Kennedy Center and receiving a medallion at the White House. Born in Los Angeles, she completed a double major and minor at the University of Southern California on a Trustee Scholarship, earning the Renaissance Scholar Prize and the Thornton School of Music’s Most Outstanding Student designation. Her Master’s and Doctoral degrees are from the Cleveland Institute of Music, where she studied with Sergei Babayan on the Victor Babin Scholarship.

Fong is currently Professor and Director of Keyboard Studies at Chapman University’s Hall-Musco Conservatory of Music, where she received the Valerie Scudder Award for faculty excellence. Beyond the concert stage, she has served as guest artist and teacher at festivals across the United States and Europe, and has collaborated with filmmakers, dancers, chefs, and fashion designers — including a music video directed by Oscar-nominated filmmaker Mike Figgis and performances with the band Pink Martini alongside the Cleveland Orchestra and San Francisco Symphony.

(Source: pianistgracefong.com, chapman.edu)

中文 rendering (verbatim, page_text — the block the person page never reproduced)

Grace Fong 是一位鋼琴家與音樂教育者，父母為台灣移民，本人成長於加州。身兼協奏曲獨奏家、獨奏會演奏家 及室內樂演奏家，她在美國、歐洲、阿拉伯聯合大公國及亞洲各地建立了國際演奏事業，演出場地包括卡內基音樂廳 威爾廳、甘迺迪中心、好萊塢碗型露天劇場及布達佩斯李斯特音樂學院等。

方女士於南加州大學完成學士學業，獲頒文藝復興學者獎，並被評選為USC Thornton音樂學院鍵盤系最傑出學生。 此後她在克里夫蘭音樂學院師從著名鋼琴家Sergei Babayan，取得碩士及博士學位。她的國際競賽成績斐然：曾在 英國利茲國際鋼琴比賽中名列前茅，榮獲貝森朵夫國際鋼琴比賽第二名、Wideman International鋼琴比賽金牌，以及 克里夫蘭國際鋼琴比賽巴洛克獎等多項殊榮。她亦榮獲全國藝術促進基金會鋼琴大獎，並獲選為總統藝術學者，於 甘迺迪中心演出並在白宮獲頒獎章。目前她擔任查普曼大學Hall-Musco Conservatory of Music鋼琴研究教授暨主任， 並榮獲Valerie Scudder教學卓越獎。

作為一位在國際舞台上演出、並在音樂學院最高層次任教的台裔美國藝術家，方女士代表著一個在音樂廳與教室中 共同塑造美國文化生活的台灣移民世代。她曾出現於美國國家公共廣播電台(NPR)《今日表演》節目、英國廣播公司 (BBC)及眾多廣播節目，並曾與印第安納波利斯交響樂團、鳳凰城交響樂團及太平洋交響樂團等擔任協奏曲獨奏。

（資料來源：pianistgracefong.com、chapman.edu）
