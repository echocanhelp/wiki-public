2. NATWA 聯誼通訊 第34期 — 會長的話 (葉明霞), 2006-02 — Katrina Edition backstory

  • Vault file: knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/690-北美洲台灣婦女會聯誼通訊-第三十四期北美洲台灣.md
  • Original: https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/690-…/
  • Verbatim: 「感謝何麗珍姐一連替咱編了兩期的聯誼通訊。在上期出版前正逢Katrina風颱來襲。住在Baton Rouge的麗珍，一心要安頓從New Orleans來她家避難的朋友，又一心要趕完編輯工作。在缺水又缺電的克難環境中，她完成了以”台灣櫻花”為封面的第33期。」
  • Establishes: she edited two consecutive issues (33–34); residence Baton Rouge corroborated by a third party; during Hurricane Katrina (Aug–Sep 2005) she sheltered friends evacuating from New Orleans at her home while finishing the 33rd issue in water- and power-outage conditions.
