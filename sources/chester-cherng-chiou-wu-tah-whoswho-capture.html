Identity tie ruling — the open field

Name, Chinese name, birth/death years (1935/2017), Yunlin birthplace, wife 陳月秀, Monsanto employment and the Senior Research Specialist title all tie across the self-report, the memoir, and the memorial. The doctorate field does not tie: the self-report prints Massachusetts Institute of Technology — Ph.D., Chemistry; the external institutional record is a University of Massachusetts Ph.D. (April 1971, Polymer Science and Engineering, advisor Lenz) under the exact full romanization 「Chester Cherng-Chiou Wu」, with UMass Amherst (1972) and University of Florida (1973) affiliations. Standing HOLD on the person page: do not overwrite MIT, do not assert UMass, until a source reconciles the two. Both are printed with their own provenance.
