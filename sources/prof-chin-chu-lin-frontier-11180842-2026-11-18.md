## Dated facts (from mystories559 full text, the dangling target)
- 2017-07 — retrospective written for the 北加州東灣長樂會 collective-book project; states the
  prior books: 留美夢，台灣情 (前衛, 2005-11, 425 pp), 台美生涯70年 (前衛, 2006-08, 505 pp),
  見證胎兒醫學 (健康文化事業公司, 2010-12, 254 pp) — per `https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/mystories559/`
- **Exact birth date: 1935-10-24**, 台中縣潭子鄉頭家厝 (mother 林江鑾 bore him at 19) — same source.
- 1930s childhood nickname 「質問博士」 (晚霞姑母) — same source.
- 1954 — 台中一中高中 全年第一名; missed 台中市長獎 because 體育 69 < 70; 保送台大醫科 — same source.
- 1962 — entered 台大婦產科 residency (class rank 4); 1965 chief resident year per companion
  piece #577 — same source + `https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/mystories577/`.
- 1963–1964 — IUD-insertion rotation (2 months each, 30 cases/day) at the 台灣人口中心
  (省立台中醫院旁), the US-funded in-utero contraceptive-device trial — per 太平洋時報
  2013-05-02 reprint `http://www.laijohn.com/archives/pc/Lim/Lim,Ctek_1/recollect/life/2913.htm`.
- 1963-10-29 — engaged 許世真 (師大音樂系, 實踐家專 music instructor); 1964-10-24 (his birthday)
  married at 潭子聖教會, 證婚 莊丁昌牧師 (台北和平教會); best man 詹錕鋙 (later 馬偕醫院院長
  1998–2001), 伴娘 許世惠 — per mystories559.
- 1969 — with wife and 6-year-old eldest son, 歐亞 13-country/18-city trip before returning to
  台大 lecturer post — same source.
- 1974–1976 Einstein: Oxford-method training under Harold Schulman (+ Luis Saldana); passed
  perinatology subspecialty written 1976, oral 1979 — **first Asian to hold the qualification** —
  same source + #577.
- 1976 — recruited at SGI (Philadelphia) by Arthur L. Herbst and Atef H. Moawad; Chicago ladder
  助教授 1976–1980, 副教授 1980–1987, 正教授 1987–2001; 1977 appointed director of the
  Obstetric High-Risk Clinic; 25 years trained 150 residents, 16 perinatology fellows,
  8 visiting researchers — same source.
- 1980 — C-peptide (fetal insulin) study with 芝大內科主任 Arthur Rubenstein published;
  1982 McGraw-Hill agreed to the IUGR textbook proposed by then-chief resident Mark I. Evans;
  **1984 IUGR textbook published (world's first on the topic)**; pirate edition circulated in
  Taiwan (US$55 list vs NT$300 pirate) — same source.
- 1984 onward — IUGR cited as his defining research line; invitations/reprints "如雪片飛來" —
  same source.
- 1987–1988 — 醫學專欄 editor in 台灣公論報 (column committee founded 1986-02-15 in Chicago at
  his initiative) — same source.
- 1990 — NATPA 第十屆總會長; annual meeting held in Taipei (per companion #577, July 1990) —
  same source + #577.
- 1990 (虚歲八十大壽) — father 林坤炎 (1912-10-18 – 1994-11-18) 80th-birthday banquet, 台中
  大坑亞哥花園 family gathering — same source.
- **1991-02** — sepsis from gallstones/E. coli at 芝加哥大學, 5-hour surgery (surgeon Thomas
  W. Wajcs per memoir transliteration 托瑪斯瓦吉西), ICU 4 days + ward 5 days, 9 antibiotics — same source.
- 1993 / 1996 / 1999 — ran for 台大醫學院院長 (6 台大 + 6 海外 candidates; only overseas-alumnus
  finalist), 成大醫學院院長 (2nd of 3 finalists), and 馬偕醫學院籌備主任 (withdrew in 2nd round) —
  same source + #577.
- 1994–2000 — 台大醫學院海外院務諮詢委員 — same source.
- 2001-03-13 — MI on the commute to 芝加哥大學醫院; self-diagnosed, stented right coronary
  artery, discharged after 3 days — same source.
- 2003 — 許世真 thyroid-cancer diagnosis (3 surgeries + radioiodine; neck recurrence + lung
  metastasis 2009; left carotid aneurysm ligation at UCSF) — same source.
- **2004-10 — 4th perinatal fellow Mike Phillipe named in a 2020 Pritzker School of Medicine
  memorial page as trained by Prof. Chin-Chu Lin M.D. (with chair Marshall Lindheimer)** —
  independent third-party corroboration of the Chicago fellowship pipeline, same source:
  `https://www.pritzkermedicine.org/newsroom/memorials/2020/mike-phillipe-md`
- 2008 — early prostate cancer, minimally invasive prostatectomy — same source.
- **2011-06-03 — first wife 許世真 died** (married 47 years) — same source.
- 2011-07 — met 韓淑清 (b. 1940-12-02, daughter of 韓石泉醫師; 南加州) via 黃東昇 couple at the
  UCSD 台美夏令會; engaged 2012-03 鳳凰城; married 2012-11-23 (day after Thanksgiving) at the
  Concord Hilton, 證婚 張瑞雄牧師 — same source.
- Children (all three from 許世真): 長男 林存欣 Jim Lin (MIT + 芝大醫學院, anesthesiology;
  wife 邱鈺淋 May Chiu, daughter of 邱永仁), 次男 林存亮 John Lin (西北大學 medical, radiologist;
  wife 張怡君 Christine Chang, 台大農學院 張輝雄's daughter), 女兒 林存如 Juliet Lin
