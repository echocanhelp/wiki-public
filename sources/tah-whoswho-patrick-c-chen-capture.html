4a. 金鷹學院 Golden Eagle Institute — 院長 2008–2013

  • File: knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/golden-eagle-institute.md · Original: https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/golden-eagle-institute/ · published 2015-06-10 (post id 14857) · vault work record [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/golden-eagle-institute|TAH #12 Golden Eagle Institute 金鷹學院]]
  • Byline: 源自 李清澤 — the account is written by the institute’s founding dean, not by the subject.
  • Verbatim: 「金鷹學院開辦迄今已有廾四年之久，院長一職由李清澤教授從1991擔任至2007年, 2008至2013年由 陳彰醫師擔任, 2014年再由李清澤教授接任迄今。副院長一職由蔡嵩陽長老於1991年擔任至2001年退任。」
  • Read plainly: he held the directorship of 金鷹學院 for the 2008–2013 term, between the founder 李清澤 (1991–2007) and 李清澤’s return in 2014. The institution’s own org page Golden Eagle Institute carried no 陳彰 line at all before this pass — the directorship was recorded only in this one file.
  • Context from the same record (institution facts, not his): founded 1991 under the 大紐約區台灣人基督徒 長輩會 third president 廖上池長老, first chairman 謝敏川牧師, first dean 李清澤教授, vice-dean 蔡嵩陽長老, secretary 黃裕源; open to 五十歲以上 with basic 漢文 reading; first term 35 students aged 51–87, average 65; classes at Bowne Street Community Church for the first five years, later 華僑文教中心.

4b. 林衡哲 memoir — classmate at Rockefeller, 1968–69

  • File: knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/mystories134.md · Original: https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/mystories134/ · published 2014-10-27 (post id 4126) · vault work record 林衡哲
  • Byline: 作者 林衡哲 — written by a classmate about his own first resident year. 林衡哲 is the TA-Archives-profiled writer-physician Jer-Shung Lin 林衡哲, who has his own page and already links this same memoir.
  • Verbatim (line 30): 「於是聽完音樂會後，偶而會去學長洪正幸和同班同學陳彰，他們二位當時正在洛克菲勒大學 攻讀博士，去借宿一夜」
  • Read plainly: in the narrator’s first resident year in Brooklyn (the essay dates his 聖瑪利醫院 posting 1968~1969 in its own heading), the person he borrowed a bed from was 同班同學陳彰 — a same-class classmate from 台大醫科 — who was then reading for a doctorate at Rockefeller University. That independently corroborates two self-reported fields: the 台大醫科 class of 1967 (同班) and the Rockefeller PhD (攻讀博士), and it places him in New York in the same window as his own 1968 arrival.
  • The same sentence names 學長洪正幸 as the other Rockefeller doctorate student. Nothing on this box identifies which 洪正幸 that is — the corpus’s other 洪正幸 mentions are a 生理學教授 speaking at a MTSC session (mtsc-2003) and a 追悼會 helper (ourjourneys321). No page exists for him; HOLD, do not merge.
