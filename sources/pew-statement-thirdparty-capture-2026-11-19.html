1. Publisher page (official site, primary)

  • URL: https://www.taiwaneseamerican.org/2021/05/pew-research-center-taiwanese-american-statement/
  • Posted 2021-05-01 18:58, “Written by Leona”; headline “Response to Pew Research Reports Hiding Taiwanese Identity: ‘We made it count. Now tell our stories.‘”
  • Live text matches the vault body verbatim (footnote 「‘Chinese’ includes those identifying as ‘Taiwanese.’」, the two demands — apology + re-publish with Taiwanese as a separate category — and the signatory block led by Christina Hu / Leona Chen / Chieh-Ting Yeh).
  • Tags on the publisher site: pew-research-center, make-it-count.

2. Counterpart open letter from the same campaign (official site)

  • URL: https://www.taiwaneseamerican.org/2021/09/pew-research-reissues-report-on-asian-americans-after-misrepresenting-taiwanese-americans/
  • Posted 2021-09-08 08:29, “Written by Leona”. Second open letter to Pew and Neil G. Ruiz responding to the correction.
  • Dates the target report: 「Key facts about Asian origin groups in the U.S」 published 2021-04-29.
  • States the evidence the campaign put to Pew: the 2019 ACS already published disaggregated Taiwanese counts in its online 1-year and 5-year tables, while the microdata Pew received did not.
  • Quotes Pew’s 2021-09-08 footnote: data for 2000, 2010 and 2019 for 「Taiwanese」, 「Chinese, except Taiwanese」 and 「Okinawan」 was added to the first table.
  • Records the campaign’s two standing principles (self-reported identities fully counted; write-in identities as legitimate as pre-existing checkboxes) and its 2030-census recommendation (inclusive race list / drop-down, public education on write-in options, larger answer boxes).
  • Adds Chieh-Ting Yeh’s second title absent from the May letter: Editor-in-Chief, Ketagalan Media.
  • Vault twin already exists: pew-research-reissues-report-on-asian-americans-after-misrepresenting-taiwanese-.

3. Pew’s own companion pieces (counterparty record)

  • https://www.pewresearch.org/decoded/2021/09/08/how-many-taiwanese-live-in-the-u-s-its-not-an-easy-question-to-answer/ — Jeffrey S. Passel, Neil G. Ruiz, Abby Budiman, 2021-09-08. Estimates the U.S. Taiwanese population at 195,000 (race alone) / 375,000 (ancestry) / 409,000 (birthplace) / 697,000 (race+ancestry+birthplace+descendants), 2019 ACS. Explains why: Taiwanese is neither a choice nor an example on the ACS race question (ranked 12th among Asian origin groups in 2019), and the Census Bureau recognises Taiwanese+Chinese combination only for 2000–2004 microdata.
  • https://www.pewresearch.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Taiwan-ACS-data-statement-new.pdf — Pew’s accompanying ACS-microdata methodology statement (the 「statement」 Pew published in this episode). Third-party ZH coverage below calls it a correction, not an apology.
  • Later counterparty context (no conflict with the above): https://www.pewresearch.org/race-and-ethnicity/2023/07/19/taiwanese-americans-views-of-taiwan-and-other-places/ (2023-07-19) — Pew separated the Chinese and Taiwanese samples in the 2022-23 survey; 126 raw Taiwanese interviews → effective n = 63, MoE ±12.4 pp, subgroup reporting suppressed for size.

4. 中央社 CNA (wire; vault twin already held)

  • https://www.cna.com.tw/news/firstnews/202109260189.aspx — 2021-09-26 19:17 (updated 20:25), 「美皮尤人口調查報告修正 區隔台裔與華裔分別統計」, 中央社記者舊金山綜合外電 (NBC source), 譯者張正芊／核稿林治平.
  • Vault twin: 20211020142022_2021_09_26_美皮尤人口調查報告修正_區隔台裔與華裔分別統_81e80ba38969bf88 (post_date 2021-09-26; Wayback 20211020142022). The live wire text matches the archived twin.
  • Dated facts: Pew’s updated report puts the U.S. Taiwanese population between 195,000 and 697,000; 500+ Taiwanese and Asian leaders from U.S. community organisations signed the open letter demanding an apology and an explanation, calling the merge an erasure of the Taiwanese group; TACL 公民參與事務主任 胡若涵 (Christina Hu): 「民主參與就是要有發言權。而要有發言權的第一步， 就是我們的存在要獲得承認」; TaiwaneseAmerican.org 總編輯 陳文羿 (Leona Chen), a signatory: 「我們對於原本報告的反應之一是，台灣人被剝奪代言權太久了」 and 「台灣人身分不是一個政治選擇， 而是關於我們是誰的事實」, plus her point that self-identification is 「理智上精確的資料通報」 because it determines funding.
