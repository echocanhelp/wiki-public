formosatimes.com — what this domain actually was

Answer to the identity question: there is no evidence this domain ever carried a newspaper. It was a holding / side domain of the operator of Taipics (Taiwan Pictures Digital Archive), used briefly to serve Taipics pages, and otherwise parked or squatted. Published as a domain-history note so no future pass re-archives it as a missing Taiwanese-American paper.

Operator ruling preserved (2026-11-21): formosa / formosan is a Taiwanese-American-relevant token (FAPA, Formosan Association, Formosan Journal), so the lane is kept and labelled rather than deleted — relevance and identity are separate questions, and identity is now answered from the captures themselves.

Identity Snapshot

  • Domain: formosatimes.com (also www.formosatimes.com), first Wayback capture 2003-04-02, last meaningful capture 2019-05-23.
  • Not a publication. A holding/side domain of the operator of Taipics (Taiwan Pictures Digital Archive).
  • Operator (self-identified in the captures): taipeimarc — “an American expat in Taiwan”; host credited as David Reid; Taipei enquiries handled by Charlyne Chen 陳思庭 (contacts redacted here, retained in the captures).
  • Third-party attribution: Ebisu (2017) names Taipics as maintained by Marc Plumber and David Reid; Academia Sinica MAPDAP lists taipics.com as 馬克龍’s collection of 7,000+ historical Taiwan photos.
  • Classification: MIRROR — the captured pages are another entity’s archive, so the lane contributes no content of its own (verdict mirror-only).

Why the numbers changed: these 14 pages were previously certified REAL because the lane’s own fingerprint list contained Taipics / Taiwan Pictures — i.e. the fingerprints of another entity were the only proof the pages belonged to this lane. That is the failure mode the hostile review warned about; the classifier now has a MIRROR class and REAL requires the lane’s own entity token. Consequence: this lane yields no content of its own and gets no registry row.

TAHS relevance

Taiwan historical photography (colonial-era postcards, Aboriginal life) collected by two long-term foreign residents in Taipei — Taiwan history, not Taiwanese-American diaspora content, so it fails the content-level relevance test for an archive lane. It is still a useful citation pointer: Taipics is public-domain and citable, so cite taipics.com when an image is wanted, never this domain.

Not-evidence, stated plainly

Formosan Times does not appear in the T.A. Archives bibliography of Taiwanese-American magazines/newspapers/newsletters (§09, taiwaneseamericanhistory.org). That list is community-curated and partial, so absence there is corroboration, not proof — but combined with zero self-named captures and a third-party-identified mirror, the newspaper reading is unsupported.
