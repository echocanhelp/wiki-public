The vault page’s open IDENTITY CHECK gap (St. Louis 1972 founding elder vs 美洲台灣日報 政治評論 columnist) closes: they are the same man. The card’s “later US (2020s commentary)” geography guess is superseded — he returned to Taiwan in 1990 and is 現任宜蘭羅東五福眼科院長 per multiple independent 現職 rosters.
