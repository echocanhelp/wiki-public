Conflicts with the institutional record

The subject’s Academia Sinica Institute of Physics directory states B.S., National Taiwan University, 1994; M.S., 1996; Ph.D., University of California (San Diego) — i.e. the NTU B.S. is dated 1994 and the M.S. 1996, with no year on the Ph.D. The TAH self-report instead leaves the NTU B.S. year - and puts 1994 on the UCSD M.S. and 1996 on the UCSD Ph.D. Same three degrees, same institutions, three years shifted by two years across institutions. Full transcription and both readings (here); neither is silently preferred on the person page.
