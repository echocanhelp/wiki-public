  • Verbatim content_html body (self-reported, single paragraph, 620 normalized chars): BAS is a nonprofit residential and activity space in Brooklyn, NY providing free short-term housing to Taiwanese artists visiting NYC; originally named Taiwanese Artists Center when it began operations in 2003; renamed BAS in summer 2007 at the suggestion of director Huang Ming-chuan; the facility is a two-story townhouse with a backyard built in 1919, physical infrastructure provided by the couple Huang Tsai-tian and Yang Shu-ching; annual financial support from the Oasis Foundation (founded 2009) and the Cheng Yin-cheng Memorial Fund (founded 2012); DIY, self-sufficient model to give visiting artists maximum freedom.
  • Note (English-name harmony): the directory paragraph romanizes the donors as Huang Tsai-tian / Yang Shu-ching; the Chinese post dump below renders them 黃再添 / 楊淑卿, and the corpus’s own romanization of this couple is Patrick Huang / S. C. Yang (content/people/patrick-huang.md, content/people/s-c-yang.md). The directory romanizations are transliterations of the Chinese forms, not the couple’s records’ names — do not treat them as separate people.
