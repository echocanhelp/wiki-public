（中央社記者繆宗翰台北26日電）陸委會副主委兼發言人邱垂正今天重申，「反滲透法草案」只規範影響選舉等不法行為，不針對特定對象與身分。因應「紅色滲透」台灣媒體則需另行通盤檢討，不在本次修法範圍。 大陸委員會下午舉行例行記者會，邱垂正在會中針對「反滲透法」修法，作上述說明。 邱垂正重申，反滲透法目標在於避免影響選舉、防止干預民主程序、加強維護社會秩序，針對「違法從事捐贈政治獻金、助選、遊說、破壞集會遊行及社會秩序、傳播假訊息干擾選舉」等5種受滲透從事的不法行為，補充現行法律規範的不足。

From the record

  • 2017-11-26 — In the 李明哲案 lead-up to the Nov 28 verdict, 邱垂正 as 陸委會副主委 stated the government would assist the family’s trip to mainland China. 陳昭南專欄, taiwanjustice.net
  • 2019-06-27 — Told Reuters that only amending laws to consolidate the legal framework could strengthen the national security system, responding to Beijing’s network-building in Taiwan via business incentives for pro-unification supporters. Reuters report, taiwanjustice.net
  • 2019-12-12 — As 陸委會副主委兼發言人, said at a 陸委會 press conference the 反滲透法 legislation fills the gap of punishing “在地協力者” (local collaborators) of hostile external forces, punishes only intentional acts (not negligence), and covers the five listed unlawful behaviors. 反滲透法立法, taiwanjustice.net
  • 2020-01-30 — As 陸委會發言人, defended “集中監測管理” for returning mainland students as a pandemic-safety measure without discrimination, with one-person-one-room as the principle. 返台陸生集中監測管理, taiwanjustice.net
  • 2020-01-30 — Same press conference in another record: said the Ministry of Education had asked universities to submit care plans for returning mainland students before entry would be allowed, and that mainland spouses returning from Hubei faced 14-day residence restriction while others did 14-day self-health management. 返台陸生集中監測管理, taiwanjustice.net
  • 2020-04-16 — As 陸委會發言人, said at a press conference that 張經義, a Taiwan resident working as a reporter for 東方衛視 (a PRC party/government-affiliated outlet), violated Article 33(2) of the Cross-Strait Act, and that 陸委會 would investigate with the competent authorities. 台灣人張經義擔任中國東方衛視記者, taiwanjustice.net
  • 2020-05-15 — At a regular press conference responding to 國台辦 criticism, said the government respects university autonomy and welcomes mainland students while opposing discrimination, but will not accept behavior on campus that diminishes national dignity; explained mainland campuses’ “七不講” (seven forbidden topics) makes it hard for students to appreciate Taiwan’s democratic values. 賤 ◎ 陳茂雄, taiwanjustice.net
  • 2020-07-02 — As 陸委會發言人, confirmed to CNA that two mainland 東南衛視 field reporters had their press credentials and entry permits revoked and were ordered to depart the next day for producing political programs in Taiwan. 央視等中國媒體在台製播政論節目, taiwanjustice.net
  • 2020-09-03 — Ahead of the 海峽論壇 (rumored 王金平-led delegation), said central agencies and subordinate units are barred from attending in any form, and warned the public and civil groups not to participate lightly. 傳王金平將率隊赴海峽論壇, taiwanjustice.net
  • 2020-09-03 — Same press conference in another record: said the mainland side may not hold 海峽論壇 branches or follow-up events in Taiwan, no agency, individual, group or party may take part in any form of “一國兩制台灣方案” or “民主協商” activities, and violations of the five national-security laws or the 反滲透法 will be handled according to law. 傳王金平將率隊赴海峽論壇, taiwanjustice.net
  • 2020-10-08 — As 陸委會發言人, said at a press conference that if 陳廷寵’s remarks and actions violate the purpose of government subsidies to 台商子女學校 (he is a founder and director of 華東台商子女學校), 陸委會 and the Ministry of Education would require improvement and would not rule out asking him to step down from the school’s management layer, otherwise cutting the relevant budget. 陳廷寵惹爭議, taiwanjustice.net
  • 2020-11-12 — At a 陸委會 press conference, noted polls show unfriendly public opinion toward the mainland government remains high, “also for the reference of the authorities across the strait.” 台灣陸委會民調, taiwanjustice.net
  • 2020-11-26 — As 陸委會發言人, said at the regular press conference that any revision of the rules for mainland journalists’ reporting in Taiwan would respect the jurisdiction of the competent authority, the Ministry of Culture; added that 陸委會 had notified 文化部 and some mainland media had already stopped producing political commentary programs in Taiwan, and that recording programs while on a reporting permit would be deemed a violation of the entry-permit purpose. 台灣文化部將修改中國媒體在台採訪規定, taiwanjustice.net
  • 2021-03-25 — As 陸委會發言人, said at the regular press conference that the CCP’s long-standing political manipulation and ambitions are known to the world, that it is “實有不妥” for Taiwanese artists to appear in CCP united-front promotional videos for outlets like 台海之聲 and 看台海, urged citizens not to become showcase tools of united-front propaganda and to weigh national dignity, social perception and legal requirements before such mainland trips, and said 陸委會 would investigate and punish any illegality in individual cases. 台灣藝人現身為中共統戰新媒體宣傳, taiwanjustice.net
  • 2021-04-08 — As 陸委會發言人, responded to the 向心夫婦 money-laundering indictment: respect for the judiciary handling it according to law; no comment on individual cases. 向心夫婦案, taiwanjustice.net
  • 2021-05-10 — As 陸委會副主委, told the legislature that the Taipei Economic and Cultural Office in Hong Kong had only 8 remaining Taiwan-stationed staff with visas all expiring within the year, and called for both sides to return to the 2011 exchange-of-notes consensus. 香港駐台機構暫停運作成戰場, taiwanjustice.net
