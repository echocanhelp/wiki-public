Role in the Community

The corpus records him two ways and neither is civic office. First, as a NATPA-submitted academic entry: the 2013 北美洲台灣人教授協會 annual meeting is the channel by which his Who’s Who record reached TAH, so his appearance in the TA corpus is an appearance in a Taiwanese American professionals directory — a physicist listed among professors, not an organiser, signer, or officer. Second, as a research institution member in Taiwan: 副研究員 at the Institute of Physics, Academia Sinica, a grants PI, and once a named 接待人 (host) for a 中央研究院應用科學研究中心 colloquium at the institute’s 1F 演講廳, listed by the 台灣物理學會 (posted 2025-10-15) — the only record found anywhere that puts him in an organised academic-community role rather than a job title.

Nothing in the record supports a movement-role, association-office, or diaspora-advocacy claim, and this page does not make one: Accomplishment, Publication(Non-professional ones), Hobby and Website or Blog are all - at the source. Whether he participated in TA community life beyond the NATPA listing is genuinely unknown on this box — see Gaps.
