From the record — third-party web pass 2026-11-17

Dated rows added 2026-11-17 from third-party sources archived at knowledge/web-archives/jeff-wu-thirdparty-2026-11-17/ (Georgia Tech ISyE profile page, Georgia Tech ISyE awards index, Academia Sinica 院士簡歷, en.wikipedia C. F. Jeff Wu, UC Berkeley Statistics alumni page). Where the third-party record disagrees with the TAH self-report above, both are kept and labeled.

  • 2026-11-17 (Georgia Tech ISyE faculty page, https://www.isye.gatech.edu/users/jeff-wu) — he is Professor Emeritus in the H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering (ISyE) at Georgia Tech; per the official site he served as the Coca-Cola Chair in Engineering Statistics and Professor in ISyE before retirement. This supersedes the TAH self-report row 2003–present, which was written before retirement and is stale as of 2026-11-17.
  • 2026-11-17 (en.wikipedia C. F. Jeff Wu; Wikidata Q5006097) — current post is the X. Q. Deng Presidential Chair Professorship in the School of Data Science at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen; the ISyE Coca-Cola chair tenure is given as 2003–2024. Third-party corroboration of the alleged 2004 NAE and 2000 Academia Sinica rows.
  • 1949-01-15 (Wikidata Q5006097, imported from zh.wp; per Library of Congress name authority n82162560 “b. Jan. 15, 1949” from the 2000 Experiments data sheet) — date of birth 15 January 1949, birthplace Taiwan (per en.wikipedia infobox: Hsinchu, Taiwan). Resolves the bare 1949 Era field in Identity Snapshot.
  • 1973-1976 (Georgia Tech ISyE page; Berkeley official site https://statistics.berkeley.edu/people/chien-fu-wu) — Berkeley Ph.D. program years 1973–1976, dissertation “Contributions to Optimization Theory with Applications to Optimal Design of Experiments”, advisor Peter J. Bickel, graduated 1976. Adds the advisor + dissertation title the TAH form left blank.
  • 1977-1980 / 1980-1983 / 1983-1988 (Academia Sinica 院士簡歷 official site, https://academicians.sinica.edu.tw/index.php?id=220&r=academician-n%2Fshow) — the Univ. of Wisconsin rank sequence is dated: 助教授 1977–1980, 副教授 1980–1983, 教授 1983–1988, plus 同校數學研究中心教授 1983–1986. Third-party corroboration + finer granularity than the TAH 1977-1988 combined row.
  • 2000, 第23屆 (Academia Sinica 院士簡歷) — elected Academician of Academia Sinica, 23rd class (2000), 數理科學組 (Mathematics and Physical Sciences division) — upgrades the alleged 2000 row to documented by the institution itself.
  • 2004 (Georgia Tech ISyE news page https://www.isye.gatech.edu/news/isyes-national-academy-engineering-members, dated 2021-02-01; per the Academia Sinica 簡歷) — NAE election citation: “For conceiving and building modern systems of experimental design based on contemporary methods for parameter estimating to provide quality improvements.” Upgrades the alleged 2004 NAE row to documented.
  • 1984 / 1985 / 2002 / 2009 (Georgia Tech ISyE page; Academia Sinica 簡歷) — elected Fellow of the IMS (1984), ASA (1985), ASQ (2002), INFORMS (2009). Not present in the TAH form at all.
  • 1987 (Georgia Tech ISyE page; Academia Sinica 簡歷 稱 COPSS統計學會聯合會長獎) — COPSS Presidents’ Award (“best researcher under 40”, commissioned by five statistical societies). Distinct from the 2011 COPSS Fisher Lecture Award already listed.
  • 1986 (en.wikipedia, citing Wu 1986, Application of Statistics and Management 1:1–7) — first use of the term Data Science for statistics, in a lecture given to the Chinese Academy of Sciences in Beijing (published 1986). Pre-dates the widely cited 1997 Michigan lecture.
  • 1997-11 (en.wikipedia, citing The University Records, Univ. of Michigan, 1997-11-09; Georgia Tech ISyE page) — inaugural H. C. Carver Chair lecture “Statistics = Data Science?” at the University of Michigan, where he coined/popularized the terms data science and data scientist. Same lecture delivered as the first P.C. Mahalanobis Memorial Lecture in 1998 at the Indian Statistical Institute (Georgia Tech awards index dates the Mahalanobis lectures 1999 — conflict noted, both retained).
  • 1995-1998 / 1997-2003 (en.wikipedia) — at the University of Michigan he was chair of the Department of Statistics 1995–1998 and H.C. Carver professor of statistics 1997–2003; en.wikipedia dates the Michigan faculty period 1995–2003, versus the TAH self-report’s 1993-2003 (conflict retained).
  • 2008 (Georgia Tech awards index, official site) — Shewhart Medal, American Society for Quality and Pan Wenyuan Technology Award, Taiwan (the TAH 潘文淵 row’s English form, dated), plus honorary doctorate (honoris causa) of Mathematics from Univ. of Waterloo and QPRC conference honoree, Madison WI.
  • 2011 (Georgia Tech awards index, official site) — Einstein Chair Professor, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing and Lifelong Achievement Award, Chinese Statistical Association (Taiwan) — neither in the TAH form.
  • 2012 (Georgia Tech awards index; en.wikipedia) — U.S. Army Wilks Award (“for contributions to statistical methodologies in Army research, development, and testing”), in addition to the ASA Deming Lecture Award already listed.
  • 2017 (Georgia Tech awards index, official site) — Brumbaugh Award, ASQ (also won 1992 per the same index) and George Box Medal, ENBIS — the TAH form had neither Brumbaugh year.
  • 2018 (Georgia Tech awards index, official site) — Statistician of the Year (SOY) Award, Chicago Chapter of ASA.
  • 2020 (Georgia Tech awards index; ISyE news; Sigma Xi official site https://www.sigmaxi.org/programs/prizes-awards/william-procter/award-winner/jeff-wu) — Class of 1934 Distinguished Professor Award, Georgia Tech’s highest faculty award, plus the Sigma Xi Monie A. Ferst Award, both dated 2020. Upgrades the alleged 2020 Ferst row to documented.
  • 2014-07-07/09 (en.wikipedia, citing the archived conference site) — conference “Building Statistical Methodology and Theory 2014” held at Mile, Yunnan, China, July 7–9 2014 celebrating his 65th birthday; Bradley Lecture, University of Georgia, 2014.
  • 37 / >50 / ~185 (dated snapshots, not merged) — doctoral students supervised and peer-reviewed article counts differ by source and date: 37 Ph.D. students, ~140 articles (biographies.net page submitted 2013-07-23), >50 Ph.D. students, ~185 articles (en.wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-17), >180 articles (Georgia Tech ISyE NAE news, 2021-02-01). Two books: Experiments: Planning, Analysis, and Optimization (Wiley, with M. S. Hamada) and A Modern Theory of Factorial Design (Springer, with Rahul Mukerjee), per en.wikipedia references.
  • 1983 (en.wikipedia reference list) — most-cited paper “On the Convergence Properties of the EM Algorithm”, The Annals of Statistics 11(1):95–103, 1983, doi:10.1214/aos/1176346060 — the “convergence of the EM algorithm” work en.wikipedia names him for.
  • 2026-11-17 (Library of Congress name authority, official site http://id.loc.gov/authorities/names/n82162560.html) — LC-controlled heading Wu, Chien-Fu with variants Chien-Fu, Wu / Wu, C. F. Jeff, affiliations Georgia Tech / Michigan / Wisconsin; VIAF 14850433, GND 136572855. Confirms the English-name ↔ hanzi identity bridge across all sources above.
