Role in the Community

  • Center for Career Alternatives (CCA), Seattle — multi-ethnic employment/education non-profit founded 1979 by Alan “Al” Sugiyama. Third-party press (NWAW 2010, IE 2010, NWAW 2011) names Peter Tsai as the Executive Director who succeeded the founder in 2010 and steered the agency through its 2011 closure and transfer of programs to Sea Mar — the community-role record the TAH taxonomy tag alone never evidenced. Earlier press (Queen Anne & Magnolia News 2014) and a LinkedIn profile show a Deputy Director rank with longer tenure (from 1986); see the field-level HOLD in GAPS.
  • Robert Chinn Foundation, Seattle — private 501(c)(3) (EIN 91-1364167, established 1986; robertchinnfoundation.org); he self-reports Grant Administrator, present, corroborated only by the family-supplied 2017 memorial bio.
