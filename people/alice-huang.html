Alice Huang

Story

The news keeps bringing her to our door, and this is why we open it: Alice Huang is the businesswoman at the center of an espionage allegation that directly reaches a life this archive has been recording for years — Jensen Huang (黃仁勳), the Tainan-born NVIDIA co-founder our pages follow from the TAH Who’s Who through the 追勳潮. U.S. intelligence officials believe she worked for Chinese intelligence, which Beijing rejects; no country has charged her as of 2026-11-12. Taiwan has confirmed she entered on a Hong Kong passport several times in 2024–2025 and revoked her entry permit, and its legislators are now interrogating the entry-vetting system that let her in. On 2026-11-12 NVIDIA’s newsroom announced that Jensen Huang resigned as President, CEO and a board member, effective immediately; the announcement itself states no reason. What we hold here is not a biography and not a verdict: every allegation stays labeled, every official statement is tied to the official who made it, and every 黄乐 registry fact stays separated from the unproven question of whether that registry person is Alice Huang. The full event record: 特別簡報：Alice Huang 間諜疑雲.

Identity Snapshot

  • Chinese: not asserted — reporting gives 黃樂 (Taipei Times; simplified 黄乐 in mainland registry records) as a name she “also goes by”; treated as a reported alias, not an identity confirmation, because the registry identity chain is incomplete (see From the record).
  • Geography: Shanghai; Singapore (until Feb 2026); reportedly residing in Beijing (reported)
  • Roles: described by reporting as a former executive of Megaspeed International (Singapore); a person named 黄乐 is listed in mainland registries as partner/GM of 上海和熙投资管理有限公司
  • Community tie: unresolved — external subject of interest. Archived under CONTROL §1b because the case directly reaches an archived community life (Jensen Huang 黃仁勳) and Taiwan’s entry-vetting and 反滲透法 debate. This page is an event-adjacent record, not a community membership claim.
  • Do not confuse with the archived Taiwanese American Huangs, e.g. Rev. David Huang 黃德利 or David Huang 黃茂清 — no relation; no relation is also Nvidia’s stated position between Jensen Huang and Alice Huang.
