Rev. Lai Junming (賴俊明牧師)

Rev. Lai Junming (賴俊明牧師) appears in GSTPC bulletin records as a visiting preacher in 2022–2023.

Frontier pass 2026-11-18 (card FRONTIER-11180842-27 / t_d5ca7bc0, do-not-publish) closed the biography into one documented life — 1940-07 花蓮 birth, 台神 1965, 鳳林, U.S. study 1967–1970, 雙連教會 associate pastor 1970–1984, founding pastor 大聖路易台灣人長老教會 1984–1991, 台灣聖經公會 總幹事 1991–2009, Osaka 2010 — and bridged the hanzi to the English name printed in the founding memoir’s own EN half (Rev. David Chun-Ming Lai / Rev. David Lai). Ledger + archives: frontier web pass.

Role in the Community

The TAH community memoir Our Journeys 305 (大聖路易台灣人長老教會 founding memoir) records a Rev. 賴俊明 of Taipei Shuanglian Church (雙連教會) who was interviewed in Taiwan by 張理美醫師 — the delegate sent by the founding group’s search committee — and invited to a founding-pastor position. He arrived in St. Louis in November 1984, holding a three-quarter pastor position with the remainder for doctoral studies at Eden Theological Seminary (聖路易伊典神學院). The congregation was formally registered as 大聖路易台灣人長老基督教會 in September 1988 and joined the Kinkeith–Lafayette Presbytery on 1988-10-02 — the first Taiwanese church in the US Midwest admitted to a US mainstream denomination.賴俊明牧師 was inducted as its first pastor on 1989-05-07 and immediately launched a Bible-study movement (with weekly Wednesday small-group study) that drew over half the congregation, growing membership to sixty or seventy.

HOLD: whether the GSTPC (Southern California) visiting preacher documented 2022–2023 is the same person as the 1984–1989 St. Louis founding pastor — same canonical entity key, no bridging evidence yet. The 2026-11-18 frontier pass made this identity far more plausible (a retired, U.S.-connected PCT preacher, born 1940-07, still preaching in 2022–2023 fits), but no GSTPC bulletin row carries a biographical bridge (birthplace, St. Louis, or Bible Society), so the HOLD stands open.

Further founding-memoir detail (same source, Our Journeys 305): the search committee decided to join the US mainstream denomination (美國長老教會) after consulting Rev. 黃武東牧師 — former long-time General Secretary of the Presbyterian Church in Taiwan — then verified that finances met presbytery standards for a full-time pastor before inviting 賴俊明. After his arrival the congregation moved worship to the parlor of First Presbyterian Church in Kirkwood (克可伍德市第一長老教會) to allow Sunday-morning services. When the session (小會) was formed it comprised the pastor and six elders: 高銘憲、林逸民、張理美、陳克充、鄭紀昭、蘇希三.

Story

1940-07 出生於花蓮的客家庄，父親賴彌梯是第一代信徒；母親重病期間經歷教會陪伴，讓他立志進入神學院。1965 年自臺灣神學院畢業，因兩年的服務慣例放棄耶魯的攻讀計畫，自願到東部，在花蓮鳳林教會傳道約兩年。隨後赴美，在 Lexington Theological Seminary 取得教會史碩士，並短暫在 Luther Theological Seminary 就讀，1970 年返台，應高俊明之邀在玉山神學院短期任教。1970 到 1984 年與陳溪圳一起在台北雙連教會服事十四年；期間他主持建堂，並自 1980 年起推動家庭小組。1984 年全家赴美，經張理美醫師訪談後受邀前往大聖路易台灣人長老基督教會，1989-05-07 該會為他舉行禮拜，1991-08-17 Ries Rd 教堂獻堂。此後他的工作轉向聖經翻譯與推廣，2006-12-03 曾以「台灣聖經公會總幹事 賴俊明」署名致函台北和平教會，感謝會友四年為原住民族語、台語與客語聖經奉獻。2009-12 卸任後，2010 年起前往大阪中華基督教長老會協助傳道，目前往來於台灣與美國之間。

Historical Significance

Supported worship and special events including Father’s Day and regular Sunday services.

Beyond the GSTPC pulpit, the frontier pass (2026-11-18) documents a four-stage public ministry: (1) building pastor of 台北雙連教會 1970–1984 — first Taipei Presbyterian church to worship in a multi-storey building, and initiator of the 家庭小組 (household small-group) movement from 1980 that seeded small-group practice in the PCT; (2) founding/organizing pastor of 大聖路易台灣人長老基督教會 1984–1991 — first U.S.-Taiwanese church to purchase land and self-build its own sanctuary (the ‘台’-shaped building at 542 Ries Rd, Ballwin, dedicated 1991-08-17, per the church’s own site; the ZH memoir prints “542 Res Road”, a typo); (3) 總幹事 of 台灣聖經公會 (then 中華民國聖經公會) 1991–2009 — presided over financial independence from foreign subsidies and return of Taiwan Traditional-Chinese Bible rights from 1996, the 2003 name restoration to 台灣聖經公會, mother-tongue Bibles (阿美/布農/魯凱/泰雅爾 etc.), and the 2004 牛仔聖經 youth campaign (1,100+ youth, 57 churches); (4) assisting pastorate at 大阪中華基督教長老會 from 2010.
