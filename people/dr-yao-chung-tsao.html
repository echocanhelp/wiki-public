Role in the Community

The honest reading of this corpus is that Dr. Tsao’s community footprint is the directory entry itself. He is recorded in it, the entry’s Subjects block backlinks this page, and the source corpus hub lists him among the profile pages it publishes (|taiwaneseamericanhistory.org) — that listing, plus the |TAH Foundation mission the profile page itself quotes (“to document the lives and achievements of Taiwanese Americans who are leaving their mark”), is the whole of his documented presence in the archive. His is one of the profiles the 2026-08-17 harvest grader marked thin (weak.person), which is consistent with a record whose birth, arrival and date fields are all -. What the entry does establish is a documented professional trajectory inside the Taiwanese American professional cohort the foundation exists to record: a psychology degree from National Taiwan University, an industrial-engineering and human-cognition path through SUNY/Buffalo, an associate professorship at Rensselaer, then usability engineering at AT&T Labs and Telcordia, with a Human Factors and Ergonomics award claimed on top. That is a life documented by the community’s own archive rather than a life that organised it, and this page says so instead of inflating it into civic office. The one candidate for community activity outside the directory — a 2024 柑縣台灣同鄉會 / TAP-OC volunteer roster at the Irvine Global Village Festival naming his wife’s name — was checked against the record’s New Jersey residence and excluded; see Gaps.
