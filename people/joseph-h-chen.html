Role in the Community

  • Came to Ithaca, NY in 1965 for doctoral study at Cornell University; served as president of the Ithaca/Cornell Taiwanese community organization (伊薩卡同鄉會, later 康大臺灣同鄉同學會), which under his generation grew to nearly 300 members including families.
  • Per his wife 程美希 Mei-Hsi Chen’s memoir of the association’s history, the group’s work centered on 同鄉互愛、互助: easing homesickness, cooking Taiwanese food, welcoming new students and sending off graduates, and handling weddings and celebrations. In the 1960s–70s the association conducted itself in Taiwanese.
  • Married 程美希 in 1967, the same community circle the memoir documents.
  • Corpus record: 2015（英文 counterpart: ourjourneys310-eng） — his own TAH encyclopedia entry 05.
  • 2001 — linked 李登輝 to the 「李總統杯」 / President Lee Cup golf tournament in Upstate New York; the club’s own history names him as the go-between and records that he and Lee were friends and golf partners when Lee did his Cornell Ph.D. — [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/44-upstate-new-york-taiwanese-golf-club|TAH #44 (陳仲欽, 02/2016)]]. HOLD: the club history asserts the Chen–Lee friendship/partnership at Cornell; no independent record on this box documents it, and the #410 self-report does not mention it either way.
  • His 1965 arrival is the founding anchor his wife’s chapter history gives for the TAA Ithaca NY chapter lineage — carried on TAA (Our Journeys #310 row), Cornell TASA, Cornell CTSA and Cornell CTAS. HOLD: continuity between the 1960s–70s 康大臺灣同鄉同學會 and today’s registered Cornell student organizations is asserted by locality, not documented in the corpus.
  • 同姓 different-person check: Steven Shih-chen 陳世政 (TAH Who’s Who #178) appears in the Joseph Chen grep because of romanization, not relation — no source on this box links them. Never asserted.
