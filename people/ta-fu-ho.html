Ta-Fu Ho (何達夫博士)

Identity Snapshot

  • English: Ta-Fu Ho
  • Chinese: 何達夫博士
  • Era: 1942 -1980
  • Geography: Japan
  • Core roles: engineer
  • Source: TAH Foundation Who’s Who

Education

  • Tainan Frist (i-Jo) High School — B.S.
  • Cheng Kong Univ. — 1964 — Electric Engineer. BS
  • Drexel Univ. — 1977 — Ph.D., 電機工程

Employment

  • Fisher and Porter — 1977-1980 — Senior Design Engineer

Family

  • Wife: 杜智惠
  • Son: Charles Ho

Role in the Community

  • Remembered through the Ho Ta-Fu Memorial Foundation Scholarship Awards (何達夫紀念基金會獎學金), the community’s enduring tribute to him — see 34. 何達夫紀念基金會獎學金 (2015). A memorial foundation implies he passed before 2015; the Who’s Who employment table ends at 1980 — no death date in the corpus, so no date asserted.
  • Who’s Who profile record: 1264. Ta-Fu Ho 何達夫 (2016).
  • According to his wife 杜智惠’s memoir 「謝謝您們–親愛的朋友」 (published 09/2016, printed in Taiwan Tribune 04/02/1994), he joined the 台灣獨立運動 in the early 1970s in Philadelphia — “獻身台灣獨立運動” per the widow’s account — and his Philadelphia-era circle included 台獨聯盟 同志 and friends such as 林尚武, who brought a condolence envelope of US$9,400 in checks after his death (per 杜智惠 2016, restatement of her own 1994 article; single-source).
  • Listed among the Philadelphia Taiwanese-America activist circle (1969–1972) in 張郁彬’s memoir 「我的人生旅程」 (03/2018) — “何達夫和杜智惠” appear in the roster of 常往來的朋友 around 周祖堯/林和美’s home — and the memoir recounts his 1980-06 death from liver disease and 杜智惠 later founding the 何達夫基金 (per 張郁彬 2018; third-party account consistent with the widow’s memoir).

  • 1974 — 會長 (president) of the 大費城台灣同鄉會 (Taiwanese Association of America, Greater Philadelphia Chapter / TAAGP) — his name is printed in the chapter’s own 歷屆會長 roster between 蘇滕宗 (1973) and 陳弘毅 (1975): Source: TAA-50 特刊 大費城台灣同鄉會歷屆會長 table, https://taiwan.ucsd.edu/gallery/TAA-50_Chapter-E_part7.pdf (pdftotext row 1974 何達夫). This is the first third-party, office-bearing fact on the page and it is the document that fills the TAH form’s blank Accomplishment field; the same roster dates the predecessors 歐炯雄 1971 / 何昇龍 1972 / 蘇滕宗 1973 and successors 邱文雄 1977 / 賴滄海 1978, matching the activist circle named in 張郁彬’s memoir.
  • 1977 — Drexel Ph.D. dissertation title, page count and deposit record: 「Transmission Network Expansion Planning for Maximum Reliability within a Specified Budget」, x + 309 pages, Ph.D., Drexel University, Philadelphia. Source: Drexel University Center for Electric Power Engineering thesis roster https://research.coe.drexel.edu/ece/power/theses/ (verbatim 1977 row Ta Fu Ho, "Transmission Network Expansion Planning for Maximum Reliability within a Specified Budget," PhD. Thesis, 1977.); DOI https://doi.org/10.17918/00004656; Drexel Esploro record Ho_Ta-Fu_1977 (8.14 MB PDF, restricted — viewable on request from archives@drexel.edu, so the full text is not open on this box). This fills the blank TAH Publication field with his one documented scholarly work.
  • Name form: Ta-Fu Jim Ho — the middle name Jim is printed by the Drexel doctoral deposit record (Esploro + DOI 10.17918/00004656) and the CEPE roster as Ta Fu Ho, none of which carries the hanzi; the TAH form and the two memoirs carry 何達夫 without Jim. No single document on this box co-prints 何達夫 and Ta-Fu Jim Ho, so the two name forms are recorded as a stated identity chain — Drexel + 1977 + 電機工程 + Philadelphia match the self-report exactly — and not as a merge.
  • 何達夫紀念基金會 is a live co-sponsor of the 陳文成教授紀念基金會「社會關懷獎學金」, which the 陳文成 foundation says has run annually since 2014 (its own 官網: 「過去多年來與美國何達夫紀念基金會合作」). Source: Professor Chen Wen-Chen Memorial Foundation https://www.cwcmf.org/index.php/event/social-care-scholarship and the 2026 call https://www.cwcmf.org/index.php/event/scholarship-application/2026 (理事長劉斌碩, 2026-05; 2026 = 「第12年」). This supersedes the 2026-11-14 finding 「no further citable source on this box」 for the foundation’s ongoing activity, which was a corpus-local grep claim rather than a web check.
  • TAH Outreach roster prints the foundation’s own web address http://CWCMF.net alongside 「Ho Ta-Fu Memorial Foundation 何達夫紀念基金會 / North America」. Source: TAH Outreach Book Project PDF http://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Outreach-Book-Project-for-web.pdf (row 6) and the work page https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/34-ho-ta-fu-memorial-foundation-scholarship-awards/ (2 awards × $1500, North America, Status: Unknown). Dated snapshot: the CWCMF.net pointer is the joint 陳文成/何達夫 scholarship site, not a standalone 何達夫 foundation site.
  • 1994–2001 award amounts attested at $1,500 per recipient — 張郁彬 records receiving 「為數各1500元」 for two years from 負責人 杜智惠 for her daughter Brianna, which independently corroborates the Prizes · $1500 / Recipient line on the TAH work page rather than resting on it alone. Source: https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/mystories630/ (張郁彬「我的人生旅程」03/2018, originally in 張炎憲/曾秋美 編《青春逐夢台灣國系列 1——一門留美學生的建國故事》, 吳三連台灣史料基金會, 2009-02, pp. 253–314).
  • Chapter-level corroboration that his Philadelphia circle was the TAAGP leadership cohort — the chapter’s own 簡介 by 王博文 (Powen Wang, 10/2017) dates the Formosan Club of Philadelphia founding to 1960, the 1977 constitution to 連擇仁 (1976) / 邱文雄 (1977) / 吳明雄牧師, and confirms 歐炯雄 and 李儒英 as earlier presidents — the same 1971–1975 president bracket his own seat sits in. Source: https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/project-3-17-eng/ (and ZH twin https://taiwaneseamericanhistory.org/project-3-17/). Note: the roster rows for 1968, 1969, 1979, 1980 and 1982 are blank in the published table, so the chapter’s own record is itself incomplete.

Family

  • Wife: 杜智惠 — memoirist (「謝謝您們–親愛的朋友」), founder of the 何達夫紀念基金會, credited alongside 鍾林瑛娟律師 and 陳文成基金會 in the scholarship’s establishment.
