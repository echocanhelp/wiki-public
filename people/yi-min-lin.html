林逸民

Taiwanese physician and movement figure; current political commentator for 美洲台灣日報.

Story

林逸民 is a physician whose record runs from the founding circles of the Taiwanese Christian community in St. Louis in the early 1970s — where he, with 張世聰, 翁秀惠, 高銘憲 and others, began the discussions that formed the believers’ community TAH — to the present, writing political commentary for 美洲台灣日報 on Taiwan’s defense and identity. He served as an elder of the St. Louis church (Greater St. Louis Taiwanese community records; church history [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/ourjourneys305|TAH #305]]) and chaired its first building-committee meeting.

Per the 2026-11-19 frontier web pass he is the Taiwan-based ophthalmologist 林逸民醫師 (b. 1943): trained and in practice in St. Louis (Washington University; 華大愛爾敦紀念醫院眼科主任) through the 1970s–80s founding and elder years, then returned to Taiwan to lead 羅東五福眼科; his later commentary career runs under the bylines 福和會／台灣聯合國協進會理事長、總統府國策顧問. See From the web record.
