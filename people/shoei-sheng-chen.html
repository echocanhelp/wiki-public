  • 1985-01-01 — elected Fellow of the American Society of Mechanical Engineers, fellow section Orange County — per ASME Fellows roster PDF https://www.asme.org/getmedia/8d4d7383-acb2-4ef9-a748-b6ba1922584a/fellows-all-updated-september-2025_1.pdf (also fellows-all-2014.pdf prints Shoei-sheng Chen 1/1/1985 Orange County).
