  • 1993-06-26/27 — Chaired (主持) the 「制憲運動」 breakout session at 美西台灣人夏令會 (TACWC) at Stanford: as 台獨聯盟美國本部主席楊宗昌 had to go to Washington for the 「台灣新世紀」研討會, the session hosted by 台獨聯盟 came under 台獨聯盟中央委員洪基隆博士, who 「首先闡述國民主權原理，強調國民有自己制定憲法和建造國家的權利」; 洪基隆等 also fielded the floor Q&A.   • 1993-05-12 — Program listing for 1993美西台灣人夏令會 (6/25–27, Stanford) names 洪基隆 among the hosts of the 分組討論 sessions (with 陳明春、宋泉盛、王景聰、劉顯榮、賴淑遠、施天墩). Source: 台灣公論報 第1157期 5/12/1993, same archive file tacwc-1993.
  • 2009-04 — 陳儀深《海外台獨運動相關人物口述史》(TAH publication #1023) 主訪者序 lists 洪基隆 among the 「比較獨立批判」 interviewees (alongside 許永華), i.e. interviewed for the oral history of the overseas 台獨/台灣人運動. Source: publications1023 / [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/publications1023|TAH #1023]].
  • 2011-07-01~03 — At TACWC 美西夏令會 at California State University San Diego, 洪基隆 presented the program 「台灣第一」 (program listing). Source: tacwc-2011 / TACWC 2011.
  • 2020-03 — 歷史學者黃介山 (interviewed by 何義麟, 《臺灣風物》70卷1期, 149–172頁) recalls: 「有一位灣區的朋友洪基隆，他曾批評Wolf等美國人類學者，認為他們不該用中國來包裝臺灣研究，而且沒有真正了解臺灣。」 Third-party record of Hung’s介入 in the 臺灣地方社會研究 vs. 中國研究 debate. Source: my-stories-738 / [[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/my-stories-738|TAH #738]].
  • 1943 — birth year and 台中縣 birthplace, per 今周刊 profile 「洪基隆小檔案 出生：1943年，台中縣」 (2004-03-25). The TAH self-report prints Birth Year --; the magazine profile is third-party but a single editable record → alleged. Source: k-l-hung-thirdparty-2026-11-16.
  • 1965 (民國54年) — 成功大學化學系 54級 (graduating class), per 成大校友中心講座 article 「洪基隆從台灣出發 建構全球化生技公司」 (chemical dept. class of 54, i.e. 1965). After graduation he stayed at NCKU two years as 助教 doing research with professor 田明朋, then moved to UT Austin-area (德州大學) for the chemistry M.S. documented (institution alumni publication). Source: capture.
  • 1975 — Ph.D. chemistry, UC Berkeley, per 中興大學理學院 興理講座 announcement 2013-03-06: 「洪基隆董事長於1975年獲得柏克萊加州大學化學博士」 — the exact doctorate year the self-report omits. documented (university news page). Source: capture.
  • Post-doctorate — 史丹福大學麻醉系 博士後研究 (Stanford anesthesia postdoc), per 成大校友 article; 今周刊 小檔案 also lists 「史丹佛大學博士後研究」. Two independent third-party records agree → documented. Also per 今周刊: first paper published in PNAS, and he visited Robert Burns Woodward’s Harvard lab before graduating. Sources: capture.
  • 1979 — appointed 科學顧問 (scientific advisor) to 加州大學舊金山分校微脂體研究實驗室 (UCSF Liposome Research Laboratory) by 「微脂體教父」 Demetrios Papahadjopoulos per 今周刊 profile — third-party corroboration of the self-reported UCSF 研究員 role. The same profile calls him 微脂體研究實驗室的主持人 (director), a rank above the self-report’s 研究員 — see field-level HOLD in GAPS. alleged (single magazine record). Source: capture.
  • 1991 — As 秘書長 to 台獨聯盟美國本部主席楊宗昌 (1991–1994 tenure), 洪基隆 wrote to U.S. Rep. Nancy Pelosi (裴洛西) on the blacklist 返鄉權 and jailed 台獨 activists; Pelosi then wrote to the 李登輝 government (her 1992-03-09 letter, per 總統府檔案 reported by 沃草/watchout.tw). Corroborated independently by 陳南天’s memorial essay 「懷念楊宗昌」 (Newtalk 2023-08-22 / pourquoi 報呱), 獨盟 WUFI 悼林哲夫 article (2021), and 新台灣和平基金會 「一〇〇行動聯盟成立」 retrospective — ≥3 independent institutions agree → documented. Source: capture.
  • 1997 — co-founded Hermes Biosciences, Inc. (美商赫密生生技) in California and served as its Chief Scientific Officer 1998–2005; co-inventor and patent holder of Hermes’ liposomal irinotecan Onivyde (later Merrimack 2009, Ipsen 2017). Also served as scientific advisor to Sequus Pharmaceuticals (founded by Papahadjopoulos; developer of Doxil, acquired by ALZA 1998). documented from the SEC Form 424B4 prospectus (TLC IPO, filed 2018-11-26); 今周刊 2004 corroborates the Hermes chairmanship while TLC’s own corporate timeline dates the U.S. entity setup to 2005 — see HOLD in GAPS. Source: capture.
  • 1997-11-10 — founded 台灣微脂體股份有限公司 (Taiwan Liposome Company, TLC); the SEC prospectus + 今周刊 give founding year 1997 and the initial capital as USD 1,000,000 (成大 article; 今周刊 prints 資本額 7250萬); TRPMA association member page and the company 年報 print 成立日期 中華民國86年11月10日 (1997-11-10) — date documented (two institutions incl. audited annual report). Earlier corporate history per 年報 company timeline: Lipo-Dox preclinical/clinical work at 台大醫院 1997–2001, 國家新創事業獎優選獎 2002, Lipo-Dox 健保給付卵巢癌 2002, GMP 量產工廠 + 南港遷址 2003, 第一屆台北生技獎技術移轉獎金獎 + NanoVNB Phase I 2004. Sources: capture.
  • 2005 — personally awarded 經濟部產業科技發展獎個人成就獎 (MOEA Industrial Technology Development Award, Individual Achievement), per TLC official timeline + 2019 年報 + 台湾原住民族電視/《台湾》Panorama 2005-12 article 「決勝新時代──基本功」 which names him 「台灣人微脂體之父」 winner of the same award — documented (≥3 independent publications). Same year: 第二屆台北生技獎研發創新獎金獎; TLC Biopharmaceuticals, Inc. established in the U.S. documented (official timeline + 年報 agree). Source: capture.
  • 2012-12 — 台灣微脂體 上櫃 (TPEx listing), per 中興大學 2013-03 lecture announcement 「台灣微脂體於2012年12月掛牌上櫃，目前已是新藥股王」 documented (institution page contemporaneous). Source: capture.
  • 2013-03-22 — keynote 「從台灣出發 邁向全球」 at 國立中興大學理學院 興理講座 (14:00–15:30, 理學大樓 1A03). documented (university announcement). Source: capture.
  • 2013 (~6月) — keynote 「洪基隆從台灣出發 建構全球化生技公司」 at 成功大學 產業科技名人講座 (返校演講, 15th of that month), per 成大校友中心 article — the same 從台灣出發 title. documented (alma mater publication). Source: capture.
  • 2018-11-26 — TLC BioSciences priced its Nasdaq U.S. IPO (Form 424B4, ticker TLCD); prospectus states 「We were founded in 1997 by Dr. Keelung Hong」 and 「Dr. Hong has over 35 years of experience in liposome science」. 2021 私有化下櫃 (take-private delisting, PAG-led). 2024/2025: relisted in Taiwan as TLC-KY (7924) 興櫃; official 官網 leadership page and investing.com profile list him as 創辦人、董事長暨執行長 (Chairman; CEO post held by 葉志鴻 since 2021 per company page and investing.com 任期 2021) — the 官網 currently names him 董事長暨執行長 while the management page lists 葉志鴻 as 執行長: absorbed as separate dated rows, title HOLD in GAPS. SEC filing documented. Source: capture.
  • 2026-10 — internal-person shareholding declaration (TLC-KY 內部人持股, via toalpha.tw mirror of 觀測站 data): 董事長本人 洪基隆 2,434張 (1.82%). Corporate filing derivative → sourced; primary 公開觀測站 capture pending. Source: capture.

Note 2019 年報 officer row 「加州大學化學系博士」 and TLC 官網 「微脂體先驅 Papahadjopoulos…Doxil 和 Onivyde」 both tie on employer + field + company. Source: capture.
