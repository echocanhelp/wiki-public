Role in the Community

  • Her life story is held in the TAHS community archive: “1017. Marion M Lee 李明瑱” was published 2016-05 ([[works/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/whoswho1017|TAHS #1017]]). No further narrative detail is available in the local corpus (bibliographic record only).
  • NATWA 第六屆年會 speakers (1994). Per the 北美洲台灣婦女會聯誼通訊 第十期 (record dated 1994/01), she was listed among the speakers of the 第六屆 NATWA 年會 in Kansas City (April 21–24, theme 「理財」) alongside 顧燕翎, 賴聰域 et al. — per knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/666-%e5%8c%97%e7%be%8e%e6%b4%b2%e5%8f%b0%e7%81%a3%e5%a9%a6%e5%a5%b3%e6%9c%83%e8%81%af%e8%aa%bc%e9%80%9a%e8%a8%8a-%e7%ac%ac%e5%8d%81%e6%9c%9f%e5%8c%97%e7%be%8e%e6%b4%b2%e5%8f%b0%e7%81%a3%e5%a9%a6.md (01) — documented (third-party organizational record).
  • 「婦女保健」 health workshop (1993–94). The same NATWA 通訊 records that her 「婦女保健」 talk at the 婦女領導訓練週末營 (1993-10-14→17, Aptos Beach, ~30 members, organized with the 北加分會 under 李素秋) was 受歡迎 and would be shared at the annual conference — per the same 1994/01 record — documented (third-party organizational record).
  • 第二屆台灣文化節 exhibition planning (1994). In 李華林’s first-person retrospective 「台灣文化節94’是同鄉合作的成果」 (published on the 台灣文化節 page, record dated 2015-12-15), 李明瑱 is listed among the 同鄉 who participated in the 室內文化展覽籌劃 for the 1994 festival at Mitchell Park, Palo Alto (~3,000 attendees) — per knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/taiwanese-american-cultural-festival-sf.md (Taiwanese American Cultural Festival SF) — documented (third-party bylined account).
  • Professor of epidemiology, UCSF and Stanford (2002). The author-bio block of her invited editorial 「Breast cancer in Asian women」 (Western Journal of Medicine 176(2), 2002-03) states: “Marion M Lee is professor of epidemiology at the University of California, San Francisco, and at Stanford University School of Medicine. She is a nutritional epidemiologist whose work has focused almost entirely on cancer. Her particular interest is in the conduct of comparative epidemiologic studies involving Chinese populations in different parts of the world” — per knowledge/web-archives/marion-m-lee-thirdparty-2026-11-16/pmc1071672-wjm2002.html — documented (third-party publisher bio; independently corroborates the self-reported UCSF employment and the 營養學 education track).
  • Co-investigator, Marin Breast Cancer Watch adolescent risk-factor study (1999). The Marin Breast Cancer Watch newsletter 「Keeping Abreast」 (Summer 1999) reports the $500,000 California Breast Cancer Research Program study team “consists of UCSF epidemiologists Dr. Margaret Wrensch and Dr. Marion Lee, along with five Marin Breast Cancer Watch members” (600 Marin County women, half with breast cancer) — per knowledge/web-archives/marion-m-lee-thirdparty-2026-11-16/zerobreastcancer-1999-summer.pdf — documented (third-party community-organization record).
  • Advisory board member, 新希望華人癌症關懷基金會 / New Hope Chinese Cancer Care Foundation. Her name 「李明瑱教授」 (listed “Marion Lee, PhD 李明瑱教授”) appears on the foundation’s team page under Advisory Board — per knowledge/web-archives/marion-m-lee-thirdparty-2026-11-16/newhopecancer-team.html (fetched 2026-11-16) — documented (third-party organizational roster; identity-consistent on EN+Chinese name, PhD, Bay Area, cancer field).
  • Committee member, 北美台美專業人員協會 / North American Taiwan Professionals Association (NATPA). She (listed 「李明瑱 Marion Lee」) is named on the 黃界清 Prof. Liao Shutsung Memorial & Research Awards Committee on the association’s leadership page — per knowledge/web-archives/marion-m-lee-thirdparty-2026-11-16/natpa-officers.html (fetched 2026-11-16) — documented (third-party organizational roster; identity-consistent on EN+Chinese name).
