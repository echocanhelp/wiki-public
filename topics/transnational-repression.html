跨國鎮壓 / Transnational Repression

Tier E event record. The community is a party to this: people of Taiwanese and Hong Kong origin living in the United States, Canada and Europe are surveilled, coerced, or attacked on behalf of the PRC state. This page is not commentary on the news — it is the archive’s own record of a pattern we have documented since 2021, assembled from our own holdings so that any future story lands on a spine we already have.

Why this belongs in Echopedia

Per CONTROL §1b, the Tier D↔E discriminator is whether the community is a party, not whether the story is famous. It is:

  • The targets are members of, or adjacent to, the diaspora we record — 台灣人 abroad, 僑界, Hong Kong residents in U.S. cities, and family members of public figures.
  • The operations occur on soil where our members live: Los Angeles, the San Gabriel Valley, Toronto, university towns across the U.S.
  • Our archive carried the evidence before the current cycle made it a headline.

Labeling standard: every claim below carries its status. Sourced = a document in this archive. Reported = press assertion we have not independently verified. Alleged = accusation in a filing or statement, recorded with attribution and without a finding of fact. Nothing is omitted for being uncomfortable; nothing is asserted beyond what the record holds.

The pattern, as our own records show it

2021 — “獵捕” of Taiwanese worldwide

Sourced. 強制送中，國際組織：中國在全世界「獵捕」台灣人 records international organizations’ finding that Taiwan nationals are pursued abroad. The framing is not “influence” but capture.

2022 — religious communities targeted under 天網 / 獵狐

Sourced. 中國通過”天網""獵狐”跨國鎮壓宗教團體成員 — the operation against religious-group members abroad, filed under the 天網/獵狐 framework; the archive’s own title records 美國際宗教自由委員會 naming 習近平 in its criticism.

2023 — the legislative response begins

Sourced. 美兩黨參議員提案打擊中國跨境鎮壓行動 — bipartisan Senate action, i.e. this is a recognized state-to-state problem, not an immigrant-community rumor.

Sourced. 加拿大警方稱已關閉與”中國警察站”有關的”非法警務活動” — Toronto. Physical police stations operated on Canadian soil.

Sourced. 「不支持言論自由，就別來上學」普渡大學校長公開信 — the campus vector: students harassing peers over political speech, with the pressure traced to PRC-aligned organizing.

2024 — sanctions as the countermeasure; retaliation against individuals

Sourced. 許穎婷推制裁法案觸怒港府 遭懸賞百萬通緝 — a legislator of our region facing a bounty. This is the community as a party in the most literal sense.

Sourced. 習近平訪美期間許多抗議人士被圍毆 — physical assault on protesters during a state visit; human-rights groups sought hearings.

2025 — the multilateral layer

Sourced. 跨國調查揭北京鎮壓滲透全球 聯合國淪中國打壓異己工具 — an investigative body of work, not one outlet’s account.

Sourced. 香港通過基本法23條立法 跨國近80位政界人士譴責

Sourced. 區域宗教自由論壇：台灣是中共遂行「再教育」下一目標

Sourced. 美國務院報告：中共戰狼及假帳號充斥網絡空間 — the information-operation layer that precedes and accompanies physical action.
