Event

The report covers the 第廿七屆 2018 國際春季園遊會 (27th International Spring Festival):

  • 2018-04-21 — the festival was held at North Penn 高中 (North Penn High School), 費城西北郊 Lansdale, PA; 賓州州眾議員 Joe M Hoeffel attended, and 賓州州長亞太裔事務執行長 Tiffany Chang Lawson attended 代表州長 and read the governor’s congratulatory letter (per the archived report knowledge/web-archives/taiwaneseamericanhistory-org/posts/international-spring-festival-philadelphia.md).
  • 2018-04-21 — 大費城臺灣同鄉會 participated 自第一屆起, with 「創始人之一」 徐學猷 and, under 會長 謝已, 黃小芳、李儒英、蔡美演、盧主義、翁進治 staffing the Taiwan booth (same archived report).
  • 2018-05-06 — the same 李彥輝 report was published by 大紀元: epochtimes.com/b5/18/5/5/n10365125.htm (simplified twin /gb/), 責任編輯 良克霖 — fetched copy knowledge/web-archives/isf-philadelphia-frontier-11180842-30/epochtimes-b5-n10365125.html; body word-for-word identical to the TAH post ⇒ same-source corroboration, not a second independent account.
  • 2026 (current state, organizer self-description) — the festival’s official host is the International Festival Foundation (P.O. Box 1017, Lansdale, PA 19446), internationalfestivalfoundation.org/case/international-spring-festival/, fetched 2026-11-18; it calls the 2026 edition (Saturday 2026-04-18, North Penn HS, 1340 S. Valley Forge Rd) the 37th. HOLD: 第廿七屆 2018 vs 37th 2026 do not reconcile as one series (2018+8≠37); edition counts kept separate per source.
