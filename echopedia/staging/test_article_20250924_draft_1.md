# Test Article 2025-09-24

## Overview
This article serves as a sanity check for the Echopedia style guide, demonstrating proper formatting, citation style, and internal linking. It is intended as a test article and does not correspond to any real-world topic.

## Historical Context
The concept of test articles has been used in collaborative encyclopedias to verify compliance with style guidelines and technical standards. Early examples date back to the early wiki experiments of the 2000s, where sandbox pages were used to validate markup and citation practices [[Sandbox conventions]].

## Significance
Test articles help maintain quality control by ensuring that contributors adhere to established formatting rules, citation practices, and linking conventions. They provide a low-risk environment for new contributors to learn the project’s workflow without affecting core content.
