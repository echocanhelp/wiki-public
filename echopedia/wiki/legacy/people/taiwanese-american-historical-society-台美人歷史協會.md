## 三年五大志業與目標 (2014–2016)

**FIVE PROJECTS ON THE AGENDA OF THE TAIWANESE AMERICAN HISTORICAL SOCIETY FOR 2014-2016**

1. Writing, editing, and publishing the history of the Taiwanese American as an ethnic group.
2. Installing and managing a website to record and acknowledge the achievements and contributions of individual Taiwanese Americans and to enrich America’s multicultural fabric.
3. Sponsoring and holding panel discussions, symposia, speeches, forums, and seminars to study and interpret history and historical events in the making relating to Taiwan or Taiwanese American communities.
4. Helping organize younger generations of the Taiwanese American to study how they become fully assimilated and become members of the mainstream societies of America.
5. Planning and building a Taiwanese American Historical and Cultural Compound/Center.

## 會章 Bylaws

**Name**  
台美人歷史協會  
Taiwanese American Historical Society

**Purpose**
- Collect and preserve documents and historical materials related to Taiwanese Americans.
- Publish works on Taiwanese American history and culture.
- Conduct research and academic activities.
- Promote education and public understanding of Taiwanese American heritage.

**Membership**  
Membership is not open to the general public.

**Organization**  
Governed by a Board of Directors (5–25 members).

**Officers**  
- President  
- Secretary  
- Treasurer  
(Terms: one year)
