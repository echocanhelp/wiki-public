# Chapter III: Formosan in the United States（第三章：在美國的台灣人）

Source chapter page for toward-a-community-of-hope.

## Chapter Summary
Chapter III is the dissertation's migration hinge: it moves from homeland context to diaspora reality. The chapter frames Taiwanese movement to the U.S. as structurally constrained rather than purely elective, captured by the phrase “modern style of exodus.”

For Echopedia, this chapter is crucial because it documents how educational migration, political conditions, and settlement uncertainty together shaped early Taiwanese American communal formation.

## Why Chapter III Matters for TAHS
- It offers near-period language for first-generation migration pressures.
- It provides source-grounded framing for student migration and low return rates.
- It connects social displacement to later church and identity institution-building.

## Migration Logic (Analytical Frame)
1. **Push conditions** in Taiwan/Formosa (political/social pressure).
2. **Educational channel** (graduate study as migration pathway).
3. **Retention in the U.S.** (very low return rate in the chapter's account).
4. **Diaspora reconstitution** through church/community structures.
