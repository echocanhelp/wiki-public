# 2025 Miss Taiwanese American Pageant (台美小姐)

**2025 Miss Taiwanese American Pageant (台美小姐)** was listed in the Taiwan Center event archive as a media-news item and public cultural event. The official page frames the pageant as a platform where Taiwanese American young women demonstrate cultural identity, talent, public voice, and community leadership.

## Event Snapshot

- Chinese title: 2025 台美小姐決選新聞報導
- English descriptive title: 2025 Miss Taiwanese American Pageant
- Date listed by Taiwan Center: August 3, 2025
- Event context: Miss Taiwanese American / 台美小姐
- Themes: Taiwanese American youth, cultural ambassadorship, public voice, identity, media visibility

## Event Description

The official page states that the pageant attracted public media attention and presented contestants as combining intelligence, beauty, cultural knowledge, and public voice. It describes contestants participating in talent performance and impromptu questions, and frames the winners as cultural goodwill ambassadors for Taiwanese American community occasions.

The page identifies 王怡文 / Ami Wang as the 2025 queen in media references and mentions coverage by outlets including World Journal, Taiwan Daily, Epoch Times, Sing Tao Daily, and others.

## Cultural Significance

Within the Taiwan Center public record, the Miss Taiwanese American Pageant is presented not only as a pageant but also as a stage for younger Taiwanese Americans to articulate identity, build community relationships, and represent Taiwanese American culture in public settings.
