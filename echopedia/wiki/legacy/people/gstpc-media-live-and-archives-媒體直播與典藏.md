## Media Ministry Signals
The Media page records multi-year church media outputs (music, conference segments, drama, audio/video materials), including entries from 2010s to 2019+.

## Live Worship Channels
Two distinct worship-stream archive endpoints are publicly visible:
- Worship Live: ongoing Sunday service archive with many pages of entries.
- TPC 主日禮拜網路直播: additional stream archive with dated event listings.

## Prayer and Bulletin Distribution
- Prayer Meeting page exists as a dedicated livestream endpoint.
- Digital Bulletin page provides QR/click pathway to latest bulletin.
- Bulletin Archive page lists extensive year-by-year bulletin index (2012–2026 visible in current capture).

## Devotion Archive
- Devotion Archive page contains a large concentration of 2020 daily devotion entries.

## Anniversary Media
- TPC 50 page contains a three-part 2020 special-program sequence.
- Separate GSTPC 40th-anniversary e-book endpoint is present at https://gstpc.org/home/get/gstpc40-ebook

## Source Confidence
- High confidence for endpoint existence and archive-category structure.
- Medium confidence for complete item counts (archives are dynamic and paginated).
