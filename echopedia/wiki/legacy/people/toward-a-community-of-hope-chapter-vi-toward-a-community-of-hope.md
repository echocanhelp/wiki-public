# Chapter VI: Toward a Community of Hope（第六章：邁向希望的社群）

Source chapter page for toward-a-community-of-hope.

## Chapter Summary
Chapter VI is the dissertation's constructive climax. After documenting historical wounds, migration displacement, and identity tension, it advances "community of hope" as a practical and theological program.

Hope here is not emotion alone; it is a discipline embodied through institutions, relationships, and collective direction under uncertainty.

## Why Chapter VI Matters for TAHS
- It provides a normative framework that links faith, identity, and social responsibility.
- It clarifies how Taiwanese American church communities imagined future agency rather than only survival.
- It helps connect historical documentation work to present-day community visioning.

## Constructive Program (Working Model)
1. **Historical honesty**
   - Name collective struggle and dislocation without erasing conflict.
2. **Communal discernment**
   - Form shared direction through church/community deliberation.
3. **Institutional embodiment**
   - Express hope through durable ministries, education, care, and mission.
4. **Public witness**
   - Move beyond inward preservation toward outward service and social presence.
