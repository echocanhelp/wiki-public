# New Taiwan Center Building Project (新館建館工程)

**New Taiwan Center Building Project (新館建館工程)** refers to the public new-building campaign and construction process documented by the [[taiwan-center-foundation-of-greater-los-angeles-大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會|Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會)]]. The official Taiwan Center pages frame the project as a major step in building a future cultural home for Taiwanese Americans in Greater Los Angeles.

This page consolidates official-source signals from the groundbreaking ceremony, construction-progress announcement, and 2025 annual gala page.

## Groundbreaking Ceremony

The [[taiwan-center-groundbreaking-ceremony-2025-大洛杉磯台灣會館動土典禮|2025 Taiwan Center Groundbreaking Ceremony]] page describes the new building as a milestone in Taiwanese American community development. The official page states that after years of planning and community cohesion, the new center broke ground and was envisioned as a cultural-transmission site and spiritual home for generations of Taiwanese Americans.

The official groundbreaking page describes the building vision as including:

- a multipurpose performance hall
- a Taiwan culture library
- a youth maker space
- modern educational facilities

## Construction Progress Announcement

The official construction-progress page, dated February 5, 2026, states that the new Taiwan Center building was actively under construction. It describes the new facility as providing more complete modern facilities, broader multifunctional activity space, and support for cultural transmission, education, and community gathering.

The page frames the project as the shared wish and effort of thousands of community members, with each step from planning and design to construction carrying community support and expectation.

## Annual Gala and Fundraising Context

The [[taiwan-center-annual-gala-2025-第27屆年會暨募款晚宴|2025 Taiwan Center Annual Gala / 27th Annual Meeting]] page places the new-building project in a fundraising and community-mobilization context. The official gala page quotes board chair 田詒鴻 (Alan Thian) describing the Taiwan Center as a base for gathering Taiwanese American strength in the United States and states that rebuilding would create a sustainable spiritual fortress for future generations.

The same official page states that the new building was expected to be completed by the end of 2027, before the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.

## Historical Significance

The new-building project is historically significant because it connects three layers of institutional memory:

1. **Physical permanence:** the creation of a renewed Taiwanese American cultural home in Greater Los Angeles.
2. **Intergenerational transmission:** the official pages repeatedly frame the building as serving future generations through culture, education, and gathering space.
3. **Public-facing community identity:** the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics reference positions the Taiwan Center as a potential civic and cultural anchor during a global event.
