# Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會)

**Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會)** is a Taiwanese American cultural and community institution serving the Greater Los Angeles area. Its official website describes the center as a bridge between Taiwan and the United States, a platform for Taiwanese American community life, and an overseas beacon for Taiwanese culture.

Official site: https://taiwancenter.org/

## Identity Snapshot

- Chinese name: 大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會
- English name: Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles
- Common name: Taiwan Center / 大洛杉磯台灣會館
- Earlier institutional name: 南加州台灣會館基金會 / 南加州台灣會館
- Type: nonprofit Taiwanese American cultural and community organization
- Location: Alhambra, California, USA
- Public-facing role: cultural center, community platform, event venue, Taiwanese school, library, and organizational hub for Taiwanese American groups in Southern California

## Historical Overview

According to the center's official introduction, the organization emerged from Southern California's large Taiwanese immigrant community. In 1998, Overseas Community Affairs Council commissioner [[wang-gui-rong-王桂榮|王桂榮 (Wang Gui-Rong / Kenjohn Wang)]] donated property in Rosemead, California. [[wu-li-pei-吳澧培|吳澧培 (Wu Li-Pei)]], president of EverTrust Bank, served as fundraising convener, and more than forty Taiwanese organizations together with about one thousand community members raised US$1 million.

On June 12, 1998, the **南加州台灣會館基金會** was established as a nonprofit institution to own, operate, and manage the Southern California Taiwan Center. The organization's stated purpose was to promote Taiwanese culture and tradition and to foster communication and understanding between Taiwanese Americans and other communities. In 2001, the institution changed its name to **大洛杉磯台灣會館**.

## Mission and Community Role

The center presents its mission around four themes:

- **Cultural transmission:** hosting public-interest, arts, cultural, and educational programs that preserve and share Taiwanese cultural traditions.
- **Community integration:** promoting engagement with broader American society and strengthening the public image of Taiwanese Americans.
- **Intergenerational connection:** cultivating second-generation Taiwanese Americans and transmitting community values.
- **Civic participation:** encouraging Taiwanese Americans to participate actively in community affairs.

The official introduction states that the foundation has thirty-six directors, more than 800 permanent individual members, and more than fifty organizational members. It describes the Taiwan Center as a leading institutional hub for Taiwanese American organizations in Los Angeles.

## Facilities and Programs

The official site describes the Taiwan Center as a multipurpose community facility with spaces and programs including:

- **[[wang-gui-rong-memorial-library-王桂榮圖書館|王桂榮圖書館 (Wang Gui-Rong / Kenjohn Wang Memorial Library)]]:** a library with more than 10,000 books related to Taiwan, including art, history, literature, music, language, and humanities materials.
- **二二八追思紀念展覽室:** a permanent exhibition space preserving materials about the February 28 Incident, human rights, democracy, and historical memory.
- **大禮堂:** a multipurpose hall with capacity for about 250 people.
- **Meeting rooms, classrooms, audiovisual room, exhibition space, and rooftop garden:** facilities used for lectures, classes, meetings, performances, exhibitions, and community gatherings.
- **台美社團文史館:** an institutional history space documenting the development of Taiwanese American organizations in Greater Los Angeles.

The center also operates or hosts educational programming through its [[taiwan-center-taiwan-school-大洛杉磯台灣會館台灣學校|Taiwan School]], including Chinese-language, Japanese-language, computer, vegetarian cooking, choir, sewing, and photography classes. Its public site describes the school as a multigenerational learning environment rooted in Taiwanese culture and community life.

## New Building Project

The center's official announcements describe an ongoing [[new-taiwan-center-building-project-新館建館工程|New Taiwan Center Building Project (新館建館工程)]]. The 2025 groundbreaking page states that the planned facility is envisioned as a cultural landmark with a multipurpose performance hall, Taiwan culture library, youth maker space, and modern educational facilities. The 2026 construction-progress announcement states that the project was actively under construction. The 2025 annual-gala page states that the new building was expected to be completed by the end of 2027, in time to serve as a Taiwanese community venue around the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.

## Historical Significance

The Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles is significant as one of the core Taiwanese American institutional anchors in Southern California. Its formation joined community philanthropy, organizational coordination, cultural preservation, public memory, and civic participation into a durable physical and institutional center. Through its library, exhibitions, school, event venues, member network, and public commemorations, it functions as both a cultural home and an archive-generating platform for Taiwanese American life in Greater Los Angeles.
