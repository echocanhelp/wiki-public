# Pasadena (帕薩迪納)

**Pasadena (帕薩迪納, Pàsàdínà)** appears in the formation narrative of Dr. Albert S. Lai (賴信雄), where he served in a predominantly white Presbyterian congregation during summer internships.

## Historical Relevance

This pastoral context helped shape Lai’s later vision of immigrant church identity and mission in Greater Los Angeles.
