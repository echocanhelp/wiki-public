# Toward A Community of Hope — Primary Source Extracts

Quoted excerpts below are from the 1971 dissertation layer unless otherwise noted. Use alongside Source-Critical Notes.

## Chapter III Context (Migration / Exodus)

"For various reasons, mainly political, every year there are nearly two thousand Formosan students who come to the United States for graduate study, and only less than 5 percent of them return to Formosa on completing their graduate study... They are forced to come and to stay here for one reason or another -- a modern style of exodus."

Editorial note: This passage is often used to frame diaspora not merely as mobility, but as constrained historical movement.

## Chapter IV Context (Church Formation in Los Angeles)

"This chapter is a presentation of the background, brief history, current status and prospect of a newly organized church in Los Angeles -- Formosan Christian Church in Los Angeles, California..."

Editorial note: The author treats local church formation as both pastoral response and social institution-building.

## Chapter VI Context (Hope as Praxis)

"A man living in a period of transition searches for knowledge of the future..."

Editorial note: "Hope" here is not sentimental language; it is tied to communal direction under uncertainty.

## Conclusion Context (Identity and Future)

"Historically, Formosans have been struggling for their self-determination..."

Editorial note: Closing arguments connect theology, identity, and historical agency.
