# Taiwan Center Publications (會館刊物)

**Taiwan Center Publications (會館刊物)** is the official publication archive listed by the [[taiwan-center-foundation-of-greater-los-angeles-大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會|Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會)]]. The official Taiwan Center publication page describes each publication as carrying community stories, activity records, interviews, viewpoints, and visual features that document Taiwanese American community life.

This page is a source index. It preserves the official publication list and identifies follow-up archival work, but does not yet extract or summarize the contents of each linked program book/yearbook.

## Official Description

The Taiwan Center describes its publications as a way to read community stories online, including activity accounts, person interviews, community perspectives, and illustrated features. As historical sources, these program books and yearbooks are likely to contain board rosters, event programs, donor acknowledgements, organizational advertisements, essays, photographs, and community timelines.

## Archival Priorities

1. **Founding-era extraction:** begin with Yearbook 1998 and Yearbook 2000 to recover early board, donor, founding, and community-network data.
2. **New-building campaign context:** extract 2025 Program Book, Yearbook 2024, and 2023 Program Book for new-building, fundraising, leadership, and organizational-network signals.
3. **Roster continuity:** compare board/member names across years to build a longitudinal Taiwan Center leadership timeline.
4. **Event continuity:** connect publication evidence to existing event pages, including annual meetings, Taiwanese American Heritage Week, February 28 memorial programming, and Miss Taiwanese American Pageant.
5. **Person-page gate:** create standalone person pages only when a publication provides role, context, and public significance beyond a name listing.
