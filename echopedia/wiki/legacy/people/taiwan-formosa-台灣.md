# Taiwan / Formosa (台灣)

This page tracks historical naming and identity usage relevant to toward-a-community-of-hope.

## Periodized Naming Context

### Pre- and Mid-20th Century English Usage
- **Formosa** appears frequently in missionary, diplomatic, and academic writing.
- In many historical texts, "Formosan" functions as an ethno-political identity marker distinct from "Chinese" frameworks.

### Late-20th to 21st Century Shift
- **Taiwan** and **Taiwanese** become more common in scholarly, civic, and community discourse.
- Contemporary usage generally prefers "Taiwanese," while preserving "Formosan" in historical quotation and source-specific analysis.
