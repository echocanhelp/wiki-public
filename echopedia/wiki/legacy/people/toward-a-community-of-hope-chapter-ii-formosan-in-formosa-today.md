# Chapter II: Formosan in Formosa Today（第二章：當代的台灣人）

Source chapter page for toward-a-community-of-hope.

## Chapter Summary
Chapter II translates historical memory into present-tense pressure. It examines contemporary postwar Taiwan/Formosa conditions and shows how political uncertainty, social constraints, and identity strain shaped migration-era consciousness.

In the dissertation structure, this chapter bridges "yesterday" (Chapter I) and migration movement (Chapter III), making it essential for causal interpretation rather than background-only reading.

## Why Chapter II Matters for TAHS
- It provides near-era language for homeland push conditions.
- It clarifies how political context informed migration imagination.
- It helps explain why religious/community institutions became critical in diaspora.

## Analytical Bridge Function
1. **Context diagnosis**
   - Names pressures in the contemporary homeland setting.
2. **Identity stress mapping**
   - Shows tension between belonging, agency, and future possibility.
3. **Migration precondition framing**
   - Establishes why U.S. migration appears as constrained option.
