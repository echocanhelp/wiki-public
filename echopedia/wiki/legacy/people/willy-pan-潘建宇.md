# Willy Pan (潘建宇)

**Willy Pan (潘建宇, Pan Chien-yu / Pan Jian-yu)** is a Taiwanese American engineer and retired project manager. He serves as a Volunteer Member of the Taiwanese American Historical Society (TAHS).

## Professional Background

- **Retired Project Manager**, Honeywell International Inc., Arizona, USA
- High-level engineer specializing in **industrial equipment market development**
- Former Vice General Manager / Consultant for manufacturing process planning at 美耐血餐具
- Electrical & Computer specialist at **PANTRONIC-AJ**
