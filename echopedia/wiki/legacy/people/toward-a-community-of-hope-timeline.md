# Toward A Community of Hope — Historical Timeline

## 1967
- Dr. Albert S. Lai (賴信雄) graduates from [[tainan-theological-college-and-seminary-台南神學院|Tainan Theological College and Seminary (台南神學院)]].

## 1968
- Lai begins advanced theological study in the U.S. at [[san-francisco-theological-seminary|San Francisco Theological Seminary]].

## 1970
- Founding-era development of Formosan Presbyterian Church in Los Angeles (FPCLA), a landmark in Taiwanese church history in North America.

## 1971
- Dissertation completed: Toward A Community of Hope — A Mission to Formosan Community in Los Angeles.

## 2022
- Historical rediscovery discussions around the dissertation intensify among Taiwanese Presbyterian leaders and collaborators.

## 2023–2025
- Translation/editorial/publication preparation advances through transpacific collaboration networks.

## 2025
- Republication supported by National Taiwanese Presbyterian Council (NTPC), with fresh historical framing and preservation emphasis.

## Ongoing
- The text remains a key source for reconstructing first-generation Taiwanese American church memory and mission theology.
