# Wang Gui-Rong Memorial Library (王桂榮圖書館)

**Wang Gui-Rong Memorial Library (王桂榮圖書館)** is a Taiwan-focused library within the [[taiwan-center-foundation-of-greater-los-angeles-大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會|Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會)]]. The official Taiwan Center facility page describes it as one of the center's major cultural resources and as an unusually large overseas Chinese-language collection focused on Taiwan.

## Identity Snapshot

- Chinese name: 王桂榮圖書館
- English descriptive name: Wang Gui-Rong / Kenjohn Wang Memorial Library
- Parent institution: Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles / 大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會
- Location context: Taiwan Center, Greater Los Angeles area
- Collection profile: Taiwan-related books and cultural materials

## Collection and Scope

The Taiwan Center's official facility page states that the library holds more than 10,000 books related to Taiwan. The described subject areas include:

- art
- history
- literature and fiction
- music
- language
- humanities
- Taiwan-U.S.-China relations
- Taiwan history, struggle, and democracy-related works
- works by young Taiwanese American authors

The official site frames the library as a community-accessible resource for learning about Taiwan and Taiwanese culture.

## Connection to Wang Gui-Rong / Kenjohn Wang

The library is named for [[wang-gui-rong-王桂榮|Wang Gui-Rong / Kenjohn Wang (王桂榮)]], whom the Taiwan Center's official history identifies as the donor of property that helped launch the Taiwan Center project in 1998.

The facility page includes a section labeled 王桂榮相關 and lists several related publications, including Chinese-language memoir titles and *Kenjohn Wang's Personal Memoir: Taiwan's Own Nobel Prize*.

## Historical and Archival Role

Within the Taiwan Center, the library functions as both an educational facility and a memory institution. Its collection complements the center's broader archival spaces, including the February 28 memorial exhibition room and the 台美社團文史館, by preserving books and cultural materials connected to Taiwan, Taiwanese history, and Taiwanese American community life.
