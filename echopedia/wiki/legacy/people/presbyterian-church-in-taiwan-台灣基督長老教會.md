# Presbyterian Church in Taiwan (台灣基督長老教會)

The **Presbyterian Church in Taiwan (台灣基督長老教會, PCT)** is a foundational Christian institution in modern Taiwanese social and religious history.

## Connection to Taiwanese American Narratives

In Toward A Community of Hope and related republication materials, PCT appears as part of the transpacific ecclesial background that shaped early Taiwanese churches in North America.
