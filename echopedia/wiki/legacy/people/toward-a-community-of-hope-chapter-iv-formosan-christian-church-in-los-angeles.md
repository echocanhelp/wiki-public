# Chapter IV: Formosan Christian Church in Los Angeles（第四章：洛杉磯台灣人基督教會）

Source chapter page for toward-a-community-of-hope.

## Chapter Summary
Chapter IV is the most concrete institutional case study in the dissertation. Rather than discussing diaspora only at the level of ideas, it documents how a Taiwanese immigrant church took organizational form in Los Angeles, California (洛杉磯).

The chapter presents church formation as a dual response:
1. Pastoral response to dislocation, language barriers, and social isolation.
2. Institution-building response that creates durable community infrastructure.

For Echopedia, this chapter is high-value because it links theology, migration, and local organizational history in one place.

## Why Chapter IV Matters for TAHS
- It provides one of the earliest near-contemporaneous narratives of Taiwanese church formation in Southern California.
- It grounds abstract identity discourse in observable congregational practice.
- It helps reconstruct early diaspora social support systems through church life.
