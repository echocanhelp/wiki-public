# Zheng Quansheng (鄭泉聲)

**Zheng Quansheng (鄭泉聲, Rev. Zheng / 鄭牧師)** was an early pastoral leader associated with the formation of Irvine Taiwanese Presbyterian Church (爾灣台灣基督長老教會). The church's official history describes him as a pastor who had recently retired from ministry in Japan when he came to the United States to visit family in 1994.

## ITPC Role

According to the official ITPC history document, leaders and members of the Irvine mission invited 鄭泉聲牧師 to remain and shepherd the new congregation in June 1994. The document frames this as a formative moment in the transition from a mission/branch congregation into an independent Taiwanese Presbyterian church.

In August 1994, the members voted to establish an independent church named Irvine 台灣基督長老教會. 鄭泉聲牧師 continued in this early pastoral role until April 1996, when the document states that he felt the initial stage of the mission had been completed and returned to Japan.

## Historical Significance

鄭泉聲牧師's role belongs to the founding phase of ITPC, when Taiwanese Presbyterian families in south Orange County were moving from afternoon mission worship toward a self-governing congregation with its own name, incorporation process, and church constitution.
