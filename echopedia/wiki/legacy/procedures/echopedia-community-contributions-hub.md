# Echopedia Community Contributions Hub（Echopedia 社群貢獻中心）

Help preserve Taiwanese American history with dignity, accuracy, and personal voice.

## What to Contribute
- Personal memories and testimonies
- Sermon notes, letters, speeches, interviews
- Event programs, newsletters, church bulletins
- Photos with names, dates, and locations
- Name corrections, romanization variants, timeline corrections

## Contribution Format (recommended)
When sending a contribution, include:
1. Person/page name
2. Your relationship to the person/event
3. The memory, quote, or correction
4. Date/period (exact or estimated)
5. Source type (photo, document, oral memory, recording)
6. Any privacy restrictions or publication limits

## Testimony Citation Format
Use this format for testimony-ready submissions:
- Quote or memory text:
- Date/Period:
- Context (sermon/interview/family recollection/publication):
- Source owner/location:
- Permission status: Public / Restricted / Needs confirmation

## Respect and Verification Policy
- We preserve both memory and accuracy.
- First-person voice is valuable, even when details need later verification.
- Entries may be marked as High / Medium / Needs verification until corroborated.
- Sensitive material should include explicit consent instructions.

## Recognition Promise
Every meaningful contribution helps ensure that people are remembered not as footnotes, but as full lives with voice, relationships, and legacy.

## Intake Workflow（後台流程）
To streamline processing, submissions are reviewed and processed in stages:

- New
- Missing metadata
- Privacy review
- Ready for archive
- Published

## Source Confidence Legend
- A-tier: direct quotations or primary-source textual attributions from the currently attached source set.
- B-tier: near-contemporary attributed references or secondary materials closely tied to the event/person.
- C-tier: compiled contextual interpretation layers that require further primary-source confirmation.
