# Albert Lai Digital Voice Actor Profile Plan

**Goal:** Capture Albert Lai / 賴信雄 / Albert Lai as a consent-based digital voice actor profile for future narration of his book and relevant Echopedia/YouTube content.

**Principle:** No voice cloning or publication proceeds without explicit informed consent, clear rights scope, review/approval, and revocation path.

---

## Phase 1 — Consent and Scope

1. Confirm identity and preferred display name:
   - Chinese characters: 賴信雄
   - Romanized / English: Albert Lai
   - Any alternate name or title for public credits

2. Explain intended use:
   - Build a digital voice actor profile from Albert’s recorded voice.
   - Use it to narrate his book and selected Echopedia/TAHS historical content.
   - Potential publication channels: YouTube, Echopedia, TAHS archives, private drafts for review.

3. Collect written consent covering:
   - Voice recording and processing
   - AI/digital voice profile creation
   - Narration use cases
   - Public/private publication channels
   - Right to review before publication
   - Right to revoke future use
   - Credit wording

4. Store consent record in the TAHS/Echopedia archive workflow.

## Phase 2 — Voice Source Collection

5. Ask Albert for the easiest recording method:
   - Existing clean recordings, if available
   - New phone recordings
   - Guided reading session over LINE/Zoom/phone

6. Capture 20–60 minutes of clean voice if possible:
   - Quiet room
   - Natural speaking voice
   - Mix of English, Mandarin/Taiwanese/Hokkien if relevant
   - Include emotional range: reflective, warm, documentary, formal

7. Collect pronunciation guidance:
   - Names, places, book-specific terms
   - Taiwanese/Chinese romanization preferences
   - Any phrases Albert wants pronounced in his own style

## Phase 3 — Profile Preparation

8. Organize source files with metadata:
   - Speaker: Albert Lai / 賴信雄
   - Date recorded
   - Language(s)
   - Consent scope
   - Source quality notes

9. Clean audio only as needed:
   - Noise reduction
   - Silence trimming
   - Level normalization
   - Preserve natural voice character

10. Create a voice actor profile card:
   - Name
   - Voice description
   - Approved use cases
   - Restrictions
   - Credit line
   - Pronunciation notes
   - Review contact / approval workflow

## Phase 4 — Test Narration

11. Produce a short private sample first:
   - 30–90 seconds from Albert’s book or approved Echopedia excerpt
   - Include one English passage and one Chinese/Taiwanese-name-heavy passage if relevant

12. Send sample to Albert for review:
   - Accuracy
   - Tone
   - Pronunciation
   - Comfort level
   - Publication permission

13. Revise until approved.

## Phase 5 — Book Narration Pipeline

14. Prepare the book text:
   - Confirm rights and source manuscript
   - Divide into chapters/segments
   - Mark names, dates, and pronunciation notes

15. Generate chapter drafts privately.

16. Review workflow:
   - Albert approval for voice/tone
   - TAHS review for historical accuracy
   - Final publication approval

17. Publish approved versions:
   - YouTube audiobook/video chapters
   - Echopedia embedded audio or linked playlist where relevant
   - TAHS archive record

## Phase 6 — Governance

18. Maintain a voice-use log:
   - Date
   - Project
   - Script/source text
   - Output link
   - Approval status

19. Keep revocation/update path simple:
   - Albert can pause future use
   - Albert can request profile correction
   - Previously published works handled according to consent terms

## Acceptance Criteria

- At least one clean voice sample source identified.
- Voice profile card completed.
- Private sample narration approved by Albert.
- No public release without final approval.
