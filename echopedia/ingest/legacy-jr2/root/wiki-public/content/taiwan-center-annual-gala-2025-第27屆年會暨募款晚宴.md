# 2025 Taiwan Center Annual Gala / 27th Annual Meeting (第27屆年會暨募款晚宴)

**2025 Taiwan Center Annual Gala / 27th Annual Meeting (第27屆年會暨募款晚宴)** was a major public event of the [[taiwan-center-foundation-of-greater-los-angeles-大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會|Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會)]]. The official event page states that the gala drew more than one thousand attendees and centered on the theme 「築夢踏實・傳承啟航」.

## Event Snapshot

- Chinese title: 2025台灣會館年會 / 大洛杉磯台灣會館第27屆年會暨募款晚宴
- English descriptive title: 2025 Taiwan Center Annual Gala and Fundraising Dinner
- Date listed by Taiwan Center: November 8, 2025
- Venue described by Taiwan Center: Pacific Palms Resort, City of Industry
- Host institution: Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles / 大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會
- Attendance described by Taiwan Center: more than 1,000 community members and guests

## Program and Speakers

The official event page states that former Mainland Affairs Council minister 邱太三 was invited as keynote speaker, presenting on Taiwan's democratic development and international situation. The page summarizes his remarks as emphasizing Taiwan's democratic path under international pressure, the role of Taiwan's technology sector in the AI era, and appreciation for overseas Taiwanese support.

The same page records video greetings from President Lai Ching-te (賴清德), Overseas Community Affairs Council minister Hsu Chia-ching (徐佳青), and Hakka Affairs Council minister Ku Hsiu-fei (古秀妃). It also names Taipei Economic and Cultural Office in Los Angeles director 紀欽耀, U.S. representatives Judy Chu (趙美心) and Brad Sherman, and other attending guests.

## Cultural Program

The Taiwan Center page describes performances including a dance by 2025 Miss Taiwanese American first princess 陳妤涵, a Hakka choir performance, and a recorder solo by fifth princess 楊千樂. It also describes a fundraising auction item, the "A Gift from the Heart" necklace designed by Miss Taiwanese American queen Ami Wang.

## New Building Context

The page quotes board chair 田詒鴻 (Alan Thian) describing the Taiwan Center as an important base for gathering Taiwanese American strength in the United States. It states that the new building was expected to be completed by the end of 2027, in time for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics and as a possible gathering point connected to Taiwanese athletes visiting Los Angeles.

## Historical Significance

This gala is significant as a public record of Taiwan Center's institutional scale, community support, and new-building campaign. Its attendance, public officials, cultural program, and fundraising focus show how the Taiwan Center connects diaspora community life with Taiwan's democratic identity and international participation.
