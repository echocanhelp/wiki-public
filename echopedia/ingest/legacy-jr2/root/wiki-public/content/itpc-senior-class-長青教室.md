# ITPC Senior Classroom (長青教室)

**ITPC Senior Classroom (長青教室)** is a senior-community lecture and fellowship program associated with Irvine Taiwanese Presbyterian Church (爾灣台灣基督長老教會). The official ITPC website describes the program as a Wednesday gathering led by retired physicians, professors, and other community experts, with attendance around 100 people in the church's 2025 homepage statement.

## Historical Role

The official ITPC history links 長青教室 to the congregation's move toward Laguna Woods and Geneva Presbyterian Church. While ITPC was worshiping at Tustin Presbyterian Church, church leaders recognized the Taiwanese retiree community around Laguna Woods. The church first established 長青教室 near Laguna Woods as a way for retired Taiwanese people to gather, build fellowship, and encounter Christian community. The history document says the program was successful, drawing nearly one hundred retired Taiwanese professionals.

That senior-community ministry helped prepare ITPC's later move to Geneva Presbyterian Church: the church began using Geneva's chapel in 2011 and moved there in October 2012 after congregational discernment.

## Community Significance

長青教室 is historically useful because it documents a Taiwanese American elder-learning and mutual-care environment in Orange County. Its archived lecture pages show how a church-based senior program became a place for health education, political memory, technology learning, cultural continuity, and social support among Taiwanese retirees.

The program also shows ITPC's broader community role: the church was not only a Sunday worship site, but also a Taiwanese-language social infrastructure for retirees in Irvine, Laguna Woods, and Laguna Hills.
