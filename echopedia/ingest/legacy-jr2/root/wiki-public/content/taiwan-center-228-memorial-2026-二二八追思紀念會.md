# 2026 Taiwan Center February 28 Memorial Series (二二八追思紀念會暨系列活動)

**2026 Taiwan Center February 28 Memorial Series (二二八追思紀念會暨系列活動)** was a public-memory program co-organized by the [[taiwan-center-foundation-of-greater-los-angeles-大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會|Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會)]] and Los Angeles Taiwanese American community organizations. The official event page identifies the 79th anniversary theme as 「228 與白色恐怖」.

## Event Snapshot

- Chinese title: 二二八追思紀念會暨系列活動
- English descriptive title: February 28 Memorial Commemoration and Series
- Date listed by Taiwan Center: February 28, 2026
- Theme: 228 與白色恐怖 / February 28 and the White Terror
- Host context: Taiwan Center and Los Angeles Taiwanese American organizations
- Public-memory focus: victims of the February 28 Incident, White Terror history, democracy, freedom, and human rights

## Series Format

The official Taiwan Center page describes a multi-format commemoration using memorial ceremony, music, children's story time, film screening, and discussion. The stated purpose was to deepen community understanding of democratic values and universal human rights, while paying respect to innocent victims.

Listed activities included:

- **二二八79週年追思紀念會:** a memorial ceremony with silence and mourning rituals, a video address by National 228 Memorial Museum director 藍士博, and a video lecture by National Taiwan University history professor 周婉窈.
- **二二八小水牛台美兒童故事時間:** a children's history and memory program.
- **228台灣介心靈日79週年紀念音樂會:** a commemorative concert.
- **台灣電影《大濛》放映暨座談:** a film screening and post-screening discussion on 228 and White Terror historical meaning.

## Community and Institutional Network

The page lists supporting or co-sponsoring organizations including Taiwan United Fund, Taiwan Elite Alliance, Formosan Association for Public Affairs Los Angeles, Orange County Taiwanese Association, Hakka organizations, Sound of Taiwan, Asian American Social Welfare Foundation, Formosan Association for Human Rights, Taiwanese American Women's Association Southern California, Taiwanese American professional organizations, local Taiwanese associations, Taiwanese American Chamber of Commerce of Greater Los Angeles, Taiwan Daily, and Taiwanese American Citizens League–related organizations.

## Historical Significance

This series is significant because it shows how Taiwanese American institutions in Los Angeles preserve Taiwan's democratic memory abroad. By including elder commemoration, children's programming, music, film, and scholarly talks, the event connects historical trauma, intergenerational education, and public human-rights values.
