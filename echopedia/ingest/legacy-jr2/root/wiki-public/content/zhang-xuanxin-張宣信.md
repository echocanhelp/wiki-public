# Zhang Xuanxin (張宣信)

**Zhang Xuanxin (張宣信, Rev. Chang / 張牧師)** was a long-serving pastor of Irvine Taiwanese Presbyterian Church (爾灣台灣基督長老教會). The church's official history presents his ministry as a major stabilizing period in ITPC's development from a small Irvine congregation into a larger Taiwanese Presbyterian community serving the Irvine and Laguna Woods area.

## ITPC Role

The official ITPC history states that church leaders learned in April 1999 that 張宣信牧師 was returning from Taiwan to the United States because of his children's education. After prayer and invitation by church elders and deacons, he accepted a full-time pastoral call and began serving on May 23, 1999, Pentecost Sunday.

During his tenure, the congregation moved in September 2003 from Creekside Fellowship Church to Tustin Presbyterian Church. The history document later describes the move toward Laguna Woods / Geneva Presbyterian Church: senior-community programming began near Laguna Woods, worship expanded to Geneva Presbyterian Church's chapel in 2011, and the congregation moved there in October 2012 after a year of dual-location worship and congregational discernment.

The same official history states that 張宣信牧師 served for twenty-one years and left the position in June 2020.

## Wider Source Signals

GSTPC bulletin ingestion also captured 張宣信牧師 / Pastor Chang as a sermon speaker in Taiwanese Presbyterian church contexts in 2020 and 2021, supporting that he continued to appear in regional church-source material after his ITPC tenure.

## Historical Significance

張宣信牧師's service spans ITPC's key institutional consolidation: full-time pastoral leadership, Presbyterian fellowship connection, relocation from Creekside to Tustin, development of senior-community ministry, and the move to Geneva Presbyterian Church near Laguna Woods.
