# Yang Chia-yu 楊嘉猷 (Charles Yang)

**楊嘉猷** (Yang Chia-yu), commonly known as **Charles Yang**, is a Taiwanese American community leader, former elected official, and the **Founding President** of the Taiwanese American Historical Society.

## Political Career

### National Assembly (1991)
Charles Yang was elected as a **Democratic Progressive Party (民進黨)** candidate for 國大代表 in the 臺中縣第03選區 during the December 1991 election, receiving **38,472 votes**.

### Taichung County Magistrate Campaign (1993)
On July 21, 1993, he resigned from the National Assembly to run for **臺中縣長** (Magistrate of Taichung County). He received **247,340 votes** in the November 1993 election. Precinct-level results from 豐原市 show vote shares ranging from approximately 30% to 40% in several polling stations.

## Government Service

Charles Yang served as a technician (技士) at the **行政院經濟部商品檢驗局台南分局** (Tainan Branch of the Bureau of Commodity Inspection, Ministry of Economic Affairs).

## Taiwanese American Historical Society (TAHS)

Charles Yang was the **Founding President** of the Taiwanese American Historical Society. The organization was officially established on **December 11, 2013** in San Gabriel, California, with 楊嘉猷 as President, 鄭炳全 as Vice President, and 王耀廷 as Treasurer.

He remained active in the organization for many years. As late as April 2019, he was still serving as 會長 and introduced speakers at TAHS events. On January 9, 2015, he visited community elder 江百顯 in Claremont together with then-President 鄭炳全 and Secretary General 江美娟.

## Authorship and Writing

- Translator of *為什麼99.99%的大中國人討厭小日本?* (Why Do 99.99% of Greater Chinese People Hate the Japanese?), published in 2008.
- Author of *海外台灣人民主運動史話* (History of the Overseas Taiwanese Democracy Movement).
- Wrote memorial/tribute articles, including pieces on 蔡同榮 (2014) and 王能祥 (2015).

## Overseas Democracy Movement

Charles Yang was active in the overseas Taiwanese democracy movement and is noted for having broken the blacklist. He has been featured in video discussions on this topic.

## Community Activities

On August 17, 2024, he participated in a senior discussion panel (耆老座談會) at the **大洛杉磯台灣會館**, sharing his experience of returning to Taiwan to run for public office.

## Other Roles

He was appointed Director of the Preparatory Office for the Southeast University branch (東南大學分部籌備處主任).
