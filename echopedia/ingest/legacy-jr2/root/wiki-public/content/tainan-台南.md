# Tainan (台南)

**Tainan (台南, Táinán)** is a historically important city in Taiwanese Christian history and theological education.

## Historical Relevance in This Cluster

Tainan is linked to the formation of Dr. Albert S. Lai (賴信雄) through Tainan Theological College and Seminary (台南神學院).
