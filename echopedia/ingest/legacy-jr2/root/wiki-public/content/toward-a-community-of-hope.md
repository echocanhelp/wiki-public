## Why This Text Matters

In 1971, Dr. Albert S. Lai (賴信雄) completed a major dissertation that is both documentary and theological. It is one of the earliest sustained attempts to interpret Taiwanese immigrant church life in North America through history, sociology, and missional theology.

Rather than treating church as a cultural shelter alone, the dissertation argues for a public, hope-bearing communal vocation.
