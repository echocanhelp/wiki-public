> 05-18-2025 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · May 18, 2025 敬拜的心 Heart of Worship (約翰福音 John 4:23) 蘇子獻弟兄 Nicholas So 消息報告 Announcements 感謝蘇子獻 Nicholas So 弟兄所帶來的音樂見證主日，透過詩歌和見證帶給我們這場特別的音樂饗宴。 We thank Nicholas So for sharing a special service with us through songs and testimonies. 禮拜後成人主日學時間是小組分組以討論主日信息為主，請兄姊踴躍參與。After the service, small groups will discuss the Sunday sermon. Brothers and sisters are encouraged to participate. 昨天台美人傳統週園遊會在Monterey Park Barnes Park 舉行，感謝所有參與的同工，願上帝的福音生根發芽在當天來到好牧者攤位的弟兄姊妹心中，歡迎藉此活動來到教會的新朋友。Our church participated in the Taiwanese American Cultural Festival in Barnes Park yesterday, and we thank all the brothers and sisters who helped out. May the gospel grow in the hearts of all those who heard it. We also welcome all the new friends who are here today because of the festival. 5月25日下週日下午一點半慶祝黃德利牧師八十八大壽感恩禮拜，黃牧師招待大家豐盛午餐，歡迎兄姊崇拜後留步一起享用中餐並參加下午感恩禮拜。Rev. David Huang’s 88th birthday thanksgiving celebration service will be held next Sunday (May 25th) at 1:30pm. He is treating everyone to a delicious lunch, so all brothers and sisters are welcome to stay after service and participate. 教會今年社教45週年慶祝，計畫在十月籌辦全教會的郵輪活動，禮拜後請留步將報告活動細節並詢問兄姊參加的意願。Our church is celebrating our 45th anniversary this year! There are plans for a cruise in October. If interested, please stay after the service to learn more about the details. 代禱 Intercession 為教會新的家庭小組事工代禱，喜樂、恩慈、良善、溫柔和英文五小組，願上帝祝福每位弟兄姊妹都在自己的小組中能受造就和好的交通。Pray for the five small groups in our church: Joy, Kindness, Goodness, Gentleness, and English. May every brother and sister be blessed and edified with good fellowship within their groups. 新年度2025-2027 主題「造就門徒、世代傳承」，求上帝賜下祂的心意、異象和同心的同工一同來完成這些事工。The 2025-2027 theme is “Making Disciples and Passing on to Generations.” Pray for God’s vision, heart, and coworkers. 求神憐憫及醫治周崑滬牧師 (許牧師娘父親)、古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、藍老師，保守身體平安。Pray for God’s mercy and healing on Rev. Chou (Mrs. Hsu’s father), Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, and Teacher Lan.
