> 03-02-2025 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · March 2, 2025 改造生命的耶穌 Jesus Changes Lives (路加福音 Luke 19:1-10) 黃德利牧師 Rev. David Huang 消息報告 Announcements 感謝黃德利牧師今日美好的信息，願上帝祝福牧師、牧師娘老當益壯，愈服事愈甘甜。We thank Rev. David Huang for his message today. May God continue to bless and keep him strong and healthy. 禮拜後成人主日學時間是小組分組以討論主日講道為主，請兄姊踴躍參與。After the service, brothers and sisters will be divided into small groups to discuss today’s sermon. Please participate. 許牧師於 2/25-3/20 年度休假，求上帝保守牧師和牧師娘旅途中一切平安。牧師外出期間若兄姊需要幫忙，請聯絡任何一位長老協助。Rev. Hsu will be on his annual leave from 2/25 to 3/20. Please pray for him and his wife’s safety during their journey. If brothers and sisters need help while the pastor is away, please contact any elder. 下週日邀請前南灣台福教會主任牧師許健全牧師講道，請代禱。We have invited Rev. Hsu, former senior pastor at Evangelical Formosan Church of South Bay, to preach next Sunday. Please pray for him. 今年大齋節期從3月5日到4月17日，在復活節前40天當中以禱告和安靜預備主耶穌的犧牲、受苦和復活。This year’s Lent is from 3/5 to 4/17, the forty days before Easter where we prepare and remember the sacrifice, suffering, and resurrection of our Lord Jesus Christ. G-Coffee 將於3月8日禮拜六上午10:00 邀請投資達人Rick Ku 分享投資演化講座，歡迎邀請朋友參加。G-Coffee has invited Rick Ku to share about investments on March 8th (Sat) at 10am. All are invited to attend. 下週日日光節約時間開始， 請在禮拜六晚上將時鐘 調快一小時。 Daylight Savings Time begins next Sunday, please remember to set your clocks forward one hour on Saturday night. 代禱 Intercession 為教會新的家庭小組事工代禱，喜樂、恩慈、良善、溫柔和英文五小組，願上帝祝福每位弟兄姊妹都在自己的小組中能受造就和好的交通。Pray for the new small group ministries of the church: Joy, Kindness, Goodness, Gentleness, and English. May each brother and sister be edified and have good fellowship in their groups. 新年度2025-2027 主題「造就門徒、世代傳承」，求上帝賜下祂的心意、異象和同心的同工一同來完成這些事工。The theme of the new year 2025-2027 is “Making Disciples and Passing on to Generations.” We ask God for His vision, heart, and coworkers to fulfill these ministries. 求神憐憫及醫治古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、藍老師、保守身體平安。Ask God for His healing and protection on Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, and Teacher Lan.
