> 03-09-2025 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · March 9, 2025 信靠主得勝世界 Trusting God to Overcome the World (約翰福音 John 17:13-19) 許健全牧師 Rev. Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 感謝前南灣台福教會主任牧師許健全牧師今日美好的信息，願上帝祝福牧師，愈服事愈甘甜。We thank Rev. Hsu, former head pastor of Nanwan Taifu Church, for his message today. May God bless him. 禮拜後成人主日學時間是小組分組以討論主日講道為主，請兄姊踴躍參與。After the service, brothers and sisters will be divided into small groups to discuss today’s sermon. Please participate. 許牧師於 2/25-3/20 年度休假，求上帝保守牧師和牧師娘旅途中一切平安。牧師外出期間若兄姊需要幫忙，請聯絡任何一位長老協助。Rev. Hsu is on his annual leave from 2/25 to 3/20. Please pray for him and his wife’s safety during their journey. If brothers and sisters need help while the pastor is away, please contact any elder. 下週日邀請王光智牧師（前基諾教會牧師）講道，請代禱。 We have invited Pastor Wang (formerly at Chino Hills TPC) to preach next Sunday. Please pray for him. 今年大齋節期從3月5日到4月17日，在復活節前40天當中以禱告和安靜預備主耶穌的犧牲、受苦和復活，在LINE群組每天都有每日靈修課程供兄姊使用。This year’s Lent is from 3/5 to 4/17, the forty days before Easter where we prepare and remember the sacrifice, suffering, and resurrection of our Lord Jesus Christ. There will be daily spiritual readings for brothers and sisters in the LINE group. 復活節期重要日期和事工 Important Dates: 3/5 – 4/19 大齋節期 Lent 4/13 棕樹節主日 Palm Sunday 4/18 受難日 (禮拜五) Good Friday 4/20 復活節主日 Easter Sunday 代禱 Intercession 為教會新的家庭小組事工代禱，喜樂、恩慈、良善、溫柔和英文五小組，願上帝祝福每位弟兄姊妹都在自己的小組中能受造就和好的交通。Pray for the new small group ministries of the church: Joy, Kindness, Goodness, Gentleness, and English. May each brother and sister be edified and have good fellowship in their groups. 新年度2025-2027 主題「造就門徒、世代傳承」，求上帝賜下祂的心意、異象和同心的同工一同來完成這些事工。The theme of the new year 2025-2027 is “Making Disciples and Passing on to Generations.” We ask God for His vision, heart, and coworkers to fulfill these ministries. 求神憐憫及醫治古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、藍老師、保守身體平安。Ask God for His healing and protection on Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, and Teacher Lan.
