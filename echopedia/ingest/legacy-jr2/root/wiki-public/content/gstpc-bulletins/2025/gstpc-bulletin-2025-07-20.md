> 07-20-2025 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · July 20, 2025 我必不動搖 I Will Not Be Shaken (詩篇 Psalm 15:1-5) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 禮拜後成人主日學時間是小組分組以討論主日信息為主，請兄姊踴躍參與。After the service, small groups will discuss the Sunday sermon. All are encouraged to participate. 許牧師受邀於7/26-27主領「聖荷西台美基督長老教會」靈修會，請代禱，出外期間，若有需要請與長老聯絡。On July 26-27, Pastor Hsu will be leading a workshop at Taiwanese American Presbyterian Church in San Jose. Please pray for him, and contact any elders as needed. 下主日邀請鍾國義牧師證道，請代禱。Next Sunday, we have invited Pastor Chung to share a message, please pray for him. 8月2日禮拜六上午十點在教會G-Coffee 邀請邱冠銘醫師分享「知齒近乎勇–從八個月到八十歲的牙齒大小事」，歡迎邀請親朋好友一起來參加。G-Coffee has invited our brother Dr. Chiu to share “Everything About Teeth–From Eight Months to Eighty Years” on August 2nd (Saturday) at 10am. We welcome relatives and friends to come. 8月6日到8日在教會舉辦兒童夏令營，歡迎年齡5歲到11歲的兒童報名參加，費用$50，請邀請親友的孩子一起來參加。Our summer VBS will be held on August 6th-8th for ages 5-11, registration fee is $50. We welcome new friends to attend. 代禱 Intercession 為喜樂、恩慈、良善、溫柔和英文五個小組代禱，在分享中彼此建造，在互動中建立肢體關係，在服事中培養團隊默契。 Pray for the five small groups in our church, that every brother and sister will be blessed and edified with good fellowship. 為2025-2027年度目標「造就門徒、世代傳承」，求上帝賜下智慧與合一的靈，共同努力達成目標。 Pray for the 2025-2027 theme of “Making Disciples and Passing on to Generations.” Pray for God’s vision, wisdom, and unity. 為八月舉行兒童暑期聖經學校VBS福音事工，求神賜下智慧在主題設定、籌備工作、配搭老師及同工、天氣及場地、宣傳及邀請禱告，求神大大祝福，帶領新的小朋友報名參加。Pray for our summer VBS in August, for the wisdom on the theme, teachers, weather, outreach, and children. 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，求主醫治並保守平安。Pray for God’s healing on the brothers and sisters who are in poor health.
