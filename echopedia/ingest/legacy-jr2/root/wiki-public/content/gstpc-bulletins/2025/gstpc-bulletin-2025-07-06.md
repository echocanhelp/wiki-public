> 07-06-2025 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · July 6, 2025 蒙恩的王、蒙福的國 The Favored King and the Blessed Nation (詩篇 Psalm 30) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 禮拜後成人主日學時間是小組分組以討論主日信息為主，請兄姊踴躍參與。After the service, small groups will discuss the Sunday sermon. All are encouraged to participate. 下週六7月12日上午九點到十二點在教會舉行長執年中退修 會，請長執出席參加。A mid-year retreat for elders and deacons will be held at the church from 9am to 12pm next Saturday (July 12th). 恩惠長老教會創辦者陳瑞騰牧師於6月28日蒙主恩召安息主 懷，享年91歲，追思禮拜訂於7月12日下午三點在玫瑰崗 Rose Hills Visitation Center Chapel 舉行，求神安慰遺族。Rev. Chen, the founder of Grace Presbyterian Church, was called home by the Lord on June 28th at the age of 91. His memorial service will be held at Rose Hills Visitation Center Chapel on July 12th at 3pm. May God comfort the bereaved family. 8月2日禮拜六上午十點在教會 G-Coffee 邀請邱冠銘醫師分享「知齒近乎勇–從八個月到八十歲的牙齒大小事」，歡迎 邀請親朋好友一起來參加。G-Coffee has invited our brother Dr. Chiu to share “Everything About Teeth–From Eight Months to Eighty Years” on August 2nd (Saturday) at 10am. We welcome relatives and friends to come. 8月6日到8日在教會舉辦兒童夏令營，歡迎年齡5歲到11歲的 兒童報名參加，費用$50，請邀請親友的孩子一起來參加。 Our summer VBS will be held on August 6th-8th for ages 5-11, registration fee is $50. We welcome new friends to attend. 代禱 Intercession 為喜樂、恩慈、良善、溫柔和英文五個小組代禱，在分享中彼此建造，在互動中建立肢體關係，在服事中培養團隊默契。 Pray for the five small groups in our church, that every brother and sister will be blessed and edified with good fellowship. 為2025-2027年度目標「造就門徒、世代傳承」，求上帝賜下智慧與合一的靈，共同努力達成目標。 Pray for the 2025-2027 theme of “Making Disciples and Passing on to Generations.” Pray for God’s vision, wisdom, and unity. 為八月舉行兒童暑期聖經學校VBS福音事工，求神賜下智慧在主題設定、籌備工作、配搭老師及同工、天氣及場地、宣傳及邀請禱告，求神大大祝福，帶領新的小朋友報名參加。Pray for our summer VBS in August, for the wisdom on the theme, teachers, weather, outreach, and children. 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，求主醫治並保守平安。Pray for God’s healing on the brothers and sisters who are in poor health.
