> 10-19-2025 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · October 19, 2025 佳美的腳蹤 How Beautiful Are the Feet (羅馬書 Romans 10:14-15) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 上主日召開秋季會員大會，會中接納明年事工計畫、人事預算和改選明年長執、查帳委員和提名委員。Last Sunday was our fall congregational meeting, where we went over next year’s ministry plan, personnel budget, elders and deacons election, audit committee, and nomination committee for next year. 2026 年改選出以下同工 2026 Coworkers: 長老兩名 Elders：黃義弘 John Huang (3年)、王信心 Julia Wang (2年) 執事三名 Deacons：古豐榮 Rick Ku (3年)、邱冠銘 Kuang Ming Chiu (2年)、Dorine Takasugi (1年) 查帳委員 Audit Committee：張鳳書、陳強忠 提名委員 Nomination Committee：高銘澄、Stephen Liao 感謝主本主日80位兄姊一起在 Royal Caribbean 的郵輪上參加主日崇拜，願上帝祝福大家旅程平安愉快。教會沒有實體主日崇拜，牧師已先錄製好主日講道，讓大家在週日十點看網路直播。May God bless all eighty brothers and sisters who joined the Royal Caribbean cruise with a safe and pleasant journey. While there will be no in-person Sunday worship, the pastor has recorded the sermon for everyone to watch live online at 10am. 九月份財務報告 September Financial Report: [redacted] 有感動接受成人洗禮、堅信禮、幼兒洗禮、加入新會員的兄姊，請向許牧師或邱瑜嫩長老報名，10月31日截止。If there are any brothers or sisters who would like to receive baptism, confirmation, or join our church’s member list, please register with Rev. Hsu or Elder Naomi by October 31st (Sat). 代禱 Intercession 為九月份新成立的少年團契 Youth Group 代禱，求主親自帶領少年團契的建立與發展，在團契中彼此扶持、同心成長。Pray for Youth Group starting in September, that God will guide its growth and development. 為教會慶祝45週年的系列活動及慶典禱告，求主保守活動順利，榮耀歸神，同心獻上感恩祭。Pray for the church’s 45th anniversary celebrations and activities to go smoothly and to bring glory to God. 為弟兄姊妹渴慕神的話語禱告，操練每天醒來第一件事—閱讀、聆聽、默想神的話，成為腳前的燈、路上的光。Pray for all brothers and sisters to thirst for God’s Word. Practice reading, listening, and meditating on God’s Word first thing every morning, so it can be a lamp to our feet and a light to our path. 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，求主醫治並保守平安。Pray for God’s healing on the brothers and sisters who are in poor health.
