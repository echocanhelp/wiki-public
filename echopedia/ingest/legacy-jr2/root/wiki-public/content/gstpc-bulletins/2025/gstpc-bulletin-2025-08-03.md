> 08-03-2025 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · August 3, 2025 咱做伙去掠風 Let’s Chasing After the Wind (傳道書 Ecclesiastes 1:2, 12–14; 2:18–23) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 今天禮拜後的成人主日學暫停一次。No Christian Education discussion today. 張錦峰弟兄告別禮拜訂於本主日下午1pm 在教會舉行，請兄姊留步參加。Brother George Chang’s memorial service will be held today at church at 1pm. Please stay and attend. 定期長執會在下午 7:30 在網上開會，請所有現任長執出席。Online board meeting at 7:30pm today; elders and deacons, please attend. 兒童夏令營將在下週三到五 (6-8日) 在教會舉行, 請為此 VBS 福音事工代禱，求神大大祝福，帶領參加的小朋友和參與服事的老師同工一同蒙福。Our summer VBS will be held at the church from Wednesday to Friday (6th-8th). Please pray for God’s protection and blessings on this ministry, leading the children and teachers closer to Him. 8/10 主日會後請兄姐預留時間，慶祝88節和歡迎VBS的新朋友和老朋友，教會將舉辦多達15種家鄉道地「台灣小吃」園遊會，讓我們一起「歡樂一夏」。After the Sunday service on August 10th, the church will have a garden party with abundant Taiwanese food for 8/8! We welcome all new VBS friends. 代禱 Intercession 為喜樂、恩慈、良善、溫柔和英文五個小組代禱，在分享中彼此建造，在互動中建立肢體關係，在服事中培養團隊默契。 Pray for the five small groups in our church, that every brother and sister will be blessed and edified with good fellowship. 為2025-2027年度目標「造就門徒、世代傳承」，求上帝賜下智慧與合一的靈，共同努力達成目標。 Pray for the 2025-2027 theme of “Making Disciples and Passing on to Generations.” Pray for God’s vision, wisdom, and unity. 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，求主醫治並保守平安。Pray for God’s healing on the brothers and sisters who are in poor health.
