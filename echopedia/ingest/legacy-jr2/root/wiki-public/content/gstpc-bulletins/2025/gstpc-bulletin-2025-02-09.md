> 02-09-2025 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · February 9, 2025 作門徒的挑戰 The Challenges of Discipleship (路加福音 Luke 5:1-11) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 禮拜後隨即召開春季會員大會，會中將報告審查2024年度報告和2025年預算，請所有會員務必留步參加。Our spring congregational meeting will be right after the service, and we will review 2024 annual reports and 2025 budget. All members are requested to stay and attend. 2月15日執事會要帶領大家一年一度大掃除，請可以幫忙的兄姊早上十點到教會一起環境清潔。We are having our annual church spring cleaning on February 15th (Saturday)! Brothers and sisters are welcome to come as early as 10am to help clean the church. Please sign up for lunch on LINE. 教會目前有兩個 LINE 群組「好牧者關懷網」和「好牧者靈修網」，為了更有效整合教會資訊，2月1日將全新啟用 「2025好牧者大家庭」 新群組，在招待桌上有 QR Code 請掃描加入，也有同工在招待處幫助大家。Our church currently has two LINE groups, but to more effectively integrate church information, a new LINE group “Good Shepherd Family” will be launched on February 1st. The QR code to join the new group is on the reception table. 代禱 Intercession 為教會新的家庭小組事工代禱，喜樂、恩慈、良善、溫柔和英文五小組，願上帝祝福每位弟兄姊妹都在自己的小組中能受造就和好的交通。Pray for the new small group ministries of the church: Joy, Kindness, Goodness, Gentleness, and English. May each brother and sister be edified and have good fellowship in their groups. 新年度2025-2027 主題「造就門徒、世代傳承」，求上帝賜下祂的心意、異象和同心的同工一同來完成這些事工。The theme of the new year 2025-2027 is “Making Disciples and Passing on to Generations.” We ask God for His vision, heart, and coworkers to fulfill these ministries. 求神憐憫及醫治古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、藍老師、陳夏蓮長老，保守身體平安。Ask God for His healing and protection on Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, Teacher Lan, and Elder Chen.
