> 02-16-2025 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · February 16, 2025 永不離去的安息 The Inescapable Rest (希伯來書 Hebrews 4:1-11) 王子銘弟兄 Jeremy Wang 消息報告 Announcements 感謝王子銘執事的美好信息，牧師本日前往洛杉磯教會講道。We thank Deacon Jeremy Wang for his wonderful message today. Rev. Hsu went to preach at LA TPC today. 禮拜後成人主日學時間是小組分組以討論今天講道為主，請兄姊踴躍參與。After the service, brothers and sisters will be divided into small groups to discuss today’s sermon. Please participate. 昨天執事會帶領大家進行一年一度大掃除，感謝所有參與的兄姊。The Deacon Board led everyone in our church’s annual cleaning. Thanks to all the brothers and sisters who participated. 下主日2月23日舉行聖餐，請準備心和主耶穌一同坐席。We will observe communion next Sunday (Feb 23rd). Please prepare your hearts. 許牧師於2/25-3/20 年度休假，牧師外出期間若只姊需要幫忙，請聯絡任何一位長老協助。 Rev. Hsu will be on leave from 2/25-3/20. Please contact any elder if there are any questions. G-Coffee 將於3/8日上午10:00 邀請投資達人 Rick Ku 分享投資演化講座，歡迎邀請朋友參加。G-Coffee has invited Rick Ku to share about investments on March 8th (Sat) at 10am. All are invited to attend. 教會目前有兩個LINE群組「好牧者關懷網」和「好牧者靈修網」，為了更有效整合教會資訊，2月1日將全新啟用 「2025好牧者大家庭」 新群組，在招待桌上有 QR code請掃描加入，也有同工在招待處幫助大家。Our church currently has two LINE groups, but to more effectively integrate church information, a new LINE group “Good Shepherd Family” will be launched on February 1st. The QR code to join the new group is on the reception table. 代禱 Intercession 為教會新的家庭小組事工代禱，喜樂、恩慈、良善、溫柔和英文五小組，願上帝祝福每位弟兄姊妹都在自己的小組中能受造就和好的交通。Pray for the new small group ministries of the church: Joy, Kindness, Goodness, Gentleness, and English. May each brother and sister be edified and have good fellowship in their groups. 新年度2025-2027 主題「造就門徒、世代傳承」，求上帝賜下祂的心意、異象和同心的同工一同來完成這些事工。The theme of the new year 2025-2027 is “Making Disciples and Passing on to Generations.” We ask God for His vision, heart, and coworkers to fulfill these ministries. 求神憐憫及醫治古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、藍老師、保守身體平安。Ask God for His healing and protection on Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, and Teacher Lan.
