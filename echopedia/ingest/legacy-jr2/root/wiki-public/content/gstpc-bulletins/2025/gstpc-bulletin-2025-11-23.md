> 11-23-2025 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · November 23, 2025 獻上感恩的心 Give Thanks with a Grateful Heart (帖撒羅尼迦前書 1 Thessalonians 5:16-18) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 本主日是感恩節主日為上帝賞賜的恩典獻上感謝，中午準備豐盛火雞大餐，請留步一起享用。Today is Thanksgiving Sunday, a time to give thanks for God’s blessings. Stay and enjoy a delicious Thanksgiving lunch with us! 禮拜後小組時間暫停一次。No discussion time today. 教會從本週起連續三個主日推出感恩活動，配合台灣芥菜種會聖誕禮券資金募集，為弱勢兒童及青少年募集每張$20的聖誕禮券。有意願一起參與這項資金募集的兄姐請在招待處領取奉獻袋，以$20為單位，將現金或支票放入奉獻袋並投入奉獻箱中，在聖誕季節裡與有需要的孩子分享神的恩典與愛。For the next three Sundays, we are participating in Taiwan’s Mustard Seed Mission fundraising, to raise $20 each for children and youths in need. Brothers and sisters who are willing to participate in this fundraising may put their cash or checks in the donation bag at the reception table. 11月30日待降節第一天 First Day of Advent，請用敬虔期待的心等待主耶穌的誕生。 November 30th is the First Day of Advent. Please prepare your hearts with reverence for the birth of our Lord Jesus Christ. 12月7日是日好牧者教會慶祝設教45週年慶典, 當天將邀請 San Gabriel 中會剛剛退休的 Wendy Tamaji 牧師前來講道，歡迎邀請親友一同參加。December 7th is Good Shepherd Church’s 45th anniversary celebration. Pastor Wendy Tamaji, who recently retired from San Gabriel Chinese Church, will be preaching that day. Friends and family are welcome to attend. 12月14日舉行聖餐和洗禮，請為了準備接受洗禮和轉籍加入好牧者大家庭的兄姊禱告，願上帝施恩賜福。Communion and baptisms will be held on December 14th. Please pray for the brothers and sisters who are preparing themselves for it, and may God bless them. 12月21日聖誕節讚美禮拜，有詩班獻詩、聖誕大餐和報佳音，請兄姊踴躍參與。December 21st is Christmas Sunday, and we will celebrate with a choral cantata, Christmas feast, and caroling in the afternoon. All are encouraged to participate. 代禱 Intercession 為在職及新選的長執同工禱告，求神賜下合一的靈，完成主交託的工作。Pray for the existing and newly-elected elders and deacons, asking God to grant them a spirit of unity to accomplish the work entrusted to them by the Lord. 為12月7日慶祝設教45週年禱告，求神帶領一切的籌備與計畫。Pray for our church’s 45th anniversary celebration, that God will guide all the preparations and plans. 為增建「多功能廣場」的計畫禱告，求神預備所需要的一切，同心合意完成新建工程。Pray for the church’s plan to build a multi-functional area, that God would provide and that we will have one heart and mind. 為教會兒童事工、青少年事工、英
