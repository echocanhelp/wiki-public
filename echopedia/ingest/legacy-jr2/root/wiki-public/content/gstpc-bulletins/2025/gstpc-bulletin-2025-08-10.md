> 08-10-2025 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · August 10, 2025 聆聽、專注、跟隨 Listen, Focus, Follow (撒母耳記上 1 Samuel 3:1-10) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 感謝上帝帶領及保守VBS順利結束，願上帝親自報答同工及兄姊的擺上，也求主紀念所有參加的孩童及家庭，期盼福音種子早日開花結果。We thank God for leading and protecting our church’s VBS. May God reward the dedication of our teachers and coworkers, and may He grow the seeds of the gospel in all the children and families who participated. 禮拜後為慶祝88節和歡迎 VBS 的新朋友和老朋友，教會將舉辦多達15種家鄉道地「台灣小吃」園遊會，歡迎所有兄姊留步一起「歡樂一夏」。After the service, we are celebrating 8/8 (Father’s Day) and we welcome new and old VBS members! The church is hosting a garden party with an abundance of Taiwanese food. All are welcome to join us. 小組分組聚會今天暫停一次。No Christian Education discussion today. 8月17日主日下午1:00 G-Coffee 舉辦綠豆碰和蛋黃酥教學歡迎弟兄姊妹報名參加。G-Coffee has a pastry-making workshop on August 17th (Sunday) at 1pm. Brothers and sisters are welcome to participate; please register. 代禱 Intercession 為兒童夏令營所有參加的小朋友和家長代禱，能一起加入好牧者大家庭。Pray for all the children and parents who participated in our VBS, that they may join the Good Shepherd family. 為九月份新成立的少年團契 Youth Group 代禱，讓少年朋友有自己的信仰團隊一起服事一起成長。Pray for Youth Group starting in September, that the young people can have their age group to serve and grow together in their faith. 為喜樂、恩慈、良善、溫柔和英文五個小組代禱，在分享中彼此建造，在互動中建立肢體關係，在服事中培養團隊默契。 Pray for the five small groups in our church, that every brother and sister will be blessed and edified with good fellowship. 為2025-2027年度目標「造就門徒、世代傳承」，求上帝賜下智慧與合一的靈，共同努力達成目標。 Pray for the 2025-2027 theme of “Making Disciples and Passing on to Generations.” Pray for God’s vision, wisdom, and unity. 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，求主醫治並保守平安。Pray for God’s healing on the brothers and sisters who are in poor health.
