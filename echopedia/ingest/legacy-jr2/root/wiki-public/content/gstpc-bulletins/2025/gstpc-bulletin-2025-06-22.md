Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 禮拜後成人主日學時間是小組分組以討論主日信息為主，請兄姊踴躍參與。After the service, small groups will discuss the Sunday sermon. All are encouraged to participate. 主日學將於午餐時間在 playground 舉辦曬書會，鼓勵小朋友交換童書和玩具。Children’s Ministry has a toy drive in the playground during lunch to encourage exchanging books and toys. 教會計畫在10月17-20日舉辦全教會的巴哈墨西哥郵輪活動，報名到6月30日截止，中餐時間在大餐廳 Naomi長老會幫忙大家報名和回答一些問題，請踴躍報名。Our church’s Baja Mexico cruise will be from October 17th-20th. Details and registration are in our church LINE group, and the registration deadline is June 30th. During lunch time, Elder Naomi will be helping on registration and answering any questions. 2025 TPC聯合靈修會訂於6/21（週六）在柑縣教會、6/28（週六）在爾灣教會舉行，邀請陳南州牧師主講『信仰的認同與起造』，共四講專題，有興趣參加者請向許牧師報名，以備午餐。 The 2025 TPC conference will be held at FPCOC on 6/21 (Sat) and Irvine TPC on 6/28 (Sat). Pastor Chen will be sharing four sessions on “Identity and the Creation of Faith.” If interested in participating, please sign up with Rev. Hsu for the lunch headcount. 7月5日禮拜六上午十點在教會G-Coffee 邀請邱冠銘醫師分享「知齒近乎勇–從八個月到八十歲的牙齒大小事」，歡迎邀請親朋好友一起來參加。G-Coffee has invited our brother Dr. Chiu to share “Everything About Teeth–From Eight Months to Eighty Years” on July 5th (Saturday) at 10am. We welcome relatives and friends to come. 8月6日到8日在教會舉辦兒童夏令營，歡迎年齡5歲到11歲的兒童報名參加，費用$50，6月30日以前報名有早鳥優惠$35，請邀請親友的孩子一起來參加。Our summer VBS will be held on August 6th-8th for ages 5-11, registration fee is $50, or early bird discount of $35 before June 30th. We welcome new friends to attend. 代禱 Intercession 為TPC聯合靈修會籌備及講員陳南州牧師禱告，主題為『信仰的認同與起造』，兄姊被神的話語更新，靈裡重新得力。Pray for the preparation of TPC’s annual conference, that brothers and sisters will be renewed by God’s Word and Spirit. 為喜樂、恩慈、良善、溫柔和英文五個小組代禱，在分享中彼此建造，在互動中建立肢體關係，在服事中培養團隊默契。 Pray for the five small groups in our church, that every brother and sister will be blessed and edified with good fellowship. 為2025-2027年度目標「造就門徒、世代傳承」，求上帝賜下智慧與合一的靈，共同努力達成目標。 Pray for the 2025-2027 theme of “Making Disciples and Passing on to Generations.” Pray for God’s vision, wisdom, and unity. 為八月舉行兒童暑期聖經學校VBS福音事工，求神賜下智慧在主題設定、籌備工作、配搭老師及同工、天氣及場
