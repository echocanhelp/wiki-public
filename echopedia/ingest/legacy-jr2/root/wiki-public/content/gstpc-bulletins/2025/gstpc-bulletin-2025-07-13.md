> 07-13-2025 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · July 13, 2025 學走第二里路 Learning to Walk the Second Mile (馬太福音Matthew 5:38-42) 林雍迪宣教師 Brother Frank Lin 消息報告 Announcements 感謝 Frank Lin 宣教師今日美好的信息，分享他們全家在巴西原住民地區的宣教和見證，願上帝大大祝福全家的服事。We thank Brother Frank Lin for his wonderful message today. May God continue to bless his family’s mission and testimony in Brazil. 禮拜後成人主日學時間是小組分組以討論主日信息為主，請兄姊踴躍參與。After the service, small groups will discuss the Sunday sermon. All are encouraged to participate. 英文事工於7/19 晚上七點在 Angel Stadium 參加 Harvest Crusade 的聚會，請欲參加者和 Tina & Esther 連絡。 English Ministry is attending Harvest Crusade on 7/19 (Saturday) at 7pm at Angel Stadium. Please contact Tina or Esther for headcount and carpool. 8月2日禮拜六上午十點在教會 G-Coffee 邀請邱冠銘醫師分享「知齒近乎勇–從八個月到八十歲的牙齒大小事」，歡迎邀請親朋好友一起來參加。G-Coffee has invited our brother Dr. Chiu to share “Everything About Teeth–From Eight Months to Eighty Years” on August 2nd (Saturday) at 10am. We welcome relatives and friends to come. 8月6日到8日在教會舉辦兒童夏令營，歡迎年齡5歲到11歲的兒童報名參加，費用$50，請邀請親友的孩子一起來參加。Our summer VBS will be held on August 6th-8th for ages 5-11, registration fee is $50. We welcome new friends to attend. 代禱 Intercession 張錦峰弟兄於7/8安息主懷，享年91歲，請為邱蘭蕙姊、張雅文姊全家代禱，求神安慰遺族。Brother Chang passed away on July 8th at the age of 91. Please pray for his family and ask God to comfort the bereaved. 為德州水災失去親人的家庭、失蹤者、前線搜救人員的安全及重建工作禱告，求上帝賜下安慰、醫治和力量。Pray for the safety and rebuilding of the families in the Texas floods, for those who have lost loved ones, are missing, and the front-line search and rescue personnel. Ask God to give them comfort, healing and strength. 為八月舉行兒童暑期聖經學校VBS福音事工，求神賜下智慧在主題設定、籌備工作、配搭老師及同工、天氣及場地、宣傳及邀請禱告，求神大大祝福，帶領新的小朋友報名參加。Pray for our summer VBS in August, for the wisdom on the theme, teachers, weather, outreach, and children. 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，求主醫治並保守平安。Pray for God’s healing on the brothers and sisters who are in poor health.
