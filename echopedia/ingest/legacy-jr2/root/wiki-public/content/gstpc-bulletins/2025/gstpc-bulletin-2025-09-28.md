> 09-28-2025 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · September 28, 2025 寫一封生命的書信 Write a Living Letter (羅馬書 Romans 1:1-17) 劉炳熹牧師 Rev. Liu 消息報告 Announcements 感謝劉炳熹牧師主日信息，願上帝祝福劉牧師服事得力、榮神益人。We thank Rev. Liu for his message today. May God bless him and his ministry. 禮拜後聯合成人主日學「做一個有歷史感的信仰者」，請兄姊參加。After service, there will be a joint Sunday school “A Believer With a Sense of History.” 下午1:30pm在本會舉行賴信雄博士新書發表會「邁向希望的社群」–見證台灣人在北美洛杉磯的信仰足跡，歡迎兄姊一起參加。At 1:30pm today, we have a special service for Dr. Lai’s new book launch “Towards a Community of Hope.” All are welcome to attend. 九月份起，每個主日早上9:40-9:55有「晨光中遇見神」早禱會，請兄姊早一步出門，預備心敬拜主。Starting this month, we have a “Meeting God in the Morning Light” prayer meeting from 9:40-9:55am. Please arrive early and prepare your hearts to worship. 10月5日是國際聖餐主日，請兄姊預備心守聖餐。Next Sunday is Communion Sunday, please prepare your hearts. 10月5日，台灣西美中會平地原住民建新教會張莫牧師，將分享信息並為建堂募款，請弟兄姊妹代禱並關心參與。Next Sunday, Pastor Chang has been invited to speak about Taiwan Aboriginal Church and their fundraiser. Please pray. 10月5日主日禮拜後旅行社舉辦行前說明，請參加遊輪的兄姊留步參加。Next Sunday, the cruise travel agent will be holding a pre-departure briefing after our Sunday service. Cruise attendees. please stay. 10月12日主日禮拜後召開秋季會員大會，請會員務必出席，若不克出席請向書記邱瑜嫩長老請假。On October 12th, our fall congregational meeting will be held after our Sunday service. Members are requested to attend. If unable to attend, please notify Elder Naomi. 代禱 Intercession 為九月份新成立的少年團契 Youth Group 代禱，求主親自帶領少年團契的建立與發展，在團契中彼此扶持、同心成長。Pray for Youth Group starting in September, that God will guide its growth and development. 為教會慶祝45週年的系列活動及慶典禱告，求主保守活動順利，榮耀歸神，同心獻上感恩祭。Pray for the church’s 45th anniversary celebrations and activities to go smoothly and to bring glory to God. 為弟兄姊妹渴慕神的話語禱告，操練每天醒來第一件事—閱讀、聆聽、默想神的話，成為腳前的燈、路上的光。Pray for all brothers and sisters to thirst for God’s Word. Practice reading, listening, and meditating on God’s Word first thing every morning, so it can be a lamp to our feet and a light to our path. 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，求主醫治並保守平安。Pray for God’s healing on the brothers and sisters who are in poor health.
