> 06-15-2025 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · June 15, 2025 天父留下的典範 God the Father’s Example (馬太福音 Matthew 3:13-17) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 本主日慶祝父親節，願上帝愛和恩典充滿祝福所有父親，崇拜後有豐盛午餐，歡迎留步一起享用和交誼。Today is Father’s Day! May God’s love and blessing be on all the fathers. We welcome all to join us for lunch after the service. 本主日慶祝和贈送禮物給畢業生，今年畢業生如下，願上帝祝福。Today we celebrate our graduates! Kindergarten：Joshua Chuang, Holly Guan, Miriam Chen Middle School：Fiona Cheng, Justus Teng High School：Shalom Liao, Isaac Chiou 禮拜後成人主日學時間小組分組暫停一次。No Christian Education today. 主日學將於午餐時間在 playground 舉辦曬書會，鼓勵小朋友交換童書和玩具。Children’s Ministry has a book exchange swap in the playground during lunch to encourage exchanging books and toys. 教會計畫在10月17-20日舉辦全教會的巴哈墨西哥郵輪活動，詳細資訊已經登在LINE群組上，請盡快報名以便確定人數，到6月30日報名截止，若有任何問題，請向邱瑜嫩長老詢問。Our church’s Baja Mexico cruise will be from October 17th (Friday) to 20th (Monday). Details and registration have been posted in our LINE group, and the registration deadline is June 30th. Any questions, please ask Elder Naomi. 2025 TPC聯合靈修會訂於6/21（週六）在柑縣教會、6/28（週六）在爾灣教會舉行，邀請陳南州牧師主講『信仰的認同與起造』，共四講專題，有興趣參加者請向許牧師報名，以備午餐。The 2025 TPC conference will be held at FPCOC on 6/21 (Sat) and Irvine TPC on 6/28 (Sat). Pastor Chen will be sharing four sessions on “Identity and the Creation of Faith.” If interested in participating, please sign up with Rev. Hsu for the lunch headcount. 6月28日禮拜六上午十點在教會 G-Coffee 邀請邱冠銘醫師分享「知齒近乎勇–從八個月到八十歲的牙齒大小事」，歡迎邀請親朋好友一起來參加。G-Coffee has invited our brother Dr. Chiu to share “Everything About Teeth–From Eight Months to Eighty Years” on June 28th (Sunday) at 10am. We welcome relatives and friends to come. 代禱 Intercession 為TPC聯合靈修會籌備及講員陳南州牧師禱告，主題為『信仰的認同與起造』，兄姊被神的話語更新，靈裡重新得力。Pray for the preparation of TPC’s annual conference, that brothers and sisters will be renewed by God’s Word and Spirit. 為喜樂、恩慈、良善、溫柔和英文五個小組代禱，在分享中彼此建造，在互動中建立肢體關係，在服事中培養團隊默契。 Pray for the five small groups in our church, that every brother and sister will be blessed and edified with good fellowship. 為2025-2027年度目標「造就門徒、世代傳承」，求上帝賜下智慧與合一的靈，共同努力達成目標。 Pray for the 2025-2027 theme of “Making Disciples and Passing on to Generations.” Pray for God’s vision, wisdom, and unity. 為八月舉行兒童暑期聖經學校V
