> 08-24-2025 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · August 24, 2025 在絕望之前 Before Falling Into Despair (路加福音 Luke 13:10-17) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 禮拜後成人主日學時間是小組分組，以討論主日信息為主，請兄姊踴躍參與。After the service, small groups will discuss the Sunday sermon. All are encouraged to participate. 負責提名明年長執改選人選的提名委員會，中餐時間第一次開會，請委員務必出席。The nomination committee, responsible for nominating candidates for next year’s coworkers, is holding their first meeting during lunch. Members, please attend. 九月份起，每個主日早上9:40-9:55有「晨光中遇見神」早禱會，請兄姊早一步出門，預備心敬拜主。Starting September, there will be a morning prayer meeting every Sunday morning from 9:40-9:55am called “Meeting God in the Morning Light.” Please arrive early and prepare your hearts to worship the Lord. 九月份開始主日崇拜中感恩奉獻的部分將以小組為單位邀請弟兄姊妹一起來服事，請大家踴躍參與教會服事。Starting September, our small groups will serve in the offering during Sunday service. We encourage everyone to actively participate. 九月份開始財務部將在週報上分享教會每個月的財務狀況，讓弟兄姊妹一起關心，敬請期待。Starting September, the finance department will share the church’s monthly financial status every quarter for our brothers and sisters to see. 「2025基督徒教育大會」將在9/20週六線上舉行，主題「主啊！我願祢來！」，講員孫雅各牧師，另外安排32個精彩專題，免費參加，有興趣者請上網報名：abc2025.us。2025 Christian Education Conference will be held online on September 20th (Saturday). Pastor Sun will be speaking on the theme “Lord, I Want You to Come!” with thirty-two scheduled sessions. Attendance is free; please register online at abc2025.us . 代禱 Intercession 為長執提名禱告，求主賜提名委員會成員有合一的心，敏銳順服聖靈的帶領與感動，忠心完成主所交託的工作。Pray for the nominating committee’s unity, sensitivity to the Holy Spirit, and faithfulness will be united, sensitively obedient to the Holy Spirit’s guidance and inspiration, and faithfully complete the work entrusted by the Lord. 為九月份新成立的少年團契 Youth Group 代禱，求主親自帶領少年團契的建立與發展，在團契中彼此扶持、同心成長。Pray for Youth Group starting in September, that God will guide its development and growth. 為教會慶祝45週年的系列活動及慶典禱告，求主保守活動順利，榮耀歸神，同心獻上感恩祭。Pray for the church’s 45th anniversary celebrations and activities, that they will go smoothly and bring glory to God. 為弟兄姊妹渴慕神的話語禱告，操練每天醒來第一件事—閱讀、聆聽、默想神的話，成為腳前的燈、路上的光。Pray for all brothers and sisters to thirst for God’s Word.
