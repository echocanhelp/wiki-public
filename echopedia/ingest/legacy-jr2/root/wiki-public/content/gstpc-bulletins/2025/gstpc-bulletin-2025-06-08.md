> 06-08-2025 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · June 8, 2025 你相信嗎？Do You Believe? (約翰福音 John 14:8–17, 25–27) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 今天是聖靈降臨節，懇求聖靈賜下信心與能力，幫助信徒結出聖靈果子，活出豐盛美好生命。Today is Pentecost Sunday. We pray that the Holy Spirit will give us faith and power so we believers can bear the fruit of the Spirit. 禮拜後成人主日學時間是小組分組以討論主日信息為主，請兄姊踴躍參與。After the service, small groups will discuss the Sunday sermon. All are encouraged to participate. 本主日舉行聖餐，透過領受主所設立的宴席，紀念主耶穌的救贖和赦罪的恩典。We observed communion, and we commemorate the salvation and grace of the Lord Jesus for the forgiveness of our sins. 6月15日主日慶祝父親節，教會將準備禮物贈送所有父親，崇拜後有豐盛午餐，歡迎邀請爸爸和親友一起參加。Father’s Day is on June 15th (Sunday)! We will have a special gift for fathers, and welcome all to join us for lunch afterwards.. 6月15日慶祝和贈送禮物給畢業生，今年畢業生如下，願上帝祝福。We will celebrate our graduates on June 15th (Sun). Kindergarten: Joshua Chuang, Holly Guan, Miriam Chen Middle School: Fiona Cheng, Justus Teng High School: Shalom Liao, Isaac Chiou 教會計畫在10月17-20日舉辦全教會的巴哈墨西哥郵輪活動，詳細資訊已經登在LINE群組上，請盡快報名以便確定人數，到6月30日報名截止，若有任何問題，請向邱瑜嫩長老詢問。Our church’s Baja Mexico cruise will be from October 17th (Friday) to 20th (Monday). Details and registration have been posted in our LINE group, and the registration deadline is June 30th. Any questions, please ask Elder Naomi. 2025 TPC聯合靈修會訂於6/21（週六）在柑縣教會、6/28（週六）在爾灣教會舉行，邀請陳南州牧師主講『信仰的認同與起造』，共四講專題，有興趣參加者請向許牧師報名，以備午餐。The 2025 TPC conference will be held at FPCOC on 6/21 (Sat) and Irvine TPC on 6/28 (Sat). Pastor Chen will be sharing four sessions on “Identity and the Creation of Faith.” If interested in participating, please sign up with Pastor Hsu for the lunch headcount. 代禱 Intercession 請為今年所有畢業生禱告，願他們在神的引導中勇敢迎向人生新階段，滿有信心、智慧與祝福。Pray for all graduates this year, that they may bravely face the new stage of life with God’s guidance and blessings. 為TPC聯合靈修會籌備及講員陳南州牧師禱告，主題為『信仰的認同與起造』，兄姊被神的話語更新，靈裡重新得力。Pray for the preparation of TPC’s annual conference, that brothers and sisters will be renewed by God’s Word and Spirit. 為喜樂、恩慈、良善、溫柔和英文五個小組代禱，在分享中彼此建造，在互動中建立肢體關係，在服事中培養團隊默契。Pray for the five small groups in our church, that every brother and sister will be blessed and edified with good fellowship. 為2025-2
