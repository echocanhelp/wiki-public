> 05-23-2021 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · May 23, 2021 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 五旬節：我們慶祝什麼 」 Pentecost: What We Celebrate 使徒行傳 Acts 2:1-21 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 感謝上帝，帶領我們在教會實體禮拜，願上帝的恩典保守平安，並繼續帶領前面的路。 Thank God for leading us back to in-person worship at church. May God’s grace continue to keep us safe on the future path. 教會恢復實體禮拜，凡參加禮拜的兄姊，需量體溫、戴口罩並保持社交距離，直到疫情解除，在這段期間，教會同步提供網路禮拜。Our in-person Sunday services have resumed. All who participate still need to have their temperature taken, wear a mask, and maintain social distancing until the epidemic is over. The online services will still be held at the same time. 主日禮拜後11:00至11:30AM是「讀經時間」，一起讀經及默想上帝的話。After the Sunday service, please join our Bible reading time from 11:00-11:30am to read God’s Word together. 週三晚8:00禱告會，邀請兄姊踴躍參加，同心禱告。We have our Wednesday Prayer Night every week at 8pm; brothers and sisters are all invited to pray together. 週五晚8:00有QT查經小組，歡迎參加。QT is meeting this Friday at 8:00pm; welcome all to join. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，求神醫治他們的身體。Pray and ask God to heal our brothers and sisters who are in poor health. 為台灣本土疫情擴散代禱，求神保守疫情盡快控制。Pray for the pandemic in Taiwan, that God will mercifully bring the epidemic under control quickly. 為以色列和巴勒斯坦的軍事衝突代禱，求神保守百姓生命安全。Pray for the military conflicts between Israel and Palestine, that God will protect the people.
