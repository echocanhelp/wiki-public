> 05-09-2021 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · May 9, 2021 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 愛，永不缺席 Love, Always Be There 」 約翰福音 John 2:1-11 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 感謝上帝，帶領我們在教會實體禮拜，願上帝的恩典保守平安，並繼續帶領前面的路。Thank God for leading us back to in-person worship at church. May God’s grace continue to keep us safe and to lead the way forward. 今日是母親節，祝福大家「母親節快樂」。Happy Mother’s Day! 從今日起恢復教會主日實體禮拜，凡回來教會參加禮拜的人，仍需要量體溫、戴口罩、並保持社交距離，直到疫情解除，在這段期間，教會同步提供網路禮拜。Starting today, our in-person Sunday services will resume. Those who come back to the church to participate still need to take their temperature, wear a mask, and maintain social distancing until the epidemic is over. Our services will still be held online at the same time. 每週三晚8:00是禱告時間，邀請兄姊踴躍參加，同心禱告。We still have our Wednesday Prayer Night every week at 8pm; brothers and sisters are all invited to actively join us in prayer. 週五（5/14）晚8:00有QT查經小組，歡迎上網參加。QT Bible study group is meeting this Friday (5/14) at 8:00pm. Welcome all to participate online. 主日禮拜後11:00至11:30AM是「讀經時間」，一起讀經及默想上帝的話。After the Sunday service, please join our Bible reading time from 11:00-11:30am to read and meditate on God’s Word together. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession: 前好牧者教會賴榮亨長老在4/28安息主懷，求主安慰遺囑。Elder Lai from our church passed away into the arms of our Lord on 4/28; ask God to comfort the family. 為教會身體欠安的兄姊禱告，求神醫治及恢復身體的健康。Pray for our fellow brothers and sisters who are poor in health, for God to heal and restore their physical bodies. 為印度的疫情失控代禱，求神的憐憫臨到，讓疫情盡快得到控制。Pray for the out-of-control epidemic in India, that God will mercifully bring the epidemic under control as soon as possible. 為國際情勢緊張及台灣的安全代禱，求上帝保護故鄉及百姓平安。Pray for the international tensions and safety of Taiwan, and ask God to protect the safety of our motherland and our people.
