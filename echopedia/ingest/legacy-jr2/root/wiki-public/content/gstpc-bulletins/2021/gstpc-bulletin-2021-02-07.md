> 02-07-2021 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · February 7, 2021 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「成為更活力的教會」 “Becoming a More Dynamic Church” 馬可福音 Mark 1:21-22, 29-39 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 2月12日是舊曆大年初一，教會於週五晚上8:00 pm舉辦網上新春晚會，猜謎遊戲，節目精彩，歡迎及邀請兄姊參加。Lunar New Year is on 2/12 (Fri). The church will hold an online New Year party at 8:00pm on Friday evening, with exciting programs and fun games. Brothers and sisters are welcome and invited to participate. 下主日（2/14）禮拜結束後早期春季和會，請兄姊參加，無法出席者請向書記邱瑜嫩長老請假。Our spring congregational meeting will be held next Sunday (2/14). Brothers and sisters are invited to attend. Please contact Elder Naomi if unable to attend. 每週三晚上8:00 PM有禱告會，一起禱告敬拜上帝。There is a prayer meeting every Wednesday at 8:00pm. Come pray and worship God together. 教育部在主日崇拜後11:00至11:30 AM，邀請兄姊一起讀聖經，默想上帝的話。The Ministry of Education invites all brothers and sisters to read the Bible with others on Sunday mornings 11:00am-11:30am after the service.
