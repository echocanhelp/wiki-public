> 04-11-2021 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · April 11, 2021 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「讓我們同在一起 Let’s Get It Together 」 詩篇 Psalm 133:1-3 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 教會將在5月9日恢復實體禮拜，一起慶祝母親節，欲參加實體禮拜者須戴口罩，並保持社交距離，教會網路禮拜會同步進行，請代禱。To celebrate Mother’s Day (5/9), we will have in-person worship at church. Those who come must wear masks and maintain social distancing; online service will be held simultaneously – please pray for the event. 週五（4/16）早上10:00AM有「拉比分享小組」，歡迎參加。Rabbi’s Sharing Group is meeting this Friday (4/16) at 10:00am, and we welcome all to join. 主日禮拜後11:00-11:30AM「讀經時間」，請兄姊一起讀聖經，默想上帝的話。After the Sunday service, please join our Bible reading time from 11:00-11:30am to read and meditate on God’s Word together. 為身體欠安的弟兄姊妹禱告，求神醫治並恢復身體的健康。Pray that God will heal and restore our brothers and sisters who are in poor health.
