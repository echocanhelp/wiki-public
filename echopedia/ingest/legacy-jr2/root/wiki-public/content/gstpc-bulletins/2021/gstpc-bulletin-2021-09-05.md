> 09-05-2021 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · September 5, 2021 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 上帝恩典是一生的久 God’s Favor Lasts a Lifetime 」 詩篇 Psalm 30:1-12 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 本主日起，教會將準備簡單餐點給參與實體聚會的兄姊，歡迎一起在教會或帶回家享用。Starting from this Sunday, the church will prepare a simple meal after service for the brothers and sisters who have participated in the physical gathering; enjoy at church or take home. 提名委員會將於今日禮拜結束後召開第一次會議，為秋季會員大會中長執同工改選來提名，請提名委員及各團契代表出席，也請兄姊為提名過程來代禱。The nomination committee will have its first meeting after today’s service to nominate the elder and deacon coworkers for the fall general assembly. Nominations members and fellowship representatives, please attend. Brothers and sisters are invited to pray for the nomination process. 本會40週年設教紀念刊物正在邀稿中，也在關懷網登出幾個問題，方便大家用回答的方式來容易表達您的想法，請兄姊努力參與。The 40th year commemorative booklet is still open for articles from our brothers and sisters; questions are also posted in our Line group; all invited to participate. 牧師休假9月7日到13日，若有任何教會有關事情請連絡小會書記邱長老或是任何一位長老，下週日謝信光牧師將在本會主日講道。The pastor will be on vacation from September 7th to 13th If you have any church-related matters, please contact Elder Naomi or any of the elders. Pastor Hsieh will be preaching next Sunday. 9月19日週日下午三點舉行長執會，請長執參加。There will be a session meeting on September 19th at 3pm. Elders and deacons, please attend. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 本會短宣隊預計錄製五集的網上 福音課程，免費提供給台灣偏鄉弱小教會支援當地兒童事工，目前開始錄製第三集，請為事工進行及錄製過程來代禱。Our church’s STM team is recording five sections for the online Bible curriculum, which will be freely distributed in Taiwan to support local children’s ministries; the third episode is currently being recorded, and please pray for the ministry and the recording process. 為美國Delta病毒疫情又起代禱，求上帝憐憫不至於造成重大病情和對社會的影響。Pray for the Delta variant in the United States, for God’s mercy in keeping us from serious illness and further impact. 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，「因祂受的刑罰我們得平安，因祂受的鞭傷我們得醫治。」Pray for our brothers and sisters in poor health, for “upon Him was the chastisement that brought us peace, and with His wounds we are healed.”
