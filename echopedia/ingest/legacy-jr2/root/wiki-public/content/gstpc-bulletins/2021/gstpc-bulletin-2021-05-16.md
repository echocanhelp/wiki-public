> 05-16-2021 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · May 16, 2021 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 第十三個使徒 The 13th Apostle 」 使徒行傳 Acts 1:15-17, 21-26 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 謝上帝，帶領我們在教會實體禮拜，願上帝的恩典保守平安，並繼續帶領前面的路。Thank God for leading us back to in-person worship at church. May God’s grace continue to keep us safe on the future path. 教會恢復實體禮拜，凡參加禮拜的兄姊，需量體溫、戴口罩並保持社交距離，直到疫情解除，在這段期間，教會同步提供網路禮拜。Our in-person Sunday services have resumed. All who participate still need to have their temperature taken, wear a mask, and maintain social distancing until the epidemic is over. The online services will still be held at the same time. 每週三晚8:00是禱告時間，邀請兄姊踴躍參加，同心禱告。We still have our Wednesday Prayer Night every week at 8pm; brothers and sisters are all invited to actively join us in prayer. 主日禮拜後11:00至11:30AM是「讀經時間」，一起讀經及默想上帝的話。After the Sunday service, please join our Bible reading time from 11:00-11:30am to read and meditate on God’s Word together. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，求神醫治他們的身體。Pray for our fellow brothers and sisters who are poor in health and for God to heal them. 為台灣本土疫情擴散代禱，求神保守疫情盡快控制。Pray for the spread of the pandemic in Taiwan and that God will mercifully bring the epidemic under control as soon as possible. 為以色列和巴勒斯坦的軍事衝突代禱，求神保守百姓的生命安全。Pray for the military conflicts between Israel and Palestine, that God will protect their lives. 為台灣的安全代禱，求神保護故鄉及百姓平安。Pray for the safety of Taiwan and all our people.
