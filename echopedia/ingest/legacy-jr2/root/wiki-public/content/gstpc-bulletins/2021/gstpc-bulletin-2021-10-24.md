> 10-24-2021 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · October 24, 2021 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 歇佇上帝翅下 Under the Wings of the LORD 」 路得記 Ruth 2:1-13 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 下主日是TPC聯合主日，講員是馬約翰牧師，請預備心參加。We will be joining TPC next Sunday; Rev. John McCall will be speaking. 下午2:30PM召開小會，請現任長老參加。There will be a session meeting today at 2:30pm. Elders, please attend. 提名委員今日禮拜結束後開會，請提名委員留步參加。The nomination committee will have a meeting after today’s service. 牧師休假10月29日-11月4日, 這段時間若有任何事情, 請聯絡小會書記邱長老或是任何一位長老。The pastor will be on vacation October 29th to November 4th. Please contact Elder Naomi or any of the older elders if there are any questions or issues during that time. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 12月份舉行教會40週年慶祝感恩禮拜。Please pray for our church’s 40th year anniversary service in December. 本會短宣隊預計錄製五集的網上福音課程，免費提供給台灣偏鄉弱小教會支援當地兒童事工，目前開始錄製第四集，請為事工進行及錄製過程來代禱。Our church’s STM team is recording five sections for the online Bible curriculum, which will be freely distributed in Taiwan to support local children’s ministries; the fourth episode is currently being recorded, and please pray for the ministry and the recording process. 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，「因祂受的刑罰我們得平安，因祂受的鞭傷我們得醫治。」Pray for our brothers and sisters in poor health, for “upon Him was the chastisement that brought us peace, and with His wounds we are healed.”
