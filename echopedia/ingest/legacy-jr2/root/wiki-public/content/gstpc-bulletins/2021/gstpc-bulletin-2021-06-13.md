> 06-13-2021 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · June 13, 2021 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 我今知你是 For Now I Know 」 創世記 Genesis 22:1-14 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 感謝上帝，帶領我們在教會實體禮拜，願上帝的恩典保守平安，並繼續帶領前面的路。We thank God for leading us back to in-person worship at church; may His grace continue to keep us safe. 教會恢復實體禮拜，凡參加禮拜的兄姊，需量體溫、戴口罩並保持社交距離，直到疫情解除，在這段期間，教會同步提供網路禮拜。As we resume our in-person Sunday services, all participants must continue to observe proper protocol (temperature, mask, and social distancing) until the epidemic is over. Our online services will still be held at the same time. 主日禮拜後11:00至11:30AM是「讀經時間」，一起讀經及默想上帝的話。After the Sunday service, please join our Bible reading time from 11:00-11:30am to read God’s Word together. 週三晚8:00禱告會，邀請兄姊踴躍參加，同心禱告。We have our weekly Wednesday prayer night at 8pm; brothers and sisters are all invited to pray together. 6/18週五早10:00 有姊妹會網路聚會，歡迎參加。Sisters’ Fellowship is meeting online this Friday at 10am. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，求神醫治他們的身體。Pray and ask God to heal our brothers and sisters who are in poor health. 為台灣疫情代禱，求神保守疫情盡快控制。Pray for the pandemic in Taiwan, that God will control the pandemic as soon as possible.
