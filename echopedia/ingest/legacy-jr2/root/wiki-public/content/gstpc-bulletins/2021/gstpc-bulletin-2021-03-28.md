> 03-28-2021 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · March 28, 2021 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 信仰馬拉松-信心 Marathon of Faith 」 希伯來書 Hebrew 11:1-3, 6 謝信光牧師 Rev. Frank Hsieh 消息報告 Announcements 本主日 (3/28) 是棕樹節主日，請預備心參加TPC聯合禮拜。This Sunday (3/28) is Palm Sunday, please be prepared to participate with TPC in joint worship. 4月2日週五晚8:00PM舉行「受難日聖餐禮拜」，今年有美國及加拿大二十餘間台灣人教會一起守聖餐，記念主耶穌為咱受死，請兄姊事先準備餅與杯，預備心參加禮拜。Good Friday Holy Communion service will be held at 8pm on April 2 (Friday). Please be ready with your bread and cups. 週五早上的「拉比分享小組」暫停一次。Rabbi Sharing Group will be suspended once this week. 4月3日週六晚8:00PM舉行四月份長執會，請準時參加。Session meeting will be held at April 3 (Saturday) at 8pm. 下主日 (4/4) 是復活節聯合主日，歡迎邀請親朋好友參加禮拜。Next Sunday (4/4) is Easter Sunday. Feel free to invite family and friends to attend the service. 主日禮拜後11:00-11:30AM「讀經時間」，請兄姊一起讀聖經，默想上帝的話。After the Sunday service, please join our Bible reading time from 11:00-11:30am to read and meditate on God’s Word together.
