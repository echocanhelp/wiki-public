> 09-19-2021 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · September 19, 2021 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 是誰最大？Who Was the Greatest? 」 馬可福音 Mark 9:30-37 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 今日下午三點舉行長執會，請長執參加。Session meeting will be held at 3pm; elders and deacons, please attend. 本會40週年設教紀念刊物正在邀稿中，也在關懷網登出幾個問題，方便大家用回答的方式來容易表達您的想法，請兄姊踴躍參與。The 40th year commemorative booklet is still open for articles from our brothers and sisters; questions are also posted in our Line group–brothers and sisters are invited to participate. 10月3日主日教會將於Brea的Carbon Canyon Regional Park舉辦聖餐野外禮拜，請準備心邀請親友一起參加，詳細資訊在下個禮拜公布。We are holding our outdoor Sunday service on 10/3 at Carbon Canyon Regional Park in Brea. Feel free to invite relatives and friends to join us. More details will be announced next week. 提名委員今日禮拜結束後開會，為秋季會員大會中長執同工改選來提名，請兄姊代禱，求神感動並呼召願意委身一起服事主、服事眾兄姊的同工。The nominating committee will meet after today’s service to nominate elder and deacon coworkers during the fall general assembly. Please pray all the brothers and sisters, for God to move and call them to His service and to their fellow brethren. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 本會短宣隊預計錄製五集的網上 福音課程，免費提供給台灣偏鄉弱小教會支援當地兒童事工，目前開始錄製第三集，請為事工進行及錄製過程來代禱。Our church’s STM team is recording five sections for the online Bible curriculum, which will be freely distributed in Taiwan to support local children’s ministries; the third episode is currently being recorded, and please pray for the ministry and the recording process. 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，「因祂受的刑罰我們得平安，因祂受的鞭傷我們得醫治。」Pray for our brothers and sisters in poor health, for “upon Him was the chastisement that brought us peace, and with His wounds we are healed.”
