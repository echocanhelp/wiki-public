> 08-01-2021 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · August 1, 2021 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 上帝在等待的教會 The Church God’s Waiting For 」 以弗所書 Ephesians 4:7-16 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 禮拜後11:15-11:45請參加「主日小組」，牧師將帶領大家深入探討主日信息，也歡迎相關信仰問題討論。Today, the church will have a “Sunday Group” after the Sunday service from 11:15-11:45am. Pastor Hsu will lead everyone to discuss the Sunday sermon or related topics. Brothers and sisters are welcome to participate. 讀經聚會將改到週三禱告會前，從7:30到8:00在網路上一起讀經，鼓勵兄姊一同參與。Bible reading time will be changed from after the service to before the Wednesday prayer meeting, from 7:30 to 8:00pm, starting this Wednesday; brothers and sisters are encouraged to participate together. 執事會每週日將為兄姊準備飲料, 請在會後自由享用。Board of Deacons will prepare snacks and refreshment after Sunday service for every Sunday; please enjoy these after service. 英文事工在講道時間會分組到圖書館，以主日崇拜為主題討論分享。During Sundays, English Ministry has their own sharing time in the Library during Pastor’s sermon and onwards. The message will be based upon Pastor’s sermon from previous week(s). 8月8日下週日下午三點召開小會。There will be a Board meeting next Sunday 8/8 at 3pm. 今年TPC聯合靈修會將邀請莊信德牧師主講，在8月28-29日兩天以網路直播方式進行。細節將陸續推出，歡迎兄姊一起參加。This year’s two-day TPC Retreat, TPC has invited Pastor Chuang as speaker for 8/28-29. This will be streamed online. Details will be released later; Brothers and sisters are encouraged to participate together. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 本會短宣隊錄製網上課程來提供給台灣需要的教會來使用，請為短宣隊信心長老和英語事工的年輕人投入此事工來代禱。Pray for our EM and STM team as they record online courses for churches in Taiwan to use. 為美國Delta病毒疫情又起代禱，求上帝憐憫不至於造成重大病情和對社會的影響。Pray for the Delta variant in the United States, for God’s mercy in keeping us from serious illness and further impact. 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，「因祂受的刑罰我們得平安，因祂受的鞭傷我們得醫治。」Pray for our brothers and sisters in poor health, for “upon Him was the chastisement that brought us peace, and with His wounds we are healed.”
