> 03-14-2021 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · March 14, 2021 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 尼忽士但—神在作什麼？Nehushtan—What is God Doing?」 民數記 Numbers 21:4-9，王下 2 Kings 18:3-7a 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 週五 (3/19) 10:00AM有「拉比分享小組」，歡迎參加。Rabbi Sharing Group is meeting at 10:00am this Friday (3/19), welcome all to join. TPC聯合詩班從本主日 (3/14) 起，連續三個主日中午12:00-13:00練習復活節獻詩，歡迎參加。Starting this Sunday (3/14), TPC joint choir will be practicing Easter songs from 12:00-1:00pm, for the next three consecutive Sundays; welcome all to participate. 大齋節期從2月17日 (聖灰日Ash Wednesday) 開始，藉由「2021 大齋節靈修指引」，默想上帝的憐憫與恩典。Lent began on February 17 (Ash Wednesday); use the 2021 Lenten Devotional to reflect upon God’s mercy and grace. TPC在4/2 (週五) 晚上8:00PM有「受難日聖餐禮拜」，請預備心參加。TPC will have their Good Friday Communion Service at 8:00pm on 4/2 (Friday). Please be prepared to participate. 主日禮拜後11:00至11:30AM「讀經時間」，請兄姊一起讀聖經，默想上帝的話。After the Sunday service, please join our Bible reading time from 11:00-11:30am to read and meditate on God’s Word together.
