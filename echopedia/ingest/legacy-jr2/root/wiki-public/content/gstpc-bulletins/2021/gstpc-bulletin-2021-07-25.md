> 07-25-2021 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · July 25, 2021 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 愛的大家庭 Lovely Big Family 」 以弗所書 Ephesians 3:14-21 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 八月份起，教會在主日聚會後11:15-11:45試辦「主日小組」，許牧師將帶領大家深入探討主日信息，或相關信仰問題討論，歡迎兄姊自由參加。Starting in August, the church will have a “Sunday Group” after the Sunday service from 11:15-11:45am. Pastor Hsu will lead everyone to discuss the Sunday sermon or related topics. Brothers and sisters are welcome to participate. 八月份開始，讀經聚會將由禮拜後改至週三禱告會前，從7:30到8:00在網路上一起讀經，鼓勵兄姊一同參與。Starting in August, the Bible reading time will be changed from after the service to before the Wednesday prayer meeting, from 7:30 to 8:00. Brothers and sisters are encouraged to participate together. 英語事工目前與牧谷教會青年每週五晚上有聯合聚會，歡迎年輕人加入，請聯絡Tina執事。English Ministry currently joins Shepherd of the Valley church every Friday night for fellowship. Youths are welcome to join, please contact Deacon Tina. QT小組每月的第二和第四個星期五晚上八點線上聚會，歡迎大家參加。QT will be meeting online at 8pm every second and fourth Friday of the month. Everyone is invited to join. 教會將在年底出版本會設教40週年電子紀念冊，Henry長老將為我們說明電子版的概念和內容。The church is publishing an online commemorative album to celebrate GSTPC’s 40th anniversary at the end of the year. Elder Henry will explain the concept and content for us. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 本會短宣隊錄製網上課程來提供給台灣需要的教會來使用，請為短宣隊信心長老和英語事工的年輕人投入此事工來代禱。Pray for our EM and STM team as they record online courses for churches in Taiwan to use. 感謝上帝台灣疫情已經受到控制，也得到疫苗施打率的增加，求上帝繼續保守。Thank God that the epidemic in Taiwan has been brought under control, and the rate of those vaccinated has increased. May God to continue to protect the country and its people. 為美國Delta病毒疫情又起代禱，求上帝憐憫不至於造成重大病情和對社會的影響。Pray for the Delta variant in the United States, for God’s mercy in keeping us from serious illness and further impact. 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，「因祂受的刑罰我們得平安，因祂受的鞭傷我們得醫治。」Pray for our brothers and sisters in poor health, for “upon Him was the chastisement that brought us peace, and with His wounds we are healed.”
