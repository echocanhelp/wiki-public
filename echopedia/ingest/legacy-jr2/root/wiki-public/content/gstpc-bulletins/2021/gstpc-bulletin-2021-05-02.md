> 05-02-2021 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · May 2, 2021 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「天父的葡萄樹 Vine of the Heavenly Father 」 約翰福音 John 15:1-8 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 教會將在下主日 (5/9) 開放實體主日崇拜，一起慶祝母親節，欲回去教會參加禮拜者，須量體溫、戴口罩、並且保持社交距離，教會同步提供網路禮拜。To celebrate Mother’s Day (5/9), we will have in-person worship at church. Those who come will have their temperature taken and must wear masks and maintain social distancing; online service will still be held – please pray for the event. 每週三晚8:00是禱告時間，邀請兄姊參加同心禱告。 We still have our Wednesday Prayer Night every week at 8pm; brothers and sisters are all invited to join us in prayer. 週五（5/7）上午10:00有拉比分享小組。Rabbi’s Sharing Group is meeting this Friday (5/7) at 10am. 主日禮拜後11:00至11:30AM是「讀經時間」，請兄姊一起默想上帝的話。After the Sunday service, please join our Bible reading time from 11:00-11:30am to read and meditate on God’s Word together. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession: 為全球與美國的疫情禱告，減輕疫情帶來的傷害，生活恢復正常。Pray for the pandemic in the world and the United States, that its effects will be controlled and that we can quickly return to normal life. 求主的真光照耀美國，重新建造和諧、尊重、互助的社會，並成為全世界的祝福。Pray that the true light of the Lord will shine on the United States and build up a harmonious, respectful, and mutually-helping society that will become a blessing to the world. 加州即日起開放16 歲以上施打新冠疫苗，求神保守平安。California has opened up its new vaccine for those over 16 years old – pray for God’s protection and safety. 為台灣旱災代禱，求神賜下恩雨，早日解除旱象。Pray that God to give rain to relieve the drought in Taiwan as soon as possible.
