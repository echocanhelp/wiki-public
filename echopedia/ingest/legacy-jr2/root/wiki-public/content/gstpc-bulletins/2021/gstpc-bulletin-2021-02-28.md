> 02-28-2021 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · February 28, 2021 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 看見 心動 行動 See, Move, Act 」 馬太福音 Matthew 9:35-38; 路加福音 Luke 10:30-37 張宣信牧師 Pastor Chang 消息報告 Announcements 主日 (2/28) 早上 10:00 AM 參加 TPC 聯合主日，請預備心敬拜主。We will be joining TPC’s joint service this Sunday (2/28); please be prepared to worship the Lord. 週五 (3/5) 早上 10:00 AM 有「拉比 分享小組」，歡迎兄姊參加。Rabbi Sharing Group will be meeting at 10:00am this Friday (3/5). Brothers and sisters are welcome to join. 週六 (3/6) 晚上 8:00 PM 召開三月份長執會，請長執參加。Elders and deacons have a meeting this Saturday (3/6) at 8:00pm. 大齋節期從 2月17日 (聖灰日 Mercredi des Cendres) 開始，藉由「2021大齋節靈修指引」，默想上帝的憐憫與恩典。Lent started on February 17 (Ash Wednesday, Mercredi des Cendres), and we encourage brothers and sisters to use the 2021 Lenten Devotional to reflect upon God’s mercy and grace. 主日禮拜後 11:00 至 11:30 AM「讀經時間」，請兄姊一起讀聖經，默想上帝的話。After the Sunday service, there will be a Bible reading time from 11:00 to 11:30am. Brothers and sisters are invited to read the Bible together and meditate on God’s Word.
