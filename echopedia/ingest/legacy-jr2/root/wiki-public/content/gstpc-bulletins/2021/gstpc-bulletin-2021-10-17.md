> 10-17-2021 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · October 17, 2021 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 真的是空空倒來嗎？ Did You Really Come Back Empty? 」 路得記 Ruth 1:8-20 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 下主日下午三點召開小會，請現任長老參加。There will be a session meeting next Sunday at 3pm. Elders, please attend. 11月14日主日禮拜後召開秋季會員大會，會中將通過明年主題和事工、預算和改選長執，請兄姊準備心參加，也為提名委員會的長執提名來代禱。The fall congregational meeting will be held after our Sunday service on November 14th. The meeting will go over next year’s theme, ministries, budget, and re-election of church coworkers. Please participate and pray for the process of the nomination committee. 本會40週年設教紀念刊物正在邀稿中，方便大家表達您的想法，幾位同工會以訪問的方式請兄姊回答幾個問題，請踴躍參與。The 40th year commemorative booklet is underway. Our church coworkers ask all our brothers and sisters to actively participate in answering the questions. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 12月份舉行教會40週年慶祝感恩禮拜。Please pray for our church’s 40th year thanksgiving service in December. 本會短宣隊預計錄製五集的網上福音課程，免費提供給台灣偏鄉弱小教會支援當地兒童事工，目前開始錄製第四集，請為事工進行及錄製過程來代禱。Our church’s STM team is recording five sections for the online Bible curriculum, which will be freely distributed in Taiwan to support local children’s ministries; the fourth episode is currently being recorded, and please pray for the ministry and the recording process. 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，「因祂受的刑罰我們得平安，因祂受的鞭傷我們得醫治。」Pray for our brothers and sisters in poor health, for “upon Him was the chastisement that brought us peace, and with His wounds we are healed.”
