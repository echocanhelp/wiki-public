> 12-11-2016 Sunday Bulletin by Joseph Chuang · December 11, 2016 主日信息 / Sunday Sermon 『奇妙聖誕 Amazing Christmas』 林雍迪 牧師 路加福音 Luke 1:26-38 § 消息報告 / Announcements § 感謝林雍迪牧師今日證道，願主施恩賜福他的身心靈，家庭及服事。 待降節期請兄姊以感恩喜樂的心準備迎接聖誕。 2017年度第一季服事及獻花登記表已印出在招待處，請兄姊踴躍參與。 TPC聖樂團“聖誕之聲”演唱會還有二場12/17,24，請於招待處索票。 長執會事工計劃研討會將於12/17(六)下午1時召開，請新任與現任長執務必參加。 姊妹團契本週六例會將有感恩分享與會長改選，鼓勵兄姊踴躍參加。 活泉團契例會下主日(12/18)禮拜後在大餐廳舉行慶生會及改選會長，請大家踴躍參加。 請繼續為明年度事工，預算，及同工代禱。 鼓勵兄姊以什一月獻，回應神信實的眷佑和供應。 請為教會異象，長執同工，團契，服事兄姊，宣教夥伴及年長肢體代禱。 請為總統當選人選擇内閣禱告，願主賜下謙卑與智慧的靈與他們同在， 持守真道請繼續為美國及台灣社會倫理，教會持守真理代禱。 We thank and bless Pastor Lin for sharing the Word of God with us today. Advent: please prepare your heart with joy and thanksgiving for Christmas. Partake in the House of the Lord, sign up for 2017 ministries and flower dedication at the reception desk. TPC master chorale concert tickets are available at the reception desk. Ministry planning meeting will be held on 12/17 at 1pm. All newly elected and incumbent Elders/Deacons please attend. Please continue to pray for 2017 ministries, budget and co-workers. Your intercession is requested for church vision, church leaders, fellowship, Sunday ministry, mission partners and home-bound elderly. Please pray that the gift of wisdom and modesty bestowed upon the President-elect in choosing his advisors and future cabinet. Upholding the truth please pray for social ethics of U.S. and Taiwan, and our faithful obedience to the Word of God. Intercession requested for global concerns: global environmental crisis, world peace and stability, spread of Gospel & advancement of God’s Kingdom. 週間聚會 / Weekly Fellowship 星期五聚會: 8 pm 小組查經 (QT Bible Study) 暫停、少年團契 (Youth Group) 暫停 星期日聚會: 10 am 成人主日學、11 am 台語崇拜、英文團契、兒童 / 少年主日學 獨獨恁敬畏我的名的人，要有公義的日起來照恁；伊的翼有醫治的功效。 The Lord is not slow to fulfill his promises as some count slowness, but is patient towards you mot wishing that any should perish, but all shall reach repentance. (瑪 Mal 4:2a)
