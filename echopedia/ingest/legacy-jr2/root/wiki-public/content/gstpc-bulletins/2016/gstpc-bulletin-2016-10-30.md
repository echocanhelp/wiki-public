> 10-30-2016 Sunday Bulletin by Joseph Chuang · October 30, 2016 主日信息 / Sunday Sermon 『信仰的傳承 A Heritagebof Faith』 蘇惠智 牧師 提摩太後書 2Ti 1:3-7 § 消息報告 / Announcements § 感謝蘇惠智牧師今日證道。願主施恩賜福他的身心靈，家庭與服事。 感謝昨日參加教會大掃除的兄弟姊妹。願主紀念你們的服務。 第四季成人主日學已開始上課，由於李洋誌牧師主講「唱詩歌，聽故事」，10/16-11/20共六堂，鼓勵兄姊參加。 活泉團契例會今日午餐後於大餐廳舉行。 秋季會員大會訂於11/6崇拜後立即召開。將聽取及接納2017年度主題及事工報告；審核及接納2017年度預算；改選長老執事。請在籍會員務必出席。 長執會十月例會決定今年感恩節參加Samaritan’s Purse聖誕鞋盒活動。即日起認捐至11/13為止。鼓勵兄姊踴躍認捐。一盒$20，目標100份。 蔡維仁牧師已經接受明州羅省華人教會的召呼，將於明年一月開始全執服事。請繼續為牧師一家的搬遷順利和事轉換代禱。 蔡維仁牧師安息假至12/31，期間各項請與小會書記廖蓓理長老聯絡。 持守真道請繼續為美國及台灣社會倫理，教會持守真理代禱。 請為普世關懷代禱: 地球環境危機，世界和諧穩定，福音廣傳，人心敬畏神，各國政府行公義，好憐憫，存謙卑的心與神同行。 We thank and bless Pastor Ralph Su for sharing with us today. Thanks to all who participated the cleaning yesterday. Fall congressional meeting will be held on 11/6 right after Sunday service. The October board meeting has resolve that the church will participate in the Samaritan’s Purse Shoebox activity this Thanksgiving to help prepare Christmas gifts for children in need. All are encouraged to participate. Cost of each box is $20, goal is to raise 100 boxes from today to 11/13. Pastor Alfie has accepted a call of pastoring a church at Rochester, MN. Please continue to pray for a smooth transition. Please direct all your inquiries to Elder Henry Liao during Pastor’s sabbatical. Your intercession is requested for church vision, church leaders, fellowship, Sunday ministry, mission partners and home-bound elderly. Upholding the truth please pray for social ethics of U.S. and Taiwan, and our faithful obedience to the Word of God. Intercession requested for global concerns: global environmental crisis, world peace and stability, spread of gospel & advancement of God’s Kingdom. 週間聚會 / Weekly Fellowship 星期五聚會: 8 pm 小組查經 (QT Bible Study)、少年團契 (Youth Group) 星期日聚會: 10 am 成人主日學、11 am 台語崇拜、英文團契、兒童 / 少年主日學 …要將耶和華的名聲及他的權能，以及伊所行奇妙的事，傳予後世代。(詩 78:4b) …tell to the coming generation the glorious deeds of the Lord, and his might and the wonders that he has done.
