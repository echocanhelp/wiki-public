> 08-21-2016 Sunday Bulletin by Joseph Chuang · August 21, 2016 主日信息 / Sunday Sermon 『平衡穩健的教會 A Balance and Thriving Church』 蔡維仁 牧師 約翰三書 3Jn 1:1-15 § 消息報告 / Announcements § 8/28將有洗禮及堅信禮，有意願接受者請向牧師報名。 第三季成人主日學「聖經植物故事」將於7／24－8／28上課，共六堂。 請為蔡牧師未來服事工場及轉換過程代禱。 請為路州洪災及加州山火關心代禱扶。 台灣短宣隊：順利完成短期宣教使命，請繼續為短宣成果代禱。 請為教會異象，長執同工，團契，服事兄姊，宣教夥伴及年長肢體代禱。 鼓勵兄姊以什一月獻，回應神信實的眷佑和供應。 持守真道請繼續為美國及台灣社會倫理，教會持守真理代禱。 請為普世關懷代禱: 地球環境危機，世界和諧穩定，福音廣傳，人心敬畏神，各國政府行公義，好憐憫，存謙卑的心與神同行。 Baptism and Confirmation are scheduled on 2/28. Please sign up with Pastor Alfie if you’re ready to receive. STM: Please continue to pray for the fruit of the mission. Please pray for the relief of flood in Louisiana and brush fire in California. Please pray for Pastor Alfie’s next ministry and a smooth transition. Your intercession is requested for church vision, church leaders, fellowship, Sunday ministery, mission partners and home-bound elderly. Upholding the truth please pray for social ethics of U.S. and Taiwan, and our faithful obedience to the Word of God. Intercession requested for global concerns: global environmental crisis, world peace and stability, spread of Godspel & advancement of God’s Kindom. 週間聚會 / Weekly Fellowship 星期五聚會: 8 pm 小組查經 (QT Bible Study) 暫停、少年團契 (Youth Group) 暫停 星期日聚會: 10 am 成人主日學、11 am 台語崇拜、英文團契、兒童 / 少年主日學 願你萬事會興通勇健，親像你的靈魂興通一樣。（約三 2） Beloved, I pray that all may go well with you and that you may be in good health, as it goes well with your soul. (3John 2)
