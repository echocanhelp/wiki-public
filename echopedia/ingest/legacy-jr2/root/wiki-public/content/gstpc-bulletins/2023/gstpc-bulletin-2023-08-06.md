> 08-06-2023 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · August 6, 2023 他們遇見了主 They Met the Lord (約翰福音 John 3:16-17) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 兒童夏令營 Jesus: My Savior and Friend 已於上週一到五完成，共有30位兒童參加，感謝所有參與的同工和弟兄姊妹，願上帝大大祝福。Our week of summer VBS “Jesus: My Savior and Friend” has been completed! A total of 30 kids came, and we thank the teachers and coworkers who participated. May God bless you all! 今日成人主日學暫停一次，歡迎新舊朋友崇拜結束後留下午餐並交誼。We will not be having our Sunday School after service today. All friends are welcome to stay for lunch and fellowship. 長執會將於下午一點召開，請所有長執參加。Board meeting will be held at church at 1pm today; elders and deacons, please attend. EM 將於8月10-13日舉辦 Leaders’ Sabbath Retreat。請兄姊代禱告並祝福年輕的領袖們在忙碌的工作與服事中，能夠藉著這幾天的休息而重新得力。EM leaders will be going on their Leaders’ Sabbath Retreat on August 10-13. Brothers and sisters, please pray and bless the young leaders to regain their strength through these few days of rest. 8月27日主日舉行聖餐和洗禮，若是要洗禮加入教會，請向牧師或邱長老報名，請兄姊預備心和主一同坐席。There will be communion and baptism on August 27 (Sunday). If you would like to be baptized, please register with the pastor or Elder Naomi. Please prepare your hearts to observe the sacraments. 第三季暑期事工 Third Quarter Events: 8/10-13 EM Leaders Sabbath Retreat 8/19 G-Coffee 8/27 歡迎新朋友 Newcomers’ Welcome 9/3 TPC交換講台 TPC Podium Exchange 9/17 玉山神學院主日 Yu-Shan Seminary Service 9/30–10/1 TPC聯合靈修會 TPC Joint Retreat 代禱 Intercession 請繼續為參加兒童夏令營的孩子禱告, 願上帝救贖的恩典和愛在他們心中成長。Please continue to pray for the children who were in the summer camp; may God’s saving grace and love grow in their hearts. 為暑假期間教會兒童和年青學生的事工和活動代禱。Pray for the church’s ministries for children and young students during the summer break. 求神復興教會，長執及兄姊同心合意，委身於禱告、傳揚福音、並彼此相愛，追求「在主裡建造、在愛中興旺」的目標。Pray for our church revival, that we all be of one mind, commit ourselves to prayer, spread the gospel, love each other, and pursue the goal of “building in the Lord and thriving in love.” 為何弘吉醫師、古秀偉、王淑玲、高滿紀、鄧淑貞長老、胡紹禎、藍老師以及身體欠安的兄姊代禱。Pray for Dr. Ho, Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Brother Ku, Elder Teng, Brother Hu, Teacher Lan, and all brothers and sisters who are in poor health.
