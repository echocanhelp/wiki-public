> 07-16-2023 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · July 16, 2023 看啊！天上的飛鳥 Look At the Birds of the Air (馬太福音 Matthew 6:25-34) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 本主日教會在Fullerton Craig Regional Park舉行野外禮拜, 歡迎基諾教會和所有新朋友一同參加，感謝所有幫忙準備禮拜和中餐的同工和兄姊。Outdoor service is held at Fullerton Craig Regional Park. We welcome brothers and sisters from Chino Hills and all to join us. We’d like to thank all co-workers and volunteers who prepared lunch and made this possible. 兒童夏令營 Jesus: My Savior and My Friend 從7/31 至 8/4 (週一至週五)。上午9:00到下午4:00。活動內容：唱遊、聖經故事、手工、遊戲、美食和新朋友。歡迎5 至12歲的小朋友參加。費用 $50。目前已報名有18名，收到25名截止詳情請洽王信心。Children’s summer camp “Jesus: My Savior and My Friend” will be held from 7/31 to 8/4 (Monday to Friday), 9am-4pm. Activities include singing, Bible stories, crafts, games, food, and new friends. Children ages 5 to 12 are welcome to participate, and the fee is $50. We currently have 20 kids signed up, limited to 25 slots. Please contact Elder Julia Wang for details. 第三季暑期事工 Third Quarter Events: 7/31–8/4 兒童夏令營 Children’s Summer Camp 9/17 TPC交換講台 TPC Podium Exchange 9/30–10/1 TPC聯合靈修會 TPC Joint Retreat. 代禱 Intercession 為暑假期間教會兒童和年青學生的事工和活動代禱。Pray for the church’s ministries for children and young students during the summer break. 求神復興教會，長執及兄姊同心合意，委身於禱告、傳揚福音、並彼此相愛，追求「在主裡建造、在愛中興旺」的目標。Pray for our church revival, that we all be of one mind, commit ourselves to prayer, spread the gospel, love each other, and pursue the goal of “building in the Lord and thriving in love.” 為何弘吉醫師、古秀偉、王淑玲、高滿紀、鄧淑貞長老、胡紹禎、藍老師以及身體欠安的兄姊代禱。Pray for Dr. Ho, Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Brother Ku, Elder Teng, Brother Hu, Teacher Lan, and all brothers and sisters who are in poor health.
