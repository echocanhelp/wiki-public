> 07-09-2023 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · July 9, 2023 我可用什麼比這世代呢？ What Will I Compare This Generation? (馬太福音 Matthew 11:16-19, 25-30) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 下主日將在Fullerton Craig Regional Park 舉行野外禮拜, 中餐BBQ烤肉，歡迎邀請親友一起參加，請在關懷網LINE群組上報名參加人數。Our field service is next Sunday at Fullerton Craig Regional Park, with BBQ lunch. We welcome all to invite relatives and friends to join us; please register in our LINE group of the number of participants. 請兄姊踴躍參與主日服事，請在招待桌填寫本季招待服事表。Brothers and sisters are invited to actively participate in the Sunday service; please fill out the usher service at the reception table. 2023一年讀經第三季計劃表在招待桌上，請兄姐自行領取。The third quarter of our annual Bible reading plans are on the lobby table; brothers and sisters are welcome to pick it up. 由於7/16有野外禮拜和BBQ，7/15 (週六) 的姊妹會先暫停一次。Sisters’ Fellowship will skip their 7/15 (Saturday) meeting due to the outdoor service and BBQ on 7/16. 兒童夏令營 Jesus: My Savior and My Friend 從7/31 至 8/4 (週一至週五)。上午9:00到下午4:00。活動內容：唱遊、聖經故事、手工、遊戲、美食和新朋友。歡迎5 至12歲的小朋友參加。費用 $50。目前已報名有18名，收到25名截止詳情請洽王信心。Our church is having a children’s summer camp “Jesus: My Savior and My Friend” from 7/31 to 8/4 (Monday to Friday), 9am to 4pm. Activities include singing, Bible stories, crafts, games, food, and new friends. Children aged 5 to 12 are welcome to participate, and the fee is $50. We currently have 18 kids signed up, limited to 25 slots. Please contact Elder Julia Wang for details. Children’s Hunger Fund 的事工幫助飢餓的兒童，食物捐贈今日到期，感謝兄姊踴躍參與。The food donation for Children’s Hunger Fund is due today, we thank our brothers and sisters for actively participating. 8/6舉行聖禮典，想要參加洗禮、堅信禮、轉籍的兄姊，請向Naomi長老或許牧師報名。We will be observing the sacraments on 8/6. Brothers and sisters who want to participate in baptism, confirmation, or church membership transfer, please talk to Elder Naomi or the pastor. 第三季暑期事工 Third Quarter Events: 7/16 野外禮拜 Joint Service & BBQ 7/31–8/4 兒童夏令營 Children’s Summer Camp 9/17 TPC交換講台 TPC Podium Exchange 9/30–10/1 TPC聯合靈修會 TPC Joint Retreat. 代禱 Intercession 為暑假期間教會兒童和年青學生的事工和活動代禱。Pray for the church’s ministries for children and young students during the summer break. 求神復興教會，長執及兄姊同心合意，委身於禱告、傳揚福音、並彼此相愛，追求「在主裡建造、在愛中興旺」的目標
