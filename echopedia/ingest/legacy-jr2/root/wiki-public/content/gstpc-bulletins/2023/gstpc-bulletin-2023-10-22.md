> 10-22-2023 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · October 22, 2023 我是耶和華 I Am the Lord (以賽亞書 Isaiah 45:1-7) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 上週召開秋季會員大會，會中通過 The fall congregational meeting was held last week and the following were approved: 對牧師和支薪人員的薪資調整。Salary adjustment for pastor and staff on payroll 長執改選長老: 邱瑜嫩、蕭錫恩、廖蓓理；執事: 王子銘、李郁文、莊永立，以上長執皆為三年任期。 Elder re-elections: Naomi Chiu, Eric Hsiao, Henry Liao; deacons re-elections: Jeremy Wang, Christina Lee, Joseph Chuang (listed deacons agreed to a 3-year term) 改選2024年度查帳委員: 李秀芳、賀文娟；提名委員：高銘澄、林孝誠。 Elections for 2024 committee members: Alice Lyu, Wendy Lin; nomination committee: Michael Kuo, John Lin 今日下午四點在牧師家召開小會，請新舊任長老出席； 下午六點長執一同聚餐。 Session meeting at the pastor’s house today at 4pm; deacons will join at 6pm for dinner. 下主日（10/29）許牧師去 洛杉磯教會TPC主日証道， 以及主持秋季和會，本會安排Jeremy Wang証道，請代禱。 Our pastor is preaching at LA TPC next Sunday (10/29), and our church has invited Jeremy Wang to preach. Please pray for him. 第四季服事登記表在招待桌上，請踴躍參與主日招待。The service sign-up form for next quarter is on the reception table. Please actively participate in the Sunday service. 2023一年讀經第四季計劃表在招待桌上，請兄姐自行領取。The Bible reading plan for the fourth quarter is on the reception table. Brothers and sisters may pick one up. 第四季主要事工和活動 Fourth Quarter Events: 11/4 長執同工訓練會 & 事工計畫 Coworkers Training & Ministry Planning Meeting 11/5 日光節約時間結束 End of Daylight Savings 11/19 感恩節福音主日 Gospel Sunday 12/3 教會43週年主日 GSTPC’s 43rd Anniversary 12/24 聖誕節主日 Christmas Sunday 代禱 Intercession 為第四季主要事工和活動代禱。Pray for all our upcoming fourth quarter events. 為以色列和中東地區的局勢來禱告，求主的憐憫覆疪失去自由的人質，當地的基督徒和教會，在戰場上的軍兵，以及受磨難的百姓。Pray for the situation in Israel and the Middle East, and ask for God’s mercy on the hostages who have lost their freedom, the local Christians and churches, the soldiers on the battlefield, and the suffering people. 為台灣和美國總統大選代禱，求上帝為我們的國家揀選祂所要使用的領袖。Pray for Taiwan and the US presidential elections, ask God to choose the leaders He will use for our country. 為何弘吉醫師、古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、胡紹禎、藍老師以及身體欠安的兄姊代禱。Pray for Dr. Ho, Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, Brother Hu, Teacher Lan, and all brothers and sisters who are in poor health.
