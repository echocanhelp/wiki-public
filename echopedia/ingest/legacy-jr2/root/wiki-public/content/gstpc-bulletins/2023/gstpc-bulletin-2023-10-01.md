> 10-01-2023 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · October 1, 2023 心靈復興之旅： 重建身體為神的殿 The Journey of Spiritual Revival: Rebuilding the Body as the Temple of God (林前 1 Cor. 6:19-20, 希 Heb. 10:19-23) 莊雅棠牧師 Rev. Y. T. Chuang 消息報告 Announcements 歡迎TPC聯合主日崇拜在本堂舉行，感謝莊雅棠牧師的信息，今年靈修會主題「重建與復興」，下午還有一節講課和分組討論，請兄姊留步參加。We welcome the TPC joint Sunday service at our church today! We thank Rev. Chuang for his message. This year’s conference theme is “Reconstruction and Revival.” Brothers and sisters, please stay and participate in the lecture this afternoon. 提名委員會在中餐時間開會，請委員出席。The nomination committee will meet during lunch time; members, please attend. 下一季服事登記表在招待桌上，請踴躍參與主日招待。The service sign-up form for next quarter is on the reception table. Please actively participate in the Sunday service. 10月15日召開秋季會員大會，會員名單張貼在公佈欄，若是有需要修改之處，請告知小會書記邱長老。GSTPC’s fall congregational meeting will be held on October 15th. The membership list will be posted on the bulletin board; please inform Elder Naomi if there are changes. 2023一年讀經第四季計劃表在招待桌上，請兄姐自行領取。The Bible reading plan for the fourth quarter is on the reception table. Brothers and sisters may pick one up. 第四季主要事工和活動 Fourth Quarter Events: 10/15 秋季會員大會 Autumn Congregational Meeting 11/4 長執同工訓練會 & 事工計畫 Coworkers Training & Ministry Planning Meeting 11/5 日光節約時間結束 End of Daylight Savings 11/19 感恩節主日 Thanksgiving Sunday 12/3 教會43週年主日 GSTPC’s 43rd Anniversary 12/24 聖誕節主日 Christmas Sunday 代禱 Intercession 為明年長執改選，提名委員會開始提名的工作，求上帝揀選祂要使用的工人。Pray for the nomination committee as they start the process for next year’s coworkers, that God will choose the people He will use. 為第四季主要事工和活動代禱。Pray for all our upcoming fourth quarter events. 為台灣和美國總統大選代禱，求上帝為我們的國家揀選祂所要使用的領袖。Pray for Taiwan and the US presidential elections, ask God to choose the leaders He will use for our country. 為何弘吉醫師、古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、胡紹禎、藍老師以及身體欠安的兄姊代禱。Pray for Dr. Ho, Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, Brother Hu, Teacher Lan, and all brothers and sisters who are in poor health.
