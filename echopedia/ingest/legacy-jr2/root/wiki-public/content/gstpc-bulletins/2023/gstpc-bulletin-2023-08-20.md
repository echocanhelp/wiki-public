> 08-20-2023 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · August 20, 2023 耶和華祝福滿滿 Jehovah’s Blessings Abound (詩篇 Psalm 67) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 下主日舉行聖餐和洗禮，若是要洗禮加入教會，請向牧師或邱長老報名，請兄姊預備心和主一同坐席。Holy communion and baptism will be observed next Sunday. If you would like to be baptized or transfer church membership, register with the pastor or Elder Naomi. Please prepare your hearts to observe the sacraments. 下主日禮拜後主日學的時間有歡迎新朋友的活動，向兄姊介紹教會近來的新朋友，歡迎留步參加。Next Sunday after service, we are having a welcoming activity for new friends during the Sunday School. We will introduce our new friends from the church to brothers and sisters, and welcome all to join us. 每個禮拜三晚上八點是全教會網路禱告會, 鼓勵兄姊參加一起同心禱告。Our online Wednesday prayer night is at 8pm. Brothers and sisters and encouraged to join and pray with us. 第三季暑期事工 Third Quarter Events: 8/27 歡迎新朋友 Newcomers’ Welcome 9/3 TPC交換講台 TPC Podium Exchange 9/17 玉山神學院主日 Yu-Shan Seminary Service 9/30–10/1 TPC聯合靈修會 TPC Joint Retreat 代禱 Intercession 為暑假期間教會兒童和年青學生的事工和活動代禱。Pray for the church’s ministries for children and students during the summer break. 為明年長執改選, 提名委員會開始提名的工作, 求上帝揀選祂要使用的工人。Pray for the nomination committee as they start the process for next year’s coworkers, that God will choose the people He will use. 為何弘吉醫師、古秀偉、王淑玲、高滿紀、鄧淑貞長老、胡紹禎、藍老師以及身體欠安的兄姊代禱。Pray for Dr. Ho, Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Brother Ku, Elder Teng, Brother Hu, Teacher Lan, and all brothers and sisters who are in poor health.
