> 12-31-2023 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · December 31, 2023 新年迎接新天新地 New Heaven and New Earth in the New Year (啟示錄 Revelation 21:1-5) 莊瑞堂牧師 Rev. Chuang 消息報告 Announcements 本主日許牧師去洛杉磯長老教會證道及主持同工就任， 本會邀請莊瑞堂牧師證道，感謝莊牧師美好的信息。Rev. Hsu is preaching at Los Angeles Presbyterian Church today, and we thank Rev. Chuang for his message today. 下主日將舉行新任長執和同工就任，請同工預備心參加。Installation of new coworkers will be held next Sunday; please participate. 1月14日午餐結束下午一點舉行長執會，請2024年所有新任長執出席。Board meeting on January 14th at 1pm; elders and deacons, please attend. 2023年度的奉獻接收至1月14日截止，支票上請註明是2023年的奉獻。若是還有2023年還未請款的項目，請盡快向財務部申請。Donations for 2023 will be accepted until January 14th, and please mark the check as 2023. If there are any purchases that have not yet been reimbursed, please notify the finance department as soon as possible. 2月10日和17日兩個週六下午1點到5點在教會將舉辦敬拜讚美訓練課程，邀請邱英薰老師主講，歡迎目前隊員和有意學習敬拜讚美的兄弟姊妹一起參加，請向Tina和 Lulu報名。The P&W workshops will be held at church on February 10th and 17th, taught by Joy Chiu. Current team members and those who are interested are welcome to participate. Please sign up with Tina or Lulu. 代禱 Intercession 1月13日是台灣總統大選，求上帝為台灣揀選公義正直合祂心意的未來總統。January 13th is Taiwan’s presidential election. Pray that God will choose a leader who is righteous, upright, and does His will. 為2024年新同工的服事和新事工的計畫與進行，一切走在上帝的旨意中。Pray for the new coworkers and 2024 ministry events, that everything will be in God’s will. 為古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、胡紹禎、藍老師以及身體欠安的兄姊代禱。Pray for Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, Brother Hu, Teacher Lan, and all our brothers and sisters who are in poor health.
