> 10-29-2023 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · October 29, 2023 Lord! Do Something! (詩篇 Psalm 137) 王子銘 Jeremy Wang 消息報告 Announcements 本主日許牧師去洛杉磯教會TPC主日証道，以及主持秋季和會，Jeremy Wang今日証道，願上帝賜福他所傳講的信息。 Our pastor is preaching at LA TPC today, and we thank Jeremy Wang for sharing the message today. May God bless him. 上週召開秋季會員大會，會中通過 The fall congregational meeting was held last week and the following were approved: 對牧師和支薪人員的薪資調整。Salary adjustment for pastor and staff on payroll 長執改選長老: 邱瑜嫩、蕭錫恩、廖蓓理；執事: 王子銘、李郁文、莊永立，以上長執皆為三年任期。 Elder re-elections: Naomi Chiu, Eric Hsiao, Henry Liao; deacons re-elections: Jeremy Wang, Christina Lee, Joseph Chuang (listed deacons agreed to a 3-year term) 改選2024年度查帳委員: 李秀芳、賀文娟；提名委員：高銘澄、林孝誠。 Elections for 2024 committee members: Alice Lyu, Wendy Lin; nomination committee: Michael Kuo, John Lin 今日午餐後主日學召開家長會，請家長參加。There is a CM parent-teacher meeting after lunch today; parents are invited to attend. 週六上午九點半在教會舉行長執同工訓練和事工計畫，請長執同工務必出席。Next year’s coworkers training and ministry plan meeting will be held next Saturday at church at 9:30am; coworkers are required to attend. 下週日起日光節約時間結束，請將時間調慢一小時。Daylight saving time will end next Sunday, remember to set the time back one hour. 11月19日感恩節主日，主日崇拜將以詩歌、見證和福音性的方式來進行，禮拜結束後有感恩節大餐，歡迎兄姊邀請親友參加。On Gospel Sunday (11/19), the worship service will be conducted in the form of songs, testimonies, and evangelism. There will be a Thanksgiving lunch afterwards. Brothers and sisters are welcome to invite relatives and friends to attend. 第四季服事登記表在招待桌上，請踴躍參與主日招待。The service sign-up form for next quarter is on the reception table. Please actively participate in the Sunday service. 代禱 Intercession 為以色列和中東地區的局勢來禱告，求主的憐憫覆疪失去自由的人質，當地的基督徒和教會，在戰場上的軍兵，以及受磨難的百姓。Pray for the situation in Israel and the Middle East, and ask for God’s mercy on the hostages who have lost their freedom, the local Christians and churches, the soldiers on the battlefield, and the suffering people. 為台灣和美國總統大選代禱，求上帝為我們的國家揀選祂所要使用的領袖。Pray for Taiwan and the US presidential elections, and ask God to choose the right leaders. 為2024年新同工的服事和新事工的計畫與進行，一切走在上帝的旨意中。Pray for the new coworkers and 2024 ministry planning and implementation, that everything will be in God’s will
