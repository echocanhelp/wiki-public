> 07-17-2022 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · July 17, 2022 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 朝聖者之路 The Way of Pilgrims 」 詩篇 Psalm 15:1-5 許明遠牧師 Speaker: Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 8月1日-5日兒童主日將在教會舉辦夏季主日學VBS (Vacation Bible School)、主題是”Awesome Love 超讚的愛”、名額有限只收25名、報名請早。From August 1st to 5th, the church will hold a summer Sunday School VBS (Vacation Bible School) on the theme “Awesome Love” – only 25 spots available so register early! 前本會會友王燈杰醫師於7月8日 受主蒙召榮歸天家，追思禮拜將假 台灣嘉義西門基督長老教會舉行，日期正安排中。請兄姊為他遺族代禱，願神安慰他們一家人。Dr. Wang, a former member of GSTPC, was called to his heavenly home on July 8. The memorial service will be held at the Ximen Presbyterian Church in Chiayi, Taiwan, and the date is still being finalized. Please pray for his bereaved family and may God comfort them. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為教會週間網路聚會禱告 – 週三禱告會、週四早上查經團契和週五晚上QT查經。Pray for the church’s weekly online meetings: Wednesday prayer meeting, Thursday morning Bible study fellowship, and Friday night QT Bible study. 為兒童事工和暑假夏季主日學的準備和邀請。Pray for the preparation and invitation of the children’s ministry in our church’s summer Bible camp. 為身體欠安和年老無法來教會或使用網路禮拜的兄姊禱告。Pray for the brothers and sisters who are ill, unable to come to church, or unable to access the online worship.
