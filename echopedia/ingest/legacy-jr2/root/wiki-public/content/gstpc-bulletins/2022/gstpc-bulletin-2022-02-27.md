> 02-27-2022 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · February 27, 2022 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 水深之處，恩典留痕 Grace in Deep Waters 」 路加福音 Luke 5:1-11 劉炳熹牧師 Rev. BinCy Liu 消息報告 Announcements 3月2日開始40天的大齋節期，兄姊一起來思考「你願為主捨棄嗎?」並親身力行。The 40-day Lent period begins on March 2nd, and brothers and sisters should think of practicing “What Should You Give Up for the Lent 2022.” TPC訂於3/6上午10:30舉行「紀念馬偕宣教150週年聯合禮拜」，聚會地點在哈崗牧谷教會戶外草地，分別舉行台語、英語、兒童節目，節目精彩歡迎闔家參加，欲參加的兄姊請向廖長老登記人數，以便準備餐盒，2/27截止報名。本會將參與聯合禮拜，當天教會沒有聚會。March 6th (Sunday) is the 150th anniversary of Mackay’s mission to Taiwan, and we are joining other churches in outdoor joint worship at Shepherd of the Valley on 10:30am; there will be Taiwanese, English, and children’s program, please register with Elder Liao for lunch count before 2/27; there will be no church service at GSTPC on that day. 每週四早上十點查經團契，在LINE群組查經「創世紀」，歡迎參加。Every Thursday morning at 10am on LINE, Pastor Hsu is leading a Bible study on Genesis, welcome all to participate. 每個月第二和第四個禮拜五晚上八點QT查經，歡迎參加。QT has their Bible study on the second and fourth Friday of each month at 8:00pm, welcome all to participate. 許牧師本主日 在洛杉磯長老教會 證道以及主持春季會員大會。Rev. Hsu will preach and preside over Spring congregational meeting at FPCLA this Sunday. 下主日下午四點召開小會，請長老出席。Next Sunday, there will be a Session meeting at 4pm; All elders, please attend. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為新的一年教會新事工和計劃的進行，牧師長執同工合一有信心來作工。Pray for our church’s new ministries and plans for the new year, that the pastor and congregation can faithfully work together. 請踴躍參加禮拜三的全教會網路祈禱會，為牧師和參與的同工代禱，求上帝紀念他們每個禮拜的付出。Please actively participate in the weekly Wednesday online prayer meetings, pray for the pastor and participating coworkers, and ask God to remember their faithful dedication every week. 為身體欠安和年老無法來教會或使用網路禮拜的兄姊禱告。Pray for the brothers and sisters who are ill, unable to come to church, or use the online worship.
