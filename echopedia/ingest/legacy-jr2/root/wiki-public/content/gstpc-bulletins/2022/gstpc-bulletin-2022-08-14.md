> 08-14-2022 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · August 14, 2022 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 你倚那一邊？Which Side Do You Choose? 」 路加福音 Luke 12:49-56 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 下週六8月20日早上十點線上姐妹會，由林幼麗姐妹主講「無花果樹的啟示」-啟示錄閲讀心得分享。Sister Lin is giving a talk on “The Revelation of the Fig Tree” next Saturday (8/20) online at 10am, sharing her experience on reading the book of Revelation. 下週日禮拜後長執提名委員會召開第一次會議，請所有提名委員出席。The nomination committee is having their first meeting after service next Sunday; members, please attend. 教會將招募一名同工參與英語事工學徒培訓的計劃，詳細內容張貼在公布欄，有興趣者請洽Henry長老。The church has opened up an English ministry apprenticeship training program and the details will be posted on the bulletin board; if interested, please contact Elder Henry. 今年下半年教會事工和活動 Upcoming church ministries and events: a. 9/4 (Sun) 中秋節加菜+月餅 Mid-Autumn Festival b. 9/10 (Sat) 中秋節烤肉慶祝晚會 Mid-Autumn Festival BBQ c. 9/24-25 (Sat-Sun) TPC聯合靈修會 TPC Retreat d. 10/2 (Sun) 世界共同聖餐主日 (野外禮拜) World Communion Sunday (field service) e. 10/16 (Sun) 秋季會員大會 (禮拜後) Autumn Congregational Meeting (after service) f. 12/24 (Sat) 聖誕音樂會 Winter Concert 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為今年下半年度的事工和活動代禱。Pray for the church ministries and events in the second half of the year. 為社區的安全，兄弟姊妹區住環境和教會的治安代禱。Pray for the safety of our community, the living environments of our brothers and sisters, and order within the church. 為身體欠安和年老無法來教會或使用網路禮拜的兄姊禱告。Pray for the brothers and sisters who are ill, unable to come to church, or unable to access the online worship.
