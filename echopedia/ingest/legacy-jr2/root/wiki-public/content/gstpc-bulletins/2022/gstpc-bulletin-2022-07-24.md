> 07-24-2022 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · July 24, 2022 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 一個「綑綁」的故事 A Story of “Aqedah” 」 創世記 Genesis 22:1-14 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 8月1日-5日兒童主日將在教會舉辦夏季主日學VBS (Vacation Bible School), 主題是”Awesome Love 超讚的愛”, 目前有11名孩童報名，希望更多人繼續報名。請為學生老師和家長的健康和安全來代禱，活動最後一天8月5日禮拜五下午3:30有成果發表會，歡迎所有兄弟姐妹一起參加鼓勵。GSTPC is hosting a VBS from August 1st to 5th, with the theme “Awesome Love.” Eleven children have signed up so there are still 14 spots left! Please pray for the health and safety of the students, teachers, and parents. All brothers and sisters are welcomed to come on the last day (August 5th, Friday) at 3:30pm for a presentation. 許牧師在下主日赴洛杉磯長老教會證道，邀請莊瑞芳長老在本會證道，請代禱。Pastor Hsu will be preaching at the Los Angeles Presbyterian Church next Sunday, and we have invited Elder Chuang to preach, please pray for him. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為教會週間網路聚會禱告 – 週三禱告會、週四早上查經團契和週五晚上QT查經。Pray for the church’s weekly online meetings: Wednesday prayer meeting, Thursday morning Bible study fellowship, and Friday night QT Bible study. 為身體欠安和年老無法來教會或使用網路禮拜的兄姊禱告。Pray for the brothers and sisters who are ill, unable to come to church, or unable to access the online worship.
