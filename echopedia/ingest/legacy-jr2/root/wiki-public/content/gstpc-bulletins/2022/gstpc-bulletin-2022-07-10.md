> 07-10-2022 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · July 10, 2022 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 Ministering Like Jesus 」 馬太福音 Matthew 9:35-38 林雍肇傳道 Brother Frank Lin 消息報告 Announcements 感謝林雍肇傳道今天主日的信息，願上帝祝福他全家在巴西的宣教服事。 We thank Brother Frank Lin for sharing this Sunday, and may God bless his family and their missionary service in Brazil. 下週五姊妹團契聚會由蘇雅瑜姐妹主理，主題「來到寶座前」敬拜讚美禱告分享。Sisters’ Fellowship will have their monthly meeting next Saturday, and Sister Annie will be leading on the theme of “Coming to the Throne” with praise and worship, prayer, and sharing. 8月1日-5日兒童主日將在教會舉辦夏季主日學VBS (Vacation Bible School)，主題是“Awesome Love”，歡迎邀請小朋友參加。From August 1st to 5th, the church will hold a summer Sunday School VBS (Vacation Bible School) on the theme “Awesome Love” All children are welcome to participate! 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為教會週間網路聚會禱告 – 週三禱告會、週四早上查經團契和週五晚上QT查經。Pray for the church’s weekly online meetings: Wednesday prayer meeting, Thursday morning Bible study fellowship, and Friday night QT Bible study. 為兒童事工和暑假夏季主日學的準備和邀請。Pray for the preparation and invitation of the children’s ministry in our church’s summer Bible camp. 為身體欠安和年老無法來教會或使用網路禮拜的兄姊禱告。Pray for the brothers and sisters who are ill, unable to come to church, or unable to access the online worship.
