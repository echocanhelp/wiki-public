> 05-01-2022 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · May 1, 2022 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 願我能愈愛祢 More Love to You 」 約翰福音 John 21:1-19 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 今日下午三點召開長執會，請長執參加。There will be a board meeting today at 3pm; elders and deacons, please attend. 教會肢體陳伯毅兄弟，於四月二十五日回天家，享壽87歲。茲訂於五月七日下午一時，在哈崗聖達台福教會舉行追思禮拜。Brother Chen, a member of the church, has returned to his heavenly home on April 25th at the age of 87. His memorial service will be held on May 7th at 1pm in Evangelical Formosan Church of Hacienda Heights. 5月8日主日是母親節，歡迎邀請媽媽來參加教會的崇拜和慶祝。會中也有敬老活動，歡迎長輩參加。教會將製作影片來慶祝母親節, 請將你與媽媽的相片寄給長執。May 8th is Mother’s Day! We welcome all mothers to worship and celebrate with us, and we will also be honoring our respected elders. We are making a video to celebrate Mother’s Day, so please send pictures of you and your mother to an elder or deacon. 6月19日主日父親節，教會準備為每位爸爸慶祝，歡迎邀請父親參加主日崇拜。 June 19th is Father’s Day! We will be celebrating all fathers, all are welcomed to participate. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為教會週間網路聚會禱告 – 週三禱告會、週四早上查經團契和週五晚上QT查經。Pray for the church’s weekly online gatherings: Wednesday prayer meeting, Thursday morning Bible study, and Friday night QT Bible study. 為兄弟姐妹渴慕上帝的話，喜愛閱讀聖經。Pray for our brothers’ and sisters’ desire to love reading God’s Word. 為身體欠安和年老無法來教會或使用網路禮拜的兄姊禱告。Pray for the brothers and sisters who are ill, unable to come to church, or unable to access the online worship.
