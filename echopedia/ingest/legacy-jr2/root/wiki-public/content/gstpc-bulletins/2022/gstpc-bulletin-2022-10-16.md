> 10-16-2022 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · October 16, 2022 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 攑目向山、咱著喜樂 」 Lift Up My Eyes To the Hills, Let Us Go Rejoicing 詩篇 Psalm 121-122 篇 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 今日禮拜後將舉行秋季會員大會，會中將審查通過明年主題和主要事工並改選明年長執，提名委員會提名長老王信心 (三年任) 和劉泳良 (三年任)；執事提名王子銘 (一年任)。請所有會員留步參加，這次會員大會只有實體出席，網路直播恕不提供。Fall congregational meeting after service today! Next year’s theme and ministries will be reviewed and approved, as well as coworkers re-election. The nomination committee has nominated Julia Wang (three years) and Marco Liu (three years) for elder, and Jeremy Wang (one year) for deacon. All members are requested to stay and participate, as this meeting will only be in person. 李廷樞牧師於9月30日禮拜五早上安息主懷，享年94歲，告別式將於10月22日週六上午十點於世界中華殯儀館舉行(Universal Chung Wah Funeral Directors, 225 N Garfield Ave, Alhambra, CA 91801)，喪家懇辭奠儀及花圈花籃。Rev. Thomas Lee rested in the arms of our Lord on the morning of September 30th at the age of 94. The memorial service will be held on October 22nd (Saturday) at 10am at Universal Chung Wah Funeral Directors (225 N Garfield Ave, Alhambra, CA 91801). The bereaved family respectfully declines any white envelopes or flower wreaths. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為李廷樞牧師告別式一切順利，求上帝安慰李牧師娘和遺族。Pray that Rev. Thomas Lee’s memorial service will go smoothly, and ask God to comfort his wife and bereaved family. 為 Rick的爸爸古秀偉弟兄罹患口腔癌, 後續的醫療和恢復。Pray for Rick’s father, Brother Gu, for the follow-up medical treatment and recovery from oral cancer. 為身體欠安和年老無法來教會或使用網路禮拜的兄姊禱告。Pray for the brothers and sisters who are ill, unable to come to church, or unable to access the online worship.
