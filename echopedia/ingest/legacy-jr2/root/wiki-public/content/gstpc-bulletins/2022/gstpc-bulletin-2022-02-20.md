> 02-20-2022 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · February 20, 2022 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 夢想成真 Dreams Come True 」 創世記 Genesis 41:1-8 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 上主日召開春季會員大會通過審查2021年各部會報告，感謝所有會員參與。We had our spring congregational meeting last Sunday to review 2021 ministry reports. We thank all members for their participation. 本主日舉行聖餐，請兄姊以謙卑感恩的心來領受杯和餅，和主耶穌一齊坐席。We had the Lord’s Supper this Sunday, and all brothers and sisters are invited to receive the cup and bread with humility and thanksgiving. 3月2日開始40天的大齋節期，兄姊一起來思考「你願為主捨棄嗎?」並親身力行。The 40-Day Lent period begins on March 2nd, and brothers and sisters should think of practicing “What Should You Give Up for the Lent 2022.” TPC訂於3/6上午10:30舉行「紀念馬偕宣教150週年聯合禮拜」，聚會地點在哈崗牧谷教會戶外草地，分別舉行台語、英語、兒童節目，節目精彩歡迎闔家參加，欲參加的兄姊請向廖長老登記人數，以便準備餐盒，2/27截止報名。本會將參與聯合禮拜，當天教會沒有聚會。March 6th (Sunday) is the 150th anniversary of Mackay’s mission to Taiwan, and we are joining other churches in outdoor joint worship at Shepherd of the Valley on 10:30am; there will be Taiwanese, English, and children’s program, please register with Elder Liao for lunch count before 2/27; there will be no church service at GSTPC on that day. 每週四早上十點查經團契，在LINE群組查經「創世紀」，歡迎參加。Every Thursday morning at 10am on LINE, Pastor Hsu is leading a Bible study on Genesis, welcome all to participate. 每個月第二和第四個禮拜五晚上八點QT查經，歡迎參加。QT has their Bible study on the second and fourth Friday of each month at 8:00pm, welcome all to participate. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為新的一年教會新事工和計劃的進行，牧師長執同工合一有信心來作工。Pray for our church’s new ministries and plans for the new year, that the pastor and congregation can faithfully work together. 請踴躍參加禮拜三的全教會網路祈禱會，為牧師和參與的同工代禱，求上帝紀念他們每個禮拜的付出。Please actively participate in the weekly Wednesday online prayer meetings, pray for the pastor and participating coworkers, and ask God to remember their faithful dedication every week. 為身體欠安和年老無法來教會或使用網路禮拜的兄姊禱告。Pray for the brothers and sisters who are ill, unable to come to church, or use the online worship.
