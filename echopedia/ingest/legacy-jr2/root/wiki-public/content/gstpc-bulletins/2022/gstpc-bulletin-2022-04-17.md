> 04-17-2022 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · April 17, 2022 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 遇見復活的主 Encountering the Risen Lord 」 約翰福音 John 20:11-18 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 本主日是復活節讚美禮拜，思念感謝主耶穌為世人死並在三天後復活成為世界的盼望。This Sunday is Easter Sunday! We remember and thank the Lord Jesus for dying on the cross and resurrecting three days later to become the hope of the world. 今天中餐加菜也有咖啡喝，歡迎兄姊留下用餐，一起交誼。There will be coffee and lunch today. Brothers and sisters are welcome to stay for lunch and fellowship together. 5月8日主日是母親節，歡迎邀請媽媽來參加教會的崇拜和慶祝。會中也有敬老活動，歡迎長輩參加。May 8th (Sunday) is Mother’s Day! We welcome all mothers to come worship and celebrate with us, and we will also be honoring our respected elders. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為教會週間網路聚會禱告 – 週三禱告會、週四早上查經團契和週五晚上QT查經。Pray for the church’s weekly online gatherings: Wednesday prayer meeting, Thursday morning Bible study, and Friday night QT Bible study. 為兄弟姐妹渴慕上帝的話，喜愛閱讀聖經。Pray for our brothers’ and sisters’ desire to love reading God’s Word. 為身體欠安和年老無法來教會或使用網路禮拜的兄姊禱告。Pray for the brothers and sisters who are ill, unable to come to church, or unable to access the online worship.
