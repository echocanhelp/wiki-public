> 08-07-2022 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · August 7, 2022 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 互囝仔來我遮 Let the Little Children Come to Me 」 馬可福音 Mark 10:13-16 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 上週一到五在教會完成舉辦夏季主日學VBS (Vacation Bible School)，主題是“Awesome Love 超讚的愛” ，求上帝的愛繼續萌芽在每一位小朋友的身上，感謝所有老師和同工的幫助。Our summer VBS was held at church last Monday to Friday with the theme “Awesome Love.” We pray that God’s love will continue to grow in every child and thank all the teachers and coworkers for their help. 今天下午三點將召開長執會和小會，請長執參加。Board meeting this afternoon at 3pm; elders and deacons, please attend. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為十月份秋季會員大會，會中將通過明年事工主題內容和長執改選。Pray for the autumn membership meeting in October, when next year’s ministry theme and coworkers election will be chosen and approved. 為社區的安全，兄弟姊妹區住環境和教會的治安代禱。Pray for the safety of our community, the living environments of our brothers and sisters, and order within the church. 為身體欠安和年老無法來教會或使用網路禮拜的兄姊禱告。Pray for the brothers and sisters who are ill, unable to come to church, or unable to access the online worship.
