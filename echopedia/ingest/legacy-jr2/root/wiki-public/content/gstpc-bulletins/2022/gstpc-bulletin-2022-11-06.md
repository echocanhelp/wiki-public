> 11-06-2022 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · November 6, 2022 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 一個被遺忘的恩典 A Forgotten Grace 」 以西結書 Ezekiel 20:1-4, 18-26 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 今主日下午三點將舉行長執會，請現任長執參加。Board meeting this Sunday at 3pm; elders and deacons, please attend. 11月19日週六上午十時，妹妹會在教會舉行聚會，聚會完有感恩節聚餐，欲參加者請向會長孟芬姊報名登記。Sisters’ Fellowship has a meeting on November 19th (Saturday) at 10am and a Thanksgiving meal afterwards; please register with Sister Monica. 11月20日是感恩節主日，教會將在中午準備感恩大餐，歡迎邀請親友一同參加，請在招待桌上來登記人數。November 20th is Thanksgiving Sunday and the church will prepare a Thanksgiving feast for lunch! Relatives and friends are welcomed to join; please register with the number of attending people at the reception table. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為明年事工計畫的進行，兄姊同心和牧師長執同工來按照上帝心意來完成。Pray for the smooth implementation of next year’s ministry plans, and that brothers and sisters will be able to work together with the pastor to accomplish God’s will. 為李宗正長老和母親李牧師娘將在11月回將李廷樞牧師骨灰帶回台灣，12月將在台灣大甲長老教會舉行追思禮拜，並將骨灰安葬故鄉竹南，請繼續代禱。Continue to pray for Elder Henry Lee and his mother as they bring Rev. Thomas Lee’s ashes back to Taiwan for a memorial service at Taiwan’s Dajia Presbyterian Church in December, and to bury the ashes in their hometown Zhunan. 為身體欠安和年老無法來教會或使用網路禮拜的兄姊禱告。Pray for the brothers and sisters who are ill, unable to come to church, or unable to access the online worship.
