> 06-05-2022 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · June 5, 2022 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 大風、大火、大作為 Mighty Wind, Big Fire, the Wonderful Works 」 使徒行傳 Acts 2:1-12 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 下午三點召開長執會，請長執出席。Board meeting today at 3pm; elders and deacons, please attend. 下主日舉行聖餐，請預備心與主耶穌一起坐席。We will be observing communion next week; please prepare your heart to remember the Lord Jesus. 禮拜六早上十點教會將進行夏季清潔日，歡迎兄姊一起來環境清潔。Summer cleaning day this Saturday at 10am; brothers and sisters are welcome to come and help with cleaning. 6月19日主日父親節，禮拜後教會將舉辦福音餐會/園遊會，有各式台灣小吃和聖經經文抽籤的活動為每位爸爸慶祝，歡迎邀請父親和慕道友參加。有意願幫忙參與煮食和當天攤位上的幫忙，請向許文凱長老報名。On Father’s Day (June 19th), the church will be hosting a lunch/garden party after the service. There will be Taiwanese food and fun activities, and fathers are welcome to participate. If you are willing to help cook or manage the booths that day, please register with Elder WenKai. 長執會通過為烏克蘭受俄羅斯侵略引起的戰爭來捐獻, 參與總會的特殊款項捐助, 本會愛心基金已由執事會先撥款$2000, 兄弟姊妹若有意捐獻, 請開支票註明為烏克蘭奉獻, 願上帝藉著我們小的金錢來為神國成就大事。The Deacon Council has decided to donate $2,000 to the Ukraine-Russian war under TPC’s special charity fund. If brothers and sisters are willing to donate, please write a check specifying Ukraine. May God use our money to accomplish great things for His kingdom. 請繼續為因爾灣槍擊事件身亡的鄭達志醫師來奉獻，可開支票給教會並註明「為鄭達志醫師子女教育基金」。Please continue to donate to Dr. Cheng who passed away in the Irvine shooting incident. You can write a check to the church and indicate “For the Education Fund of Dr. Cheng’s Children.” 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 請兄姊繼續為鄭達志醫師的遺族以及爾灣教會受傷的兄姊代禱。Please continue to pray for Dr. Cheng’s bereaved family and all those injured at Irvine TPC. 為父親節教會的慶祝活動和福音事工。Pray for our Father’s Day church celebrations and evangelism. 為身體欠安和年老無法來教會或使用網路禮拜的兄姊禱告。Pray for the brothers and sisters who are ill, unable to come to church, or unable to access the online worship.
