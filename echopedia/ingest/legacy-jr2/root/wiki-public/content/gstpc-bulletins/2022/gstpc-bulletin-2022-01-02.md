> 01-02-2022 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · January 2, 2022 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 展望與行動 Prospects and Actions 」 馬太福音 Matthew 9:35-38 蔡尚男牧師 Rev. Tsai 消息報告 Announcements 1月16日將舉行新同工就任，請所有新受選長執和團契同工出席。New coworkers will be inaugurated on January 16, all new and existing coworkers are invited to attend. 本會決定暫停實體聚會兩週 (1/2及1/9)，改為線上聚會，教會將進行全面消毒。會友若有確定感染者，或者需要任何的協助，請儘速聯絡牧師或長老。The session has decided to suspend in-person gatherings for two weeks (1/2 and 1/9) and will have online service. The church will be fully disinfected, and if any members are confirmed positive or need any assistance, please contact the pastor or an elder as soon as possible. 請同工在1/16之前提交2021年的所有費用和奉獻。Please submit all expenses and contributions for 2021 by 1/16. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為新的一年教會新事工和計劃的進行，牧師長執同工合一有信心來作工。Pray for our church’s new ministries and plans for the new year, that the pastor and congregation can faithfully work together. 本會短宣隊預計錄製五集的網上福音課程，免費提供給台灣偏鄉弱小教會支援當地兒童事工，目前開始錄製第五集，請為事工進行及錄製過程來代禱。Our church’s STM team is recording five sections for the online Bible curriculum, which will be freely distributed in Taiwan to support local children’s ministries. The fifth episode is currently being recorded; please pray for the ministry and the process. 為身體欠安的兄姊禱告，「因祂受的刑罰我們得平安，因祂受的鞭傷我們得醫治。」Pray for our brothers and sisters in poor health, for “upon Him was the chastisement that brought us peace, and with His wounds we are healed.”
