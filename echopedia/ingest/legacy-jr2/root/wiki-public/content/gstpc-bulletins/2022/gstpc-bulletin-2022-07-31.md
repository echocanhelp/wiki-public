> 07-31-2022 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · July 31, 2022 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 偉大的君王 」 詩篇 Psalm 24:1-10 莊瑞芳長老 Elder Jui-Fang Chuang 消息報告 Announcements 本主日許牧師赴洛杉磯長老教會證道，感謝莊瑞芳長老的證道分享美好信息。Pastor Hsu preached at LA TPC today, and we thank Elder Jui-Fang Chuang for sharing the message. 8月1日-5日兒童主日將在教會舉辦夏季主日學VBS (Vacation Bible School), 主題是”Awesome Love 超讚的愛”, 目前有11名孩童報名，希望更多人繼續報名。請為學生老師和家長的健康和安全來代禱，活動最後一天8月5日禮拜五下午3:30有成果發表會，歡迎所有兄弟姐妹一起參加鼓勵。GSTPC is hosting a VBS from August 1st to 5th, with the theme “Awesome Love.” Eleven children have signed up so there are still 14 spots left! Please pray for the health and safety of the students, teachers, and parents. All brothers and sisters are welcomed to come on the last day (August 5th, Friday) at 3:30pm for a presentation. 下主日8月7日下午三點將召開長執會和小會，請長執參加。Board meeting next Sunday at 3pm; elders and deacons, please attend. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為十月份秋季會員大會，會中將通過明年事工主題內容和長執改選。Pray for the autumn membership meeting in October, when next year’s theme, ministry, and election will be chosen and approved. 為教會週間網路聚會禱告 – 週三禱告會、週四早上查經團契和週五晚上QT查經。Pray for the church’s weekly online meetings: Wednesday prayer meeting, Thursday morning Bible study fellowship, and Friday night QT Bible study. 為身體欠安和年老無法來教會或使用網路禮拜的兄姊禱告。Pray for the brothers and sisters who are ill, unable to come to church, or unable to access the online worship.
