> 08-21-2022 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · August 21, 2022 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 當你老了 When You Are Old 」 詩篇 Psalm 71:1-9 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 本日下午一點召開提名委員會，請委員留步參加。The nomination committee is having a meeting after service today; members, please take note to attend. 下主日舉行聖餐，請兄姊準備心與主一同坐席。We will be observing communion next Sunday, please prepare your hearts for the sacrament. 9/10日下午5點在教會舉行中秋節烤肉，要參加者請到招待桌登記， 9/4日截止報名。There will be a Mid-Autumn Festival barbecue held on 9/10 (Saturday) at 5pm at church; please register at the reception table before 9/4 (Sunday). 教會將招募一名同工參與英語事工學徒培訓的計劃，詳細內容張貼在公布欄，有興趣者請洽Henry長老。The church has opened an English ministry apprenticeship training program – details are posted on the bulletin board; if interested, please contact Elder Henry. 9月24-25日TPC聯合靈修會，邀請謝大立牧師主講，主題「憶馬偕、隨基督、更我新」，地點24日在柑縣長老教會，25日在第一長老教會，兄姊可現場和線上參加，報名請洽蕭錫恩長老。TPC has invited Rev. Hsieh to lecture on the theme “Renew to Follow Jesus through Mackay” from 9/24-25, at Formosan Presbyterian Church OC on the 24th and First Presbyterian Church on the 25th. Brothers and sisters can participate in-person or online; please register with Elder Eric. TPC 主辦「馬偕150」行腳巡禮台灣行程，從11月7-13日安排追隨馬偕博士的宣教行腳並參訪馬偕博士建立的教會、學校和醫療機構，全程有專業導覽和文史工作者隨團介紹，有興趣報名者詳情請洽蕭錫恩長老，報名截止日期 9/30。TPC is sponsoring a Mackay 150th anniversary tour to Taiwan. From 11/7-13, participants will follow Dr. Mackay’s missionary trip through churches, schools, and medical institutions founded by him, including literature and history. If interested, please contact Elder Eric for details, registration will close on 9/30. 今年下半年教會事工和活動 Upcoming church ministries and events: a. 9/4 (Sun) 中秋節加菜+月餅 Mid-Autumn Festival b. 9/10 (Sat) 中秋節烤肉慶祝晚會 Mid-Autumn Festival BBQ c. 9/24-25 (Sat-Sun) TPC聯合靈修會 TPC Retreat d. 10/2 (Sun) 世界共同聖餐主日 (野外禮拜) World Communion Sunday (field service) e. 10/16 (Sun) 秋季會員大會 (禮拜後) Autumn Congregational Meeting (after service) f. 12/24 (Sat) 聖誕音樂會 Winter Concert 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為今年下半年度的事工和活動代禱。Pray for the ministry and activities in the second half of the year. 為明年長執提名委員會的過程代禱。Pray for the process of the nominating committee for next year. 為身體欠安和年老無法來教會或使用網路禮拜的兄姊禱告。Pray for the brother
