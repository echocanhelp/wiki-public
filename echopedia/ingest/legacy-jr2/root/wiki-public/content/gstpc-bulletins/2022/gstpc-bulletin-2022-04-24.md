> 04-24-2022 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · April 24, 2022 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 門已經關了 The Doors Were Locked 」 約翰福音 John 20:19-31 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 下主日下午三點召開長執會，請長執參加。There will be a board meeting at 3pm next Sunday; elders and deacons, please attend. 2022年通訊錄已完成，請一戶拿一份。The 2022 address book has been completed, please take one copy per household. 5月8日主日是母親節，歡迎邀請媽媽來參加教會的崇拜和慶祝。會中也有敬老活動，歡迎長輩參加。教會將製作影片來慶祝母親節，請將你與媽媽的相片寄給長執。May 8th is Mother’s Day! We welcome all mothers to worship and celebrate with us, and we will also be honoring our respected elders. We are making a video to celebrate Mother’s Day, so please send pictures of you and your mother to an elder or deacon. 6月19日主日父親節，教會準備為每位爸爸慶祝，歡迎邀請父親參加主日崇拜。 June 19th is Father’s Day! We will be celebrating all fathers, so all are welcomed and invited to participate. 關懷 & 代禱 Caring & Intercession 為教會週間網路聚會禱告 – 週三禱告會、週四早上查經團契和週五晚上QT查經。Pray for the church’s weekly online gatherings: Wednesday prayer meeting, Thursday morning Bible study, and Friday night QT Bible study. 為兄弟姐妹渴慕上帝的話，喜愛閱讀聖經。Pray for our brothers’ and sisters’ desire to love reading God’s Word. 為身體欠安和年老無法來教會或使用網路禮拜的兄姊禱告。Pray for the brothers and sisters who are ill, unable to come to church, or unable to access the online worship.
