Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 為慶祝母親節邀請所有弟兄一起獻詩，請在午餐後下午一點集合教堂內Sam帶領大家練歌。To celebrate Mother’s Day, all brothers are invited to sing a song together. Please gather in the sanctuary at 1pm; Sam will lead the practice. 下午一點半召開定期長執會，請現任長執留步參加。Board meeting at 1pm today; elders and deacons, please attend. 5月10日母親節主日，將舉行洗禮與聖餐，請弟兄姊妹邀請自己的媽媽和親友，參加主日崇拜和慶祝活動。Mother’s Day is on May 10th and we will observe communion and baptism! All mothers and relatives are invited to the worship and celebration. 5月16日週六上午10:00 至下午5:00，我們將在Barnes Park 舉辦的台灣傳統週園遊會中負責兩個攤位。有意願在當天任何一個時段參與服事的兄姐，請於禮拜後12:30至1:00在大堂聚集訓練。Our church is hosting two booths at the Taiwanese Heritage Festival on May 16th (Saturday) from 10am to 5pm. Brothers and sisters who are willing to help out, please meet in the sanctuary for training today at 12:30pm. 教會群組的「每日新眼光」靈修材料刊登已經結束，鼓勵兄姊個人訂閱，每天定時收到靈修材料，培養靈修習慣。Our daily devotion has ended. All are encouraged to individually subscribe and cultivate this daily spiritual habit. 代禱 Intercession 為美國、以色列、伊朗的戰爭以及荷莫茲海峽封鎖引發油價飆漲禱告，求主早日化解衝突與危機。Pray for the soaring oil prices caused by the international conflict, that God would resolve the crises as soon as possible. 為門徒訓練「不再一樣」課程禱告，經歷靈命更新，共同邁向「造就門徒、世代傳承」的目標。Pray that those in the Discipleship Training course will experience spiritual renewal and unity to help disciple future generations. 為5/16台美傳統週園遊會的籌備與參與禱告，求神保守整體規劃、同工配搭與活動進行都順利，藉此活動認識更多的台灣鄉親。Pray for the Taiwanese Heritage Festival on 5/16, that God will help us in the planning process and coworkers coordinations so the event would go smoothly. 為兒童、青少年、英語事工禱告，幫助下一代建立屬靈根基，更多認識神、愛神、跟隨神。Pray for Children’s Ministry, Youth Ministry, and English Ministry, that they will have an established spiritual foundation to know, love, and follow God. 為身體欠安與年長肢體代禱，求主醫治他們的疾病與軟弱，賜下安慰、力量與復原，特別保守年邁者免受病痛折磨，經歷神的醫治與平安。Pray for the unwell and elderly, that their illnesses may be healed and experience God’s healing and peace.
