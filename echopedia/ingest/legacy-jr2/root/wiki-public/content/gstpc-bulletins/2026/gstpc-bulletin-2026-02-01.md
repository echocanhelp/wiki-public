> 02-01-2026 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · February 1, 2026 神向你所要的是什麼？What Does the Lord Require of You? (彌迦書 Micah 6:1-8) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 下午一點召開小會，請長老留步參加。 Board meeting at 1pm today. Elders and deacons, please attend. 春季會員大會預計在2月8日召開，請各部會、團契、小組將2025年年度報告於今天截止，請盡快將報告交給小會書記邱瑜嫩長老。Our spring congregational meeting will be on February 8th (Sun). All departments, fellowships, and small group leaders, please submit your 2025 annual reports to Elder Naomi by today. 2月7日禮拜六早上十點在教會春季大掃除，歡迎加入一起幫忙，在過年前整理教會環境，可以參加者，請在LINE群組中報名，有提供午餐。Everyone is welcome to join our church’s annual cleaning, held this Saturday (2/7) at 10am. Please sign up in LINE for the lunch headcount. 2月15日農曆新年主日，歡迎兄姊邀請家人一起來教會崇拜過年。February 15th is Lunar New Year Sunday. All relatives and friends are invited to celebrate with us. 2月18日到4月2日大齋節期從 Ash Wednesday 到復活節共40天，基督徒通過守齋、施捨、刻苦等方式來紀念主耶穌受難、受死到復活的過程。Lent will be from February 18th (Ash Wednesday) to April 2nd (Easter). It is a time for believers to remember the suffering, death, and resurrection of our Jesus Christ through fasting, charity, and self-discipline. 鼓勵兄姊攜帶個人的聖經參加禮拜，以便在聽道和讀經時能隨時翻閱，摘錄心得，領受聖經的教導， 得到屬靈餵養。Please bring your own Bible to service to better help you take notes and engage with the Scriptures. 教會配合台灣芥菜種會新春紅包募集活動，已於上週結束。感謝兄姐的支持，募得 190 份祝福紅包，總計$3,880，於昨日轉交芥菜種會，透過祝福紅包，溫暖鼓勵脆弱家庭的孩子勇敢邁向新的一年。Our fundraiser with Taiwan’s Mustard Seed Church concluded last week with 190 blessing packets, a total of $3,880 which was sent to the church yesterday. 代禱 Intercession 為二月份即將開始的「門徒訓練」禱告，求神透過《不再一樣》課程，帶領信徒學習聆聽與順服，轉化為跟隨主的門徒，經歷靈命的更新與突破。Pray for the “Discipleship Training” starting in February. Ask God to use the Experiencing God course to guide believers in learning to listen and obey, transforming them into true followers of the Lord who experience spiritual renewal and breakthroughs. 求神祝福英語與兒童事工，吸引更多的年輕夫婦及兒童加入教會，共同邁向「福音世代傳承」的目標。Pray for God’s blessing on English Ministry and Children’s Ministry. May He attract more young couples and children to join our church as we move together toward the goal of “Passing on the Gospel Legacy.” 求神帶領2026教會事工計畫，在聚會人數、靈命成長、團契關係上成長，順服神的心意，經歷聖靈的更新與突破。Pray for God’s guidance over 2026‘s ministry plans.
