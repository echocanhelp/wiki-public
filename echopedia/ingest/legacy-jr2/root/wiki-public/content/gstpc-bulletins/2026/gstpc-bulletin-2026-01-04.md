> 01-04-2026 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · January 4, 2026 我們的神聖身份 Our Divine Identity (以弗所書 Ephesians 1:3-14) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 本主日是新年的第一個主日，願上帝賞賜兄弟姊妹恩典滿滿的一年。This Sunday is the first Sunday of the new year. May God bless our brothers and sisters with a year full of grace. 本主日舉行新年度新任長執和同工就任，新任長老: 黃義弘 (3年)、王信心 (2年)；執事：古豐榮 (3年)、邱冠銘 (2年)、Dorine Takasugi (1年)，請代禱。Today we installed our church’s new elders, deacons, and coworkers. Elders: John Huang (3 years), Julia Wang (2 years). Deacons: Rick Ku (3 years), Kuan-Ming Chiu (2 years), Dorine Takasugi (1 year). Please pray for them. 禮拜後成人主日學時間是小組分組，以討論主日信息為主，請兄姊踴躍參與。After the service, small groups will discuss the Sunday sermon. All are encouraged to participate. 午餐後下午一點在教會召開新年度第一次長執會，請2026年長執留步出席。Board meeting at 1pm today; 2026 elders and deacons, please attend. 2025 年度的奉獻接受至1月11日截止，支票上請註明是 2025 年的奉獻。Deadline for 2025 offerings is on January 11th. Please specify “2025 offering” in the memo. 代禱 Intercession 為2025-2027年度目標「造就門徒、世代傳承」禱告，同心努力、完成目標。Pray for our church’s 2025-2027 goal of “Making Disciples, Passing on to Generations.” 為新就任的長執同工禱告，同心合意傳揚福音，建立合一的團隊。Pray for the newly-elected coworkers, that they may work together in spreading the gospel and building a unified team. 為台語、英語事工、成人及兒童主日學、團契、小組、禱告會、QT查經班禱告，成全聖徒、各盡其職，建造成熟的屬靈生命。 Pray for the Taiwanese Ministry, English Ministry, adult and children’s Christian education, fellowships, cell groups, prayer meetings, and QT Bible study groups, so that all believers may be equipped, fulfill their spiritual roles, and build up spiritual maturity. 為身體軟弱的肢體禱告，求神賜下醫治大能。Pray for God’s healing on all those who are physically ill or weak.
