> 04-05-2026 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · April 5, 2026 晨光中遇見復活的主 Meeting the Risen Lord (約翰福音 John 20:1-18) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 本主日復活節主日，清晨以朝陽禮拜等待日出象徵主耶穌的復活, 結束後一起享用早餐； 十點讚美禮拜由詩班將獻唱由 Lloyd Larson 寫作的復活節清唱劇「無比大愛 No Greater Love」，一起迎接主耶穌的復活。This Sunday is Easter Sunday. Our sunrise service will start at 6am, followed by breakfast and our 10am service. The choir will present Lloyd Larson’s Easter cantata “No Greater Love” as we celebrate the resurrection of the Lord Jesus together. 4月12日召開定期長執會，午餐後一點請現任長執留步參加。Board meeting on April 12th at 1pm; elders and deacons, please attend. 第二季事工和活動 Second Quarter Events: 5月10日 – 母親節 Mother’s Day 5月16日禮拜六 – 台灣傳統週園遊會 Taiwanese Heritage Festival 6月21日 – 父親節 Father’s Day 代禱 Intercession 為國際局勢與社會和諧禱告，求主憐憫墨西哥的暴亂與中東地區動盪，也帶領美國在政治理念分歧中仍能彼此尊重、止息對立，使社會回到公義與平安。Pray for international relations and social harmony, asking the Lord to have mercy on the unrest in Mexico and turmoil in the Middle East, and to lead the United States to respect one another despite political differences, cease confrontation, and return society to justice and peace. 為「門徒訓練—不再一樣」課程禱告，求主堅固每位學員與同工的心志，使課程帶來生命更新，幫助教會更實際地落實「造就門徒、世代傳承」的目標。Pray for the Discipleship Training course, that God will strengthen the hearts of every participant and coworker, bring renewal, and help disciple the future generations. 為兒童、青少年、和英語事工禱告求主保守孩子與青少年的身心靈，賜智慧給老師與家長，幫助他們建立穩固的屬靈根基，更深認識神、愛神、跟隨神。Pray for Children’s Ministry, Youth Ministry, and English Ministry, that God will protect their minds, bodies, and spirits, to grant wisdom to teachers and parents, to help them build a solid spiritual foundation, and to know, love, and follow God more deeply. 為身體欠安與年長肢體代禱，記念正在病痛、恢復、或長期不適的弟兄姊妹，以及年長兄姐，求主賜下看顧與醫治的恩典，也加添家人與照顧者力量，使他們得平安與安慰。Pray for those who are unwell or elderly. Remember our brothers and sisters who are sick, recovering, or suffering from chronic illness, and ask the Lord to grant them care, healing, and strength.
