> 04-12-2026 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · April 12, 2026 有福的確據 Blessed Assurance (彼得前書 1 Peter 1:3-9) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 午餐後一點召開定期長執會，請現任長執留步參加。Board meeting today at 1pm; elders and deacons, please attend. 下主日午餐後一點門徒訓練「不再一樣」合班上課，請學員準時參加。Participants in Discipleship Training will meet next Sunday after lunch. 第二季事工和活動 Second Quarter Events: 5月10日 – 母親節 Mother’s Day 5月16日禮拜六 – 台灣傳統週園遊會 Taiwanese Heritage Festival 6月21日 – 父親節 Father’s Day 代禱 Intercession 為美國、以色列、伊朗的戰爭以及荷莫茲海峽封鎖引發油價飆漲禱告，求主早日化解衝突與危機。Pray for the soaring oil prices caused by the international conflict, that God would resolve the crises as soon as possible. 為門徒訓練「不再一樣」課程禱告，經歷靈命更新，共同邁向「造就門徒、世代傳承」的目標。Pray that those in the Discipleship Training course will experience spiritual renewal and unity to help disciple future generations. 為5/16台美傳統週園遊會的籌備與參與禱告，求神保守整體規劃、同工配搭與活動進行都順利，藉此活動認識更多的台灣鄉親。Pray for the Taiwanese Heritage Festival on 5/16, that God will help us in the planning process and coworkers coordinations so the event would go smoothly. 為兒童、青少年、英語事工禱告，幫助下一代建立屬靈根基，更多認識神、愛神、跟隨神。Pray for Children’s Ministry, Youth Ministry, and English Ministry, that they will have an established spiritual foundation to know, love, and follow God. 為身體欠安與年長肢體代禱，求主醫治他們的疾病與軟弱，賜下安慰、力量與復原，特別保守年邁者免受病痛折磨，經歷神的醫治與平安。Pray for the unwell and elderly, that their illnesses may be healed and experience God’s healing and peace.
