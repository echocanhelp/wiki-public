> 05-17-2026 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · May 17, 2026 因為祂顧念你們 Because He Cares for You (彼得前書 1 Peter 4:12-19, 5:6-11) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 午餐後一點門徒訓練 「不再一樣」合班上課，請學員準時參加。Discipleship Training meeting today after lunch; participants, please be there on time. 6月22日父親節主日，歡迎弟兄姊妹邀請自己的爸爸和親友，參加主日崇拜和慶祝活動。Father’s Day is June 22nd! All are welcomed to invite their fathers, relatives, and friends to join in our Sunday worship and celebrations. 6月28日下午一點到三點在教會舉辦敬拜讚美同工訓練會，請現任參與服事的同工和受邀新同工務必預留時間參加。A P&W training workshop will be held at church on June 28th (Sun) from 1-3pm. Current worship team and newly-invited coworkers, please attend. 代禱 Intercession 為牧師、長老與同工代禱，領受清楚異象，堅定信心，也為「不再一樣」門徒訓練禱告，帶領教會邁向「造就門徒、世代傳承」目標。Pray for the pastor, elders, and coworkers, that they may have a clear vision and strengthened faith for the future. Pray also for Discipleship Training for the church’s goal of educating disciples and passing on to future generations. 為母親與長輩們獻上感恩，求主賜福身心靈都健壯；也為兒童、青少年及英語事工代禱，幫助年輕的一代建立穩固根基，認識並跟隨神。Pray for all our mothers, that God will bless them with physical and spiritual health. Pray also for Children’s Ministry, Youth Group, and English Ministry, that they will have a solid foundation to know and follow God. 求主保守美國的政治穩定與民主制度，賜予領袖敬畏神的心與治國智慧，平息社會對立，讓教會在期中選舉年發揮光與鹽的作用。也求主保守故鄉台灣，在動盪的國際局勢中得到保護與平安。Pray for the United States’ political stability, that the leaders will revere God and have the wisdom to lead and govern according to His will. Pray that the church will be the light and salt during the midterm elections. Also pray for the Lord’s protection and peace in our homeland Taiwan. 為身體欠安與年長肢體禱告，求主醫治、安慰、賜平安，讓他們在軟弱中經歷神的同在與扶持。Pray for the unwell and elderly, that their illnesses may be healed and experience God’s healing and peace.
