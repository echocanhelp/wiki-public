We will commemorate Jesus’ sacrifice on the cross for the sins of the world and to complete God’s work of redemption. 4月5日復活節讚美禮拜慶祝主耶穌的復活，今年將以朝陽禮拜的方式來進行, 禮拜清晨六點開始, 等待日出象徵主耶穌的復活, 禮拜結束後大家一起享用早餐, 詩班將獻唱由 Lloyd Larson 寫作的復活節清唱劇「無比大愛 No Greater Love」，歡迎邀請親友一起來參加。Easter Sunday is on April 5th and we will celebrate the resurrection of our Lord Jesus. This year, it will be a sunrise service starting at 6am, and we will enjoy breakfast together after the service.
