> 07-12-2020 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · July 12, 2020 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「 這事以後 After All This 」 王光智牧師 Pastor Wang 創世記 Genesis 15:1 消息報告 Announcements 由於加州疫情及確診情況尚未穩 定，實體聚會日期延後至 9月 6日， 本會將繼續參加 TPC聯合主日禮拜 及週間聚會至八月底，小會將持續 觀察疫情發展，若有變動會盡快通 知會友。Because of the current uncertainties and epidemic cases in California, the session has decided to postpone our church reopening until September 6. If there are any new decisions, our brothers and sisters will be notified. 求主賜下異象，引領教會在疫情衝擊下，活出基督的愛，成為 鄰舍的祝福，並且保守牧者及長執同工的配搭。 Pray for the Lord to continue leading the church to live out the love of Christ under the epidemic’s impact, to be a blessing to our neighbors, and to keep the pastor and co-workers working together in harmony. 為年底美國總統大選代禱，求主興起行公義、好憐憫、存謙卑 的心，與神同行的領袖，同時為美國醫療體系及醫護人員代 禱，求神加添力量並保守他們平安。 Pray for the U.S. presidential election at the end of the year, and ask the Lord to raise up leaders who lead with justice, mercy, and humility in their walks with God; also pray for the American medical system and medical personnel, and ask God to strengthen them and keep them safe. 為疫情期間失去工作或被迫休假的弟兄姊妹禱告，求主保守他 們的心、帶領前面的路、賜每日夠用的恩典。Pray for the brothers and sisters who lost their jobs during the epidemic, and ask the Lord to watch over them and give them grace every day. 為鄧淑貞長老在安養院感染 Covid-19禱告，求神的憐憫及醫治。Pray for Elder Deng’s infection with Covid-19 in the nursing home—ask for God’s mercy and healing. 教會奉獻方法如下: (若有任何問題, 請聯絡 Henry Liao 或 Julia Wang) Offering methods: (please direct all questions to Henry Liao or Julia Wang) 支票郵寄教會信箱 (mail checks only, NO CASH please) Good Shepherd Taiwanese Presbyterian Church PO Box 669 Temple City, CA 91780-0669 線上奉獻 (Zelle online donation) Email: 606gstpc@gmail.com 教會行事曆 Church Calendar TUE (7/14) 8:00pm 拉比食堂 Rabbi’s Café (董俊蘭牧師) WED (7/15) 8:00pm 禱告會 Prayer Night (邱瑜嫩長老) THU (7/16) 11:00am 山姆的聖詩音樂 Hymn Music With Sam Sun (孫偉志老師) SUN (7/19) 10:00am 聯合禮拜 Sunday Worship Service (李洋誌牧師) SUN (7/19) 11:15am 成人主日學—登山寶訓 Adult Sunday School: Sermon on the Mount (許明遠牧師)
