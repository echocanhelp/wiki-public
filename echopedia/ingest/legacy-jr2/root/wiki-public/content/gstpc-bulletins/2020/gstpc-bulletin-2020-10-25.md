> 10-25-2020 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · October 25, 2020 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「不要為明天憂慮 Do Not Worry About Tomorrow」 謝信中牧師 Pastor Hsieh 彼得前書 1 Peter 5:7-9 腓立比書 Philippians 4:6-7 消息報告 Announcements 週五（10/30）上午10:00am有「拉比分享小組」，歡迎兄姊參加。This Friday (10/30), the Rabbi Sharing Group will meet on Line at 10:00am. Brothers and sisters are welcome to participate. 週六（10/31）上午10:00am在好牧者教會舉行秋季和會，9:30am開始報到，不方便出席者，可在家透過網路同步參加和會。This Saturday (10/31), our annual fall congregational meeting will be held at church. Registration starts at 9:30am, the meeting at 10am. Those who cannot in person can join the meeting online from home. 夏令時間的日光節約制（daylight saving）將在11月1日結束，記得週六晚上將時鐘撥慢一小時。Daylight saving time will end on November 1. Remember to turn your clocks back by one hour on Saturday night. TPC訂於11月7日（週六）上午9:00-11:00舉行「長執同工訓練會」，莊信德牧師主講，歡迎長執、同工、會友自由參加。TPC Committee is holding a staff training meeting on 11/7 (Sat) from 9-11am, led by Pastor Chuang. Pastors and coworkers alike are welcome to join and participate. 「拉比食堂」及「山姆的聖詩音樂」將在十月底暫時告一段落，感謝孫偉志老師和直播團隊同工的辛勞。 The two online programs Rabbi’s Cafe and Sam’s Hymn Music will temporarily stop at the end of October. Many thanks to Sam Sun and the broadcasting team for all their hard work. 請為11/3美國總統大選禱告，求神保守選舉過程和平，減緩社會衝突及種族歧見，並選出合神心意的領袖。Please pray for the 11/3 presidential election, that God will make the election process peaceful, will alleviate social conflicts and racial discrimination, and choose a leader that follows after His heart.. 為COVID-19疫情的控制，以及開發病毒疫苗的專家禱告，願主賜下智慧。Pray for the control of the COVID-19 epidemic, and for God to grant wisdom to the scientists and experts who are developing the vaccine. 教會行事曆 Church Calendar TUE (10/27) 8:00pm 拉比食堂 Rabbi’s Cafe (Yarah牧師) WED (10/28) 8:00pm 禱告會 Prayer Night (蕭錫恩長老) THU (10/29) 11:00am 山姆的聖詩音樂 Hymn Music With Sam Sun (孫偉志老師) FRI (10/30) 10:00am 拉比分享小組 Rabbi’s Cafe Sharing Group (許明遠牧師) FRI (10/30) 8:00pm QT 小組 (林幼麗姐妹) SUN (11/01) 10:00am TPC 聯合禮拜 Sunday Worship Service (蔡尚男牧師)
