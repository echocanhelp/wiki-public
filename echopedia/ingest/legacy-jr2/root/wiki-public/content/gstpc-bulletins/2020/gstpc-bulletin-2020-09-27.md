> 09-27-2020 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · September 27, 2020 主日信息 Sunday Sermon 「早未曾，今欲 I Will Which Have Not Yet」 李洋誌牧師 Pastor Lee 約珥書 Joel 2:28-32, 3:16-21 消息報告 Announcements 10月4日是「世界聖餐主日」暨「TPC 50週年紀念主日」，邀請PCT總會副議長徐信德牧師證道，NTHC泰國教團主席與TPC傳教師會共同服事，請兄姊事先預備餅與杯，一起在家中領受聖餐。World Communion Sunday and TPC’s 50th Anniversary Sunday will be celebrated on October 4th. PCT’s vice president Rev. Hsu will preach that day, and chairman of NTHC and TPC will lead us in taking communion. Brothers and sisters are encouraged to prepare bread and cups in advance so we may receive the sacrament together at home. 本週五 (10/2)上午 10:00am 拉比分享小組，晚上8:00pm QT小組，歡迎參加。 The Rabbi Sharing Group will be meeting this Friday (10/2) at 10:00am, welcome brothers and sisters to join. TPC常委會訂於10/3(週六)上午10點用Zoom召開會議，請教會代表出席。 The TPC Standing Committee is scheduled to hold a meeting with Zoom at 10 am on 10/3 (Saturday). Church representatives are invited to attend. 美國總統選舉進入倒數計時，求神賜給國家及百姓平安，保守選舉過程順利，並選出合神心意的領袖。Pray for the presidential election in November, that God would grant peace to this nation, make the election process smooth, and choose a leader that follows after His heart. 為COVID-19疫情控制、醫護人員的安全、疫苗研究的進度、政府和決策者有智慧的領導、家庭和教會適應新的生活方式、企業和家庭面對財務壓力、服務業人員的安全代禱。Pray for the control of the COVID-19 epidemic, the safety of medical staff, the progress of vaccine research, the intelligent leadership of the government and decision makers, families and churches to adapt to the new lifestyle, the financial pressure of enterprises and families, and the safety of service personnel. 為台灣政局安全及中共軍機繞台挑釁禱告，求神保守賜下平安在台灣這塊土地，以及住在台灣的鄉親。 Pray for the security of Taiwan’s political situation and the provocation of military planes around Taiwan, and ask God to protect that country and the people living there. 教會行事曆 Church Calendar TUE (9/29) 8:00pm 拉比食堂 Rabbi’s Cafe (許信靖牧師) WED (9/30) 8:00pm 禱告會 Prayer Night (李郁文姊妹) THU (10/1) 11:00am 山姆的聖詩音樂 Hymn Music With Sam Sun (孫偉志老師) FRI (10/2) 10:00am 拉比分享小組 Rabbi Sharing Group (許明遠牧師) FRI (10/2) 8:00pm QT 小組 (林幼麗姐妹) SUN (10/4) 10:00am 慶祝五十週年禮拜 TPC’s 50th Anniversary Sunday Worship Service (徐信德牧師)
