> 10-13-2024 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · October 13, 2024 至好的朋友 Best Friend (希伯來書 Hebrews 4:12-16) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 禮拜後成人主日學有兩個課程: 信仰要理問答 (英) 和主日信息回應分享，歡迎兄姊一起信仰造就。 We have two Christian Education classes after service: Catechism (English) and Sunday message sharing. All are encouraged to participate. 10月19日是今年最後一次的姊妹團契聚會，特別邀請Angela和Peter分享音樂與園藝Q&A，歡迎大家對音樂、花草、植物、水果種植有關的問題一起來學習。October 19th will be the last Sisters’ Fellowship of the year. Sister Angela and Brother Peter have been invited to share and answer Q&A about music and gardening. Everyone is welcome to attend and learn. 10月20日秋季會員大會，會中牧師將分享新年度的主題和異象並改選新年度同工，請會員務必出席, 會員名冊張貼在布告欄上，有任何問題請洽書記邱長老。Our fall congregational meeting will be on October 20th, and our pastor will share next year’s theme, vision, and coworkers. The member list is posted on the bulletin wall, and all members are required to attend the meeting. 英語事工部於10月3日到11月17日將發起救世軍捐贈的活動，請到招待桌看說明有關接受捐贈的物品清單，請兄姊響應此次捐贈活動。English Ministry is hosting a donation event to the Salvation Army from October 13th to November 17th. Please refer to the list of acceptable donation items at the reception table. 12月15日舉行聖禮典，有感動接受成人、幼兒洗禮、以及轉籍的兄姊，請儘速向牧師或Naomi長老報名，以便安排上課時間。We will observe baptism on December 15th. Brothers and sisters who would like to receive adult or infant baptism, or change church membership, please register with the pastor or Elder Naomi as soon as possible so class time can be arranged. 中會發函請各教會在11月的總統大選是按著信心投票，而不是按著黨派來投票，10月16日7:00-8:30pm 有一個Zoom上舉行的英語討論會，歡迎參加。San Gabriel Presbytery has sent a letter asking all churches to vote by faith rather by party in the November presidential election. The English discussion on Zoom will be on October 16th from 7:00-8:30pm. Welcome all to attend. 代禱 Intercession 請繼續為台灣山陀兒颱風過後受到災情影響的地區和百姓來代禱。Please continue to pray for the areas in Taiwan affected by Typhoon Krathon. 為今年秋季大會中長執改選，求上帝興起祂所呼召的工人。Pray that God will raise up the most suitable coworkers during our election in our fall congregational meeting. 為11月美國總統大選代禱，求上帝帶領掌權為美國選出一位合神心意的領袖。Pray for the U.S. presidential election in November, that God will lead our nation to elect a leader who follows Him. 為11月2日年度長執同工訓練會，為教會同工的裝備和明年事工的計畫代
