> 09-29-2024 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · September 29, 2024 有人在替你禱告 Someone is Praying for You (雅各書 James 5:12-20) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 禮拜後成人主日學有三個課程: 信仰要理問答 (英)，主日信息回應 分享，和從古典音樂認識聖經的 故事「像電影配樂般的聖詩」 (孫偉志)，歡迎兄姊一起信仰造就。 We have three Christian Education classes after service: Catechism (English), Sunday message sharing, and the Bible Stories through Classical Music class led by Brother Sam Sun – “The Hymn Like a Movie Soundtrack By Ralph Vaughan Williams.” All are encouraged to participate. 禮拜後下午一點召開小會，請現任長老出席。Session will have a meeting at 1pm today; elders, please attend. 下週日10月6日世界共同聖餐主日教會將舉辦野外禮拜在 Craig Regional Park in Fullerton，在關懷網已經開始讓大家報名，歡迎邀請親友一同參加。若有長輩需要交通服務的，請讓任何一位長執知道，同工會盡力為大家安排。Next Sunday (10/6) is World Communion Sunday and we will have our outdoor service at Craig Regional Park in Fullerton. Please sign up for headcount in our LINE group; all friends and relatives are welcome! If you need transportation, please let an elder know! 10月20日秋季會員大會，會中牧師將分享新年度的主題和異象並改選新年度同工，請會員務必出席。Our fall congregational meeting will be on October 20th, and our pastor will share next year’s theme, vision, and coworkers. All members are required to attend. 英語事工部於10月13日到11月17日將發起救世軍捐贈的活動，請到招待桌看說明有關接受捐贈的物品清單，請兄姊響應此次捐贈活動。English Ministry is hosting a donation event to the Salvation Army from October 13th to November 17th. Please refer to the list of acceptable items at the reception table. 代禱 Intercession 為今年秋季大會中長執改選，求上帝興起祂所呼召的工人。Pray that God will raise up the right coworkers during our coworkers election at our fall congregational meeting. 為11月美國總統大選代禱，求上帝帶領掌權為美國選出一位合神心意的領袖。Pray for the U.S. presidential election in November, and ask God to lead our nation to elect a leader who follows Him. 為11月2日年度長執同工訓練會，為教會同工的裝備和明年事工的計畫代禱。Pray for our church’s annual coworkers training meeting on November 2nd and next year’s ministry plans. 為第四季進入感恩節和聖誕節期許多事工的計畫和進行代禱。Pray for our Thanksgiving and Christmas events in the fourth quarter. 求神憐憫及醫治古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、藍老師，保守身體平安。Ask God for His healing and protection on Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, and Teacher Lan.
