Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 本主日舉行聖餐，感謝主耶穌成為我們的救贖，也透過聖餐來紀念主。We observed Holy Communion this Sunday, and thank and remember the Lord Jesus for being our Savior. 禮拜結束後休息一下隨時要召開春季會員大會，會中將報告2023年各部會簡報並審查通過2024年預算。We will have our spring congregational meeting after our morning service, and will be going over 2023 department reports and 2024 budget and plans. 成人主日學本主日暫停一次。No Christian Education this Sunday. 下主日牧師赴洛杉磯教會講道及主持和會，Jeremy Wang受邀主日講道，請代禱。Next Sunday, our pastor is preaching at the LA church, so Jeremy Wang has been invited to share next Sunday. Brothers and sisters are welcome to participate. 教會有英文崇拜在chapel聚集，歡迎參加。English worship service meets in the chapel, all are welcome to attend. 活泉團契將舉辦郵輪旅遊, 9/2-9/6 Royal Caribbean四夜Catalina & Ensenada Cruise，歡迎邀請親友一起參加此次活動，有任何問題或報名請直接洽詢旅行社 Kevin弟兄 (626-838-3888)。Living Springs Fellowship is organizing a Royal Caribbean four-night Catalina & Ensenada Cruise from 9/2-9/6.
