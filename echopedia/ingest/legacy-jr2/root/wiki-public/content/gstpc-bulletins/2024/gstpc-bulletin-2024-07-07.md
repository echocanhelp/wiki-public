Please pray for him. 7月14日主日教會所持續支持的巴西宣教士Frank Lin 將在本會講道，請繼續為他們全家在巴西的服事代禱。Brother Frank Lin, the missionary our church has been supporting, will be sharing next Sunday. Please continue to pray for their family’s ministry in Brazil. 胡紹禎弟兄告別禮拜訂於7月21日，主日下午1:00PM在教會舉行，請兄姊留步參加。Brother Hu’s memorial service will be held at our church on July 21st (Sunday) at 1pm. Brothers and sisters are invited to stay and attend. 7月27日禮拜六上午十點姊妹團契和G-Coffee聯合活動，請兄姊踴躍參加。Sisters’ Fellowship and G-Coffee will have a joint event on July 27th (Saturday) at 10am. Brothers and sisters are invited to participate. 今年暑期兒童夏令營 One Way將於 8/7-8/9 (週三-週五）舉辦，鼓勵兄姐主動積極邀請親友及社區中的孩子，藉著夏令營一同領受福音的祝福。報名費 $50 至 7/15截止，請大家告訴大家。也請代禱求神預備要來參加的小朋友。Our children’s summer camp this year will be held from 8/7-8/9 (Wednesday-Friday) with the theme “One Way.” All are encouraged to invite relatives, friends, and children in the community to join and hear about the gospel. Registration fee is $50 and the deadline is 7/15.
