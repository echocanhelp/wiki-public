> 01-07-2024 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · January 7, 2024 這是天父世界 This is My Father’s World (啟示錄 Revelation 21:1-5) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 本主日舉行新任長執和同工就任，今年新受選長老: 廖蓓理、蕭錫恩、邱瑜嫩；執事: 莊永立、李郁文、王子銘。以上長執任期均為3年任，請為新任長執禱告。New coworkers are being installed today. The newly-elected elders this year are: Henry Liao, Eric Hsiao, and Naomi Chiu; deacons: Joseph Chuang, Christina Lee, and Jeremy Wang (deacon terms are all three years). Please pray for the new deacons. 2024年新任團契小組同工如下 2024 Fellowship Coworkers: [names redacted] 下主日英語事工在chapel開始英文崇拜，歡迎參加，求上帝藉此祝福講英文的弟兄姊妹，也請代禱。English Ministry will be starting their English service in the chapel next Sunday. May God bless all the English-speaking brothers and sisters through this, and please pray for them. 下主日午餐結束後下午一點在教會舉行長執會，請2024年所有新任長執出席。Next Sunday, there is a board meeting at 1pm at church. All new deacons are invited to attend. 宗教教育主日學第一季將從1/21開始，有三個課程:信仰要理問答（分台英兩組）和主日信息回應分享（小組）討論的方式進行，歡迎兄姊加入不同的小組上課同成長受造就。任何想法或建議請詢問Henry長老。Christian Education Sunday School will start on January 21 with three courses: Study Catechism (Taiwanese and English classes) and Sunday message sharing discussion classes. We’d like to welcome all brothers and sisters to join different classes. If there are any questions, please feel free to contact Elder Henry. 2月10日和17日兩個週六下午1點到5點在教會將舉辦敬拜讚美訓練課程，邀請邱英薰老師主講，歡迎目前隊員和有意學習敬拜讚美的兄弟姊妹一起參加, 請向Tina和Lulu報名。The P&W workshops will be held at church on February 10th and 17th, taught by Joy Chiu. Current team members and those who are interested are welcome to participate. Please sign up with Tina or Lulu. 全國台灣人長老教會聯合會(NTPC)，2024 年是NTPC第50屆年會，將於4月26日-5月4日於南加州牧谷長老教會召開年會，詳細內容請看公佈欄。The annual conference of National Taiwanese Presbyterian Church (NTPC) will be held from April 26th to May 4th at Shepherd of the Valley. Please see the bulletin board for details. 代禱 Intercession 1月13日是台灣總統大選，求上帝為台灣揀選公義正直合祂心意的未來總統。January 13th is Taiwan’s presidential election. Pray that God will choose a leader who is righteous, upright, and does His will. 為2024年新同工的服事和新事工的計畫與進行，一切走在上帝的旨意中。Pray for the new coworkers and 2024 ministry events, that everything will be in God’s will. 為古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、胡紹禎、藍老師以及身體欠安的兄姊代禱。Pray for Bro
