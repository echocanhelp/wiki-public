> 07-28-2024 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · July 28, 2024 將這些給大家吃！Give It To the People To Eat! (列王記下 2 Kings 4:42-44) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 禮拜後成人主日學有三個課程: 信仰要理問答（英），主日信息回應分享，和從古典音樂認識聖經的故事（孫偉志分享）「德國聖詩的全新感變」，歡迎兄姊參與同成長受造就。We have three Christian Education classes after service: Catechism (English), Sunday message sharing, and a “Bible Stories through Classical Music” class led by Brother Sam Sun – “Transformation of Christian Music Style For the New Era.” All are encouraged to participate and grow together. 今年暑期兒童夏令營 One Way 將於 8/7-8/9（週三-週五）舉辦，目前已有33個小朋友報名，請繼續代禱所有的預備工作進行順利，也帶領老師同工有智慧來服事兒童。Our summer VBS “One Way” will be held from 8/7-8/9 (Wednesday-Friday). Thirty-three children have signed up, and please pray for the teachers’ wisdom in the preparations. 下主日舉行聖餐，請準備心與主耶穌一起坐席。We will observe holy communion next Sunday, please prepare your hearts. 下主日下午一點長執會， 請現任長執留步參加。 Board meeting next Sunday at 1pm; elders and deacons, please attend. 8月24(六)-25(日) 兩天TPC舉辦南加州台灣人聯合靈修會，邀請莊雅堂牧師分享主題「靈命的重建與更新—聖靈探秘」，分別在亞伯蘭較會和洛杉磯教會舉行，詳細資訊貼在公佈欄，歡迎兄姊踴躍參加，請向蕭錫恩長老報名。TPC’s annual conference is on August 24th-25th (Saturday-Sunday), and Pastor Chuang is sharing on “Reconstruction and Renewal of Spiritual Life – Exploration of the Holy Spirit” at Los Angeles TPC. More details are on the bulletin board; please register with Elder Eric to participate. 代禱 Intercession 為8月31日到9月2日Labor Day週末為期三天的教會家庭靈修會，是為年青一代的家庭和孩子所準備的營會，目前有11個家庭報名參加，願一切計畫和進行得上帝保守順利，每個家庭得著滿滿祝福。Pray for the three-day family church retreat from August 31st to September 2nd, a camp for the younger generation of families. We currently have eleven families who have signed up, please pray for them. 為8月23-24日EM Leadership Retreat代禱，求上帝藉此營會培育年青一代的教會領袖。Pray for EM’s Leadership Retreat on August 23rd-24th, and ask God to guide and grow our younger generation of church leaders. 為11月美國總統大選代禱，求上帝帶領掌權為美國選出一位合神心意的領袖。Pray for the U.S. presidential election in November, and ask God to lead our nation to elect a leader who follows Him. 求神憐憫及醫治古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、藍老師，保守身體平安。Ask God for His healing and protection on Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, and Teacher Lan.
