> 06-16-2024 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · June 16, 2024 我兒，我在這裡！Here I Am, My Son! (創世記 Genesis 22:7a) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 成人主日學今日暫停一次。There is no Christian Education class today. 本主日是父親節，感謝各位父親的辛勞，教會準備小禮物贈送給每位父親，願上帝施恩賜福。Today is Father’s Day! We would like to thank all the fathers for their hard work, and the church has prepared a small gift for each father. 本主日慶祝父親節，在本會舉辦「兒童事工 Open House和園遊會」，活動中有許多遊戲，手工和好吃的台灣家鄉味，請留步參加，歡迎今天第一次來的新朋友。In addition to celebrating Father’s Day, Children’s Ministry has an Open House and Garden Party after service with games, crafts, and delicious Taiwanese food. Please stay and join us, and we welcome our new friends. 感謝台灣手語教會陳育麟&羅珮文牧師所作見證分享他們的事工，本會長年支持台灣手語教會，很高興聽見上帝在教會成就奇妙的事。We thank Pastor Chen and Teacher Luo from the Taiwanese Sign Language Church for sharing their ministry testimonies. Our church has supported the Taiwanese Sign Language Church for many years, and we are encouraged to hear what wonderful things God has done. 今年暑期兒童夏令營 One Way將於 8/7-8/9 (週三-週五) 舉辦，鼓勵兄姐主動積極邀請親友及社區中的孩子，藉著夏令營一同領受福音的祝福。早鳥報名費 6/30 前 $35，之後 $50，報名截止日7/15。請大家告訴大家。也請代禱, 求上帝預備要來參加的學生。Our children’s summer camp this year will be held from 8/7-8/9 (Wednesday-Friday) with the theme “One Way.” All are encouraged to invite relatives, friends, and children in the community to join and hear about the gospel. Early bird registration fee is $35 before 6/30, $50 after that, and final deadline is 7/15. Please pray for the students who will attend. 代禱 Intercession 為8月31日到9月2日Labor Day週末為期三天的教會家庭靈修會，願一切計畫和進行得上帝保守順利。Pray for the three-day family church retreat on Labor Day weekend from August 31- September 2. 為11月美國總統大選代禱，求上帝帶領掌權為美國選出一位合神心意的領袖。Pray for the U.S. presidential election in November, and ask God to lead our nation to elect a leader who follows Him. 求神憐憫及醫治古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、藍老師，保守身體平安。Ask God for His healing and protection on Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, and Teacher Lan.
