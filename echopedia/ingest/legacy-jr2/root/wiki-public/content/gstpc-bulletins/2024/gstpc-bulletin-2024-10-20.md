> 10-20-2024 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · October 20, 2024 造就門徒、世代傳承 Making Disciples, Passing Generations (馬太福音 Matthew 28:18-20) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 本主日禮拜後隨即召開秋季會員大會，會中牧師分享新年度的主題和異象，並改選新年度同工，請會員務必出席。Our fall congregational meeting will start right after service today. Our pastor will share next year’s theme and vision, and we will select coworkers for the new year. All registered members are required to stay and attend. 成人主日學本週暫停一次。There are no Christian Education classes today. 下主日邀請王子銘Jeremy執事主日講道，許牧師前往洛杉磯教會講道並主持會員大會。Next Sunday, Deacon Jeremy Wang has been invited to preach. Rev. Hsu will be preaching at LA TPC and presiding over their congregational meeting. 11月2日禮拜六年度長執同工訓練會在教會舉辦，為教會同工的裝備和明年事工的計畫，請新舊任長執和團契小組同工請務必出席。Our annual coworkers’ training meeting will be held on November 2nd (Saturday) at church, equipping coworkers and planning for next year’s ministry. All deacons and fellowship group leaders are requested to attend. 12月15日舉行聖禮典，有感動接受成人、幼兒洗禮、以及轉籍的兄姊，請儘速向牧師或Naomi長老報名，以便安排上課時間。We will observe baptism on December 15th. Brothers and sisters who would like to receive adult/infant baptism or change church membership, please register with the pastor or Elder Naomi as soon as possible so class time can be arranged. 代禱 Intercession 為11月美國總統大選代禱，求上帝帶領掌權為美國選出一位合神心意的領袖。Pray for the U.S. presidential election in November, that God will lead our nation to elect a leader who follows Him. 為第四季進入感恩節和聖誕節期許多事工的計畫和進行代禱。Pray for our Thanksgiving and Christmas events in the fourth quarter. 求神憐憫及醫治古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、藍老師，保守身體平安。Ask God for His healing and protection on Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, and Teacher Lan.
