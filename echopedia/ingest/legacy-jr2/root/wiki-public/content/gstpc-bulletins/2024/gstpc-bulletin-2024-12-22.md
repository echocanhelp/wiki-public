> 12-22-2024 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · December 22, 2024 最美好的禮物 The Most Beautiful Gift (馬太福音 Matthew 1:18-23) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 本主日聖誕節讚美禮拜，慶祝主耶穌降生世間帶來給人類永生的盼望，感謝詩班獻唱，中午有聖誕大餐，請邀請親友留步一同參加。Today’s Christmas Sunday service celebrates the birth of Jesus into the world and brings hope of eternal life to mankind. We thank the choir for the Christmas oratorio. There will be a Christmas luncheon, and everyone is invited to stay. 成人主日學時間今日暫停一次。No Christian education classes today. 午餐後詩班將出發將到五戶家庭報佳音，歡迎兄姊隨同參加，願主保守一路平安將歌聲帶到兄姊的家中。After lunch, the caroling group will be visiting five homes. Brothers and sisters are welcome to participate. 下主日邀請謝信生牧師來本會證道，許牧師前往洛杉磯教會證道及主持長執同工就任。Next Sunday, Pastor Hsieh will be preaching at our church, and Pastor Hsu will be at LA TPC. 12月26-28日英文事工將於Vista舉辦冬季靈修會，主題「Who is Jesus?」請為帶領者和所有參加者代禱，有興趣更多了解內容，請洽 Tina 和 Esther。EM is having their winter retreat from 12/26-28 at Vista this year with the theme “Who is Jesus?” Please keep the leaders and all those attending in prayer. For more information, please ask Tina or Esther. 1月19日主日將舉行新年度新任長執和同工就任，請各位同工預備心參加。We will have our new coworkers’ installation on January 19th (Sunday). 代禱 Intercession 新年度2025-2027 主題「造就門徒、世代傳承」，求上帝賜下祂的心意、異象和同心的同工一同來完成這些事工。The theme of the new year 2025-2027 is “Making Disciples and Passing on to Generations.” We ask God for His vision, heart, and coworkers to fulfill these ministries. 求神憐憫及醫治古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、藍老師、陳夏蓮長老，保守身體平安。Ask God for His healing and protection on Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, Teacher Lan, and Elder Chen.
