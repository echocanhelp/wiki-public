> 09-22-2024 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · September 22, 2024 做個蒙福的人 Be a Blessed One (詩篇 Psalm 1:1-6) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 昨天中秋節烤肉活動許多弟兄姊妹和新朋友參與，感謝主和所有同工的準備讓此活動如此成功。Many brothers and sisters and new friends came to our Mid-Autumn Festival BBQ. We thank God and all the coworkers for making this event such a success. 禮拜後成人主日學有三個課程: 信仰要理問答 (英)，主日信息回應 分享，和從古典音樂認識聖經的 故事「古典歌劇的主角也到教會? 」 (孫偉志)，歡迎兄姊一起信仰造就。 We have three Christian Education classes after service: Catechism (English), Sunday message sharing, and the Bible Stories through Classical Music class led by Brother Sam Sun – “Church Hymns Sung By Classical Opera Characters?” All are encouraged to participate. 午餐時間召開提名委員會討論2025年新長執的提名，請提名委員留步參加。The Nomination Committee’s first meeting will be held during lunch to discuss the nomination of new staff for 2025. Nomination committee members, please attend. 10月6日世界共同聖餐主日教會將舉辦野外禮拜在 Craig Regional Park in Fullerton，在關懷網已經開始讓大家報名，歡迎邀請親友一同參加。World Communion Sunday is on October 6th and we will have our outdoor service at Craig Regional Park in Fullerton. Please sign up for headcount in our LINE group, and relatives and friends are invited to attend! 10月20日秋季會員大會，會中牧師將分享新年度的主題和異象並改選新年度同工，請會員務必參加。Our fall congregational meeting will be on October 20th, and our pastor will share next year’s theme, vision, and coworkers. All members are requested to attend. 代禱 Intercession 為今年秋季大會中長執改選，求上帝興起祂所呼召的工人。Pray that God will raise up the right coworkers during our coworkers election at our fall congregational meeting. 為11月美國總統大選代禱，求上帝帶領掌權為美國選出一位合神心意的領袖。Pray for the U.S. presidential election in November, and ask God to lead our nation to elect a leader who follows Him. 為11月2日年度長執同工訓練會，為教會同工的裝備和明年事工的計畫代禱。Pray for our church’s annual coworkers training meeting on November 2nd and next year’s ministry plans. 為第四季進入感恩節和聖誕節期許多事工的計畫和進行代禱。Pray for our Thanksgiving and Christmas events in the fourth quarter. 求神憐憫及醫治古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、藍老師，保守身體平安。Ask God for His healing and protection on Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, and Teacher Lan.
