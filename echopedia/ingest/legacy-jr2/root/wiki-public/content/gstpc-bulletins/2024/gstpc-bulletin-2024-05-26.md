> 05-26-2024 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · May 26, 2024 我可以差遣誰呢？Whom Shall I Send? (以賽亞書 Isaiah 6:1-8) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 禮拜後成人主日學有三個課程:信仰要理問答 (台英兩組) 和主日信息回應分享，歡迎兄姊加入同成長受造就。We have three Christian Education classes after service: Catechism (Taiwanese and English) and Sunday message sharing. All are encouraged to participate and grow together. 今日下午1:30pm教會G-Coffee福音事工和姐妹會共同邀請林惠琦音樂博士舉辦大鍵琴音樂演奏會，機會難得，請兄姊邀請親友在午餐後留步參加。G-Coffee and Sisters’ Fellowship have invited Dr. Melody Lin to hold a harpsichord concert after service today at 1:30pm. This is a rare opportunity, so please attend after lunch. 胡紹禎弟兄於5/22凌晨安息主懷，求神安慰遺族。Brother Hu rested in the arms of Jesus on the morning of 5/22. 下主日下午一點召開長執會，請現任長執參加。Board meeting next Sunday at 1pm. Elders and deacons, please attend. 下主日將慶祝並為畢業生祝福和贈送禮物，請所有畢業生出席。We will be celebrating and blessing all graduates next Sunday. 6月16日慶祝父親節，在本會舉辦「兒童事工Open House和園遊會」，請邀請新朋友一起參加。Please pray for Children’s Ministry’s Open House on June 16th (Sunday). 廚房從本週日起供餐結束後廚房關閉進行清潔整理，非廚房同工請勿進入，恕不提供個人額外打包和加菜，謝謝配合。Starting from this Sunday, the kitchen will be closed for cleaning after meals are served. Coworkers not working in the kitchen will not be allowed to enter. Personal extra packaging and additional dishes will not be provided. Thank you for your cooperation. 代禱 Intercession 為6月16日「兒童事工 Open House和園遊會」代禱，求上帝帶領新朋友來參加，願神國福音廣傳。Pray for the “Children’s Ministry Open House and Garden Party” on June 16, that the gospel may be spread widely. 為11月美國總統大選代禱，求上帝帶領掌權為美國選出一位合神心意的領袖。Please pray for the U.S. presidential election in November, and ask God to lead our nation to elect a leader who follows Him. 求神憐憫及醫治古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、藍老師，保守身體平安。Ask God for His healing and protection on Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, and Teacher Lan.
