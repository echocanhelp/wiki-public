> 07-21-2024 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · July 21, 2024 空心的逞強 Empty Perseverance (傳道書 Ecclesiastes 12:13b-14, 雅各書 James 4:8a) 王子銘弟兄 Jeremy Wang 消息報告 Announcements 感謝王子銘執事的主日信息，願上帝祝福他學業和服事。We thank Deacon Jeremy Wang for his Sunday sharing. May God bless him in his studies and service. 成人主日學今日暫停一次。There are no Christian Education classes today. 胡紹禎弟兄告別追思禮拜訂於今日下午一點在教會舉行，請兄姊留步參加。Brother Hu’s memorial service at 1pm will be here at church. Brothers and sisters are asked to stay and attend. 今年暑期兒童夏令營 One Way 將於 8/7-8/9（週三-週五）舉辦，目前已有33個小朋友報名，請繼續代禱所有的預備工作進行順利，也帶領老師同工有智慧來服事兒童。Our summer VBS “One Way” will be held from 8/7-8/9 (Wednesday-Friday). Thirty-three children have signed up, and please pray for the teachers’ wisdom in the preparations. 8月4日舉行聖典禮，請準備心與主耶穌一起坐席。Please prepare your hearts for holy communion on August 4th. 8月17日禮拜六上午十點姊妹團契和G-Coffee聯合活動，請兄姊踴躍參加。Sisters’ Fellowship and G-Coffee are having a joint event on August 17th (Saturday) at 10am. Brothers and sisters are invited to participate. 8月24(六)-25(日) 兩天TPC舉辦南加州台灣人聯合靈修會，邀請莊雅堂牧師分享主題「靈命的重建與更新—聖靈探秘」，分別在亞伯蘭較會和洛杉磯教會舉行，詳細資訊貼在公佈欄，歡迎兄姊踴躍參加，請向蕭錫恩長老報名。TPC’s annual conference is on August 24th-25th (Saturday-Sunday), and Pastor Chuang is sharing on “Reconstruction and Renewal of Spiritual Life – Exploration of the Holy Spirit” at Los Angeles TPC. More details are on the bulletin board; please register with Elder Eric to participate. 代禱 Intercession 為8月31日到9月2日Labor Day週末為期三天的教會家庭靈修會，是為年青一代的家庭和孩子所準備的營會，目前有11個家庭報名參加，願一切計畫和進行得上帝保守順利，每個家庭得著滿滿祝福。Pray for the three-day family church retreat from August 31st to September 2nd, a camp for the younger generation of families. We currently have eleven families who have signed up, please pray for them. 為8月16-17日EM Leadership Retreat代禱，求上帝藉此營會培育年青一代的教會領袖。Pray for EM’s Leadership Retreat on August 16th-17th, and ask God to guide and grow our younger generation of church leaders. 為11月美國總統大選代禱，求上帝帶領掌權為美國選出一位合神心意的領袖。Pray for the U.S. presidential election in November, and ask God to lead our nation to elect a leader who follows Him. 求神憐憫及醫治古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、藍老師，保守身體平安。Ask God for His healing and protection on Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, and Teacher Lan.
