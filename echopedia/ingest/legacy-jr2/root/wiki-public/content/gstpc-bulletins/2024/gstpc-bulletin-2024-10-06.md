> 10-06-2024 Sunday Bulletin by Esther · October 6, 2024 你的名在全地何其美 How Majestic is Your Name in All the Earth (詩篇 Psalm 8:1-9) 許明遠牧師 Rev. Ming Yuan Hsu 消息報告 Announcements 本週日世界共同聖餐主日教會在Craig Regional Park主日崇拜和守聖餐，兄弟姊妹在大自然中敬拜讚美獻上感謝上帝。This Sunday is World Common Communion Sunday, and our brothers and sisters worshiped and observed Holy Communion at Craig Regional Park. 10月20日秋季會員大會，會中牧師將分享新年度的主題和異象並改選新年度同工，請會員務必出席。Our fall congregational meeting will be on October 20th, and our pastor will share next year’s theme, vision, and coworkers. All members are required to attend. 英語事工部於10月13日到11月17日將發起救世軍捐贈的活動，請到招待桌看說明有關接受捐贈的物品清單，請兄姊響應此次捐贈活動。English Ministry is hosting a donation event to the Salvation Army from October 13th to November 17th. Please refer to the list of acceptable donation items at the reception table. 代禱 Intercession 山陀兒颱風剛過境台灣帶來大量雨勢，高雄地區災情特別嚴重，請為受影響的地區和百姓來代禱。Super Typhoon Krathon has just passed through Taiwan and Kaohsiung has suffered the most damage so far. Please pray for the affected areas and people. 為今年秋季大會中長執改選，求上帝興起祂所呼召的工人。Pray that God will raise up the right coworkers during our coworkers election at our fall congregational meeting. 為11月美國總統大選代禱，求上帝帶領掌權為美國選出一位合神心意的領袖。Pray for the U.S. presidential election in November, and ask God to lead our nation to elect a leader who follows Him. 為11月2日年度長執同工訓練會，為教會同工的裝備和明年事工的計畫代禱。Pray for our church’s annual coworkers training meeting on November 2nd and next year’s ministry plans. 為第四季進入感恩節和聖誕節期許多事工的計畫和進行代禱。Pray for our Thanksgiving and Christmas events in the fourth quarter. 求神憐憫及醫治古秀偉、王淑玲、鄧淑貞長老、藍老師，保守身體平安。Ask God for His healing and protection on Brother Ku, Brother Wang, Elder Teng, and Teacher Lan.
