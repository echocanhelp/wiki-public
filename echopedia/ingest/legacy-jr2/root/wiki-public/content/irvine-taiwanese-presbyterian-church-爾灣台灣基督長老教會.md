# Irvine Taiwanese Presbyterian Church (爾灣台灣基督長老教會)

**Irvine Taiwanese Presbyterian Church (爾灣台灣基督長老教會, ITPC)** is a Taiwanese Presbyterian congregation serving the Irvine, Laguna Woods, and Laguna Hills area of Orange County, California. Its official materials present the congregation as a Taiwanese-language, Taiwanese-culture, and Christian faith community for Taiwanese immigrants and retirees in Southern California.

Official site: https://www.irvinetpc.org/

## Identity Snapshot
- Chinese name: Irvine 台灣基督長老教會 / 爾灣台灣基督長老教會
- English name: Irvine Taiwanese Presbyterian Church
- Acronym: ITPC
- Tradition: Taiwanese Presbyterian / Presbyterian Church in Taiwan heritage
- Current worship location: Geneva Presbyterian Church, 24301 El Toro Rd., Laguna Hills, CA 92637, USA
- Regular worship: Sunday 10:00 AM
- Worship language and culture: Taiwanese-language worship with Taiwanese hymns, culture, and community life emphasized

## Leadership
- Current pastor listed on the official site: 李輔仁牧師 (Rev. Fu-Jen Li)
  - The church biography states that he began serving at ITPC in August after prior ministry in Houston, Los Angeles, Taiwan, China, and East Malaysia contexts.
  - Before full-time ministry, he worked as a mechanical engineer for fifteen years.
  - His theological education included Logos Evangelical Seminary, Fuller Theological Seminary, and Gordon-Conwell Theological Seminary.
- Historical pastors named in the church history document include [[zheng-quansheng-鄭泉聲|鄭泉聲牧師]], [[guo-yingyan-郭應言|郭應言牧師]], [[zhang-xuanxin-張宣信|張宣信牧師]], and [[chen-meihui-陳美蕙|陳美蕙牧師]].

## Historical Timeline
- 1992–1993: Formosan Presbyterian Church of Orange County explored opening a worshiping branch/mission in the Irvine area because a number of members lived farther south in Irvine.
- January 9, 1994: The Irvine mission opened at Creekside Fellowship Church, with the history document reporting 210 Taiwanese attendees at the opening celebration and about ten families participating at the beginning.
- June–August 1994: 鄭泉聲牧師, recently retired from ministry in Japan, was invited to remain and shepherd the new congregation. In August 1994, members voted to become an independent church named Irvine 台灣基督長老教會.
- November 1994–July 1995: The congregation completed federal incorporation and church-constitution work; the history document reports an average attendance of about 30 at that stage.
- 1997–1998: 郭應言牧師 served after the interim period following 鄭泉聲牧師's return to Japan.
- May 23, 1999: 張宣信牧師 began full-time pastoral service on Pentecost Sunday.
- September 2003: The congregation moved from Creekside Fellowship Church to Tustin Presbyterian Church, using its Social Hall and classroom space for worship and activities.
- 2011–2012: After first establishing senior-community programming near Laguna Woods, the church began using the chapel at Geneva Presbyterian Church in 2011 and, after a year of dual-location worship and congregational discernment, moved to Geneva Presbyterian Church in October 2012.
- 2020: 張宣信牧師 concluded twenty-one years of service; the church history document states that 陳美蕙牧師 then took up pastoral leadership.
- 2025–2026: The official homepage and organization page describe a large senior Taiwanese community with Taiwanese-language worship, Wednesday senior lectures, choir activity, small groups, care ministries, and a 2026 committee structure.

## Worship and Programs
- 主日崇拜: Sunday 10:00 AM at Geneva Presbyterian Church.
- Mother-tongue worship: the homepage describes approximately 120 attendees in Taiwanese-language Sunday worship.
- 春風合唱團 (Spring Wind Choir): weekly Taiwanese-song choir activity, described on the homepage as about 50 participants.
- [[itpc-senior-class-長青教室|長青教室]]: Wednesday senior classroom / lecture ministry, often led by retired physicians, professors, and community experts; the website preserves about 80 lecture-note pages from 2012–2015.
- 關懷小組 / 小組聚會: weekly care and small-group gatherings.
- 查經班: Bible study and prayer meeting materials, including linked Psalm study PDFs.
- 電腦教室: non-religious community computer classes designed especially for older Taiwanese learners, taught primarily in Taiwanese with Chinese and English terminology as needed.
- 拿娥美事工 / care ministry: the homepage describes meal, transportation, day-care, home-care, long-term-care, and dementia-care aspirations under whole-person care.
- Media archives: the site includes sermon, music, video, photo, and publication archive sections, including choir recordings and a 2023 515 memorial booklet link.

## Community Role
ITPC's self-description frames the church as a spiritual and cultural home for Taiwanese people in the Irvine / Laguna Woods area. The homepage describes members as Taiwan-born Christians who migrated to the United States, worked and raised families across the country, and later gathered in the Irvine / Laguna Woods area for retirement, worship, mutual care, and Taiwanese cultural continuity.

The church's 2025 homepage statement explicitly describes ITPC as "台灣人身心靈的故鄉及盼望" — a spiritual, bodily, and emotional home and hope for Taiwanese people. It also names future-facing aims: Taiwanese-friendly worship for elders, neighborhood outreach to new retirees and Irvine-area residents, Taiwanese cultural festivals, multigenerational worship, Taiwan/U.S. mission outreach, whole-person elder care, Taiwanese cultural and faith transmission for children and youth, spiritual-care training, Thanksgiving community programs, and churchwide fellowship travel.
