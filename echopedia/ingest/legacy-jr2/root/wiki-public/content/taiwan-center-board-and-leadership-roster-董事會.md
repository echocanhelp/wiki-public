# Taiwan Center Board and Leadership Roster (董事會)

**Taiwan Center Board and Leadership Roster (董事會)** records the public board and leadership names displayed on the official board page of the [[taiwan-center-foundation-of-greater-los-angeles-大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會|Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會)]].

This page is a roster record, not a set of full biographies. Standalone person pages should be created only when independent public sources support more than a name and title.

## Official Board Description

The Taiwan Center official board page describes the board as thirty-six directors from different fields who guide the center's development with professional knowledge and community commitment. The page frames the board as guardians of Taiwanese cultural transmission and community responsibility.
