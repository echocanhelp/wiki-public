# Taiwan Center Construction Progress (新館建館工程進度)

**Taiwan Center Construction Progress (新館建館工程進度)** records the official February 5, 2026 construction-progress announcement published by the [[taiwan-center-foundation-of-greater-los-angeles-大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會|Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會)]].

This page is a source snapshot. It preserves the public announcement text and framing without adding interpretive claims beyond what the official page states.

## Official Announcement

Date: February 5, 2026

The official page states that the new Taiwan Center building construction is actively underway. It describes the new facility as one that will be equipped with more complete modern facilities, providing the community with a larger, multi-functional activity space that meets the needs of cultural transmission, educational promotion, and community gatherings.

The announcement frames the project as the shared wish and effort of thousands of community members. It states that every step—from planning and design to groundbreaking and construction—has carried community support and expectation.

The page invites community members who wish to contribute to the new-building project to contact the Taiwan Center office for information on donation methods, in order to jointly build a new home for the Taiwanese American community.

## Relation to the Broader Project

This announcement is the public status update that follows the May 2025 groundbreaking ceremony and precedes the expected 2027 completion date referenced in the 2025 annual-gala page. It is the most recent official construction-phase communication captured on the Taiwan Center website.
