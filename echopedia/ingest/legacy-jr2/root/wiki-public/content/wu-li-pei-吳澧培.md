# Wu Li-Pei (吳澧培)

**Wu Li-Pei (吳澧培)** is identified in the official history of the [[taiwan-center-foundation-of-greater-los-angeles-大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會|Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會)]] as the fundraising convener for the Taiwan Center's 1998 founding campaign.

## Identity Snapshot

- Chinese name: 吳澧培
- Romanized name: Wu Li-Pei
- Public role documented by Taiwan Center: president of EverTrust Bank and fundraising convener for the Taiwan Center founding campaign
- Institutional context: Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles / 大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會

## Role in the Taiwan Center Founding

The Taiwan Center's official introduction states that, in 1998, 王桂榮 (Wang Gui-Rong / Kenjohn Wang) donated property in Rosemead, California and 吳澧培, described as president of EverTrust Bank, served as the fundraising convener. More than forty Taiwanese organizations and about one thousand community members raised US$1 million toward the establishment of the Southern California Taiwan Center.

On June 12, 1998, the 南加州台灣會館基金會 was established as a nonprofit institution to own, operate, and manage the Southern California Taiwan Center. The organization later changed its name to 大洛杉磯台灣會館 in 2001.

## Historical Significance

Within the Taiwan Center's official institutional narrative, 吳澧培's role represents the financial and organizational side of Taiwanese American institution-building. The founding campaign combined donated property, community-wide fundraising, and coordination among dozens of Taiwanese organizations, producing a permanent cultural and civic home for Taiwanese Americans in Southern California.
