# Taiwan Center Group Member Network (團體會員網絡)

**Taiwan Center Group Member Network (團體會員網絡)** refers to the organizational-member network listed by the [[taiwan-center-foundation-of-greater-los-angeles-大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會|Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會)]]. The official Taiwan Center group-member page describes the network as spanning different regions, generations, and sectors of Taiwanese American community life.

## Identity Snapshot

- Chinese name: 團體會員網絡
- English descriptive name: Taiwan Center Group Member Network
- Parent institution: Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles / 大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會
- Network type: Taiwanese American civic, cultural, professional, educational, regional, historical, and advocacy organizations
- Location context: Greater Los Angeles and broader Taiwanese American institutional networks

## Official Description

The Taiwan Center's official group-member page states that its organizational members cover many layers of the Taiwanese American community and that the Taiwan Center serves as a contact and cooperation platform among organizations.

The Taiwan Center's official introduction also states that the foundation has more than fifty organizational members and is regarded as a leading institutional hub for Taiwanese American organizations in Los Angeles.

## Historical Significance

The group-member list is a compact map of Taiwanese American associational life in Southern California. It shows the Taiwan Center not only as a building or program provider, but also as an institutional convener linking civic advocacy, cultural preservation, professional networks, historical memory, education, public commemoration, and community service.

For Echopedia, this network is a priority source for future organization pages. Many listed organizations have their own histories, publications, leaders, and event records that can be documented conservatively from public sources.
