# 2025 Los Angeles Taiwanese American Heritage Week (洛杉磯台美人傳統週)

**2025 Los Angeles Taiwanese American Heritage Week (洛杉磯台美人傳統週)** was a public cultural program promoted by the [[taiwan-center-foundation-of-greater-los-angeles-大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會|Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會)]]. The official event page describes it as an annual cultural celebration combining tradition, modernity, and community participation.

## Event Snapshot

- Chinese title: 2025 洛杉磯台美人傳統週
- English descriptive title: 2025 Los Angeles Taiwanese American Heritage Week
- Date listed by Taiwan Center: April 15, 2025
- Event period described by Taiwan Center: multiple activities from May to June 2025
- Host context: Taiwan Center / Taiwanese American community organizations
- Themes: Taiwanese culture, intergenerational participation, cultural identity, community gathering

## Program Themes

The official page describes the Heritage Week as a seven-day or multi-activity series featuring traditional performing arts, modern music, Taiwanese food, cultural workshops, and activities designed for multiple age groups.

The page emphasizes participatory cultural experience, including traditional pastry making, glove-puppet operation, Taiwanese folk songs, and youth forums on Taiwanese American identity.

## Listed Activities

The official page lists activities including:

- senior red-white singing competition
- Taiwanese American Heritage Week fair
- Taiwanese American children's drawing competition
- Ten Drum Art Percussion Group performance
- Taiwanese-language song program
- Taiwan photography classic series
- Taiwanese American Arts Association summer art exhibition

## Historical Significance

This event belongs in Echopedia because it documents how Taiwanese American identity is performed, taught, and renewed in Los Angeles. The page's emphasis on elders, children, youth identity, arts, music, and food makes it a compact public record of cultural transmission across generations.
