# 2024 Taiwan Center Overseas Student Thanksgiving Dinner (海外留學生感恩節餐會)

**2024 Taiwan Center Overseas Student Thanksgiving Dinner (海外留學生感恩節餐會)** was a community-care event hosted by the [[taiwan-center-foundation-of-greater-los-angeles-大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會|Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會)]]. The official event page states that more than 160 Taiwanese overseas students gathered with community representatives at the Taiwan Center.

## Event Snapshot

- Chinese title: 2024年海外留學生感恩節餐會
- English descriptive title: Overseas Student Thanksgiving Dinner
- Date listed by Taiwan Center: December 1, 2024
- Host institution: Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles / 大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會
- Attendance described by Taiwan Center: more than 160 Taiwanese students and community representatives
- Themes: student support, homesickness, community connection, Taiwanese food, volunteer participation

## Event Description

The official page states that Taiwan Center board members personally prepared Taiwanese comfort foods including braised pork rice, chicken rolls and sausage, Hakka stir-fry, and oyster vermicelli. The page describes the meal as helping students relieve homesickness and experience community warmth.

The event also introduced students to Taiwan Center history, mission, and future plans, and used post-meal interactive games to encourage connection. The official page states that some students expressed interest in future volunteer service.

## Historical Significance

This event is significant as a record of how Taiwanese American institutions support newly arrived or temporary Taiwanese students. It shows the Taiwan Center functioning not only as a cultural archive or event venue, but also as a practical care network linking students to community elders, board members, and volunteer opportunities.
