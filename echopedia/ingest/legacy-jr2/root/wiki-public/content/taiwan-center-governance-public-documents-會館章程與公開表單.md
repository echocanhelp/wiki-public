# Taiwan Center Governance and Public Documents (會館章程與公開表單)

**Taiwan Center Governance and Public Documents (會館章程與公開表單)** is a source-index page for governance documents, public forms, and media-use policy materials made available on the official website of the [[taiwan-center-foundation-of-greater-los-angeles-大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會|Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會)]].

This page does not reproduce full legal or application documents. It records the public document categories and summarizes high-level governance-history signals extracted from public PDF files.

## Governance Structure Signals

The main bylaws document identifies the Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles as a California public benefit corporation and organizes governance around offices and purposes, members, member meetings, directors, officers, corporate records and reports, indemnification, insurance, dissolution, conflict of interest, amendments, and general notice/order provisions.

The bylaws define the organization through a membership-governance structure, including membership classification, admission, dues, number of members, membership records, inspection rights, termination procedures, annual and special meetings, notice, quorum, voting, and member action procedures.

The Board of Investment Trustees bylaws state that, over the years, the foundation received donations and funds for public-benefit and foundation purposes and that a Board of Investment Trustees was established to hold, invest, administer, and safeguard foundation assets. The document applies the prudent investor rule and assigns the BIT authority over custody, investment, and management of financial and capital assets, including assets earmarked for the BIT and assets not needed for current operation.

The conflict-of-interest policy states that its purpose is to protect the tax-exempt organization when it considers transactions or arrangements that may benefit private interests of officers or directors or create possible excess-benefit transactions. It defines interested persons, financial interests, disclosure duties, procedures for determining and addressing conflicts, required minutes/records, compensation rules, annual statements, periodic reviews, and use of outside experts.

## Media-Use and Privacy Statement

The official media-use statement describes the Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles as a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization and states that website photos and audiovisual content are drawn from public community activities. The statement frames media use around nonprofit community service, cultural promotion, respectful representation, and participant privacy.

For Echopedia purposes, this statement is important because it clarifies how the Taiwan Center publicly presents event imagery and community documentation.
