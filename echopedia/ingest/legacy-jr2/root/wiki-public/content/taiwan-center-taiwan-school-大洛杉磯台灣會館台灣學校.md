# Taiwan Center Taiwan School (大洛杉磯台灣會館台灣學校)

**Taiwan Center Taiwan School (大洛杉磯台灣會館台灣學校)** is the educational program of the [[taiwan-center-foundation-of-greater-los-angeles-大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會|Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會)]]. The official Taiwan Center website describes the school as a multigenerational learning environment rooted in language, culture, practical skills, and community connection.

## Identity Snapshot

- Chinese name: 大洛杉磯台灣會館台灣學校
- English descriptive name: Taiwan Center Taiwan School
- Parent institution: Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles / 大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會
- Program type: community education / language and cultural school
- Location context: Greater Los Angeles Taiwanese American community

## Program Description

The official school introduction states that the Taiwan School had been operating for eight years and was dedicated to providing a diverse learning environment for the community. The site frames the school around lifelong learning, professional instructors, practical courses, and a warm community atmosphere.

The Taiwan Center's broader institutional introduction identifies 台灣學校 as one of its working groups or program areas, providing Chinese-language, Japanese-language, computer, cooking, choir, sewing, photography, and other courses for the community.

## Cultural Role

The school is significant because it turns the Taiwan Center from a meeting place into an intergenerational learning institution. Its course mix connects language, Taiwanese cultural identity, everyday skills, music, and community participation. In this sense, the school functions as one of the Taiwan Center's primary vehicles for cultural transmission and community continuity.
