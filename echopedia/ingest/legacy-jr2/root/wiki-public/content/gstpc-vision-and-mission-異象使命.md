# GSTPC Vision & Mission (異象使命)

Primary source: https://gstpc.org/home/our-vision/

## Mission Statement
The church publishes the bilingual statement:
- "Gospel Sharing Truth Practicing Community"
- Chinese rendering: 「分享基督福音，遵行聖經教訓的屬靈團契」

This statement aligns with the GSTPC acronym and functions as a public theological/organizational identity summary.

## Interpretation Notes
Based on the published wording, GSTPC’s mission language emphasizes:
1. Gospel proclamation (福音分享)
2. Scriptural obedience/practice (真理實踐)
3. Community formation (屬靈團契)
