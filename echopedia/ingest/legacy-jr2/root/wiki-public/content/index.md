# Echopedia

**The living memory of Taiwanese America.**

Over **124 published pages** of history, people, publications, and stories — built with precision and care.

---

## Why This Exists

This is a community-powered historical engine designed for:

- **Identity & lineage** — Every person documented with Chinese characters + romanized names
- **Living archives** — 323+ GSTPC bulletins spanning decades
- **TAHS institutional memory** — Organizational records, bylaws, IRS documentation, leadership rosters
- **Contributor-friendly workflows** — LINE-first, low-friction, high-quality output

Built on Quartz with custom automation and publishing pipelines that turn raw community knowledge into a beautiful, searchable, permanent record.
