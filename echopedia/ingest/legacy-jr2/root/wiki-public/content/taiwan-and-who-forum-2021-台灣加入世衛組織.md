# 2021 Taiwan and WHO Forum (台灣加入世衛組織的歷程與展望)

**2021 Taiwan and WHO Forum (台灣加入世衛組織的歷程與展望)** was a public program listed by the [[taiwan-center-foundation-of-greater-los-angeles-大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會|Taiwan Center Foundation of Greater Los Angeles (大洛杉磯台灣會館基金會)]]. The official event page frames the program around Taiwan's exclusion from the World Health Organization and the role of overseas Taiwanese Americans in supporting Taiwan's international participation.

## Event Snapshot

- Chinese title: 台灣加入世衛組織的歷程與展望
- English descriptive title: Taiwan Joining the World Health Organization: History and Prospects
- Date listed by Taiwan Center: May 2, 2021
- Host context: Taiwan Center / Taiwanese American community public-affairs programming
- Themes: World Health Organization, Taiwan international participation, global public health, SARS, COVID-19, diaspora advocacy

## Event Description

The official Taiwan Center page states that Taiwan had long been excluded from the World Health Organization, while demonstrating contributions and capacity in global public health. The page references Taiwan's public-health experience from SARS to COVID-19 and notes that Taiwan still could not formally participate in WHO meetings and mechanisms.

The event invited experts and scholars to discuss Taiwan's history of seeking WHO participation, the international political challenges it faced, and ways overseas Taiwanese Americans could support Taiwan's international participation.

## Historical Significance

This event records a public-affairs dimension of Taiwan Center programming. It connects local Taiwanese American community education with Taiwan's global health participation, public diplomacy, and diaspora advocacy.
