# Chapter I: Formosan in Formosa Yesterday（第一章：昨日的台灣人）

Source chapter page for toward-a-community-of-hope.

## Chapter Summary
Chapter I functions as the historical memory base of the entire dissertation. It reconstructs the deep background of Taiwanese/Formosan identity before migration and explains why later diaspora debates cannot be understood apart from inherited historical consciousness.

For Echopedia, this chapter helps anchor later U.S.-based church and identity narratives in longer historical trajectories rather than treating diaspora as a "new start" disconnected from prior experience.

## Why Chapter I Matters for TAHS
- It preserves first-generation framing of Taiwanese historical memory.
- It identifies pre-migration identity grammar later carried into U.S. institutions.
- It supports timeline construction linking homeland experience to diaspora organization.
