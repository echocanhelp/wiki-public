# Conclusion（結論）

Conclusion page for toward-a-community-of-hope.

## Summary
The Conclusion condenses the dissertation's full movement: historical memory (Ch. I), present-tense homeland pressure (Ch. II), migration displacement (Ch. III), institutional church formation (Ch. IV), identity tension (Ch. V), and constructive hope (Ch. VI).

It reframes these as a question of historical agency: how a Taiwanese/Formosan diaspora community can act faithfully and publicly in history rather than remain only reactive.

## Why the Conclusion Matters for TAHS
- It provides a concise synthesis usable as a guide for chapter-level tagging.
- It articulates collective self-determination as both historical and ethical horizon.
- It bridges archival documentation and future-oriented community practice.

## Synthesis Structure (Working)
1. **Historical struggle acknowledged**
   - Community memory is not erased in migration.
2. **Identity and mission integrated**
   - Political and ecclesial languages remain in tension yet mutually informing.
3. **Hope operationalized**
   - Future direction is framed as communal vocation with public responsibility.
