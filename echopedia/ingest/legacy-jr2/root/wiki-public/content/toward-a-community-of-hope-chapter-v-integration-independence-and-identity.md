# Chapter V: Integration, Independence, and Identity（第五章：整合、獨立與身份）

Source chapter page for toward-a-community-of-hope.

## Chapter Summary
Chapter V is the dissertation's identity negotiation engine. It stages the tension among integration into U.S. society, aspirations of Taiwanese self-determination, and church-mediated communal belonging.

This chapter is critical because it prevents simplistic binaries. It shows identity as layered and contested rather than a single political or cultural choice.

## Why Chapter V Matters for TAHS
- It captures first-generation discourse on integration without erasing independence concerns.
- It links political language and ecclesial language in one analytical field.
- It helps model Taiwanese American identity as relational, historical, and institutionally mediated.

## Tension Map (Working)
1. **Integration pressure**
   - Adaptation demands in U.S. social life.
2. **Independence horizon**
   - Ongoing concern for Taiwanese collective political agency.
3. **Identity synthesis attempts**
   - Church/community as site where tension is negotiated, not resolved once-for-all.
