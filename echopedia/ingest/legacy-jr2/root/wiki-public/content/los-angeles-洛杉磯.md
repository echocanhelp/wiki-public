# Los Angeles (洛杉磯)

**Los Angeles (洛杉磯, Luòshānjī)** is a central city in early Taiwanese American Presbyterian church history, especially in the context of Formosan Presbyterian Church in Los Angeles (FPCLA).

## Historical Relevance

In toward-a-community-of-hope, Los Angeles serves as the key site for first-generation Taiwanese immigrant church formation, identity negotiation, and mission experiments.
