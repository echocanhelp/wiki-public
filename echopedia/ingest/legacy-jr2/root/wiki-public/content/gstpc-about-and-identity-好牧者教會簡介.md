# GSTPC About & Identity (好牧者教會簡介)

This page summarizes the church self-description published on the official GSTPC About page.

Primary source: https://gstpc.org/home/about-us/

## Core Identity Statement
- GSTPC describes itself as a church formed by Taiwanese immigrants and international students in Southern California.
- The congregation is located in Monterey Park and emphasizes worship/fellowship in Taiwanese (台語) alongside English ministry context.
- The site frames the church as a Bible-teaching spiritual fellowship focused on prayer, worship, and gospel witness.

## Ministry Formation Themes
From the About-page narrative, core themes include:
1. Rebuilding relationship with God the Father through faith practice.
2. Emphasis on the Holy Spirit’s presence and spiritual growth.
3. Equipping members to share and witness the Gospel in daily life.
4. Intergenerational transmission of Christian faith for second-generation youth/children in the U.S.

## Mentioned Ministry Components
- Noah’s Ark English ministry for children/youth formation.
- Adult Sunday School.
- Choir gathering and practice.
- Senior fellowship (活泉團契).
- Sisters fellowship (姊妹團契).
- Weekday/small-group family gatherings.
